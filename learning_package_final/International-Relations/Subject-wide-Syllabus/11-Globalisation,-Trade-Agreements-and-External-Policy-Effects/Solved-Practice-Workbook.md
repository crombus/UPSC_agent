## PYQS AND ANSWER PRACTICE

**Provenance:** Demand wording and routing below are retained from the original workbook and its audited Basic/Advanced owners; a ledger-only neutral rendering is not a verbatim paper transcription. No official Mains solution or objective key is invented.

### Source-status and institutional recall beyond the question bank

The dated Basic/Advanced owners distinguish WTO MC13 (Abu Dhabi, 26 February–2 March 2024: ministerial declaration and decisions on dispute-settlement reform, special and differential treatment, LDC graduation and e-commerce) from MC14 (Yaoundé, 26–30 March 2026: decisions concerning small economies and SPS/TBT implementation, continued fisheries talks, unresolved e-commerce moratorium and no consensus to incorporate the investment-facilitation agreement despite support from 165 of 166 members). The WTO remains member-driven rather than governed by a corporate board; its ministerial decisions and weakened appellate enforcement are different institutional questions.

The same dated owner lists **India-UAE CEPA** and **India-Australia ECTA** as in force from 1 May and 29 December 2022; **India-EFTA TEPA** from 1 October 2025; **India-UK CETA and its separate Double Contributions Convention** from 15 July 2026.
