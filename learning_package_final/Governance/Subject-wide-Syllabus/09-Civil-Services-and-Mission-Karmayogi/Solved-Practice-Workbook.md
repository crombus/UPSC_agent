### PYQ DEMAND CARD 1 — 2020 General Studies Paper-II

**Verified demand (10 marks, 150 words):** Institutional quality is a crucial driver of economic performance. In this context suggest reforms in Civil Service for strengthening democracy.

**Provenance:** Audited 2018–2023 Mains routing ledger; wording checked against the locally held official 2020 GS-II paper. Shared with the Polity ministries-and-departments owner; this answer emphasises civil-service design. No official Mains answer key is available; this is an authored solution.

**Model answer (~153 words):** Institutional quality matters because predictable, impartial administration lets public investment and citizen entitlements translate into results. Civil-service reform must therefore change incentives, not merely train individuals. First, record transfer reasons, apply transparent tenure norms and permit review: an officer executing an unpopular but lawful welfare decision should not risk arbitrary displacement. Pair this protection with differentiated, evidence-based appraisal and due process, lest permanence shield non-performance. Second, combine generalist coordination with domain specialists; bounded lateral entry can strengthen complex policy work but needs transparent selection and conflict-of-interest safeguards. Third, use Mission Karmayogi's role-based competency plans and verify whether training improves decisions and grievance resolution, rather than counting iGOT enrolments. Fourth, maintain candid advice and officer-level service-rule accountability even though ministers answer publicly for policy. Elected executives must likewise respect neutral postings. Together these reforms improve reliable implementation, equal treatment and public trust; course certificates or tenure guarantees alone establish neither growth nor democratic accountability.

**Why this earns marks:** Links institutional quality to economic and democratic outcomes; prescribes posting, personnel, capacity and accountability reforms with an explicit trade-off and named Indian programme.

**Provenance:** Audited 2024–2025 Mains routing ledger; wording checked against the locally held official 2025 GS-I paper. No official answer-level key is available; the following is an authored model, not a UPSC marking scheme.

**Model answer (~149 words):** The ethos of Indian civil service joins professional competence to loyalty to constitutional India, not to a government or party. Professionalism entails expertise, reasoned advice, impartial application of rules and responsibility for results. An officer implementing a newly elected state government's welfare policy should apply the same eligibility standards as before the election; continuity under Articles 309–311 protects that candour, not poor performance. Nationalistic consciousness means commitment to the public interest, equal citizenship and territorial as well as social cohesion: a district official must reach excluded citizens, not merely meet a dashboard target. Mission Karmayogi's role-based competencies can strengthen service capacity; its success must be tested in field decisions and accessible grievance redress, not enrolments. A specialist may improve climate-policy design, while a generalist connects departments and state execution. National commitment without lawful restraint risks partisan zeal; technique without civic purpose risks efficient exclusion. Constitutional impartiality, capability and answerability make the two mutually reinforcing.

**Why this earns marks:** Explains both elements, shows their operational interaction with Indian examples, and rejects party loyalty masquerading as nationalism.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 1 — 10 MARKS

**Question:** Explain why political neutrality, permanence and anonymity were designed as a single package rather than as three separate virtues of the Indian civil service. Answer in about 150 words.

**Model answer (~153 words):** India's civil service must implement the choices of successive elected governments without becoming a partisan instrument. Permanence supplies continuity and institutional memory across elections; otherwise a new government would repeatedly rebuild its administrative capacity. Neutrality gives that continuity legitimacy: a collector must apply a welfare scheme's lawful eligibility criteria regardless of the ruling party. Anonymity, in modified Westminster practice, shields frank internal advice while the minister publicly defends policy. The traits depend on one another: protected continuity enables candid non-partisan advice, and ministerial responsibility keeps unelected expertise under democratic control. Yet anonymity is not immunity—file manipulation still attracts departmental or vigilance scrutiny. Similarly, tenure protects service across governments, not an unreviewable entitlement to one post; arbitrary transfers can defeat neutrality even without dismissal. Articles 309–311 structure service conditions and specified disciplinary safeguards, but they do not themselves guarantee trust. Transparent postings, professional appraisal and documented advice make the package work in practice.

**Why this earns marks:** Relates all three traits causally, distinguishes ministerial from officer accountability, and qualifies constitutional protection.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 2 — 10 MARKS

**Question:** Discuss why capacity building alone cannot resolve the politicisation of the Indian civil service. Answer in about 150 words.

**Model answer (~148 words):** Capacity building improves what officers can do; politicisation changes what they are rewarded or punished for doing. Mission Karmayogi, Cabinet-approved in 2020, maps competencies to roles, and the Capacity Building Commission supports annual plans; iGOT provides courses. These can improve advice and field decisions, but no module prevents an officer who applies eligibility rules impartially from being transferred to a powerless post. Conversely, secure tenure without appraisal can protect persistent underperformance. Thus published posting criteria, written reasons for early transfers and a review route should accompany transparent, evidence-based performance assessment. Where a minister demands partisan outcomes, the political executive also has a reciprocal duty to respect lawful neutrality; officer training cannot substitute for that restraint. Specialist recruitment adds domain depth but requires fair selection and conflict checks. Judge the combined reform by stable lawful postings, reasoned decisions and fewer justified grievances—not platform logins alone. Skills matter, but incentives and remedies determine their democratic use.

**Why this earns marks:** Separates capacity from incentives, names the programme, identifies the executive-side constraint and gives outcome tests.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 3 — 15 MARKS

**Question:** Examine the generalist and specialist debate in the Indian civil services and assess whether lateral entry and competency-based capacity building adequately address it. Answer in about 250 words.

**Model answer (~246 words):** Indian administrative leadership has relied heavily on generalists who can coordinate portfolios, districts and levels of government. Complex energy, digital and financial regulation, however, demands sustained subject expertise. The choice is not between an omniscient generalist and an isolated specialist: both policy design and delivery need integrated teams.

A generalist can align a renewable-energy programme with finance, state agencies and district execution; a sector specialist can test technology and market assumptions. Senior lateral entry into specified Union posts supplies expertise faster than cadre progression, but its small and bounded scope cannot redesign all postings. Fair competition, conflict-of-interest declarations, continuity and accountability are necessary when external experts acquire public authority. Existing officers also need pathways to specialise rather than being rotated before expertise matures.

Mission Karmayogi's role-based shift offers the complementary route: the Capacity Building Commission's standards and annual capacity plans can identify task-specific gaps, while iGOT delivers learning. Yet watching a course does not confer domain mastery or ensure placement into a matching post. Train and assess officers against real tasks; link post descriptions, deployment and appraisal to demonstrated competencies. Keep a generalist coordination function, but staff technically demanding decisions with appropriate specialists and document who signs off on what.

Neither lever alone resolves politically motivated postings, vacancies or federal differences in state implementation. Compare error rates, timeliness, justified complaints and applied skills before claiming success; recruitment notices and course completions measure inputs. A hybrid cadre, transparently deployed and answerable for outcomes, reconciles breadth and depth without treating lateral entrants as a wholesale replacement.

**Why this earns marks:** Balances both capabilities, tests each proposed reform and its limits, and links specialisation to actual staffing and citizen outcomes.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 4 — 15 MARKS

**Question:** Examine the claim that civil-service legitimacy in a democracy is as much perceptual as structural. Answer in about 250 words.

**Model answer (~245 words):** Civil servants exercise unelected discretion on benefits, inspections and enforcement. Their formal authority rests on constitutional and service-law structures, but citizens' willingness to accept decisions also depends on whether officials appear impartial and committed. The 2024 GS-II Doctrine of Democratic Governance question exposes this second, perceptual condition; it is an analytical claim, not a newly codified constitutional doctrine.

Articles 309–311 frame service conditions and safeguards, while ministerial responsibility places public policy accountability on elected officials. These structures allow a collector to serve successive state cabinets and give candid internal advice. But a pattern of transfers after politically inconvenient eligibility decisions can make neutral service appear implausible even where formal safeguards remain intact. Equally, anonymity that protects candour may look like impunity when complaint routes fail, although officers remain disciplinarily answerable for misconduct.

Trust cannot be produced by publicity. Record reasons for transfers and adverse decisions; audit comparable beneficiary cases; preserve independent review and make grievance correction accessible to marginalised citizens. The executive must not demand partisan outcomes, while officers must follow lawful instructions rather than turn neutrality into veto. Mission Karmayogi can enhance competence; consistent frontline application, not course certificates, is the trust-relevant evidence.

Perception is not infallible: a false allegation may temporarily erode confidence, while a favourable survey can conceal actual exclusion. Compare grievance resolution, case fairness and audit findings alongside citizen experience. Legitimacy is strongest where structural protection, procedural accountability and visibly equal treatment converge; none can replace the others.

**Why this earns marks:** Distinguishes legal, procedural and perception variables, uses the actual PYQ scope and gives an evidence-based rather than cosmetic remedy.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 5 — 20 MARKS

**Question:** Assess Mission Karmayogi as a reform by identifying what it correctly diagnoses, what it deliberately leaves untouched, and how its success should be measured. Answer in about 300 words.

**Model answer (~289 words):** Mission Karmayogi, the National Programme for Civil Services Capacity Building approved by the Union Cabinet on 2 September 2020, diagnoses a mismatch between generic rule-centred training and the varied competencies needed in actual public roles. A health administrator, a procurement officer and a district coordinator require different knowledge and behaviours. Role-based learning, supported by the Capacity Building Commission's standards and Annual Capacity Building Plans, seeks to close that gap. Karmayogi Bharat operates the iGOT platform; online delivery is an instrument, not the whole reform.

The programme connects policy guidance, interagency coordination, competency frameworks, digital learning, personnel systems and evaluation. Map a post's tasks, diagnose skill gaps, deliver suitable learning, test application and revise the plan. That sequence is stronger than attendance-based training. It may also mitigate the generalist-specialist gap by cultivating domain capability within the service, without eliminating a generalist's coordination role.

Its remit is capacity, not constitutional recruitment or independent guarantees against partisan transfers. Articles 309–312 continue to govern the service framework; political-executive incentives, posting rules, promotion practices and conflict-of-interest safeguards require separate action. A trained officer moved away after refusing an unlawful favour illustrates why competency alone cannot ensure neutral performance. State administrations and field offices may also differ in connectivity, staff time and authority to apply what was learned.

Evaluation needs a ladder: document role maps and staff access; check demonstrated skills rather than mere completion; audit application in real files; compare avoidable errors, grievance outcomes and beneficiary experience against a baseline. Disaggregate by role, location and citizen group, and avoid attributing every improvement to courses without assessing staffing or concurrent reforms. Publish corrective feedback so plans respond to measured gaps. The verdict is conditional: Mission Karmayogi modernises capacity architecture, but earns its democratic promise only when placement, posting security, fair appraisal and visible citizen outcomes accompany learning.

**Why this earns marks:** Covers diagnosis, architecture, boundaries, federal variation, measurement and an explicit qualified verdict with named instruments.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 6 — 20 MARKS

**Question:** Assess the reforms needed to make institutional quality rather than individual calibre the driver of civil-service performance in a democracy. Answer in about 300 words.

**Model answer (~288 words):** Exceptional officers can overcome a weak system temporarily; a good institution makes fair, competent service repeatable after transfers and elections. In India the permanent service supplies continuity, but arbitrary postings, poorly differentiated appraisal and unclear responsibility can turn individual integrity into a substitute for institutional design.

First, protect lawful independence without granting impunity. Transparent transfer criteria, recorded reasons and review of exceptions make retaliation against an officer enforcing welfare eligibility harder; time-bound disciplinary procedures and appeal address sustained non-performance. Articles 309–311 provide the service-law setting, not a guaranteed posting. Ministers must allow candid advice and respect lawful neutrality while remaining publicly answerable for policy. Officers remain answerable internally for file decisions despite the anonymity convention.

Second, fit people to tasks. Senior lateral recruitment can add renewable-energy or finance expertise; fair selection and conflict checks prevent capture. Generalists should still coordinate across departments and levels. Mission Karmayogi, approved in 2020, and the Capacity Building Commission's annual plans can build competencies for actual roles. Training must inform placement and appraisal: otherwise expertise acquired on iGOT goes unused.

Third, institutionalise feedback. Publish reasons for eligibility decisions, provide an accessible grievance path, audit similar cases for unequal treatment and measure timeliness plus justified complaints. Distinguish course enrolments and recruitment counts from demonstrated competence and citizen outcomes. Account for district staffing and state service-rule variation rather than imposing a uniform causal claim on Union and state administrations.

There are genuine conflicts: fixed tenure can harbour poor performers; political discretion over appraisal can punish neutrality; specialist autonomy can obstruct coordination. Couple tenure with independent, evidence-based assessment, appeals and explicit decision rights. The strongest evidence of institutional quality is not a celebrated officer or a dashboard but predictable equal treatment even when an incumbent leaves. Such structures underpin both credible economic administration and democratic legitimacy.

**Why this earns marks:** Moves from diagnosis to linked institutional levers, uses constitutional and current programme evidence, addresses trade-offs and defines measurable democratic performance.
