### PYQ DEMAND CARD 1 — 2021 General Studies Paper-II

**Demand:** Has digital illiteracy, particularly in rural areas, coupled with lack of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) accessibility hindered socio-economic development? Examine with justification. Answer in 250 words for 15 marks.

**Status:** Routed by the audited 2018-2023 Mains ledger to the Advanced owner as the single direct Mains demand for this topic, answered from the Basic owner under core routing, and confirmed word for word in the locally held official 2021 General Studies Paper-II, where the printed stem is partly truncated in the scan. No official answer key is held locally.

**Model answer (authored):** Yes, rural ICT barriers impede development when access to a benefit, market or learning resource becomes conditional on a digital transaction. The disadvantage is a **stack**, not merely inability to operate a phone. A shared device may be controlled by another household member; nominal coverage may hide unaffordable or unreliable connectivity. English error messages block a vernacular user even where the landing page is translated. Functional literacy is the ability to finish an application, not scroll through a handset. Fear of irreversible mistakes discourages use; biometric or address mismatches can turn an eligible applicant away. These constraints compound for women, older residents and people with disabilities through differing mobility and device control. An illustrative rural ration claimant denied after an Aadhaar authentication failure loses access because of a design gate, not because eligibility was adjudicated. Digital payments and document exchange can cut costs and improve access for others; these gains do not cancel the exclusion tail. A Common Service Centre can restore access, but an operator may charge extra, enter errors or retain credentials. Publish assisted-service fees, protect credentials, design low-bandwidth and vernacular recovery, provide a reasoned refusal and human review without repeating the failed step. An auditable offline alternative for entitlements must balance inclusion against leakage and inconsistent records. Investment in digital literacy matters, but public-service design must also accommodate the least-equipped eligible user; otherwise an information gap becomes a denial of socio-economic opportunity. Do not infer a national incidence rate from this mechanism.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 1 — 10 MARKS

**Question:** Distinguish digital public infrastructure from e-governance with Indian examples and explain why the distinction changes the governance question. Answer in about 150 words.

**Model answer (authored):** DPI consists of reusable identity, payments, document and consent rails used by multiple public and private actors; e-governance is a particular department's digital service. UIDAI's Aadhaar can authenticate a claimant and NPCI's UPI can make a payment, but a State pension portal using both remains responsible for an applicant's eligibility decision and appeal. DigiLocker can supply an issuer-verified certificate, not correct an erroneous source register. RBI-regulated financial Account Aggregators carry consent-based information between authorised entities; they are not a pension officer. The governance questions therefore differ: interoperability, cyber-resilience and system-wide exclusion for shared rails; timely completed service and a named duty-holder for the pension portal; purpose-limited processing and legal safeguards for data. As of the canonical August 2026 status, DPDP's major duties and Data Principal rights are scheduled for May 2027, not already operative. A failed biometric check should trigger an accessible service fallback rather than automatic denial. Distinguishing layers identifies who must remedy each failure.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 2 — 10 MARKS

**Question:** Distinguish the governance risks arising at different layers of India's digital public infrastructure stack. Answer in about 150 words.

**Model answer (authored):** Each DPI layer removes a different friction and creates a different risk. Aadhaar can help verify identity and reduce duplicates, but a worn fingerprint can bar an eligible ration claimant; offer auditable manual review. NPCI's UPI joins bank payments, but unstable connectivity or a shared handset can prevent a transfer; preserve continuity and alternative channels. DigiLocker shares issuer-verified records, yet an incorrect issuing register makes portable errors; fix the source. RBI-regulated Account Aggregators offer purpose-specific finance data sharing without viewing or storing underlying data, but a dense notice creates consent fatigue; test comprehension. ABDM's voluntary ABHA and health-record exchange heighten re-identification and discrimination concerns; apply sensitive-use controls. ONDC's open-commerce protocol can reduce lock-in but may not ensure small-seller visibility; assess market fairness. These are not interchangeable privacy defects: service-side appeals address denial, while financial consent disputes follow the RBI AA framework. The DPDP statute is an enacted overlay with phased commencement; it cannot substitute for layer-specific design and reachable human remedy.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 3 — 15 MARKS

**Question:** Examine whether digital illiteracy in rural areas, coupled with limited access to information and communication technology, has hindered socio-economic development. Answer in about 250 words.

**Model answer (authored):** Digital illiteracy is not one scalar deficit. A rural household can share one phone controlled by a different member, have a signal too unstable for authentication, encounter English-only error text, possess only entertainment-app skills, fear mistaken payment and face a failed fingerprint. When a public benefit is digital-only, these factors convert difficulty into exclusion. An illustrative eligible ration claimant blocked by biometric failure demonstrates the difference between genuine eligibility and successful verification; a learner may understand the scheme while lacking a functioning channel. Women, elderly residents and disabled users may face additional device, mobility and accessibility constraints within the same settlement. Still, DigiLocker document portability and NPCI's UPI payments can reduce travel and transaction friction for connected users; abandoning infrastructure is not the answer. Provide low-bandwidth and vernacular interfaces, including recovery text; offer functional task-based training and affordable devices. A CSC can assist, but its operator may charge unauthorised fees, introduce mistakes or retain credentials: publish fees, issue receipts and audit errors. Preserve an accountable offline route for entitlements and a reasoned human review after failed authentication; manual override itself needs a record to constrain discretion. Track successful completion and corrected refusals by access route rather than inventing a single rural literacy percentage. Investment in skill and redesign of the service are complementary: the administration should adapt to the eligible user, not demand perfect digital capacity as a condition of development.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 4 — 15 MARKS

**Question:** Examine the adequacy of India's current data-protection framework as a safeguard for its digital public infrastructure. Answer in about 250 words.

**Model answer (authored):** Adequacy requires separating **statutory design, commencement and practical enforcement**. India's DPDP Act, 2023 names the Data Fiduciary and Data Principal and envisages the Data Protection Board of India under section 18; the Rules were notified by MeitY on 13 November 2025. But the August 2026 source records most substantive duties and Data Principal rights as scheduled for 13 May 2027, while section 6(9) on Consent Managers is scheduled for 13 November 2026. The Board's legal establishment does not establish appointed members or adjudicated complaints: no appointment order had been located at the source cut-off. Section 44(3) has already substituted RTI section 8(1)(j); do not confuse it with section 44(2), the deferred IT Act amendment. These distinctions prohibit the claim that every DPI user presently enjoys a fully operative DPDP remedy. A financial-data user can employ RBI's finance-specific AA consent architecture, but that is not the general DPDP Consent Manager registration. Aadhaar authentication failure can still exclude a ration claimant; ABDM records raise heightened sensitivity, and ONDC can face seller-visibility issues even under good privacy law. Apply *Puttaswamy* proportionality, data minimisation, purpose-specific permission, accessible fallback and reasoned human appeal across these layers. Such design measures reduce risk but require empirical testing and competent enforcement, not statutory labels alone. The framework is a significant enacted safeguard-in-progress, not a currently proven solution to every exclusion or consent-quality problem.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 5 — 20 MARKS

**Question:** Assess the claim that the interoperability logic of digital public infrastructure is in structural tension with the purpose-limitation principle of data-protection law. Answer in about 300 words.

**Model answer (authored):** DPI creates value when an identity, payment or consent rail can be reused across many applications. Yet the ability to link data is not permission to reuse it for any purpose. The DPDP framework names the entity choosing processing purpose a Data Fiduciary and the affected person a Data Principal; its substantive rights and duties must be dated to their phased commencement. A hypothetical pension service using Aadhaar to verify identity should not silently repurpose pension information for unrelated profiling. Record each purpose, collect the minimum necessary and design separate, understandable permission and withdrawal flows where applicable. In the finance sector, RBI-regulated NBFC-AAs under DEPA enable consent-based transfer without retaining underlying data: a techno-legal constraint, not proof that every consumer comprehends a dense notice. DPDP Consent Managers are a distinct, general registration mechanism scheduled under section 6(9) for 13 November 2026, not a synonym for AAs. DigiLocker issuer-verified documents can be portable without being openly published. ABDM health-record exchange merits particular care about re-identification and discrimination; pseudonyms alone do not guarantee anonymity. Open protocol and purpose limitation can therefore coexist through access controls, purpose tagging, auditable logs and sector-specific permissions, but each join increases possible linkage risk. *Puttaswamy* supplies a constitutional proportionality lens: examine legitimate aim, necessity and a less intrusive way to deliver the benefit. For users lacking devices or experiencing authentication failure, an assisted route and human review avoid making consent or identity a barrier to entitlement. An enactment is not present enforcement; audit purposes and affected-user outcomes rather than inferring a resolved tension from technical interoperability alone. A re-identification test should compare what a partner can infer by linking records, not merely whether names were removed; monitor affected users' actual ability to withdraw or contest sharing.

**Question:** Assess how the accountability of a digitally mediated adverse decision should be secured in India. Answer in about 300 words.

**Model answer (authored):** A digitally mediated adverse decision remains a **public decision** with an identifiable duty-holder. Map the chain: lawful service eligibility rule, authoritative source data, identity/authentication gate, automated flag, human decision, notice and review. In a hypothetical ration case, an Aadhaar fingerprint mismatch is not evidence of ineligibility; provide an auditable manual override so an eligible person is not denied food. Audit the override too, since a fallback can reopen discretion and leakage. A false negative wrongly denies entitlement; a false positive wrongly includes someone: their distributional harms differ and should be stated in system design. For an algorithmic flag, keep records of input provenance, group-wise errors and decision reasons, and permit a human officer to examine the underlying case without demanding that the citizen repeat the failed login. MeitY's India AI Governance Guidelines of 5 November 2025 articulate people-first, fairness, accountability and understandable design, but are voluntary guidance, not an AI Act creating a new penalty. Route a service delay to the responsible department and applicable grievance machinery; information denial to RTI channels; financial-consent issues to RBI's AA framework. Do not refer a present ration-service appeal automatically to the DPBI: although established under section 18, no member appointment order was located at the canonical August 2026 cut-off and most DPDP duties and rights await May 2027. *Puttaswamy* proportionality asks whether the data and automation are necessary and proportionate to the service aim; minimisation and purpose controls reduce spillovers. Shared rails concentrate outage risk, making recoverable pending claims and accessible non-digital fallback essential.
