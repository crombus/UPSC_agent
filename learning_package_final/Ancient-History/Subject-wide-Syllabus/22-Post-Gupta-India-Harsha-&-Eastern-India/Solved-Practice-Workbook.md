### Matching answer key and option-by-option reasoning

#### MCQ 01 - Q1

**Answer: A.** Statements 1 and 2 are correct; statement 3 wrongly places the Maitrakas at Pataliputra instead of Valabhi.

- [Q01-A] Correct: Later Guptas persisted in the middle Ganga belt and the Maukharis were centred at Kannauj before the Pushyabhutis inherited it through marriage; only statement 3 is wrong.
- [Q01-B] Wrong: this accepts statement 3, but the Maitrakas were centred at Valabhi in the west, not Pataliputra.
- [Q01-C] Wrong: this drops the correct statement 1 and keeps the wrong statement 3.
- [Q01-D] Wrong: this keeps only the incorrect statement and drops both correct ones.

**Examiner trap:** Candidates who know "Maitrakas = post-Gupta polity" often default-associate them with the Gangetic core rather than checking their actual western Valabhi base.

#### MCQ 02 - Q2

**Answer: B.** *Harshacharita* presents him as the general whose rise made the dynasty formidable.

- [Q02-A] Wrong: this describes Grahavarman's fate, not Prabhakaravardhana's role.
- [Q02-B] Correct: *Harshacharita* presents Prabhakaravardhana as the general whose rise made the Pushyabhuti dynasty formidable.
- [Q02-C] Wrong: this describes Pulakeshin II, a Chalukya ruler, not a Pushyabhuti general.
- [Q02-D] Wrong: this describes Shashanka of Gauda, an eastern rival, not Prabhakaravardhana.

**Examiner trap:** Candidates confuse the four named rulers of the accession crisis (Prabhakaravardhana, Rajyavardhana, Grahavarman, Shashanka) because they appear close together in the same narrative.

#### MCQ 03 - Q3

**Answer: C.** This is Bana's actual causal sequence.

- [Q03-A] Wrong: this places Grahavarman's death before Prabhakaravardhana's, reversing the actual sequence.
- [Q03-B] Wrong: this places Rajyavardhana's death before Prabhakaravardhana's, which is chronologically impossible since Rajyavardhana succeeds his father.
- [Q03-C] Correct: Bana's sequence runs death of Prabhakaravardhana, then Grahavarman's killing and Rajyashri's imprisonment, then Rajyavardhana's campaign and death by stratagem, then Harsha's rescue.

**Examiner trap:** Sequencing questions punish candidates who remember the named actors but not the causal order linking them.

#### M3. 15 marks - Assess the importance of Kannauj in the post-Gupta political order.
