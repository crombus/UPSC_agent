## Mandatory classification override

Political Theory is a UPSC Prelims and General Studies Mains foundation subject
in this repository. It is not Philosophy Optional and not PSIR Optional.

- Own only UPSC Prelims and General Studies Mains use.
- State GS ownership honestly: primarily GS-II conceptual foundations, with
  GS-I, GS-IV and Essay cross-application only where the topic genuinely earns it.
- Remove learner-facing `Optional`, `PSIR Optional`, `Philosophy Optional`,
  `GS/Optional relevance`, optional-paper answer architecture, optional-paper
  marks/word conventions and any statement that Political Theory is an optional
  subject.
- Philosophy Optional and PSIR material may be consulted only as cross-source
  conceptual evidence. Their questions are never Political Theory-owned PYQs.
- Verified PYQ sections may contain only relevant UPSC Prelims and GS Mains
  questions. Where no direct owned PYQ is verified, say so and use clearly
  labelled original GS practice.
- Original Political Theory Mains models use only the GS pattern:
  10 marks/150 words and 15 marks/250 words. Use six models as three of each.
  Do not include a 20-mark Political Theory model.
- Prelims MCQs must test close conceptual distinctions, thinkers, propositions,
  applications and elimination traps suitable for GS/Prelims.
- `OPTIONAL ADVANCED DEPTH — NOT REQUIRED FOR A CORE ANSWER` is a universal
  learner-v2 content-depth label only. It means enrichment beyond a core GS
  answer and never denotes an optional subject.
