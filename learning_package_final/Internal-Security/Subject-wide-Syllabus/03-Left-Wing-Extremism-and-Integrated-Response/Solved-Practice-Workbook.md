## Evidence and use note

> Left Wing Extremism (LWE) is showing a downward trend, but still affects many parts of the country. Briefly explain the Government of India's approach to counter the challenges posed by LWE. **(Answer in 150 words) 10**

**Demand decoding:** Separate structural cause, organisational mechanism, civilian impact, role allocation, current status and the security–development–rights qualification demanded by the question.

**Original model answer (148 words; limit 150):**

India's approach is integrated rather than force-only. The National Policy and Action Plan, 2015 combines **security measures, development interventions, and rights and entitlements of local communities**.

Security action strengthens State police, intelligence, specialised units, fortified stations and inter-State coordination to protect people and infrastructure. SAMADHAN adds smart leadership, training, actionable intelligence, technology, theatre plans, development dashboards and financial denial.
