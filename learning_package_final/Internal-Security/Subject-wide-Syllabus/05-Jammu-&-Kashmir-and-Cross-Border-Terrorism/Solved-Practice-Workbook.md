# Jammu & Kashmir and Cross-Border Terrorism — Solved Practice Workbook

> **Scope:** GS-III operational internal security, not the constitutional merits of Article 370. The 24 distinct MCQs test historical foundations, proxy mechanisms, evidence, institutions, separate security metrics, civilian protection and the bounded CPEC route. Attempt the questions before opening the separate key. Model answers are practice solutions, not official UPSC keys.
>
> **Evidence and status:** `upsc-ai-kit\knowledge\Internal-Security\basic\05_Jammu-Kashmir-and-Cross-Border-Terrorism.md` and its Advanced companion; audited 2018, 2019 and 2023 GS-III demand routes; Ashok Kumar Singh, *Challenges to Internal Security of India*, PDF pp. 23–30. Historical group descriptions and scheme names are not current force or performance data. MHA's 2026 migrant-relief material is a dated implementation anchor, not proof of safe return or lasting peace.

## PYQS AND ANSWER PRACTICE

The 2018 and 2019 stems below are **audited neutral demand renderings**, not quotations of official paper wording. The 2023 stem follows the Core owner's inspected wording. No official descriptive answer key is claimed. Later updates are excluded from timed answers to older questions.

### PYQ 1 — 2018 GS-III, Q9 · 10 marks · 150 words

**Demand (neutral rendering):** Enumerate India's strategic objections to the China–Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC), a component of China's One Belt One Road initiative.

**Model answer:**

CPEC is the China–Pakistan connectivity corridor within the wider OBOR initiative. India's objection is not to connectivity in the abstract, but to a route and strategic arrangement that affects its sovereign claims.

First, the corridor traverses territory claimed by India and administered by Pakistan. Proceeding without India's consent implicates sovereignty and cannot be treated as recognition of Pakistan's title. Second, transport and energy infrastructure may improve China's and Pakistan's strategic logistics in a contingency; a possible capability is not proof of a particular present military movement. Third, China's deeper economic and political presence in a disputed theatre can complicate India's diplomatic and security environment. The project also raises wider questions of transparent, sustainable connectivity; those financing and trade issues principally belong to IR and Economy.

India's response should distinguish territorial objection from opposition to regional development generally: insist on sovereignty while assessing corridor use on evidence, not conjecture.

**Examiner focus:** Name the disputed route, enumerate distinct strategic concerns, and qualify speculative operational claims. Do not turn a CPEC question into a generic militancy essay.

### PYQ 2 — 2019 GS-III, Q9 · 10 marks · 150 words

**Demand (neutral rendering):** Examine how over-ground workers assist terrorist organisations in insurgency-affected areas and suggest ways to neutralise their influence.

**Model answer:**

An over-ground worker is an alleged civilian facilitator of an armed organisation, not a legal identity established by political opinion or place of residence. Such networks can connect armed cadres to local resources.

Assistance may include reconnaissance of targets, shelter and transport, communications, recruitment, propaganda or moving funds. Each function calls for different evidence: witnessed logistics, authenticated messages, financial records or other admissible corroboration. In J&K's proxy-war setting, finding a suspect near the LoC cannot by itself establish a chain to an external state institution.

Neutralisation combines protected local intelligence, financial and digital investigation, witness safeguards and prosecution where a specific offence is proved. Prevention, community grievance redress and rehabilitation can reduce recruitment where appropriate. Indiscriminate labelling of civilians would impair reporting and strengthen alienation.

Thus the measure of success is proved disruption of support networks alongside civilian trust, not merely the number of people labelled OGWs.

**Examiner focus:** Functions **and** matched responses; avoid both collective suspicion and immunity for proven facilitation.

### PYQ 3 — 2023 GS-III, Q9 · 10 marks · 150 words

**Question:** "Winning of 'Hearts and Minds' in terrorism-affected areas is an essential step in restoring the trust of the population. Discuss the measures adopted by the Government in this respect as part of the conflict resolution in Jammu and Kashmir."

**Model answer:**

Winning hearts and minds in J&K is a test of whether citizens can trust security and civil institutions while cross-border militancy is countered; it is not a substitute for preventing attacks.

Government measures include protection of civilians and border communities, counter-infiltration and evidence-led policing. Development and livelihood interventions—such as the Prime Minister's Reconstruction Plan and UDAAN in their documented book-period forms—address economic vulnerability. Relief and employment support for displaced Kashmiri Pandits address a distinct loss of home and livelihood. The 2019 reorganisation altered the operational governance chain; with police and public order outside J&K Assembly competence, effective Union–Lieutenant-Governor coordination must still allow responsive local grievance handling.

However, a development package or a fall in LoC firing is not proof of safe return or recovered trust. Proportionate force, avenues for participation, remedy for wrongful action and transparent assessment of rehabilitation determine whether security measures earn durable cooperation.

**Examiner focus:** Evaluate government action under security, delivery, rehabilitation and accountability; do not treat spending as proof of outcomes. The 2026 MHA migrant-relief update is deliberately not used inside this 2023 exam answer.

### ORIGINAL MAINS PRACTICE — answer each before checking its model

#### Original 1 · 10 marks · about 150 words

**Question:** Explain why a LoC ceasefire cannot by itself establish the end of cross-border terrorism.

**Model answer:**

A ceasefire regulates firing between armed forces across a line; it does not necessarily dismantle the people, routes or finances behind an insurgent network. The November 2003 India–Pakistan LoC understanding, reaffirmed by the two DGMOs on 25 February 2021, is a military-to-military arrangement, not a peace treaty or a settlement of the territorial dispute.

Less firing can save lives in border villages and should be counted as a real gain. But attempted infiltration, successful crossings, drone or tunnel delivery, local recruitment and hinterland incidents measure different mechanisms. They may continue even when the line is quiet. Conversely, a higher number of detected attempts need not mean more successful crossings.

Policy needs border monitoring, corroborated attribution, financial investigation and accountable local policing alongside civilian protection. Evaluate each indicator separately; only then can one assess whether the broader proxy mechanism is weakening. A quiet LoC is a humanitarian gain, not a sufficient measure of lasting peace.

#### Original 2 · 10 marks · about 150 words

**Question:** Examine the role attributed to over-ground workers and propose evidence-led measures to neutralise unlawful facilitation.

**Model answer:**

In J&K, the term over-ground worker describes alleged non-combatant facilitation, not a status proved by dissent or identity. An armed intermediary may need local intelligence, safe houses, communications, transport, recruitment and funds. Proving one function does not automatically prove every other function or a direct link to a foreign sponsor.

Investigation should follow the alleged vector. Preserve travel or financial records for logistics and finance; authenticate communications for operational liaison; corroborate witness accounts without exposing informants. Lawful investigation and prosecution should distinguish a knowing facilitator from someone acting under coercion or merely expressing political views. Protected community reporting, youth opportunities and grievance redress reduce incentives for recruitment; suitable prevention or rehabilitation can complement prosecution.

Success requires durable disruption of proven networks without alienating the population whose information helps detect them. Mass labelling would create false positives, damage trust and weaken the evidentiary quality of cases.

#### Original 3 · 15 marks · about 250 words

**Question:** Analyse the proxy-war mechanism in Jammu and Kashmir while preserving attribution and rights safeguards.

**Model answer:**

J&K's proxy-war mechanism joins an external sponsor, armed intermediaries and local vulnerabilities; the three should never be treated as the same actor. Singh's historical account describes Pakistani state support through the ISI for militant groups such as LeT, JeM and Hizbul Mujahideen. The disputed 1987 election supplied one legitimacy trigger for the late-1980s insurgency, while cross-border training, funding and weapons amplified violence. These are historical mechanisms, not a current verified roster of groups.

Deniability lets a sponsor seek effects without conventional war. Potential routes include infiltration across the LoC, financing, propaganda, logistics and alleged civilian facilitation. Deliberate cross-LoC firing may also help infiltration, as Singh argues, but a ceasefire violation and an attack in the hinterland are not identical events. An effective account names which link is evidenced: recovered materiel, traceable transfers, authenticated communications or a verified training connection. Foreign origin of a suspect does not alone establish direction by a foreign state institution.

Responses must match the vector: border surveillance for crossings; financial tracing for funds; protected intelligence and admissible investigation for local logistics; and rehabilitation and grievance mechanisms for recruitment vulnerability. The Armed Forces (Jammu and Kashmir) Special Powers Act, 1990 is a separate legal instrument from North-East AFSPA; any exceptional powers must be analysed with their applicable notifications and accountability. Civilian safety and remedy matter because community cooperation is a security capability.

The test of progress is disaggregated: LoC firing, attempted and successful crossings, recruitment and hinterland harm may move differently. Durable security means lawful disruption plus trustworthy governance, not a single rising arrest count.

#### Original 4 · 15 marks · about 250 words

**Question:** Discuss the operational internal-security consequences of the 2019 reorganisation without entering the constitutional merits.

**Model answer:**

The Jammu and Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 divided the former State into J&K, a Union Territory with a legislature, and Ladakh, a Union Territory without one. Its relevance to GS-III is the allocation of security responsibility, not a rehearing of Article 370 litigation.

The J&K Assembly's legislative competence excludes the State List subjects of police and public order. Consequently, the Lieutenant Governor and Union have a distinct operational role even where elected representatives address local services and grievances. A security response must therefore coordinate policing and counter-infiltration with civil administration without assuming an ordinary State's Chief-Minister-led policing chain. One possible benefit is faster alignment of central and territorial security priorities; a risk is inadequate local ownership if political participation and grievance redress are neglected. Both are analytical possibilities, not automatic outcomes of a statute.

On the ground, intelligence about infiltration and local facilitation must be converted into admissible investigation; deployment or an arrest alone cannot prove a network dismantled. Border-village protection and SP/SSP supervision of Village Defence Guards require training and accountability if local volunteers are armed. Migrant rehabilitation likewise needs safe, voluntary and dignified return rather than counting assistance alone. These are complementary tasks with different ministries, actors and outcome measures.

The operational lesson is to pair clear Union–Lieutenant-Governor responsibility with community-responsive policing and transparent evaluation. The 2019 institutional change does not by itself establish that cross-border sponsorship, local recruitment or civilian insecurity has ended.

#### Original 5 · 20 marks · about 300 words

**Question:** Critically evaluate the hearts-and-minds approach as part of conflict resolution in Jammu and Kashmir.

**Model answer:**

Hearts and minds denotes an operational legitimacy strategy: civilians must be safer, heard and treated fairly for security gains to endure. In J&K it complements, rather than replaces, the response to externally enabled proxy violence.

**Civilian protection:** Counter-infiltration, intelligence and proportionate policing reduce an armed group's capacity to intimidate communities. Border villagers benefit from fewer firing incidents; the 2003 LoC ceasefire and its 2021 DGMO reaffirmation illustrate a protective instrument. Yet even a stable ceasefire does not measure local recruitment or hinterland risk.

**Participation and remedy:** Responsive local government, accessible grievance channels and review of wrongful security action can build confidence and improve voluntary reporting. The 2019 reorganisation placed police and public order outside J&K Assembly competence, making it especially important that Union–Lieutenant-Governor security decisions remain attentive to elected local representatives and civilian complaints. Participation should not be confused with a transfer of statutory policing powers.

**Livelihood and return:** The PM Reconstruction Plan, UDAAN and the 2008 migrant package are historical examples of development and rehabilitation approaches. MHA's dated 2026 relief material shows continuing state activity for Kashmiri migrants; it does not show that every family can return safely, recover property and resume life without fear. Test actual access to services, voluntary return, livelihood and perceived safety.

**Limitations:** Packages can fail if poorly delivered or disconnected from rights and local voice. Indiscriminate OGW allegations may punish innocent citizens and suppress the information needed to identify real facilitators. Conversely, community initiatives alone cannot stop armed infiltration or financing.

Therefore judge the strategy by independently evidenced declines in civilian harm and proven unlawful facilitation, effective grievance resolution and dignified rehabilitation. Where those indicators diverge, report the divergence rather than announcing either total success or total failure.

#### Original 6 · 20 marks · about 300 words

**Question:** Assess India's integrated response to cross-border proxy warfare across ceasefire, infiltration, facilitation, finance, governance and rehabilitation dimensions.

**Model answer:**

An integrated response must attack the proxy mechanism without confusing its distinct actors or claiming that improvement in one indicator settles the conflict. Singh's historical account traces externally backed militant intermediaries and local facilitation in J&K; each present-tense attribution requires dated evidence.

**Ceasefire and border:** The November 2003 LoC understanding, reaffirmed by DGMOs in 2021, can reduce firing and protect border residents. Army, other border forces and local police need coordinated roles against attempted crossings. Track violations, detected attempts and successful infiltration separately: a quiet line cannot prove that weapons or cadres no longer enter.

**Facilitation and finance:** Identify the alleged function—reconnaissance, shelter, transport, propaganda or funds—before selecting the investigative route. Protected sources, authenticated records and lawful financial tracing can connect actors and transactions. Arrests without admissible proof are not convictions; collective suspicion can destroy community cooperation. Attribute any alleged sponsor through corroborated material, communications, training or financing links, not mere geographic proximity.

**Governance and civilian safety:** The 2019 Act created J&K as a Union Territory with a legislature but excluded police and public order from that legislature's competence. Union–Lieutenant-Governor coordination should work with responsive local services and grievance redress. SP/SSP-supervised Village Defence Guards may aid warning in remote areas but require training, command accountability and civilian remedy.

**Rehabilitation:** Historical Kashmiri Pandit displacement calls for livelihood support, safety, agency and possible dignified return. Dated MHA updates document measures, not whether return has been universally safe or voluntary. Book-period schemes should not be passed off as present performance data.

Overall, publish an outcome dashboard that separates border firing, attempted/successful infiltration, proven disruption of finance and facilitation, local recruitment, civilian harm, grievance resolution and safe-return outcomes. Coupling lawful security with trust-building is the route to durable reduction of vulnerability, not a substitute for evidentiary scrutiny.
