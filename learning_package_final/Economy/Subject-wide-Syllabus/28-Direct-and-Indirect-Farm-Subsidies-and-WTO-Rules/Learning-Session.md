**Current-status cutoff:** 10 September 2026. Budget figures are labelled BE; WTO negotiating status is not presented as adopted law.

### Core Session 02: Direct, indirect, explicit and implicit

```text
DIRECT: cash/premium support
INDIRECT: cheap power/credit
EXPLICIT: budget line
IMPLICIT: price/tax wedge
```
*Visual first:* Direct, indirect, explicit and implicit as a decision chain.

**Definition:** Direct support reaches a named beneficiary; indirect support changes an input or market price. Explicit support appears as expenditure; implicit support may arise through administered pricing or foregone revenue.

**Answer line:** The two pairs answer different questions and must not be used as synonyms.

**Keywords:** direct, indirect, explicit, implicit

**Claim -> named evidence -> analysis -> qualification**
- **Claim:** The two pairs answer different questions and must not be used as synonyms.
- **Named evidence:** Budget statements show explicit outlays, while state tariff orders and administered-price rules may create implicit support.
- **Analysis:** A direct payment can be explicit, while an indirect concession can also be explicitly budget-compensated.
- **Qualification:** Fiscal accounts may not reveal the full economic subsidy, and an accounting provision does not prove final incidence.

**Evidence:** Budget statements show explicit outlays, while state tariff orders and administered-price rules may create implicit support.

**UPSC trap:** Do not say every direct subsidy is cash or every indirect subsidy is off-budget.

**Mains use:** Use a 2x2 taxonomy before evaluating reform.

**Recap:** Direct support reaches a named beneficiary; indirect support changes an input or market price. Explicit support appears as expenditure; implicit support may arise through administered pricing or foregone revenue. Fiscal accounts may not reveal the full economic subsidy, and an accounting provision does not prove final incidence.

## Optional Advanced

### A. Incidence decomposition
Decompose each rupee into statutory recipient, price pass-through, quantity response, rent capitalisation and external cost. This avoids equating accounting outlay with welfare gain.

### B. Coupling ladder
Rank support from tightly coupled input/output subsidy through partially coupled risk support to decoupled income and public-good provision. Lower coupling usually reduces distortion, but land-linked transfers may still exclude tenants or raise rents.

### C. Political-economy constraint
Visible concentrated benefits and diffuse delayed costs create reform resistance. Credible compensation, service reliability, federal bargains and transparent review matter as much as textbook efficiency.

### D. Legal calculation protocol
For any WTO claim, preserve document code, member category, notification period, currency, fixed external reference price, eligible-production rule, value-of-production denominator and exemption invoked. Never reconstruct a notification value from a news report.

## Consolidated Register Notes

### Definitions and distinctions
- Direct/indirect and explicit/implicit are separate classification axes.
- Legal recipient differs from economic incidence.
- MSP announcement, procurement expenditure, fiscal subsidy and WTO market-price support are not synonyms.
- Fertiliser DBT reimburses companies on verified PoS sales; PM-KISAN transfers income to eligible families.
- NBS covers notified P&K grades; urea remains under a separate framework.

### Dated current anchors
- Union Budget 2026-27 Statement 7 BE: urea subsidy Rs 1,16,799 crore; NBS Rs 54,000 crore; combined fertiliser subsidy Rs 1,70,799 crore.
- PM-KISAN official guidelines provide Rs 6,000 yearly in three Rs 2,000 instalments to eligible landholding farmer families; Statement 7 gives it Rs 60,000 crore as FY 2026-27 BE.
- Fertiliser DBT began in October 2016 and completed pan-India rollout by March 2018.
- NBS has operated from 1 April 2010; the Department of Fertilisers page checked at the cutoff lists 28 P&K grades.
- Bali public-stockholding decision: 7 December 2013; WT/L/939: 28 November 2014; no adopted permanent solution located by the cutoff.

### WTO calculation card
- AoA pillars: market access, domestic support, export competition.
- MPS = (applied administered price - fixed external reference price) x eligible production.
- Developing-country de minimis: product-specific below 10% of product value and non-product-specific below 10% of total agricultural production, tested separately.
- Green Box is legally conditional, not environmentally labelled; Blue Box requires production-limiting design; Article 6.2 is conditional.
- Public-stockholding due restraint is conditional and not a permanent AMS exemption.

### Reform spine
- Diagnose the exact failure; target the beneficiary; protect tenants and smallholders.
- Correct relative prices and meter use while providing lifeline support.
- Shift toward public goods, balanced nutrients, risk tools and decoupled income where suitable.
- Use transition compensation, grievance redress, sunset review and ecological metrics.
