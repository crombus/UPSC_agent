### A. Household portfolio model

### B. Poverty dynamics

### C. Genetics-by-environment production frontier

## CONSOLIDATED REGISTER NOTES

### Sector boundary and central thesis

- Livestock and poultry are terrestrial rearing; aquaculture is managed aquatic farming; capture fisheries harvest a wild common-pool stock.
- Animal = productive asset + recurring output + by-products + terminal value - mortality/infertility/ageing.
- Core verdict: maximise healthy lifetime productivity, net producer margin and sustainable aquatic stock, not headcount or gross output alone.

### Dated evidence rail

- **20th Livestock Census, reference year 2019:** 536.76 million livestock and 851.81 million poultry; cattle about 193.46 million, buffaloes 109.85 million, goats 148.88 million and sheep 74.26 million. The 21st round launched on 25 October 2024; final population tables were not public by 10 September 2026.
- **Livestock GVA, reference year 2023-24:** 30.87% of agriculture GVA and 5.5% of national GVA at current prices, reported in DAHD Annual Report 2025-26.
- **BAHS 2025, reference year 2024-25:** milk 247.87 million tonnes; eggs 149.11 billion; meat 10.50 million tonnes.
- **Economic Survey 2025-26 Table 1.23, reference year 2024-25:** fish production 19.775 million tonnes.
- **MPEDA, FY 2024-25:** seafood exports 1,698,170 tonnes, Rs 62,408.45 crore, US$7.45 billion.

### Production and margin

- Net income = all product/by-product revenue + asset-value change - feed, labour, breeding, health, housing, finance, mortality, spoilage and marketing cost.
- Realised productivity = suitable genetics x nutrition x health/reproduction x management x climate adaptation x market access.
- Feed is a recurring-cost and land-water problem; breeding cannot overcome undernutrition or disease.
- Disease surveillance and vaccination are public goods; compensation and insurance improve reporting incentives.
- One Health joins zoonoses, AMR, residues and environmental pathways.
- Emissions intensity can fall while absolute methane/manure load rises.

### Dairy

- Chain: feed/breed/health -> milking -> collection -> fat/SNF testing -> chilling -> transport -> processing -> product mix -> market.
- Operation Flood began in 1970: Phase I 1970-80; Phase II 1981-85; Phase III 1985-96.
- Anand model: village society -> district union -> state federation. Test producer ownership, governance, services, payment and buyer choice.
- Cooperative, producer-company, private and informal models have different capital, governance and market-power trade-offs.

### Schemes and current-status rail

- **RGM:** genetics/breed systems; April 2026 approval temporarily through 30 September 2026 or 16th FC approval, whichever earlier; Rs 800 crore.
- **NLM:** breed/enterprise, feed/fodder, innovation/extension/insurance; same temporary-extension rule; FY 2026-27 BE Rs 808 crore.
- **LHDCP:** NADCP, CADCP, ASCAD, MVU and Pashu Aushadhi; same temporary-extension rule; FY 2026-27 BE Rs 2,010 crore.
- **Dairy Development:** procurement/quality/processing institutions; same temporary-extension rule; FY 2026-27 BE Rs 1,055 crore.
- **IDF/AHIDF:** infrastructure finance; AHIDF revised outlay Rs 29,110.25 crore through 31 March 2026, then temporary IDF extension to 30 September 2026; FY 2026-27 BE Rs 465 crore.

### Poultry and other livestock

- Broiler = meat; layer = egg; backyard and commercial systems have different capital and biosecurity.
- Integration: integrator supplies chick/feed/vet/market; farmer supplies shed/labour/utilities. Risk is reallocated, not removed.
- Sheep/goats depend on dryland commons and mobility; piggery combines quick reproduction with severe outbreak risk.
- Meat, wool, hides and by-products require lawful handling, inspection, cold chain, welfare, effluent control and traceability.
- Apiculture creates honey/wax income plus an underpriced pollination externality.

### Fisheries

- Capture = wild stock/common-pool; culture = managed stock. Marine/inland/brackish and extensive/intensive are separate axes.
- States regulate fisheries within 12-nautical-mile territorial waters; Union competence covers fishing beyond territorial waters; CAA Act 2005, amended 2023, governs coastal aquaculture.
- MSY is biological yield; MEY is economic rent; ecosystem and equity targets may require lower effort.
- RAS: mechanical filter removes solids; biofilter nitrifies ammonia; oxygenation/disinfection/monitoring remain separate.
- PMMSY: May 2020 original approval for 2020-21 to 2024-25, Rs 20,050 crore; FY 2026-27 BE Rs 2,500 crore, without inventing a new multi-year tenure.
- FIDF: Rs 7,522.48 crore, extension through 2025-26; no later extension located at cutoff.
- KCC extended to AH/fisheries in 2018; PM-MKSSY/NFDP links formalisation with credit, insurance and value-chain support.

### Examiner traps and answer spine

- Census stock != annual output != GVA != net income.
- Allocation != expenditure != asset != utilisation != outcome.
- Cooperative form != democratic control; integration != elimination of risk.
- Higher per-unit productivity != lower absolute emissions.
- Higher fish production != sustainable capture stock.
- Scheme answer: define -> date status -> mechanism -> implementation -> distribution -> externality -> qualified verdict.
- Cross-link Topic 14 for inputs/credit/insurance and Topic 15 for processing/cold-chain depth.
