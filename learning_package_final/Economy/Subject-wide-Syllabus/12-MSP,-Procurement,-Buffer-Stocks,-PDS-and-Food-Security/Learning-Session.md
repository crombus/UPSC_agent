### CORE SESSION 1 — Food-policy chain

#### VISUAL FIRST

```text
MSP SIGNAL -> PROCUREMENT -> CENTRAL POOL -> STORAGE / MOVEMENT
                       -> NFSA / OMSS -> FARMER, CONSUMER, PRICE
```

*Visual reading:* Fix the institutional or accounting sequence before drawing the conclusion.

#### DEFINITION

Food management is the linked system through which price signals, physical purchase, public stocks and household or market releases pursue farm and consumer objectives.

> **ANSWER-GRABBING LINE:** Food policy must separate price announcement, actual purchase, stockholding and entitlement before judging success.

**MUST-WRITE KEYWORDS:** food-policy, chain, food, policy, must, separate, price.

#### HOW TO WRITE THE ANSWER PARAGRAPH

- **Claim:** Food policy must separate price announcement, actual purchase, stockholding and entitlement before judging success.
- **Named evidence:** The Indian system uses CACP recommendations, Cabinet decisions, designated procurement agencies, FCI or State stocks and NFSA distribution.
- **Analysis:** Each stage changes the instrument's beneficiaries and fiscal or logistical consequences.
- **Qualification / demand link:** A strong price signal can remain ineffective where procurement access is absent, while large stocks can be wasteful without timely release.

#### EVIDENCE AND INFERENCE

- **Fact/evidence:** The Indian system uses CACP recommendations, Cabinet decisions, designated procurement agencies, FCI or State stocks and NFSA distribution.
- **Analytical inference:** Each stage changes the instrument's beneficiaries and fiscal or logistical consequences.
- **Qualification:** A strong price signal can remain ineffective where procurement access is absent, while large stocks can be wasteful without timely release.

#### PRELIMS TRAP

### CORE SESSION 2 — MSP purpose and limits


- **Analysis:** The announcement can influence sowing expectations even beyond procured quantities, but credibility depends on market and agency presence.
- **Qualification / demand link:** Quality, season, crop, location and procurement operations condition realised support.

#### EVIDENCE AND INFERENCE

## CONSOLIDATED REGISTER NOTES

### 1. MSP and institutions

- MSP is a pre-season administered price for specified crops; it is not universal procurement or income.
- CACP recommends; CCEA takes the Union decision; designated agencies procure.
- MSP covers 22 mandated crops, with derived toria and de-husked-coconut prices; sugarcane uses FRP.
- Current seasonal anchors: RMS 2026-27 approved 1 October 2025; KMS 2026-27 approved 13 May 2026.

### 2. Cost concepts and formula

- A2 = paid-out cultivation costs.
- A2+FL = A2 plus imputed unpaid family labour.
- C2 = A2+FL plus imputed rent on owned land and interest on owned fixed capital.
- Official policy benchmark = at least 1.5 times all-India weighted average A2+FL.
- C2-plus-50 is a separate demand; averages do not guarantee each farm's margin.

### 3. Procurement and central pool

- Procurement requires crop, FAQ, season, place, agency and actual purchase.
- Open-ended paddy-wheat procurement remains bounded by operating area, period and quality.
- PSS operations for pulses/oilseeds are conditional and not universally unlimited.
- DCP lets participating States procure, store and distribute NFSA requirements with central adjustment and reimbursement.
- FCI bridges procurement, storage, movement, buffer management, NFSA and OMSS.

### 4. Cost and stock accounting

- Acquisition cost = MSP plus procurement incidentals.
- Economic cost = acquisition cost plus distribution cost.
- Carrying cost grows with stock quantity, duration, financing, storage, handling and preservation.
- Norm != actual stock != allocation != offtake != household receipt.
- Quarterly total norms: 21.04 MMT on 1 April; 41.12 on 1 July; 30.77 on 1 October; 21.41 on 1 January.

### 5. Release instruments

- NFSA release fulfils household entitlement; OMSS sells into market channels.
- Price Stabilisation Fund is a separate commodity-intervention route.
- Excess-stock judgement must consider pipeline obligations, location, quality and shock risk.

### 6. NFSA and TPDS

- Coverage ceiling: up to 75% rural and 50% urban; States identify households within ceilings.
- PHH: 5 kg per person per month. AAY: 35 kg per household per month.
- Free NFSA foodgrain: 1 January 2024-December 2028 under the approved PMGKAY arrangement.
- Union handles national procurement/allocation support; States handle identification, ration cards, FPS and intra-State delivery.
- ONORC enables portability, not universal eligibility.
- NFSA accountability includes disclosure, social audit, vigilance, DGROs, State Food Commissions and food-security allowance.

### 7. Nutrition and reform

- Food security = availability + access + utilisation + stability.
- Cereal security does not equal nutrition security.
- Fortified rice continuation: July 2024-December 2028; still requires quality monitoring and dietary diversity.
- Reform mix: decentralised procurement, smart storage, dynamic stocks, transparent OMSS, authentication fallback and nutrition-sensitive PDS.

### 8. WTO and boundaries

- WTO market price support uses administered price minus 1986-88 external reference price times eligible production.
- Developing-country product-specific de minimis is 10% of production value.
- Bali peace clause is conditional and interim; detailed subsidy architecture belongs to Topic 28.
- APMC, e-NAM, FPOs and general supply-chain regulation belong to Topic 13.

### 9. Mains architecture

```text
CACP EVIDENCE -> CCEA PRICE -> ELIGIBLE PROCUREMENT
 -> CENTRAL POOL -> DYNAMIC STORAGE / OMSS / NFSA
 -> FARM INCOME, CONSUMER ACCESS, NUTRITION, FISCAL AND ECOLOGICAL EFFECTS
```

**Final answer line:** India needs a rights-safe food system in which transparent price policy, diversified procurement, dynamic stocks and portable nutrition-sensitive delivery reinforce rather than undermine one another.
