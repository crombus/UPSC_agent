## PYQS AND ANSWER PRACTICE

### Verified routed PYQs — independent models, not official keys

### PYQ — 2019/2024 Prelims — HCNG and fuel-cell exhaust

**Provenance/demand:** Paired audited routing covers hydrogen-enriched CNG for public transport and the direct exhaust of a hydrogen fuel-cell vehicle. These are distinct question demands; no original official answer letter is locally mapped.

**Independent model:** Hydrogen-enriched compressed natural gas is a methane-based gaseous transport fuel enriched with hydrogen; it remains a combustion-fuel pathway, not a fuel-cell electricity pathway. A hydrogen PEM fuel cell electrochemically combines supplied hydrogen and oxygen to drive an electric motor, with water as a direct reaction/exhaust product. Tailpipe water cannot establish that upstream hydrogen is green; its climate impact depends on whether production used renewable electrolysis, fossil feedstock without capture or fossil feedstock with capture. Nor should a 2019 HCNG public-transport question be retroactively treated as proof of today’s nationwide procurement or emissions outcome. For each objective stem inspect whether it asks fuel composition, traction mechanism or direct exhaust. Without the original mapped choices, do not assert an official key.

### PYQ — 2025 Prelims — BEV, FCEV and hybrid

**Provenance/demand:** Verified routed objective powertrain comparison. The paired record does not securely supply an official objective option mapping.

**Independent model:** A BEV stores electricity in a charged battery and drives an electric motor without combustion. An HEV carries an engine and motor, charging its small battery through engine operation and regenerative braking; it is not externally plug-chargeable. A PHEV retains the engine but adds external charging and meaningful electric-only operation. An FCEV generates electricity onboard in a hydrogen fuel cell and drives an electric motor. Thus the presence of an electric motor does not uniquely identify a BEV. Nor does an FCEV’s water exhaust prove carbon-free fuel production. For any multi-statement question, test source of electricity, external charging, combustion and exhaust separately; a locally unmapped official letter cannot be manufactured.

### PYQ — 2023 GS-III — EV carbon reduction and wider benefits

**Provenance/demand:** Verified descriptive GS-III demand on how electric vehicles contribute to reducing carbon emissions and other benefits.

**Independent model:** Electric vehicles can reduce urban tailpipe pollution by replacing combustion at the point of use. A battery electric bus converts stored electrical energy to traction efficiently; an HEV still burns liquid fuel and an FCEV requires hydrogen production. Climate mitigation must be evaluated across the lifecycle: coal-heavy charging power, mineral extraction, cell manufacture and end-of-life disposal can offset some tailpipe benefits. Cleaner generation, managed charging and Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 producer-responsibility recycling strengthen the climate case. Wider gains for India include lower oil-import exposure, quieter urban transport and opportunities for domestic advanced-chemistry-cell production; these require reliable chargers, safe packs, interoperable connections and competent vehicle approval. PM E-DRIVE is a demand-side instrument while ACC PLI builds manufacturing capability. A subsidy notification is not the same as measured emissions reductions or a functioning city-bus fleet. Different routes suit different segments: battery swapping may serve high-use small vehicles while long-haul hydrogen remains infrastructure- and cost-constrained. The qualified conclusion is segment-wise deployment plus greener electricity, standards and circular materials rather than a claim that every EV has zero lifecycle emissions.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 1 — 10 MARKS

**Question:** Distinguish BEV, HEV, PHEV and FCEV architectures. Answer in about 150 words.

**Independent model answer:** The four powertrains differ principally in where traction electricity originates. A battery electric vehicle stores grid-charged electricity and powers a motor without a combustion engine or tailpipe exhaust. A hybrid electric vehicle has an engine and motor; its small battery charges through the engine and regenerative braking and cannot be plugged in. A plug-in hybrid has both an engine and externally chargeable larger battery, allowing meaningful short electric-only trips before liquid fuel takes over. A fuel-cell electric vehicle uses stored hydrogen in a low-temperature PEM cell to make electricity onboard for its electric motor; direct exhaust is water, but hydrogen production can generate upstream carbon emissions. A city e-bus depot may organise BEV charging, whereas a hydrogen corridor also needs safe production, storage and refuelling. Therefore a motor is not enough to classify a vehicle, and neither water exhaust nor zero tailpipe carbon proves zero lifecycle emissions. Choose the pathway by duty cycle, infrastructure and fuel supply.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 2 — 10 MARKS

**Question:** Explain a lithium-ion battery from cell electrochemistry to module and pack integration. Answer in about 150 words.

**Independent model answer:** A lithium-ion cell has cathode, anode, separator and ion-conducting electrolyte. In a common cell, lithium ions move through the electrolyte between electrodes during charge and discharge; discharge electrons move through the external circuit to power the electric motor. Graphite commonly serves as anode. Cathode choice changes cost and resource needs: NMC uses nickel and cobalt, while lithium iron phosphate avoids both but still needs lithium. Cells combine into modules and then a pack with mechanical protection, electrical connections, cooling and a battery management system. The BMS tracks voltage, current and temperature, balances cells and constrains overcharge or over-discharge. Stored energy in kWh differs from output power in kW, while Wh/kg describes mass-specific energy density. An Indian e-bus pack must satisfy safety and vehicle approval as a whole; a high-performing cell alone does not certify the thermal or charging safety of its assembled pack.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 3 — 15 MARKS

**Question:** Compare lithium-ion chemistries and distinguish energy density from power density. Answer in about 250 words.

**Independent model answer:** Chemistry changes the technical performance and strategic mineral bill of an Indian electric vehicle. NMC and NCA cathodes offer comparatively high energy density, which can support longer range at a given pack mass, but rely on nickel and cobalt with cost and concentration risk. LFP uses lithium iron phosphate, avoids nickel and cobalt and is comparatively cheaper and thermally stable, though often less energy dense. It is inaccurate to describe LFP as lithium-free. Sodium-ion instead replaces the lithium carrier; its resource-security appeal must be weighed against typically lower energy density and its technology maturity. Solid-state electrolyte designs promise safety and density gains but remain pre-commercial at automotive scale in the owners.

Energy density expresses stored energy per mass or volume, for example Wh/kg, and influences range and vehicle weight. Power density measures the rate of delivery per mass; high stored kWh does not alone assure hill-climbing or fast acceleration. Likewise a charger’s kW rating is power, not a cell’s kWh capacity. Chemistry does not decide safety in isolation: voltage monitoring, battery management, thermal rejection, cell qualification and fire propagation protection affect the actual pack. For two-wheelers and buses, compare range requirement, payload, daily charging and total cost rather than naming one universal winning chemistry. Domestic ACC manufacturing, safe recycling and diversified processing chains are necessary even if vehicle purchases receive subsidies.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 4 — 15 MARKS

**Question:** Examine EV charging, BMS, thermal management, degradation and safety as one engineering system. Answer in about 250 words.

**Independent model answer:** An electric vehicle is only as dependable as its coupled energy, control and infrastructure chain. Grid alternating current must be conditioned to the controlled direct current needed to charge a cell; fast chargers require suitable connectors, protection, tariffs, transformer capacity and site readiness. The Ministry of Power issues charging infrastructure guidelines, BIS standards address equipment, CEA governs electrical safety and MoRTH/CMVR governs vehicle type approval. A plugged connector is therefore not proof of an operational and compliant depot.

Inside the pack, a battery management system monitors individual cell voltages, current and temperature, balances cells and limits overcharge and excessive discharge. It cannot physically remove heat by software alone: thermal paths, cooling, cell spacing and containment are necessary to limit cell-to-cell thermal-runaway propagation. Cycle ageing from repeated charging and calendar ageing over time reduce usable capacity, while aggressive charging without thermal management can accelerate stress. A vehicle maker must validate the cell–module–pack assembly rather than extrapolate from a single laboratory cell rating.

For an Indian bus fleet, plan charging around duty cycles and distribution capacity, inspect real pack degradation and maintain safe replacement and disposal processes. Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 impose extended producer responsibility at end of life. The policy test is measured charger uptime, safe cycles and end-of-life recovery, not an announcement of charger count or a blanket claim that all fires arise from lithium itself.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 5 — 20 MARKS

**Question:** Discuss the role of fuel cells, hydrogen and alternative fuels in a segment-wise clean-mobility transition. Answer in about 250 words.

**Independent model answer:** No one energy carrier suits all transport duty cycles. BEVs often work well for urban two-wheelers, three-wheelers and predictable bus routes where depot or home charging can be organised. For heavier or long-haul transport, refuelling time and payload constraints motivate hydrogen fuel-cell pilots; a PEM fuel cell converts stored hydrogen to motor electricity and emits water directly. A high-temperature SOFC is more associated with stationary use. Hydrogen’s climate contribution depends on production: green uses renewable-powered electrolysis, blue fossil feedstock with capture, grey fossil without capture. None of these colour labels changes the hydrogen molecule or establishes a fully zero-emission supply chain.

Other transition fuels meet distinct needs. E20 blends ethanol with petrol, while flex-fuel engines accommodate a wider range of blends; neither makes a vehicle battery-electric. Compressed bio-gas from agricultural or urban waste can be purified for vehicle use under SATAT, and biodiesel comes from processed oils, including used cooking oil. CNG/LNG can diversify conventional fuels but still have combustion and leakage implications.

In India MNRE’s February 2024 green-hydrogen transport-pilot guidelines mark an exploration stage, not proof of national fleet deployment. MoPNG anchors blending and bio-gas pathways; MoRTH assesses vehicle approvals. Evaluate lifecycle emissions, feedstock sustainability, safety, refuelling logistics, price and infrastructure by segment. The aim is pragmatic oil-import and pollution reduction with documented outcomes, rather than treating a pilot, a fuel label or a policy notification as a measured transition.

### ORIGINAL MAINS 6 — 20 MARKS

**Question:** Critically evaluate India’s electric-mobility strategy through lifecycle emissions, critical minerals, recycling, institutions and the announcement-to-deployment boundary. Answer in about 250 words.

**Independent model answer:** India’s electric-mobility strategy has three linked dimensions: vehicle adoption, secure industrial inputs and environmentally sound operations. A BEV removes urban tailpipe exhaust but still draws on grid electricity, energy-intensive cell manufacture and mined materials. Cleaner power and managed charging increase climate gains. NMC’s nickel and cobalt bill differs from LFP’s; LFP still needs lithium, while sodium-ion remains a different development pathway. No chemistry by itself supplies safe packs: BMS and thermal management must be tested.

The Ministry of Heavy Industries’ PM E-DRIVE is a demand measure, distinct from ACC PLI for domestic cell production; Ministry of Power charging rules, BIS standards, CEA electrical safety, MoRTH vehicle approvals and MoHUA’s urban PM-eBus Sewa address different failures. PM E-DRIVE was approved in September 2024, initially notified through March 2026 and extended by the August 2025 order to March 2028, but individual vehicle categories had different deadlines. As at the source’s August 2026 verification, one must not infer that every segment remains eligible. Battery Waste Management Rules, 2022 place collection and recycling obligations on producers, yet recycled feedstock cannot instantly replace all virgin imports.

A sanction, procurement target or installed charging socket is not a delivered safe bus service. Monitor actual fleet operation, charger uptime, lifecycle carbon, cell localisation, recycling recovery and access for smaller cities. India should combine segment-specific support with grid upgrades, robust standards and accountable mineral processing rather than count subsidised purchases alone.
