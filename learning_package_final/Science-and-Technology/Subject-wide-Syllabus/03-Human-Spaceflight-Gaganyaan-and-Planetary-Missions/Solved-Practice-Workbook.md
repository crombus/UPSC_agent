## Six original Mains questions with independent solved models

### Original Mains 1 — 10 marks
**Question:** Explain why human rating is more than an ordinary launcher success record. Answer in about 150 words.
**Model answer:** A conventional launch record asks whether a vehicle can deliver a payload into a specified orbit. Human rating asks whether a complete mission can keep people alive, including when something goes wrong. For Gaganyaan, the LVM3 base must be assessed for adequate design margins, redundant critical functions, continuous vehicle-health monitoring and predictable failure responses. The Crew Escape System must separate the inhabited Crew Module from a failing launcher in relevant pad and ascent phases. An escape motor test addresses only one phase. The orbital spacecraft also needs environmental control, power and propulsion, guided re-entry, parachutes, splashdown localisation, extraction and medical response. Ground-component, abort, air-drop and uncrewed orbital tests answer different questions and should not be conflated with crewed flight. Dated official evidence of each gate matters more than an announced target year. This disciplined approach allows India to gain sovereign capability while refusing to treat an unrelated successful satellite launch as certification of crew survival.

### Original Mains 2 — 10 marks
**Question:** Distinguish Axiom-4 experience from indigenous Gaganyaan capability. Answer in about 150 words.
**Model answer:** Axiom-4 was an international ISS mission on a foreign launch and spacecraft system; the canonical source records Shubhanshu Shukla’s June–July 2025 participation as mission pilot and scientific experiments aboard the station. It offered valuable microgravity, procedures and crew-operations experience to an Indian astronaut. Gaganyaan has a different objective: develop and qualify India’s own human-rated LVM3, inhabited Crew Module, Service Module, abort system, life support and sea-recovery chain. Training and experience can inform operations but cannot by themselves certify domestic propulsion, re-entry or emergency escape. Pad Abort Test, TV-D1 and subsequent parachute air-drop tests are distinct qualification steps, not an Indian crewed orbital flight. As of the canonical source’s August 2026 check, the first Gaganyaan orbital precursor was still prospective. State each milestone with date and ownership: international participation is a genuine completed achievement, while an indigenous crewed launch remains a separately tested goal.

### Original Mains 3 — 15 marks
**Question:** Analyse the Gaganyaan qualification ladder before crewed flight. Answer in about 250 words.
**Model answer:** Crew safety must be built by progressively testing the components and then their integration. On the ground, qualify propulsion, avionics, redundant controls, environmental control and life support, Crew Module structure and Service Module interfaces. Human-rated LVM3 adds margins, health monitoring and failure response beyond a routine payload launch. Escape tests evaluate whether solid motors pull the Crew Module away from a failing vehicle: the July 2018 Pad Abort Test and October 2023 TV-D1 test-vehicle demonstration answer specific abort questions rather than proving orbital readiness. Aircraft-dropped mass simulators in the August 2025 and April 2026 IADT tests, followed by a July 2026 parachute qualification test in the source, examine deceleration and recovery without rocket launch. A full uncrewed orbital precursor must then integrate launch, separation, onboard systems, orbital operation, re-entry and sea recovery under flight conditions. Only after examining its findings can a crewed mission be responsibly attempted. A splashdown is not sufficient unless recovery teams locate the module, extract occupants and deliver medical support. Crew training at Bengaluru and Navy participation in recovery are complementary, not substitutes for hardware certification. The canonical source stated G1 had not flown as of its August 2026 verification; later completion requires a new dated official source. This status-first ladder protects astronauts and distinguishes a schedule target from a demonstrated capability.

### Original Mains 4 — 15 marks
**Question:** Explain India’s lunar capability progression without overstating sample-return readiness. Answer in about 250 words.
**Model answer:** Lunar exploration proceeds through distinct operations with distinct evidence. Chandrayaan-1 contributed orbital science, including sourced hydroxyl/water findings; observing the Moon did not demonstrate surface landing. Chandrayaan-2 entered the lunar-orbiter science track, but its lander did not achieve the intended soft landing.
