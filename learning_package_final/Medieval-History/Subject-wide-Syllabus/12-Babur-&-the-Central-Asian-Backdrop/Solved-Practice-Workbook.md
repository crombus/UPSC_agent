### Part II — Answer key, option-wise explanations and examiner traps

#### Workbook MCQ 1 — solution

**Correct answer: A**

**Option-wise explanations:**
- **A.** Correct. Samarqand carried Timurid prestige, urban wealth and strategic value, which is why Babur repeatedly risked so much to capture it.
- **B.** Incorrect. The sources treat Samarqand as materially important as well as symbolically prestigious; calling it only a shrine-city strips away the fiscal and strategic dimension.
- **C.** Incorrect. Safavid influence mattered later and indirectly, but Samarqand in Babur's youth was contested above all through the Timurid-Uzbek struggle.
- **D.** Incorrect. Samarqand mattered long before Panipat; the Central Asian struggle is the precondition for Topic 12, not a sequel to India.

**Examiner trap:** prestige city reduced to sentimental nostalgia

#### Workbook MCQ 2 — solution

**Correct answer: B**

**Option-wise explanations:**
- **A.** Incorrect. Babur fought the Lodi state; he cannot be collapsed into Afghan-Lodi political identity.
- **B.** Correct. This wording preserves genealogy, political legitimacy and court culture without turning them into a biological label.
- **C.** Incorrect. The topic repeatedly warns that “Mughal” or “Mongol” cannot serve as a racial master-key for Babur's politics.
- **D.** Incorrect. Safavid help mattered at moments, but Babur was not a Safavid governor holding Kabul on delegated Iranian authority.

**Examiner trap:** genealogy flattened into race

#### Workbook MCQ 3 — solution

**Correct answer: C**

**Option-wise explanations:**
- **A.** Incorrect. Merv concerned the Safavid defeat of Shaybani Khan near the Oxus sphere; it did not transfer Delhi or Indian territory.
- **B.** Incorrect. Babur did make use of Safavid support, so total refusal is the opposite of the problem.
- **C.** Correct. The opening was temporary because Babur still faced the old resource problem once Uzbek recovery resumed.
- **D.** Incorrect. Ottoman involvement mattered through the regional balance after Chaldiran, not through a direct conquest of Kabul in 1510.

**Examiner trap:** external ally treated as permanent substitute for state base

#### Workbook MCQ 4 — solution

- **C.** Incorrect. Babur's Samarqand interest survived beyond 1514; what changed was the feasibility of holding it.
- **D.** Correct. That is the safe connection: Chaldiran matters indirectly by shifting the wider balance against Safavid leverage.

**Examiner trap:** Chaldiran converted into a one-step cause of Panipat
