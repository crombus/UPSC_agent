### MCQ 1. Which formulation most accurately defines Urban disaster risk?

A. Urban disaster risk arises where hazards interact with dense populations, concentrated assets, unequal vulnerability and tightly coupled services; it is not merely a rural hazard occurring inside municipal limits.
B. Interdependency mapping identifies physical, digital, geographic and organisational dependencies among water, power, transport, health, telecom, sanitation and emergency systems before prioritising protection.
C. Safer construction requires risk-sensitive standards, adoption, competent design, supervision, occupancy control, maintenance and retrofit; publication of a code proves none of the later links.
D. ULBs, utilities, development authorities, districts, SDMAs and regulators retain distinct mandates; city coordination must connect them without erasing accountability.

**Answer: A**

- **A:** Correct: This is the precise meaning of **Urban disaster risk**.
- **B:** Incorrect: This accurately describes **Interdependency mapping**, but not the concept asked.
- **C:** Incorrect: This accurately describes **Building-code and retrofit chain**, but not the concept asked.
- **D:** Incorrect: This accurately describes **Urban institutional coordination**, but not the concept asked.

### ORIGINAL 1 — 10 marks, 150 words

**Question:** Distinguish critical infrastructure, lifelines, robustness, redundancy and rapid recovery. Answer in about 150 words.

**Model answer (137 words):**

Critical infrastructure is an asset, network or service whose disruption has disproportionate effects; a lifeline is the essential service it enables, such as water, power, health, transport or telecom. A hospital may remain standing while its lifeline function fails through loss of power, oxygen, staff access or communications.

Robustness is resistance to stress: a flood-protected substation. Redundancy is spare capacity or an alternate route: a second feeder. Diversity means the alternative does not share the same failure mode: a separately sited microgrid rather than two co-located feeders. Rapid recovery is planned restoration through repair crews, spares, contracts and sequencing.

Criticality determines what must be protected first; robustness limits initial damage; redundant and diverse capacity sustains minimum service; recovery restores normal function. Effectiveness must be tested through continuity and restoration outcomes, not inferred from ownership or plan publication.

### ORIGINAL 3 — 15 marks, 250 words

**Question:** Examine how risk-informed land use, building-code enforcement and retrofit together reduce urban disaster risk. Answer in about 250 words.

Urban disaster risk is produced by where development occurs, how it is built and whether existing stock remains safe. The three instruments operate at different points of exposure and vulnerability.
