# Indian Legal and Institutional Architecture — Solved Practice Workbook

## BASIC MCQS / REMEDIATION
### Q1. A district collector needs statutory authority — not mere administrative custom — to assign prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery duties across three tiers of government. Which instrument supplies that authority?

A. The Disaster Management Act, 2005 establishes national, state and district authorities and assigns prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery functions.
B. The National Disaster Response Fund under section 46 finances eligible response and relief needs and is institutionally distinct from the identically abbreviated response force.
C. Section 41A enables a State Government to constitute an Urban Disaster Management Authority by notification; the statutory permission does not prove constitution in every eligible city.
D. Sections 8A and 8B inserted in 2025 give statutory status to the National Crisis Management Committee and High Level Committee.

**Answer: A.**
**Option-by-option explanation:**
- **Option A (correct):** The Disaster Management Act, 2005 establishes national, state and district authorities and assigns prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response and recovery functions. — the DM Act, 2005 is the parent statute that creates the national/state/district authorities and assigns the five functions themselves; it is the source of the tiered mandate the scenario asks for, not a later fund, enabling section or 2025 insertion.
- **Option B (incorrect):** The National Disaster Response Fund under section 46 finances eligible response and relief needs and is institutionally distinct from the identically abbreviated response force. — section 46 funds relief and response expenditure; it does not create the three-tier authority structure the scenario asks about.
- **Option C (incorrect):** Section 41A enables a State Government to constitute an Urban Disaster Management Authority by notification; the statutory permission does not prove constitution in every eligible city. — section 41A only permits an optional additional city-level authority; it presupposes the Act's tiered structure rather than creating it.
- **Option D (incorrect):** Sections 8A and 8B inserted in 2025 give statutory status to the National Crisis Management Committee and High Level Committee. — these 2025-inserted committees add crisis-coordination bodies; they do not establish the base national/state/district tiers.
**Trap:** Do not credit a later amendment, fund section or committee insertion with creating the foundational tiered structure that the parent 2005 Act itself establishes.

- **Option B (correct):** Sections 8A and 8B inserted in 2025 give statutory status to the National Crisis Management Committee and High Level Committee. — statutory crisis-committee status for the NCMC and HLC was inserted only in 2025 and is a coordination/command layer, not one of the Act's original five functions (prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response, recovery).

**Trap:** Do not mistake a body that operationalises one of the five functions (force, institute) for a sixth function; the trap option must name something genuinely outside prevention/mitigation/preparedness/response/recovery.

**Demand:** Discuss how the Government's proactive disaster-management approach replaced the earlier reactive strategy.

**Status:** Verified direct routing: Discuss · 15 marks · 250 words.

**Demand decoding:** 'Discuss' demands a two-sided narrative — trace the institutional shift the DM Act made possible, then weigh it against evidence of real implementation, rather than simply listing bodies.
