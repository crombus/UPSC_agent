### MCQ 1. Which statement best defines Response, relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction and recovery?

A. Response controls the incident, relief meets urgent needs, rehabilitation restores basic functioning, reconstruction restores systems more fully, and recovery spans the wider process.
B. Forecast-based pre-positioning shortens response where lead time exists; sudden hazards rely more on standing capacity and distributed stocks.
C. Last-mile distribution converts bulk supply into safe, timely and accessible delivery through transparent criteria, schedules, outreach, queue safety and feedback.
D. Public criteria, records, segregation of duties, spot checks, community monitoring, confidential complaints and correction reduce exclusion, duplication and diversion.

**Answer: A**

- **A:** Correct: This is the precise meaning of **Response, relief, rehabilitation, reconstruction and recovery**.
- **B:** Incorrect: This describes **Preparedness and pre-positioning**, not the concept asked.
- **C:** Incorrect: This describes **Last-mile distribution**, not the concept asked.
- **D:** Incorrect: This describes **Grievance and anti-diversion controls**, not the concept asked.

**Model answer (150 words):**
