# Political Theory Mains-Focused Generation Rules

These rules supplement the repository's live-session generation, validation and release
rules. The stricter rule applies where requirements overlap.

## Scope

1. Preserve the comprehensive topic session unchanged.
2. Create a separate edition with 8-12 dependency-led lessons.
3. Target GS-II, GS-IV and Essay use. Political Theory remains supplementary conceptual
   support and must not be presented as a directly named GS question owner.
4. Use the comprehensive session as a coverage map, not as an unquestioned source.
   Canonical Markdown, verified local books and verified PYQ ledgers remain controlling.
5. Remove optional-only detail, bibliographic accumulation and repeated source disputes
   unless they change a Mains argument or prevent a factual error.

## Lesson contract

Every lesson must include:

- a progress line followed by the fully delimited pre-teach checklist;
- one visual-first explanation;
- a compact doctrine or argument spine;
- explicit GS-II, GS-IV or Essay deployment;
- one answer framework or paragraph architecture;
- 8-12 revision notes;
- exactly one concept check, concise model and misconception note;
- one original Mains question, complete model and unique quantified rubric.

## Topic contract

- Teach the minimum complete conceptual chain needed to write analytical answers.
- Prefer contrasts, causal chains, objection-reply structures and usable thinker
  formulations over encyclopaedic coverage.
- Carry only genuine current linkages and label Political Theory PYQs separately from GS.
- Use concept-check mode; do not create a compiled four-option MCQ corpus.
- Retain the standard final H1 arc required by `tools\validate_live_session.py`.
- Keep consolidated register notes as the final teaching section.
- Validate and independently review every exact hash before indexing or release.

## Size discipline

- Lessons: 8-12.
- Lesson-local 10-mark model: no more than 150 words.
- Final 15- and 20-mark models: no more than 250 words.
- Compression must remove low-utility depth, not definitions, causal logic, major
  objections, balanced conclusions or answer-writing practice.
