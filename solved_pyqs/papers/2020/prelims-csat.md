## Q1. Passage Inference
**Primary route:** CSAT → [Reading Comprehension](../../topics/csat/reading-comprehension.md) → Passage inference · **Backlink:** [Paper-wise index](../../catalogue/PAPERWISE-INDEX.md)

**Passage:** In India, over the last decade or so, labour has been departing agriculture, but is only going to construction and unregistered manufacturing which are not markedly better jobs. Services, where labour tends to be most productive, are not generating the additional jobs the country needs. India will need 24 million or so jobs over the next decade. The new sector, e-commerce, can at best close only half the jobs gap. Only those sectors that drive domestic demand such as health and education can comfortably fill the other half.

Which one of the following is best implied in the passage?

(a) Strong measures need to be taken to reduce the rural to urban migration of labour.

(b) The working condition in unregistered construction and manufacturing needs to be improved.

(c) Service sector has been reducing the problem of unemployment.

(d) Increased social sector spending is imperative for large-scale job creation.

**Metadata:** Family 01 · Reading Comprehension → Passage inference · Slug: `passage-inference-2020-q01`  
**Canonical owner:** `CSAT/basic/01_Reading-Comprehension.md`

**FINAL answer: (d).**

**Full reasoning:** The passage says e-commerce can fill only half the jobs gap and that health and education can fill the other half. Those are social sectors, so greater social-sector spending is the necessary implication. (a) and (b) prescribe measures not argued; (c) reverses the passage, which says services are not generating enough jobs.

**Shortest safe method:** Follow the passage's express claim and reject added prescriptions or absolutes.

## Q8. Cyclic-sum Divisibility
**Primary route:** CSAT → [Number Systems and Number Sense](../../topics/csat/number-systems-and-number-sense.md) → Cyclic-sum divisibility · **Backlink:** [Paper-wise index](../../catalogue/PAPERWISE-INDEX.md)

Let XYZ be a three-digit number, where (X + Y + Z) is not a multiple of 3. Then (XYZ + YZX + ZXY) is not divisible by

(a) 3

(b) 9

(c) 37

(d) (X + Y + Z)

**Metadata:** Family 02 · Number Systems and Number Sense → Cyclic-sum divisibility · Slug: `cyclic-sum-divisibility-2020-q08`  
**Canonical owner:** `CSAT/basic/02_Number-Systems-and-Number-Sense.md`

**FINAL answer: (b).**

**Full reasoning:** XYZ + YZX + ZXY = 111(X+Y+Z) = 3×37×(X+Y+Z). It is always divisible by 3, 37 and (X+Y+Z). Because X+Y+Z is not divisible by 3, the expression has only one guaranteed factor 3 and is not divisible by 9.

**Shortest safe method:** Factor the cyclic sum as 111(X+Y+Z).

Let p, q, r and s be natural numbers such that p − 2016 = q + 2017 = r − 2018 = s + 2019 Which one of the following is the largest natural number?

(a) p

(b) q

**Metadata:** Family 05 · Algebra, Inequalities and Data Sufficiency → Equal-expression ordering · Slug: `equal-expression-ordering-2020-q09`  
**Canonical owner:** `CSAT/basic/05_Algebra-Inequalities-and-Data-Sufficiency.md`

**FINAL answer: (c).**

## Q16. Digit-constraint Sum Puzzle

Let A3BC and DE2F be four-digit numbers where each letter represents a different digit greater than 3. If the sum of the numbers is 15902, then what is the difference between the values of A and D?

(a) 1

(b) 2

(c) 3

(d) 4

**Metadata:** Family 05 · Algebra, Inequalities and Data Sufficiency → Digit-constraint sum puzzle · Slug: `digit-constraint-sum-puzzle-2020-q16`

**Shortest safe method:** Do column addition from units with carries.

| | Average marks in English | Average marks in Hindi |
|---|---:|---:|
| Girls | 9 | 8 |
| Boys | 8 | 7 |
| Overall average marks | 8.8 | x |

What is the value of x in the above table?

(a) 7.8

(b) 7.6

(c) 7.4

(d) 7.2

**Metadata:** Family 03 · Arithmetic and Commercial Mathematics → Weighted group average · Slug: `weighted-group-average-2020-q18`
