## Family-specific checks applied

- **Prelims:** key status, answer, option/statement analysis, elimination and provenance.
- **CSAT:** complete reasoning/calculation or passage proof, plus a safe exam method.
- **Qualifying languages:** source-verified structure, idiomatic accuracy, précis discipline and translation only where officially present.
- **Essay:** interpretation, thesis, transition-bearing structure, counter-view, synthesis and model treatment.
- **GS-I/II/III:** demand fidelity, proportionality, named evidence, qualification and examiner takeaway.
- **GS-IV:** theory application and stakeholder–values–options–consequences–safeguards architecture.
- **Philosophy:** English-first terminology, IAST where relevant, argument–objection–reply–evaluation and exact syllabus routing.
