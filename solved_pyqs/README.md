# UPSC Solved PYQ Catalogue

This workspace is the released paper-wise and topic-wise solved-PYQ library for the latest eight
repository-held years, **2019–2026**.

## Final release coverage

- **88 paper files**: 8 years × 11 in-scope paper families.
- **88 paper-specific review records**, all with final release status **PASS**.
- **3,416 answerable solved questions/parts**.
- **3,600 routed stable anchors**, plus one preserved 2026 English source-fidelity note.
- **353 canonical topic pages** with exact paper-anchor links and no duplicated full solutions.
- **32,000 represented Mains/Philosophy marks**; attempted-paper totals remain separately stated.

## Key-provenance discipline

- 2019–2025 released Prelims keys remain official-final, including every recorded drop or
  multiple-answer outcome.
- Both repository-held 2026 keys remain explicitly **provisional**.
- 2026 Prelims GS-I Q64 remains dropped (`X`) in the provisional key.
- No provisional or independently inferred answer is promoted to official-final status.

## Completion rule

A paper is complete only after source, transcription, solution, evidence, classification, count,
bidirectional-link and mandatory post-pass reviews all succeed under [INSTRUCTIONS.md](INSTRUCTIONS.md).
