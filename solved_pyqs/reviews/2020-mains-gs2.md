## High-risk facts specifically checked

- **Q1:** Section 8A disqualification follows the President’s decision on the Election Commission’s opinion; it is not conflated with conviction-based Section 8.
- **Q2:** The amendment shifted tenure and service conditions from the Act to Union prescription; the 2019 Rules’ three-year term is distinguished from the original five-year framework.
- **Q4:** UK courts may issue Human Rights Act declarations of incompatibility but do not thereby invalidate primary legislation.
- **Q5:** Article 110 certification, Article 100 casting vote and Tenth Schedule adjudication are distinct Speaker functions.
- **Q9:** WHO dates and instruments are attributed to the 2020 pandemic context; limitations are not converted into powers WHO legally lacks.
- **Q11:** EDA Section 2A concerns Union measures for international travel/ports; DMA central powers and State implementation are kept distinct. Farm-law competence is presented as contested, not judicially settled in the answer.
- **Q12:** *Vishaka* guidelines are described as temporary rights-vacuum safeguards later replaced by the 2013 statute.
- **Q15:** State ratification is not incorrectly made mandatory for every constitutional amendment creating a commission.
- **Q16:** The answer uses the Global MPI 2020 and its survey-period qualification; India’s approximately 273 million reduction refers to 2005/06–2015/16.
- **Q18:** NEP 2020’s 50% higher-education GER target is dated to 2035, vocational exposure to 2025, and 6% of GDP remains an expenditure aspiration.
- **Q19:** Quad is described as neither a treaty alliance nor a preferential trade bloc; Malabar participation is not treated as a collective-defence obligation.
- **Q20:** LEMOA, COMCASA and BECA dates and functions remain distinct; Russian legacy support is not treated as an Indo-Pacific alliance.
