## Evidence and factual checks

- Q1 presents MGNREGA as a statutory claim to employment and Kudumbashree as an enabling
  collective institution; it does not claim that either removes the need for social protection.
- Q2 uses Odisha’s cyclone preparedness and the Green Revolution as qualified illustrations of
  speed supported by institution-building and feedback, not as claims of flawless success.
- Q3 uses Lal Bahadur Shastri and E. Sreedharan only as illustrative associations with restraint
  and professional execution; it explicitly rejects personality worship.
- Q4 accurately treats the Preamble’s justice, liberty, equality and fraternity and the
  Fundamental Duties’ scientific temper, harmony and environmental protection as civic anchors.
  Gandhi and Tagore are used for broad educational orientations without fabricated quotation.
- Q5 does not deny technological displacement or claim that every present occupation will survive.
  Its distinction between tasks, jobs and responsibility is analytically qualified.
- Q6 uses constitutional morality as a normative framework and does not falsely attribute a
  numerical economic trend or declare India to be in a technical recession.
- Q7 presents scientific, welfare and institutional achievements without unsupported rankings,
  percentages or targets. Its claims are normative and clearly framed as national aspirations.
- Q8 accurately treats post-1991 reforms as expanding competition and modern services without
  claiming that reform automatically produced inclusion. Directive Principles are described
  within their welfare and anti-concentration orientation, not as a ban on private enterprise.

## Defects found and corrected

1. The canonical 2015 paper and owned review files were absent; both were created.
2. Source transcription was checked directly against the rendered page-2 scan rather than copied
   from an unverified web list.
3. Section B restarts printed numbering at 1–4; stable IDs correctly continue Q5–Q8 while metadata
   preserves the printed number.
4. Q6 was initially entered with an ASCII hyphen. Visual source review restored the printed en
   dash in “Crisis faced in India – moral or economic.”
5. The source’s unusual grammar in Q4 and the space before the question mark in Q8 were preserved
   rather than silently modernised.
6. Q3 was expanded to test performance against fairness and the treatment of less powerful
   institutional members, strengthening the means–ends dimension.
7. Q4 was expanded with digital literacy and assessment design so that the values argument covers
   contemporary educational power as well as philosophical ideals.
8. Unsupported numerical claims and decorative quotations were excluded. Every counter-view was
   revised to constrain the thesis and alter the final synthesis.
