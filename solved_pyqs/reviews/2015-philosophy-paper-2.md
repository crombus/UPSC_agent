## 4. Accuracy safeguards

The review specifically confirmed that:

1. Equality is not confused with mechanical sameness; differential treatment is tied to relevant,
   public and reviewable criteria.
2. Civil disobedience is defended only under conditions of publicity, non-violence,
   proportionality and serious injustice.
3. Laski’s denial of unlimited monistic sovereignty is not converted into denial of all
   constitutional finality.
4. Group rights protect cultural agency but cannot license internal domination.
5. Economic empowerment is treated as necessary and catalytic, not sufficient for ending
   patriarchy.
6. Ambedkar’s caste critique retains endogamy, graded inequality, “division of labourers,”
   fraternity and constitutional morality.
7. Parliamentary democracy is distinguished from simple electoral majoritarianism.
8. Ambedkar’s “spiritual” growth is read ethically through dignity, reason and fraternity, not
   ritual otherworldliness.
9. Religious nationalism is distinguished from the legitimate participation of religious
   citizens in public reason.
10. Buddhism and Jainism are not assigned a creator God; Buddhism is not assigned an immortal
    transmigrating soul.
11. Divine causation of natural evil is distinguished into direct and permissive responsibility;
    free will is not used to explain natural evil.
12. Non-falsifiability is correctly rejected as a mode of verification.
13. Advaitic identity is not equated with Sufi *fanāʾ*; Sufi experience remains situated within
    Islamic divine transcendence and *tawḥīd*.
14. Revelation receives possible cumulative warrant, not circular self-certification or
    demonstrative proof.
15. Plantinga’s defence addresses logical compatibility more successfully than the evidential
    weight of horrendous evil.
16. “Absence of karma” is qualified as absence of binding karma capable of renewed rebirth.
17. Nyāya’s God is presented primarily as intelligent efficient cause and karmic governor, not
    simplistically as the material cause of eternal atoms.
18. Kant’s objection to the ontological argument is accurately stated; a first cosmological cause
    is not assumed automatically to possess every theistic attribute.
19. Vedānta is internally differentiated: Advaita knowledge and non-duality, Viśiṣṭādvaita
    devotion/surrender and qualified unity, and Dvaita difference, devotion and grace.
