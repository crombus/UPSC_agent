## Evidence and factual checks

- Q1’s constitutional references are used within their proper scope: equality, non-discrimination
  and permissible special provisions. Kudumbashree is presented as evidence of collective agency,
  not as a universal causal claim.
- Q2 describes Articles 39(b) and 39(c) as Directive Principles and does not convert Gandhian
  trusteeship into an enforceable substitute for regulation.
- Q3 correctly locates Parliament’s interstate-river adjudication power in Article 262 and does not
  imply that tribunals eliminate all judicial or implementation questions.
- Q4 does not claim that Aadhaar or UPI alone guarantees inclusion; both are qualified by privacy,
  access, accountability and interoperability.
- Q5 avoids claiming that constitutional cooperation requires unanimity or identical political
  control. It distinguishes fiscal transfer from meaningful policy voice.
- Q6 accurately uses *K.S. Puttaswamy* for recognition of privacy as a fundamental right and treats
  legality, legitimate purpose and proportionality as constitutional constraints rather than a ban
  on data use.
- Q7 uses MGNREGA as a statutory rural employment floor, not as a substitute for structural
  transformation, and makes no unsupported numerical employment claim.
- Q8 distinguishes account ownership and connection from autonomous use; no contested estimate is
  presented as fact.

## Defects found and corrected

1. The 2016 paper solution and owned review files were absent; both were created.
2. The held PDF contains no extractable text. Topics were transcribed visually from the validated
   page-2 scan rather than reconstructed from secondary lists.
3. Section B restarts printed numbering at 1–4. Stable IDs correctly continue Q5–Q8 while metadata
   preserves the official printed number.
4. Source punctuation and wording were retained, including the spaces before colons in Section B,
   capitalised “States” in Q3 and the singular “human civilization” in Q6.
5. Initial treatments of Q1 and Q2 required stronger named Indian evidence. The final paper adds
   relevant constitutional provisions, Kudumbashree and Gandhian trusteeship without converting
   examples into slogans.
6. Q6’s privacy analysis was strengthened with the *K.S. Puttaswamy* judgment and a precise
   institutional qualification.
7. Q7’s reform analysis was strengthened with MGNREGA as a qualified employment and social-security
   example rather than a complete jobs strategy.
8. Brittle numerical claims and decorative quotations were excluded. Counter-views were revised to
   change each synthesis rather than function as token “however” paragraphs.
