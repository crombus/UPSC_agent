## Defects found and corrected

1. The image-only PDF had no text layer; every question was transcribed from rendered official
   page images rather than reconstructed from a coaching source.
2. Initial answers for Q1–Q3, Q5–Q6, Q8–Q19 exceeded or approached the printed limit under
   table-aware counting; they were tightened without removing the controlling evidence or
   qualifications.
3. Q2’s Ashtadiggaja formulation was qualified because the exact canonical list is shaped by later
   tradition.
4. Q5 was revised to avoid reducing Bose to militarism or Gandhi to passivity, while preserving
   their substantive strategic differences.
5. Q10’s identification procedure and constitutional provisions were separated so “Scheduled”
   was not incorrectly treated as a sociological label.
6. Q11, Q16, Q18 and Q19 were audited for temporal leakage; only institutions and developments
   available by the 2016 examination were retained.
7. All anchors, slugs, required fields, answer counts and relative links were mechanically
   rechecked after correction.
