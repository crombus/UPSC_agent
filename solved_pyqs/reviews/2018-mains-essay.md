## Evidence and depth review

- Q1 joins distributed clean energy, agriculture, water, cities, warnings and ecosystems to
  governance, affordability and a just transition; technology is never treated as a standalone fix.
- Q2 treats love as enlarged concern and knowledge as factual, practical and self-knowledge; it
  addresses paternalism, technical domination, self-care and institutional justice.
- Q3 distinguishes poverty from low income and prosperity from enclave wealth, then traces lost
  capability, weak demand, health externalities, political distrust and cross-border risk.
- Q4 uses safe named illustrations—the India–Bangladesh land-boundary settlement and the Indus
  Waters Treaty—without implying that one model mechanically fits every frontier.
- Q5 acknowledges custom’s coordinating and meaning-giving roles before testing it through dignity,
  equal agency, evidence, constitutional morality and public justification.
- Q6 covers personal identity, language, constitutional memory, trauma, nostalgia, reconciliation,
  digital memory and the reinterpretation of inherited value.
- Q7 avoids equating unequal outcomes with privilege and expressly protects justified remedial
  differentiation; its mechanism is loss of reciprocity, trust and institutional restraint.
- Q8 makes ideals revisable rather than evidence-proof and distinguishes legitimate compromise from
  surrender, partial progress from completion and aspiration from coercive blueprint.

## Defects found and corrected

1. The canonical 2018 solved-paper and review files were absent; both owned files were created.
2. Direct PDF extraction fragments the page layout and OCR-reads the second English heading as
   `SECTION-E`. The instruction says Sections A and B, and the verified corpus records Section B;
   the discrepancy is disclosed without altering any prompt.
3. The corpus adds terminal full stops as ledger punctuation, while the held paper prints none
   after its English prompt lines. Canonical headings follow the paper display exactly, retaining
   the em dash in Q4, quotation marks around `'The past'` in Q6 and the comma in Q8.
4. Every initial framework was tested for a genuine counter-view. Syntheses were revised where
   needed so they constrain the thesis rather than merely append “however.”
5. Unsupported numerical claims and author attributions were excluded; named examples are used for
   analytical function rather than decoration.
