## Evidence and depth review

- Q1 uses profiling, recommendation loops, automated assessment and assistive access as bounded
  mechanisms rather than unsupported technological claims.
- Q2 distinguishes observer projection from real conduct and uses gendered leadership judgments,
  public administration and due process to prevent radical subjectivism.
- Q3 uses capability, positional consumption, inequality and ecological limits without fabricated
  data or romantic claims about poverty.
- Q4 uses science, institutions, colonial contradiction and constitutional transformation while
  explicitly rejecting the inference that whatever exists is rationally justified.
- Q5 recognises unpaid care and public early-life support without assigning childcare naturally or
  exclusively to women.
- Q6 separates open outcomes from methodless inquiry and includes research ethics, replication and
  collaboration without attaching an author to the official prompt.
- Q7 uses recurring mechanisms rather than superficial event-pairing and does not attribute the
  unattributed printed proposition.
- Q8 distinguishes transferable principles from copied forms and qualifies experimentation in
  high-risk fields through standards, safeguards and evaluation.
