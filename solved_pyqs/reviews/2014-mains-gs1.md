## Defects found and corrected

1. The image-only source was rendered and reread page by page; punctuation and the complete
   Q1–Q25 order were restored from the paper rather than secondary transcriptions.
2. Initial solution drafts exceeded the common 150-word limit; all were tightened while retaining
   controlling claims, named evidence, analysis, qualification and conclusions.
3. Q1 was qualified to avoid claiming a direct municipal lineage from Harappan cities.
4. Q2 was revised to separate Roman features from the broader Greek-Hellenistic vocabulary and to
   retain the Indian-Buddhist purpose of Gandhara art.
5. Q3 preserves traditional associations without treating every Taxila biography as equally proven.
6. Q5 distinguishes appreciable devotional-cultural change from limited transformation of caste,
   patriarchy and material structure.
7. Q7 treats the naval revolt as an accelerator and coercive warning, not the sole cause of freedom.
8. Q10 distinguishes Lenin’s NEP from later Soviet central planning and from India’s multiple
   indigenous, liberal, Keynesian and nationalist influences.
9. Q12 avoids equating regional prosperity with sex selection by testing family institutions,
   technology, fertility and migration separately.
10. Q17 distinguishes subduction volcanism from continent-collision seismicity.
11. Q19 corrects the official stem’s geographical overstatement while still explaining why the
    three named basins are prominent.
12. Q20 records differentiated glacier behaviour and does not project uniform retreat.
13. Q21 preserves Kangra as a real western tea region and uses the 1905 earthquake without
    accepting the stem as total failure outside Darjeeling.
14. Q25 was audited against a 2014 time boundary; no later summit, corridor or policy was used.
15. All headings, anchors, slugs, required fields, answer counts and relative links were
    mechanically rechecked after correction.
