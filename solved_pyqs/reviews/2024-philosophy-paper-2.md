## 4. Conceptual and evidence audit

Answers were checked against both complete 2018–2025 Paper-II PYQ banks, the official syllabus and
all relevant canonical owners. High-risk controls include:

- Plato’s justice is functional harmony, not modern distributive equality.
- Social-contract development distinguishes Hobbesian security, Lockean trust and rights, and
  Rousseauian collective self-legislation.
- Marxian alienation retains all four dimensions and is not reduced to low wages.
- Aristotle’s comparative success over Plato is not misreported as modern liberalism.
- Deterrence is treated as marginal effectiveness over feasible imprisonment, separately from
  retribution; *Bachan Singh* (1980) and Law Commission Report No. 262 (2015) are correctly typed.
- Kauṭilya’s *saptāṅga*, *daṇḍanīti*, *maṇḍala*, *ṣāḍguṇya* and *yogakṣema* remain distinct.
- Female foeticide is distinguished from abortion generally; technology is an enabler rather than
  the sole cause, and women’s autonomy is retained.
- Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika *apavarga* preserves the self while ending pain-producing conditions.
- Aquinas’s analogical predication distinguishes the perfection signified from the creaturely mode
  of signification.
- Spinoza’s God is the one infinite substance expressed through attributes and modes, not a
  transcendent personal artisan.
- Buddhism includes both the practical Middle Path through the Noble Eightfold Path
  (*āryāṣṭāṅgamārga*) and the doctrinal middle grounded in dependent origination
  (*pratītyasamutpāda*).
- Anselm’s ontological proof, Gaunilo’s parody, Kant’s predicate objection and the modal possibility
  burden are separated.
- R. B. Braithwaite is presented as analysing religious assertion through an agapeistic moral
  intention and sustaining stories, without denying that believers often intend realist claims.
- English-first terminology is used; Sanskrit terms follow immediately in accurate IAST.

Every answer supplies a defensible thesis, named doctrine or evidence, argument, objection or
