  or later security event is presented as contemporaneous evidence.
- The Companies answer remains with the 2013 Bill architecture and does not import later CSR
  penalty amendments or subsequent rules as operating evidence.
- The food-security answer uses the 2013 Bill/legal-entitlement context and the contemporary WTO
  public-stockholding dispute; it does not rely on later peace-clause outcomes.
- GST is treated as a proposed regime delayed by constitutional and federal disagreement, not as
  the operational tax launched in 2017.
- The Green Energy Corridor is treated as an envisaged grid-integration programme in 2013, not as
  a completed later phase.
- The patent answer uses only the April 2013 *Novartis* judgment and the 2005 amendment.
- Disaster management remains under the Disaster Management Act, 2005 and does not invoke the
  later Sendai Framework as contemporary policy.
- The mining answer describes the Go/No-Go debate and transition toward inviolate-area criteria as
  known by 2013; it does not imply that the original binary list remained settled law.
- Cyber preparedness uses the National Cyber Security Policy, 2013, CERT-In, NTRO and the amended
  IT Act; later defence cyber agencies and data-protection statutes are excluded.
- Border and Left Wing Extremism answers use institutions and legislation available by 2013 and
  avoid later agreements, operations and policy outcomes.

## Defects found and corrected

1. The validated image-only paper was locally rendered and OCR-assisted, then inspected page by
   page; question wording and the 25-question/28-marked-unit pattern were not reconstructed from an
   unverified coaching transcript.
2. Initial drafts above the 200- or 100-word ceiling were compressed while preserving each
   question limb, named evidence, causal analysis and qualification.
3. The CSR treatment was corrected to the 2013 spending-or-explain design rather than an
   unqualified mandatory-spending claim.
4. Green Energy Corridor wording was bounded to its 2013 programme stage and does not claim later
   commissioning or storage capability.
5. The Novartis answer was checked against the exact Section 3(d) therapeutic-efficacy holding and
   qualified so that the judgment is not misrepresented as prohibiting every incremental patent.
6. The UDRS answer distinguishes infrared, acoustic, camera and trajectory evidence and presents
   silicone tape as an allegation affecting Hot Spot rather than an established defeat of DRS.
7. The Go/No-Go answer records both the ecological-screening logic and the policy’s contested,
   non-final character.
8. All anchors, metadata blocks, answer counts, source digest and outbound links were mechanically
   recomputed after final edits.
