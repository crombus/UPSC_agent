## 8. Defects found and corrected

1. The PDF text layer merges words and corrupts Indic transliteration; exact sequence and wording
   were controlled against page text and the verified banks, with terms normalised to IAST.
2. “Transcendence of Space and Time” was interpreted through Kant’s transcendental ideality rather
   than incorrectly claiming that space and time are transcendent noumenal entities.
3. The Cārvāka answers distinguish formal validity from warrant for a universal premise and avoid
   attributing an unqualified rejection of practical probability.
4. The Buddhist momentariness answer does not claim that dependent origination alone deductively
   proves instant destruction; it supplies the additional causal-efficiency argument.
5. The Sāṃkhya unity answer identifies *samanvayāt* as the principal proof and treats coordinated
   dissolution as reinforcement rather than conflating all proofs.

No known material defect remains in the owned scope.
