## Attribution and precision audit

1. Kant’s printed dictum is interpreted through intuition and concepts without changing the official heading.
2. Hegelian dialectic is not reduced to a mechanical thesis–antithesis–synthesis formula.
3. Spinozistic freedom is self-determination through understood necessity, not exemption from causality.
4. Kierkegaard’s individual is presented as an existential corrective, not proof that social universality is meaningless.
5. Kant’s synthetic a priori claims are restricted to possible experience.
6. Hume’s psychological account distinguishes observed conjunction from necessary connection.
7. Aristotelian potentiality is determinate capacity, not sheer non-being.
8. Descartes’ dispositional reply is distinguished from crude occurrent innatism.
9. Logical positivist anti-metaphysics is primarily semantic, with epistemic and indirect ontological consequences.
10. Husserlian bracketing suspends rather than denies worldly existence; constitution is not arbitrary creation.
11. Sartrean consciousness is intentional non-coincidence, not a Cartesian substance.
12. Strawson’s primitive person blocks conceptual reconstruction from two substances but does not by itself prove metaphysical monism.
13. Russell’s existence, uniqueness and predication analysis includes negation-scope and Strawson’s presupposition objection.
14. Later Wittgenstein’s use theory retains representation as one language-game and is not mere conventional frequency.
15. Jaina conditionality is distinguished from unrestricted relativism and all seven predications are present.
16. Śaṅkara’s critique of Sāṃkhya targets relation, agency and teleology; the Sāṃkhya natural-relation reply is retained.
17. Advaita’s *tat tvam asi* reading uses mutual exclusion of incompatible adjuncts and does not deify the empirical ego.
18. Vaiśeṣika absolute absence retains a determinate counterpositive; it is not sheer nothing.
19. Aurobindo’s Supermind includes evolutionary ascent and supramental descent.
20. Rāmānuja’s critique includes qualityless cognition, the seven difficulties of ignorance, scripture, world, devotion and liberation.
21. Bhāṭṭa non-cognition is conditioned failed apprehension, not mere ignorance.
22. Nyāya ordinary and extraordinary perception, including universal-character perception, are distinguished.
23. All five marks of a valid reason and all five major fallacies are correlated without claiming a simplistic one-to-one mapping.
24. Advaita liberation is recognition of an always-existing identity, supported by the necklace and tenth-man illustrations.
25. Yoga’s five mind-field modifications are complete; cessation is not destruction or blank unconsciousness.
26. Buddhist soteriology includes both the practical Middle Path and the doctrinal middle, the Four Noble Truths, twelve links, no-self and Eightfold Path.
27. Prior absence supports Nyāya novelty without falsely implying creation from sheer nothing.
28. Nyāya qualified-particular semantics is distinguished from Mīmāṃsā’s greater priority to universals and sentence context.
