## High-risk facts specifically checked

- **Q1:** Environmental principles are paired with the correct cases; judicial supervision is not presented as a substitute for regulation.
- **Q4:** The Chairman has only a casting vote in an equality of votes and cannot preside during consideration of the Vice-President’s removal under Article 92.
- **Q5:** Article 338B(9) consultation is qualified by the Article 342A(3) State-list exception inserted through the 105th Amendment.
- **Q9:** Assistance is described as close to US$4 billion in the 2022 crisis period without converting every component into a grant.
- **Q10:** BIMSTEC has seven members and is treated as complementary to, not legally replacing, SAARC.
- **Q11:** Election petitions lie to the High Court; Section 100 grounds and Section 116A Supreme Court appeal are kept distinct.
- **Q12:** Article 213 is treated as a temporary legislative power; laying and six-week expiry are retained; serial re-promulgation is not legitimised.
- **Q14:** India’s weighted, indirect federal college is distinguished from France’s direct two-round majority election.
- **Q15:** MCC is correctly described as non-statutory; ECI action is not inaccurately converted into an unlimited penal power.
- **Q18:** RTE coverage is 6–14; the 25% obligation and minority-institution exception are matched to the controlling cases.
- **Q19:** I2U2 is not described as a military alliance or treaty organisation.
- **Q20:** The updated 2022 NDC uses 45% emissions-intensity reduction from 2005 and about 50% cumulative installed electric-power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2030; Mission LiFE is not backdated before the examination.
