## Scope and method

The two-page image-only paper was read directly from the repository-held archived official scan.
Its SHA-256 was recomputed and matched the source manifest. The topic page and instruction page
were visually checked, then the English transcription was independently compared with IAS Score.
All four topics were reviewed against `solved_pyqs/INSTRUCTIONS.md` and the completed 2014 Essay
standard.

The review tested source wording and order; the distinctive 2013 instruction to write one essay
within 2500 words for 250 marks; stable identities, anchors and slugs; interpretation and
misreadings; qualified thesis; multidimensional progression; named India-centric evidence;
counter-view and synthesis; model-essay completeness; examiner takeaway; and owned-link
integrity. In accordance with the assignment’s “No shared edits” boundary, no catalogue, topic
owner, status, coverage or shared review-log file was changed.

## Evidence and factual checks

- Q1 treats the stem’s “Gandhiji” attribution as official paper wording and does not claim a
  separately authenticated verbatim source. Champaran, the Salt March, Savitribai Phule’s
  educational work, Ambedkar’s Mahad satyagraha and the Constitution’s rights/duties structure
  are used within defensible historical roles.
- Q2 accurately dates Macaulay’s Minute to 1835, the Official Secrets Act to 1923 and the Right to
  Information Act to 2005. Article 51A(h), ISRO and the dairy cooperative movement are not
  presented as culturally sealed or purely indigenous creations; they illustrate adaptive
  institutional confidence.
- Q3 defines GDP without claiming that it measures welfare. It does not attribute a fabricated
  quotation or statistic to Simon Kuznets. Kerala and cooperative production are used as
  qualitative Indian illustrations, not as unsupported numerical comparisons. HDI is correctly
  described as combining income, health and education.
- Q4 accurately uses Article 51A(h) for scientific temper. The Green Revolution is qualified by
  institutional complements and ecological costs; ISRO’s civilian and strategic capabilities,
  affordable pharmaceutical capacity and Bhopal’s regulatory lesson are stated without
  unsupported numerical claims.

## Proportionality check

The 2013 paper awards the full 250 marks to one essay and permits up to 2500 words, unlike the 2014
two-section pattern of two essays worth 125 marks each. Each solution therefore retains the
2014 repository architecture but gives the model essay additional conceptual and evidentiary
space. All four remain comfortably below the official ceiling and are complete enough for
expansion under examination conditions without encouraging padding.

## Defects found and corrected

1. No canonical 2013 Essay solution or owned review existed; both were created.
2. The held scan, not a carried-forward web list, was made controlling and its manifest hash and
   exact archived-official source were recorded.
3. The paper’s unsectioned one-of-four pattern was preserved rather than incorrectly imposing the
   2014 two-section structure.
4. The space before Q2’s question mark, the capitalised “Colonial,” the paper’s exact “GDH (Gross
   Domestic Happiness),” and “Science and Technology is” wording were retained.
5. Q1 was protected from becoming individualistic self-help by adding collective organisation,
   rights and institutional reform.
6. Q2 was protected from monocausal colonial blame and cultural chauvinism by distinguishing
   origins from present functions and adding precolonial hierarchy and post-independence agency.
7. Q3 was revised away from a binary GDP-versus-happiness debate towards resources, subjective
   experience, capabilities, rights, distribution and ecological sustainability.
8. Q4 was prevented from becoming an invention list by making “panacea,” institutional
   complements, dual-use risk, inclusion and democratic governance the controlling argument.
9. Unsupported numerical claims and decorative quotations were excluded. Each counter-view
   materially changes the final verdict.
