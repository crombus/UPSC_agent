- **Q5:** Indian secularism is not inaccurately reduced to strict state–religion separation.
- **Q10:** The US funding stop after Palestine’s 2011 admission is distinguished from withdrawal effective in 2018.
- **Q11:** Section 8 conviction, Section 8A corrupt-practice disqualification, a stay of conviction, Section 11 relief and Section 116A appeal are kept distinct.
- **Q12:** Article 368(4) and (5), inserted by the 42nd Amendment, are correctly linked to *Minerva Mills*.
- **Q13:** The constitutional minimum of one-third is distinguished from States’ higher 50% reservations.
- **Q14:** The Attorney-General’s parliamentary participation under Article 88 carries no vote.

- **Q19:** India’s Global South role is described as transformed, not falsely declared abandoned.
- **Q20:** India–US convergence is not equated with alliance membership; strategic autonomy remains the analytical qualification.
