   lengths range from 188 to 200 words.
2. Sixteen initially proposed canonical-owner paths did not match repository filenames. Each was
   replaced with an existing owner, and all relative links were retested.
3. Q1 was checked to prevent later Delhi-government Supreme Court holdings from being projected
   into a 2016 answer.
4. Q2 was checked to prevent post-2016 alteration of Article 370 from contaminating the historical
   answer.
5. Q12 now distinguishes the question’s “integrity index” phrase from Transparency International’s
   Corruption Perceptions Index without changing official wording.
6. Q15 excludes the later 2017 National Plan of Action for Children and uses only institutions and
   law applicable by 2016.
7. Q20 preserves the source spelling “McBride” while accurately identifying Seán MacBride and the
   Commission in the answer.
8. Counts, anchors, slugs, links, word lengths, replacement characters and whitespace were rerun;
   `git diff --check` passed.
