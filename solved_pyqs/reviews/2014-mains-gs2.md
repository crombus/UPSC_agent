## Directive and answer-quality audit

- Every response covers each explicit limb and follows **claim → named evidence/example →
  analysis → qualification**.
- “Evaluate,” “critically evaluate/examine” and “analyse” answers test the proposition and end with
  graded verdicts rather than descriptive summaries.
- Q3, Q5, Q8, Q11, Q13, Q15, Q18, Q19 and Q20 visibly answer their reform, comparison, concurrency
  or institutional-design limbs.
- Q4 separately defines speech, treats hate speech and explains the distinct constitutional plane
  of films.
- Q8 and Q19 compare the institutions named in the stems rather than discussing them separately.
- Q9 treats NHRC as a complement to, not a substitute for, courts and other accountability bodies.
- Q20 distinguishes WTO legal obligations from direct domestic-law supremacy.

## Defects found and corrected during review

1. Initial Q11, Q17, Q18 and Q20 drafts exceeded the common 200-word limit; all were edited and
   mechanically recounted.
2. Seven initial canonical-owner links used plausible but non-existent filenames; each was replaced
   with the exact existing owner path and the complete link audit was rerun.
3. Q1 was checked not to import post-2014 NJAC holdings.
4. Q4 was checked not to rely on the 2015 *Shreya Singhal* judgment.
5. Q5 preserves the distinction between a reasonable-time framework and automatic acceptance or
   rejection after a rigid deadline.
6. Q8 identifies the 2014 Rail Tariff Authority as advisory rather than overstating it as a fully
   independent statutory regulator.
7. Q15 preserves Aadhaar’s legal status and Supreme Court safeguards as they stood in 2014 rather
   than importing the later Aadhaar Act or privacy judgment.
8. Q19 describes NDB and AIIB at their 2014 founding-design stage and does not rely on later lending
   records.
9. Q20 incorporates developments completed before the December 2014 examination and excludes later
   Nairobi or subsequent WTO outcomes.
10. Counts, anchors, headings, slugs, relative links, replacement characters and whitespace were
    rerun after corrections.
