## High-risk facts specifically checked

- **Q1:** Judicial independence is tied to the basic structure without claiming that the collegium is itself constitutional text; accountability is not presented as executive control.
- **Q2:** Section 12 beneficiary classes are retained, including custody, disability, trafficking/begar, industrial workmen, disaster/atrocity victims and income-qualified persons. Lok Adalats are not described as coercive adjudicators.
- **Q3:** Twelfth Schedule functions are not described as automatically devolved; Articles 243W and 243X correctly preserve a major State legislative role.
- **Q4:** UK parliamentary sovereignty is treated as legal doctrine subject to political constraints; India is correctly placed under constitutional supremacy and basic-structure review.
- **Q5:** Speaker powers, Money Bill certification and Tenth Schedule adjudication are distinguished. Judicial review is not converted into prior judicial control of proceedings.
- **Q7:** Dominance itself is not called unlawful; the answer targets abuse under Section 4 and does not claim that the named CCI orders finally settled every appellate issue.
- **Q9:** India is assigned a convening and agenda-shaping role, not an unsupported power to mediate or eliminate SCO conflicts.
- **Q11:** Article 21 expansions are paired with the relevant holdings; Article 21A is presented as a later textual guarantee rather than a judicially invented amendment.
- **Q12:** Protective provisions are qualified by agency and anti-stereotyping; each judgment is used narrowly for its gender-justice proposition.
- **Q13:** The decline in Article 356 use is not attributed to *Bommai* alone; coalition and regional-party politics explain the mid-1990s timing.
- **Q15:** GST Council recommendations are correctly described as persuasive rather than binding after *Mohit Minerals*; compensation is identified as statutory.
- **Q16:** PAC and CoPU are 22-member joint-House committees; the Estimates Committee is 30-member and Lok Sabha-only. Ministers are excluded from all three.
- **Q17:** Constitutionally permitted affirmative differentiation is distinguished from arbitrary discrimination; targeted welfare is not presumed valid regardless of design.
- **Q18:** Training supply, certification, employability and actual job creation remain distinct.
- **Q19:** NATO is not conflated with the Quad, and transatlantic cohesion is not treated as an automatic Indian benefit.
- **Q20:** IMO is accurately treated as a standard-setting and capacity-building body whose rules depend on State implementation; it is not presented as a naval enforcement agency.
