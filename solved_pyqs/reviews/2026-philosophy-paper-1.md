2. Distinguished Hegel’s pure Being at the opening of the *Logic* from the completed Absolute.
3. Distinguished Aristotle’s actuality sense of entelechy from Leibniz’s substance sense.
4. Treated Russell’s hierarchy as a formal-semantic solution, not Wittgenstein’s own proposal.
5. Distinguished Pātañjali’s YS 1.17 factors from the samāpatti varieties and asamprajñāta from nirbīja.
6. Distinguished Nyāya, Bhāṭṭa and Prābhākara theories of sentence meaning.
7. Distinguished Prābhākara non-apprehension from Nyāya misplacement in error theory.
8. Preserved all seven Jaina predications in canonical order and indexed affirmation/negation to different respects.
9. Clarified that Madhva’s dependent realities are distinct without being independently ultimate.
