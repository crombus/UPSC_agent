## 8. Defects found and corrected

1. The held PDF’s image-derived text layer corrupts words and Indic transliteration. Exact English
   sequence and wording were controlled against the page text and verified 2018 banks.
2. Initial English glosses inserted into several Indic question headings were removed so all
   headings match the verified official transcription; English-first glosses remain in metadata
   and answers.
3. Hume’s sunrise prediction was kept probabilistic rather than called logically necessary.
4. Leibnizian freedom was qualified as compatibilist and distinguished certainty from absolute
   necessity.
5. Q5(c) distinguishes Brahman’s apophatic transcendence from Māyā’s ontological indeterminacy.
6. Q6(a) does not conflate Nyāya’s creator/administrator with Yoga’s special consciousness.
7. Q6(b) preserves causal continuity without reintroducing a substance-self and includes the
   practical as well as doctrinal Middle Path.
8. Q7(c) answers the unusual printed wording without falsely attributing Advaita’s indefinable
   ignorance to Rāmānuja.
9. Q8(b) distinguishes the Cārvāka challenge to certain universal inference from a blanket denial
   of every practical expectation.
