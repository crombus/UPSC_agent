## Chronology and anachronism audit

- No post-2015 scheme, judgment, statute or operation is presented as contemporaneous evidence.
- The National Agriculture Market is described as proposed, not as the operational e-NAM platform
  launched in 2016.
- IRNSS is described with four satellites launched by December 2015; completion of the
  seven-satellite constellation in 2016 and the later NavIC name are not projected backwards.
- The AFSPA answer relies on the 1997 *Naga People’s Movement* ruling and does not import the
  Supreme Court’s later 2016 extra-judicial-executions ruling.
- The cyber answer retains the National Cyber Security Policy, 2013 architecture and does not
  import later defence cyber agencies, data-protection statutes or institutional arrangements.
- The solar answer uses the then-current National Solar Mission, competitive bids and Green Energy
  Corridor rather than later storage economics or 2030 commitments.
- Namami Gange is treated at its 2015 programme stage; later statutory authorities and project
  outcomes are excluded.
