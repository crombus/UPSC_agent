## 4. Accuracy safeguards

The review specifically confirmed that:

1. Corruption is not reduced either to private immorality or to monetary loss.
2. Fundamental Duties are not treated as superior to or substitutes for Fundamental Rights.
3. Equal respect concerns equal civic standing, not State endorsement of contradictory doctrines.
4. Democratic socialism is distinguished from authoritarian State ownership.
5. Caste is treated as Ambedkarite graded inequality while democratic mobilisation retains an
   emancipatory possibility.
6. Democracy is not equated with elections or unqualified majority rule.
7. Minority rights remain limited by individual voice, equality and exit.
8. Economic independence is treated as necessary but insufficient for gender equality.
9. Women are not essentialised as possessing one uniform political interest.
10. Aristotle’s formal distinctions are separated from his exclusionary account of citizenship.
11. Rawls’s first principle has priority; fair equality of opportunity precedes the difference
    principle within the second.
12. Sen’s *nyāya* is realised justice, not merely another ideal institutional blueprint.
13. Buddhism and Jainism establish religion without a creator-God without being collapsed into one
    metaphysics.
14. Buddhist liberation is not attributed to divine grace; the Noble Eightfold Path
    (*āryāṣṭāṅgamārga*) remains a human discipline.
15. Natural evil is distinguished from moral evil and the counterpart thesis is assessed for
    necessity, degree and distribution.
16. Faith beyond demonstration is distinguished from belief against decisive evidence.
17. Nyāya’s world-as-effect inference (*kāryāt*) is distinguished from Aquinas’s and Leibniz’s
    Western formulations.
18. The cosmological argument does not begin from the crude premise that everything has a cause.
19. Darwin directly challenges Paley’s biological examples without by itself settling cosmic
    fine-tuning.
20. Mystical phenomenology is separated from interpretation and metaphysical truth.
21. Advaita self–*Brahman* identity, Buddhist no-self (*anātman*) and Jain enduring soul are not
    falsely harmonised.
22. Personal prima-facie warrant is distinguished from universally compelling public proof.
23. Non-cognitivism is not misrepresented as the claim that religious discourse is meaningless.
24. Hick’s eschatological verification is recognised as logically possible but asymmetrical.
25. Cognitivist replies succeed only if religious claims retain content and evidential risk.

English concepts lead throughout. Relevant accurate IAST includes *telos*,
*sarva-dharma-samabhāva*, *pratītyasamutpāda*, *nirvāṇa*, *bhakti*, *prapatti*,
*āryāṣṭāṅgamārga*, *epieikeia*,
*nīti*, *nyāya*, *kāryāt*, *Nyāyakusumāñjali*, *Brahman*, *anitya*, *anātman* and *śabda*.
