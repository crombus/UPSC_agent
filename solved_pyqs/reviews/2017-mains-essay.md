## Evidence, balance and originality review

- Q1 uses dairy cooperation, rain-fed vulnerability, tenants, women farmers and rural non-farm
  work to explain mechanisms rather than decorate a predetermined answer.
- Q2 correctly identifies Article 279A and treats GST as pooled sovereignty; it also tests usable
  fiscal space through cesses, schemes, borrowing and local-government finance.
- Q3 uses the classroom as a “miniature republic” while retaining teacher conditions, nutrition,
  assessment and labour-market opportunity as necessary qualifications.
- Q4 preserves NAM’s anti-colonial and autonomy core without confusing present-day Indian
  multi-alignment with a revival of Cold War equidistance.
- Q5 refuses compulsory cheerfulness and makes shared joy ethically credible only when it protects
  grief, rights, inclusion and reciprocal responsibility.
- Q6 avoids both triumphalism and victim-only narration. Its original synthesis replaces the one
  marketable “new woman” with plural lives made equally free.
- Q7 uses civil disobedience and cyclone preparedness to distinguish resistible institutions from
  unavoidable causality while showing that disaster losses are not simply “natural.”
- Q8 moves beyond a standard pros-and-cons answer by locating selfishness in the interaction of
  profile metrics, personalisation, attention economics and human needs.

## Defects found and corrected

1. The 2017 paper and owned review files were absent; both were created.
2. The source is an image-only two-page scan. The English topics were transcribed visually from
   page 2 rather than inferred from secondary lists.
3. Section B restarts printed numbering at 1–4. Stable repository identities correctly continue
   as Q5–Q8 while metadata preserves each printed question number.
4. Source punctuation was preserved, including the space before the question mark in the NAM
   topic and curly single quotation marks around ‘new woman’ and ‘Social media’.
5. Early answer plans risked becoming sectoral lists in Q1, Q2 and Q8. They were revised around
   causal mechanisms and controlling ideas: risk/value distribution, pooled fiscal sovereignty,
   and affordance versus incentive.
6. Potentially brittle numerical claims were removed. Named constitutional and institutional
   evidence was retained only where it performs analytical work.
7. Counter-views were strengthened so that each changes the final synthesis rather than serving as
   a token “however” paragraph.
