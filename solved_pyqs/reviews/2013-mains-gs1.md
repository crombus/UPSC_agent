## Defects found and corrected

1. Secondary web transcriptions incorrectly generalized the paper to equal marks/common limits;
   direct scan review restored the mixed 10-mark/200-word and 5-mark/100-word pattern.
2. Split questions were assigned independent stable IDs so every separately marked answer remains
   directly linkable without altering the official numbering.
3. Capitalization, punctuation and unusual printed words were restored from the scan.
4. Draft answers exceeding printed limits were tightened without removing controlling claims,
   named evidence, analysis or qualifications.
5. Q2(a) was revised to avoid inventing an inscription that supposedly contains a full Tandava
   choreography; epigraphic institutional evidence is separated from textual and sculptural form.
6. Q5 was framed as colonial state-building, not a celebratory modernization claim.
7. Q6 avoids unreliable precision about pledged/distributed acreage and assesses legal transfer.
8. Q7 excludes post-2013 additions to the slogan.
9. Q10 distinguishes humanitarian legitimacy, security compulsion and the immediate war trigger.
10. Q12 and Q18 reject cartographic and regionalist determinism respectively.
11. Q19(b) separates worldwide basin-specific systems from the north Indian Ocean process.
12. Q22(b) and Q25 explicitly qualify absolute/uncertain premises in the official stems.
13. Q24 distinguishes geological abundance from technologically usable nuclear fuel.
14. All structures, anchors, slugs, word counts and relative links were mechanically rechecked after correction.
