## High-risk facts specifically checked

- **Q1:** Constitutional morality is anchored in constitutional text and values, not free-standing judicial morality; the Sabarimala reference is attributed to the 2018 plurality.
- **Q2:** Articles 124 and 217 are used only as the appointment setting; diversity is not described as a textual quota or a substitute for competence.
- **Q3:** The Fourteenth Finance Commission period is 2015–20 and the divisible-pool share rose from 32% to 42%; cesses and surcharges are correctly identified as outside the divisible pool.
- **Q4:** Collective responsibility is correctly tied to Article 75(3); committees are not presented as possessing binding executive power.
- **Q6:** Article 47 is a Directive Principle, while the health–Article 21 connection is described as jurisprudential.
- **Q7:** NAPS, NSQF and NEP 2020 are treated as complementary frameworks; apprenticeship is not equated with unprotected casual labour.
- **Q8:** The SHG–Bank Linkage Programme is dated to NABARD’s 1992 pilot phase; microfinance is qualified by markets, public services and over-indebtedness risks.
- **Q11:** Sections 5 and 6 of the DSPE Act are kept distinct; constitutional courts’ power to order a CBI probe without State consent follows the 2010 Constitution Bench decision and is described as exceptional.
- **Q12:** NHRC recommendations remain recommendatory; the one-year bar, armed-forces procedure and civil-court inquiry powers are assigned to the correct PHRA provisions.
- **Q13:** US equal protection and Indian equality provisions are not treated as identical; India’s express affirmative-action clauses and anti-arbitrariness doctrine are retained.
- **Q14:** Article 169 uses an Assembly resolution with total-membership majority plus two-thirds present and voting; Parliament’s resulting law is not an Article 368 amendment. The six-Council list is correct for the 2021 examination context.
- **Q15:** There were 24 DRSCs; the Health Committee’s 123rd Report was presented in 2020 and addressed the COVID-19 outbreak and management.
- **Q19:** The SCO is not described as a military alliance; India joined as a full member in 2017 and RATS is located in Tashkent.
- **Q20:** AUKUS submarines are nuclear-powered, not nuclear-armed; AUKUS is distinguished from the Quad, Five Eyes, ANZUS and ASEAN-led forums.
