## Attribution and precision audit

1. Plato's separate Form and Aristotle's immanent substantial form are contrasted without making Aristotle a simple anti-Platonist.
2. Kant's response to Hume is restricted to conditions of possible phenomenal experience.
3. Moore's common-sense method is presented as comparative certainty, not an uncontested neutral proof.
4. Wittgenstein's private-language argument targets private rule-governed meaning, not private sensation.
5. Kierkegaard's subjectivity is not equated with factual relativism.
6. Berkeley's critique of primary qualities is distinguished from Hegel's dialectical absolute idealism.
7. Spinoza's “in God” is treated as immanent ontological dependence, not spatial inclusion.
8. Kant's predicate objection is distinguished from stronger modal versions of the ontological argument.
9. Russell's incomplete symbols motivate but do not deductively prove logical atomism.
10. Logical positivist tautology and empirical verification are distinguished, with Quine's later objection identified as such.
11. Descartes, Spinoza and Leibniz are assessed under explicit libertarian and compatibilist criteria.
12. Sartrean existence-before-essence is qualified through Kierkegaard, Heidegger and situated facticity.
13. Husserlian constitution is not represented as arbitrary mental creation; anti-psychologism and intersubjectivity are retained.
14. Quine's holism is constrained by pragmatic theory-choice and does not become “anything goes.”
15. Cārvāka is described as broadly proto-positivistic only with an explicit anti-anachronism qualification.
16. Gautama's six canonical signs of self are complete and are not replaced by supplementary memory arguments.
17. “Air lacks heat” is *atyantābhāva* of intrinsic heat in air; “air is not fire” is *anyonyābhāva*.
18. Bhāṭṭa *abhihitānvaya* and Prābhākara *anvitābhidhāna* are explained causally, not merely translated.
19. Rāmānuja's body–soul analogy preserves real plurality, dependence and divine transcendence.
20. Cārvāka embodied material personhood is distinguished from Buddhist aggregate-process no-self.
21. The “void/real” contrast is correctly routed to Madhyamaka and realist Buddhism, especially Vaibhāṣika/Sarvāstivāda, not Yogācāra.
22. Jaina psychic bondage and material karmic bondage are linked without conflation.
23. Sāṃkhya's *mahat* and *buddhi* are correctly treated as one principle under cosmic and functional descriptions.
24. Yoga bondage is misidentification in *citta*; *puruṣa* itself is not literally modified.
25. Aurobindo's ascent is paired with supramental descent and transformation, not mere world-escape.
26. Nyāya *anuvyavasāya*, Bhāṭṭa *jñātata* and Prābhākara *tripuṭī-saṃvit* remain distinct; luminosity is not confused with intrinsic validity.
27. Bhāṭṭa postulation and Nyāya inferential reduction are both tested against alternative explanations.
28. Memory is denied independent *pramāṇa* status without being called necessarily false or practically useless.
