## High-risk facts specifically checked

- **Q1:** The Ram Nath Kovind High-Level Committee reported in March 2024 and proposed simultaneous Lok Sabha–Assembly elections followed by local elections within 100 days; the answer does not import the later December 2024 constitutional Bill.
- **Q2:** Ordinary Lok Adalats settle but do not adjudicate failed compromises; only compoundable criminal matters enter their jurisdiction. Arbitration remains civil/commercial and party-consensual.
- **Q4:** Articles 148–151, legality, propriety and performance audit are kept distinct; the CAG is not presented as a policy-making or enforcement authority.
- **Q9:** iCET (2023) and the IPEF Supply Chain Agreement’s 2024 entry into force are used as de-risking evidence, not as proof of alliance or complete decoupling.
- **Q10:** The first India–Central Asia Summit (2022) and India’s ten-year Chabahar port contract (May 2024) are dated without treating proposed corridors as completed connectivity.
- **Q11:** The central Public Examinations Act commenced on 21 June 2024; coverage is tied to scheduled or centrally notified authorities. University and State Board examinations are not described as automatically covered.
- **Q12:** Privacy follows *K.S. Puttaswamy* (2017); the legitimacy rule is stated under Section 116 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023, corresponding to former Evidence Act Section 112. *Goutam Kundu*, *Bhabani Prasad Jena* and *Aparna Ajinkya Firodia* support exceptional, non-routine DNA testing.
- **Q13:** GST Council recommendations are described as persuasive rather than binding following *Mohit Minerals* (2022); Governor reforms are attributed to the Sarkaria and Punchhi Commissions.
- **Q14:** PIL standing, Article 142 and the basic-structure power are distinguished; “world’s most powerful” is treated as an evaluative claim, not a formal ranking.
- **Q15:** Indian principled-distance secularism is compared with both US non-establishment and free exercise; *Kennedy v. Bremerton* (2022) prevents reliance on the superseded *Lemon* test.
- **Q18:** The Interactive Service Model is correctly treated as two-way transaction plus tracking, grievance and appeal—not merely digitisation.
- **Q19:** CTC (Resolution 1373), CTED and the separate 1267/1989/2253 sanctions architecture are not conflated; the Delhi Declaration is dated 29 October 2022.
- **Q20:** Maldives’ location, Eight Degree Channel, Operation Cactus (1988), the 2014 water crisis and Colombo Security Conclave are used without reducing Maldivian sovereignty to great-power rivalry.
