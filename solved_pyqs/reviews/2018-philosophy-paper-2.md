  Absolute (*nirguṇa Brahman*);

The review specifically confirmed that Buddhism is not assigned an enduring self or a creator,
Jainism and Buddhism are not collapsed into one theory of rebirth, Hinduism is not assigned one
exclusive God-model, and Kautilya's coercive authority is not misrepresented as unrestricted
Austin-style sovereignty.

## 6. Defects found and corrected

The review corrected or guarded against:

1. broken OCR spacing, quotation marks and the extracted Q2(c) mark by reconciling the official
   PDF with both verified banks;
2. the risk of treating Q5(d)'s printed **“immorality”** as a typo—its exact wording is retained and
   answered directly;
3. the risk of presenting non-theistic rebirth as one doctrine by separating Jain enduring soul
   (*jīva*) from Buddhist causal continuity without self (*anātman*);
4. the risk of treating Hinduism as simply polytheistic by distinguishing lived plurality,
   henotheistic devotion, theism and non-dual metaphysics;
5. serial comparison in Q8(a) by using shared axes of method, causation, ontology, inferred divine
   attributes and objections;
6. reductive accounts of multiculturalism, feminism and human rights by adding internal-minority,
   power and cultural-relativism qualifications.
