## Evidence and depth review

- Q1 uses constitutional morality, Ambedkar and Gandhi only for bounded analytical functions and
  avoids claiming that suffering automatically produces moral growth.
- Q2 distinguishes mindfulness from treatment and structural reform, and uses the Bhagavad Gita
  as an interpretive example without inventing a quotation.
- Q3 qualifies the ship metaphor through overwhelming hazards, unequal resources, collective
  rescue and prevention.
- Q4 tests simplicity in science, design and administration while retaining exceptions, appeal
  and transparent assumptions.
- Q5 treats Indian culture as plural and reformable and evaluates civilizational capability
  through dignity rather than technological display.
- Q6 avoids unsupported statistics and explains the two-way relationship between capability,
  productivity, trust, distribution and ecological responsibility.
- Q7 treats patriarchy as a patterned structure, not a claim about every individual, and
  integrates caste, class, disability, care work, agency and men's constrained roles.
- Q8 treats technology as a multiplier and organiser of power while preserving the independent
  importance of geography, strategy, institutions, alliances and legitimacy.
