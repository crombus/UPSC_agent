## Defects found and corrected during review

1. Initial drafts of Q5, Q12, Q13, Q15 and Q18 exceeded the common 200-word limit; all were edited
   and mechanically recounted.
2. Q4 was checked to exclude the Supreme Court’s 2018 *Shakti Vahini* framework and retain only
   authorities available by 2015.
3. Q5 was checked to exclude the 2017 seven-judge ordinance ruling in *Krishna Kumar Singh*.
4. Q7 was checked to exclude post-2015 firecracker orders while retaining the 2005 noise judgment.
5. Q8 now preserves the paper’s FCRA 1976 wording but accurately explains that the governing law in
   2015 was the FCRA 2010 and Rules 2011 as amended.
6. Q14 avoids anachronistically claiming that NFRA was operational; it describes the Companies Act
   provision.
7. Q15 distinguishes the bill’s parliamentary history from the enacted statute and avoids treating
   the proposed exemptions as enacted law.
8. Q19 separates official cultural-heritage objectives from analytical strategic externalities.
9. Counts, anchors, headings, slugs, relative links, replacement characters and whitespace were
   rerun; `git diff --check` passed.
