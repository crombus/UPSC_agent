continuous model essay contains approximately 750–828 words before the separate interpretation,

## Evidence and factual checks

- Q1 uses Fundamental Rights, constitutional review, the CAG and Election Commission within their
  accountability roles. Bhopal is used as an industrial-responsibility lesson, not for an
  unsupported numerical claim. *K.S. Puttaswamy* is accurately used for privacy.
- Q2 makes no unsupported claim about examination volumes or mental-health prevalence. Reservation,
  scholarships and public education are framed as equalising conditions, not guarantees of equal
  outcome.
- Q3 does not claim that a standardized test measures innate potential. It accurately distinguishes
  reliability, validity and longitudinal progress; post-2014 reforms are clearly used as later
  analytical evidence rather than source-paper context.
- Q4 accurately uses *satyagraha*, the Preamble and *Shreya Singhal*; no quotation is falsely
  attributed. Section 66A is described as invalidated for unconstitutional breadth, not as proof
  that all online speech is immune.
- Q5 does not reduce the historical slowdown to governance alone. The GST Council is identified as
  a later illustration of structured federal negotiation, not an institution operating in 2014.
- Q6 treats Tehelka and cash-for-query as examples of covert exposure without claiming that a media
  recording itself proves criminal guilt. *K.S. Puttaswamy* is explicitly identified as a later
  constitutional development.
- Q7 names Karnam Malleswari, Mary Kom, Saina Nehwal and P. V. Sindhu only as Indian women who
  widened sporting aspiration; it does not falsely describe all of them as Olympic gold medallists.
- Q8 avoids unsupported arrival, revenue or employment statistics. ASI, local bodies and carrying
  capacity are used within defensible institutional and ecological roles.

## Defects found and corrected

1. No canonical 2014 paper or owned review existed; both were created.
2. Existing subject-wide Essay material marked the prompts as carried-forward and requiring
   re-check. The final transcription was instead made directly from a downloaded historical scan,
   whose checksum is recorded above.
3. Secondary web transcriptions commonly normalise Q6 to “invasion of privacy.” The scan’s exact
   “invasion on privacy” wording was restored.
4. The scan’s spaces before question marks in A3 and B1–B4 were preserved rather than silently
   modernised.
5. Section B restarts printed numbering at 1–4; stable IDs correctly continue Q5–Q8 while metadata
   preserves the printed number.
6. Q5 was protected from partisan hindsight by adding external macroeconomic conditions, democratic
   review and a policy–implementation feedback loop.
7. Q6 was revised around a clear necessity, proportionality, verification and minimisation test so
   that neither privacy nor watchdog journalism became absolute.
8. Q7 and Q8 were prevented from becoming scheme or asset lists by making institutions, rights,
   local capability and sustainability the controlling arguments.
9. Unsupported numerical claims and decorative quotations were excluded. Every counter-view was
   made strong enough to alter the final synthesis.
