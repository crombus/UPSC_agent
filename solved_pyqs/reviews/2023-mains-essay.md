## Scope and method

All eight English prompts were reviewed separately against the Essay standard in
`solved_pyqs/INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Checks covered:

- exact wording, punctuation, section and printed order;
- stable explicit anchor, metadata, primary route, tags and proposed slug;
- interpretation, operative tension and likely misreadings;
- a qualified and contestable thesis;
- multidimensional progression with explicit transitions;
- a fair counter-view that changes the synthesis;
- substantial continuous model treatment rather than a GS listicle;
- analytical examples through claim → evidence/example → analysis → qualification;
- a direct, earned conclusion;
- absence of invented quotation, author attribution, unsupported statistics and false precision.

## Evidence review

- Constitutional references are confined to well-established provisions: the equality and
  Directive Principles framework at a general level and Article 51A(h).
- Statutory and institutional illustrations include MGNREGA, social audits, the Election
  Commission and ISRO’s Chandrayaan-2/Chandrayaan-3 learning sequence.
- Historical illustrations such as universal adult franchise, the Green Revolution, social
  reform and India’s freedom struggle are used for defined analytical functions and qualified.
- No numerical claim, author attribution or purported official model answer is asserted.
- Examples do not substitute for argument; each is linked to a claim and its limit.

## Defects found and corrected

1. Absolute readings were bounded: opposition versus hostility, intuition versus impulse,
   wandering versus drift, attention versus romanticised scarcity, gender symmetry versus
   structural asymmetry, quantification versus judgment, charity versus justice, and knowledge
   versus content-free skills.
2. The em dash in Question 5 and all official wording were preserved from the verified source and
   corpus ledger.
3. Planning headings remain outside the model essays; the essays themselves use continuous linked
   paragraphs rather than GS-style dimension lists.
4. Each counter-view changes the final synthesis instead of appearing as a token concession.
5. Each conclusion returns directly to the prompt in its refined form.
