## Defects found and corrected

1. The source OCR represented `12½` inconsistently; the printed common specification was restored
   uniformly from the paper instruction and marks line.
2. Q2’s official phrase “rock cut architecture” was preserved verbatim, while the answer correctly
   distinguishes Mesolithic rock art from later rock-cut architecture.
3. Q1 was revised to avoid claiming direct, unchanged descent from every Harappan motif and to
   avoid denying continuity in the comparison civilizations.
4. Q3 was framed as a constrained counterfactual rather than attributing independence solely to
   Gandhi or removing his distinctive causal role.
5. Q7 differentiates Germany’s responsibility across the two wars instead of extending the
   Versailles sole-guilt formula to both.
6. Q10 was checked for category essentialism: aggregate ST advantage is explained without treating
   all tribal communities as matrilineal or gender-equal.
7. Q11, Q15, Q17 and Q20 were audited for temporal leakage; no post-2015 scheme, territorial
   reorganization, disaster or policy outcome was used.
8. Initial drafts above the printed limit were tightened without deleting controlling evidence,
   analysis or qualification.
9. All exact headings, anchors, slugs, required fields, answer counts and relative links were
   mechanically rechecked after correction.
