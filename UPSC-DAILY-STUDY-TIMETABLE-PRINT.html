7. The Five Non-Negotiable Command Rules
1. The 10:00 AM Mains Answer Rule: Never postpone answer writing until "syllabus is complete". Write 1 answer daily between 10:00 and 10:30 AM following examiner structure: Claim → Named Evidence/Doctrinal Grounding → Analysis → Qualified Conclusion.
2. English-First Terminology: Philosophy requires the English concept first, followed immediately by the bracketed Sanskrit/Pali gloss: e.g., dependent origination (pratītyasamutpāda).
3. Backward Engineering via PYQs: Before starting a new topic, inspect its 2013–2025 questions in solved_pyqs/. Let examiner prompts define your depth boundary.
4. Zero AI Waste on MCQ Drills: Solve questions in final-mcq/ locally on paper or markdown. Save AI tokens exclusively for answer evaluations and conceptual synthesis.
5. Evening Retrieval Lock: No new theoretical reading after 18:30. Evenings are strictly reserved for testing, CSAT, and retrieval to prevent passive mental fatigue.
