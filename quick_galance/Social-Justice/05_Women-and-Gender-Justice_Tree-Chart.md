                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Gender justice redistributes safety, time, assets, opportunity, representation
and decision-making power. Equality supplies equal status; equity addresses
unequal starting conditions; empowerment expands agency and voice. Protective
laws against violence and harassment are necessary but insufficient without
care infrastructure, economic access, health, education, political
representation, gender-responsive budgeting and institutions capable of
delivering remedies across caste, class, tribe, disability and location.
