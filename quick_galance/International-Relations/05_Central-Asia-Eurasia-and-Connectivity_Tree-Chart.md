                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Central Asia is India's extended neighbourhood and a bridge across continental
Eurasia, but geography creates a fundamental access problem because India lacks
a direct land boundary with the region. Policy therefore combines political
engagement, security cooperation, energy and development partnership with
multi-modal corridors such as Chabahar and INSTC. Strategic intent must be
judged by operational links, customs efficiency, commercial viability and
regional consent rather than by maps, memberships or summit declarations alone.
