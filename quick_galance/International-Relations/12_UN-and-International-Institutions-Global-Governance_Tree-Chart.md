                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
International institutions remain indispensable because they supply rules,
legitimacy, information, coordination and forums for collective action, but
their effectiveness depends on representative governance, adequate finance,
member-state compliance and enforceable mandates. India's approach is reformist,
not rejectionist: a "renewed, not retired" United Nations, stronger voice for
developing countries, responsible participation and precise distinction between
political declarations, legal obligations and actual implementation.
