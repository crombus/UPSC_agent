                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The Global South is a political-development category, not a treaty organisation
or homogeneous bloc. Its strongest common thread is a representation and
development deficit in global governance, while its members differ on income,
security, climate, energy and great-power alignments. India can convene, frame
norms and deliver development partnerships, but it speaks within the Global
South rather than possessing an automatic mandate to speak for it.
