                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
India's West Asia policy is a non-exclusive portfolio built around energy
security, diaspora welfare, trade, technology and connectivity. It must manage
three parallel relationships: the Gulf and Arab world, Israel, and Iran.
Supplier diversification reduces concentration risk but does not itself reduce
aggregate import dependence. Corridor diplomacy, strategic declarations and
minilateral forums must be separated from financed construction and operational
services.
