# International Relations 11 — Globalisation, Trade Agreements and External-Policy Effects

> **Status cut-off:** 26 September 2026
> **Core thesis:** Globalisation has not ended; interdependence is being reorganised around
> security, technology, climate and domestic legitimacy.
