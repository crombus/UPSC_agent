                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Globalisation and sovereign nationalism are co-present forces rather than
successive eras. Cross-border trade, finance, technology and data continue, but
states increasingly use tariffs, subsidies, sanctions, export controls,
investment screening and climate measures to protect strategic interests.
India's task is to remain open enough to gain markets and technology while
building resilience and shaping external rules that transmit foreign policy
choices into domestic costs and opportunities.
