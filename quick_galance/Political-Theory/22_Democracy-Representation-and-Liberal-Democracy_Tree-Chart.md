                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Democracy means that ultimate authority belongs to the people, but modern scale makes
continuous direct rule impracticable. Representation converts popular sovereignty into
government through elections, parties and legislatures. Liberal democracy adds
constitutional restraint, civil liberties, opposition, accountability and minority
rights so that majority rule does not become majority domination. Electoral and
representation systems distribute voice differently, while democracy as a way of life
requires participation, communication and social conditions extending beyond polling.
