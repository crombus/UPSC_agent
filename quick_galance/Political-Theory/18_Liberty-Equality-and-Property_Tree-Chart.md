                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Liberty is not licence but freedom from unreasonable restraint, freedom through
enabling conditions and, in republican theory, freedom from arbitrary domination.
Equality means equal moral worth rather than sameness and requires examination of both
formal rules and substantive opportunities. Property can protect independence and
personhood, yet concentrated ownership can become power over others' labour and
life-chances. A just order therefore interprets liberty as equal liberty, equality as
compatible with relevant difference, and property as a socially bounded institution.
