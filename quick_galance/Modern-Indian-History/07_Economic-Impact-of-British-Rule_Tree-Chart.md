                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Colonial rule did not merely remove wealth from India; it reorganised
land, trade, finance, production, infrastructure and law around British
imperial needs. The result was modernisation of circulation without
autonomous development: India supplied revenue, raw materials and
markets while handicrafts, cultivators and food security absorbed
unequal costs. Effects varied by region, sector, class and period.
