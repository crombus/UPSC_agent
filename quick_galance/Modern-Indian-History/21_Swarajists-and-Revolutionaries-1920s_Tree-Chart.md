                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The 1920s were not an empty interval between Non-Cooperation and Civil
Disobedience. The withdrawal of 1922 produced a tactical debate inside
Congress, while constructive workers sustained rural organisation,
Swarajists exposed dyarchy from within legislatures, and revolutionary
groups moved from HRA republicanism toward HSRA socialism. Together these
streams widened nationalist method, ideology and social reach.
