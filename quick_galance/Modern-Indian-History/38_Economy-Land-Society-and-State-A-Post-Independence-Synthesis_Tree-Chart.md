                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Independent India transformed its economy and democratic politics more deeply
than its social hierarchies. Planning created an industrial base, food policy
secured self-sufficiency and democracy endured; controls later produced
inefficiency and 1991 altered the instruments. Land reform succeeded where it
attacked weak intermediaries and failed where it confronted the ruling
coalition's rural base. Legal equality advanced faster than caste, gender and
communal transformation, while institutional decay coexisted with democratic
deepening.
