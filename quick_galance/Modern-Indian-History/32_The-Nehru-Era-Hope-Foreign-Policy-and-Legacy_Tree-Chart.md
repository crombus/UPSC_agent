                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Nehru's central achievement was the creation of a durable framework rather
than the fulfilment of every promise: universal-franchise democracy, secular
citizenship, planning, a mixed economy, scientific capability and active
non-alignment were institutionalised together. These choices built state
capacity and strategic autonomy but underweighted mass literacy, agriculture,
employment, land reform, party renewal and defence preparedness. The 1962 war
exposed real misjudgement without erasing the framework that survived Nehru.
