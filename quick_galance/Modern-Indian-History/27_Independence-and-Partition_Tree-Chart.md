                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Independence and Partition were simultaneous outcomes of a long anti-colonial
struggle, decades of communalised representation and a short sequence of
contingent constitutional failures. The Cabinet Mission left unity available
in 1946; incompatible sovereign claims, organised violence and an accelerated
British timetable closed it. The result combined political liberation, legal
state creation and a humanitarian catastrophe whose regional and human
complexity cannot be reduced to one villain, one date or one instrument.
