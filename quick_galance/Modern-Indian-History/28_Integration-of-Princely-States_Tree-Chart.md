                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Independence did not automatically create territorial unity because British
paramountcy lapsed rather than passing to either dominion. India therefore had
to construct sovereignty over more than 560 princely units through a sequence
of accession, merger, democratisation and constitutional incorporation. Patel,
V.P. Menon, the States Department, popular movements and carefully graduated
leverage made rapid accession possible; Junagadh, Hyderabad and Kashmir show
why law, demography, geography and security required different methods.
