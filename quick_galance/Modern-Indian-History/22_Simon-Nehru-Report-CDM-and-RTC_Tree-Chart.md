                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The all-British Simon Commission converted constitutional exclusion into
mass protest. The Indian response produced the Nehru Report but failed to
settle minority safeguards or secure Dominion Status, pushing Congress to
Purna Swaraj. Salt then translated complete independence into repeatable
civil disobedience. Negotiations proved Congress indispensable but did not
transfer power; the Communal Award and Poona Pact exposed the unresolved
conflict between national integration and independent political voice.

The phase began with Britain's refusal to include Indians in constitutional
