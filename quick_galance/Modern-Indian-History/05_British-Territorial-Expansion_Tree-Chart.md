                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The Company built paramountcy through a cumulative but contingent
fiscal-military system. Bengal revenue, Indian sepoys, professional
command, artillery, credit, naval reinforcement and diplomacy allowed
it to isolate and defeat capable Indian states serially. Expansion moved
through buffer policy, subsidiary dependency, paramountcy and multiple
forms of annexation; it was neither a single pre-planned conquest nor
proof of racial, technological or political incapacity among Indians.
