                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The period moved India through three simultaneous transitions: from a record
single-party majority to coalition government, from state-led planning toward
market reform, and from Congress-centred social aggregation toward caste,
religious and regional mobilisation. Rajiv Gandhi's technological and federal
initiatives endured, but identity-policy misjudgements and Bofors destroyed his
credibility. Mandal, Mandir and Market then restructured political competition,
making coalition governance the normal form by the end of the century.
