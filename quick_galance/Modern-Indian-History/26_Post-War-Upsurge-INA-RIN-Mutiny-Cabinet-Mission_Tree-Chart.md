                              v
                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The post-war endgame joined three crises: the INA trials turned military defeat
into nationalist political victory; naval and urban unrest raised doubt about
the reliability of the Raj's coercive instruments; and elections produced two
powerful but incompatible representative mandates. The Cabinet Mission offered
the last serious constitutional route to one India, but compulsory grouping,
separate claims to sovereignty and an unworkable Interim Government destroyed
the compromise.
