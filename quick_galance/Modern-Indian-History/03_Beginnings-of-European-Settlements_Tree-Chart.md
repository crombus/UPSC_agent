# The Beginnings of European Settlements — Quick Glance

## Central thesis

European entry moved through non-identical stages; no voyage, charter or factory made conquest inevitable.

```text
SEA CONTACT -> NEGOTIATED TRADE -> FACTORY -> FORTIFIED SETTLEMENT
-> PRESIDENCY / ARMED DIPLOMACY -> POSSIBLE COMPANY-STATE

Every arrow required Indian permission, finance, force, allies and contingency.
```

## Institutional vocabulary

- Charter: European sovereign creates monopoly corporation.
- Farman: Indian sovereign grants privilege.
- Factory: warehouse-office-residence, not manufacturing plant.
- Fort: defended enclave, not automatic hinterland rule.
- Presidency: regional council coordinating factories and defence.
- Company-state: enforceable war, justice, taxation and territorial authority.

## High-yield evidence

- Cartaz: coerced Portuguese shipping-pass regime.
- Child's War: Mughal victory shows early EIC weakness.
- Colachel 1741: Travancore defeats the Dutch.
- Amboyna 1623: Southeast Asian event redirects English priorities.
- Broach: early Surat-centred EIC factory network.
- Xavier: founding Jesuit companion; died off China, remains in Goa.
- Papaya, pineapple and guava: officially keyed 2025 Portuguese-transfer demand.
- Charnock: conventional Calcutta association, not uncontested sole founder.

## English-advantage answer

Corporate finance + naval support + diversified bases + Indian credit/alliances
+ organisational continuity + later Bengal revenue.

Qualification: Bengal revenue is a later multiplier and cannot explain the seventeenth-century footholds.

## PYQ traps

- Wandiwash was Anglo-French, not Portuguese.
- Aix-la-Chapelle ended the First Carnatic War; Paris ended the third phase.
- 1717 farman granted commercial privileges, not Diwani.
- Bombay came through Portuguese-Crown-Company transfer; Charnock is linked to Calcutta.
- Treaty of Paris curtailed French political ambition but did not expel all French commerce.

## Mains spine

Define the stage -> compare institutions -> name ports/documents/events
-> insert Indian agency -> separate early causes from later multipliers
-> reject inevitability -> give a graded verdict.
