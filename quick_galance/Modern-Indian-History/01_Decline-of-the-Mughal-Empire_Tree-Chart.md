                    QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Between 1707 and the 1740s Mughal authority contracted because the
fiscal-military institutions that had tied rank, revenue and service to
the emperor ceased to produce reliable income, disciplined nobles or
coordinated defence. Succession and Nadir Shah accelerated this failure,
while provincial regimes captured Mughal offices and symbols to build
new states. The period was therefore both imperial decline and regional
transformation, creating an opportunity, but not a predetermined victory,
for the English East India Company.
