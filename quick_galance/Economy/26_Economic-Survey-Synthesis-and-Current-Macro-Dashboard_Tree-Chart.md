                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A macroeconomic dashboard is a disciplined synthesis, not a list of headline
numbers. Every value must carry its concept, unit, period, estimate vintage,
price basis, denominator and source. Growth, inflation, fiscal conditions,
investment, finance, external stability, employment and social outcomes interact
and can diverge; the Economic Survey diagnoses and interprets, the Budget
proposes, and later official releases establish outturns.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The Economic Survey is most useful as a framework for connecting indicators,
not as a warehouse of timeless facts. A defensible macro assessment labels
every number, checks revisions and tests headline growth against inflation,
investment, employment, external financing and social outcomes. Stability is
meaningful only when it supports broad productive capacity and resilience.
