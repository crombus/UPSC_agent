# Economy 03 - Inflation, Price Indices and Business Cycles

## Quick-Glance Tree Chart

```text
SYLLABUS BOUNDARY
price-level change -> measurement -> transmission -> distribution
-> Phillips curve / business cycle -> matched Indian policy response
  |
  v
CORE VOCABULARY
price level = index reading | inflation = % change
inflation -> sustained general rise
disinflation -> slower rise | deflation -> sustained fall
reflation -> deliberate recovery from depressed demand/prices
stagflation -> inflation + weak growth/jobs
hyperinflation -> extreme self-reinforcing loss of money value
  |
  v
CAUSE AND COMPOSITION
demand-pull -> spending > available output / positive gap
cost-push -> wages, tax, freight, input cost
supply shock -> crop, storage, logistics, administered supply
imported -> global commodity + freight + exchange-rate pass-through
built-in -> expectations + contracts + wage/mark-up second round
headline -> full basket | CFPI -> food | fuel -> energy
core -> analytical exclusion, commonly food and fuel removed
  |
  v

GROSS DOMESTIC PRODUCT (GDP) DEFLATOR
(nominal GDP / real GDP) x 100
domestic final goods + services | variable production basket
exports included | imports excluded directly
broad but national-account vintage/revision dependent
  |
  v
INDEX LOGIC
Laspeyres = sum(pt*q0)/sum(p0*q0) x 100
Paasche = sum(pt*qt)/sum(p0*qt) x 100
Fisher = sqrt(L x P)
risks -> substitution | quality | new goods | outlets | seasonality
base effect = old comparison arithmetic; momentum = current movement
series break -> official linking/back series only
  |
  v
REAL AND DISTRIBUTIONAL EFFECTS
real rate ~= nominal rate - expected inflation
exact: 1+r = (1+i)/(1+pi_e)
nominal wage/income -> deflate for purchasing power
unexpected inflation -> fixed borrower relief / lender loss
inflation tax -> erosion of cash balances
indexation -> partial protection; basis + lag remain
poor/informal households -> larger food exposure, weaker indexation
  |
  v
PHILLIPS CURVE
short run -> less slack can raise inflation
expectations adjust -> short-run curve shifts
long run -> vertical; no permanent trade-off
NAIRU -> estimated non-accelerating-inflation unemployment rate,
         not fixed, observable or socially optimal
RBI Nov 2021 -> Indian curve estimated convex:
 flatter at low/negative gaps, steeper at high positive gaps
  |
  v
BUSINESS CYCLE
trough -> recovery -> expansion -> peak -> contraction
output gap = (actual-potential)/potential x 100
leading -> new orders/expectations
coincident -> output/sales/employment
lagging -> duration/default stress
technical recession -> common two negative seasonally adjusted q-o-q rule
caveat -> not complete dating; revisions + breadth matter
India -> no single statutory recession-dating committee
RBI Nov 2024 -> material seasonality in GDP components/indicators
  |
  v
POLICY ASSIGNMENT
demand/persistence -> Reserve Bank of India (RBI) /
                      Monetary Policy Committee (MPC)
                      rates, liquidity, communication

food/logistics -> production, buffers, storage, movement, competition
imported fuel -> tax/subsidy calibration, diversification, efficiency
welfare -> targeted support, not blanket price suppression
cross-link -> detailed monetary instruments belong to Economy Topic 4
  |
  v
EXAMINER TRAPS
lower inflation != lower prices | CFPI != core
WPI != services/COL | GDP deflator != fixed consumer basket
base effect != new shock | slowdown != recession
negative output gap != immunity from supply inflation
borrower gain requires unexpected inflation + fixed nominal contract
  |
  v
```
