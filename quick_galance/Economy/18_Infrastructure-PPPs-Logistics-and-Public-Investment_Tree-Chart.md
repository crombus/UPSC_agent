## PPP MODEL DECODER

- **EPC:** government-funded works contract; no long-term private service finance.
- **BOT Toll:** private finance/build/O&M; user toll and traffic risk.
- **BOT Annuity:** authority payments; traffic risk largely public.
- **DBFOT:** private design-build-finance-operate, then transfer.
- **HAM:** road model; 40% milestone public support, developer-arranged balance.
- **OMT:** operation and maintenance of an existing asset.
- **TOT:** operational toll rights against upfront concession value.
- **Lease:** operating use for a term; narrower obligations than many concessions.

## RISK DECODER

| Risk | Core allocation logic |
|---|---|
| Construction/design | party controlling delivery |
| Land/sovereign clearance | public authority |
| Traffic/demand | toll versus availability-payment model |
| O&M | operator with measurable standards |
| Currency/refinancing | hedging and transparent relief |
| Force majeure | defined shared relief/termination |
| Change in law | public or expressly compensated |

## FINANCE DECODER

- **VGF:** viability grant; maximum ceiling != automatic award.
- **IIPDF:** transaction-adviser/project-development support.
- **PPPAC:** central-sector PPP appraisal/recommendation.
- **NaBFID:** statutory infrastructure DFI; long-term debt/market development.
- **NIIF:** sovereign-anchored professional equity fund manager.
- **InvIT:** pooled operating infrastructure asset vehicle.
- **Takeout:** transfer of seasoned loan exposure.
- **Credit enhancement:** improves debt quality but creates support exposure.
- **Municipal bond:** ULB borrowing requiring credible accounts and revenues.

## LOGISTICS DECODER

- **GatiShakti:** GIS planning platform; no single project fund.
- **NLP:** National Logistics Policy; processes, data, standards and skills.
- **ULIP:** Unified Logistics Interface Platform; API integration.
- **LEADS:** Logistics Ease Across Different States benchmarking.
- **LPI:** World Bank international supply-chain composite; not cost/GDP.
- **Logistics cost:** transport + warehousing + handling + inventory-related components under stated method.

## PRELIMS TRAPS

1. PPP != privatisation; EPC != PPP.
2. BOT Toll != BOT Annuity; HAM's 40:60 is road-specific.
3. OMT/TOT operate or monetise brownfield assets; they do not create the original road.
4. Pipeline/project value != expenditure, private investment or completion.
5. VGF != loan; guarantee != no fiscal risk.
6. NIIF != NaBFID != NIP.
7. GatiShakti != NLP; ULIP activity != logistics-cost outcome.
8. NMP potential != transaction proceeds != reinvestment.
9. BE != RE != Actual; public capex != total national infrastructure investment.
10. Energy-sector detail belongs Topic 31.
