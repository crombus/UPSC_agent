                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Infrastructure creates value only when an economically justified project is
appraised, financed, completed, connected, operated and maintained as a usable
service. PPPs do not create free money: they allocate construction, demand,
operation, policy and force-majeure risks through contracts. Risk should sit
with the party best able to control it, while contingent fiscal liabilities and
service obligations remain transparent.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Infrastructure supports inclusive growth when projects are selected for real
service needs, not merely construction spending. PPPs can mobilise capability
and discipline, but weak appraisal and misallocated risks create hidden fiscal
costs. India should prioritise project preparation, multimodal connections,
maintenance and transparent contingent liabilities alongside headline capital
outlays.
