## Prelims traps

| Trap | Correction |
|---|---|
| MFN gives one ally special treatment | MFN is general partner non-discrimination, subject to exceptions |
| National treatment bans customs tariffs | It disciplines internal treatment after entry |
| Quota stops all imports above a volume | A TRQ permits above-quota imports at the higher rate |
| Cheap imports prove dumping | Dumping, injury and causation require investigation |
| Safeguard requires unfair trade | It addresses serious injury from an import surge |
| Every farm subsidy is Amber | Classification depends on design and conditions |
| FTA means immediate zero duty everywhere | Schedules, exclusions, phase-downs and origin rules apply |
| Signed means in force | Conclusion, signature, ratification and operation are distinct |
| Trade deficit proves failure | Composition, productive imports, services and financing matter |
| WTO dispute settlement is absent | Panels continue; appellate review is impaired |

## Answer spine

1. Define the instrument and legal perimeter.
2. Identify the authority and dated status.
3. Trace landed price, quantity, sourcing, investment and employment.
4. Separate efficiency from distribution and adjustment.
5. Test trade creation, utilisation, value addition and resilience.
6. Conclude with predictable openness plus targeted, reviewable support.
