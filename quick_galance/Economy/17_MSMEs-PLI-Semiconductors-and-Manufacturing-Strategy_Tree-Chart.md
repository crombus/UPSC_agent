## MSME TOOL DECODER

- **MSMED Act, 2006:** classification/development plus delayed-payment provisions.
- **Udyam:** official enterprise identity; no automatic loan, tender or quality guarantee.
- **SIDBI:** statutory development-finance institution for MSMEs.
- **CGTMSE:** lender-risk guarantee for eligible MSE credit; appraisal remains.
- **MSE payment rule:** agreed period cannot exceed 45 days; statutory interest/remedy follows delay.
- **MSEFC:** Micro and Small Enterprise Facilitation Council.
- **ODR:** Online Dispute Resolution; lowers process cost but does not guarantee recovery.
- **TReDS:** Trade Receivables Discounting System; digital factoring through multiple financiers.
- **Factoring:** assignment of receivables; not ordinary asset-backed term lending.
- **MSE procurement:** 25% Central annual target; 4% SC/ST-owned; 3% women-owned.
- **MSE-CDP:** common facilities and cluster infrastructure.
- **ZED:** Zero Defect Zero Effect; Bronze/Silver/Gold under revised 2022 guidelines.
- **RAMP:** Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance; institutional and firm-capability reform.

## PLI DECODER

**Fourteen sectors:** mobile/specified electronics; IT hardware; telecom/networking; pharmaceuticals; bulk drugs; medical devices; automobiles/components; ACC batteries; high-efficiency solar PV; white goods; food processing; MMF/technical textiles; speciality steel; drones/components.

| Instrument | Trigger/channel |
|---|---|
| PLI | verified eligible incremental production/sales |
| Capex subsidy | eligible investment expenditure |
| Tariff | raises import price |
| Procurement preference | changes public-buyer demand |

**Evaluation chain:** outlay -> approval -> investment -> production -> claim -> disbursement -> DVA/productivity/jobs/exports/spillovers -> counterfactual additionality -> sunset.

## SEMICONDUCTOR DECODER

- **Semiconductor:** material/device enabling switching, processing, memory, sensing or power control.
- **Fabless:** designs chips without owning a wafer fab.
- **Foundry:** fabricates customer designs.
- **IDM:** Integrated Device Manufacturer; combines design and manufacturing.
- **EDA:** Electronic Design Automation tools.
- **IP core:** reusable protected design block.
- **Fab:** wafer fabrication; front-end process.
- **ATMP:** Assembly, Testing, Marking and Packaging.
- **OSAT:** Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test.
- **Node:** technology-generation label; not a universal literal dimension or quality rank.
- **Yield:** good dies as a share of fabricated dies; decisive for unit cost.

## PRELIMS TRAPS

1. Both MSME classification limbs apply.
2. MSE procurement and delayed-payment protections are not automatically medium-enterprise entitlements.
3. TReDS platform != financier; invoice upload != acceptance != discounting.
4. CGTMSE limit != cash grant or guaranteed payout.
5. PLI has sector-specific rates and periods.
6. Gross sales/exports != domestic value added or causal additionality.
7. Electronics assembly != semiconductor fabrication.
8. ATMP/OSAT != wafer fab.
9. Smaller node != best node for every product.
10. Project announcement != operation.
