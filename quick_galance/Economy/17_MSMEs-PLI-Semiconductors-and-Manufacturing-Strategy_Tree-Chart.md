                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
India needs both competitive MSMEs and large manufacturing anchors. PLI can
accelerate scale, but its legitimacy depends on verified additional output,
domestic value, jobs and supplier upgrading. Semiconductor policy must build a
stage-wise ecosystem rather than label packaging or final assembly as
fabrication. Timely finance, standards and technology diffusion remain the
bridge from headline investment to broad industrial capability.
