                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Energy infrastructure succeeds only when primary resources or imports are
converted, transported and distributed into reliable, affordable, acceptable
and resilient services at the point of use. Installed capacity, a connection,
an approved project or a low generation tariff is not itself an energy-service
outcome. Economic analysis must integrate networks, dispatch, utility finance,
fuel security, environmental externalities and transition costs.
