
## ACCOUNT DECODER

- **BoP:** period flow; **IIP:** end-period external asset-liability stock.
- **Trade balance:** goods only; **current account:** goods + services + primary + secondary income.
- **Primary income:** labour/capital return; **secondary income:** transfers without current quid pro quo.
- **Capital account:** capital transfers + non-produced non-financial assets.
- **Financial account:** FDI, FPI, derivatives, other investment and reserve assets.
- **Errors and omissions:** data reconciliation, not a policy financing instrument.
- **Autonomous/accommodating:** legacy analytical motive/financing labels; not modern BPM6 account headings.

## EXCHANGE-RATE DECODER

- Quote first: under **INR per USD**, a rise means rupee depreciation.
- **Depreciation/appreciation:** market movement.
- **Devaluation/revaluation:** official parity change.
- **Nominal:** observed price; **real:** relative-price adjusted.
- **NEER:** trade-weighted nominal basket; **REER:** relative-price adjusted basket.
- RBI series: 40 currencies, base 2015-16=100.
- RBI policy, 8 Apr 2026: market-determined; intervention smooths excessive/disruptive volatility; no specific level or band.
- IMF 2024 Article IV, published Feb 2025: de jure floating; de facto stabilised for Dec 2022-Nov 2024. This retrospective label is not an RBI target.

## FOREX MARKET AND RBI

```text
RBI SELLS USD -> USD SUPPLY UP + INR LIQUIDITY ABSORBED
RBI BUYS USD  -> RESERVES UP + INR LIQUIDITY INJECTED
STERILISATION -> OFFSETS DOMESTIC LIQUIDITY EFFECT
VALUATION     -> CHANGES STOCK WITHOUT BoP TRANSACTION
```

- **Spot:** near-immediate exchange.
- **Forward:** rate fixed for future exchange.
- **Swap:** linked currency legs across dates.
- **Hedge:** reduces exposure; basis, rollover and counterparty risk remain.

## RESERVE DECODER

- FCA != only dollar cash.
- SDR = IMF-created reserve asset.
- Reserve position in IMF != ordinary government deposit.
- Reserve stock != fiscal revenue.
- Adequacy dashboard: imports + original/residual short-term debt + broad money + gross financing need + stress.

## CONVERTIBILITY AND DEBT

- India accepted IMF Article VIII current-account obligations in August 1994.
- FEMA 1999 effective 1 June 2000; current transactions generally permitted subject to rules/prohibitions.
- Capital account remains calibrated, not fully convertible.
- Tarapore 1997/2006 = sequencing recommendations, not automatic law.
- Original maturity = contract at birth; residual maturity = due within next 12 months.
- ECB risk = global interest cost + currency mismatch + rollover.

## PRELIMS TRAPS

1. Residence != citizenship.
2. Overall BoP accounting balances; one account can be in deficit.
3. Remittances mainly belong to secondary income.
4. Modern capital account != FDI/FPI/loans.
5. Trade deficit != CAD.
6. Rising INR/USD means rupee depreciation.
7. Managed intervention != fixed peg or declared band.
8. Higher REER does not automatically improve competitiveness.
9. Weekly reserve change != intervention volume.
10. Import cover alone != adequacy.
11. All external debt is neither sovereign nor dollar-denominated.
12. Depreciation can worsen trade balance initially through the J-curve.
