                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Monetary policy sets the price and availability of central-bank liquidity to
anchor inflation while supporting growth. The policy rate is a signal; daily
liquidity operations keep the overnight money-market rate aligned with that
signal. Transmission then passes through bank funding, lending, expectations,
asset prices and exchange rates with lags and frictions.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The RBI's effectiveness depends not merely on changing the repo rate but on
aligning overnight liquidity, preserving credibility and recognising the limits
of monetary action against supply shocks. A sound framework distinguishes
liquidity from solvency, price stability from exchange-rate targeting and
central-bank balance-sheet operations from fiscal expenditure.
