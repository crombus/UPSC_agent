## Prelims traps

| Trap | Correction |
|---|---|
| IMF finances roads | IMF core is surveillance and external stabilisation |
| SDR is currency or grant | It is a reserve asset with an allocation liability |
| Gold tranche is a separate loan | It is the historical reserve-tranche label |
| World Bank means five institutions | World Bank usually means IBRD + IDA |
| IDA membership equals eligibility | Only eligible borrowers receive IDA resources |
| IFC lends to sovereigns like IBRD | IFC's ordinary client is private enterprise |
| ICSID is a lender and India is a member | It is dispute settlement; India is outside the Convention |
| AIIB/NDB replaced older MDBs | They complement a plural architecture |
| Approved capital equals disbursement | Capital, approval, commitment and disbursement differ |
| FSB/BCBS rules directly bind India | Domestic authorities must implement standards |

## Mains answer spine

1. Diagnose liquidity, solvency, development or regulatory problem.
2. Match institution, client and instrument.
3. State dated membership, governance and resource status.
4. Trace financing or standard through domestic implementation.
5. Test conditionality, ownership, safeguards, procurement and debt.
6. Evaluate additionality, representation and measurable outcomes.
7. Conclude with reformed multilateralism, not supranational government.
