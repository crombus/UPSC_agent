                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Digital public infrastructure lowers identity, payment, information and
transaction costs, while private and public applications convert shared rails
into services. Scale can improve inclusion and innovation but also creates
network power, data concentration, cyber risk, exclusion and precarious work.
Open interoperability must therefore be paired with clear liability,
competition, privacy and consumer-protection rules.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
India's digital economy is strongest where public rails are interoperable and
private applications compete on service rather than control of users and data.
Payments, currency, identity and commerce networks require separate legal
classification. Inclusion must include fallback access, privacy, cyber safety,
consumer redress and worker protection, while innovation remains possible under
clear responsibility.
