## Prelims traps

- UPI moves bank deposits; e-rupee is RBI money.
- NPCI operates UPI; RBI regulates the payment-system perimeter.
- NEFT remains batch-settled despite 24x7 availability; RTGS is real-time gross.
- Account Aggregator neither lends nor supports customer transactions.
- DLG does not transfer underwriting responsibility away from the regulated lender.
- ONDC is an interoperable network, not one government marketplace.
- Tax and PMLA treatment do not make crypto legal tender.
- An NFT does not automatically convey copyright.
- A draft digital-competition Bill is not current enacted law.
- A connection, account, download or transaction is not by itself productivity or inclusion.

## Mains answer spine

1. Define the service subsector, digital rail or platform market.
2. Separate regulator, operator, regulated principal, agent and application.
3. Date every statistic, direction, pilot or legislative phase.
4. Trace lower costs, scale, matching, productivity and formal visibility.
5. Test network power, cyber fraud, privacy, exclusion, worker and consumer effects.
6. Conclude with open standards, activity-based rules, portability, resilience and remedy.
