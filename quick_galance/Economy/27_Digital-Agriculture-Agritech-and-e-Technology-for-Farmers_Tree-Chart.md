## Decoding

- **DAM:** Digital Agriculture Mission.
- **IDEA:** India Digital Ecosystem of Agriculture.
- **DPI:** Digital Public Infrastructure.
- **DCS:** Digital Crop Survey.
- **DGCES / CCE:** Digital General Crop Estimation Survey / Crop Cutting Experiment.
- **Krishi-DSS:** Krishi Decision Support System.
- **MNCFC:** Mahalanobis National Crop Forecast Centre.
- **GIS / GNSS / GPS / NavIC:** spatial information system / satellite navigation class / US constellation / Indian regional navigation system.
- **IoT / AI / ML:** Internet of Things / Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning.
- **PM-KISAN / DBT / eKYC:** income-support scheme / Direct Benefit Transfer / electronic Know Your Customer.
- **KCC / KRP:** Kisan Credit Card / Kisan Rin Portal.
- **PMFBY / YES-TECH / WINDS:** crop insurance / technology-based yield estimation / weather information network.
- **e-NAM / SFAC / FPO:** electronic National Agriculture Market / Small Farmers' Agribusiness Consortium / Farmer Producer Organisation.
- **DPDP:** Digital Personal Data Protection.

## Prelims traps

- Farmer ID is not Aadhaar, title, cultivation proof or guaranteed eligibility.
- Remote sensing is not ground truth; GIS is not GNSS.
- IoT sensing, AI inference and automated action are separate.
- Digital Crop Survey coverage is not the same as accurate validated crop data.
- e-NAM does not abolish APMC law or provide physical logistics by itself.
- KCC is bank credit, not a grant; alternative data is not a universal mandatory underwriting rule.
- PMFBY technology reduces some verification cost but does not remove basis risk.
- Open standards do not mean open personal data.
- Drone certification does not authorise every pesticide, crop or operation.

## Mains spine

**Farm-stage problem -> technology/data -> named dated evidence -> welfare mechanism -> weakest physical/institutional complement -> exclusion/error/platform risk -> correction/reform -> net-income, resilience and farmer-control verdict.**

## Evaluation scorecard

| Weak activity measure | Better outcome question |
|---|---|
| IDs created | Are records accurate, inclusive and correctable? |
| Plots surveyed | Are crop records validated and useful without exclusion? |
| Advisories sent | Were they understood, acted upon and beneficial? |
| Drones distributed | Was safe service utilisation financially viable? |
| Users registered | Did active use improve price, cost or risk? |
| Claims digitised | Did timeliness, accuracy and appeal improve? |
