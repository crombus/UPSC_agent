# Economy Topic 14 — Irrigation, Inputs, Credit, Insurance and Sustainable Agriculture

## CENTRAL THESIS

```text
RELIABLE WATER + SUITABLE SEED + BALANCED NUTRIENTS + KNOWLEDGE
 -> TIMELY CREDIT + CREDIBLE INSURANCE + RESOURCE GOVERNANCE
 -> PRODUCTIVE, EQUITABLE AND RESILIENT FARMING
```

## 1. IRRIGATION ACCOUNTING

- Potential created != potential utilised != reliable delivery != water productivity.
- Net irrigated area: land irrigated at least once.
- Gross irrigated area: all irrigated crop instances.
- Irrigation intensity = gross irrigated area / net irrigated area x 100.
- Command area: land served by a project. Watershed: catchment draining to one outlet.

## 2. TYPES AND RISKS

| Type | Strength | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Canal | Shared surface system | head-tail inequity, seepage, waterlogging, salinity |
| Well/tubewell | flexible on-demand supply | common-pool aquifer depletion and energy nexus |
| Tank | local runoff storage | siltation, encroachment, catchment maintenance |
| Lift | reaches higher terrain | energy and source reliability |
| Drip/sprinkler | controlled application | cost, clogging, maintenance and rebound |
| Watershed | ridge-to-valley moisture/recharge | collective maintenance and tenure constraints |

Field saving != basin saving: lower water per hectare may expand irrigated area or water-intensive crops.

## 3. PMKSY DECODED

- 2021-26 approved architecture: **AIBP** (Accelerated Irrigation Benefit Programme), **HKKP** (Har Khet Ko Pani) and **WDC** (Watershed Development Component).
- HKKP includes **CADWM** (Command Area Development and Water Management), Surface Minor Irrigation and Repair, Renovation and Restoration of water bodies.
- **PDMC** (Per Drop More Crop) supports micro-irrigation under **RKVY** from **2022-23**.
- Official June 2026 material continues PMKSY programme context; component period/location must still be dated.
- WUA = Water Users Association; creation does not prove participation.

## 4. SEEDS

```text
NUCLEUS -> BREEDER (golden yellow)
 -> FOUNDATION (white) -> CERTIFIED (blue)
```

- Formal: recognised variety, testing/certification or label and organised sale.
- Informal: farm-saved, exchange and local channels.
- Seeds Act, 1966 + Seeds Rules, 1968 remain the core law.
- Draft Seeds Bill, 2025 was circulated for consultation; it was not enacted by the cutoff.
- Tissue culture and bud-chip settlings are propagation methods.

## 5. FERTILISER / SOIL / PEST

- N: vegetative/protein; P: roots/energy; K: water regulation/stress/quality.
- Urea: controlled MRP and separate subsidy.
- Eligible P&K fertilisers: Nutrient Based Subsidy using periodically notified N/P/K/S rates.
- Fertiliser DBT: company paid after authenticated PoS retail sale; farmer does not receive cash subsidy.
- Soil Health Card: dated soil test and recommendation; under RKVY Soil Health and Fertility from 2022-23.
- INM = integrated nutrient management; IPM = integrated pest management.
- Nano-product notification != universal replacement evidence.

## 6. MECHANISATION

- Mechanisation includes sowing, transplanting, weeding, harvesting, residue and post-harvest operations—not tractors alone.
- CHC = Custom Hiring Centre: shared machinery services for farmers who cannot efficiently own equipment.
- Sanctioned asset != maintained, timely service.

## 7. CREDIT ARCHITECTURE

```text
COMMERCIAL BANKS + RRBs + COOPERATIVES
PACS -> DCCB -> STATE COOPERATIVE BANK
NABARD -> REFINANCE / DEVELOPMENT / SPECIFIED SUPERVISION
RBI -> BANK REGULATION + PSL DIRECTIONS
```

- Institutional credit differs from moneylender/trader/landlord credit.
- RBI PSL Directions 2025, updated **7 August 2026**: agriculture **18%** of ANBC/CEOBSE; within it NCF **14%**, SMF **10%** for applicable banks.
- KCC = Kisan Credit Card product for crop/allied working capital.
- Budget 2025-26 announced Rs **5 lakh** overall KCC limit.
- FY2025-26 MISS retained **7%** concessional short-term loans up to Rs **3 lakh**; **3%** prompt repayment incentive yields **4%** for eligible timely payers.
- JLG/SHG routes can help tenants, but documentation and appraisal remain.

## 8. CROP INSURANCE

- Insurance: premium -> pooled risk -> specified trigger -> assessment -> claim.
- PMFBY August 2026 farmer premium shares: **2% Kharif / 1.5% Rabi food-oilseed / 5% annual commercial-horticultural**.
- Farmer enrolment voluntary since Kharif 2020.
- Widespread yield loss: insurance-unit area approach using CCEs.
- Localised/post-harvest events: separate reporting and assessment.
- Basis risk: individual loss differs from area-yield or weather-index result.
- YES-TECH = technology-based yield estimation; WINDS = Weather Information Network and Data System; CROPIC = crop photo/observation technology initiative.
- Insurance covers specified production risk, not every price or household-income loss.

## 9. SUSTAINABLE AGRICULTURE

- Conservation agriculture: minimum disturbance + permanent cover + crop diversity/rotation.
- Organic: production/certification standards. Natural farming: distinct low-external-input approach. Permaculture: ecological design.
- NMNF = National Mission on Natural Farming, approved **25 November 2024** through **2025-26**; later status needs fresh official authority.
- Sikkim: 2003 resolution -> phased transition -> January 2016 full-organic declaration.
- Agroforestry integrates trees; IFS integrates crops and allied enterprises.
- Rainfed Area Development under NMSA promotes Integrated Farming Systems.
- Climate-smart agriculture = productivity + adaptation + mitigation where feasible.
- CCAFS was a CGIAR research programme; ICRISAT is a dryland research institution.

## 10. PRELIMS TRAPS

1. Potential created != utilised.
2. Irrigation intensity != water-use efficiency.
3. Command area != watershed.
4. PDMC current location != unchanged PMKSY listing.
5. Draft Seeds Bill != enacted law.
6. Urea != NBS-covered P&K fertiliser.
7. Fertiliser DBT != cash transfer to farmer.
8. KCC product != PSL portfolio target != MISS interest support.
9. PMFBY premium rate != claim rate.
10. Organic != natural != permaculture.
