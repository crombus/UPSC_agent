level.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Inclusive growth is achieved not when a single poverty ratio falls, but when
people gain productive work, quality services, assets and protection against
shocks across regions and generations. India should retain rights-based floors
while improving databases, service quality and local accountability. Poverty,
inequality and capability indicators must be read together and always with
their methodological vintage.
