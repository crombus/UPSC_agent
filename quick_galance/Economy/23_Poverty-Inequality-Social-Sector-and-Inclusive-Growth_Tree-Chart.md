## Prelims traps

- Poverty can fall while inequality rises.
- Consumption inequality is not income or wealth inequality.
- 2022-23 HCES redesign limits direct comparison with 2011-12.
- International PPP lines are not Indian statutory eligibility rules.
- NITI's 11.28% for 2022-23 is an estimate, not a fresh NFHS observation.
- MGNREGA is rural, household-based and demand-driven.
- NFSA's 35 kg entitlement is per AAY household; PHH receives 5 kg per person.
- PM-JAY hospitalisation insurance does not replace primary care or cover every expense.
- A budget outlay, account, registration or beneficiary count is not an outcome.

## Mains answer spine

1. Define the welfare concept and unit.
2. State line/index, data source, year, price or PPP basis and methodology.
3. Trace growth, jobs, assets, taxes, transfers and services.
4. Disaggregate region, gender, caste, lifecycle and migration status.
5. Test adequacy, coverage, incidence, quality, portability and fiscal sustainability.
6. Conclude with capability, agency, resilience and qualified inclusion.
