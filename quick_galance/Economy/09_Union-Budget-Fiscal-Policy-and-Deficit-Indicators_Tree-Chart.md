                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The budget is an annual constitutional authorisation and a macroeconomic policy
instrument. Fiscal analysis must identify the government perimeter, receipt and
expenditure classification, deficit identity, estimate vintage and off-budget
risk. The quality of a deficit depends on timing, composition, financing,
multiplier effects and debt sustainability, not on its size alone.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Fiscal prudence is not synonymous with the smallest possible deficit. A credible
budget distinguishes current and capital transactions, reports all relevant
liabilities and uses borrowing where social returns justify future costs.
Countercyclical flexibility should operate within a transparent medium-term debt
framework, with expenditure quality and realised outcomes assessed alongside
headline ratios.
