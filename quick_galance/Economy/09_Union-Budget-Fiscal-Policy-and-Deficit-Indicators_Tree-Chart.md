# Economy Topic 9 — Union Budget, Fiscal Policy and Deficit Indicators

**Current-law/data cutoff:** 9 September 2026  
**Budget publication anchor:** Union Budget 2026-27, presented 1 February 2026.

```text
CONSTITUTION -> FUNDS -> RECEIPTS/EXPENDITURE -> DEFICITS
             -> FISCAL STANCE -> DEBT -> TRANSPARENCY
```

## 1. Constitutional route

- **Article 112:** President causes the Annual Financial Statement (AFS) to be laid before both Houses.
- **Article 113:** charged expenditure may be discussed but is not voted; other expenditure becomes
  Demands for Grants voted by the Lok Sabha on the President's recommendation.
- **Article 114:** Appropriation Bill authorises withdrawal from the Consolidated Fund of India (CFI).
- **Article 115:** supplementary, additional and excess grants.
- **Article 116:** Vote on Account, vote of credit and exceptional grant.
- **Article 117:** special rules for financial Bills; read with Article 110 for Money Bills.

## 2. Three funds

| Fund | Decode | Withdrawal boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CFI | Consolidated Fund of India: revenue, loans raised and loan recoveries | Appropriation required |
| Public Account | Trust-like, repayable balances such as provident funds and small savings | Routine repayment does not need appropriation |
| Contingency Fund | Imprest for urgent unforeseen expenditure | Advance first; ex-post approval and recoupment |

**Current dated fact:** the Key to Budget Documents 2026 states a Rs 30,000 crore Contingency Fund
corpus. This is not the constitutional amount and must be rechecked in later Budgets.

## 3. Bills, grants and motions

| Device | Function |
|---|---|
| Finance Bill | Gives effect to tax proposals |
| Appropriation Bill | Authorises CFI withdrawal for voted grants and charged sums |
| Policy cut | Demand reduced to Re 1 |
| Economy cut | Demand reduced by a specified amount |
| Token cut | Demand reduced by Rs 100 to ventilate a grievance |
| Vote on Account | Advance pending ordinary budget passage |
| Supplementary/additional/excess | Insufficient amount/new service/already exceeded |

**Trap:** Vote on Account is not synonymous with interim budget.

## 4. Receipt and expenditure tree

```text
RECEIPTS
  revenue -> tax + non-tax
  capital -> debt + non-debt (loan recovery, disinvestment)

EXPENDITURE
  revenue -> current services/transfers/interest
  capital -> asset creation/acquisition or liability reduction
```

Accounting label is not a moral verdict. Revenue spending may build human capability; poor capex may
create low-value assets.

## 5. Formula strip

- RD = Revenue Expenditure - Revenue Receipts.
- ERD = RD - Grants-in-Aid for Creation of Capital Assets.
- FD = Total Expenditure - Revenue Receipts - Non-Debt Capital Receipts.
- PD = FD - Interest Payments.
- Effective Capex = Capital Expenditure + Grants-in-Aid for Creation of Capital Assets.

## 7. FRBM decoded

- **FRBM:** Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management Act, 2003.
- **Section 3 statements:** MTFP = Medium-term Fiscal Policy Statement; FPS = Fiscal Policy
  Strategy Statement; MEFS = Macro-Economic Framework Statement; MTEF = Medium-term Expenditure
  Framework Statement.
- **Section 4 statutory references:** FD up to 3% by 31 March 2021; Central debt up to 40% by
  end-FY2024-25; additional guarantees up to 0.5% GDP in a financial year.
- **Escape grounds:** national security, act of war, national calamity, severe agricultural collapse,
  structural reform with unanticipated fiscal implications, or quarterly real-output growth at least
  three percentage points below its previous-four-quarter average. Annual FD deviation is capped at
  0.5 percentage point GDP; reasons and a return path must be laid before Parliament.
- **Section 5:** no routine direct Central borrowing from RBI; temporary WMA and specified exceptions;
  secondary-market operations remain possible.
- **Current-path caveat:** statutory target, committee recommendation, annual Budget target and Actual
  are four different statuses. The FY2026-27 deviation statement explains non-achievement of the old
  statutory dates; it does not state that the FY2026-27 target itself uses escape-clause headroom.

## 8. Monetisation history

Ad hoc Treasury Bills enabled automatic RBI financing until their replacement by **Ways and Means
Advances (WMA) from 1 April 1997**. WMA bridges temporary cash mismatch. RBI secondary-market
government-security operations are not automatically direct deficit monetisation.

## 9. Fiscal transmission

```text
tax/spend/transfer/borrow
 -> demand + distribution + capacity
 -> inflation + output + employment
 -> interest + debt + future fiscal space
```

- Automatic stabilisers use existing rules; discretionary policy needs a fresh decision.
- Multiplier varies with slack, targeting, saving, imports, inflation, implementation and monetary response.
- Public borrowing may crowd out under tight financial conditions or crowd in by removing bottlenecks.
- Debt dynamics depend on primary balance, effective interest-growth differential and stock-flow adjustments.

## 10. Transparency and public-sector perimeter

- Off-budget borrowing: entity borrows while future public budgets effectively service the liability.
- EBR: Extra-Budgetary Resources; the 2026 budget guide discloses GoI fully serviced bonds in Statement 27A.
- Guarantee: contingent exposure; distinguish annual addition, outstanding stock and invocation.
- CGA = Controller General of Accounts; compiles Union accounts.
- CAG = Comptroller and Auditor General; independently audits and reports.
- Union Budget < general government < wider public sector. Never compare unlike perimeters.

## 11. State-finance boundary

- Article 280 creates the Finance Commission recommendation route.
- Article 281 requires recommendations and the explanatory memorandum on action taken to be laid before Parliament.
- Detailed tax devolution, GST and fiscal federalism belong to Topic 10.

## 12. Prelims traps and Mains spine

1. Charged expenditure is discussable and appropriated.
2. Public Account is not government revenue.
3. Borrowing is a debt capital receipt.
4. FD is not debt and not necessarily monetised.
5. Zero RD does not imply zero FD.
6. ERD and effective capex use the same asset-creating-grant bridge in opposite accounting directions.
7. BE, RE and Actuals are not interchangeable.
8. The 0.5% escape cap and 0.5% additional-guarantee ceiling are separate rules.
9. A deviation statement does not automatically prove escape-clause invocation.
10. Union numbers do not represent all States or public enterprises.

**Mains spine:** define and date -> constitutional/FRBM source -> formula and composition -> multiplier,
crowding and debt -> transparency/perimeter -> rule-bound flexibility and outcome-based conclusion.
