# Economy Topic 15 — Food Processing, Cold Chains and Value Addition

## CENTRAL THESIS

```text
FARM + AGGREGATION + CONDITIONING + CONTINUOUS LOGISTICS
 -> PROCESSING + SAFETY + PACKAGING + MARKET ACCESS
 -> VALUE CREATION
 -> FARMER/JOB/NUTRITION/EXPORT OUTCOME ONLY IF GAINS TRANSMIT
```

## 1. SCOPE DECODED

| Level | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | basic conditioning/preservation | cleaning, grading, milling |
| Secondary | manufacturing food from primary material | bread, cheese, fruit pulp |
| Tertiary | higher-convenience preparation/service | ready-to-eat formulation |

- Value added = output value minus intermediate consumption.
- Value capture depends on quality rules, contracts, competition and payment.
- Backward linkage: farms/inputs to processor. Forward linkage: processor to buyer/retail/export.

## 2. COMPLETE VALUE CHAIN

```text
FARM -> AGGREGATE -> ASSAY/GRADE -> PACKHOUSE -> PRE-COOL
 -> STORE -> REEFER/AMBIENT MOVE -> PROCESS -> TEST -> PACK
 -> RETAIL/EXPORT -> CONSUMER -> WASTE/BY-PRODUCT MANAGEMENT
```

- Assaying measures attributes; grading classifies lots.
- Packaging contains, protects, informs and traces.
- Traceability follows a batch backward and forward; a QR code alone proves nothing.

## 3. LOSS, WASTE AND NUTRITION

- Food loss: mainly production-to-processing stages.
- Food waste: mainly retail/consumer stages.
- No universal Indian loss percentage without commodity, stage, method and date.
- Processing can preserve, pasteurise or fortify; nutritional effect depends on formulation and diet.
- Ultra-processed != every packaged food; food safety != nutritional quality.

## 4. COLD CHAIN

```text
FIELD HEAT -> PRE-COOL -> PACKHOUSE -> CHILLED/FROZEN/CA STORAGE
 -> REEFER -> DESTINATION COLD ROOM -> LAST MILE
```

- Cold storage = one location. Cold chain = uninterrupted product-suited condition.
- CA = controlled atmosphere; MA = modified atmosphere.
- Chilled, frozen, dairy, fish and fresh-produce chains are not interchangeable.
- Reefer usually maintains temperature; pre-cooling removes field heat.

## 5. BOTTLENECKS

| Constraint | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Small lots | high procurement/testing cost |
| Seasonality | idle capacity or peak congestion |
| Working capital | inability to buy raw material/use plant |
| Power/skills | downtime, spoilage, unsafe output |
| Fragmented rules | delay and fixed compliance cost |
| Weak buyers/contracts | poor throughput or unfair value capture |

Sanctioned -> installed -> operational -> utilised -> throughput -> verified outcome.

## 6. REGULATORY MAP

- **FSSAI:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India under Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006; science-based domestic food-safety standards and regulation.
- **Section 31:** applicable registration/licensing of food businesses.
- **APEDA:** Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority under APEDA Act, 1985; scheduled-product export development.
- **SPS:** sanitary and phytosanitary rules protecting human, animal or plant health.
- **HortiNet / TraceNet:** specified APEDA-linked traceability systems.

### ABBREVIATION DECODER

- **PM Kisan SAMPADA Yojana:** Pradhan Mantri Kisan Scheme for Agro-Marine Processing and Development of Agro-Processing Clusters.
- **ICCVAI:** Integrated Cold Chain and Value Addition Infrastructure.
- **PMFME:** Pradhan Mantri Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises.
- **ODOP:** One District One Product.
- **PLISFPI:** Production Linked Incentive Scheme for Food Processing Industry.
- **AIF:** Agriculture Infrastructure Fund.
- **TOP:** Tomato, Onion and Potato.
- **CA / MA:** Controlled Atmosphere / Modified Atmosphere.
- **FDI:** Foreign Direct Investment.
- **ASI:** Annual Survey of Industries.
- **FBO:** Food Business Operator.

## 8. INVESTMENT AND TRADE

- Food processing: 100% FDI under automatic route.
- Retail of food manufactured/produced in India: 100% FDI under government route.
- MoFPI 2025 review: processed food = 20.4% of agri-food exports in 2024-25.
- Sector FDI = USD 7.33 billion, April 2014-March 2025.
- ASI 2023-24: 12.83% of registered-manufacturing employment; informal segment excluded.

## 9. PRELIMS TRAPS

1. Processing != factory cooking only.
2. Sales value != value added.
3. Cold store != cold chain.
4. Pre-cooling != freezing.
5. APEDA != domestic food-safety regulator.
6. SPS != tariff.
7. PM Kisan SAMPADA != irrigation PMKSY.
8. Mega Food Parks are not open for fresh approvals.
9. PMFME != PLISFPI.
10. ODOP != GI status.
11. AIF != grant.
12. Approval != operation or outcome.
