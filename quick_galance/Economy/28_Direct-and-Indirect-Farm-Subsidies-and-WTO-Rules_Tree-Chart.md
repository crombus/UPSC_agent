## Prelims Decoder

| Term | Decode | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| DBT | Direct Benefit Transfer; design can reimburse a supplier or pay a beneficiary | Fertiliser DBT is not PM-KISAN cash |
| NBS | Nutrient Based Subsidy for notified phosphatic and potassic grades | Urea is outside NBS |
| MSP | Minimum Support Price announcement | Not identical to procurement |
| AMS | Aggregate Measurement of Support | Not total Budget spending |
| AAP | Applied administered price | One term in WTO MPS formula |
| FERP | Fixed external reference price, generally 1986-88 | Not current spot price |
| TRQ | Tariff-rate quota | Market-access tool, not Amber Box |
| P&K | Phosphatic and potassic fertilisers | Distinct from urea regime |
| PMFBY | Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana | Premium support does not erase basis risk |
| Article 6.2 | Conditional developing-country development exemption | Not blanket immunity |
| Peace clause | Conditional due restraint for qualifying public stocks | Not permanent AMS deletion |

## Mains 7-Step Spine

1. Define subsidy and counterfactual.
2. Classify direct/indirect and explicit/implicit.
3. Trace recipient, pass-through and incidence.
4. Add fiscal, distributional and ecological effects.
5. Apply AoA pillar, box, formula and de minimis separately.
6. Date Bali/WT-L-939 current status.
7. Conclude with targeted support, public goods, corrected incentives and transition protection.
