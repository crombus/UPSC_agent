The Satavahanas built an early durable Deccan network-polity
linking shifting western/eastern centres, agrarian zones,
regional chiefs, merchants, ports and plural religious donors;

                 QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Satavahana importance lies in mediating between the agrarian
Deccan and wider inland, coastal and religious networks. Royal
authority operated through aharas, officials, local chiefs,
marriage alliances, merchants and donees rather than uniform
bureaucratic command. Naneghat and Nashik reveal sacrifice,
genealogy and royal ideology; Nahapana overstrikes show political
reversal; grants, paddy, cotton, towns, lead coinage and cave-
stupa donors reveal a productive and connected economy. Their
Brahmanical self-presentation coexisted with extensive Buddhist
patronage. Later contraction was regionally uneven, producing
successor states while Deccan agriculture, exchange, religious
institutions and cultural forms continued in transformed ways.
