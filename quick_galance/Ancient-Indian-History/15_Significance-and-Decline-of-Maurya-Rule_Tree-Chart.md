                 QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Mauryan rule mattered because it joined unprecedented imperial
scale with strong administrative capacity, transregional routes,
monumental stone, multilingual inscriptions and Ashoka's public
ethics. These achievements remained uneven and did not create a
modern national or command state. Its decline likewise cannot be
reduced to pacifism, Brahmanical reaction, bankruptcy or weak
successors. Thin later evidence supports a cumulative process in
which succession disputes, provincial reassertion, communication
and fiscal-military strain, frontier pressure and court politics
reinforced one another. The dynasty and paramount network ended,
but towns, exchange, religions, artistic forms and imperial
memory continued through transformed regional trajectories.
