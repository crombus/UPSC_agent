                 QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The transition to food production in the subcontinent was
neither a single revolution nor a one-way diffusion. Regional
communities combined cultivation, herding, wild resources,
mobility, storage and craft in different sequences. Copper
later overlapped with rather than simply replacing stone.
Neolithic and Chalcolithic cultures should therefore be read
as interconnected regional adaptations—not rigid ethnic boxes
or steps in an inevitable march toward Harappan urbanism.
