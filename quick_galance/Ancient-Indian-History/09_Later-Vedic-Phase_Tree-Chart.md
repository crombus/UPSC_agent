# Ancient Indian History 09 - The Later Vedic Phase

## Quick-Glance Tree Chart

THE LATER VEDIC PHASE
|
+-- 1. SOURCE AND DATE CONTROL
|   +-- conventional horizon: c.1000-600 BCE
|   +-- later Rigvedic layers + Sama/Yajur/Atharva Samhitas
|   +-- Brahmanas -> Aranyakas -> early Upanishads
|   `-- composition/redaction + prescription/practice must remain separate
|
+-- 2. GEOGRAPHICAL REORIENTATION
|   +-- north-west baseline -> Kuru-Panchala core
|   +-- Indo-Gangetic divide + upper Ganga-Yamuna doab
|   +-- later Kosala/Videha and middle-Ganga extensions
|   `-- regional settlement/interaction, not one empty-land conquest
|
+-- 3. PGW AND IRON
|   +-- PGW = pottery/settlement horizon; named anchors: Hastinapur, Atranjikhera, Jakhera
|   +-- can support chronology, habitation, crops and associated tools
|   +-- cannot prove Vedic language, ethnicity, one state or epic event
|   +-- shyama-ayas/krishna-ayas commonly linked to iron
|   `-- iron enabled change; labour, fuel, oxen, crops, ecology and institutions mediated it
|
+-- 4. AGRARIAN POLITICAL ECONOMY
|   +-- plough + oxen + rice/barley/wheat/pulses + storage
|   +-- agriculture intensified; cattle remained economically and ritually important
|   +-- persistent villages -> appropriable surplus -> specialists/rulers/priests
|   +-- crafts diversify: smith, carpenter, chariot maker, potter, weaver, leather worker
|   `-- nishka/satamana/krishnala = valuables/value measures; no automatic coinage
|
+-- 5. JANA -> JANAPADA
|   +-- Bharata + Puru traditions -> Kuru consolidation
|   +-- Panchala = major related centre
|   +-- people/kin identity gains territorial attachment
|   `-- lineage and local bodies persist inside emerging territorial polity
|
+-- 6. KINGSHIP AND RATNINS
|   +-- hereditary and territorial tendency strengthens
|   +-- ten-generation kingdoms, but ratification/exceptional expulsion remains
|   +-- purohita, senani, gramani, queen, suta, bhagadugha, sangrahitri
|   `-- transitional support network, not modern cabinet or permanent departments
|
+-- 7. REVENUE AND ASSEMBLIES
|   +-- bali more obligatory; bhaga = ruler's share; shulka context-sensitive
|   +-- dana/dakshina + booty continue; extraction also redistributes
|   +-- sabha/samiti continue with reduced/narrowed weight; vidatha recedes
|   `-- no Mauryan-style assessment, province system or universal standing army
|
+-- 8. RITUAL SOVEREIGNTY
|   +-- rajasuya = consecration/renewal
|   +-- ashvamedha = paramountcy claim
|   +-- vajapeya = strength/abundance/chariot prestige
|   `-- ritual performs sovereignty and redistribution; it does not map actual borders
|
+-- 9. VARNA AND SOCIAL HIERARCHY
|   +-- sharper Brahmana-Kshatriya-Vaishya-Shudra ideology
|   +-- brahma <-> kshatra = cooperation plus status competition
|   +-- Vaishya = production/tribute; Shudra = subordination/restricted access
|   `-- varna is not the complete later jati or untouchability system
|
+-- 10. HOUSEHOLD, GOTRA AND WOMEN
|   +-- grihapati controls production, ritual, property and lineage
|   +-- son preference + ancestor rites + inheritance -> stronger patriliny
|   +-- gotra descent/exogamy develops; later detailed system not automatic
|   +-- patriarchal norms restrict women
|   `-- wives/queens, productive work, Gargi/Maitreyi = unequal domain-specific agency
|
+-- 11. YAJNA AND RELIGIOUS CHANGE
|   +-- hotri recites; udgatri chants; adhvaryu performs; brahman supervises
|   +-- Prajapati, Vishnu and Rudra gain relative prominence
|   +-- older deities and sacrifice continue
|   `-- complexity converts specialist knowledge + surplus into authority
|
+-- 12. UPANISHADIC TURN
|   +-- Brihadaranyaka/Chandogya: dialogue, analogy and debate
|   +-- brahman, atman, karma and rebirth develop unevenly
|   +-- critique/interiorisation from within the Vedic world
|   `-- not one doctrine, total ritual rejection or completed later Vedanta
|
+-- 13. EARLY -> LATER COMPARISON
|   +-- north-west -> Kuru-Panchala / upper Ganga
|   +-- cattle-valued mixed economy -> stronger settled agrarian surplus
|   +-- jana chiefship -> territorial kingship
|   +-- emerging rank -> sharper varna and household patriarchy
|   `-- continuity makes this gradual reweighting, not binary rupture
