## Rapid examiner traps

| Trap | Correction |
|---|---|
| All foreigners were one dynasty | Distinct, overlapping regional formations |
| Kanishka certainly began the Shaka era | 78 CE is the era; c. 127 CE is a major modern accession chronology |
| Coin distribution equals empire | Separate circulation, influence and direct rule |
| Kushana coins prove exclusive Buddhism | Plural royal pantheon; belief cannot be read mechanically |
| Gandhara was imported Greek art | Buddhist regional production with layered resources |
| Kushanas introduced the stirrup with certainty | Cavalry influence is safer; true-stirrup dating is contested |

## PYQ answer spine

**Define contact zone → distinguish dynasties → map routes → use three named coins/inscriptions/sites → explain political/economic/cultural mechanism → add source and regional limits → conclude with reciprocal but unequal synthesis.**
