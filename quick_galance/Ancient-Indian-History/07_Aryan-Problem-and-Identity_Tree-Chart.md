              QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The strongest reconstruction accepts the secure Indo-European
and Indo-Iranian linguistic relationship and evidence for
second-millennium BCE population movement, while rejecting
both a mass Aryan invasion that destroyed Harappa and claims
of a proven, wholly indigenous, unchanged Aryan population.
Early Vedic identity was produced historically through
migration, interaction, admixture, language shift,
acculturation and regional formation; no single text,
pottery complex, horse remain or genome can define it alone.
