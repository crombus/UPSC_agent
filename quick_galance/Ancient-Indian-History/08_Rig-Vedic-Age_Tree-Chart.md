                QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The Rig Vedic world was neither a purely nomadic egalitarian
society nor a mature territorial, caste-bound agrarian state.
It combined cattle-centred values with cultivation, crafts and
exchange; kin-based chiefship with collective assemblies;
emerging hierarchy with still-fluid social categories; and
sacrificial religion with rich cosmological questioning.
Its institutions supplied the baseline from which the more
settled, territorial, stratified and ritually elaborate Later
Vedic order developed through a gradual and uneven transition.
