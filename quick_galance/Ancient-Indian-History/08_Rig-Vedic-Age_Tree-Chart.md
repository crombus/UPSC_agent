THE RIG VEDIC AGE (EARLY VEDIC)
|
+-- 1. SOURCE + CHRONOLOGY CONTROL
|   +-- Rig Veda = ten-maṇḍala oral ritual-poetic saṃhitā
|   +-- Books 2-7 = conventionally older family books
|   +-- Books 1, 8, 9, 10 contain later/ritual/cosmological layers
|   +-- composition -> performance -> oral preservation -> recension
|   |   -> later manuscript; do not confuse these dates
|   +-- working older Rigvedic horizon: c. 1500/1200-1000 BCE
|   `-- source != census, law code, annual chronicle or neutral battle report
|
+-- 2. SAPTA-SINDHU + NORTH-WESTERN GEOGRAPHY
|   +-- Sindhu/Indus -> Indus
|   +-- Vitasta -> Jhelum | Asikni -> Chenab
|   +-- Parushni/Iravati -> Ravi | Vipasha -> Beas
|   +-- Shutudri -> Sutlej | Kubha -> Kabul
|   +-- Suvastu -> Swat | Krumu -> Kurram
|   +-- Gomati in this context -> Gomal, not automatically UP Gomti
|   +-- Sarasvati -> often Ghaggar-Hakra, identification debated
|   `-- river hymn = poetic textual geography;
|       palaeochannel, occupation and textual layer need separate dating
|
+-- 3. MIXED AGRO-PASTORAL ECONOMY
|   cattle wealth + pasture/mobility
|       ↕
|   cultivation + wells + draught oxen
|       ↕
|   crafts + gifts + warfare
|   +-- cattle: gomat wealth, prestige, traction, offerings and booty
|   +-- gaviṣṭi -> cattle-search/raid and conflict association
|   +-- field (kṣetra), plough (sīra/laṅgala), sowing/harvest/threshing
|   +-- barley (yava) = clearest cereal reference
|   +-- wells (avata) and channels/ditches (kulyā) = water management
|   |   -> not proof of large canal irrigation or agrarian bureaucracy
|   +-- oxen/bullocks -> ploughing and carts
|   `-- pastoral predominance = textual/elite emphasis, not pure pastoralism
|
+-- 4. CRAFTS, GIFTS + REDISTRIBUTION
|   +-- wood/chariot, leather, weaving/spinning, pottery, metal and weapons
|   +-- dāna -> chiefly gift and social relationship
|   +-- dakṣiṇā -> sacrificial fee to ritual specialists
|   +-- bali -> offering/tribute, not mature assessed land tax
|   +-- niṣka -> valuable metal object/ornament, not automatic coin
|   `-- gift/ritual chain:
|       chief -> priest -> deity/ritual -> prestige, protection, redistribution
|
+-- 5. KINSHIP + SOCIAL UNITS
|   kula -> grāma -> viś -> jana
|   +-- heuristic scale, not a rigid administrative ladder
|   +-- grāma can mean a mobile/fighting group, not only a village
|   +-- viś = wider people/clan grouping
|   +-- jana = broad kin-political community, not a modern nation
|   +-- household = production + kinship + ritual + alliance
|   `-- social differentiation:
|       chiefs/priest lineages/warriors/producers/artisans/dependants
|       -> not classless equality and not later caste fully formed
|
+-- 6. CHIEFTAINSHIP + ASSEMBLIES
|   +-- rājan = protector, war leader, ritual patron, wealth distributor
|   |   -> kin-based chief, not yet mature territorial emperor
|   +-- purohita -> priestly adviser and political support
|   +-- senānī -> war leadership
|   +-- grāmaṇī -> leader associated with grāma; context-sensitive
|   +-- sabhā -> deliberative/social/ritual or adjudicative functions
|   +-- samiti -> wider gathering/political support
|   +-- vidatha -> ritual, military, distributive and social activity
|   +-- gaṇa -> collective body/war-band
|   `-- assemblies show collective politics
|       -> not modern democracy, elections or equal adult participation
|
+-- 7. WARFARE + DĀŚARĀJÑA
|   cattle + pasture + water + routes + prestige
|       -> raids, alliances and chiefship conflict
|   +-- Battle of Ten Kings (Dāśarājña)
|   |   -> Rig Veda Book 7
|   |   -> Sudās of the Bharatas vs confederacy
|   |   -> Paruṣṇī/Ravi setting
|   `-- victory hymn preserves a political memory
|       -> not a neutral order of battle, fixed ethnic blocs or empire proof

+-- 8. DĀSA/DASYU + VARṆA CAUTION
|   +-- dāsa/dasyu can mark enemy, outsider, ritual rival,
|   |   linguistic/cultural outsider, subordinate or mythic adversary
|   +-- meanings vary by hymn, speaker, alliance and layer
|   |   -> not one biological race or archaeological culture
|   +-- Puruṣa Sūkta (Rig Veda 10.90):
|   |   Brahmana, Rajanya, Vaishya, Shudra
|   `-- Book 10 is generally later within the corpus
|       -> emerging fourfold ideology, not rigid later jati everywhere

+-- 9. HOUSEHOLD, GENDER + FAMILY
|   +-- patriarchy/patrilineal tendencies, son preference and kin authority
|   +-- household joins herding, cultivation, craft and ritual
|   +-- marriage creates household/alliance; elite polygyny possible
|   +-- female seer traditions: Ghosha, Apala, Lopamudra, Vishvavara
|   |   -> attribution and textual layer require caution
|   `-- female ritual/textual visibility qualifies inequality
|       -> does not establish gender equality or erase patriarchy

+-- 10. RELIGION, RTA + RITUAL
|   +-- Indra -> battle, storm, rain and victory
|   +-- Agni -> sacrificial mediator and domestic/ritual fire
|   +-- Varuna -> ṛta, cosmic/moral order and sovereignty
|   +-- Soma -> ritual drink, inspiration and deity
|   +-- Ashvins -> healing/rescue | Ushas -> dawn/renewal
|   +-- Maruts/Rudra -> storm/danger/healing | Vishnu -> early cosmic role
|   +-- yajña:
|   |   offering + correct speech/metre -> deity-human exchange
|   `-- no secure monumental temple or image-centred institution
|       defines the Early Vedic baseline

+-- 11. COSMOLOGY, FUNERARY LIFE + ORAL EDUCATION
|   +-- Nāsadīya Sūkta (10.129) -> creation speculation and epistemic doubt
|   +-- Puruṣa Sūkta -> cosmic-social scheme
|   +-- funerary hymns, ancestors, cremation and burial traditions vary
|   +-- memorisation, accent, metre, recitation and poet-priest lineages
|   |   -> specialised oral preservation, not universal schooling
|   `-- ritual-poetic speculation != completed Upanishadic philosophy

+-- 12. HORSE, CHARIOT + METAL
|   +-- aśva/ratha -> elite mobility, prestige and warfare
|   +-- vehicle -> wheel form -> draught animal -> function
|   +-- archaeological horse/chariot correlations belong mainly to Topic 07
|   +-- ayas in Early Vedic context -> metal, generally copper/bronze
|   `-- śyāma ayas/iron belongs to later trajectory;
|       textual prominence does not quantify all herds or tools

+-- 13. ARCHAEOLOGICAL CORRELATION BOUNDARY
|   +-- Late Harappan/Cemetery H -> possible continuity/interaction
|   +-- Swat/Gandhara Grave -> north-west frontier sequence
|   +-- OCP/copper hoards -> partial upper-Ganga association
|   +-- PGW -> mainly Later Vedic/early Iron Age comparison
|   `-- archaeology tests settlement, diet, tools and chronology
|       -> it does not identify language automatically

+-- 14. EARLY -> LATER VEDIC BASELINE
|   Early: north-west + cattle-centred mixed economy + kin chiefship
|      -> Later: Kuru-Panchala/upper Ganga + agrarian surplus
|      -> territorial kingship/stronger hierarchy/iron and elaborate ritual
|   `-- transition is gradual, regional and uneven;
|       older pastoralism, kinship and ritual did not simply vanish

+-- 15. OWNERSHIP BOUNDARIES
|   +-- Topic 08 owns Rig Veda, Early Vedic geography, economy,
|   |   chiefship, assemblies, social differentiation, religion and baseline
|   +-- Topic 07 owns Aryan origin, migration, genetics and identity debate
|   `-- Topic 09 owns complete Later Vedic transformation and 2024 change route

+-- 16. VERIFIED PYQ ROUTE
|   +-- 2023 GS-I Q11 (15 marks):
|   |   society/religion -> kinship, chiefship, assemblies, varṇa layer,
|   |   deities, ṛta, yajña, gender; continuity is not unchanged identity
|   +-- 2026 Prelims Q10 (provisional key):
|   |   wells/water lifting, axes/sickles, oxen and draught power
|   |   -> do not inflate into large canal irrigation
|   +-- 2026 Prelims Q13 (provisional key):
|   |   river pairs; trap Gomati/Gomal, Parushni/Ravi, Sarasvati qualification
|   +-- 2026 Prelims Q20 (provisional key):
|   |   kṣetra-patnī -> Atharvaveda; distinguish from kṣetra-pati
|   |   (Rig Veda 4.57); do not transfer the masculine route
|   `-- 2024 GS-I society/economy change -> Topic 09;
|       Topic 08 supplies the Early Vedic baseline only

`-- 17. MAINS SPINE + CLOSE-OPTION TRAPS
    Rig Veda as layered source -> geography -> mixed economy
      -> kinship/chiefship/assemblies -> warfare and Dāśarājña
      -> society/gender/varṇa -> religion/ṛta/yajña
      -> archaeology and Early-Later qualifier
    traps:
      Rig Veda = chronicle -> no, ritual-poetic source
      Early Vedic = pure pastoralism -> no, farming/wells/crafts present
      grāma = fixed village -> context-dependent
      rājan = territorial emperor -> kin-based chief
      sabhā/samiti = modern democracy -> participation uncertain/unequal
      bali = regular land tax -> offering/tribute
      four varnas = rigid all-India caste -> Book 10 emerging ideology
      ayas = always iron -> early metal generally copper/bronze
    verdict:
      an agro-pastoral, kin-ordered and ritually integrated north-western society
      with collective political forms, emerging stratification and regional transition
      rather than either pure nomadic equality or mature territorial state.
