                 QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The Harappan civilisation was a geographically extensive,
technically coordinated and regionally diverse urban-rural
system. Its cities reveal sophisticated planning, water
management, crafts and exchange, but the undeciphered script
prevents confident identification of rulers, language or creed.
Its end was a multi-causal process of deurbanisation and
localisation—not extinction—combining cultural continuity
with the disappearance of major urban institutions.
