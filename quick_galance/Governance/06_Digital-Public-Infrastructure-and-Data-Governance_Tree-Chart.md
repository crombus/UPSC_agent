                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Digital public infrastructure consists of reusable, interoperable digital rails
for identity, payments, documents, consented data exchange, health and commerce;
e-governance is a particular service built on those rails. Population-scale
infrastructure lowers transaction costs and enables innovation, but also
concentrates exclusion, privacy and cybersecurity risks. Legitimacy requires
lawful purpose, minimum data, meaningful consent where applicable, open
standards, accountable institutions, assisted alternatives, correction rights
and human review.
