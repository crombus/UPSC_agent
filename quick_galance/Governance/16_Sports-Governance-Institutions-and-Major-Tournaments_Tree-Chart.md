                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Sports governance distributes authority among government, autonomous Olympic
and sport bodies, federations, event organisers, anti-doping institutions,
tribunals and athletes. Technical autonomy protects sport from partisan control,
but monopoly authority over recognition, selection, eligibility, funding and
careers cannot mean immunity from law, audit, safeguarding or fair procedure.
India's 2025 statute changes the legal basis of governance, yet enactment,
commencement, rules, institutional constitution and operational jurisdiction
must be verified separately.

Sports bodies need autonomy to preserve technical expertise and international
