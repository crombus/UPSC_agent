                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Civil services translate democratic mandates into lawful, continuous and
competent administration. Their legitimacy rests on political neutrality,
professional permanence, candid advice, impartial implementation and
answerability for administrative conduct. Mission Karmayogi seeks to move
capacity building from generic, rule-centred training toward role-based
competencies and continuous learning. Training is only an input: credible
reform must improve post-job fit, workplace behaviour, service outcomes,
ethical judgment and citizens' trust.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The democratic civil service must be permanent without becoming inert, neutral
without becoming unresponsive, and expert without escaping political
accountability. Mission Karmayogi can improve this balance only if competency
maps influence postings, teams and workplace behaviour rather than merely
expanding digital course completion. Public trust is earned when professional
advice, lawful execution, stable tenure, ethical restraint and citizen-facing
performance reinforce one another.
