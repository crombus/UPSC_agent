                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Transparency makes records and reasons visible; answerability requires an
identified authority to explain; enforceability supplies correction, remedy or
sanction. RTI, proactive disclosure, social audit, grievance systems, vigilance,
Lokpal and CAG therefore perform different functions. Accountability fails when
information has no remedy, complaints are merely closed, community findings
lack follow-up, or citizens are routed to an institution without jurisdiction.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Accountability is a chain rather than an institution. RTI exposes records,
social audit tests them against lived reality, grievance systems correct
individual failure, and vigilance, audit and courts impose specialised
consequences. None can substitute for the others. Reform should prioritise
proactive records, correct routing, independent verification, reasoned closure
and mandatory follow-up so that transparency results in enforceable correction
rather than merely visible failure.
