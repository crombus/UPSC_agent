                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
India's environmental governance is a specialised institutional ecosystem, not a
single regulator. Ministries make and coordinate policy; statutory boards and
authorities exercise defined legal powers; state and local bodies implement;
scientific institutes generate evidence; enforcement bodies investigate; and
tribunals adjudicate. Similar subject matter never makes mandates interchangeable,
and coordination must preserve the legal actor accountable for each decision.
