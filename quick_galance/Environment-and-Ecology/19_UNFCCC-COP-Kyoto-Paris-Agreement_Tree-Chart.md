                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Global climate governance evolved from a framework convention, to Kyoto's
binding but narrower developed-country commitment architecture, to Paris's
universal but nationally determined cycle. Paris binds Parties to prepare,
communicate, maintain, report and progressively revisit contributions, while
substantive national ambition remains self-determined. Effectiveness therefore
depends on transparency, stocktaking, accounting integrity, finance, equity and
verified delivery rather than adoption of texts or announcements alone.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The Paris system gained durability and participation by replacing Kyoto's narrow
top-down architecture with nationally determined ambition embedded in a common
procedural cycle. Its legitimacy now depends on whether transparency and stocktake
produce stronger action, Article 6 preserves environmental integrity, and finance
and technology make differentiated ambition feasible. Adopted text matters, but
verified delivery remains the ultimate test.
