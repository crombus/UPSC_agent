                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Succession is the temporal reorganisation of a community after a new substrate
or disturbance; a biome is a large spatial ecological type shaped mainly by
temperature and precipitation but modified by soil, fire, herbivory and land
use. Restoration must match the starting substrate, successional stage and
native biome. Tree planting is not automatically ecological restoration.
