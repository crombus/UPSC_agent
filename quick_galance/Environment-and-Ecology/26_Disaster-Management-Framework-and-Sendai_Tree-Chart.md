                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A hazard becomes a disaster through exposure, vulnerability and insufficient
capacity. Disaster governance must therefore prevent new risk, reduce existing
risk, prepare for residual risk, respond to immediate needs and recover without
recreating vulnerability. India's domestic statutory architecture and Sendai's
global voluntary framework are aligned but legally distinct; resilience is proved
by reduced loss and functioning systems, not plans, warnings or assets alone.
