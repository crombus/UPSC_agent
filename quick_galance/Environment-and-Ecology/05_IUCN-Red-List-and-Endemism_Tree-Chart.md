                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The IUCN Red List is a global, criteria-based scientific assessment of
extinction risk; it is not a statute or automatic legal-protection schedule.
Endemism describes restricted natural distribution and compounds risk because
range loss leaves no geographic refuge. Effective conservation translates
scientific assessment into domestic law, habitat protection, threat reduction,
monitoring and recovery.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The Red List is valuable because it standardises extinction-risk evidence, but
assessment alone cannot conserve a species. India must translate dated global
and regional assessments into appropriate domestic protection, habitat
connectivity and threat-specific action. Endemic and Data Deficient taxa require
particular precaution because range restriction and information gaps can hide
irreversible risk.
