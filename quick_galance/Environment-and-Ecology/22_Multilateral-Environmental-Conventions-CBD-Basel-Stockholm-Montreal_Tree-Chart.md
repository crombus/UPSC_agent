                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Multilateral environmental agreements work when their mechanism fits the problem,
obligations are supported by finance and technology, and international decisions
translate into domestic enforcement and measured outcomes. Montreal demonstrates
the power of a bounded target, binding schedule and funded transition; CBD, Basel
and Stockholm show why diffuse drivers, trade incentives and substitution require
broader, adaptive and justice-sensitive governance.
