# Environment and Ecology 22 — Multilateral Environmental Conventions

> **Quick-glance status:** verified to 30 September 2026

```text
MULTILATERAL ENVIRONMENTAL CONVENTIONS
│
├── MASTER RULE: match environmental object → legal mechanism
│   ├── treaty/adoption ≠ party status
│   ├── parent convention ≠ protocol/amendment acceptance
│   ├── proposal ≠ scientific recommendation ≠ COP decision
│   └── international target ≠ domestic law ≠ measured outcome
│
├── 1. CBD FAMILY — BIODIVERSITY
│   ├── CBD, 1992: three cumulative objectives
│   │   ├── conservation
│   │   ├── sustainable use
│   │   └── fair/equitable benefit-sharing
│   ├── Cartagena Protocol, 2000
│   │   ├── living modified organisms + biosafety
│   │   ├── advance informed agreement for specified movements
│   │   └── trap: not ABS
│   ├── Nagoya Protocol, 2010
│   │   ├── access → prior informed consent where required
│   │   ├── mutually agreed terms → utilisation
│   │   └── monetary/non-monetary benefits + compliance
│   ├── KMGBF, CBD COP15, 2022
│   │   ├── 4 goals for 2050
│   │   │   ├── A: ecosystems, species, genetic diversity
│   │   │   ├── B: sustainable use/nature's contributions
│   │   │   ├── C: benefit-sharing, including DSI
│   │   │   └── D: finance, capacity, technology, cooperation
│   │   ├── 23 targets for 2030
│   │   └── Target 3 “30x30”
│   │       └── area + effectiveness + representation
│   │           + connectivity + equity + rights
│   ├── COP16
│   │   ├── Cali 2024
│   │   │   ├── DSI multilateral mechanism → Cali Fund
│   │   │   ├── permanent Article 8(j) subsidiary body
│   │   │   └── session suspended after loss of quorum
│   │   └── resumed Rome, 27 Feb 2025
│   │       └── finance + monitoring/review + indicators completed
│   └── INDIA
│       ├── NBSAP 2024–2030
│       ├── Biological Diversity Act
│       └── NBA → SBB → BMC
│           └── PYQ 2023 Q79: BMCs support Nagoya/ABS
│
├── 2. BASEL — TRANSBOUNDARY WASTE
│   ├── Basel Convention, 1989
│   │   ├── minimise waste generation/movement
│   │   ├── notification → written consent → tracked movement
│   │   ├── environmentally sound management
│   │   └── illegal traffic/return responsibility
│   ├── Ban Amendment
│   │   ├── in force globally: 5 Dec 2019
│   │   └── Annex VII → non-Annex VII covered exports prohibited
│   ├── India boundary
│   │   └── Basel party; not listed as accepting Ban Amendment
│   ├── plastic-waste amendments
│   │   ├── effective 1 Jan 2021
│   │   └── most mixed/contaminated streams → stricter PIC control
│   └── trap: original Basel ≠ ban on all waste trade
│
├── 3. BRS DISTINCTION
│   ├── Basel → waste movement/disposal
│   ├── Rotterdam → PIC for listed hazardous chemicals/pesticides
│   └── Stockholm → listed persistent organic pollutants
│       └── coordinated/back-to-back COPs ≠ one legal instrument
│
├── 4. STOCKHOLM — POPs
│   ├── POP identity
│   │   └── persistence + bioaccumulation
│   │       + long-range transport + adverse effects
│   ├── annexes
│   │   ├── A: elimination
│   │   ├── B: restriction → DDT/vector-control route
│   │   └── C: unintentional releases → dioxins/furans
│   ├── listing pipeline
│   │   └── proposal → Annex D screen → Annex E risk profile
│   │       → Annex F management evaluation → POPRC recommendation
│   │       → COP decision → party-specific legal effect
│   ├── India: ratified 13 Jan 2006
│   └── traps
│       ├── toxic chemical ≠ automatically a listed POP
│       ├── POPRC recommendation ≠ COP listing
│       └── mercury → Minamata, not Stockholm
│           └── PYQ 2023 Q59: mining + coal-source boundary
│
├── 5. VIENNA–MONTREAL–KIGALI
│   ├── Vienna Convention, 1985
│   │   └── ozone-layer framework
│   ├── Montreal Protocol, 1987
│   │   ├── binding production/consumption schedules
│   │   ├── reporting + licensing + trade controls
│   │   ├── differentiated timetables
│   │   └── Multilateral Fund → eligible incremental costs
│   ├── name trap
│   │   └── aviation “Montreal Convention” ≠ ozone Montreal Protocol
│   ├── success logic
│   │   └── bounded list + metrics + schedules + finance
│   │       + science/adjustment + domestic capacity
│   ├── Kigali Amendment, 2016
│   │   ├── HFC phase-down in CO2-equivalent terms
│   │   └── HFCs: high-GWP, not ozone-depleting
│   ├── India
│   │   ├── ratification deposited 27 Sep 2021
│   │   ├── baseline: HFC 2024–26 average + HCFC component
│   │   ├── freeze: 2028
│   │   └── 15% of baseline from 2047
│   └── PYQ 2023 Q20
│       └── HFCs: aerosols + foam + fire suppression; not lubricants
│
├── 6. ADVANCED COMPARISON
│   ├── CBD: diffuse drivers → targets need mainstreaming/finance
│   ├── Basel: PIC vulnerable to misclassification/illegal traffic
│   ├── Stockholm: listing lag + regrettable substitution
│   └── Montreal: transferable design lessons, not one-size template
│
├── PYQ ANSWER ROUTES
│   ├── 2018 GS-III Q17
│   │   └── India's biodiversity variation → Act → NBA/SBB/BMC/ABS
│   │       → implementation limits
│   └── 2026 GS-III Q8
│       └── KMGBF aim → Goals A–D → target clusters
│           → NBSAP/India institutions → finance/quality qualification
│
└── MAINS SPINE
    └── define object
        → identify exact instrument/status
        → explain mechanism
        → add finance/science/compliance
        → show India translation
        → test outcome
        → conclude: problem-fit + equity + capacity
```
