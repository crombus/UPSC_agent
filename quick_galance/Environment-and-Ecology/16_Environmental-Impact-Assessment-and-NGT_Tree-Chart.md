                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Environmental Impact Assessment is preventive project appraisal before an
environmentally significant commitment is made; the National Green Tribunal is
a specialised statutory adjudicatory forum for defined environmental disputes
and appeals. Their legitimacy depends on correct project classification, credible
baseline and alternatives analysis, meaningful participation, reasoned decisions,
enforceable conditions, post-clearance monitoring and remedies matched to
jurisdiction. Later paperwork cannot recreate the lost choice of prior appraisal.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
A credible development decision is not merely a fast clearance but a reasoned
choice made before irreversible commitment, with alternatives, affected voices
and uncertainty visible. The NGT supplies specialised correction and restitution,
but prevention remains superior to after-the-fact repair. Reform should remove
administrative duplication while preserving prior appraisal, meaningful
consultation, independent monitoring and enforceable judicial remedies.
