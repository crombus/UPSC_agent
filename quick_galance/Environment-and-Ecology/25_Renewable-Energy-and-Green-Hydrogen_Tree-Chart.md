                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Renewable electricity is a generated energy output; hydrogen is a manufactured
energy carrier. India's transition has moved beyond adding nameplate solar and
wind capacity toward integrating variable generation through transmission,
forecasting, flexibility and storage. Green hydrogen is most valuable where
direct electrification is difficult, and its climate claim depends on renewable-
electricity attributes, lifecycle boundaries, water, logistics and certification.
