India's climate architecture is layered: NAPCC is a domestic mission framework,
SAPCCs translate priorities to states, Panchamrit is a five-part political
announcement, the NDC is the formal Paris contribution, LT-LEDS is a long-term
strategic pathway, and BURs report backward-looking inventory and progress.

depends on dated sectoral delivery, adaptation, equity, finance and just transition.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
India's climate architecture is coherent only when each instrument is used for
its proper function: missions organise domestic action, NDCs communicate near-term
commitments, LT-LEDS frames the long transition and BURs report past performance.
Its credibility cannot be read from one installed-capacity milestone or net-zero
date; it depends on integrated power, transport, industry, land and adaptation
delivery, supported by equity-consistent finance and transparent accounting.
