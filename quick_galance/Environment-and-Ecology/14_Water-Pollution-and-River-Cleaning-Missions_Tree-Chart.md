                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
River rejuvenation requires more than a treatment asset or a mission label. India
must prevent pollution at source, connect sewage to continuously operated plants,
regulate industrial discharge, manage diffuse catchment loads and preserve
ecological flow. Progress should be reported as pollutant-load reduction and
site-, season- and indicator-specific river recovery, while maintaining clear
boundaries between regulation, infrastructure delivery and water-security policy.
