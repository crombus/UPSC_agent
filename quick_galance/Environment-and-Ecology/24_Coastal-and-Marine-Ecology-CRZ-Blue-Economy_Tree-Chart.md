                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The coast is a coupled land-sea system shaped by sediment, waves, tides, currents,
ecosystems, aquifers, settlements and economic use. CRZ is an administrative
regulatory framework, not an ecosystem or maritime entitlement. A sustainable
Blue Economy treats mangroves, seagrass, reefs, dunes, estuaries and fisheries as
productive natural capital, and tests every sector against cumulative ecological
limits, community access, hazard exposure, jurisdiction and monitored outcomes.
