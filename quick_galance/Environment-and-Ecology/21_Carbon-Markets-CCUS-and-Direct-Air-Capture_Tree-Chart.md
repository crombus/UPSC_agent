                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Carbon markets price defined mitigation outcomes; CCUS captures carbon dioxide
from concentrated sources; Direct Air Capture separates carbon dioxide from
ambient air. These instruments serve different functions and make valid climate
claims only when unit identity, baseline, additionality, MRV, ownership,
corresponding adjustment, lifecycle emissions, storage durability and reversal
risk are explicit. Removals complement, but never excuse delay in, direct cuts.
