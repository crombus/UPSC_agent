                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Emerging ethics addresses domains where technological and environmental change outruns
settled rules. AI can improve consistency and scale yet amplify biased data, obscure
reasons and diffuse accountability. Data use can serve welfare yet violate autonomy and
privacy. Development can strengthen security and livelihoods yet create irreversible
ecological and intergenerational harm. The ethical response is anticipatory: preserve
human accountability, rights, contestability, purpose limitation, proportionality and
precaution while assessing distribution, long-term effects and alternatives. Innovation
is legitimate only when its risks remain governable and its burdens are not hidden.
