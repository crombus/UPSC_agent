                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Transparency is the continuing visibility of public power; disclosure is one release;
information sharing is purpose-bound exchange; open data supports reuse; accountability
requires a forum able to judge and remedy. RTI transforms citizens from supplicants into
rights-holders, but access is not absolute. Ethical information governance begins with
reliable records and proactive disclosure, then applies narrow harm-based exemptions,
public-interest balancing, severability, privacy, security, reasoned refusal, appeal and
penalty. After the 2025 DPDP-related amendment to personal-information exemption, precise
statutory text, phased commencement and constitutional privacy remain essential.
