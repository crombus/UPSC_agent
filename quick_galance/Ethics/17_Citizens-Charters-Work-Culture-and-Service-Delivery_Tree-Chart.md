                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A Citizens' Charter is a public commitment about services, standards, responsibility
and grievance redress; it is not automatically a contract or statutory guarantee.
Credibility requires realistic time-quality-fairness standards, named owners, accessible
disclosure, monitoring, remedy and periodic review. Work culture is the lived pattern
of what an office rewards, tolerates and practises, often contradicting formal mission
statements. Citizen-centric reform redesigns the service journey before digitising it,
structures street-level discretion, uses complaints as learning data and keeps assisted
offline access and human appeal so convenience does not become exclusion.
