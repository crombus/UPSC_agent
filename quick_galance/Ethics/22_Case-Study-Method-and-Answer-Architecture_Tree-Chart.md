                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A GS-IV case-study answer is an auditable administrative decision, not an emotional
narrative or list of virtues. The complete architecture moves through verified facts,
stakeholders, binding constraints, at least three realistic options, explicit ethical
evaluation, a reasoned decision, implementation and communication, and residual-risk
mitigation. Legality and genuine conflict of interest normally operate as hard
thresholds; public interest, proportionality, transparency and compassion are weighted
checks. A mature answer decomposes nested dilemmas, steelmans the strongest objection,
uses lawful institutional channels and acknowledges what could still go wrong.
