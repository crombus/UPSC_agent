                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Vigilance exists to improve managerial efficiency and integrity, not to criminalise
risk-taking. An adverse outcome is not proof of corruption, just as official rank or
a sanction requirement is not immunity. The ethical system must guard symmetrically
against over-suspicion that demoralises honest officials and under-scrutiny that shields
the corrupt. The ARC's answer is calibrated screening: specific, credible and
verifiable allegations; confidential preliminary verification; competent evaluation;
technical expertise; prosecution only on strong evidence; transparent transfer policy;
and accountability for investigating agencies themselves.
