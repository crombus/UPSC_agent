                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Named cases are evidence units, not moral decorations. Each should be used with its
verified facts, causal mechanism, institutional response and residual limitation.
Manjunath and Dubey show courage without adequate protection; Mudgal and cash-for-
questions show that parliamentary self-discipline can punish exposed wrongdoing but
does not ensure prevention; MKSS and Parivartan show information becoming accountability
through community verification; Bhoomi versus CARD shows process redesign matters more
than computerisation; comparative models show that enforcement, prevention, public
education and institutional culture must be adapted rather than copied.
