                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Foundational values are not adjectives in an appraisal form; they are decision rules
tested under discretion, political pressure, uncertainty and citizen vulnerability.
Integrity keeps principle consistent when unobserved; impartiality applies transparent
criteria without personal bias; non-partisanship brackets party preference; objectivity
uses evidence and reason; dedication pursues public outcomes beyond minimum compliance;
empathy understands another's frame; compassion converts understanding into action; and
tolerance protects diversity. Each value needs an institutional safeguard, because
individual virtue without tenure, transparency, review and service design is fragile.
