                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Geography begins by fixing scale and relation. Astronomical processes explain
Earth's origin and solar environment; planetary shape and motions create the
coordinate, time and seasonal framework; latitude, longitude and extent locate
India; mountains, seas, neighbours and island chains convert position into
situation. Location enables and constrains but never determines outcomes:
climate requires circulation and relief, maritime importance requires ports and
capacity, and strategic proximity is not control. Every coordinate, boundary,
coastline and map label must identify its spatial category and data date.
