                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Rock is both material and historical record. Igneous, sedimentary and
metamorphic rocks are classified by genesis, texture and composition, and are
continually transformed through the rock cycle. At continental scale, plate
creation, movement and destruction open and close ocean basins, assemble
mountains and leave sutures, rifts and sedimentary basins. India's surface is
the modified expression of three interacting structural provinces: an ancient
composite Peninsular shield, an active Himalayan fold-thrust orogen and an
alluvium-filled foreland basin. Structure creates templates and resource
potential; climate, erosion, hydrology, technology and society determine actual
landforms, usable resources and risk.
