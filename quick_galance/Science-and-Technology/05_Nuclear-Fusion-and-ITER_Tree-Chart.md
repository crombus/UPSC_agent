                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Fusion is a research-stage effort to combine light nuclei in controlled plasma;
current nuclear electricity uses fission. Fusion readiness has separate scientific,
engineering and commercial thresholds. ITER targets burning-plasma physics and
integrated technology, has no turbine and will not generate electricity. India's
value today lies in plasma science, in-kind engineering and industrial learning,
while tritium breeding, neutron damage, exhaust, net electricity and cost remain.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Fusion's promise is real but conditional on solving an integrated plant, not just
heating plasma. ITER can validate burning-plasma physics and major technologies;
Indian participation already builds cryogenic, RF, vacuum and manufacturing depth.
Yet tritium self-sufficiency, neutron-resistant materials, heat exhaust, net
electricity and competitive operation keep fusion a long-horizon option rather
than an immediate substitute for present energy systems.
