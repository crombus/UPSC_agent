                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Vaccines present an antigen or its instructions so the recipient develops active
immunity and memory. Monoclonal antibodies are ready-made targeted proteins that
provide passive prophylactic or therapeutic action, while antivirals inhibit pathogen
replication through pharmacological mechanisms. Biopharma capability requires
discovery, clinical evidence, process-controlled manufacturing, regulatory approval,
batch release, pharmacovigilance, cold-chain integrity and equitable access. These
stages and product classes must never be merged.
