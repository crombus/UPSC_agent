                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Life is organised across molecules, cells, tissues, organs, organisms, populations
and ecosystems. Structure enables function, and regulation maintains homeostasis.
Genes influence phenotype through expression, but environment and development also
matter. Disease control depends on pathogen biology, host response, transmission,
surveillance and public systems. A detected molecule, named microbe or immune response
does not by itself establish viable infection, causation, severity or treatment.
