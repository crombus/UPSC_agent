                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Chemistry connects atomic structure and bonding to material properties, reactions,
industrial processes and environmental effects. A chemical name alone does not
establish concentration, hazard, usefulness or sustainability: dose, physical form,
reaction pathway, energy, by-products, exposure and disposal matter. UPSC-relevant
reasoning moves from atom or molecule to process, application and lifecycle while
preserving units, oxidation state, pH scale and equilibrium conditions.
