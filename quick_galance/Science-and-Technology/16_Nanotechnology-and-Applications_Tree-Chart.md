                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Nanotechnology deliberately studies, engineers and applies matter at roughly
1-100 nanometres, where increased surface-area-to-volume ratio, quantum confinement
and nanoscale structure can alter optical, electrical, magnetic, mechanical,
catalytic and biological behaviour. "Nano" is a scale and property description,
not proof of usefulness or safety. Translation requires reproducible synthesis,
characterisation, formulation, standards, field or clinical evidence, lifecycle
assessment and material-specific toxicology.
