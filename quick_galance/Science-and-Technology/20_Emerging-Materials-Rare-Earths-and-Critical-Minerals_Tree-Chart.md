                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A material's technological value follows from composition, bonding, structure,
microstructure, defects and processing. A mineral occurrence is not a secure
technology supply chain: resources must become reserves, mines, refined materials,
alloys, components and recyclable products. Rare earth elements are a defined
17-element chemical group; critical minerals are a periodically reassessed policy
category based on importance and supply risk. Ore ownership alone does not create
magnet, battery, semiconductor or defence autonomy.
