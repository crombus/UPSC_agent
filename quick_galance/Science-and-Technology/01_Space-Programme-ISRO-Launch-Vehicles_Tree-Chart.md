                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Sovereign access to space is a system, not merely a rocket. It requires mission-
matched launch vehicles, propulsion, manufacturing quality, integration, pads,
range safety, ground control, liability and reliable cadence. India's post-2023
architecture separates public R&D, private authorisation and commercial execution;
future vehicles, infrastructure approvals and technology transfers must never be
reported as flown, commissioned or commercially proven capabilities.
