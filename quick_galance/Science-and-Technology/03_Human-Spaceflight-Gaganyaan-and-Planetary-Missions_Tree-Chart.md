                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Human spaceflight is governed by crew survival, not ordinary mission success.
It requires a human-rated launcher, habitable spacecraft, abort coverage, life
support, re-entry and recovery validated through a staged qualification ladder.
Lunar, solar, planetary, station and planetary-defence missions follow different
capability chains. Approved, tested, launched, operational, completed, partial,
failed and concluded statuses must never be substituted.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
India's crewed and deep-space programme is a sequence of safety and capability
gates, not a collection of headlines. Tests and international experience reduce
risk, while lunar landing, solar observation and approved sample-return or station
plans build parallel strengths. Credibility requires conservative crew-safety
decisions and exact status language for every mission.
