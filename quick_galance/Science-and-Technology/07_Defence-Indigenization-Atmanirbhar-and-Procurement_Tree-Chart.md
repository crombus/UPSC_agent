                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Defence self-reliance is not the final assembly of imported kits. It is sustained
Indian control over design, critical technology, manufacturing, testing, upgrades,
repair, spares and lifecycle support. Procurement is the demand-and-contract chain;
indigenisation is the capability-building outcome. Category labels, indigenous
content and programme status must therefore be read separately.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Atmanirbharta in defence is achieved when Indian institutions can design, produce,
maintain and upgrade credible systems at scale. Procurement preference can create
demand, but durable autonomy also needs patient R&D, competitive industry, service
feedback, critical-technology control, transparent governance and global cooperation
chosen on terms that deepen rather than disguise domestic capability.
