                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Defence capability is a chain from service requirement to R&D, subsystem
integration, trials, production, induction, training and lifecycle support.
Missile classification depends on flight profile, propulsion, launch platform and
target role, not range alone. Public-domain status language must remain exact:
announced, developmental trial, user-evaluated, contracted, inducted and deployed
are separate, and sensitive performance or operational detail must not be invented.
