                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A prize question is an evidence-matching exercise, not personality trivia. Exact
category, award year, official recipient spelling, official citation, mechanism,
award-time affiliation and any birthplace or India link must align. Theory,
observation, experiment, discovery, invention, application, commercialisation and
social outcome are distinct. A Nobel Prize recognises the cited contribution; it
does not by itself prove that a later technology is scalable, affordable, approved
or deployed.
