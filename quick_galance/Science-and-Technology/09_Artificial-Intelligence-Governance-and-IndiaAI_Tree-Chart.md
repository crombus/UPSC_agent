                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
AI governance must follow the whole lifecycle: data, model design, training,
evaluation, deployment, monitoring, incident response and retirement. Artificial
intelligence, machine learning, deep learning and generative AI are nested but not
identical. A model can be technically capable yet unsafe, biased, opaque, insecure
or unsuitable for a high-impact decision. Innovation policy, voluntary guidance,
sector regulation and binding law must be labelled separately.
