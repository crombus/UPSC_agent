                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Semiconductor capability is a value chain, not a factory count. It begins with
materials, design intellectual property and EDA tools; proceeds through masks,
wafer fabrication, packaging, testing and reliability qualification; and ends in
boards, modules and finished systems. India may gain strategic depth through design,
mature-node fabrication, compound semiconductors and advanced packaging even before
leading-edge silicon. Approval, construction, inauguration, qualified output and
commercial-scale supply are separate evidence stages.
