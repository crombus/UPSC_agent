                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
India's three-stage programme is a resource-and-closed-fuel-cycle strategy, not
a timetable or proof that thorium is already commercial fuel. Stage 1 PHWRs use
natural uranium and create plutonium-bearing spent fuel; Stage 2 breeders expand
fissile inventory; Stage 3 aims at thorium-derived U-233. Reactor physics,
reprocessing, remote fabrication, safety, liability, safeguards and exact
milestone status must remain connected.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
India's nuclear strategy links present PHWR electricity to breeder learning and a
long-term thorium option, but every stage depends on the closed fuel cycle,
independent safety capacity and patient capital. PFBR criticality is a major bridge
milestone, not completion of Stage 2 or Stage 3. Expansion is credible only when
legal commencement, liability, waste and public-risk governance advance with reactors.
