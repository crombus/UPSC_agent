                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Biotechnology uses genes, cells, tissues, enzymes and organisms with engineering
and processing to create knowledge, services or products. It is broader than
genetic engineering. Discovery becomes public value only through validation,
scale-up, downstream processing, regulation, manufacturing, procurement and access.
DBT designs and supports biotechnology missions; BIRAC bridges research and
enterprise; product-specific regulators remain separate.
