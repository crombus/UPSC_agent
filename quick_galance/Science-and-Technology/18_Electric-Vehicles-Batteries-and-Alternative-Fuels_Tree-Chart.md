                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Clean mobility is a system transition across vehicle architecture, energy carrier,
charging or refuelling infrastructure, electricity or fuel production, manufacturing,
critical materials, safety and end-of-life recovery. A BEV, HEV, PHEV and FCEV all
use electric drive to different degrees, but obtain electricity differently. Zero
tailpipe emission is not zero lifecycle emission. Scheme approval, subsidy payment,
cell capacity, charger installation, vehicle sale and verified carbon reduction are
separate evidence stages.
