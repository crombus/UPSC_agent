                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A national research system is a pipeline, not a list of abbreviations. Policy
direction, competitive funding, laboratory performance, university capacity,
standards, mission execution, industrial translation and public outcomes require
different institutions. DST is a Department and horizontal policy anchor; ANRF is
a statutory apex research-funding and ecosystem body; CSIR is an autonomous
research-performing society under DSIR. SERB is a historical predecessor dissolved
under the ANRF Act, not a parallel current statutory body.
