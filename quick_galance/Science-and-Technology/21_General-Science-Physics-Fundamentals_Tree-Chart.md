                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Physics links measurable quantities through laws that apply only under stated
conditions. UPSC rewards correct units, direction, boundary conditions and
mechanism-to-application reasoning more than lengthy derivations. Force, energy,
heat, waves, light, electricity, fields, radiation and matter are related but not
interchangeable. A device claim must be traced from physical principle to component,
system, operating condition, measurement and limitation.
