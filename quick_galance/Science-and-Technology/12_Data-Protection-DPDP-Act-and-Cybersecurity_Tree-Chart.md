                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Data protection and cybersecurity overlap during a breach but answer different
questions. Data protection asks whether digital personal data is collected, used,
retained, shared and erased on a lawful basis with rights and accountability.
Cybersecurity protects systems, networks and critical infrastructure against
compromise, disruption and exfiltration. Enactment, notification, commencement,
institutional staffing, adjudication and enforcement are distinct legal-status
stages and must not be collapsed.
