                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Genetic engineering deliberately changes genetic material, but transgenic,
cisgenic, intragenic and gene-edited products are not identical biological or
regulatory categories. CRISPR is a targeting tool; the repair pathway and final
product determine the edit. India's governance separates contained research,
environmental release, food regulation, crop-variety release and post-release
monitoring. Precision can reduce some uncertainty but does not erase biosafety,
ecological, livelihood or ethical questions.
