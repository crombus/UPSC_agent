                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Babur's Indian conquest arose from failed Timurid ambitions in Central
Asia, a durable Kabul-Qandahar base, Punjab politics and Lodi faction.
His victories depended on an integrated battlefield system of carts,
matchlocks, cavalry manoeuvre and command; Panipat opened an empire but
Khanwa and Ghagra were required before even fragile consolidation.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Babur founded a durable Mughal claim by combining Timurid legitimacy,
Kabul-Punjab logistics, tactical adaptation and exploitation of Lodi
