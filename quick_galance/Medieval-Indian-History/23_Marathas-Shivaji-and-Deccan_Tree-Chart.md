                       THESIS
Shivaji built regional sovereignty by combining hill-fort geography, mobile
warfare, local landed networks, revenue reform, sea power and ritual
legitimation. Aurangzeb's failed settlement, followed by Bijapur/Golconda
annexation and Sambhaji's execution, decentralised Maratha resistance and
fused Deccan war with the Mughal jagirdari crisis.

                    INSTITUTIONS
ASHTAPRADHAN: Peshwa/Amatya/Sachiv/Mantri/Senapati/Sumant/Nyayadhish/
Panditrao — departmental officers each answerable to the ruler, adapted from
Deccani practice; NOT a modern cabinet or collective-sovereign executive.
REVENUE: measurement-based assessment (Annaji Datto, completed 1679) curbs
illegal exaction and over-mighty deshmukhs — supervises, does not abolish,
local rights; exact incidence is debated, avoid one universal percentage.
CHAUTH / SARDESHMUKHI: chauth = ~1/4 protection-cum-political claim;
sardeshmukhi = a distinct, additional 10% superior-deshmukh claim. Neither
is a religious tax nor random loot; in the later war kamaishdars turn chauth
into a parallel collection machinery inside Mughal jagirs.
ARMY / NAVY: mobile cavalry + Maval infantry + multi-officer fort command
for raids, intelligence, sieges, defence and set battles alike (mobility did
not exclude siege/defence: Salher, Panhala). Navy = gunboats, grabs/
gallivats, trading-supply craft and Sindhudurg — a bounded littoral force
checking Sidi/European presence, never Indian-Ocean blue-water command.

Purandar 1665 (fort/service settlement, not final peace) -> Agra 1666

ANNEXATION PARADOX: 1686 Bijapur + 1687 Golconda annexed -> territorial gain
but administrative overreach (new forts/nobles/soldiers displaced, long
routes, inflated jama) -> Aurangzeb won the Deccan states but could not turn
them into cheap, stable government.

SAMBHAJI EFFECT: 1689 execution removes the recognised head, not the
resistance -> commanders/forts act independently -> Rajaram to Gingee
(falls 1698, after his escape) -> Tarabai leads for Shivaji II (1700) ->
decentralised resilience, cautiously bridging toward later autonomous
commanders/confederate tendencies (full development remains Topic 25).
                              |
                              v
JAGIRDARI CRISIS MECHANISM: rising mansabdar/Deccani/Maratha claimant
numbers + rank inflation -> pressure on a limited productive-jagir pool
(paibaqi = unsettled/awaiting-assignment paper pool, distinct from realised
income) -> be-jagiri (assignment delay/shortage) + inflated jama vs low
actual hasil in war zones + chauth/private accommodation -> short tenures,
corruption, troop-maintenance failure and predatory extraction -> imperial
revenue authority erodes from below (fiscal + administrative + military +
social, not one variable).

                 HISTORIOGRAPHICAL TRAPS
"Guerrilla genius" alone ignores forts/revenue/diplomacy/navy/institutions.
"Aurangzeb lost due to religion" is too narrow; Deccan strategy and the
jagirdari mechanism are central. "Jagirdari crisis" is a strong structural
explanation but interacted with succession/ecology/resistance, not sole cause.
Ashtapradhan != cabinet. Chauth != sardeshmukhi. Deshmukhs curtailed, not
abolished. Hindavi Swarajya = bounded autonomy/sovereignty language, not
modern nationalism.
