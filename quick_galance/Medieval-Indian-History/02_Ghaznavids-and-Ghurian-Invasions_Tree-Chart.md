                 QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Mahmud breached and reorganized the north-western frontier while using
deep raids to sustain a wider Ghaznavid state; Muizz al-Din and his
commanders learned from reverses and transformed frontier advantage
into a staged conquest. Their success arose from mobility, logistics,
military-fiscal command and political opportunity, yet remained
contingent, resisted and dependent on long post-battle consolidation.
