MANSABDARI
Mansab = numerical service rank, not landed property
Late distinction: zat = personal rank/pay basis
                  sawar = cavalry obligation
Verification: dagh + chehra
        |
        v
JAGIR CHAIN
Jagir = revenue assignment for service remuneration
Khalisa = directly retained crown revenue
Jama = assessed demand on paper
Hasil = actual realization
Jagirdar != zamindar
        |
        v
REVENUE
Team reform: Muzaffar Khan Turbati + Todar Mal + Shah Mansur
Measurement: jarib, Ilahi gaz, bigha
Land categories: polaj, parauti, chachar, banjar
Zabt = larger measured-assessment frame
Dahsala = ten-year averaging technique within zabt
Alternatives remain: batai, kankut, nasaq
        |
        v
LOCAL POWER AND LIMITS
Zamindars and village intermediaries remain crucial
Paper demand != guaranteed realization
Regional conditions prevent total uniformity
        |
        v
BEST CONCLUSION
Akbar created a centralised but negotiated state:
capable of ranking, recording and redistributing,
yet dependent on local mediation and uneven implementation.
