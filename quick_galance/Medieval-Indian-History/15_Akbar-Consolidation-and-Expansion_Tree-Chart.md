## Answer line to remember
**Akbar expanded the empire by first stabilising a vulnerable throne, then
disciplining nobles, integrating selected Rajput houses, conquering key revenue
and route zones and building frontier depth, though Mewar and the Deccan kept
the empire uneven even in 1605.**
