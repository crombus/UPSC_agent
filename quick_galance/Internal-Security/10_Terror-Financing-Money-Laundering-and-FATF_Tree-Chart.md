                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Terror financing supplies value for a terrorist purpose and may use lawful or
unlawful funds. Money laundering disguises proceeds of crime so that they appear
legitimate. They overlap but are not the same offence or evidentiary chain. Effective
AML/CFT follows money from reporting and intelligence through predicate-offence
investigation, freezing or provisional attachment, adjudication, prosecution and
final confiscation. A transaction alert, designation, frozen account or attached
asset is not a conviction.

India's AML/CFT architecture can identify and disrupt suspicious financial flows,
