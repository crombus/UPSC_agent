                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Jammu and Kashmir is an internal-security theatre where an externally enabled,
deniable proxy strategy exploits local political, social and geographic
vulnerabilities. Security operations can disrupt infiltration, violence, finance and
facilitation, but durable conflict resolution also requires lawful investigation,
civilian protection, political participation, development, rehabilitation, credible
communication and accountability. LoC firing, attempted and successful infiltration,
local recruitment, hinterland incidents and public trust are separate metrics.
