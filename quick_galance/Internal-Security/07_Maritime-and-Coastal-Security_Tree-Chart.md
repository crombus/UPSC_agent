                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Maritime security protects sea lanes, maritime zones, ports, offshore assets,
shipping, trade and wider national interests. Coastal security is its near-shore
subset, protecting the coastline and maritime approaches against sea-origin threats.
Effective governance must connect maritime-domain awareness, lawful interdiction,
investigation, port and cyber resilience, fishermen participation and recovery.
Radar tracks and databases create awareness; vessels, trained personnel, clear
authority and evidence chains create response capability.

India's maritime security protects trade, energy, ports, offshore assets, island
