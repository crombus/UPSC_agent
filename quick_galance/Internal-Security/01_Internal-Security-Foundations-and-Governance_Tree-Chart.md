                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Internal security is the protection of sovereignty, territorial integrity,
constitutional order, public peace, rule of law, communal harmony and freedom from
fear within India's territory. Threats become harmful when an actor exploits a
vulnerability and exceeds available preventive or response capacity. Durable security
therefore combines political settlement, socio-economic inclusion, accountable
governance, professional policing, intelligence, border and cyber resilience, lawful
force and recovery. Security success is legitimate constitutional order, not merely
the absence of an incident.
