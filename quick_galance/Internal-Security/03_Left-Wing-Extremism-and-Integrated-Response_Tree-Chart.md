                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Left-Wing Extremism is a Maoist armed challenge seeking capture of State power,
not territorial secession. It grows where land and forest exclusion, extractive
economies, weak administration and social alienation meet organised coercion,
extortion and deliberate obstruction of development. Durable response is an
integrated, sequenced chain of lawful security, permanent civil administration,
rights and entitlements, infrastructure and livelihoods, surrender-rehabilitation,
inter-State coordination and trust restoration. Falling violence is not identical
to resolved grievances or irreversible peace.
