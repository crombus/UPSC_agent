                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
India's security architecture must convert information into lawful prevention,
response, investigation and prosecution while preserving State-police primacy,
clear command, civilian authority and constitutional rights. CAPFs, armed forces,
police, intelligence agencies, data-sharing platforms and investigators are not
interchangeable. Intelligence is assessed information, not admissible proof by
itself; a force deployment is not an investigation; an arrest is not a conviction.
Capability and accountability strengthen each other because public trust improves
reporting, evidence quality and institutional legitimacy.
