                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Social media and encrypted messaging are dual-use infrastructures. Their speed,
reach, participation and privacy support democratic communication, commerce,
journalism and safety, while the same properties can enable disinformation,
radicalisation, covert coordination, harassment and identity-conflict amplification.
The correct response is layered and proportionate: lawful content process, platform
due diligence, targeted investigation, trusted public communication, media literacy,
international cooperation and reviewable network measures. Blanket encryption
weakening or indefinite shutdowns reduce security and rights together.
