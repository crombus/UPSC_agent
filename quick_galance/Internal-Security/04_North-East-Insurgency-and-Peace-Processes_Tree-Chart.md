                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The North-East is not one insurgency. It contains State-, community-, faction- and
demand-specific conflicts shaped by historical isolation, identity and autonomy,
inter-ethnic boundaries, migration, uneven development, difficult terrain and
cross-border sanctuary or trafficking. Durable policy combines constitutional
accommodation, dialogue, ceasefire or Suspension of Operations, accountable security,
development and border governance. A ceasefire, framework agreement, settlement and
implemented peace are different rungs, and accommodation for one group can create
new concerns for another.
