                      CENTRAL THESIS
Define the real as origination + decay + persistence together, and everything else
follows: many-sidedness in the object, standpoint-relativity in the knower, the syat
operator in the sentence, and a material karma that explains why the knower is finite.
Strength = permanence and change equally real in one thing. Weakness = a formless soul
bound by matter, and higher knowledges that cannot be checked from outside.

                    QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Jainism is genuinely pluralistic and genuinely realistic, but its realism is QUALIFIED
realism: the aspects are objectively in the thing while finite judgement reaches them
only under a stated respect. Its ethics is derived rather than sentimental, since
non-violence follows from innumerable souls and from a karma that is literally matter.
Its costs are equally real -- the interaction of formless soul with subtle matter, the
external unverifiability of the higher knowledges, and the relocation of the
unconditioned to omniscience. Write: "realism without dogmatism", then state the cost.
