                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Later Wittgenstein abandons the search for one logical essence of language and redirects
philosophy toward the varied, norm-governed uses of expressions within language-games
and forms of life. Meaning is use for a large class of cases, not private mental
reference or arbitrary convention. Rule-following rests in trained practice rather than
an endless chain of interpretations. A logically private language fails because it
cannot distinguish correct application from merely seeming correct. Philosophy becomes
therapy: grammatical description dissolves confusions created when language goes idle.
