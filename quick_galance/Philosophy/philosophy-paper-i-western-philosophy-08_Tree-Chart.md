# Philosophy Optional — Paper I — Western Philosophy 08 — Later Wittgenstein

> **Quick-glance revision tree.** Concise and revision-oriented. It does not
> duplicate the complete learning session, the ASCII master flow diagram or the
> graphical at-a-glance package; it is the thirty-second refresher that carries
> the central thesis, the exact examinable data, the traps and the PYQ answer
> routes.
> **Syllabus, verbatim:** *Later Wittgenstein : Meaning and Use; Language-games; Critique of Private Language.*
> **Terminology and citation:** English concept first, source term immediately after in parentheses — *language-game (Sprachspiel)*, *form of life (Lebensform)*, *criterion (Kriterium)*, *symptom (Symptom)*, *avowal (Äußerung)*, *when language idles (wenn die Sprache feiert)*. Remarks are cited by **§ number**, which is stable across editions for Part I (§1–§693). Almost every text here is **posthumous**, and the dates are examinable.

QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
 The later Wittgenstein is best assessed by his TRIPLE REFUSAL - no single
 CRITERION, no explanatory THEORY, no LEGISLATION over ordinary use. The method
 dissolves a large and identifiable class of confusions; its price is a severe
 narrowing of what philosophy may attempt, and the standing difficulties -
 self-application, the normativity of use, and the absence of any theory of
 adjudication between practices - are conceded rather than answered.
