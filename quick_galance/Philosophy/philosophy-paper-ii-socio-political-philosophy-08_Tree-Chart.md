+-- SYLLABUS, VERBATIM
|   +-- "Development and Social Progress."
|   +-- TWO WORDS, NO SUB-HEADS. That is exactly why the clause is misread as a policy

+-- CENTRAL THESIS
|   +-- The clause asks ONE question: WHAT ENTITLES ANYONE TO CALL A SOCIAL CHANGE AN
|   |   IMPROVEMENT - AND WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THE CLAIM TO HOLD?
|   +-- PREREQUISITE: DEVELOPMENT IS A CONTESTED NORMATIVE CONCEPT. Until the stem's
|   |   assumed conception is NAMED, the question has no determinate answer.
|   +-- MASTER THESIS: GROWTH CAN FINANCE DEVELOPMENT, BUT ONLY CAPABILITIES, JUSTICE,
|   |   PARTICIPATION AND ECOLOGICAL LIMITS CONVERT MATERIAL CHANGE INTO SOCIAL PROGRESS.
|   +-- EVERY VERDICT HERE IS CONDITION-SPECIFYING: it names the CONVERSION CONDITIONS,
|       not a yes or a no.

+-- THE THREE CONTESTS THAT GENERATE EVERY OWNED PART
|   +-- METRIC: in which INFORMATIONAL SPACE is development judged? Marks lie in naming
|   |   what an indicator DISCLOSES and what it CONCEALS - not in ranking indices.
|   +-- AGENCY: is the person a BENEFICIARY of development or its AUTHOR? Information ->
|   |   consultation -> participation -> co-decision -> EMPOWERMENT is a ladder, not a
|   |   synonym list.
|   +-- LIMIT: what may development NOT do, whatever the gains? Irreversibility, cultural
|       destruction and foreclosed future options are LIMITS, not costs to be priced.
