|   +-- A. intensity -> truth ("it felt overwhelming, therefore it was real").
|   +-- B. naming -> meeting ("my tradition calls it God, therefore God was met").
|   +-- C. mediation -> fabrication ("it has a neural correlate, therefore it is nothing").

+-- II. JAMES - MARKS GRADED TWO-PLUS-TWO  [2022 PYQ]
|   +-- DEFINING: INEFFABILITY ("defies expression"; the "handiest" mark) + NOETIC QUALITY
|   |   ("states of insight into depths of truth unplumbed by the discursive intellect").
|   +-- USUAL: TRANSIENCY (brief; may recur and develop) + PASSIVITY (grasped and held by
|   |   a superior power, though preliminaries may facilitate onset).
|   +-- Scope: marks of MYSTICAL STATES, not of every religious experience.
|   +-- Authority for the SUBJECT only; none over outsiders -> the veridicality hinge.
|   +-- Payoff: transiency being only usual lets stable Indian jnana count as mystical.

|   +-- VARIETY (the second deliverable): healthy-mindedness | the sick soul | the divided

|   +-- Object: the "MORE", reached through the SUBCONSCIOUS - deliberately thin.

+-- III. OTTO AND SCHLEIERMACHER
|   +-- The HOLY = non-rational core (the NUMINOUS) + later rational/moral SCHEMATISATION.
|   +-- mysterium (wholly other, incommensurable) + tremendum (awefulness, majestas,
|   |   energy -> CREATURE-FEELING) + fascinans (attracts while it terrifies).
|   +-- NON-RATIONAL is NOT IRRATIONAL: not reducible to conceptual-moral judgement.
|   +-- Schleiermacher: FEELING OF ABSOLUTE DEPENDENCE - about the subject; the numinous

|   +-- Objection: psychology explains awe -> Reply: he describes INTENTIONAL STRUCTURE;
|   |   but description is not existence. Say both halves.
|   +-- Cross-cultural limit: India often organises the sacred by PURITY/AUSPICIOUSNESS

|   +-- STACE 1960: EXTROVERTIVE (the One shining THROUGH sensed multiplicity) and
|   |   INTROVERTIVE (all content excluded; unitary CONTENTLESS consciousness; the higher
|   |   type). Five shared marks: objectivity/reality, blessedness, holiness,
|   |   paradoxicality, ineffability. Move: separate EXPERIENCE from INTERPRETATION
|   |   -> perennialist COMMON CORE.

|   |   MONISTIC (kaivalya; Advaita) | THEISTIC (loving union PRESERVING creature-creator
|   |   distinction). NO common core, plus a ranking.
|   +-- Ranking criticised as CONFESSIONAL. Exam move: KEEP the classification, DROP the
|   |   ranking; what survives is that IDENTITY and RELATION differ in LOGICAL FORM.
|   +-- KATZ 1978: "there are no unmediated experiences" - tradition constitutes the

+-- IX. MYSTICAL EXPERIENCE AND REVELATION  [2023 PYQ]

+-- X. PRAYER AND WORSHIP  [2020 PYQ]
|   +-- PRAYER = ADDRESS (presupposes a responsive God). WORSHIP = ASCRIPTION OF SUPREME
|   |   WORTH (presupposes only a supremely valuable reality). ADORATION is the overlap.
|   +-- Kinds of prayer: A-C-T-S = adoration, confession, thanksgiving, supplication
|   |   (+ intercession, contemplative prayer).
|   +-- Petition problem: omniscient + perfectly good + immutable -> petition idle.
|   |   AQUINAS: obtain what God has ORDAINED SHALL BE OBTAINED THROUGH PRAYER - a genuine
|   |   SECONDARY CAUSE. STUMP 1979: asking PROTECTS THE RELATIONSHIP. Residue: justifies
|   |   petition's VALUE, not causal dependence.
|   +-- Worship problem: RACHELS 1971 - worship entails unconditional obedience; autonomy
|   |   forbids abdication; so nothing is worthy of worship. Replies: WORTH not blind
|   |   obedience | a perfectly good being's commands coincide with moral judgement |
|   |   the autonomy assumed is atomistic.
|   +-- Deciding Indian cases: JAIN vitaraga Tirthankara CANNOT RESPOND (caitya-vandana =
|   |   worship without answerable petition) | MIMAMSA apurva, deity as GRAMMATICAL DATIVE |
|   |   ADVAITA upasana efficacious at vyavaharika, TRANSCENDED at paramarthika |
|   |   BUDDHIST puja commemorative and dispositional.
|   +-- Placement: prayer = RELATIONAL organ; worship = EVALUATIVE and COMMUNAL organ.
|       A religion can survive the loss of petition; none survives the loss of worship.
