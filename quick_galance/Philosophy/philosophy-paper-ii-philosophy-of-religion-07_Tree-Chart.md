# Philosophy Optional — Paper II — Philosophy of Religion 07 — Religion without God

## Quick-Glance Tree Chart

```text
RELIGION WITHOUT GOD
|
+-- CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
|   +-- The clause owns the NECESSITY question - is a personal creator required for
|   |   religion? - NOT the existence question. Nothing here turns on whether God exists.
|   +-- Standing verdict: religion without God is possible; religion without a SACRED
|   |   ORDER is not. Creator-belief is SUFFICIENT for many religions, not NECESSARY.
|   +-- Every stem is decided by the DEFINITION OF RELIGION adopted in the first three
|       lines. State it, defend it in one line, apply it consistently.
|
+-- SCOPE / OWNERSHIP FIREWALL
|   +-- Owns: substantive / functional / multidimensional / family-resemblance definitions;
|   |   Buddhism, Jainism, Mimamsa, Samkhya-Yoga and the modern non-theistic cases; ritual,
|   |   sacred order, community and liberation without a creator; how far prayer, worship,
|   |   revelation, grace and providence require a deity; naturalism and fictionalism as
|   |   BOUNDARY cases.
|   +-- Not owned: full concepts of God (Notions of God); complete school doctrine (Paper I
|   |   owners); nature/object of experience (Religious Experience); faith and revelation
|   |   (Reason, Revelation and Faith); dependence of morality on religion (Religion and
|   |   Morality); humanism and complete language theories (their own owners).
|   +-- 2025 Q5(c) "Nietzsche's criticism of religion AND morality" is owned by RELIGION AND
|       MORALITY. Keep only the post-theistic bridge here.
|
+-- THE FIREWALL THAT DECIDES THE FIRST PARAGRAPH
|   +-- NON-THEISM = about the PATH: a creator is absent from diagnosis and prescription.
|   +-- ATHEISM = about EXISTENCE: the positive denial that God exists.
|   +-- AGNOSTICISM = about KNOWLEDGE: weak "not known" | strong "unknowable".
|   +-- INDIFFERENCE = the question does not arise. (Adjacent: anti-theism; practical atheism.)
|   +-- NON-THEISTIC != ATHEISTIC. Theravada Buddhism permits cosmological devas while denying
|       their CREATORHOOD and SALVIFIC ULTIMACY.
|
+-- SOURCE / ATTRIBUTION CAUTION
|   +-- Datable: Marx 1843-44 (Introduction, Critique of Hegel's Philosophy of Right) .
|   |   Huxley coins "agnostic" 1869 . Dewey, A Common Faith 1934 . Cupitt, Taking Leave of
|   |   God 1980 . Le Poidevin, Arguing for Atheism 1996 . Goodenough, The Sacred Depths of
|   |   Nature 1998 . Dworkin, Religion without God 2013.
|   +-- Cite by WORK, never by an invented year: Nietzsche (The Gay Science 125; On the
|   |   Genealogy of Morality); Feuerbach (The Essence of Christianity); Freud; Comte
|   |   (Religion of Humanity); Spinoza (Deus sive Natura); Ninian Smart (dimensions);
|   |   Julian Huxley; Crosby; Rue; Eshleman; Wettstein.
|   +-- Verified Indian anchors: Jaimini, Mimamsa-sutra 1.1.2 (codana-laksano 'rtho dharmah);
|   |   Kumarila, Slokavarttika against a creator; Brahmajala Sutta DN 1; Agganna Sutta DN 27.
|   +-- (?) DOXOGRAPHIC ONLY: the Brhaspati verses and the hedonist couplet survive solely in
|       opponents' works, chiefly Madhava's Sarvadarsanasamgraha; Purandara's acceptance of
|       ordinary inference is a REPORTED strand. Never present any of these as primary text.
|
+-- I. THE DEFINITION TEST
|   +-- SUBSTANTIVE (by object: God/gods/sacred beings) -> TOO NARROW; excludes Buddhism by
|   |   STIPULATION = question-begging.
|   +-- FUNCTIONAL (by what it does: ultimacy, practice, community, meaning, liberation)
|   |   -> TOO BROAD unrestricted; admits nationalism = empty category.
|   +-- MULTIDIMENSIONAL - Smart's seven: doctrinal | mythic | ethical | ritual |
|   |   experiential | social | material. Creator-belief is NOT on the essential list.
|   +-- FAMILY RESEMBLANCE: no single feature is shared by every member; "religion" names a
|   |   family of historically varied practices, not one essence.
|   +-- WORKING SUFFICIENT CLUSTER: a socially embodied, disciplined orientation to an
|       ULTIMATE/SACRED ORDER through doctrine, ritual, ethical formation, experience and
|       community, with a TRANSFORMATIVE/SOTERIOLOGICAL goal. TWO LOAD-BEARING CONDITIONS:
|       sacred order AND soteriological practice - both, never either.
|
+-- II. WHICH FUNCTIONS REQUIRE A DEITY  [the separability result]
|   +-- REQUIRE A PERSON: petitionary prayer (a responsive addressee is built in) |
|   |   propositional revelation FROM God (a speaker by definition) | grace and providence
|   |   in their personal-agency sense.
|   +-- DO NOT: worship/veneration | ritual efficacy (apurva; Buddhist and Jain discipline) |
|   |   moral motivation (compassion, ahimsa, karma, vows, insight) | liberation (nirvana,
|   |   Jain release, kaivalya).
|   +-- ANSWER MOVE: concede petition, grace and covenant. The concession identifies where
|       the theist's real case lies - DEVOTIONAL, not definitional.
|
+-- III. BUDDHISM  [2024 Q7(a), 20 marks]
|   +-- Diagnosis: Four Noble Truths - suffering (duhkha) | origin in craving and ignorance |
|   |   cessation | the path. Engine: DEPENDENT ORIGINATION (pratityasamutpada) + karma.
|   +-- The claim is REDUNDANCY, not refutation: a creator adds no explanatory and no
|   |   practical step. Presupposition to state: dependent causation suffices FOR THE PROBLEM
|   |   BUDDHISM ADDRESSES.
|   +-- Cluster discharged: doctrine | Eightfold Path and meditation | sila | experience |
|   |   SANGHA | NIRVANA | DHARMA as sacred order.
|   +-- Compulsory qualification: Dharma, Sangha and nirvana are NOT ONE-FOR-ONE SUBSTITUTES
|   |   for a personal creator.
|   +-- Devas and Brahma figures = impermanent beings INSIDE samsara (DN 1, DN 27).
|   |   Avyakata = SPECIFIC speculative issues, not the whole case. Poisoned arrow = urgency.
|   +-- Devotional Mahayana: Buddhas and bodhisattvas perform religious FUNCTIONS without
|       becoming an omnipotent creator. NON-THEISTIC DOES NOT MEAN DEVOTION-FREE.
|
+-- IV. JAINISM  [2018 route]
|   +-- MOTIVE argument: a perfect being has no unfulfilled desire creation could satisfy.
|   +-- EMBODIMENT argument: acting on matter needs a body; a body is made and limited.
|   |   Same family as Kumarila's Slokavarttika against a bodiless agent.
|   +-- Machinery: eternal jiva + ajiva | KARMIC MATTER binding and shed | vows and
|   |   asceticism | liberation as the soul's OWN achievement.
|   +-- Tirthankara TEACHES; siddha has COMPLETED. An ACHIEVED divinity, never a GOVERNING one.
|   +-- Theist: "vitaraga worship is really commemoration." Reply: vows, ritual calendar,
|       temple practice, asceticism and a soteriological goal are not commemoration.
|
+-- V. MIMAMSA  [2020 Q6(c) route - the sharpest counterexample]
|   +-- APAURUSEYA: the Veda is AUTHORLESS, so no speaker's reliability need be established.
|   |   NEVER call it "revelation" - that reinstates the speaker the school removed.
|   +-- SVATAH-PRAMANYA: injunctions are intrinsically valid. Word-referent relation ETERNAL
|   |   (Nyaya denies it as conventional - note the dispute).
|   +-- Jaimini, Mimamsa-sutra 1.1.2: codana-laksano 'rtho dharmah - dharma is known ONLY
|   |   from injunction, not from perception, inference or any deity's command.
|   +-- APURVA in five steps: injunction promises a result -> result is delayed -> the act is
|   |   momentary and perishes -> a perished cause cannot produce a later effect -> therefore
|   |   an unseen potency persists. STATUS: a POSTULATE (arthapatti), never perceived.
|   +-- KUMARILA: apurva = a POWER (sakti) in act/agent => consequential mechanism of DESERT.
|   |   PRABHAKARA: NIYOGA/KARYA, the sheer ought-to-be-done => a DEONTOLOGY of the injunction.
|   +-- Nyaya: an unconscious potency cannot SELECT the right fruit (karmadhyaksa needed).
|   |   Reply: the relation is LAW-LIKE, not deliberative; the objection begs the question.
|   +-- Mantra-deities = elements within the ritual structure, the dative of the oblation -
|   |   not agents who hear, choose and grant.
|   +-- (?) Later Mimamsa authors drift toward theism: the atheistic reading is of the
|       CLASSICAL system.
|
+-- VI. SAMKHYA AND YOGA  [2021 route]
|   +-- PRAKRTI (one, unconscious, active; three gunas) + PURUSA (plural, conscious, inactive)
|   |   -> evolution -> misidentification -> VIVEKA-KHYATI -> KAIVALYA (isolation, NOT the
|   |   annihilation of nature).
|   +-- Yoga's ISVARA is a SPECIAL SELF, not straightforwardly a creator. DO NOT read it back
|   |   into classical Samkhya - that single slip destroys the counterexample.
|   +-- Nyaya: unconscious principles cannot organise ENDS. Reply: observed regularity need
|       not be modelled on ARTEFACT PRODUCTION (milk seeming to nourish the calf).
|
+-- VII. CARVAKA - THE LIMITING CASE  [2025 Q5(a), 10 marks]
|   +-- PRATYAKSA alone is a pramana: a pramana must yield CERTAINTY; perception alone does;
|   |   every other candidate depends on a rule perception cannot certify.
|   +-- Ontology: the FOUR gross elements (bhutacatustaya); ETHER REJECTED as imperceptible;
|   |   purusarthas reduce to kama and artha.
|   +-- THE VYAPTI ATTACK - all four moves or the argument has not been given:
|   |   (1) not by perception - vyapti is UNIVERSAL, perception grasps particulars
|   |   (2) not by inference - regress or circularity
|   |   (3) not by testimony - itself not a pramana here
|   |   (4) UPADHI: an unnoticed conditioning factor can never be excluded (fire with smoke
|   |       only when the fuel is wet) => vyabhicara remains possible => merely PROBABLE.
|   +-- Application: God unperceived | consciousness EMERGENT (dehatmavada; the madasakti
|   |   analogy) => no survival, transmigration or liberation | karma and adrsta are props |
|   |   Vedic authority denied | order by SVABHAVA-VADA (thorn's sharpness, peacock's colour).
|   +-- Five replies: self-refutation | pragmatic | Nyaya's defence (bhuyodarsana,
|   |   vyabhicara-adarsana, tarka, samanya-laksana-pratyasatti) | parity (perception errs
|   |   too) | (?) the reported PURANDARA strand admitting laukika inference while denying it
|   |   for the atindriya.
|   +-- Two rejoinders to self-refutation: the PRASANGA (dialectical) reading; the moderate
|   |   Purandara thesis.
|   +-- CLASSIFICATION: NOT a religion without God - no soteriology, no sacred, no community
|   |   of practice, no ultimate concern. Buddhism/Jainism/Mimamsa survive the LOSS OF GOD;
|   |   Carvaka closes the EPISTEMIC ROUTE to any supersensible object => NO religion at all.
|   +-- Verdict formula: the critique succeeds exactly as far as its epistemology does -
|       strong thesis self-refuting and unliveable; MODERATE thesis unrefuted and fatal to
|       every anumana-based natural theology, including Udayana's.
|
+-- VIII. WESTERN CRITIQUES AND POST-THEISM
|   +-- FEUERBACH: "theology is anthropology" - God is the PROJECTION of humanity's idealised
|   |   essence (The Essence of Christianity).
|   +-- MARX 1843-44: religion is "the OPIUM OF THE PEOPLE" - consolation masking exploitation;
|   |   quote the fuller sentence, which is more sympathetic than the slogan.
|   +-- FREUD: religion as ILLUSION - wish-fulfilment and cosmic father-projection.
|   +-- COMTE: the RELIGION OF HUMANITY - devotion to human values without a deity; the bridge
|   |   to secular humanism (routed onward to the Humanism/Secularism owner).
|   +-- SHARED LIMIT: these are GENETIC explanations. ORIGIN DOES NOT SETTLE TRUTH. What they
|   |   do establish: belief is not SELF-AUTHENTICATING.
|   +-- NIETZSCHE: "God is dead" = collapse of the Christian-metaphysical horizon and the
|   |   danger of NIHILISM - a CULTURAL DIAGNOSIS, not a proof and not an empirical event.
|   |   Four steps: truthfulness erodes belief -> values depended on the framework -> collapse
|   |   creates nihilism -> revaluation and self-creation. Example: RESSENTIMENT.
|   +-- Comparative point: a horizon LOST is not a horizon NEVER HAD. Post-theism != non-theism.
|
+-- IX. MODERN NON-THEISTIC RELIGION  [promoted to Core, 15 Aug 2026]
|   +-- RELIGIOUS NATURALISM: ultimacy and reverence located WITHIN NATURE.
|   |   DEWEY, A Common Faith 1934 - "religion" (institution) vs "THE RELIGIOUS" (a quality of
|   |   experience unifying the self toward inclusive ideal ends); "God" = the ACTIVE RELATION
|   |   BETWEEN THE IDEAL AND THE ACTUAL, not a being.
|   |   DWORKIN, Religion without God 2013 - "RELIGIOUS ATHEISM": objective intrinsic meaning
|   |   of human life + the sublimity of the universe; VALUE REALISM, not theism, is the core.
|   |   Also: Julian Huxley; Goodenough 1998; Crosby and Rue; Spinoza as ancestor.
|   +-- RELIGIOUS FICTIONALISM: the claims are not ASSERTED, yet the practice is accepted.
|   |   Cupitt 1980 (non-realism, "Sea of Faith"); Le Poidevin 1996; Eshleman; Wettstein.
|   +-- THREE-WAY DISCRIMINATION: ERROR THEORY = false, so stop | NON-COGNITIVISM = never had
|   |   truth-conditions | FICTIONALISM = grants them, declines to assert, stays in the practice.
|   +-- Objections: insincerity | motivational hollowing | the internal objection (believers
|   |   reject the description).
|   +-- INDIAN CONTROL - do not omit: Advaita's vyavaharika worship is efficacious and is
|   |   SUBLATED, not falsified, at the paramarthika level; Buddhism's two truths (samvrti/
|   |   paramartha) permit serious conventional practice. SUBLATION IS NOT FALSIFICATION.
|   |   Indian systems offer GRADED TRUTH where Western fictionalism offers PRETENCE.
|   +-- BOUNDED CAUTION: civil religion and secular spirituality are BOUNDARY TESTS, not
|       owners; without a transformative discipline the category becomes too broad.
|
+-- X. THE THEIST'S DENIAL AND THE REBUTTAL LEDGER  [2019 Q6(c) - construct it FIRST]
|   +-- 1 DEFINITIONAL (category error) -> Smart's dimensions; stipulation is question-begging.
|   +-- 2 DEVOTIONAL (worship needs a recipient) -> siddha as ACHIEVED, not governing.
|   +-- 3 MORAL (obligation needs a commander) -> compassion, ahimsa, karma, vows, insight.
|   +-- 4 COSMIC ORDER (Nyaya's Isvara as karmadhyaksa) -> apauruseya Veda + apurva; law-like
|   |   is not deliberative.
|   +-- 5 SOTERIOLOGICAL (grace needs a giver) -> Buddhism's complete diagnosis-and-path.
|   +-- THE ASSESSMENT THAT SCORES: the strongest SURVIVING theistic point is WORSHIP/GRACE,
|       not the definition - and it costs the non-theist real devotional structure.
|
+-- XI. FOUR STANDING CRITICISMS
|   +-- DEFINITION: religion necessarily means relation to God -> tradition-specific; cannot
|   |   accommodate established non-theistic religions.
|   +-- HIDDEN-GOD: dharma/nirvana/humanity merely substitute for God -> functional similarity
|   |   does not establish ONTOLOGICAL IDENTITY.
|   +-- MOTIVATION: without divine reward, discipline weakens -> compassion, insight, karma,
|   |   communal vows.
|   +-- NIHILISM: removing God removes objective value -> Buddhist ethics, Jain vows, humanist
|       normativity; their objectivity still requires argument.
|
+-- XII. PYQ ROUTES, DIRECTIVE DECODER AND VERDICT LADDER

|   +-- (2022 is the only year 2018-2025 with no primary-owned part in this clause.)
|   +-- LADDER: WEAK ~40% no definition, "atheistic" loose | AVG ~50% theist's case missing,
|   |   Mimamsa missing | GOOD ~60% functionalism unpoliced, devotional Mahayana ignored |
|   |   STRONG ~65-70% naturalism/fictionalism absent, Carvaka misclassified | TOP ~70%+ all

+-- XIII. TOP-SCRIPT DISCRIMINATIONS (the four that separate a 70%+ script)
|   +-- non-theistic != atheistic
|   +-- apauruseya != revelation
|   +-- sublation != falsification (Advaita is not fictionalism)
|   +-- Carvaka is the LIMITING CASE, not an instance

+-- QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
    +-- Religion without God is not merely conceivable but ACTUAL: Mimamsa secures authority,
    |   efficacy and desert without a deity, and Buddhism supplies a complete diagnosis-and-
    |   path structure.
    +-- God remains a prerequisite for SOME religious goods - petition, grace, covenant - so
    |   the theist's strongest argument is DEVOTIONAL, not definitional, and should be met on
    |   that ground rather than dismissed.
    +-- What is NOT possible is religion without a SACRED ORDER - which is why Carvaka ends in
```
