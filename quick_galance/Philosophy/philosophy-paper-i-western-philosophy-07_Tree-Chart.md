QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
Logical positivism proposed a SINGLE TEST to separate what can be said from
what only looks as though it were said, and the test failed in FOUR DIFFERENT
WAYS - two of them discovered by positivists themselves, in the strong/weak
fork and in the protocol dispute. What survives is not the doctrine but a
DISCIPLINE: Schlick's difference-argument as a heuristic of significance, the
demand for EXPLICIT RULES rather than obscurity, the permanent separation of
criteria of MEANING, of TRUTH and of SCIENTIFIC STATUS, and an agenda -
confirmation, demarcation, holism, analyticity - that is simply the list of
problems the Circle could not solve. STATE THE ACHIEVEMENT AND ITS MATCHING
COST IN THE SAME SENTENCE.
