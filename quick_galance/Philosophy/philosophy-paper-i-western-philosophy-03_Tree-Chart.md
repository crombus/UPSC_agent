CENTRAL THESIS
Empiricism is a claim about the ORIGIN OF CONTENT, not hostility to reason:
every simple cognitive content must trace to experience - Locke's outer
SENSATION and inner REFLECTION, Hume's IMPRESSIONS - while the mind's
operations of comparing, combining and abstracting are never denied. The
three systems are one rule enforced with rising severity, so the movement
from represented matter to no matter to no substance at all measures
CONSISTENCY, not decline.

## Memory chain — say it in fifteen seconds

One rule about the origin of content, applied three times. Locke keeps matter he
cannot inspect; Berkeley deletes matter and keeps spirits and God; Hume deletes
substance, the simple self and objective necessity, and keeps perceptions, custom
and natural belief. Necessity moves from the object to the observer. Kant enters
only where a question routes him — apperception for the self, the Second Analogy
for causation.
