+-- VI. EQUALITY OF WHAT?

|   |   resources yield different real freedoms because conversion differs with

+-- QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
    +-- Liberty and equality are indispensable but each is indeterminate about which
        liberties and which equalities matter, so neither can adjudicate their own
        conflict. Justice supplies the ordering criteria - desert, fairness,
        entitlement, need, capability, rectification and public reason - and assigns
        each criterion to its proper sphere. Because rival theories of justice order
        the same ideals differently, the defensible position is not that justice
        harmonises everything, but that the ordering criterion must be made explicit
        and publicly defensible rather than assumed.
