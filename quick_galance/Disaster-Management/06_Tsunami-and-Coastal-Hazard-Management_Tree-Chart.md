# Disaster Management 06 - Tsunami and Coastal Hazard Management

## Quick-Glance Revision Chart

```text
SOURCE -> DISPLACEMENT -> PROPAGATION -> SHOALING
-> RUN-UP / INUNDATION / RETURN FLOW -> LOCAL CONSEQUENCE

SEISMIC ANALYSIS -> MODEL -> BPR / TIDE GAUGE
-> ITEWC ADVISORY -> LOCAL EVACUATION -> ALL-CLEAR

-> RESPONSE -> LIVELIHOOD-SENSITIVE RECOVERY -> UPDATED READINESS
```

### Source and coast

- Triggers: earthquake, landslide, volcanic collapse and rare impact.
- Tsunamigenic conditions require rapid water displacement; magnitude alone is insufficient.
- Deep water: fast, long waves with low visible amplitude.
- Shallow water: slowing, wavelength contraction, height/current growth and shoaling.
- Coastal geometry, bathymetry, elevation and channels make impact site-specific.
- India: Andaman-Sumatra eastern source and Makran western source.

### Warning architecture

| Stage | Instrument or institution |
|---|---|
| Source detection | National/international seismic data |
| Scenario forecast | Numerical model including TUNAMI-N2 |
| Open-ocean confirmation | Bottom Pressure Recorders |
| Coastal observation | Tide gauges |
| Technical advisory | ITEWC at INCOIS under UNESCO-IOC IOTWMS |
| Protective decision | State, district and local authorities |

ITEWC has operated since 15 October 2007. Keep about-10-minute earthquake
detection distinct from about-20-minute advisory dissemination.

### Evacuation and readiness

- Natural signs: strong/long shaking, unusual sea movement or ocean roar.
- Near-source rule: self-evacuate; do not approach the coast to observe.
- Horizontal evacuation where safe ground is reachable.
- Vertical evacuation only in engineered, accessible and designated refuges.
- First wave need not be largest; return only after official all-clear.
- Tsunami Ready is renewable community recognition, not national certification.
- Venkatraipur and Noliasahi, Odisha: first Indian recognitions, 7 August 2020.

### Protection and recovery

- CRZ 2019 categories plus inundation maps guide risk-sensitive siting.
- Engineered: shelters, retrofits, walls, breakwaters and barriers where justified.
- Ecosystem: dunes, mangroves, forests, reefs, wetlands and bio-shields.
- 2004 lesson: heavier damage where dunes were mined.
- Protect ports, fisheries, tourism, utilities, islands and hazardous cargo systems.
- Build Back Better must restore services and livelihoods without recreating exposure.

### PYQ scope

- 2025 GS-I tsunami formation/consequences: use mechanism plus warning/evacuation application.
- 2023 GS-I coastline potential: use the coastal-preparedness half only.
- 2024 GS-III resilience: apply warning redundancy, land-use discipline,
  buffers, evacuation and renewable community readiness.
- No verified 2026 direct route to this topic.
