# Disaster Management 02 - Indian Legal and Institutional Architecture

## Quick-Glance Revision Chart

### Core architecture

```text
DM Act, 2005 as amended
  |
  +-- National: NDMA (policy) + NEC (coordination)
  |              + NCMC/HLC + NDRF force + NIDM
  |
  +-- State: SDMA (policy/plan) + SEC (implementation)
  |           + optional UDMA + optional specialist State force
  |
  +-- District: DDMA + District Plan
  |
  +-- Local: Panchayats, municipalities and other local authorities
```

### Chair and function table

| Body | Chair | Exam function |
|---|---|---|
| NDMA | Prime Minister | National policy, plans and guidelines. |
| NEC | Union Home Secretary | National coordination, monitoring and implementation. |
| SDMA | Chief Minister | State policy and State Plan approval. |
| SEC | Chief Secretary | State coordination and implementation monitoring. |
| DDMA | District Collector/Magistrate | District Plan/coordination; elected representative Co-Chair |
| NCMC | Cabinet Secretary | Major disasters with serious or national ramifications. |

### Current-law map

- Amendment Act 2025: assented 29 March; commenced 9 April 2025.
- Section 8A: NCMC; section 8B: HLC.
- Section 41A: State may notify UDMA; Delhi and Chandigarh excluded.
- Section 44A: State may constitute a specialist response force.
- Sections 2(da), 6(2)(v), 18(2)(p): disaster-database definition and duties.
- Sections 11 and 23: periodic National and State Plan review/update.
- Sections 46-48: national, state and district response/mitigation fund architecture.
- Sections 60A and 76A: hazard-action orders and NDMA regulation-making.
- There is no section 9B; NIDM was not abolished.

### Plan, force and fund precision

| Item | Correct recall |
|---|---|
| NDMP 2016 | India's first National Disaster Management Plan. |
| NDMP 2019 | Latest published revised plan. |
| NDRF force | Specialist national response force; 16 operational battalions reported on 22 July 2025. |
| NDRF fund | Section 46 financial instrument for eligible response and relief. |
| State response force | Optional force under section 44A. |
| SDRF fund | State financial instrument under section 48. |

### Evidence ladder

```text
law -> notification -> staff/budget -> plan/drill/expenditure -> output -> outcome
```

Stop at the highest rung directly proved. An enabling section does not prove
constitution; an allocation does not prove expenditure; a force count does not
prove resilience.

### Ten recall lines

1. India moved from relief administration to whole-cycle statutory governance.
2. No dedicated constitutional entry is titled disaster management; related
   Concurrent List entries support shared action.
3. NDMA sets direction; NEC coordinates implementation.
4. SDMA sets state policy; SEC coordinates implementation.
5. DDMA is the local conversion point and prepares the District Plan.
6. NIDM builds capability; NDRF deploys specialist capability.
7. NCMC/HLC received statutory status in 2025.
8. UDMA and the State response force are enabling, notification-dependent reforms.
9. Response and mitigation funds serve different purposes; force and fund are never interchangeable.
10. India's architecture is comprehensive, but outcomes depend on funded and measurable district/local capacity.
