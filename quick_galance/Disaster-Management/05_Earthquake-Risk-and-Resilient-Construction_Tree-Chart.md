## Traps
- The whole Himalayan belt is not Zone V.
- Richter/Mw and MMI/MSK do not measure the same thing.
- Microzonation is not prediction.
- Code adoption is not verified compliance.
- Priority list is not completed retrofit.
- BhooKamp is not a prediction app.
- The withdrawn 2026 zonation proposal is not operative.
