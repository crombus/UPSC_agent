                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Earthquakes release accumulated tectonic strain; they cannot presently be predicted
with reliable exact time, location and magnitude. Most disaster loss comes not from
ground motion alone but from unsafe sites, vulnerable buildings, non-structural
failures, dense exposure, fragile lifelines and weak enforcement. Resilience therefore
depends on hazard and microzonation evidence, code-compliant ductile construction,
retrofitting, non-structural safety, land-use discipline, public preparedness,
functional lifelines and rapid damage-based recovery.
