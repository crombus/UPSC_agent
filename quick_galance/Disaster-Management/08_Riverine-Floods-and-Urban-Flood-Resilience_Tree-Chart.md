                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Flood risk is produced across a catchment, river, reservoir, floodplain, city and
coast. Urban flooding is not merely river flooding inside a city: impervious surfaces,
lost wetlands, blocked drains, altered channels, floodplain occupation, dense exposure
and weak municipal maintenance produce much faster runoff and local waterlogging.
Resilience requires basin-scale management plus city-scale drainage and land-use
governance, forecast-to-action systems, room for water, safe dams, inclusive services
and recovery that does not restore encroachment or shift risk downstream.
