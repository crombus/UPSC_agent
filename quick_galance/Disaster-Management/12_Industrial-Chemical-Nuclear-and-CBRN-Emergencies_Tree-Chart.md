                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Technological disasters are usually system failures across design, siting, manufacture,
storage, transport, operation, maintenance, waste and emergency planning. Chemical,
biological and radiological hazards have different transmission, protection, specialist
command and medical requirements and must not be merged into a generic CBRN label.
India's dense legal framework is necessary but insufficient: enforcement, independent
regulation, worker and community information, buffer-zone governance, off-site planning,
NaTech screening, compensation, health surveillance and environmental remediation
determine whether industrial growth remains safe.
