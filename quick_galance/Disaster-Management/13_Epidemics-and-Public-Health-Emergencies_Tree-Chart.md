                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
An epidemic emergency is not managed by lockdown, hospital beds or an app alone.
Resilience rests on prevention, One Health surveillance, laboratories, trained local
workforces, transparent risk communication, proportionate public-health measures,
clinical surge, essential-service continuity, social protection and recovery. India
combines State responsibility for health with Union coordination through concurrent
and disaster-law powers. COVID-19 demonstrated both the value and limits of using a
generic disaster law for a specialised health emergency. Technology must support
public-health functions without replacing local staff, equity, privacy or trust.
