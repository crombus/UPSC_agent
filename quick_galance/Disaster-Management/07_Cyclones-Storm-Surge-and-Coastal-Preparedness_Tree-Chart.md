                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A tropical cyclone is a rotating warm-core low-pressure system whose disaster
footprint combines destructive wind, extreme rain, storm surge, waves, river and
urban flooding, landslides and prolonged lifeline disruption. Forecast skill saves
lives only when graded IMD information becomes timely evacuation, maintained shelter,
safe critical services and livelihood recovery. Storm surge is neither a tsunami nor
astronomical tide. Coastal resilience requires differentiated risk investment, mixed
engineered and ecosystem protection, last-mile trust and routine maintenance after
the completed NCRMP construction phases.
