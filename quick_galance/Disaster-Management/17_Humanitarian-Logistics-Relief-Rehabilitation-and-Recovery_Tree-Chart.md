                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Humanitarian logistics is a time-critical public service chain from forecast and
needs assessment through procurement, staging, transport, distribution, tracking and
recovery handover. Forecastable hazards permit anticipatory pre-positioning; sudden
hazards require standing readiness and redundancy. Relief protects immediate life,
rehabilitation restores temporary function, and reconstruction restores or transforms
systems for long-term development. Speed without equitable assessment can misallocate
aid; commodity delivery without dignity, protection, psychosocial care, livelihoods,
service continuity and accountability is incomplete. Build Back Better begins during
relief decisions, not after reconstruction starts.
