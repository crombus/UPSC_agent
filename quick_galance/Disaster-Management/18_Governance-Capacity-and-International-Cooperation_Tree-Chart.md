                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Disaster governance succeeds when global frameworks, national law, State capability,
district plans and community action form one accountable chain. India has substantial
institutional and diplomatic architecture through the DM Act system, NPDRR, NIDM,
regional exercises, UN mechanisms and CDRI. Yet convening conferences, signing
agreements, publishing plans or training participants does not prove local readiness.
Capacity is demonstrated through skilled people, financed plans, interoperable data,
repeated exercises, inclusive warnings, public accountability and measured outcomes.
International cooperation is a capacity multiplier under affected-State leadership,
not a substitute for domestic duty or local ownership.

India's international DRR leadership and extensive institutional network are genuine
