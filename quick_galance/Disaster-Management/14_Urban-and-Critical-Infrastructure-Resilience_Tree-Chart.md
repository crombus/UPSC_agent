                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
A city is a system of interdependent lifelines, not a collection of independent
assets. A single flood, earthquake, cyclone, heat wave, fire, cyber incident or crowd
failure can cascade through power, water, telecom, transport, health, food, finance
and governance. Resilience therefore requires criticality and dependency mapping,
risk-sensitive land use, standards and retrofits, redundancy, continuity planning,
inclusive service delivery, interoperable command, resilient finance and Build Back
Better for management systems as well as structures. Membership, sanction, code or
authority creation is an input, not proof of service continuity.
