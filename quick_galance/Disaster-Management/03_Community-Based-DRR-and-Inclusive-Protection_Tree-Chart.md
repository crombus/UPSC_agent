## High-yield traps

- Community is not homogeneous; local power can exclude.
- Participation is not consultation.
- Enrolment is not deployable readiness.
- Warning sent is not warning understood or acted upon.
- Equality of packages is not equity of protection.
- Disaster displacement does not automatically create Convention refugee status.
- A guideline or scheme does not prove implementation or outcome.
