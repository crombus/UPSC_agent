                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Communities are the first exposed, first informed and usually first to act, but
community participation cannot substitute for State responsibility. Effective CBDRR
combines local and scientific knowledge, inclusive risk assessment, trained volunteers,
functional local institutions, accessible warnings, protected shelters, social
protection and accountable recovery. Equality of distribution is not equity of
protection: different groups require different support to achieve comparable safety.
