                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
An early-warning system succeeds only when authoritative hazard intelligence becomes
a trusted, understandable and feasible protective action before impact. Sensors,
models, satellites, GIS, CAP and cell broadcast are components, not the system.
End-to-end warning requires risk knowledge, monitoring and forecasting, authoritative
decision, redundant communication, local comprehension, preparedness, response and
feedback. Prediction, forecast, watch, alert and warning are not interchangeable.
