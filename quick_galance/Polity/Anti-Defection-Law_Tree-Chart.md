# Polity 42 - Anti-Defection Law: Quick-Glance Tree

```text
ANTI-DEFECTION LAW: STABILITY WITHOUT LEGISLATIVE SERVITUDE
|
+-- CONSTITUTIONAL ORIGIN
|   +-- 52nd Amendment Act, 1985 -> Tenth Schedule
|   +-- effective 1 March 1985
|   +-- Arts 102(2)/191(2) -> disqualification under Schedule
|   +-- Arts 101(3)(a)/190(3)(a) -> seat vacancy
|   +-- no crime, lifetime ban or universal public-office disability
|
+-- PARA 1 DEFINITIONS
|   +-- House -> either Parliament House / Assembly / either State House
|   +-- political party -> organisation that sets up candidate/authorises direction
|   +-- legislature party -> that party's members in the particular House
|   +-- original political party -> para 2(1) party of belonging
|   +-- Subhash Desai -> numbers in legislature do not alone create party authority
|
+-- PARA 2 GROUNDS
|   +-- PARTY MEMBER
|   |   +-- voluntarily gives up membership
|   |   |   +-- Ravi S. Naik -> conduct can prove it; resignation not necessary
|   |   +-- votes/abstains contrary to authorised direction
|   |       +-- no prior permission
|   |       +-- no condonation within 15 days
|   |       +-- speech/dissent alone != automatic para 2(1)(b)
|   +-- INDEPENDENT -> joins any party after election -> disqualified
|   |   +-- no six-month window
|   +-- NOMINATED -> may join within 6 months of taking seat
|       +-- joins after six months -> disqualified
|   +-- para 2(4) -> historical 1985 transition, not a modern grace period
|
+-- GROUP AND OFFICE DEFENCES
|   +-- PARA 3 -> one-third split omitted by 91st Amendment
|   +-- PARA 4 MERGER
|   |   +-- original political party merger
|   |   +-- >= 2/3 legislature party agree
|   |   +-- accepting members / non-accepting separate group protected by text
|   |   +-- 2/3 alone != licence; coalition != merger; ECI recognition alone != test
|   +-- PARA 5
|       +-- exact listed presiding officers may leave party because elected to chair
|       +-- must not join a party while holding office
|       +-- may rejoin former party after ceasing office
|
+-- DECIDER, PROCEDURE AND REVIEW
|   +-- PARA 6 -> Speaker/Chairman decides first instance
|   |   +-- if officer personally concerned -> House elects deciding member
|   |   +-- para 6 proceedings deemed legislative proceedings for Arts 122/212
|   +-- PARA 8 -> records + leader reports + petition procedure
|   |   +-- rules laid before House for total 30 days
|   |   +-- notice -> reply -> evidence -> hearing -> reasoned order
|   +-- PARA 7 text says no court jurisdiction
|       +-- Kihoto: invalid for no half-State ratification under Art 368 proviso
|       +-- Speaker = tribunal; review under Arts 136/226/227 survives
|       +-- grounds: constitutional breach, mala fides, natural justice, perversity
|       +-- review != routine appeal; courts ordinarily not first-instance fact-finder
|
+-- CASE-LAW CHRONOLOGY
|   +-- Kihoto Hollohan (1992) -> scheme upheld; para 7 invalid; review
|   +-- Ravi S. Naik (1994) -> voluntary giving up inferred from conduct
|   +-- G. Viswanathan (1996) -> expulsion does not erase deemed belonging
|   +-- Rajendra Singh Rana (2007) -> unsupported split/inaction cannot defeat law
|   +-- Nabam Rebia (2016) -> Speaker disabled during valid removal notice
|   |   +-- correctness referred in Subhash Desai; no later merits ruling located
|   +-- Shrimanth Balasaheb Patil (2019)
|   |   +-- resignation does not erase prior defection
|   |   +-- Speaker cannot add term-long contest ban
|   +-- Keisham Meghachandra Singh (2020)
|   |   +-- ordinarily decide within 3 months from filing
|   |   +-- reasoned exceptional circumstances; tribunal only suggested
|   +-- Subhash Desai (2023)
|       +-- political party appoints whip/leader
|       +-- Speaker identifies party through constitution/organisation
|       +-- ECI symbol and Speaker defection tracks may proceed together
|       +-- Governor cannot decide intra-party leadership
|       +-- Court did not disqualify members or restore resigned government
|
+-- 91ST AMENDMENT REWARD CONTROLS
|   +-- Art 75(1A) -> Union ministry <= 15% Lok Sabha
|   +-- Art 164(1A) -> State ministry <= 15% Assembly; minimum 12
|   +-- Arts 75(1B)/164(1B) -> specified disqualified party member barred as minister
|   +-- Art 361B -> remunerative-political-post bar
|   +-- end: old-term expiry OR earlier date declared elected
|
+-- LEGAL FIREWALL
|   +-- merger != coalition
|   +-- defection -> Speaker | symbol -> ECI
|   +-- Art 102(1)/191(1) question -> President/Governor on ECI opinion
|   +-- bribery/privilege -> Sita Soren boundary; not para 2 doctrine
|   +-- resignation != automatic end of defection jurisdiction
|
+-- DEMOCRATIC BALANCE
|   +-- gains -> mandate + stability + curb on retail office-driven crossing
|   +-- costs -> broad whip + weak dissent + party centralisation
|   |          + group engineering + resignation strategy + Speaker delay
|   +-- current law does NOT restrict whips only to confidence/money votes
|   +-- reform -> narrow high-stakes whip + neutral/time-bound decider
|                + clearer merger proof + transparent internal party authority
|
+-- PYQ AND ANSWER ROUTE
    +-- 2022 Prelims Q16 -> nominated six-month rule + no textual deadline
    +-- 2025 Prelims Q88 -> Speaker/Chairman decider + 'political party' exists
    +-- 2023 GS-II support -> impartial presiding officers
    +-- PRELIMS: category -> conduct -> time -> defence -> decider -> review
    +-- MAINS: purpose -> text -> case -> incentive -> counter -> reform -> verdict
    +-- CONCLUSION: retain stability principle, but narrow, neutralise and time-bind it
```
