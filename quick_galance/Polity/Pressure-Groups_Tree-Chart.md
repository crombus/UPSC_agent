# Polity 44 - Pressure Groups: Quick-Glance Tree

```text
PRESSURE GROUPS: ORGANISED INFLUENCE BETWEEN ELECTIONS
|
+-- CORE DEFINITION
|   +-- organised body/network -> shared interest -> influence public decision
|   +-- dominant purpose = policy influence, not general governing responsibility
|   +-- functional category; no single Indian pressure-group legal status
|
+-- CATEGORY FIREWALL
|   +-- political party -> seeks office through elections + broad programme
|   +-- social movement -> broad social/normative change; may generate organisations
|   +-- NGO/CSO -> legal/organisational sphere; advocacy is only one function
|   +-- trade union/professional body -> membership/occupational function
|   +-- think tank -> research; lobby/lobbyist -> direct representation method/service
|   +-- overlap allowed; every NGO/union/body is not automatically a pressure group
|
+-- CLASSIFICATION
|   +-- sectional/protective -> member interest
|   +-- promotional/cause -> wider normative/public cause
|   +-- identity -> caste/religious/linguistic/regional claims
|   +-- institutional | associational | non-associational | anomic
|   |   +-- anomic = spontaneous/episodic, not stable armed organisation
|   +-- insider <-> outsider strategies; status can change by issue
|   +-- peak association -> federation of affiliates
|   +-- issue network -> fluid officials-experts-groups-media-citizens link
|
+-- ACTOR MAP
|   +-- business -> FICCI/CII/ASSOCHAM/NASSCOM
|   +-- labour -> AITUC/INTUC/BMS/CITU/HMS
|   +-- farmers -> AIKS/BKU/Shetkari Sanghatana/SKM
|   +-- professional/student/caste/religious/regional groups
|   +-- environment/rights/consumer -> Chipko/Silent Valley/NBA/CSE
|   +-- no unsupported current membership/ranking or timeless party-control claim
|
+-- CONSTITUTIONAL FIREWALL
|   +-- Art 19(1)(a) speech -> Art 19(2)
|   +-- Art 19(1)(b) peaceably + without arms -> Art 19(3)
|   +-- Art 19(1)(c) association/union/co-operative -> Art 19(4)
|   +-- Art 350 -> grievance representation in Union/State language only
|   +-- Arts 32/226 -> remedy for enforceable right; PIL != group-made policy
|
+-- ASSOCIATION / LABOUR
|   +-- Damyanti Naranga (1971) -> composition/continuity protected
|   +-- All India Bank Employees' Association (1962)
|   |   +-- Art 19(1)(c) does not guarantee every object/concomitant activity
|   +-- Kameshwar Prasad (1962) -> peaceful demonstration yes; strike FR no
|   +-- T.K. Rangarajan (2003) -> government employees, no fundamental/statutory strike right
|   +-- collective bargaining/strike -> applicable labour law; violence separate
|
+-- PROTEST CASES
|   +-- Himat Lal K. Shah (1973)
|   |   +-- regulate time/place/manner; prior permission possible
|   |   +-- unguided discretion to refuse invalid
|   +-- CPI(M) v Bharat Kumar (1998)
|   |   +-- no right to call/enforce coercive bandh
|   |   +-- bandh != every strike/hartal/peaceful protest
|   +-- Ramlila Maidan Incident (2012)
|   |   +-- peaceful assembly + lawful/proportionate policing
|   +-- MKSS v Union of India (2018)
|       +-- no blanket ban; protesters + residents + traffic/public order
|
+-- METHODS
|   +-- representation/petition -> ministry/legislator/committee
|   +-- consultation -> Bill/rule/standard/budget
|   +-- lobbying -> lawful direct persuasion; bribery/quid pro quo illegal
|   +-- research/media/campaign/digital mobilisation
|   +-- litigation/PIL -> legality/remedy, not legislative substitution
|   +-- bargaining/strike/dharna/demonstration/bandh -> method-specific law
|   +-- endorsements/funding/scorecards -> electoral influence without party conversion
|
+-- INSTITUTIONAL CHANNELS
|   +-- parliamentary committees -> memoranda/evidence, no veto
|   +-- ministries -> stakeholder/pre-legislative consultation
|   +-- regulators -> paper/comments/reasoned response
|   +-- Gram Sabha/social audit/public hearing -> local implementation voice
|   +-- courts -> rights/legality; media -> agenda/scrutiny
|   +-- no comprehensive general lobbyist register statute checked 8 Sep 2026
|
+-- POLICY CYCLE
|   +-- agenda -> formulation -> adoption -> implementation -> monitoring -> feedback
|   +-- access != acceptance; consultation != control; adoption != implementation
|   +-- success != capture; policy outcome normally multi-causal
|
+-- THEORY
|   +-- pluralism -> competition/countervailing power
|   +-- neo-pluralism -> unequal resources + State autonomy
|   +-- elite/capture -> agenda, wealth, access, revolving doors
|   +-- corporatism -> selected peak bodies; coordination + outsider risk
|   +-- social movement -> identity/framing/network/norm change
|
+-- DEMOCRATIC LEGITIMACY
|   +-- value -> expertise + continuous voice + minority/diffuse interests + feedback
|   +-- risk -> opaque lobbying + capture + disruption + misinformation
|   +-- representation -> mandate + internal democracy + funding + excluded constituency
|   +-- federal/local variation -> Union/State/local venue and barrier differences
|   +-- countervailing groups + public reasons prevent monopolised influence
|
+-- REFORM
|   +-- lobby register + client/issue/meeting disclosure
|   +-- consultation paper + adequate time + reasoned response matrix
|   +-- conflicts/recusal/cooling-off + funding transparency
|   +-- inclusive public-hearing design + regional language/access support
|   +-- facilitate peaceful protest + proportionate restrictions + review
|   +-- punish coercion/violence/bribery; no blanket criminalisation
|
+-- PYQ ROUTE
    +-- 2019 farmer groups -> methods + effectiveness + representation
    +-- 2021 business associations -> expertise + access + capture qualification
    +-- 2025 environmental groups -> awareness + policy + advocacy + evidence
    +-- verdict -> organised influence is democratic when contestable, lawful and reasoned
```
