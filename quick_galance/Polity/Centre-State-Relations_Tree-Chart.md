                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Indian federal relations operate through four connected dimensions:
legislative competence, administrative implementation, fiscal capacity and
inter-state coordination. The Constitution gives the Union supremacy where
irreconcilable conflict threatens national coherence, but courts first preserve
both fields through pith and substance and harmonious construction. States
retain constitutionally assigned authority, yet depend on Union finance,
directions, common services and national institutions. Durable federal trust
therefore requires legal competence plus consultation, predictable transfers,
neutral constitutional offices and functioning dispute-resolution forums.
