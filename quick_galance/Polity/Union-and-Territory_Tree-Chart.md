│
├─ ARTICLE 1 — IDENTITY + TERRITORY
│  ├─ exact wording: "India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States"
│  ├─ Union of India = States as federal members

│  ├─ Parliament fixes terms and conditions
│  └─ distinguish from acquisition and from Article 3 internal reorganisation
│
├─ ARTICLE 3 — INTERNAL MAP-MAKING
│  ├─ form new State by separation/union
│  ├─ increase area | diminish area | alter boundary | alter name
│  ├─ President's prior recommendation
│  ├─ affected State legislature receives reference for views
│  ├─ Parliament NOT bound; State consent NOT required
│  ├─ no fresh reference required for every parliamentary amendment
