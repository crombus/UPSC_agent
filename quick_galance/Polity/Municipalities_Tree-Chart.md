# MUNICIPALITIES - QUICK-GLANCE TREE

MUNICIPALITIES: CAN THE ELECTED CITY INSTITUTION CONTROL THE SERVICE CHAIN?
|
+-- CORE
|   Article 243P -> municipality means an Article 243Q institution of self-government.
|   Constitutional status secures form; Articles 243W and 243X leave major substance to State law.
|
+-- ORIGIN AND CONSTITUTIONAL TURN
|   1688 Madras -> 1726 Bombay/Calcutta -> Mayo 1870 finance -> Ripon 1882 representation.
|   Hobhouse Commission 1907-09 -> 1919 transferred subject -> 1935 provincial subject.
|   Wattal finance; Matthai taxation; Ahmed training; A.P. Jain rural-urban linkage;
|   Zakaria resource augmentation; Mukharji budgets; Sahaya legal powers; Correa urbanisation.
|   65th Nagarpalika Bill 1989 failed -> 1990 Bill lapsed -> 74th Amendment 1992.
|   Effect from 1 June 1993: Part IXA + Twelfth Schedule + Article 280(3)(c).
|
+-- PART IXA SPINE
|   243P definitions -> 243Q types -> 243R composition -> 243S wards -> 243T reservation.
|   243U duration -> 243V disqualification -> 243W powers -> 243X finance -> 243Y SFC.
|   243Z audit -> 243ZA SEC elections -> 243ZB UT application -> 243ZC exclusions.
|   243ZD DPC -> 243ZE MPC -> 243ZF transition -> 243ZG election-petition boundary.
|
+-- TYPES AND DEMOCRATIC DESIGN
|   Nagar Panchayat = transitional; Council = smaller urban; Corporation = larger urban.
|   Governor classifies by population, density, revenue, non-farm employment and importance.
|   Industrial township = notified service-provision exception, not a fourth ordinary type.
|   Territorial seats directly elected from wards; experts may be non-voting representatives.
|   Chairperson/mayor method and executive design vary by State law.
|   Wards Committee mandatory at municipal population >= 3 lakh; other committees by State law.
|
+-- RESERVATION, TERM AND ELECTIONS
|   SC/ST seats by local population; women >= one-third inclusive; State may enhance women.
|   OBC reservation: State option + Krishna Murthy distinction + Gawali triple test.
|   Rahul Ramesh Wagh/Suresh Mahajan: unfinished compliance cannot delay polls indefinitely.
|   Five years from first meeting; hearing before dissolution; election before expiry/within six months.
|   Kishansing Tomar 2006 -> mandatory timetable; prepare in advance.
|   Fouziya 2021 -> serving government officer cannot hold SEC office.
|   243ZG -> delimitation/allotment shield; election challenged through statutory petition.
|
+-- DEVOLUTION AND SERVICES
|   243W + Twelfth Schedule 18 = enabling map, not automatic transfer.
|   Planning/land; roads/water/fire; health/sanitation/waste; environment; inclusion; amenities.
|   Test: activity + decision right + staff + asset + budget + standard + grievance authority.
|   Integrate land use, mobility, water, sanitation, public health, climate and informal settlements.
|   Parastatal scale may be useful; require council-approved outcomes and clear accountability.
|
+-- FINANCE AND AUDIT
|   243X -> State-authorised taxes/fees + assignments + grants + municipal funds.
|   Property tax -> complete base, fair valuation, appeals, billing and collection.
|   User charges -> service-linked with lifeline/cross-subsidy safeguards.
|   Bonds -> regulated debt, not tax or grant; require accounts, revenue and viable assets.
|   243Y SFC -> distribution, assignment, grants, improvement; report + action memo to Legislature.
|   280(3)(c) UFC -> augment State funds to supplement municipalities on SFC basis.
|   243Z -> State-law accounts/audit; CAG technical support is not automatic sole audit.
|
+-- DPC AND MPC
|   DPC 243ZD -> >= 4/5 elected; rural:urban population ratio; district draft plan.
|   MPC 243ZE -> >= 2/3 elected; municipal:Panchayat ratio; metropolitan draft plan.
|   Metropolitan area 243P -> >= 10 lakh + notified multi-jurisdiction/contiguous character.
|   Both integrate shared resources, infrastructure and environment; plans go to State Government.
|   Neither is automatically an executive regional government.
|
+-- EXECUTIVE, OUTSIDE FORMS AND PROGRAMMES
|   Mayor/council policy and commissioner execution must have clear statutory responsibility.
|   Cantonment Board -> Cantonments Act 2006, central/Defence statutory local authority.
|   Major Port Authority -> central Major Port Authorities Act 2021.
|   Development authorities/boards/SPVs derive only from statute, notification or company documents.
|   Smart Cities 2015, AMRUT 2.0 2021 and SBM-U 2.0 2021 are programmes, not constitutional bodies.
|
+-- REFORMS AND TRAPS
|   Activity maps -> municipal cadre -> fair OSR -> timely SFC -> functional DPC/MPC -> audit.
|   Census town != municipality; ward threshold 3 lakh != metro threshold 10 lakh.
|   DPC 4/5 != MPC 2/3; SEC != ECI; bond != own revenue; mission != devolution.
|
