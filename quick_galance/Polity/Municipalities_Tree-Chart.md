                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The 74th Amendment constitutionalised elected urban local bodies but left their
substantive powers and revenues largely dependent on State legislation.
Municipalities therefore possess secure democratic form without assured urban
self-government. The central governance problem is fragmented authority:
elected councils coexist with State-appointed commissioners, development
authorities, utilities and mission special-purpose vehicles. Effective urban
democracy requires the three Fs, empowered planning committees, buoyant own
revenue, accountable executive leadership and coordination across municipal,
metropolitan and peri-urban space.
