# Polity 34 - NITI Aayog - Quick-Glance Tree

```text
NITI AAYOG = NATIONAL INSTITUTION FOR TRANSFORMING INDIA
DECODE: PM = Prime Minister | CM = Chief Minister | UT = Union Territory
        LG = Lieutenant Governor | NDC = National Development Council
        ADP = Aspirational Districts Programme
        ABP = Aspirational Blocks Programme
        SDG = Sustainable Development Goal
        DMEO = Development Monitoring and Evaluation Office
        AIM = Atal Innovation Mission | CAG = Comptroller and Auditor General
|
+-- 1. LEGAL IDENTITY
|   +-- created by Union Cabinet Resolution: 1 January 2015
|   +-- executive + non-constitutional + non-statutory
|   +-- replaced Planning Commission; no constitutional amendment
|   `-- recommendations influence policy but do not become law automatically
|
+-- 2. CHRONOLOGY
|   +-- 15 March 1950: Planning Commission created by executive resolution
|   +-- 1952: National Development Council formed in plan-era architecture
|   +-- 2014: transition to a new institution announced
|   +-- 1 January 2015: NITI constituted
|   `-- 2017-18: plan/non-plan expenditure distinction ended by Budget reform
|       (separate from NITI's creation and from ending Five-Year Plans)
|
+-- 3. WHY / MANDATE
|   +-- shared national priorities with active State involvement
|   +-- credible plans from village level upward
|   +-- long-term strategy + policy/programme frameworks
|   +-- coordination + monitoring + feedback + capacity
|   +-- vulnerable-section, technology and knowledge focus
|   `-- two hubs:
|       +-- Team India Hub = Union-State/UT engagement
|       `-- Knowledge and Innovation Hub = think-tank capability
|
+-- 4. COMPOSITION
|   +-- Prime Minister = Chairperson
|   +-- Governing Council =
|   |   CMs of all States and UTs with legislatures
|   |   + Lieutenant Governors of other UTs
|   +-- Vice-Chairperson = appointed by PM
|   +-- full-time members
|   +-- part-time members = up to 2, rotational, relevant knowledge bodies
|   +-- ex-officio Union ministers = up to 4, nominated by PM
|   +-- CEO = PM-appointed, fixed tenure, Secretary to GoI rank
|   `-- special invitees + secretariat
|       TRAP: these are distinct categories; avoid volatile names/counts filled.
|
+-- 5. COUNCILS
|   +-- Governing Council = apex NITI development-policy forum
|   |   `-- voice and consensus, not legislation/devolution/adjudication
|   `-- Regional Council =
|       +-- specific issue affecting >1 State or a region
|       +-- specified tenure; concerned CMs/LGs
|       +-- PM convenes; NITI Chairperson or nominee chairs
|       `-- != Zonal Council != Article 263 Inter-State Council
|
+-- 6. PLANNING COMMISSION COMPARISON
|   +-- same broad legal footing: executive creation
|   +-- Planning Commission: Five-Year Plans + top-down tendency + plan funds
|   +-- NITI: strategy + bottom-up aspiration + advice/monitoring
|   +-- NITI does NOT allocate plan funds or approve State plans
|   `-- NDC's plan-era role lapsed; Governing Council is not its legal clone
|
+-- 7. FEDERALISM
|   +-- cooperative = consultation, shared priorities, coordination, capacity
|   |   `-- test: State voice + reasoned response + implementation support
|   `-- competitive = indicators + comparison + reputation + peer learning
|       `-- limit: ranking can be gamed; level != delta; score != causation
|
+-- 8. PROGRAMME / KNOWLEDGE MECHANISMS
|   +-- Aspirational Districts Programme (Jan 2018)
|   |   `-- convergence + collaboration + competition; district; delta progress
|   +-- Aspirational Blocks Programme (2023)
|   |   `-- related logic at block scale; not a new constitutional tier
|   +-- SDG India Index
|   |   `-- State/UT benchmarking and localisation; no fiscal/legal force
|   +-- DMEO
|   |   `-- Development Monitoring and Evaluation Office; attached office of NITI
|   |       monitoring != evaluation; output != outcome != impact
|   `-- Atal Innovation Mission
|       `-- initiative under NITI; not a statutory regulator
|
+-- 9. INSTITUTIONAL FIREWALL
|   +-- Finance Commission, Article 280 = tax-distribution/grants recommendations
|   +-- GST Council, Article 279A = GST recommendations
|   +-- Inter-State Council, Article 263 = inquiry/discussion/advice route
|   +-- NDC = historical executive plan-era forum
|   +-- ministries = decide, budget, notify and implement
|   `-- CAG, Articles 148-151 = public audit and legislative accountability
|
+-- 10. WHAT NITI CANNOT DO
|   +-- enact binding law or command States
|   +-- adjudicate intergovernmental disputes
|   +-- constitutionally determine tax devolution
|   +-- allocate former plan grants or approve State plans
|   +-- replace ministries, elected governments, Finance Commission or CAG
|   `-- prove outcomes merely by publishing a dashboard or rank
|
+-- 11. EVALUATION
|   +-- strengths: flexible forum, expertise, evidence, peer learning, targeting
|   +-- limits: no fiscal leverage, Union-centred agenda, uneven participation
|   +-- accountability: unclear recommendation-to-action trail
|   +-- overlap: ministries/councils/evaluators/State institutions
|   `-- reform: State agenda route + regular cycle + response matrix
|       + disclosed methods/data audits + independent evaluation + role charters
|
`-- 12. PYQ / ANSWER SPINE
    +-- 2018 GS-III: compare principles with Planning Commission
    |   same status -> federal method -> planning mode -> funds -> tools -> verdict
    +-- 2019 Prelims route: AIM institutional setup
    |   exact controlled stem/key unavailable; do not invent either
    +-- paragraph rule:
    |   claim -> named evidence -> analysis -> qualification
    `-- qualified conclusion:
        NITI's power is persuasive, coordinative and informational.
        Judge it by genuine State voice, evidence quality and policy follow-through,
        not by powers it never legally possessed.
```
