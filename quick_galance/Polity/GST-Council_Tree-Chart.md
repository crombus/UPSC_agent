## DECODED TERMS

- **CGST / SGST / UTGST / IGST:** Central, State, Union Territory and Integrated Goods and Services Tax.
- **ITC:** input-tax credit, the statutory offset of eligible input tax against output liability.
- **GSTN:** Goods and Services Tax Network, the shared digital infrastructure.
- **CBIC:** Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, the Central administration.
- **GSTAT:** Goods and Services Tax Appellate Tribunal, the statutory appellate body.
- **CIF:** cost, insurance and freight import contract; relevant to the ocean-freight setting in *Mohit Minerals*.
- **HSD / ATF:** high speed diesel / aviation turbine fuel.
- **Finance Commission:** Article 280 body recommending tax devolution and grants; it does not set GST rates.

## QUALIFIED CONCLUSION

The Council harmonises GST through constitutional persuasion and structured interdependence. Parliament and State legislatures retain law-making power; administrations, GSTN and GSTAT perform separate functions. Cooperative federalism therefore depends on valid legal implementation, transparent fiscal adjustment and an operational intergovernmental dispute route.
