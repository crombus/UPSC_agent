# Polity 38 - Lokpal and Lokayuktas: Quick-Glance Tree

```text
LOKPAL AND LOKAYUKTAS
|
+-- CORE IDENTITY
|   +-- Lokpal = statutory Union anti-corruption ombudsman under 2013 Act
|   +-- not constitutional | not police | not prosecution court | not general grievance body
|   `-- Special Court alone determines guilt and sentence
|
+-- EVOLUTION
|   1809 Sweden -> 1966 First ARC -> repeated Bills from 1968
|   -> 2011 anti-corruption movement -> 2013 enactment
|   -> assent 1 Jan 2014 -> commencement 16 Jan 2014
|
+-- COMPOSITION: SECTION 3
|   +-- Chairperson + not more than 8 Members
|   +-- 50% of Members = Judicial Members
|   +-- >=50% of Members from SC/ST/OBC/minorities/women
|   +-- Chair: CJI/former CJI OR SC judge/former judge OR qualified eminent person
|   +-- Judicial Member: SC judge/former judge OR HC Chief Justice/former CJ
|   `-- Other Member: integrity + ability + >=25 years listed expertise; minimum age 45
|
+-- APPOINTMENT / TENURE / REMOVAL
|   +-- Selection: PM + LS Speaker + LS LoP + CJI/nominee + eminent jurist
|   +-- Search Committee: >=7 experts; diversity floor; panel not exclusive
|   +-- President appoints | term 5 years or age 70 | ordinary reappointment barred
|   +-- Member -> Chair only within aggregate 5 years
|   +-- misbehaviour: 100 MPs petition -> President -> Supreme Court inquiry
|   `-- Common Cause (2017): vacancy did not make Act unworkable; no invented substitute
|
+-- JURISDICTION: SECTION 14
|   +-- PM | Union Ministers | MPs | Groups A-D | former covered functionaries
|   +-- Parliament-created / Central-controlled / Central-financed bodies
|   +-- clause (g): specified government-financed entities + notified income condition
|   +-- clause (h): specified officers of entities receiving foreign donation
|   |               > Rs 10 lakh/year or higher notified amount
|   +-- connected abettor/bribe-giver/bribe-taker/conspirator may be reached
|   `-- NOT every private person; statutory offence, office, funding and consent gates apply
|
+-- PRIME MINISTER FILTER
|   +-- excluded: international relations | external/internal security
|   |             public order | atomic energy | space
|   +-- full bench + >=2/3 approval + in-camera inquiry
|   `-- dismissed-inquiry records not published/made available
|
+-- MPs AND ARTICLE 105(2)
|   +-- section 14(2) preserves genuine parliamentary speech/vote protection
|   +-- Sita Soren (2024): bribery itself gets no Articles 105(2)/194(2) immunity
|   `-- privilege survives; corrupt bargain is not converted into legislative function
|
+-- COMPLAINT -> COURT
|   Complaint Rules 2020: Form I + identity + affidavit + particulars
|      -> scrutiny: form / jurisdiction / limitation
|      -> preliminary inquiry OR investigation
|      -> hearing + >=3-Member bench
|      -> investigate | departmental action | close
|      -> investigation report to court + Lokpal
|      -> sanction/charge-sheet | closure | departmental route
|      -> Prosecution Wing/agency
|      -> Special Court adjudication
|
+-- TIME MAP
|   +-- PI report: 60 days from reference
|   +-- PI: ordinarily 90 days + further 90 days for recorded reasons
|   +-- investigation: 6 months + <=6-month extensions at a time
|   +-- trial: 1-year target + 3-month extensions; total <=2 years
|   `-- section 53 bar: complaint after 7 years from alleged offence
|
+-- CVC / CBI / CVO FIREWALL
|   +-- CVC: Groups A/B preliminary report to Lokpal; Groups C/D under CVC Act
|   +-- CBI/DSPE: criminal investigation
|   +-- section 25: Lokpal superintendence only over referred DSPE matters
|   +-- no dictation of a particular investigation/disposal
|   +-- referred-case investigating officer transfer needs Lokpal approval
|   +-- CVO: departmental vigilance; not police, prosecutor or court
|   +-- CAG: public-account audit; not complaint investigation
|   `-- CIC: RTI disclosure/appeals; not corruption prosecution
|
+-- POWERS AND DUE PROCESS
|   +-- search through entrusted agency | specified civil-court inquiry powers
|   +-- provisional attachment <=90 days -> Special Court
|   +-- confirmation application within 30 days
|   +-- Special Court controls confirmation/confiscation/restoration
|   +-- section 23 sanction enables prosecution, not guilt
|   `-- section 46 protects good faith; failed complaint != false complaint
|
+-- ASSETS / REPORTING / FINANCE
|   +-- section 44 after 2016: declaration in prescribed form and manner
|   +-- section 45: conditional rebuttable presumption
|   +-- section 13 charged administrative expenses
|   +-- sections 40-43 budget, grants, accounts and CAG audit
|   `-- section 48 annual report -> President -> Parliament + reasons for rejected advice
|
+-- LOKAYUKTAS: SECTION 63
|   +-- State must establish by State law if not already established
|   +-- no single national composition, appointment, tenure, power or jurisdiction
|   +-- Maharashtra 1971: historical early State inquiry/report model
|   +-- Karnataka 1984: distinct Lokayukta/Upalokayukta investigation framework
|   `-- compare statutes, never universalise one State model
|
`-- EXAM VERDICT
    +-- strengths: high-office reach | plural selection | protected tenure
    |             referred-case safeguards | attachment/sanction | Special Courts
    +-- limits: complaint dependence | procedural filters | vacancies/capacity
    |          external-agency dependence | State variation | secrecy tensions
    `-- answer: strengthen appointments, staff, interoperable tracking, reasoned
               aggregate disclosure and State minimum safeguards without sacrificing
               natural justice, federal choice or judicial control
```

## PYQ route

- **2025 Prelims GS-I, Set A, Q98:** test section 1(3), Chairperson eligibility, minimum age 45 and PM jurisdiction. Official local Set-A key: **A (III only)**.
- **Mains route:** statutory status -> composition/independence -> qualified jurisdiction -> complaint-to-court process -> institutional boundaries -> federal variation -> measured reform.

## Final one-line recall

> Lokpal is a broad but bounded statutory ombudsman: it routes and supervises anti-corruption process, while agencies investigate, prosecutors prosecute, Special Courts adjudicate and State laws determine Lokayukta design.
