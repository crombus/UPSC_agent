                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The Lokpal is a statutory Union ombudsman created by the 2013 Act to receive
corruption complaints against high political and administrative office,
supervise preliminary inquiry and investigation, sanction prosecution and
protect referred CBI cases from interference. It is neither constitutional nor
a court, has no general suo-motu power and relies substantially on other
agencies. Lokayuktas are State-law institutions with widely varying design.
Effective ombudsman accountability requires independent appointments,
investigative capacity, due process, clear jurisdiction and coordinated rather
than duplicative relations with CVC, CBI and prosecution agencies.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The Lokpal's statute contains meaningful powers: high-level jurisdiction,
diverse membership, control over referred CBI cases, prosecution sanction and
asset attachment. Yet an ombudsman without suo-motu authority or an independent
investigative arm remains dependent on complaints and borrowed agencies.
Strengthening it requires secure capacity and simpler access while preserving
due process, Prime-Minister safeguards and federal diversity in Lokayukta design.
