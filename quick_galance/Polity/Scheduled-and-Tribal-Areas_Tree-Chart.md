                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Article 244 creates differentiated governance for tribal regions because equal
formal administration may not protect unequal historical conditions. The Fifth
Schedule follows a protective model led by the President, Governor and an
advisory Tribes Advisory Council. The Sixth Schedule follows a stronger
self-government model through elected Autonomous District and Regional
Councils with legislative, executive, judicial and fiscal powers. PESA and the
Forest Rights Act add participatory and resource rights to the Fifth Schedule,
but implementation depends on State law, Gram Sabha capacity and enforcement.
