                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Interpretive doctrines translate constitutional text into disciplined tests for
meaning, competence, validity, time and remedy. They are not free-standing
Articles or slogans. A sound analysis begins with the governing provision,
identifies the alleged defect, selects the narrowest relevant doctrine, applies
its elements and controlling precedent, and states the legal consequence.
Interpretation preserves constitutional government only when text, structure,
purpose, precedent, institutional competence and remedy constrain judicial
choice.
