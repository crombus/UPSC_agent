## Prelims traps

- CAT is statutory, not a constitutional body.
- Article 323B does not itself create every subject tribunal.
- Tribunals do not follow CPC/Evidence Act strictly in one universal manner.
- Article 136 is discretionary and does not routinely bypass High Court review.
- Technical expertise cannot replace judicial competence without limits.
- Tribunal decisions are neither constitutionally supreme nor never precedential.

## Verified PYQ routes

- **2018:** first-instance curtailment -> Articles 323A/323B -> L. Chandra Kumar -> independence -> qualified validity.
- **2019:** CAT source/jurisdiction -> procedure/powers -> functional independence -> structural limits -> High Court review.
- **2025:** need versus courts -> 2021 gains -> workload/access/independence costs -> reform metrics.
