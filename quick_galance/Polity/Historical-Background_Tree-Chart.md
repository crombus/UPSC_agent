# Historical Background of the Indian Constitution - Quick-Glance Tree

```text
CENTRAL THESIS
Colonial statutes built governing machinery; the Constitution changed its sovereign source,
accountability and rights framework.
|
+-- WHY REGULATION?
|   1600 charter -> Plassey 1757 -> Buxar 1764 -> Diwani 1765
|   -> corporate fiscal-territorial power without public accountability
|
+-- COMPANY RULE: CONTROL AND CENTRALISATION
|   1773 GG Bengal + Calcutta Supreme Court 1774
|   1781 jurisdiction repair
|   1784 Board of Control + Court of Directors
|   1813 monopoly ends except tea/China; sovereignty; education grant
|   1833 GG India; central legislation; Company trade ends
|   1853 legislative differentiation; competition; Macaulay 1854
|
+-- CROWN RULE: ASSOCIATION TO LIMITED RESPONSIBILITY
|   1858 Company abolished; Canning first Viceroy; Secretary of State + Council
|   1861 nominated Indians; portfolio; provincial legislation
|   1892 budget discussion/questions; indirect-election element
|   1909 separate Muslim electorates; S.P. Sinha; central official majority
|   1919 provincial dyarchy; central bicameralism/direct election; PSC route
|
+-- BRIDGE TO 1935
|   Simon 1927 -> report -> Round Tables -> White Paper -> Joint Select Committee
|   Communal Award -> Poona Pact: reserved seats within joint electorates
|
+-- 1935 CONTROL
|   PROVIDED, NOT COMMENCED: All-India Federation + central dyarchy
|   OPERATED: provincial autonomy + Federal Court from 1937
|   LISTS: 59 Federal | 54 Provincial | 36 Concurrent; residuary -> Governor-General
|   INSTITUTIONS: PSCs, Auditor-General, bicameralism in six provinces
|   LIMITS: restricted franchise, safeguards, reserved strategic subjects
|   RBI: RBI Act 1934; operations 1935
|
+-- 1947 BRIDGE
|   20 Feb Attlee -> 3 Jun Plan -> 18 Jul Act -> 15 Aug Dominions
|   sovereign Constituent Assemblies + adapted 1935 law
|   paramountcy lapses; integration is not automatic
|
+-- 1950 VERDICT
|   RETAINED/TRANSFORMED: lists, Governors, courts, services, administration
|   REJECTED: imperial sovereignty, official majorities, separate electorates,
|             restricted franchise
|   ADDED/RECAST: popular sovereignty, republic, adult suffrage, rights, remedies
|
+-- EXAM CONTROL
    1773 GG Bengal != 1833 GG India != 1858 Viceroy
    1909 separate electorate != 1919 provincial dyarchy != 1935 autonomy
    provided != commenced; separate electorate != reserved joint-electorate seat
    paragraph = claim -> named Act -> effect -> limit -> qualified verdict

FINAL LINE: inherited machinery + democratic rupture = constitutional transformation.
```
