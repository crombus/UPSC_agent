# Polity 51 — Rights and Liabilities of the Government: Quick-Glance Tree

```text
RIGHTS AND LIABILITIES OF THE GOVERNMENT
|
+-- 1. CONSTITUTIONAL SPINE
|   294-295 succession -> 296 ownerless property -> 297 maritime resources
|   -> 298 executive capacity -> 299 contract form -> 300 suits
|   -> 300A lawful deprivation of private property
|
+-- 2. COMMENCEMENT PROPERTY
|   +-- Article 294: Dominion/Governor's Province assets + rights/liabilities
|   `-- Article 295: former Indian States -> Union if Union-purpose/list nexus; otherwise State
|       LIMIT: later reorganisation follows its statute/transfer instrument
|
+-- 3. OWNERLESS / MARITIME
|   +-- Article 296: escheat + lapse + bona vacantia
|   |   property in State -> State; other case -> Union; purpose-based proviso
|   `-- Article 297: specified ocean-bed value + EEZ resources -> Union
|
+-- 4. CAPACITY BEFORE COMMITMENT
|   Article 298 -> trade/business + acquire/hold/dispose + contracts
|   LIMIT -> appropriate legislature + Article 14 + statutory/audit control
|   Article 299 -> expressed in President/Governor name
|                + executed on behalf
|                + authorised person/manner
|   Bhikraj Jaipuria + Mulamchand -> mandatory; no ratification/estoppel cure
|
+-- 5. DEFECTIVE CONTRACT
|   no Article 299 form -> no contract enforcement
|   section 70? -> lawful act/delivery + non-gratuitous intent + enjoyed benefit
|   B.K. Mondal -> restitution; Mulamchand -> proof/accounting remains
|   LIMIT -> benefit value, not automatic promised price
|
+-- 6. SUITS / PROCEDURE
|   Article 300 + CPC 79 -> Union of India / name of State
|   CPC 80(1) -> two-month notice for covered suit
|   CPC 80(2) -> court leave for urgent relief + show-cause opportunity
|   Limitation Act 15(2) -> exclude legally required notice time
|   CPC 82 -> three months unsatisfied before qualifying execution
|   LIMIT -> procedure != immunity
|
+-- 7. TORT EVOLUTION
|   P & O -> colonial divide
|   -> Vidyawati (1962): ordinary vehicle negligence; State liable
|   -> Kasturi Lal (decided 1964): older police-custody immunity line
|   -> N. Nagendra Rao (1994): broad immunity untenable; narrow residue
|   TEST -> duty + breach + causation + damage + employment nexus + defence
|
+-- 8. CONSTITUTIONAL COMPENSATION
|   Rudul Sah -> unlawful detention
|   Nilabati Behera -> Article 21 public-law remedy distinct from private tort
|   D.K. Basu -> custody safeguards + compensation
|   COEXISTS -> civil damages + prosecution + discipline + personal liability
|
+-- 9. ARTICLE 300A EVOLUTION
|   pre-44th -> Articles 19(1)(f) + 31 Fundamental Rights
|   44th Amendment -> removed them; inserted 300A outside Part III
|   Bishambhar Dayal -> executive direction alone is not authority of law
|   Jilubhai -> constitutional, non-Fundamental right; old adequacy rule not restored
|
+-- 10. PROPERTY TAXONOMY / CASES
|   acquisition -> ordinarily permanent transfer/extinguishment
|   requisition -> ordinarily temporary possession/use
|   regulation -> control may fall short of deprivation
|   K.T. Plantation -> public purpose + no confiscation/illusory compensation
|   Vidya Devi -> no unsupported occupation/adverse-possession shortcut by State
|   Bimal Kumar Shah -> notice + hearing + reasons + purpose + compensation
|                       + efficiency + conclusion
|
+-- EXAMINER TRAPS
|   +-- no blanket sovereign immunity
|   +-- defective contract not always enforceable
|   +-- restitution does not validate contract
|   +-- Article 300A not a Fundamental Right
|   +-- executive order alone cannot deprive
|   +-- compensation not always market value
|   +-- every regulation is not acquisition
|   `-- writ compensation does not replace all civil/criminal remedies
|
`-- PYQ / MAINS ROUTE
    article map -> exact wrong -> remedy and ingredients -> named case
    -> CPC boundary -> official/personal overlap -> qualified verdict

VERDICT: Public power carries special constitutional form and procedure, but remains answerable under law.
```
