                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Articles 294-300 make the Union and States continuing legal persons capable of
owning property, trading, contracting, suing and being sued. Public power does
not place government outside ordinary legality: Article 299 protects the
exchequer through mandatory contracting form, Article 300 supplies a suable
party and preserves liability, and constitutional remedies answer rights
violations. Personal official protection, procedural notice, State secrecy,
private tort and public-law compensation remain legally distinct.
