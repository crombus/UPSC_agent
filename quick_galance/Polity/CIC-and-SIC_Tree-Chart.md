                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The RTI Act 2005 converts the constitutional right to know under Article
19(1)(a) into a time-bound statutory system of proactive publication, requests,
appeals, binding disclosure orders, compensation and personal PIO penalties.
CIC and SIC are statutory quasi-judicial appellate bodies, not constitutional
commissions. Their independence is central because they adjudicate against the
governments that appoint them. The 2019 amendment shifted tenure and pay from
Parliament's fixed statute to Central rules, while the commenced DPDP amendment
has narrowed the personal-information exemption, intensifying the transparency-
privacy debate.
