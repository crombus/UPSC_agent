                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The NCRWC was an executive, temporary and advisory commission created in 2000
to review how the Constitution had worked over fifty years, not to write a new
Constitution. Its mandate expressly preserved parliamentary democracy and the
basic structure. Its 2002 report is a diagnostic and reform menu: every proposal
remains non-binding unless separately enacted, judicially recognised or
implemented through valid executive action. Similarity between a later reform
and a recommendation does not prove exact adoption or causal implementation.
