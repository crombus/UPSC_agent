                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Article 324 creates an independent constitutional manager for elections to
Parliament, State legislatures, the President and Vice-President. The
Commission's authority is broad enough to fill statutory gaps necessary for a
free and fair poll, but not to contradict enacted law or adjudicate matters
assigned to courts. Electoral integrity is a chain: accurate rolls, neutral
administration, fair nominations, transparent finance, lawful campaigning,
secure voting and counting, reasoned enforcement and timely election petitions.
Independence depends on appointments, removal, resources and public reasons.
