                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The 73rd Amendment constitutionalised rural local democracy but did not
automatically create effective self-government. Part IX guarantees institutions,
elections, reservation, tenure and fiscal review, while Articles 243G and 243H
leave much functional and financial devolution to State law. Consequently,
India has achieved deeper political decentralisation than administrative or
fiscal decentralisation. Panchayat functionality depends on the simultaneous
transfer of functions, functionaries and funds, active Gram Sabhas, accountable
planning and context-sensitive tribal self-government under PESA.
