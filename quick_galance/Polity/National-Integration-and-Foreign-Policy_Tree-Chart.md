## Prelims close-option traps

- Article 51 is a DPSP, not directly enforceable and not the sole source of foreign-affairs power.
- Article 355 retains "internal disturbance"; Article 352 now uses "armed rebellion".
- Hindi is the Union official language under Article 343, not a constitutionally declared national language.
- Article 29(1) is not confined to notified minorities.
- NIC, Zonal Councils, Inter-State Council and North Eastern Council have different legal bases.
- Treaty obligation is not automatic domestic enforceability; contrary statute/Constitution controls.
- Non-alignment is not neutrality; strategic autonomy is not isolation; Panchsheel is not constitutional law.
