# Polity Topic 35 - NHRC and SHRC - Quick-Glance Tree

```text
NHRC = National Human Rights Commission
SHRC = State Human Rights Commission
PHRA = Protection of Human Rights Act, 1993
ICCPR = International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights
ICESCR = International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights
GANHRI = Global Alliance of National Human Rights Institutions
SCA = Sub-Committee on Accreditation
CCPD = Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities
CJI = Chief Justice of India | SC = Supreme Court | HC = High Court
PM = Prime Minister | LS = Lok Sabha | RS = Rajya Sabha
LoP = Leader of Opposition | CJ = Chief Justice
NCM = National Commission for Minorities
NCSC = National Commission for Scheduled Castes
NCST = National Commission for Scheduled Tribes
NCW = National Commission for Women
NCBC = National Commission for Backward Classes
NCPCR = National Commission for Protection of Child Rights
UNGA = United Nations General Assembly | UT = Union Territory
SIT = Special Investigation Team
|
+-- 1. LEGAL IDENTITY / CHRONOLOGY
|   +-- PHRA title: 1993; deemed commencement: 28 Sep 1993
|   +-- assent: 8 Jan 1994; Act 10 of 1994
|   +-- NHRC/SHRC = statutory, not constitutional
|   +-- 2006: court-direction inquiry; wider visits; complaint transfer;
|   |          recast section 18 and State/National procedure
|   `-- 2019: broader chair eligibility; 3 NHRC experts/1 woman;
|              7 deemed members; 3-year tenure; UT adjustments
|
+-- 2. SECTION 2(1)(d) DEFINITION
|   +-- life + liberty + equality + dignity of individual
|   +-- guaranteed by Constitution OR embodied in defined International Covenants
|   `-- enforceable by courts in India
|       +-- section 2(1)(f): ICCPR + ICESCR
|       `-- another UNGA covenant/convention only if Central Government notifies
|
+-- 3. NHRC COMPOSITION - SECTION 3
|   +-- Chair: person who has been CJI or Supreme Court Judge
|   +-- 1 serving/former Supreme Court Judge
|   +-- 1 serving/former High Court Chief Justice
|   +-- 3 human-rights experts; at least 1 woman
|   `-- 7 deemed members for section 12(b)-(j), NOT 12(a):
|       NCM + NCSC + NCST + NCW + NCBC + NCPCR + CCPD
|
+-- 4. NHRC APPOINTMENT / TENURE / REMOVAL - SECTIONS 4-7
|   +-- six-member recommendation committee:
|   |   PM, LS Speaker, Union Home Minister, LoP LS, LoP RS, RS Deputy Chairman
|   +-- President appoints by warrant
|   +-- 3 years; reappointment; age ceiling 70
|   +-- resignation to President; acting member authorised by President
|   `-- President removes:
|       +-- proved misbehaviour/incapacity -> Supreme Court inquiry/report
|       `-- direct listed grounds: insolvency, outside paid work, infirmity,
|           declared unsound mind, conviction/imprisonment involving moral turpitude
|
+-- 5. SECTION 12 FUNCTIONS
|   +-- inquiry: suo motu / victim or representative / court direction
|   |   violation or abetment; public-servant negligence in prevention
|   +-- pending-court intervention WITH court approval
|   +-- jail/other State-controlled institution visits
|   +-- safeguard and inhibiting-factor review, including terrorism
|   `-- treaty study + research + literacy + NGO encouragement + promotion
|
+-- 6. INQUIRY POWER AND FAIRNESS
|   +-- section 13: summons, oath, documents, affidavits, public records, commissions
|   +-- limited entry/seizure and deemed-court/judicial-proceeding consequences
|   +-- sections 11/14: government staff/agency; concurrence; Commission control
|   +-- Commission must test agency report
|   `-- sections 15/16: protected statement + hearing/defence
|       TRAP: civil-court powers != general adjudication or compensation decree
|
+-- 7. SECTIONS 17-18: INQUIRY TO REMEDY
|   +-- call for report; proceed if delayed; close if adequate action/no inquiry needed
|   +-- recommend compensation/damages
|   +-- recommend prosecution/other action
|   +-- recommend immediate interim relief
|   +-- approach Supreme Court/High Court
|   `-- 1-month comments -> publish report/recommendation/response
|       TRAP: recommend != order; prosecution recommendation != conviction
|
+-- 8. SECTION 19 ARMED-FORCES ROUTE
|   +-- armed forces = naval, military, air and other Union armed forces
|   +-- NHRC seeks Central Government report
|   +-- then stops or recommends
|   +-- Central response: 3 months or further allowed time
|   `-- publish report + action
|       TRAP: no ordinary direct NHRC investigation; State police not automatic
|
+-- 9. SHRC - SECTIONS 21-29
|   +-- State MAY constitute
|   +-- Chair: former HC Chief Justice/Judge
|   +-- judicial member: HC Judge or District Judge with >=7 years as DJ
|   +-- 1 human-rights expert
|   +-- scope: List II + List III; already-inquired bar
|   +-- Governor appoints; resignation to Governor; PRESIDENT removes
|   +-- 3 years; age 70; reappointment
|   +-- shared Chair/member possible for States with consent and committee process
|   +-- UT other than Delhi may be assigned; Delhi handled by NHRC
|   `-- section 29 omits section 12(f) treaty-study function
|
+-- 10. COURTS / PROSECUTORS / SITS / REPORTS
|   +-- sections 20/28: annual/special reports -> legislature
|   |   + action memorandum + reasons for non-acceptance
|   +-- section 30: State may specify Sessions Court with HC CJ concurrence
|   +-- section 31: Public Prosecutor or advocate with >=7 years
|   +-- section 37: Government may constitute SIT(s)
|   `-- Commission -> recommendation; police/SIT -> investigation;
|       prosecutor -> case; court -> binding judgment
|
+-- 11. JURISDICTION BARS
|   +-- section 36(1): NHRC cannot inquire if matter pending before
|   |   SHRC/another commission duly constituted by law
|   +-- section 21(5): SHRC cannot inquire if NHRC/another commission already inquiring
|   +-- section 36(2): NHRC/SHRC cannot inquire after 1 year from alleged act
|   `-- court, criminal and other statutory remedies follow their own law
|
+-- 12. CASE CONTROL
|   +-- Paramjit Kaur (1999): Supreme Court Article 32 reference;
|   |   NHRC as expert body; ordinary section 36(2) limit not controlling that role
|   +-- N.C. Dhoundial (2003): section 36(2) jurisdictional;
|   |   continuing effects do not automatically make completed act continuing
|   `-- EEVFAM (2016): alleged extra-judicial executions/excessive force
|       require credible inquiry/accountability; no blanket immunity;
|       NHRC material remains distinct from binding criminal adjudication
|
+-- 13. INSTITUTIONAL FIREWALL
|   +-- NCSC/NCST/NCBC: constitutional, Articles 338/338A/338B
|   +-- NCM/NCW/NCPCR/CCPD: specialised statutory fields
|   +-- courts: writs, review, trials and binding orders
|   +-- police/SIT: criminal investigation; prosecutors: conduct case
|   `-- no super-commission: coordinate intake/referral/data/follow-up
|
+-- 14. PARIS PRINCIPLES / GANHRI - CONTROL 8 SEP 2026
|   +-- benchmark: broad mandate, independence, pluralism, resources,
|   |   stable tenure, methods, accessibility
|   +-- March 2025 SCA recommended alteration to B; India challenged
|   +-- public listing remained A
|   `-- alteration review scheduled 9-20 Nov 2026
|       SAFE: unresolved review; UNSAFE: completed downgrade
|
+-- 15. STRENGTHS / LIMITS / REFORM
|   +-- strengths: access, suo motu, evidence, visits, reports, publicity
|   +-- limits: recommendation, one year, armed-forces report route,
|   |           investigator dependence, vacancies, uneven SHRC/court follow-up
|   `-- reform: open plural selection + independent investigation
|       + protected resources + reasoned response/court escalation
|       + calibrated time-bar change + safeguarded armed-forces fact-finding
|
`-- 16. PYQ / ANSWER SPINE
    +-- 2018 GS-II: umbrella commission
    |   status map -> overlap -> constitutional barrier -> coordination model
    +-- 2021 GS-II: contribution + structural limits + practical limits + remedies
    +-- paragraph: claim -> named evidence -> analysis -> qualification
    `-- verdict:
        strong inquiry and public-accountability tools, but non-self-executing remedies;
        strengthen independence and follow-through without inventing judicial powers.
```
