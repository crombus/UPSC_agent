# Polity 11 - Parliamentary System

+-- ORIGIN AND INDIAN TIMELINE

+-- RESPONSIBILITY AND EXECUTIVE CORE

|   legal -> no universal British countersignature rule for every head-of-State act
|   Council of Ministers -> wider constitutional ministry
|   Cabinet -> smaller senior decision core
|   91st Amendment (2003) -> inserted Union/State ministry-size caps and
|                            defector-to-minister disqualification controls

|                 but voting follows House membership
|
+-- OPERATING FEATURES
|   dual executive | majority/coalition support | collective responsibility
|   common programme | fusion/double membership | PM/CM leadership
|   lower-House dissolution | secrecy | opposition/accountability

|   Secrecy qualification -> not immunity from Parliament, courts, audit or law
|
+-- CONFIDENCE / DISSOLUTION / CARETAKER

|   caretaker restraint -> convention and necessity-sensitive, not a rigid constitutional code

+-- PARLIAMENTARY vs PRESIDENTIAL
|   dual vs single political executive | legislative support vs separate mandate
|   flexible/confidence tenure vs fixed tenure | responsibility vs none to legislature
|   ministerial membership vs executive officers outside legislature
|   fusion with checks vs separation with checks | possible dissolution vs none by President
|   risks -> instability/dominance vs deadlock/personal concentration
|   No absolutes -> presidential != authoritarian; parliamentary != automatically democratic

+-- INDIAN ADAPTATION
