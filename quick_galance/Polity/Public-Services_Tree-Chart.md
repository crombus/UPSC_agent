                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Part XIV constitutionalises a permanent, politically neutral executive while
preserving democratic control. Article 309 authorises legislation and interim
service rules; Article 310 retains the doctrine of pleasure; Article 311
fences that pleasure with authority and inquiry safeguards; Article 312 makes
new All-India Services a federal bargain requiring Rajya Sabha consent. The
live governance problem is less arbitrary dismissal than politicised transfers,
weak specialisation, delayed discipline and uneven capacity. Neutrality must be
paired with responsiveness, integrity and measurable public service.

                  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
India's public-service Constitution deliberately combines executive control
with rule-bound tenure. Article 311 prevents arbitrary punishment without
creating immunity from discipline; Article 312 integrates administration
without eliminating State ownership. The urgent reform is to protect lawful
professional advice and stable postings while improving specialisation,
performance and citizen service. A neutral civil service is not politically
unresponsive: it serves elected governments faithfully within constitutional
law and recorded institutional process.
