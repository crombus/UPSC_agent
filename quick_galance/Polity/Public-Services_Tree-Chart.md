# Polity 41 - Public Services: Quick-Glance Tree

```text
PUBLIC SERVICES: NEUTRAL COMPETENCE UNDER DEMOCRATIC CONTROL
|
+-- CONSTITUTIONAL MAP
|   +-- Part XIV: Services under Union and States
|   +-- Articles 308-314 = service relationship
|   +-- Articles 315-323 = UPSC/SPSC/Joint PSC -> Topic 28 owns detail
|   +-- Article 308 printed J&K exclusion text -> read with 2019 reorganisation
|
+-- CATEGORY FIREWALL
|   +-- AIS = IAS + IPS + Indian Forest Service
|   +-- Indian Foreign Service = Central Civil Service, NOT AIS
|   +-- Central and State civil services/posts -> Article 309 law/rules
|   +-- defence service/posts -> Article 310; uniformed personnel outside 311
|   +-- constitutional office -> special removal code first
|   +-- PSU/statutory/contract employee -> Article 311 not automatic
|
+-- ARTICLE 309: SOURCE LADDER
|   +-- subject to Constitution
|   +-- appropriate Legislature regulates recruitment + service conditions
|   +-- until provision by/under Act:
|   |   +-- President for Union | Governor for State | directed delegate
|   +-- proviso rule = subordinate legislation with legal force
|   +-- instruction fills genuine gap only
|   +-- Constitution > Act > rule > instruction > practice
|
+-- EQUALITY / RESERVATION / PSC
|   +-- Arts 14 + 16(1): non-arbitrary equality of opportunity
|   +-- 16(2): prohibited grounds
|   +-- 16(3)-(6): residence / backward class / promotion / religious office / EWS
|   +-- Indra Sawhney: 16(4) enabling; no promotion reservation through it
|   +-- Nagaraj + Jarnail Singh: clause-specific promotion conditions
|   +-- Mukesh Kumar: no fundamental right to reservation
|   +-- Art 335: SC/ST claims reconciled with efficiency + proviso
|   +-- Art 320 consultation generally advisory unless law says otherwise
|
+-- ARTICLE 310: PLEASURE WITH FENCES
|   +-- President: Union civil + AIS + defence service/posts
|   +-- Governor: State civil services/posts
|   +-- Shamsher Singh: formal heads ordinarily act on aid/advice
|   +-- pleasure != personal whim
|   +-- fences: express Constitution + Arts 14/16/311 + rules + review
|   +-- 310(2): narrow specialist fixed-term compensation contract
|   +-- B.P. Singhal: bounded anti-arbitrariness analogy only
|
+-- ARTICLE 311: FIVE-GATE TEST
|   +-- protected civil-service member/civil-post holder?
|   +-- dismissal, removal or reduction in rank?
|   +-- punitive in substance?
|   +-- competent authority and regular inquiry?
|   +-- exact proviso exception?
|   +-- protection = fair process, NOT job immunity
|
+-- ARTICLE 311(1)
|   +-- dismissing/removing authority cannot be subordinate to appointing authority
|   +-- identical individual unnecessary
|   +-- equal/superior authority still needs rule-based competence
|
+-- ARTICLE 311(2) REGULAR ROUTE
|   +-- charges -> reply -> evidence -> defence -> findings
|   +-- inquiry report/representation where legally required
|   +-- reasoned proportional penalty -> appeal/review
|   +-- Khem Chand: meaningful opportunity to meet charges
|   +-- 42nd Amendment removed separate constitutional penalty-stage notice
|   +-- ECIL v B. Karunakar: report supply + prejudice; not restored penalty notice
|
+-- THREE EXCEPTIONS
|   +-- (a) conduct leading to criminal conviction
|   |   +-- inquiry dispensed; dismissal not automatic
|   +-- (b) inquiry not reasonably practicable
|   |   +-- competent authority + reasons in writing
|   |   +-- inconvenience != impracticability
|   +-- (c) President/Governor + security of State
|       +-- ordinary public interest/law and order != exact standard
|   +-- Art 311(3) finality does not oust judicial review
|   +-- Tulsiram Patel: exceptions exact, satisfaction reviewable
|
+-- PUNISHMENT / PROCESS DISTINCTIONS
|   +-- dismissal vs removal: both end service; dismissal ordinarily graver
|   +-- penal reduction vs bona fide reversion
|   +-- motive vs foundation/stigma -> Parshotam Lal Dhingra
|   +-- suspension = ordinarily interim, not penalty
|   +-- CCS illustrations only:
|   |   +-- Rule 10 suspension | Rule 11 penalties
|   |   +-- Rule 14 major inquiry | Rule 16 minor procedure
|   +-- criminal proof beyond reasonable doubt
|   +-- departmental proof preponderance
|   +-- acquittal/conviction cross-effect is contextual, not automatic
|
+-- ARTICLE 312: AIS FEDERAL GATEWAY
|   +-- Rajya Sabha national-interest resolution
|   +-- not less than 2/3 members PRESENT AND VOTING
|   +-- then Parliament by law creates common Union-State service
|   +-- 312(2): IAS + IPS deemed created
|   +-- Indian Forest Service constituted 1966 under AIS framework
|   +-- Union common architecture + State cadre deployment/day-to-day control
|   +-- AIJS enabled, NOT established
|       +-- no post below district judge
|       +-- clause (4): specified changes not treated as Art 368 amendment
|
+-- HISTORICAL CLOSURE
|   +-- Art 313: consistent pre-Constitution service law continues until replaced
|   +-- 28th Amendment, 1972:
|       +-- inserts 312A for specified former Secretary-of-State recruits
|       +-- repeals Article 314
|
+-- NEUTRALITY / TENURE / REFORM
|   +-- neutrality = candid non-partisan advice + lawful implementation
|   +-- anonymity supports ministerial responsibility, not secret immunity
|   +-- T.S.R. Subramanian:
|   |   +-- written directions + Civil Services Boards + tenure safeguards
|   +-- no fundamental right to particular posting/promotion
|   |   +-- applicable law may create fair-consideration protection
|   +-- lateral entry:
|   |   +-- expertise + Article 16 fairness + rules + conflict control
|   +-- generalist/specialist = coordination + domain depth
|   +-- Mission Karmayogi = role/competency capacity, not service-law substitute
|   +-- appraisal = outcomes + quality + teamwork + integrity + review
|   +-- whistleblowing = authorised channel + confidentiality + anti-reprisal
|
+-- REMEDIES
```
