# CVC and CBI - Quick-Glance Tree Chart

CVC-CBI ANTI-CORRUPTION ARCHITECTURE
|
+-- CENTRAL THESIS
|   `-- CVC supervises vigilance; CBI is the organisation; DSPE supplies
|       police power; prosecutors prosecute; courts alone determine guilt.
|
+-- DECODE THE ACTORS
|   +-- CVC = Central Vigilance Commission
|   |   `-- statutory under CVC Act, 2003; advice/review/supervision
|   +-- CBI = Central Bureau of Investigation
|   |   `-- executive organisation under Resolution dated 1 April 1963
|   +-- DSPE = Delhi Special Police Establishment
|   |   `-- statutory police establishment under DSPE Act, 1946
|   +-- CVO = Chief Vigilance Officer
|   |   `-- internal preventive/departmental vigilance node
|   +-- PIDPI = Public Interest Disclosure and Protection of Informers
|   |   `-- 21 April 2004 Resolution; CVC is designated agency
|   +-- Lokpal = complaint/referral body under 2013 Act
|   +-- Police/CBI = investigate; prosecution = present case; court = convict/acquit
|
+-- CHRONOLOGY
|   `-- 1941 SPE
|       -> 1946 DSPE Act
|       -> 1 Apr 1963 CBI Resolution
|       -> 1964 CVC Resolution after Santhanam Committee
|       -> 18 Dec 1997 Vineet Narain
|       -> 1998 CVC Ordinance
|       -> 1999 Bill process
|       -> 11 Sep 2003 CVC Act
|       -> 16 Jan 2014 Lokpal amendments operative
|       -> 14 Nov 2021 Director-extension amendment operative
|
+-- CVC DESIGN: SECTIONS 3-6
|   +-- composition = 1 Central Vigilance Commissioner (Chairperson)
|   |                  + not more than 2 Vigilance Commissioners
|   +-- qualifications
|   |   +-- Union/AIS administration including police administration
|   |   `-- central public-sector experience: finance/banking/insurance,
|   |       law, vigilance, investigations
|   +-- category guard = not more than 2 appointees from either pool
|   +-- appointment = President <- PM + Home Minister + Lok Sabha LoP/substitute
|   +-- term = 4 years or age 65, earlier
|   |   `-- VC -> CVC allowed; aggregate Commission service <=4 years
|   +-- removal
|       +-- proved misconduct/incapacity: President -> Supreme Court inquiry
|       `-- listed direct grounds: insolvency, qualifying conviction,
|           outside paid work, infirmity, prejudicial interest
|   `-- Centre for PIL (2011): selection must protect institutional integrity
