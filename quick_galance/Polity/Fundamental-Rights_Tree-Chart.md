                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
Part III converts constitutional values into enforceable limits and duties.
Fundamental Rights are not absolute islands: some protect citizens, others all
persons; most operate vertically against the State, while selected guarantees
also bind private actors; restrictions must rest on law, constitutionally named
grounds, reasonableness and increasingly proportionality. Article 32 makes
remedy part of the right architecture. The doctrinal movement from formal
classification and narrow procedure toward anti-arbitrariness, dignity, privacy
and substantive equality makes Part III a living but text-bounded charter.
