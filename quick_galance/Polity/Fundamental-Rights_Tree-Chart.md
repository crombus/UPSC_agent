## High-yield examiner traps

- Part III provisions are not all separately counted rights.
- Article 15(2) direct horizontality is distinct from Article 15(5) enabling law.
- The 50 per cent principle is an ordinary doctrinal ceiling, not an exceptionless number.
- Internet is a medium; *Anuradha Bhasin* did not create an unlimited standalone right.
- *Navtej* protected intimacy; it did not decide same-sex marriage.
- Article 30 says minorities, not “minority citizens”.
- Ninth Schedule placement is not absolute immunity.
- Article 359 suspends specified enforcement and can never include Articles 20-21.

**Qualified conclusion:** Fundamental Rights are transformative and enforceable, but their force depends on exact beneficiary language, text-bound restriction, named doctrine, effective remedy and carefully confined exceptional power.
