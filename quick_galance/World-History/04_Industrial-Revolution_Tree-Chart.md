                     CENTRAL PRINCIPLE
The Industrial Revolution was not an invention list but a cumulative
reorganisation of energy, production, labour, transport, class and state
power. Britain industrialised first because capital, coal and iron, displaced
labour, agricultural surplus, expanding markets and a commerce-friendly legal
order were simultaneously available. Mechanisation and the factory system
raised productive capacity faster than welfare, concentrating immediate costs
on workers, women, children and industrial towns. Those costs generated trade
unions, labour law and political reform, while industrial rivalry intensified
imperial pressure and produced multiple later paths of industrialisation.

                 QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
The Industrial Revolution was a decisive expansion of human productive power,
not an automatic improvement in welfare. Its British origins lay in a rare
conjunction rather than one invention; its social costs were immediate and
concentrated, while many gains were delayed and diffuse. As industry spread,
