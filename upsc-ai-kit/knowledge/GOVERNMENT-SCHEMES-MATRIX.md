# UPSC GOVERNMENT SCHEMES - QUICK REVISION MATRIX

> **Cut-off:** 14 September 2026.
> Funding type, target group and delivery mechanism are more durable than annual outlays.
> Recheck current guidelines before quoting money, coverage or deadlines.

## Funding and status legend

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CS** | Central Sector: financed primarily by Union budget |
| **CSS** | Centrally Sponsored: Centre-State sharing, usually with special ratios for NE/Himalayan States and UTs |
| **Credit** | Loan, guarantee, insurance or interest-support mechanism; not a free grant |
| **Convergence** | Uses multiple existing schemes, ministries or funding streams |
| **Legacy** | Completed, closed, subsumed or replaced; use only for chronology |
| **Pilot** | Limited rollout; not yet a universal entitlement |

## 17. High-yield exclusion traps

1. **Cash support is not universal:** PM-KISAN, PMMVY, scholarships and pensions use
   eligibility/exclusion lists.
2. **Credit is not a grant:** MUDRA, Stand-Up India, AIF, PM SVANidhi and Vidyalaxmi must be
   repaid.
3. **Insurance is not guaranteed compensation:** enrolment, premium, notified risk and claim
   procedure matter.
4. **A portal is not an entitlement:** e-Shram, DIKSHA, PM-WANI and AgriStack provide
   infrastructure or access.
5. **A mission may have no separate fund:** PM Gati Shakti, Mission LiFE, SAGY and several
   convergence initiatives.
6. **Coverage does not equal outcome:** a tap, account, card or registration does not prove
   regular service, income or welfare.
7. **Umbrella and component are different:** NHM, Mission Shakti, POSHAN 2.0, Samagra Shiksha
   and PMKSY contain multiple components.
8. **Central Sector and CSS are different:** CSS requires State implementation and often
   cost-sharing.
9. **Announcement is not implementation:** Budget proposals enter this matrix only after
   approval/launch or operative guidelines.
10. **Completion is not continuation:** never quote Smart Cities, FAME II, UDAY or HRIDAY as
    newly open schemes.

## Verification hierarchy

1. Cabinet approval and official scheme guidelines.
2. Ministry portal/dashboard and current annual report.
3. Union Budget Expenditure Profile for scheme type and allocation.
4. PIB only when it links or accurately summarises the operative guideline.
5. Current beneficiary portal for application windows and exclusions.
