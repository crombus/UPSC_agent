## 2. Executive verdict

The prior structure was content-rich but not answer-worthy at the Core
tier: all twelve Basic owners lacked a topic-specific thesis/evidence/
verdict architecture, and 2018–2023 GS-III demands pointed to Advanced.
That was a paper-affecting failure even where facts existed.

Repair promoted all answer-essential material into Core. Each Basic owner
now supplies a direct thesis, directive-sensitive 10/15/20-mark spine,
claim → named evidence/example → analysis → limitation units, a
threat–vulnerability–capability–risk chain, implementation/outcome
discipline and topic-specific federal/rights constraints. The post-repair
Core-only adversarial sweep found **0 FAIL / 0 paper-affecting PARTIAL**.
