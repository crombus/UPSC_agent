## Demand evidence

`00_Question-Demand-Ledger.md` records local held papers for 2018, 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025. They repeatedly require a 600-word essay, direct passage answers, one-third précis, translation in both directions, and vocabulary/usage. It does **not** license invention of unreadable OCR marks.

## Completion standard

Coverage is complete only when both independent 180-minute mocks total at least 120/300 **and** every component floor in `00_Master-Framework.md` is met. This is a conservative repository target, not an official qualifying declaration.
