methods, worked examples, translation controls, timed drills और diagnosis

  translation demands Core से executable;

- zero broken local Markdown links।

अंतिम hostile gate ने Core-only रूप में इन अपरिचित माँगों को सफलतापूर्वक
हल किया:

  `को`-marked object agreement;

  order;

  comprehension;
- faithful one-third précis with concession, dependent groups, source
  conclusion और title instruction;
- unfamiliar 600-word Hindi essay with thesis, evidence, balance और final
  Devanagari revision;
- Hindi-English और English-Hindi translation of modality, negation,
  conditions, idioms, proper names, institutional terms and register।
