ingestion.

## 4. Durable maintenance rule

The concepts and answer structures are now canonical and reusable. Before using
the current-linked answers in a later examination cycle:

1. Recheck enactment, notification, commencement and judicial status separately.
2. Recheck whether a mission/project is launched, commissioned, operational or
   merely announced.
3. Date-label all figures, rankings, pilot counts and programme outcomes.
4. Preserve the distinction between sourced fact and analytical inference.
