## 3. Partial mappings that are intentional ownership boundaries

These do not require duplicate content:

- Ancient and Medieval History support the GS-I culture clause, while
  `Indian-Art-and-Culture` owns art forms, literature and architecture directly.
- Geography and Environment provide hazard/ecological foundations, while
  `Disaster-Management` owns institutional and operational disaster doctrine.
- Economy, Indian Society and Social Justice divide the Prelims economic-and-social-
  development umbrella by economic mechanism, social structure and welfare architecture.
- Polity and Governance divide the mixed Prelims polity/governance line and the GS-II
  statutory/regulatory-body line.
- International Relations covers the international slice of current events, not the entire
  current-affairs universe.
- Political Theory is a conceptual support library; it must not be presented as a direct
  standalone UPSC GS syllabus subject.
- Essay's two partial rows reflect the absence of an official UPSC marking rubric for
  "concision" and "effective and exact expression"; internal practice rubrics must remain
  labelled internal.
- Ethics distributes corruption and governance-strengthening across multiple owners rather
  than creating duplicative omnibus files.
- The qualifying English and Hindi folders own language-paper skills only; they do not duplicate
  Essay Paper I's philosophical/thematic preparation.

## 4. Real strengthening priorities

These clauses have coverage, but the current architecture would benefit from clearer dedicated
ownership:

No unresolved architecture-strengthening priority remains from this audit. `PARTIAL` rows now
reflect deliberate shared ownership, source limitations or exam-scheme/rubric caveats rather than a
missing Core spine.

### Strengthening completed

- **Qualifying English and Hindi:** now have separate Core architectures for every official
  clause. English includes parts of speech, sentence grammar, punctuation/comma rules,
  vocabulary, idioms/proverbs, transformation, comprehension, précis and short essays. Hindi
  includes शब्द-भेद, व्याकरण/वर्तनी/वाक्य-शुद्धि, शब्दावली/मुहावरे/लोकोक्तियाँ, बोध,
  संक्षेपण, निबन्ध and both directions of translation. Each has a foundation test and a
  complete 300-mark mock with a separate answer key.

- **CSAT — interpersonal and communication skills:** now has a dedicated exam-complete Topic 07
  Core covering the communication cycle, channels, listening, barriers, feedback, interpersonal
  conduct, conflict, public dealing and questionnaire design. Topic 06 retains only the scenario-
  reasoning interface; optional Advanced depth cannot affect qualification.
- **CSAT — General Mental Ability:** now has an explicit Topic 08 Core integration spine joining
  number, arithmetic, rate, algebra, data-sufficiency, logical-reasoning and communication owners.
  Direct PYQs remain with the tested mechanism owner, avoiding a duplicated omnibus bank.
- **Economy — e-technology in aid of farmers:** now has an exam-complete Core owner and an
  optional Advanced companion in Economy Topic 27. The syllabus row is upgraded from
  `PARTIAL` to `COVERED`, and the 2020 Prelims drone application plus 2023 GS-III
  e-technology question are routed to the new owner.
- **Economy — direct and indirect farm subsidies:** now has an exam-complete Core owner and
  optional Advanced companion in Economy Topic 28. It consolidates subsidy incidence,
  fertiliser/power/irrigation/credit/insurance/price/income support and WTO domestic-support
  rules, and directly owns the 2023 GS-III farm-subsidy question.
- **Economy — technology missions:** now has an exam-complete Core owner and optional
  Advanced companion in Economy Topic 29. It consolidates agricultural mission-mode policy,
  historical/current mission evolution, technology diffusion, production-to-market
  convergence, evaluation and complete 2018 National Horticulture Mission PYQ closure.
- **Economy — economics of animal-rearing:** now has an exam-complete Core owner and
  optional Advanced companion in Economy Topic 30. It consolidates livestock, dairy,
  poultry, small-ruminant, meat, apiculture, fisheries/aquaculture, IFS, markets, One Health,
  environment and direct 2015 livestock-employment PYQ closure.
- **Economy — energy infrastructure economics:** now has an exam-complete Core owner and
  optional Advanced companion in Economy Topic 31. It consolidates power-sector institutions,
  generation-grid-distribution economics, DISCOM finance, tariffs, fuels, access, energy
  security, efficiency, investment and transition, with direct 2018-2025 PYQ closure.
- **Polity — comparison of constitutional schemes:** now has an exam-complete Core owner and
  optional Advanced Topic 47. It compares India with the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, France,
  Switzerland, South Africa, Germany and Japan across executive design, supremacy, federalism,
  rights, secularism, courts, appointments, amendments, elections, pardon and emergencies, and
  closes all identified 2018-2025 comparative PYQ demands.
- **Polity — Ministries and Departments of Government:** now has an exam-complete Core owner
  and optional Advanced Topic 48. It consolidates Articles 73-78, Allocation and Transaction
  of Business Rules, ministry/department organisation, Minister-Secretary relations, Central
  and Cabinet Secretariats, attached/subordinate offices, inter-ministerial coordination,
  accountability, machinery-of-government problems and reform.
- **Polity — statutory, regulatory and quasi-judicial bodies:** now has an exam-complete
  consolidated Core owner and optional Advanced Topic 49. It distinguishes legal source from
  function, maps major rights/economic/social regulators and tribunals, integrates natural
  justice, appeals, capture and accountability, and closes identified 2018-2025 body PYQs.
- **Science and Technology — computing fundamentals:** now has an exam-complete Core owner
  and optional Advanced Topic 25. It connects data representation, hardware, software,
  operating systems, networks, Internet protocols, wireless, cloud, databases, HPC, IoT,
  AR/VR, blockchain, Web3, AI/quantum boundaries and cyber foundations, with all identified
  2018-2025 computing PYQ mechanisms in Core.

The truncated supplied line `Salient features of the Representation of People's A` remains a
source-text limitation. Existing Polity files cover the Representation of the People Acts, but
the master transcription does not silently reconstruct the missing characters.
