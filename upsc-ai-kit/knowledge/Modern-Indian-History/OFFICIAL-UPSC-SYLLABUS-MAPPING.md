## Applicability at a glance

- **Direct official ownership:** **Prelims GS-I** and **Mains Paper II (GS-I)**.
- **Support only:** cross-paper help to Polity, Economy, International Relations, Governance, Social Justice, and Internal Security where those subjects need historical context.
- **Not claimed here:** the culture clause belongs primarily to `Indian-Art-and-Culture`; world history belongs to `World-History`.

## Official clauses quoted verbatim from the root source

> - "History of India and Indian National Movement."
> - "Modern Indian history from about the middle of the eighteenth century until the present-significant events, personalities, issues."
> - "The Freedom Struggle - its various stages and important contributors/contributions from different parts of the country."
> - "Post-independence consolidation and reorganization within the country."

## Ownership boundary

This folder is the direct syllabus owner for modern Indian history, the freedom struggle, and post-independence consolidation/reorganization. It may cross-link into other folders for constitutional doctrine, macroeconomics, foreign policy, or governance design, but those cross-links do not reduce its primary GS-I ownership.
