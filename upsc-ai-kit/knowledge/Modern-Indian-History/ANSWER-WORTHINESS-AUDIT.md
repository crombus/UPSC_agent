## 2. Executive verdict

**Initial audit status:** 🟠 **Strengthening required** — systemic, not topic-local.
**Status after the second pass:** ✅ Complete — **overturned by hostile review.**
**Status after the third (corrective) pass:** ✅ **Complete**

The subject had strong syllabus ownership, accurate facts, good trap-lists and clean Core/Advanced separation. It could not reliably generate marks-worthy answers, because it stored **facts without answer mechanisms**: no directive decoding, no thesis formation, no mark-scaled structure, no `claim → evidence → significance → limit` units, no counter-evidence discipline and no verdict scaffolding. A candidate reading these files would know *what happened* and would have to invent *how to argue it* under exam conditions.

A consistent, topic-specific `## 8. Answer architecture (10/15/20-mark support)` section was written into all 38 Core files. A hostile 24-question Core-only stress test then returned **10 PASS / 13 PARTIAL / 1 FAIL**, and a second corrective pass repaired the FAIL and the eight most paper-affecting PARTIALs.

**That verdict was premature.** An independent hostile review found that the folder still could not answer the transfer-of-power questions, because the **1944–46 negotiation and electoral chain existed nowhere in Core as usable evidence** (§0, finding 1). This was a genuine blocking gap on critical criteria 4 (evidence-led analysis) and 6 (analysis over narration), not a depth complaint: Topic 26's own 20-mark spine referred to an elections step that had no evidence behind it, and Topic 25 asserted an electoral consequence that no owner substantiated. The review additionally exposed six audit-integrity defects, including a mis-counted gap ledger and a Hilton-Young note that implied a closure it had not achieved.

The third corrective pass repaired all of these directly in the knowledge files. Four new Core-only stress tests, written specifically to probe the blocker (§5.1, Q25–Q28), now pass. The subject is marked ✅ Complete because the blocker is repaired in reusable Markdown, every new fact used is traceable to a locally held source book, and the figures the sources do **not** support are prohibited by explicit caution rather than supplied from memory.

**Honest caveats carried forward (not blockers):** **ten** single-fact Prelims demands routed into this folder have **no supporting content in any local source** and are recorded as explicitly bounded open gaps rather than answered from memory (§7.3, §8.3). The Hilton-Young rate is one such open gap and is **not** closed by the Economy cross-link (§7.2). Topic 38 carries **nine routed items, seven of which are current-affairs routing artefacts** with no modern-history content and are labelled as such.
