# MAJOR DISASTER MANAGEMENT COMMITTEES AND COMMISSIONS

> **Use:** trace India's movement from relief-centred administration to prevention,
> mitigation, preparedness, response and resilient recovery.

## National disaster-management architecture

| Priority | Panel/report and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **High-Powered Committee on Disaster Management - J. C. Pant** | 1999-2001 | Develop national, State and district plans covering natural and human-induced hazards | All-hazard classification and multi-level institutional planning; major pre-DM Act framework |
| A | **National Committee on Disaster Management - Prime Minister A. B. Vajpayee** | 2001 | Develop national policy and institutional mechanisms after the Gujarat earthquake | Advanced the national-policy process and mainstreaming of mitigation before the DM Act |
| A | **Second ARC Third Report - Crisis Management: From Despair to Hope** | 2006 | Review prevention, mitigation, response, recovery and governmental coordination | Local plans, incident-command systems, professional response, community capacity and development-linked mitigation |

## Hazard-specific reform

| Priority | Panel and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **National Flood Commission / Rashtriya Barh Ayog - Jaisukhlal Hathi** | 1976-80 | Assess floods and recommend a national management approach | Basin planning, floodplain zoning, forecasting, drainage and catchment treatment |
| A | **Rangachari Experts Committee** | 2001-03 | Review implementation of National Flood Commission recommendations | Exposed weak implementation and renewed emphasis on floodplain regulation and reliable damage data |
| A | **Hanumantha Rao Technical Committee** | 1993-94 | Review drought-prone and desert-development programmes | Watershed-based planning, decentralisation, community participation and programme convergence |
| A | **A. S. Arya Expert Group** | 2002-04 | Recommend disaster-safe housing, infrastructure and techno-legal reform | Model planning/building-law amendments, seismic-code enforcement and professional accountability |
| B | **Ravi Chopra Expert Body** | 2013-14 | Examine environmental degradation and hydropower contribution to Uttarakhand disaster risk | Cumulative basin assessment, environmental flows and disaster-sensitive project appraisal; detailed ecology treatment belongs to Environment |

## Disaster-finance evolution

| Priority | Commission | Year | Disaster-finance contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|
| B | **Ninth Finance Commission - N. K. P. Salve** | 1987-89 | Helped establish the State-level Calamity Relief Fund system from 1990-91 |
| B | **Eleventh Finance Commission - A. M. Khusro** | 1998-2000 | Recommended the National Calamity Contingency Fund alongside State relief funds |
| A | **Thirteenth Finance Commission - Vijay Kelkar** | 2007-09 | Aligned post-DM Act disaster funds and expanded mitigation/response financing logic |
| A | **Fifteenth Finance Commission - N. K. Singh** | 2017-21 | Recommended national and State disaster-risk-management funds with response and mitigation windows |

## High-yield distinctions

| Do not confuse | Correct distinction |
|---|---|
| J. C. Pant HPC and Second ARC | HPC built the pre-Act all-hazard framework; ARC evaluated crisis governance after the Act |
| Flood control and flood management | Control stresses structures; management also includes forecasting, zoning, catchment treatment and preparedness |
| Relief fund and mitigation fund | Relief/response finances immediate needs; mitigation reduces prospective risk |
| Finance Commission and NDMA | Finance Commission recommends fiscal transfers; NDMA frames disaster-management policy/guidelines |

> **Cross-links:** environmental appraisal after the Uttarakhand disaster belongs to
> Environment; Centre-State fiscal architecture belongs to Polity and Economy.
