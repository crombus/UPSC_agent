## B

1. One of the **applicants has** withdrawn.
2. She prefers tea **to** coffee.
3. The **information was** useful.
4. Neither the teachers nor the principal **is** ready.
5. He has been suffering **from** fever since Monday.
6. When I **arrive**, I will call you.
7. The committee **comprises** five members.
8. She is senior **to** me.
9. The child was **lying** on the floor.
10. I do not approve **of** smoking here.
