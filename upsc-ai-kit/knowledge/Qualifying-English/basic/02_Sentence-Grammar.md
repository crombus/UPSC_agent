# Sentence Grammar - MUST-DO

## 1. Sentence completeness

A complete sentence normally needs a subject and finite verb and must express a complete thought.

- Fragment: ❌ *Because the policy failed.*
- Complete: ✅ *The scheme was revised because the policy failed.*
- Run-on: ❌ *The rain stopped we resumed work.*
- Correct: ✅ *The rain stopped, and we resumed work.* / *When the rain stopped, we resumed work.*

## 2. Subject-verb agreement

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Singular subject -> singular verb | The officer **is** present. |
| Plural subject -> plural verb | The officers **are** present. |
| Each/every/either/neither -> singular | Each proposal **has** merit. |
| One of + plural noun -> singular verb | One of the answers **is** wrong. |
| A number of -> plural | A number of students **are** absent. |
| The number of -> singular | The number of students **is** rising. |
| Along with/as well as does not change subject | The minister, along with officials, **is** attending. |
| Either...or/neither...nor -> agree with nearer subject | Neither the officers nor the minister **is** ready. |

## 3. Tense control

| Use | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Habit/general truth | simple present | The Earth revolves around the Sun. |
| Completed past | simple past | She submitted the report yesterday. |
| Past action continuing when another occurred | past continuous | She was reading when I entered. |
| Earlier of two past actions | past perfect | The meeting had ended before he arrived. |
| Past-to-present duration | present perfect continuous | He has been working for five days. |
| Real future condition | if + present, will + verb | If you arrive late, I will leave. |

Do not use *will* in the ordinary if-clause: ❌ *When I will reach home...* -> ✅ *When I reach home...*

## 4. Articles

- **a** before a consonant sound: a university, a European.
- **an** before a vowel sound: an hour, an honest person.
- **the** for something specific, unique, previously mentioned, superlative or a defined group.
- No article for plural/uncountable nouns used generally: *Books are useful. Honesty matters.*

## 5. Prepositions

Memorise combinations, not isolated meanings:

| Correct combination | Common error |
|---|---|
| angry **with** a person / angry **at** conduct | angry upon |
| agree **with** a person / agree **to** a proposal | agree my proposal |
| approve **of** an action | approve to |
| provide someone **with** something / provide something **to/for** someone | provide someone something in formal exam correction |
| prefer X **to** Y | prefer X than Y |
| enter **into** an agreement | enter in |
| wait **for** a person | wait customers |
| comprise parts / consist **of** parts | comprise of |

## 6. Conjunctions

- Coordinating: **and, but, or, nor, for, so, yet**.
- Subordinating: because, although, if, unless, when, while, since.
- Correlative: either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also.

Keep parallel form:

- ❌ *The job requires planning, coordination and to monitor.*
- ✅ *The job requires planning, coordinating and monitoring.*

## 7. Modifiers

Place a modifier beside what it modifies:

- Dangling: ❌ *Walking to office, the rain began.*
- Correct: ✅ *While I was walking to office, the rain began.*
