# Short Essay Writing - MUST-DO

## 1. Six-hundred-word architecture

```text
INTRODUCTION (define issue + thesis)
        |
2-4 BODY SECTIONS
cause / significance / challenge / solution
        |
BALANCE OR LIMITATION
        |
CONCLUSION (answer + practical direction)
```

## 2. Planning in five minutes

Write:

- exact interpretation of topic;
- one-sentence thesis;
- four body headings;
- one example under each;
- conclusion direction.

## 3. Paragraph rule

Each paragraph should contain:

```text
claim -> explanation -> example/evidence -> link to topic
```

## 4. Language rules

- Prefer precise ordinary words.
- Keep most sentences between 12 and 25 words.
- Vary sentence length, but avoid multi-line sentences with several *and/but/which* clauses.
- Use first person sparingly.
- Do not invent statistics.
- A proverb or quotation is optional, never compulsory.
- Stay near the specified length.

## 5. Useful essay dimensions

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Social | Who is affected? Inclusion or conflict? |
| Economic | Costs, incentives, employment, access? |
| Political/governance | Rights, institutions, implementation? |
| Ethical | Fairness, responsibility, dignity? |
| Technological | Opportunity, risk, digital divide? |
| Environmental | Sustainability and long-term effects? |

## 6. Final revision

Check title choice, thesis, paragraph sequence, repetition, agreement, tense, spelling, punctuation and word limit.
