# UPSC Qualifying English - Knowledge Base Index

> **Paper:** Compulsory English | **Standard:** Matriculation or equivalent |
> **Nature:** Qualifying; marks are not counted for ranking.
> **Official aim:** Read and understand serious discursive prose and express ideas clearly and correctly.
> **Primary evidence:** Official syllabus; locally available 2019 and 2021 compulsory-English papers.

## Paper architecture

| Official area | Core owner |
|---|---|
| Comprehension of given passages | `basic/06_Comprehension-and-Precis.md` |
| Precis writing | `basic/06_Comprehension-and-Precis.md` |
| Usage and vocabulary | `basic/01` to `basic/05` |
| Short essays | `basic/07_Short-Essay-Writing.md` |

The available 2019 and 2021 papers use a stable **300-mark, three-hour** structure:

| Section | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Essay of about 600 words | 100 |
| Comprehension | 75 |
| Precis of about one-third length | 75 |
| Grammar, correction and vocabulary | 50 |
