# UPSC Qualifying English — Knowledge Base Index

> **Paper:** Compulsory English | **Standard:** Matriculation or equivalent  
> **Nature:** Qualifying; marks are not counted for ranking.  
> **Official aim:** Read and understand serious discursive prose and express ideas clearly and correctly.  
> **Status discipline:** Paper-format observations below are local-source evidence, not a substitute for the notification applicable to a future examination.

✅ Across these samples, the usable section pattern is: essay about 600 words (100), comprehension (5×15=75), précis about one-third (75), and usage/vocabulary (25+25). All sampled papers direct the candidate not to give a précis title; 2025 says “Do not give or suggest a title.” This kit additionally practises title questions as an unfamiliar-comprehension demand, not as a claimed past-paper précis instruction.

⚠️ **Current qualifying minimum:** The official syllabus confirms qualifying/non-ranking status, but the current-notification minimum has not been line-verified here. Do not rely on an unsourced “25%=75/300” claim. After verification against the applicable UPSC notification, record its URL, publication date and exact clause in `00_Readiness-Tracker.md`.

⚠️ **Non-official coaching safety target:** 120/300 overall, plus the section floors in `00_Master-Framework.md`. It is deliberately distinct from any official eligibility rule.

## Practice order and decision gates

1. Complete `practice/01_Foundation-Test.md` and correct it with `answer-keys/01_Foundation-Test-Key.md`. This diagnoses usage only; it cannot certify reading/writing readiness.
2. Complete the worked exercises in [Core 06](basic/06_Comprehension-and-Precis.md) and [Core 07](basic/07_Short-Essay-Writing.md) without looking at the keys.
3. Attempt [Mock 1](practice/02_Full-Length-Mock.md) in exactly three hours; use its [key](answer-keys/02_Full-Length-Mock-Key.md) only afterwards.
4. Repair every missed demand family in the Core owners and record the result in [the tracker](00_Readiness-Tracker.md).
5. Attempt the independent [Mock 2](practice/03_Full-Length-Mock-2.md) in exactly three hours. Use [its key](answer-keys/03_Full-Length-Mock-2-Key.md) only afterwards.
6. A candidate is **not mock-ready** until both timed mocks meet the non-official safety target, no section is below its floor, and every error is classified and repaired.
