# Qualifying English - Master Framework

```text
WORD CHOICE
   + GRAMMAR
   + PUNCTUATION
   + SENTENCE LOGIC
          |
          v
CLEAR PARAGRAPH -> COMPREHENSION -> PRECIS -> ESSAY
```

## Non-negotiable scoring principle

The paper tests **correct, clear and concise English**, not ornamental writing. Prefer a short correct sentence over a long sentence whose agreement, punctuation or logic is uncertain.

## Three-hour attempt plan

| Task | Suggested time |
|---|---:|
| Read paper and choose essay | 10 min |
| Essay | 50 min |
| Comprehension | 40 min |
| Precis | 50 min |
| Usage and vocabulary | 40 min |
| Revision | 10 min |

## Error-control checklist

Before submitting, check:

1. Every sentence has a finite verb.
2. Subject and verb agree.
3. Pronouns have clear antecedents.
4. Tense does not shift without reason.
5. Articles and prepositions are idiomatic.
6. Commas do not join two complete sentences by themselves.
7. Apostrophes show possession or omission, never an ordinary plural.
8. Essay paragraphs have one controlling idea.
9. Comprehension answers use the passage, not outside knowledge.
10. Precis is approximately one-third, in your own words, without examples or repetition.
