# Special Provisions for Some States — BASIC / FOUNDATION

> **Subject:** Polity · **Tier:** Core · **GS Paper:** GS-II + Prelims · **Current through:** 28 August 2026

## 1. Core idea and ownership

India uses **asymmetric federalism**: States remain constitutionally equal members of one Union, but Part XXI gives some States tailored safeguards for identity, land, representation, law and order, or regional development. This topic owns Articles **371–371J** and the minimum Article 370/35A position needed to distinguish current law. Fifth/Sixth Schedule machinery remains cross-owned by Scheduled and Tribal Areas.

## 2. Article 371–371J master table

| Article | State(s) | Origin | Exact Core mechanism | Constitutional actor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 371 | Maharashtra, Gujarat | current State-specific form after the Seventh Amendment/reorganisation era | separate regional development boards; annual Assembly reports; equitable development funds; technical/vocational and State-service opportunity | President may assign Governor special responsibility |
| 371A | Nagaland | 13th Amendment, 1962 | Parliamentary law on Naga religious/social practices, customary law/procedure, customary justice, and land/resources does not apply unless Assembly resolves | Assembly consent; Governor has bounded transitional law-order role |
| 371B | Assam | 22nd Amendment, 1969 | Assembly committee comprising members from specified tribal areas and others named by Presidential order | President constitutes/defines committee |
| 371C | Manipur | 27th Amendment, 1971 | Hill Areas Committee; Governor responsibility, annual report to President; Union directions possible | President and Governor |
| 371D | Andhra Pradesh, Telangana | 32nd Amendment, 1973; adapted by AP Reorganisation Act, 2014 | equitable opportunity in public employment/education; local areas/cadres through Presidential orders | President |
| 371E | Andhra Pradesh | 32nd Amendment, 1973 | Parliament may establish a Central University | Parliament |
| 371F | Sikkim | 36th Amendment, 1975 | integration, representation, existing-law/court continuity, and Governor responsibility for peace/equitable advancement | Parliament/President/Governor as clause specifies |
| 371G | Mizoram | 53rd Amendment, 1986 | Parliamentary law on Mizo religious/social practices, customary law/procedure, customary justice and land does not apply unless Assembly resolves; Assembly at least 40 | Assembly consent |
| 371H | Arunachal Pradesh | 55th Amendment, 1986 | Governor's bounded law-order responsibility; Assembly at least 30 | Governor after ministerial consultation, using individual judgment; President may terminate responsibility |

| 371J | Karnataka | 98th Amendment, 2012; effective 2013 | Kalyana Karnataka development board, equitable funds/opportunity, and proportionate local seats/posts under Presidential order | President assigns Governor responsibility and may specify reservation |

## 3. High-value distinctions

- **371A versus 371G:** both create subject-specific Assembly-consent shields; Nagaland additionally has resource wording and special Tuensang/law-order arrangements.
- **371B versus 371C:** Assam has a tribal-area Assembly committee; Manipur combines a Hill Areas Committee with Governor reporting and Union-direction architecture.
- **371D status:** the constitutional power survives, but the former Andhra Pradesh Administrative Tribunal was abolished by G.S.R. 30(E), 14 January 2020; do not present that tribunal as currently operating.
- **371F:** not merely a minimum Assembly clause; it is a detailed accession/integration settlement.
- **371J:** Constitution still says Hyderabad-Karnataka; the region is now officially called Kalyana Karnataka.

## 4. Article 370 and Article 35A — current legal minimum

Article 370 remains printed in Part XXI but was rendered inoperative through the 2019 orders/declaration. Article 35A, inserted through the 1954 Presidential Order, ceased to operate with that constitutional reconfiguration. *In re Article 370* (11 December 2023) upheld the constitutional result, held Article 370 temporary and rejected separate internal sovereignty. It did not finally adjudicate the State-to-UT conversion after the Union assurance on restoration.

**Current:** Jammu and Kashmir remains a Union Territory with legislature; Ladakh remains a Union Territory without legislature. No Statehood/Sixth Schedule change had been enacted by 28 August 2026.

> **Exam line:** Indian unity is sustained not by mechanical uniformity but by constitutionally bounded, purpose-specific difference.
