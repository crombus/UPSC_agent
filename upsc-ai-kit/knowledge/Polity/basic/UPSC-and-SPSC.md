## Core idea
The **UPSC** is India's central recruiting agency — the **"watch-dog of the merit system"** — recruiting for
All-India & Central services. Its advice is **only advisory**, not binding. Each state has a **State PSC (SPSC)**;
a **Joint SPSC (JSPSC)** can serve two+ states.

> 🔑 **Appointment vs removal:** UPSC → President appoints & removes. **SPSC → Governor appoints, President
> removes.** JSPSC → **Act of Parliament**, President appoints.
> 🔑 **½ members** must have **10+ years** of government service.

## Must-Know Facts
- ✅ Strength is **not fixed by the Constitution**; avoid treating a past usual range as
  a constitutional or permanent composition.
- ✅ Removal for misbehaviour needs a **Supreme Court enquiry** (advice binding).
- ✅ UPSC expenses are charged on the **Consolidated Fund of India**; SPSC expenses are
  charged on the **Consolidated Fund of the State** (Art 322).
- ✅ **Post-tenure rules are office-specific:** UPSC Chairman gets no further government employment; SPSC Chairman may become UPSC Chairman/member or another SPSC Chairman; members have the narrower Article 319 routes. **No reappointment to the same office.**
- ✅ Advice is **directory, not mandatory** (SC-held); confers **no right to the post**.
- ✅ Cadre/pay/training → **DoPT**, not UPSC.

### 5.2 Qualified thesis options

- *The UPSC's independence is judicially protected at the point of removal (a Supreme Court reference under Art 317) but institutionally thin at the point of function — it recruits without owning promotions, cadre or service-conditions policy.*
- *The SPSC embodies a deliberate federal asymmetry: a State appoints its own commission (the Governor, Art 316) but only the Union can remove its members (the President, Art 317), insulating a State body from State political pressure.*
- *The UPSC's value lies less in binding power than in the transparency of dissent: the Art 323 memorandum on non-accepted advice converts a merely advisory body into an accountability check.*
- *Because Art 320(3) is directory, the UPSC's guarantee of merit is procedural rather than enforceable — its authority rests on convention and the political cost of publicly overriding it.*

### 5.4 Bank A — Composition, appointment and tenure (Art 315–316)

- **Claim → the Constitution creates the body but leaves its size open.** ✅ **Art 315(1)** establishes a UPSC for the Union and an SPSC for each State. → **Mechanism:** members are appointed and the strength fixed by the **President** (UPSC) or **Governor** (SPSC). → **Limit:** ⚠️ the Constitution prescribes **no fixed strength** — do not state a permanent number.

| Attribute | Position | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Legal source | **Arts 315–323, Part XIV** | Directly constitutional (except the JSPSC) |
| ✅ Strength | Determined by the President/Governor; **not fixed** by the Constitution | Avoid citing a "usual" range as constitutional |
| ✅ Qualification (Art 316(1)) | **No qualifications prescribed**, but **one-half** of the members must have held office **at least ten years** under the Union or a State | This ten-year rule is the only composition requirement |
| ✅ Appointment | **President** (UPSC) · **Governor** (SPSC) · **President** (JSPSC) | |
| ✅ Term (Art 316(2)) | **6 years or 65 years** (UPSC), **6 years or 62 years** (SPSC), whichever earlier | SPSC age is **62**, not 65 |
| ✅ Acting chairman (Art 316(1A)) | The President/Governor may appoint a member to act as chairman | |

### 5.5 Bank B — Removal, independence and the post-office bar (Art 317–319, 322)

- **Claim → removal is deliberately hard and centralised in the Union.** ✅ **Art 317(1)**: a chairman or member is removed **only by the President** on the ground of **misbehaviour**, and only **after the Supreme Court**, on a reference by the President, has held an inquiry and **recommended removal** — the SC's advice is **binding**. → **Mechanism:** ✅ **Art 317(2)** lets the President **suspend** the member during the inquiry. → **Limit:** ✅ **Art 317(3)** allows removal on the objective grounds of **insolvency, paid employment outside office, or infirmity of mind/body**, and **317(4)** deems a member guilty of misbehaviour if interested in a government contract.
- **Federal point:** ✅ even an **SPSC** member — appointed by the **Governor** — can be removed **only by the President**, never by the Governor.
- **Independence safeguards:** ✅ security of tenure; ✅ conditions of service **cannot be varied to disadvantage** after appointment (Art 318 proviso); ✅ expenses (salaries, allowances, pensions) are **charged on the Consolidated Fund** of India/the State (**Art 322**), so they are non-votable.
- **Post-office bar (Art 319 — exact scope):**

| On ceasing office | Eligible for | Barred from |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ UPSC **Chairman** | Nothing further | **Any** employment under the Union or a State |
| ✅ SPSC **Chairman** | **UPSC Chairman or member**, or **Chairman of another SPSC** | Any other government employment |
| ✅ UPSC **member** | **UPSC Chairman** or **an SPSC Chairman** only | Any other government employment |
| ✅ SPSC **member** | **UPSC Chairman or member**, or Chairman of **that or another SPSC** | Any other government employment |

- **Exact-scope case:** ⚠️ *A.R. Kidwai* — appointment of a retired UPSC chairman as **Governor** was upheld because a **Governor holds a constitutional office**, not "employment under the Government" within Art 319. There is **no reappointment** to the same office (no second term).

### 5.6 Bank C — Functions, the consultation duty and its limits (Art 320–321, 323)

- **Claim → the UPSC's core function is recruitment and advice, and the advice is not legally binding.** ✅ **Art 320(1)**: conduct examinations for appointments to the services of the Union/State. ✅ **Art 320(3)**: the Commission **shall be consulted** on (a) methods of recruitment; (b) principles for appointments, promotions and transfers; (c) disciplinary matters; (d) legal-expense claims; and (e) pension/injury claims. → **Mechanism:** the **proviso to Art 320(3)** lets the President/Governor make **regulations excluding** matters from consultation. → **Limit:** ⚠️ **Art 320(3) is directory, not mandatory** — non-consultation does **not invalidate** a government decision, and UPSC selection confers **no right to the post**.
- **Extension of functions:** ✅ **Art 321** lets **Parliament** (UPSC) or a **State legislature** (SPSC) confer additional functions, including over the services of a local authority or body corporate.
- **Accountability route:** ✅ **Art 323** — the UPSC presents an **annual report** to the President, laid before Parliament **with a memorandum explaining any case where its advice was not accepted** and the reasons; the SPSC reports to the Governor and the State legislature likewise.
- **Excluded matters:** ✅ the UPSC is **not consulted** on reservations for backward classes, SC/ST claims, appointments to tribunal/commission chairmanships and top diplomatic posts, the bulk of Group C and D, and temporary appointments of a year or less.

### 5.7 Bank D — SPSC and the Joint SPSC (federal design)

| Body | Legal source | Appointed by | Removed by | Term |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ✅ **UPSC** | Constitution (Art 315(1)) | President | President (Art 317) | 6 yr / 65 |
| ✅ **SPSC** | Constitution (Art 315(1)) | **Governor** | **President** (Art 317) | 6 yr / **62** |
| ✅ **JSPSC** | **Act of Parliament** (Art 315(2)) on the States' request — **statutory, not constitutional** | President | President | 6 yr / 62 |

- ✅ **Art 315(4):** the **UPSC may serve a State's** needs on the **Governor's request** with the **President's approval**.
- **Key trap:** an SPSC member is **appointed by the Governor but removable only by the President** — a State functionary answerable, for removal, only to the Union.

### 5.8 Mechanism, institutional incentives and consequences

- **Why removal runs through the President and the Supreme Court:** ⚠️ it insulates a member from the very government — Union or State — whose recruitment and discipline it audits; the consequence is that a State cannot punish its own SPSC for inconvenient advice.
- **Why Art 320(3) is only directory:** ⚠️ it preserves the executive's ultimate responsibility for administration, but the price is that merit protection depends on convention rather than compulsion.
- **Why the Art 323 memorandum matters:** ⚠️ it forces the government to justify **publicly** any departure from the Commission's advice — accountability by transparency rather than by veto.

### 5.9 Criticism, counter-argument, variation and implementation constraints

- **Recruiting-agency-only critique:** ⚠️ the UPSC has **no say in the classification of services, cadre management, pay or training** (these belong to the **DoPT**) or in promotions **policy**; its advice is not binding (Art 320(3)) and it is excluded from wide areas — so it is, in substance, an **advisory recruiting body**, not a personnel authority.
- **Counter (independence is real):** ⚠️ constitutional status, an SC-protected removal, charged expenditure and the Art 323 memorandum give the UPSC genuine autonomy; the "watch-dog of the merit system" role survives despite the advisory limitation.
- **Variation:** ⚠️ several **State PSCs** face vacancies, delays and questions over autonomy and transparency; quality is uneven across States.
- **Implementation constraint:** ⚠️ lateral entry and examination-reform proposals test the merit-recruitment monopoly and must be designed so as not to bypass the constitutional guarantee.

### 5.10 Verdict scaffolds

- **Independence verdict:** "The UPSC is protected where it is removed but constrained where it functions — a body strong enough to resist dismissal yet too advisory to command the personnel system it guards."
- **Advisory-role verdict:** "The UPSC's authority is moral and procedural, not coercive; the Art 323 memorandum, not a binding power, is what keeps its advice consequential."
- **Federal-design verdict:** "By letting the Governor appoint but only the President remove SPSC members, the Constitution keeps State recruitment both State-rooted and Union-insulated."

### 5.12 Factual-risk and current-status controls

- ✅ Removal under **Art 317** is by the **President after a Supreme Court inquiry** — do not say the Governor removes SPSC members, and do not use the "resolution of both Houses" route (that is for judges and the CEC, not PSC members).
- ✅ The Constitution fixes **no strength** — do not assert a fixed number of members.
- ✅ **Art 320(3) is directory** — do not claim UPSC advice is legally binding.
- ✅ The **JSPSC is statutory**, not constitutional; the SPSC retirement age is **62**, not 65.
- ✅ Art 319's bar does not cover a **constitutional office** (Governor) — cite the exact scope, not a blanket ban.

### Semantic-completeness ownership and PYQ control

- **Constitutional map:** Articles 315-323 separately govern establishment,
  appointment/tenure, removal/suspension, service conditions, post-office bars,
  functions, extension of functions, charged expenditure and annual reporting
  for the UPSC, SPSCs and Joint State Public Service Commissions.
- **Composition:** the Constitution fixes no numerical strength. The President
  appoints UPSC and Joint Commission members and determines their number/service
  conditions under Article 318; the Governor performs the corresponding SPSC
  functions. As nearly as may be, one-half of members must have held government
  office for at least ten years.
- **Current UPSC control:** the official UPSC Commission page, last updated
  3 September 2026 and rechecked 5 September 2026, identifies Dr Ajay Kumar as
  Chairman. The package does not freeze an unsupported full roster or a
  constitutionally fixed member count.
- **Tenure and acting chair:** Article 316 gives six years subject to age
  sixty-five for UPSC and sixty-two for SPSC/Joint Commission members. The
  President or Governor, as applicable, may appoint a member as acting chair.
- **Removal/suspension precision:** only the President removes any PSC member.
  Misbehaviour requires a presidential reference, Supreme Court inquiry and
  recommendation under Article 317(1). Pending inquiry, the President may
  suspend a UPSC or Joint Commission member, while the Governor may suspend an
  SPSC member. Article 317(3)-(4) contains the separate objective/deemed grounds.
- **Post-office and finance:** Article 319's eligibility bars are office-specific
  and do not mean one blanket ban. Article 322 charges UPSC/Joint Commission
  expenses on the Consolidated Fund of India and SPSC expenses on the
  Consolidated Fund of the State.
- **Functions and exclusions:** Article 320 covers examinations, recruitment
  methods, appointments/promotions/transfers and disciplinary/claim advice.
  Article 320(4) excludes consultation on Article 16(4) and Article 335 matters;
  the proviso to Article 320(3) authorises valid exemption regulations.
- **Judicial rule:** State of U.P. v Manbodhan Lal Srivastava (1957) holds
  Article 320(3)(c) consultation directory: non-consultation does not by itself
  invalidate action or confer a cause of action. Advice remains institutionally
  important and Article 323 requires reports plus reasons for non-acceptance.
- **Recruitment rule:** selection/recommendation does not itself create an
  indefeasible right to appointment. Tej Prakash Pathak (2024 INSC 847) supplies
  the current Constitution-Bench control against arbitrary mid-process changes
  to recruitment rules, subject to the governing rules and non-arbitrariness.
- **Institution firewall:** UPSC/SPSC recruitment and advice are distinct from
  DoPT cadre administration, the Public Examinations Act integrity framework,
  tribunals, Election Commissions and Finance Commissions. A Joint State PSC
  follows State resolutions and parliamentary law; it is not a permanent third
  constitutionally mandated tier.
