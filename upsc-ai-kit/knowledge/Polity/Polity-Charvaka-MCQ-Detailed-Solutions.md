## Batch 1

### Q1. Administrative Tribunals

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Incorrect

All three statements were correct. Article 323A concerns recruitment and service-condition
disputes of public servants and can be implemented only by Parliament. Article 323B covers
several specified fields, such as taxation and labour, and may be implemented by Parliament or
a competent State Legislature. In *L. Chandra Kumar*, the Supreme Court held that tribunal
decisions remain reviewable by High Courts under Articles 226 and 227.

> **Trap:** Tribunals supplement constitutional courts; they do not replace High Court judicial review.

### Q2. Constitutional Amendment

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Incorrect

A Constitution Amendment Bill may originate in either House of Parliament, but a State
Legislature cannot introduce it. There is no joint sitting if the Houses disagree. After the
24th Amendment, the President must assent to a duly passed Constitution Amendment Bill and
cannot return it for reconsideration.

> **Trap:** Ordinary-bill procedures concerning joint sittings and presidential return do not apply.

### Q3. Anti-Defection Law

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

An independent member is disqualified upon joining a political party after election. A
nominated member may join a party within six months of taking the seat. The one-third split
exception was removed by the 91st Amendment; the surviving merger defence requires at least
two-thirds of the legislature party.

> **Trap:** Do not confuse the deleted one-third split rule with the two-thirds merger rule.

### Q4. Attorney General

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

The Attorney General has a right of audience in all Indian courts and may participate in
Parliament and its committees without voting. The office has no constitutionally fixed tenure:
the Attorney General serves during the President's pleasure. Private practice is permitted
subject to restrictions intended to prevent conflict with the Union government.

### Q5. CAG

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Incorrect

The CAG certifies the net proceeds of taxes under Article 279. A propriety audit examines the
wisdom, faithfulness and economy of expenditure and is discretionary. Unlike the British
comptroller model, India's CAG does not exercise prior control over withdrawals from the
Consolidated Fund; the audit generally occurs after expenditure.

> **Trap:** India's CAG is a comptroller in name but principally an auditor in practice.

### Q6. Central Information Commission

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

The CIC is a statutory body created by the RTI Act, 2005. It possesses specified civil-court
powers and can penalise a defaulting Public Information Officer. It is not a constitutional
body. After the 2019 changes, tenure and service conditions are governed by Central rules.

### Q7. CVC and CBI

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Correct

The CVC's superintendence over the CBI is limited to investigations under the Prevention of
Corruption Act. The CBI ordinarily requires state consent under the DSPE Act, but the Supreme
Court and High Courts can order a CBI investigation without such consent. The CVC is statutory;
the CBI is not a constitutional or independently constituted statutory body.

### Q8. Parliament legislating on State subjects

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Skipped

Article 249 operates through a Rajya Sabha national-interest resolution. Article 252 operates
when two or more states request parliamentary legislation. Article 253 enables Parliament to
implement treaties and international obligations. These are separate exceptions to the normal
State List distribution.

### Q9. Citizenship Amendment Act

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

The facilitated route covers Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, Parsis and Christians from
Afghanistan, Bangladesh and Pakistan who entered India by 31 December 2014, subject to the
statutory conditions. The Act does not grant citizenship automatically; an eligible person
must apply and undergo scrutiny.

### Q10. Charvaka

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

Charvaka accepts perception as the only independent valid source of knowledge. It rejects
inference as certain knowledge because universal concomitance, or *vyapti*, cannot be
conclusively established. It rejects imperceptible *akasha* and treats consciousness as an
emergent property of material elements.

## Batch 2

### Q11. Cooperative Societies

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Correct

The 97th Amendment modified Article 19(1)(c), inserted Article 43B and added Part IXB. In
*Union of India v. Rajendra N. Shah*, Part IXB was invalidated for state cooperative societies
because the amendment had not received the required state ratification. It survives for
multi-state cooperative societies, while Articles 19(1)(c) and 43B remain valid.

### Q12. DPSP amendments

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 39A on equal justice and free legal aid was inserted by the 42nd Amendment. Article 43B
on cooperative societies was inserted by the 97th Amendment. The 86th Amendment recast
Article 45 toward early-childhood care and education for children below six years.

> **Trap:** Article 43B belongs to the 97th Amendment, not the 42nd or 44th.

### Q13. Election Commission

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

The ECI conducts elections to Parliament, State Legislatures and the offices of President and
Vice-President. Local-body elections are conducted by State Election Commissions. The CEC and
Election Commissioners have equal decision-making powers, but removal safeguards differ: the
CEC is removed like a Supreme Court judge, while another EC may be removed on the CEC's
recommendation.

### Q14. Emergency provisions

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 358 is connected only with Article 19 and operates during an emergency based on war or
external aggression. Article 359 permits suspension of the right to approach courts for
specified Fundamental Rights, but Articles 20 and 21 cannot be included. Article 356 rule
beyond one year requires both an operative National Emergency and ECI certification.

> **Trap:** The two conditions for extending President's Rule beyond one year are cumulative.

### Q15. Federal System

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

India is described as a Union of States, residuary powers belong to Parliament, Article 249
enables parliamentary legislation on State subjects, and unequal Rajya Sabha representation
reflects central or unitary bias. These coexist with federal features, producing India's
characteristically centre-leaning federation.

### Q16. Finance Commission

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

The Finance Commission is constituted under Article 280 every fifth year or earlier. It
recommends vertical and horizontal tax devolution, grants-in-aid and measures to augment State
Consolidated Funds for local bodies. The CAG certifies net proceeds. Its recommendations are
advisory rather than legally binding.

### Q17. Fundamental Duties

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Incorrect

Fundamental Duties apply only to citizens. They are non-justiciable, although Parliament may
enact laws connected with them. The eleventh duty, added by the 86th Amendment, concerns a
parent or guardian providing educational opportunities to a child aged 6-14. Paying taxes is
not expressly listed.

> **Trap:** Voting and paying taxes are civic responsibilities, but neither is an Article 51A duty.

### Q18. Fundamental Rights and writs

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Skipped

Articles 17 and 23 have horizontal application and can operate against private persons.
Article 14 is framed as a prohibition against State action. Article 226 empowers High Courts
to issue writs for Fundamental Rights as well as other legal rights, making it wider in subject
matter than Article 32.

### Q19. Tribunal rationalisation

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 323A concerns service disputes. Tribunal decisions remain subject to High Court review
after *L. Chandra Kumar*. Rationalisation or abolition of a specialised tribunal does not
automatically reduce pendency; transferring its cases to an already burdened court may merely
move the backlog.

### Q20. Basic Structure case sequence

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

*Golak Nath* denied Parliament's power to amend Fundamental Rights. The 24th Amendment
expressly affirmed constituent power. *Kesavananda Bharati* permitted amendment of any part
but prohibited damage to the Basic Structure. *Minerva Mills* treated limited amending power,
judicial review and harmony between Fundamental Rights and DPSP as basic features.

## Batch 3

### Q21. Article 31C

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Incorrect

The surviving part of Article 31C protects laws implementing Articles 39(b) and 39(c) against
challenges under Articles 14 and 19. The 42nd Amendment attempted to extend this protection
to all DPSP, but *Minerva Mills* invalidated that extension. Courts can still examine whether
the law genuinely bears the required connection and whether constitutional limitations remain
respected.

### Q22. National Emergency safeguards

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

An Article 352 proclamation requires approval within one month by special majority. Once
approved, it continues for six months and may be renewed. One-tenth of Lok Sabha members may
seek a special sitting to consider disapproval, and the President must revoke the emergency if
the Lok Sabha disapproves it.

### Q23. Federal relations

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 246A creates a special GST legislative power. In *Mohit Minerals*, GST Council
recommendations were treated as persuasive rather than binding. Article 262 permits
Parliament to exclude court jurisdiction over specified inter-state water disputes. Zonal
Councils are statutory bodies under the States Reorganisation Act, 1956.

### Q24. Constitutional education scheme

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Incorrect

The 86th Amendment created an integrated scheme: Article 21A guarantees free and compulsory
education for children aged 6-14; Article 45 concerns early-childhood care below six years;
and Article 51A(k) imposes a corresponding duty upon parents or guardians for ages 6-14.

### Q25. Citizen-only Fundamental Rights

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Incorrect

The standard citizen-only group is Articles 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30. Rights such as equality
before law under Article 14 and protections under Articles 20 and 21 extend to persons,
including foreigners.

> **Mnemonic:** Citizen-only core = **15, 16, 19, 29, 30**.

### Q26. CAT and judicial review

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

CAT was established under the Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985. Parliament cannot finally
exclude constitutional judicial review by High Courts because review under Articles 226 and
227 forms part of the Basic Structure. Tribunals are supplemental forums.

### Q27. Anti-defection procedure

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Correct

Voluntarily giving up party membership may be inferred from conduct and does not require a
formal resignation. A whip violation may be condoned within 15 days. The Speaker functions as
a tribunal whose decision is judicially reviewable. Resignation does not automatically destroy
jurisdiction over an already initiated disqualification matter.

### Q28. Constitutional and statutory offices

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

The CAG and CEC have removal protection similar to a Supreme Court judge. Finance Commission
recommendations are advisory. The Attorney General serves during the President's pleasure.
The CIC is statutory, not constitutional.

### Q29. Citizenship framework

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

Article 9 addresses voluntary foreign citizenship in the commencement framework, while
Article 11 gives Parliament continuing authority over citizenship. OCI is not dual
citizenship. The CAA's facilitated route excludes specified Sixth Schedule and Inner Line
areas.

### Q30. Charvaka doctrines

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

Charvaka challenges the certainty of *vyapti*, rejects testimony as an independent *pramana*,
and explains consciousness as emerging from material elements. It accepts *artha* as an
instrument for securing *kama*, rather than accepting the traditional four-purushartha scheme.

## Batch 4

### Q31. Beneficiaries of Fundamental Rights

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Skipped

Articles 14, 20 and 21 protect persons generally. Article 19 is restricted to citizens.
Article 22 expressly withholds specified arrest and detention protections from enemy aliens.
Article 29(1) protects sections of citizens having a distinct language, script or culture.

### Q32. DPSP amendment matching

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Skipped

The 42nd Amendment inserted Articles 39A, 43A and 48A. The 86th Amendment recast Article 45.
The 97th Amendment inserted Article 43B. The statement associating Article 43B with the 44th
Amendment was incorrect.

### Q33. Articles 249-253

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Skipped

An Article 249 resolution requires two-thirds of Rajya Sabha members present and voting. A law
made under Article 250 ceases six months after the emergency ends. A parliamentary law under
Article 252 may later be adopted by another state. Article 253 does not require the prior
consent of affected states.

### Q34. CAG safeguards

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

Charged expenditure is not submitted to vote but remains auditable. Union audit reports are
submitted to the President, and the CAG is ineligible for further government office after
retirement. The CAG does not exercise prior control over public withdrawals.

### Q35. CIC, CVC and CBI

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

The CIC is statutory under the RTI Act, and the CVC is statutory under the CVC Act. The CBI
derives its investigative powers from the DSPE Act but was created through an executive
resolution; it is not a constitutional body.

### Q36. Fiscal federalism

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Skipped

The Finance Commission recommends vertical and horizontal devolution. Article 270 concerns
shared central taxes. Article 271 surcharges are retained by the Union and excluded from the
divisible pool. The GST Council is constituted under Article 279A.

### Q37. President's Rule

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 356 approval is required within two months by simple majority. The President cannot
assume the constitutional powers of a High Court. Continuation beyond one year requires both
a National Emergency and ECI certification, while the total maximum is three years.

### Q38. The 91st Amendment

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Incorrect

The 91st Amendment removed the split defence, capped a State Council of Ministers at 15% of
Assembly strength subject to a minimum of 12, and barred a disqualified defector from becoming
a minister for the relevant period. The merger threshold is two-thirds, not one-half.

### Q39. Changes outside Article 368

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Skipped

Formation or alteration of states and citizenship provisions may be changed through ordinary
legislative procedure and are treated as amendments outside Article 368. Provisions concerning
the President's election and the federal jurisdiction of the Supreme Court require the Article
368 process and state ratification.

### Q40. Charvaka critique

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Skipped

The rejection of inference attracts a self-refutation objection because the universal claim
that inference is invalid appears itself inferential. *Svabhava-vada* explains events through
the inherent nature of things. Charvaka rejects imperceptible *akasha* and accepts *artha*
instrumentally for pleasure.

## Batch 5

### Q41. Rights available to all persons

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

Articles 14, 20 and 21 extend to persons generally, including foreigners. Articles 15, 16 and
19 are citizen-specific. This citizen-person distinction is a frequent UPSC statement trap.

### Q42. Article 43B

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 43B was inserted by the 97th Amendment. It directs the State to promote voluntary
formation, autonomous functioning, democratic control and professional management of
cooperative societies.

### Q43. State List exceptions

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Skipped

Article 249 corresponds to a Rajya Sabha national-interest resolution; Article 250 to a
National Emergency; Article 252 to requests from states; and Article 253 to international
obligations. All four matches were correct.

### Q44. CAG model

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

Prior control over withdrawals is not a feature of India's CAG. Certification of net proceeds,
submission of reports and assistance to legislative financial accountability are genuine
features.

### Q45. Accountability bodies

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

The CVC and CIC are statutory bodies. The CBI exercises DSPE Act powers but was not itself
constituted by a dedicated CBI Act. This makes it an executive organisation operating through
a statutory investigative framework.

### Q46. Divisible pool

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

Surcharges under Article 271 are imposed and retained by the Union and do not enter the
divisible pool shared with states. Their growing use is a major fiscal-federalism concern.

### Q47. Article 356 extension

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Correct

President's Rule beyond one year requires both constitutional conditions: a National
Emergency operating in the whole or relevant part of the state and ECI certification of
difficulty in conducting elections. The total maximum is three years, and High Court powers
remain untouched.

### Q48. Anti-defection reform

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

The 91st Amendment removed the split defence, preserved the two-thirds merger defence and
capped State Councils of Ministers at 15%, subject to a minimum of 12.

### Q49. Ordinary legislative procedure

- **Your answer:** Skipped
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Skipped

State formation or boundary alteration and citizenship provisions may be changed outside
Article 368. The method of electing the President and federal Supreme Court provisions require
a constitutional amendment with state ratification.

### Q50. Charvaka and inference

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

The proposition that inference is an independently valid source of certain knowledge is
inconsistent with Charvaka. Charvaka may use practical expectations, but it refuses to elevate
inference to an independent certain *pramana*.

## Batch 6

### Q51. Attorney General qualifications

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

The Attorney General must be qualified for appointment as a Supreme Court judge and enjoys a
right of audience in all Indian courts. The Constitution does not prescribe a fixed salary,
and participation in Parliament does not carry voting rights.

### Q52. Selection and control of CBI

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

The CBI Director selection committee includes the PM, Lok Sabha LoP and CJI or nominee. The
CVC selection panel includes the PM, Home Minister and LoP. CVC superintendence is limited to
Prevention of Corruption Act cases. Constitutional courts can order CBI investigations without
state consent.

### Q53. Constitutional status of cooperatives

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

Cooperative societies confined to one state fall under Entry 32 of the State List. Parliament
regulates multi-state cooperatives. *Rajendra N. Shah* invalidated Part IXB only in its
application to state cooperatives while preserving Articles 19(1)(c), 43B and the multi-state
field.

### Q54. ECI jurisdiction

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

The ECI conducts national and state legislative elections and elections to the President and
Vice-President. Local-body elections belong to State Election Commissions. The CEC and ECs
have equal powers, though their removal safeguards differ.

### Q55. Information Commission powers

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Correct

Information Commissions possess specified civil-court powers, can direct disclosure in appeals
and may penalise defaulting PIOs. They are statutory appellate watchdogs of the RTI regime.

### Q56. Citizenship by birth and OCI

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

Article 11 empowers Parliament over citizenship. Under the post-2004 birth rule, both parents
must be citizens, or one must be a citizen and the other not an illegal migrant. OCI holders
cannot vote. Voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship can terminate Indian citizenship
under the statutory framework.

### Q57. Federal structure

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

Indian states possess no right to secede. Parliament may alter their areas or boundaries after
seeking, but without being bound by, the concerned legislature's views. Residuary powers and
single citizenship strengthen the Centre.

### Q58. Fundamental Duties coverage

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Incorrect

Fundamental Duties were inspired by the former Soviet Constitution and recommended by the
Swaran Singh Committee. The Verma Committee identified existing laws enforcing some duties.
They apply only to citizens, not foreigners.

### Q59. Tribunal procedure

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 323A concerns service disputes and can be implemented only by Parliament. Tribunal
decisions remain reviewable by High Courts. Tribunals generally apply their governing statute,
flexible procedure and natural justice; they are not invariably bound by the Code of Civil
Procedure.

### Q60. Centre-State institutions

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 263 contemplates the Inter-State Council. Zonal Councils are statutory. Article 312
requires a Rajya Sabha resolution supported by two-thirds of members present and voting to
create a new All-India Service. States may borrow only within India.

## Batch 7

### Q61. Attorney General tenure

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

The Attorney General serves during the President's pleasure, and remuneration is determined
by the President. There is no constitutionally fixed term, age limit or salary.

### Q62. Nature of Fundamental Duties

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Incorrect

Fundamental Duties are non-justiciable, but legislation may support their enforcement. The
education duty was inserted by the 86th Amendment. Paying taxes is not an express Fundamental
Duty, and Article 51A applies only to citizens.

### Q63. Articles 323A and 323B

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Incorrect

Article 323A covers recruitment and service conditions and can be legislated upon only by
Parliament. Article 323B covers several specified fields, including taxation, labour and land
reforms, and can be used by Parliament or a competent State Legislature.

### Q64. Protective and coordinating provisions

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

Article 355 places a duty on the Union to protect states. Article 365 permits constitutional
consequences when a state fails to comply with valid Union directions. Article 262 concerns
inter-state water disputes, while Article 263 provides for an Inter-State Council.

### Q65. Finance Commission conditions

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Incorrect

The President constitutes the Commission every fifth year or earlier. Its chairperson must
have experience in public affairs, and members may be reappointed. Its recommendations are
advisory, not legally binding.

### Q66. CAG independence and local bodies

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

The CAG is removed like a Supreme Court judge, and administrative expenses are charged upon
the Consolidated Fund of India. The CAG is the PAC's guide, friend and philosopher. Audit of
every local body is not an automatic universal constitutional function; it depends upon the
applicable law or entrustment.

### Q67. CIC and SIC appointment

- **Your answer:** C
- **Correct answer:** C
- **Result:** Correct

The CIC panel consists of the PM, Lok Sabha LoP and a Union Cabinet Minister. The SIC panel
consists of the CM, Assembly LoP and a State Cabinet Minister. The RTI Act permits up to ten
Information Commissioners besides the Chief. These commissions are statutory.

### Q68. CVC, CBI and NIA

- **Your answer:** D
- **Correct answer:** D
- **Result:** Correct

*Vineet Narain* contributed to the reforms that produced statutory CVC status. The Supreme
Court called the CBI a "caged parrot". CBI investigations ordinarily require state consent
under the DSPE framework. The NIA does not require state consent for scheduled offences.

### Q69. Electoral provisions

- **Your answer:** A
- **Correct answer:** A
- **Result:** Correct

Article 325 provides for one general electoral roll without exclusion on specified identity
grounds. Article 326 establishes adult suffrage. Article 329 restricts ordinary judicial
interference during elections. The ECI advises on legislator disqualification under Articles
103 and 192.

### Q70. Cooperative Societies recap

- **Your answer:** B
- **Correct answer:** B
- **Result:** Correct

The 97th Amendment altered Article 19(1)(c), inserted Article 43B and introduced Part IXB.
*Rajendra N. Shah* did not preserve Part IXB completely for state cooperatives; its application
there was invalidated for lack of state ratification.

- CIC and CVC are statutory.

### Charvaka core

        +-- inference rejected: vyapti cannot be certain
        +-- testimony rejected
        +-- four elements accepted; akasha rejected
        +-- consciousness emerges from matter
        +-- God, soul, karma, rebirth and moksha rejected
