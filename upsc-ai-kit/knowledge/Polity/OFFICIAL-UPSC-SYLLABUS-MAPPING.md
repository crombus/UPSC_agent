> GS-II ledger still name an `advanced/` file. Those pointers are historical. As of **2026-08-13**
> every routed demand — 2018-2023, 2024-2025 and 2026 — is owned and answerable inside the `basic/`
> Core owner named in the table below, in its `Answer architecture` section. **No verified PYQ and no
> syllabus clause requires an `advanced/` file; `advanced/` is optional enrichment only.** See
> [`ANSWER-WORTHINESS-AUDIT.md`](ANSWER-WORTHINESS-AUDIT.md), which also records the **2026-08-13
> corrective pass after hostile review** and the **same-day final certification pass** that reversed the
> corrective pass's rejection of the 106th Amendment's **16 April 2026** commencement, added the
> **Census 2027** trigger evidence, and installed the **`commenced ≠ operational`** control.
