The **Constitution (One Hundred and Sixth Amendment) Act, 2023 came into force on 16 April 2026**, notified by the **Ministry of Law and Justice** under the Act's own commencement clause, **s.1(2)** ("It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint"), published in the **Gazette of India** and reported on the Gazette text by mainstream outlets including *The Hindu* / *BusinessLine* and *The Economic Times* |

## 2. Executive verdict

### 2.1 What the pre-repair inspection found

1. **A folder-wide systemic gap.** **None** of the 49 Core files carried any answer architecture. Every
   Core file was a snapshot table + core idea + must-know facts + a current link, followed by generated

   or the criticism/counter-argument material that a GS-II answer needs. Several Core files ended with an
   explicit instruction to go to Advanced *for* cases, exceptions and traps.
3. **51 of the 62 Polity rows in the 2018–2023 GS-II ledger pointed at an `advanced/` file**, and the
   README and syllabus map both asserted that verified applications were "embedded inside" Advanced
   owners. Under the user's Must-Do rule this made the Core provably incomplete for 51 verified demands.
4. **Routed objective demands were listed, not answered.** A generated `### What this owner must now
   support` bullet is a requirement, not coverage. No Core file answered them substantively.

### 2.2 What was done

- A topic-specific **`## N. Answer architecture (10/15/20-mark support)`** section was written into
  **all 49 Core files** (**6,143 inserted lines, 0 deleted lines**), each containing a directive/demand
  map, qualified thesis options, mark-scaled structures, separate evidence banks per demand family,
  mechanism/institutional-incentive analysis, criticism and variation, verdict scaffolds, Prelims
  close-option distinctions and factual-risk controls.
- **All 52 promotable 2018–2023 routed Mains demands** were promoted into their Core owners as an
  executable `N.0 Direct Mains demands owned by this Core file` subsection, each carrying the sentence
  **"Core routing supersedes the older `advanced/NN_…` pointer for this demand."**
- A hostile stress test written **to attack absences rather than confirm headings** exposed **seven
  demand families missing from the entire folder**; all seven were repaired in Core (§8.2).
- README and the syllabus map were rewritten to Basic-first ownership.

### 2.3 Why the verdict is ✅ and not 🟠

Every Core topic now supports directive-sensitive 10/15/20-mark answers and Prelims discrimination from
Core alone; no critical criterion is at 0 or 1 in any topic; the seven absent families found by the first

**upheld and the Core corrected** in the final certification pass (§0.1, §0A).
