# National Integration and Foreign Policy — ADVANCED / OPTIONAL

> Complete Core is in `basic/National-Integration-and-Foreign-Policy.md`. This file adds analytical refinement only.

## Integration beyond assimilation
National integration is equal constitutional membership across diversity. A state-nation model accommodates multiple identities through rights, federalism and asymmetry while maintaining common citizenship and institutions. Accommodation must still satisfy equality, democratic accountability and state capacity.

## Security and constitutional loyalty
Security action is strongest when embedded in legality, necessity, proportionality, review and remedy. Article 355 does not erase rights; Article 356 remains exceptional and reviewable under *S. R. Bommai*. Dissent, migration and regional identity must not be treated as presumptive disloyalty.

## Treaty accountability
Executive flexibility under Article 73 and implementation power under Article 253 create capacity but also a scrutiny gap. A treaty-publication law, impact assessments, parliamentary committee review and structured State consultation would improve legitimacy without transferring foreign competence to States.

## Strategic autonomy
Strategic autonomy is decision independence under interdependence. It requires diversified partnerships, domestic technological and defence capacity, resilient supply chains and issue-based coalitions. 'Multi-alignment' is analytical shorthand, not a constitutional or binding official doctrine.

## Current-evidence discipline
Use the MEA Annual Report 2024 and the 17 August 2024 Voice of Global South Summit as dated evidence. Do not freeze volatile membership counts, call the NIC legally abolished, or present slogans as doctrine.
