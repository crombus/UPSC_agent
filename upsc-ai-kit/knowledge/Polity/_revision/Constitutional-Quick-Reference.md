# CONSTITUTIONAL QUICK-REFERENCE HUB

Use these three sheets together:

1. [`Constitutional-Articles-Quick-Map.md`](Constitutional-Articles-Quick-Map.md) -
   high-yield Articles arranged by constitutional theme.
2. [`Constitutional-Schedules-Quick-Map.md`](Constitutional-Schedules-Quick-Map.md) -
   all 12 Schedules, 22 languages, 29 Panchayat subjects and 18 Municipal subjects.
3. [`Constitutional-Amendments-1-to-106.md`](Constitutional-Amendments-1-to-106.md) -
   every enacted Constitutional Amendment, its provisions and substantive effect.

## Revision order

```text
Article cluster -> linked Schedule -> Amendment history -> controlling judgment
```

## Coverage rule

- The amendment ledger is exhaustive from the 1st through the 106th.
- The Article sheet is selective and examination-oriented; the official Constitution remains
  controlling for every clause and proviso.
- Pending, defeated or lapsed amendment Bills are not numbered amendments.
- Enactment, commencement and operationalisation are separate questions.
