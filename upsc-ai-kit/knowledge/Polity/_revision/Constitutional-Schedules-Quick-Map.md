# CONSTITUTIONAL SCHEDULES - QUICK MAP

| Schedule | Linked provisions | Subject matter | High-yield trap |
|---:|---|---|---|
| First | Arts 1, 4 | Names and territories of States and Union Territories | Territorial changes can alter this Schedule through Art 2/3 legislation; not every such change requires Art 368 |
| Second | Multiple office-specific Articles | Emoluments, allowances and privileges of President, Governors, presiding officers, judges and CAG | It does not contain salaries of every constitutional authority |
| Third | Arts 75, 84, 99, 124, 148, 164, 173, 188, 219 | Forms of oaths and affirmations | President and Governor use forms contained in their respective Articles, not the Third Schedule |
| Fourth | Arts 4, 80 | Allocation of Rajya Sabha seats to States and Union Territories | Rajya Sabha representation is not equal for every State |
| Fifth | Arts 244(1), 339(1) | Administration and control of Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes outside the Sixth-Schedule States | President declares Scheduled Areas; Governor has a special reporting/regulatory role |
| Sixth | Arts 244(2), 275(1) | Tribal areas and autonomous councils in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram | It does not apply to every tribal or Scheduled Area in India |
| Seventh | Art 246 and related federal provisions | Union, State and Concurrent Lists | Residuary power normally belongs to Parliament; GST introduced a special concurrent field through Art 246A |
| Eighth | Arts 344(1), 351 | Constitutionally recognised languages | Recognition does not automatically make every listed language an official language of the Union |
| Ninth | Art 31B | Laws receiving protection from Fundamental Rights challenge | Not absolute immunity: post-24 April 1973 insertions face basic-structure review under *I. R. Coelho* |
| Tenth | Arts 102(2), 191(2) | Anti-defection provisions | Speaker/Chairman's decision is subject to judicial review; merger exception requires two-thirds |
| Eleventh | Art 243G | 29 subjects potentially devolved to Panchayats | Listing does not itself transfer functions, finances and functionaries; State law must devolve them |
| Twelfth | Art 243W | 18 subjects potentially devolved to Municipalities | Listing is enabling, not automatic operational devolution |

## Eighth Schedule: 22 languages

| Original 14 | Added later |
|---|---|
| Assamese, Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu | Sindhi (21st); Konkani, Manipuri and Nepali (71st); Bodo, Dogri, Maithili and Santhali (92nd) |

> The 96th Amendment changed the English constitutional name from **Oriya** to **Odia**;
> it did not add a new language.
