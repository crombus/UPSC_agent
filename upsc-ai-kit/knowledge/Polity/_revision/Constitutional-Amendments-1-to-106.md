## Current-status capsule for the 106th Amendment

- The Act was enacted in 2023 and **commenced on 16 April 2026**.
- Commencement does **not** make the seat reservation immediately operational.
- Article 334A links operation to publication of the relevant first post-commencement census
  figures followed by delimitation.
- Seat rotation will operate after delimitation as provided by parliamentary law.
- State Legislative Councils and Rajya Sabha are outside this reservation design.

## Essential judicial-status traps

| Amendment | Status rule |
|---|---|
| 1st / Ninth Schedule | Post-24 April 1973 Ninth Schedule insertions remain reviewable if they damage basic structure |
| 25th | Protection of Art 39(b)-(c) laws survives within the limits drawn by *Kesavananda* and later doctrine |
| 39th | Art 329A clauses insulating the Prime Minister's election were struck down |
| 42nd | Several provisions were repealed by the 43rd/44th or struck down, including the attempt to make amending power unlimited |
| 52nd | Tenth Schedule survives, but paragraph 7 was invalid for lack of State ratification |
| 97th | Part IXB is invalid for State cooperatives but operative for multi-State cooperatives |
| 99th | NJAC amendment was struck down; collegium framework revived |
| 103rd | EWS reservation amendment upheld by the Supreme Court |
| 106th | Commenced but reservation remains census-and-delimitation dependent |
