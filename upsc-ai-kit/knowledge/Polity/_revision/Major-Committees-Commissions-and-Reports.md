# MAJOR POLITY COMMITTEES, COMMISSIONS AND REPORTS

> **Use:** memorise the chair, constitutional problem examined, and one lasting contribution.
> Recommendations are not law unless separately enacted or judicially adopted.

## Constitution-making committees

| Priority | Committee and chair | Year | Assigned task | Lasting contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **Drafting Committee - B. R. Ambedkar** | 1947-49 | Convert Assembly decisions and the Constitutional Adviser's draft into a coherent constitutional text | Prepared and revised the Draft Constitution |
| A | **Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities and Tribal and Excluded Areas - Vallabhbhai Patel** | 1947-49 | Recommend rights, minority safeguards and arrangements for tribal/excluded areas | Shaped Fundamental Rights and Fifth/Sixth Schedule safeguards |
| A | **Union Powers Committee - Jawaharlal Nehru** | 1947 | Determine Union legislative and executive powers | Helped design the Union, State and Concurrent distribution |
| A | **Union Constitution Committee - Jawaharlal Nehru** | 1947 | Design the Union executive, legislature and federal framework | Supported parliamentary government and principal Union institutions |
| A | **Provincial Constitution Committee - Vallabhbhai Patel** | 1947 | Design provincial executives and legislatures | Shaped the parliamentary model in the States |
| B | **Rules of Procedure Committee - Rajendra Prasad** | 1946-47 | Frame rules governing Constituent Assembly business | Structured debate, motions and committee procedure |
| B | **Steering Committee - Rajendra Prasad** | 1947-49 | Coordinate the Assembly's agenda and progress | Managed sequencing of constitutional business |

## Local government and decentralisation

| Priority | Committee and chair | Year | Assigned task | Lasting contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **Balwantrai Mehta Committee** | 1957 | Review Community Development and National Extension Service | Three-tier Panchayati Raj with Panchayat Samiti as the key development tier |
| A | **Ashok Mehta Committee** | 1977-78 | Review weaknesses of Panchayati Raj | Two-tier district-mandal structure, constitutional protection and regular elections |
| A | **G. V. K. Rao Committee** | 1985 | Review rural-development and poverty-alleviation administration | Made Zila Parishad central to district planning and development |

| B | **P. K. Thungon Committee** | 1988 | Examine political and administrative structure in districts | Constitutional status, fixed tenure and stronger finance for local bodies |

## Federalism and constitutional operation

| Priority | Commission and chair | Year | Assigned task | Lasting contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **Sarkaria Commission - Justice R. S. Sarkaria** | 1983-88 | Review Centre-State relations without weakening national unity | Article 356 as last resort, Inter-State Council and conventions for Governors |
| A | **Punchhi Commission - Justice M. M. Punchhi** | 2007-10 | Reassess Centre-State relations in a changed political, economic and security setting | Localised emergency concept, stronger intergovernmental consultation and clearer Governor conventions |

## High-yield distinctions

| Do not confuse | Correct distinction |
|---|---|
| Union Powers and Union Constitution Committees | Powers Committee allocated competences; Constitution Committee designed Union institutions |
| Balwantrai Mehta and Ashok Mehta | Balwantrai: three tiers and development administration; Ashok: district-mandal design and constitutional protection |
| Sarkaria and Punchhi | Sarkaria is the foundational Centre-State review; Punchhi revisited the framework after coalition, security and economic changes |
| Chavan and Dinesh Goswami | Chavan addressed defections; Goswami addressed the wider electoral system |
| NCRWC and a Constituent Assembly | NCRWC reviewed constitutional working within the existing basic structure; it did not draft a new Constitution |
