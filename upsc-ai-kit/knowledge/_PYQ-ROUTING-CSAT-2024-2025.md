> ## Scope and honesty rules
>
> **Controlling routing/provenance record for 2024-2025.** Each row records where a
> printed question already belongs in this knowledge base. A row is a pointer, not an
> answer key. Question wording was read only from the local `knowledge-export/` OCR text;
> nothing was reconstructed from memory, coaching sites or aggregators.
>
> **Neutral labels.** The theme column names the subject matter in neutral words and does
> not resolve the item (no option letters, no verdicts).
>
> **OCR honesty.** Rows whose English text or exact question number could not be read with
> confidence are marked `OCR-uncertain ... manual verification needed`; the missing wording
> is left missing.
