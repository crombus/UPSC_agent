**Core proposition:** This tracker file exists specifically to hold time-sensitive,
frequently updated facts (new Ramsar sites, new Tiger Reserves, species IUCN-status
changes, new COP outcomes) separately from the stable conceptual files (Topics 01-27), so
that updating current affairs never requires rewriting the underlying concept explanations —
always pair a static-concept file with this tracker's latest verified entry for a complete
answer.

## 2. Essential definitions

| Concept | Exam-ready meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Current-affairs anchor** | A specific, dated, source-attributed fact (e.g., a new protected-area notification, a species status change) used to update or exemplify a static concept. |
| ✅ **Source attribution** | Citing the exact originating document (PIB press release ID, MoEFCC notification, official convention Secretariat announcement) and its date for any dated claim. |
| ✅ **Milestone tracking** | Recording significant threshold events (e.g., India's 100th Ramsar site, a new Tiger Reserve count) as they occur, each with its precise date. |
| ✅ **Status-change tracking** | Recording changes in a species' IUCN Red List category, CITES Appendix listing, or Wildlife Protection Act Schedule, each with its effective date. |

## 3. Topic mechanism

1. Static conceptual knowledge (Topics 01-27) explains *what* a Ramsar site, Tiger Reserve,
   IUCN category or COP mechanism *is* and *how it works* — this foundational knowledge does
   not change year to year.
2. Current affairs supply the *latest specific instance* of that concept (e.g., which
   wetland became India's 100th Ramsar site, which reserve became the 58th Tiger Reserve,
   which COP made which finance decision) — this changes frequently and must be tracked
   separately with precise dates.
3. A complete, exam-ready answer combines both layers: the static concept explains the
   mechanism and significance; the current-affairs anchor supplies the specific, dated,
   verifiable fact that demonstrates active, up-to-date knowledge.
4. This tracker file should be periodically updated (whenever a genuinely new, verified
   milestone or status change occurs) rather than treated as a one-time, static document —
   its value depends on continued, disciplined updating with accurate source attribution.
5. When a specific figure or claim cannot be verified against an official primary source at
   the time of updating, it should be omitted entirely rather than estimated or
   approximated, preserving this tracker's reliability as a fact-checked reference.

## 4. Institutions and policy tools

- ✅ **PIB (Press Information Bureau):** primary official source for Government of India
  announcements, including environment-related milestones (each release carries a citable
  PRID number).
- ✅ **MoEFCC:** source for notifications on protected areas, Schedule changes, and policy
  updates.
- ✅ **NTCA:** source for Tiger Reserve count and tiger-census updates.
- ✅ **Ramsar Convention Secretariat:** source for the official global Ramsar site count and
  designations.
- ✅ **IUCN:** source for Red List category updates.
- ⚠️ Secondary sources (news reports, coaching-material summaries) should be cross-checked
  against these primary sources before being treated as verified facts in this tracker.

## 5. Verified current entries (each dated and source-attributed)

- ✅📰 **CBD COP16 — Cali 2024/resumed Rome 2025:** DSI benefit-sharing was
  operationalised through the Cali Fund, alongside finance and KMGBF-monitoring
  decisions (source: CBD Secretariat).
- ✅📰 **Ramsar sites — India reached 100 Ramsar sites on 5 June 2026** (World Environment
  Day), with the Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal), Ballia district, Uttar
  Pradesh, as the 100th site (source: PIB PRID 2269208; Ramsar Convention Secretariat). At
  this point, India held the highest number of Ramsar sites in Asia (verify any updated
  global comparative ranking against the latest Ramsar Secretariat data before citing
  further).
- ✅📰 **Tiger Reserves — India's count reached 58** with Madhav Tiger Reserve (Madhya
  Pradesh) notified in March 2025, following Ratapani Tiger Reserve (Madhya Pradesh) in
  December 2024 (source: NTCA/MoEFCC notifications; verify the latest count before citing,
  as new reserves are periodically added).
- ✅📰 **Biosphere Reserves — India has 18 notified Biosphere Reserves**; 13 are in
  UNESCO's World Network after Cold Desert (Himachal Pradesh) joined in 2025
  (MoEFCC/UNESCO, verified 2026). Keep the UNESCO subset distinct from the domestic total.
- ✅📰 **Protected areas — as of March 2025** (WII ENVIS Protected Area Database), India had
  106 National Parks, 573 Wildlife Sanctuaries, 115 Conservation Reserves and 220 Community
  Reserves (verify the latest database update before citing a specific count).
- ✅📰 **Forest cover — ISFR 2023** (released December 2024): total forest and tree cover of
  8,27,357 sq km, 25.17% of India's geographical area (forest cover 21.76%, tree cover
  3.41%).
- ✅📰 **Climate finance — COP29 (Baku, 2024):** New Collective Quantified Goal (NCQG) of at
  least USD 300 billion/year by 2035 (developed-to-developing), with a broader USD 1.3
  trillion/year aspirational total-mobilisation goal via the Baku-to-Belém Roadmap, carried
  forward at COP30 (Belém, 2025).
- ✅📰 **Great Indian Bustard:** Critically Endangered (IUCN). In **M.K. Ranjitsinh & Ors. v.
  Union of India (Supreme Court, 21 March 2024)** the Court **modified** its April 2021
  blanket direction on undergrounding overhead power lines across bustard habitat,
  substituting an expert-committee-led, area-specific feasibility assessment, and in the same
  judgment recognised a **right against the adverse effects of climate change** under
  Articles 14 and 21 — verify the latest order date and expert-committee status before
  citing case progress.

## 7. UPSC traps

- ❌ Current-affairs figures (Ramsar count, Tiger Reserve count, forest-cover percentage) are
  fixed, unchanging numbers to memorise once and reuse indefinitely. -> They are dated
  snapshots that change as new notifications/reports are issued — always verify and cite
  the source/date.
- ❌ A number seen in a coaching-material summary can be cited without independent
  verification. -> Cross-check against the primary official source (PIB/MoEFCC/NTCA/
  Ramsar/IUCN) before treating it as reliable.
- ❌ Static concept knowledge and current-affairs facts are interchangeable substitutes for
  each other. -> A complete answer needs both: the concept explains significance/mechanism,
  the current fact demonstrates up-to-date awareness.
- ❌ Every environment-related news item is equally exam-relevant. -> Prioritise items with
  clear GS-paper linkage, PYQ precedent, or genuine policy/legal significance over
  decorative or purely descriptive news items.
