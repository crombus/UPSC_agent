## 1. Visual foundation

```text
NEWS NAME / CLAIM
        ↓
IDENTITY: accepted scientific name + synonym + taxon
        ↓
PLACE: habitat + occurrence + natural range + native/endemic/migratory status
        ↓
STATUS: IUCN assessment year | WPA/Gazette date | CITES effective date | CMS effective date
        ↓
ACTION: ministry/institution + programme/order + exact status verb
        ↓
OUTCOME: population/habitat evidence + method + as-on date
        ↓
EXPIRY: next recheck date + source + unresolved field
```

**Core proposition:** Topic 28 owns the method for converting a species mention into a
dated, source-controlled record. It does not duplicate the full doctrine owned by Topic 05
(IUCN/endemism), Topic 08 (Indian wildlife law), Topic 09 (CITES) or Topic 10 (CMS).
General climate, energy, pollution, waste, Ramsar-count and protected-area dashboards belong
to their respective owners, not to this species tracker.

## 2. Essential definitions

| Concept | Exam-ready meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Species record** | One taxon-specific entry whose identity, place, ecology, threats, statuses, action and dates are kept in separate fields. |
| ✅ **Accepted name** | The currently accepted scientific name in a recognised taxonomic source; important synonyms and common/local names are retained separately. |
| ✅ **Occurrence** | Verified presence at a stated place and date; it does not automatically establish breeding, residence, abundance or complete range. |
| ✅ **Native** | Naturally occurring without human introduction. |
| ✅ **Endemic** | Naturally restricted to a defined region; not a synonym for native, rare, threatened or protected. |
| ✅ **Assessment date** | The date/year of the IUCN assessment itself, not the date on which a webpage was accessed or a news report was published. |
| ✅ **Operative date** | The date from which a statute, notification or treaty listing has legal effect. |
| ✅ **Status verb** | Proposed, adopted, notified, effective, launched, funded, implemented, assessed, recorded, rediscovered or recovered; these verbs are not interchangeable. |
| ⚠️ **Analytical inference** | A reasoned interpretation or policy conclusion that must be labelled separately from a directly sourced fact. |

## 3. Topic mechanism

1. **Resolve identity first.** Common names can be non-unique; taxonomy can be revised,
   renamed, split or lumped. Record the accepted scientific name, authority/source and
   relevant synonym before attaching range or legal status.
2. **Separate place fields.** Habitat, one occurrence, total natural range, native status,
   endemism, residence, migration, breeding range and captive presence answer different
   questions.
3. **Keep four status systems separate.** IUCN evaluates extinction risk; the Wild Life
   (Protection) Act supplies Indian legal status; CITES regulates international trade; CMS
   coordinates migratory-range conservation.
4. **Use two dates where necessary.** Record publication date separately from assessment,
   survey, operative or as-on date.
5. **Trace the threat mechanism.** Link pressure → exposure → biological sensitivity →
   consequence → intervention rather than listing generic threats.
6. **Name the actor precisely.** BSI/ZSI document taxonomy and occurrence; WII and State
   agencies study populations/habitat; MoEFCC and competent governments notify law/policy;
   WCCB, Customs and State authorities enforce.
7. **Tag evidence.** Use `✅ Fact` only for a directly supported proposition and `⚠️
   Inference` for synthesis, significance or recommendation.
8. **Expire dynamic fields.** Recheck before examination use and whenever the controlling
   assessment, Gazette, CITES/CMS Appendices, court order or survey is updated.

## 4. Institutions and policy tools

- ✅ **Taxonomy/distribution:** BSI, ZSI, Kew/recognised taxonomic databases, GBIF-backed
  accepted-name records and peer-reviewed taxonomic revisions.
- ✅ **IUCN Red List:** global/regional category, criteria and assessment date.
- ✅ **India Code, Gazette and MoEFCC:** current Indian statutory schedule, notification and
  operative date.
- ✅ **CITES Secretariat/Checklist:** Appendix, population/annotation, reservation and
  effective date.
- ✅ **CMS Secretariat/species database:** Appendix, instrument, population/range and
  effective date.
- ✅ **WII/State Forest Departments/named survey reports:** habitat, occupancy, abundance,
  mortality or movement evidence with method and reference period.
- ✅ **Judgment/order itself:** court direction and modification date.
- ⚠️ **News reports:** discovery leads only unless they reproduce and link the controlling
  primary record.

## 5. Required tracker record

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Identity | common/local name; accepted scientific name; taxon; important synonym; naming source/date |
| Place | habitat; occurrence; natural range; native/alien; endemic/not endemic; resident/migratory; breeding/stopover/wintering role |
| Ecology | activity, feeding, breeding, movement, defence, communication or life-form only where sourced |
| Threat | pressure → exposure → sensitivity → biological consequence |
| IUCN | global/regional scale; category; criteria where relevant; assessment date/year |
| Indian law | current Schedule/instrument; Gazette/India Code source; operative date |
| CITES | Appendix; population/annotation; effective date; reservation check |
| CMS | Appendix/instrument; population/range; effective date; reservation/participation check |
| News trigger | exact event, issuer, publication date and status verb |
| Programme/action | ministry/institution; intervention; announced/launched/notified/implemented status |
| Outcome | survey/report, method, area, as-on period and uncertainty |
| Evidence tags | `✅ Fact` and `⚠️ Inference` kept visibly separate |
| Maintenance | last checked; expiry/recheck date; next controlling source; unresolved field |

### 5.1 Update and expiry controls

| Dynamic field | Recheck trigger | Default control |
|---|---|---|
| IUCN category | reassessment or taxonomic revision | verify before every exam cycle; always retain assessment year |
| WPA Schedule | Gazette/Act amendment | verify current Gazette before current-status use |
| CITES | CoP amendment, reservation or annotation change | verify current Checklist and effective date |
| CMS | COP amendment, reservation or instrument change | verify revised Appendices/instrument page |
| Population/trend | newer comparable survey | record method and as-on year; never relabel publication year as survey year |
| Court/programme | later order, notification or implementation report | distinguish direction/launch from compliance/outcome |
| Taxonomy/range | revision, split/lump or verified range extension | retain former name and taxonomic concept |

No card is “permanently current.” If a controlling source cannot be rechecked, mark the field
`verification pending` rather than filling it from memory.

## 6. Must-Know Facts for Prelims

- Common name, accepted scientific name, taxon and life-form are separate fields.
- Native and endemic are not synonyms; endemic does not automatically mean threatened.
- A sighting proves occurrence at a place/date, not breeding, abundance or complete range.
- Captive presence and reintroduction do not by themselves establish wild range or a
  self-sustaining population.
- IUCN category must include scale and assessment year.
- WPA Schedule, CITES Appendix and CMS listing require separate legal sources and operative
  dates.
- CITES and CMS listings may be population-specific or qualified by annotations,
  reservations or instruments.
- Publication date and assessment/survey/effective date may differ.
- Proposed, adopted, notified, effective, launched, implemented and recovered are distinct.
- A programme launch, court order, seizure, captive output or rediscovery is not biological
  recovery.

## 7. UPSC traps

- ❌ News common name proves taxonomy. → Verify the accepted scientific name and synonym.
- ❌ One locality or protected-area record proves endemism. → It proves occurrence only.
- ❌ Range state means breeding country. → It includes all life-cycle jurisdictions.
- ❌ An IUCN category automatically fixes WPA/CITES/CMS status. → Check each instrument.
- ❌ A current webpage means a current assessment. → Use the assessment year.
- ❌ CoP adoption and legal entry into force are the same date. → Record both.
- ❌ Captive breeding/reintroduction proves wild recovery. → Require comparable wild outcome
  evidence.
- ❌ A news article can serve as the sole legal authority. → Trace the Gazette, database,
  judgment or treaty instrument.
- ❌ A precise population number is safe without method and reference year. → Omit or qualify
  it.

## 13. Core answer architecture (10/15/20-mark support)

### 13.1 Current-affairs evidence protocol

Use a current species fact only after recording: **accepted identity → issuing source →
publication date → assessment/effective/as-on date → geographic/taxonomic scale → status
verb → what the fact proves → what it does not prove → expiry/recheck control**.

| Status verb | Safe use | Unsafe substitution |
|---|---|---|
| Announced/targeted | policy intention | achievement |
| Adopted | decision taken at a meeting | entered into force for every Party |
| Notified/listed/designated | legal or administrative status | implementation or recovery |
| Launched/funded | programme or resources initiated | completed field action |
| Recorded/rediscovered | detected at a place/date | resident population or recovery |
| Reintroduced/released | human-assisted return/release | self-sustaining wild population |
| Assessed/downlisted | risk reassessed | programme-caused recovery |

### 13.2 Reusable evidence units

| Claim | Named evidence/example → significance | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Taxonomy controls every later field. | **Foxtail orchid — accepted *Rhynchostylis retusa*** → prevents common-name and synonym drift. | An accepted name does not establish endemism or legal status. |
| Assessment year prevents stale “current” claims. | **Peacock/Gooty tarantula — global CR, assessed 1 January 2008** → preserves the age of the evidence. | A recent PYQ or article does not refresh the assessment year. |
| Legal systems move on different clocks. | **WPA Gazette 28 May 2025; CITES Appendices valid 5 March 2026; CMS revisions effective 27 June 2026** → demonstrates separate operative dates. | Never infer one listing from another. |
| Capacity is not outcome. | **IUCN India assessor training, 8-11 July 2026; story 31 July 2026** → documents assessment capacity-building. | It does not prove 11,000 completed assessments or species recovery. |

### 13.3 Mark-scaled spines

- **10 marks:** define the disputed field, use one dated species evidence unit and state one
  inference limit.
- **15/20 marks:** move from identity/range to threat, four status checks, institution,
  implementation and measured outcome; use two or three dated species cases.
- Do not guess a category, Schedule, Appendix, range, population or programme outcome. Mark
  the missing field `verification pending`.
