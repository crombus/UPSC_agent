## How this folder works

- **Two tiers per topic:** `basic/NN` gives the must-do foundation (definitions, mechanism,
  Indian institutions, Prelims facts/traps, a dated current anchor, Mains framework);
  `advanced/NN` adds legal/governance depth, controversies, implementation gaps, data
  limitations and a fuller Mains-answer architecture.
- **Same number = same topic** across both tiers (e.g., `basic/07_...md` pairs with
  `advanced/07_...md`).
- **Tagging:** ✅ = directly grounded in an official/primary source; ⚠️ = analytical
  inference or standard synthesis; 📰 = a verified, dated current-affairs anchor.
- **Answer discipline:** use these files as the first reference. Every dated fact carries
  its source and date — if a newer figure supersedes one here, cite the newer source rather
  than this file's original figure, and update Topic 28's tracker accordingly.
- **Dynamic data:** prefer `28_Species-and-Current-Affairs-Tracker.md` for the latest
  verified milestones (Ramsar sites, Tiger Reserves, COP outcomes, species-status changes)
  and always retain the source/date attached to any number.
- **No fabrication:** no invented counts, percentages, targets, treaty statuses or PYQ
  question numbers appear in this folder. Where a statistic could not be verified against an
  official source, it has been omitted or explicitly flagged with a verification caution
  rather than estimated.

## Verified current anchors carried across this folder (as of the 2 August 2026 reevaluation)

- 📰 India reached **100 Ramsar sites on 5 June 2026** (Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary,
  Surha Tal, Ballia, Uttar Pradesh, as the 100th site) — source: PIB PRID 2269208; Ramsar
  Convention Secretariat (Topic 07, Topic 28).
- 📰 India's Tiger Reserve count reached **58** with Madhav Tiger Reserve (Madhya Pradesh,
  March 2025), following Ratapani Tiger Reserve (December 2024) (Topic 06, Topic 28).
- 📰 India has **18 notified Biosphere Reserves**; **13** are in UNESCO's World Network
  after Cold Desert joined in 2025 (MoEFCC/UNESCO, verified 2026) (Topic 07, Topic 28).
- 📰 ISFR 2023 (released December 2024): total forest and tree cover of 8,27,357 sq km,
  25.17% of India's geographical area — the latest ISFR edition verified as on 2 August 2026
  (Topics 01, 03, 11, 12, 28).
- 📰 COP29 (Baku, 2024) set the New Collective Quantified Goal (NCQG) on climate finance
  (≥USD 300 billion/year by 2035; ~USD 1.3 trillion/year aspirational total mobilisation);
  COP30 (Belém, 2025) advanced this Baku-to-Belém Roadmap. The **first Global Stocktake
  concluded at COP28 (Dubai, 2023)** with the "UAE Consensus" call to transition away from
  fossil fuels in energy systems and to triple renewable capacity and double the
  energy-efficiency improvement rate by 2030 (Topics 19, 20, 28).
- 📰 **Economic Survey 2025-26, Chapter 10 (Environment and Climate Change)** is the primary
  Government of India source used across this folder for 2025-26 figures. Its key anchors:
  **non-fossil sources at 51.93% of installed power capacity at end-December 2025** (the
  ~50%-by-2030 NDC target met early); **38.61 GW of renewable capacity added in 2025-26 to
  31 December 2025**; **solar 135.81 GW and wind 54.51 GW (December 2025)**; **India 4th
  globally in installed renewable capacity (IRENA, 2025)**; **adaptation expenditure at 5.6%
  of GDP in FY22**; **NAPCC now described as nine missions** (adding health); **MISHTI** at
  ~540 sq km across 9 coastal States and 4 UTs (2023-2028); **CCTS GEI targets notified in
  2025 for Aluminium, Cement, Chlor-Alkali and Pulp & Paper** with a **10-sector offset
  mechanism**; the **world's first particulate-matter ETS at Surat** (317 plants; 20-30%
  lower emissions; 11-14% lower abatement cost); **PARIVESH 3.0**; **Environment Audit Rules,
  2025**; **Environment Protection (Management of Contaminated Sites) Rules, 2025**;
  **Biological Diversity (ABS) Regulations, 2025** covering digital sequence information; the
  **EPR portal position as on 14 November 2025** (69,116 producers, 4,377 recyclers,
  ~308 lakh tonnes recycled); **Circular Economy Action Plans across 10 waste categories**;
  and the **SHANTI Act, December 2025** on nuclear energy (Topics 13-16, 20-22, 24-27, 28).
- 📰 The National Green Hydrogen Mission (approved January 2023) targets 5 MMT/year green-
  hydrogen production capacity by 2030; **862,000 t/yr has been allocated to 18 companies**,
  **3,000 MW/yr of electrolyser manufacturing awarded to 15 firms**, and **three Green
  Hydrogen Hubs** designated at Deendayal, V.O. Chidambaranar and Paradip Ports
  (Topic 21, Topic 25).
- 📰 **IPCC AR7 cycle (ipcc.ch, verified 2 August 2026):** three Working Group reports agreed
  at IPCC-60 (Istanbul, January 2024); **Special Report on Climate Change and Cities**
  outlined at IPCC-61 (Sofia, 2024); Working Group outlines agreed at IPCC-62 (Hangzhou,
  February 2025); the **2027 Methodology Report on Carbon Dioxide Removal Technologies,
  CCUS** agreed at IPCC-63 (Lima, October 2025); **AR7 Synthesis Report due by late 2029**;
  plus a **2027 Methodology Report on Short-Lived Climate Forcers**. **AR6 (2023) remains the
  latest completed assessment** (Topics 17, 18, 21, 28).
- 📰 **UNCCD COP17 is scheduled for Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, 17-28 August 2026**, theme
  "Restoring Land. Restoring Hope", with 197 Parties; UNCCD states that **up to 40% of the
  world's land** is already degraded; **2026 is the UN International Year of Rangelands and
  Pastoralists** (unccd.int, verified 2 August 2026) (Topic 23, Topic 28).
- 📰 CMS COP14 (Samarkand, 2024) and CBD COP16 (Cali 2024/resumed Rome 2025) are the latest
  completed COP anchors for Topics 10 and 22 respectively; CBD COP16 created the **Cali Fund**
  for digital-sequence-information benefit-sharing and a **permanent subsidiary body on
  Article 8(j)**.
- 📰 The Environment Protection (End-of-Life Vehicles) Rules, 2025 and the
  Disaster Management (Amendment) Act, 2025 are routed to Topics 15 and 26.
- 📰 **Forest law:** the Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 was renamed the **Van (Sanrakshan
  Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 1980** by the Forest (Conservation) Amendment Act, 2023; the
  Supreme Court's **February 2024** interim direction requires the broad *Godavarman* (1996)
  "dictionary meaning" of forest to continue applying pending State expert-committee records
  (Topics 11, 12).
- 📰 **Wildlife law:** post-2022, the Wildlife (Protection) Act's schedules are **I and II
  (animals), III (plants) and IV (CITES-listed scheduled specimens)**; the old Schedule V
  (vermin) was **omitted** (Topics 06, 08, 09).
- ⚠️ **Note on seismic zoning:** official BIS material currently and publicly supports a
  four-zone (Zone II-V) seismic classification for India; no official Zone VI has been
  verified, and this folder does not assert one.
- ⚠️ **Deliberate omissions:** this folder does not assert a CITES CoP session number, host
  or listing outcome for the most recent CoP; a Ramsar COP session number or global site
  total; a UNFCCC COP after Belém (2025); India's post-2025 NDC submission status; a global
  biodiversity-hotspot count; or India's exact Kigali HFC phase-down schedule. Each could not
  be confirmed against a first-party source during this reevaluation and is flagged for
  verification in the relevant topic file rather than estimated.
