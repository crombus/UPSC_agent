# MAJOR ENVIRONMENT AND ECOLOGY COMMITTEES

> **Use:** attach each panel to the ecological conflict it examined. Distinguish temporary
> expert reports from continuing statutory or court-supervised appraisal bodies.

## High-yield distinctions

| Do not confuse | Correct distinction |
|---|---|
| Gadgil and Kasturirangan | Gadgil favoured broader sensitivity and decentralised zoning; Kasturirangan narrowed ESA mainly to natural landscapes |
| Tiwari and T. S. R. Subramanian | Tiwari supported building environmental administration; Subramanian reviewed and proposed restructuring mature environmental laws |
| Mashelkar and Saumitra Chaudhuri | Original auto-fuel roadmap; updated Auto Fuel Vision and Policy 2025 |
| Swaminathan and Shailesh Nayak CRZ panels | Review of CRZ 1991 and hazard-sensitive ICZM; later review of CRZ 2011 and regulatory relaxation/differentiation |
| CEC and an ordinary ministry committee | CEC originated in Supreme Court-supervised forest litigation and has a distinct compliance-monitoring role |
| Draft National Water Policy and operative policy | A committee draft is not an adopted policy; verify current official status |
