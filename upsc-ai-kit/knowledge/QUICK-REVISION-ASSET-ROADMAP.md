## Item 1 specification

The constitutional quick-reference will contain:

1. A high-yield Article map grouped by constitutional theme.
2. All 12 Schedules with subject matter, linked Articles and common traps.
3. Every enacted Constitutional Amendment from the **1st through the 106th**.
4. For each amendment: year, provisions changed, what it did, continuing significance and
   any essential judicial or commencement caveat.
5. Separate comparison tables for commonly confused amendments.

Only enacted Constitutional Amendment Acts belong in the numbered amendment ledger.
Pending or lapsed amendment Bills must be listed separately and must never be presented as
part of the Constitution.
