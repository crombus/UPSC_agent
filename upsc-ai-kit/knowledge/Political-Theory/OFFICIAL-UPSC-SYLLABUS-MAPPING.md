# Official UPSC Syllabus Mapping — Political Theory

> **Applicable papers:** Main Examination - Paper II (General Studies I), Paper III
> (General Studies II), Paper V (General Studies IV), and Paper I (Essay).
>
> **Official source text:** [`../OFFICIAL-UPSC-CSE-SYLLABUS-VERBATIM.md`](../OFFICIAL-UPSC-CSE-SYLLABUS-VERBATIM.md)
>
> **Status legend:** **COVERED** = clearly owned in current files; **PARTIAL** = supplementary,
> distributed, or officially uncertain; **GAP** = no current direct owner file.
>
> **What the status measures:** status here records **syllabus-clause ownership**, not the
> completeness of this folder's own files. A clause is **PARTIAL** whenever the clause reaches
> beyond the conceptual layer into constitutional text, institutional machinery, applied ethics
> or the current international order — even where this folder's conceptual slice of it is fully
> written. Status is therefore **not** upgraded for optics; every clause below stays PARTIAL
> because every one of them has an outward, non-conceptual remainder owned elsewhere.
>
> **Tier note (post-repair):** the `basic/NN` files are **answer-complete** for the conceptual
> support they supply — each carries its own doctrine reconstruction, named-thinker evidence
> units, objection → reply chains, directive decoding and 10/15/20-mark answer architecture, so
> no marks-essential Political-Theory content sits only in `advanced/NN`, which is optional
> enrichment. Answer-complete is a statement about **conceptual support**, not about clause
> ownership: the outward Polity (constitutional/institutional), Ethics (applied moral) and
> International-Relations (current global order) facts each clause also requires remain
> **delegated** to their direct owner folders.
>
> **Ownership note:** this folder is **supplementary conceptual support only**. It is not a
> direct named GS owner here and must not be described as owning the constitutional,
> institutional, Ethics or Essay syllabus outright.
>
> **PYQ note:** 2018-2023 remains **central-routing-only** via
> [`../_PYQ-INDEX.md`](../_PYQ-INDEX.md). This folder does **not** embed a subject-owned
> 2018-2023 Political-Theory GS ledger; its verified PYQ references mainly point outward to
> the local Philosophy corpus and other application folders.
>
> **Zero central PYQ routes — recorded as an honest design state, not a gap.** ⚠️ The central
> ledgers assign every printed question to a **direct named owner**. Political Theory has **no
> direct owner route**, and the count of routes into this folder is therefore **zero by design**.
> The reason is the same one the Ownership note states: UPSC prints no Political-Theory paper, and
> every question touching these concepts appears under a paper directly owned by
> [`../Polity/README.md`](../Polity/README.md), [`../Governance/README.md`](../Governance/README.md),
> [`../Ethics/README.md`](../Ethics/README.md), [`../Social-Justice/README.md`](../Social-Justice/README.md),
> [`../International-Relations/README.md`](../International-Relations/README.md) or
> [`../Essay/README.md`](../Essay/README.md). A route pointing here would either duplicate a
> question already owned elsewhere, or assert the direct-ownership claim this folder explicitly
> disclaims — so the zero is a **consequence of the conceptual-support boundary**, not evidence of
> missing work.
>
> ❌ **No alias routing.** No proxy, alias, mirror ledger or synthetic assignment may be created to
> produce a non-zero count. ⚠️ The only genuine PYQ anchors used in this folder are the **verified
> local Philosophy Optional papers (2022-2025)**, cited as a *separate application source*; every
> other question-shaped item is labelled ⚠️ **practice prompt**.
> ✅ **The correct completeness test** for this folder is whether each clause's conceptual slice is
> answer-complete in the listed `basic/` file and reachable from the owner folders that do hold
> routes — not whether routes terminate here.
>
> **Boundary is unchanged by the Core modules added inside `basic/` files.** ⚠️ Conservatism
> ([`basic/05_Socialism-Fascism-Anarchism-and-Gandhism.md`](basic/05_Socialism-Fascism-Anarchism-and-Gandhism.md) §22),
> digital power and surveillance
> ([`basic/15_Power-Authority-and-Legitimacy.md`](basic/15_Power-Authority-and-Legitimacy.md) §14),
> republican non-domination
> ([`basic/18_Liberty-Equality-and-Property.md`](basic/18_Liberty-Equality-and-Property.md) §22),
> populism, ecological political theory and digital democracy
> ([`basic/23_Contemporary-Democracy-Social-Change-and-Development.md`](basic/23_Contemporary-Democracy-Social-Change-and-Development.md) §§21-23)
> **deepen the conceptual slice only**. They do **not** upgrade any clause status, do **not** claim
> new syllabus ownership, and expressly delegate their outward remainders: privacy and rights
> doctrine to [`../Polity/README.md`](../Polity/README.md); applied privacy/consent dilemmas to
> [`../Ethics/README.md`](../Ethics/README.md); data-governance and delivery machinery to
> [`../Governance/README.md`](../Governance/README.md); environmental facts, policy and data to
> [`../Environment-and-Ecology/README.md`](../Environment-and-Ecology/README.md); and any
> contemporary classification of parties, movements or programmes to the current-affairs and
> application folders. Every clause below therefore remains **PARTIAL**.
