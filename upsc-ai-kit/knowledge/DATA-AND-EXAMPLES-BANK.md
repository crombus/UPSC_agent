# UPSC Data and Examples Bank

> **Cutoff:** 14 September 2026.
>
> **Purpose:** supply one dated statistic or named example for major Mains themes. Every number
> carries its reference period. Recheck volatile figures before later exam cycles; never strip a
> number from its date, definition or source.
>
> **Use rule:** one statistic + one example + one qualification is stronger than a paragraph of
> unsourced numbers.

## Evidence formula

```text
CLAIM -> DATED FACT / NAMED EXAMPLE -> WHY IT PROVES THE CLAIM -> LIMIT OR COUNTERPOINT
```

---

## 13. Source hierarchy and update rules

1. **Primary statistical sources:** Census/Registrar General, MoSPI, RBI, Economic Survey,
   NFHS/MoHFW, UDISE+/AISHE, NITI Aayog, FSI/MoEFCC, NTCA, CEA/MNRE and ministry dashboards.
2. **Primary legal/institutional sources:** Constitution, India Code, judgments, ministry and
   regulator publications.
3. **No naked number:** always write the period: `7.4% real GDP growth, FY26 FAE`, not
   `India grows at 7.4%`.
4. **No false precision:** if a dashboard is volatile, prefer a dated rounded figure and retain
   the access date.
5. **No metric substitution:** card count is not service use; road length is not quality;
   forest cover is not biodiversity; enrolment is not learning; conviction is not deterrence.
6. **Refresh before exam:** macro, inflation, labour, reserves, scheme reach, Ramsar sites,
   protected-area counts and digital transactions.
