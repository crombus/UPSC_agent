## Revision rule

```text
name and chair -> year -> assigned task -> one major contribution
-> present legal/policy status -> one limitation
```

- Do not treat recommendations as enacted law.
- Do not merge committees chaired by the same person.
- Distinguish constitution year, report year and implementation year.
- Verify current legal status and numerical recommendations from the original official report.
