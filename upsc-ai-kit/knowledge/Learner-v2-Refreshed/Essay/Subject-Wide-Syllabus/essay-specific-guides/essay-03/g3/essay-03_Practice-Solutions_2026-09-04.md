# Issue-Based Prompt Scoping — Essay Practice Solutions

> Matching solutions for the question-only workbook. Models are repository-authored and are not official UPSC answers.

## MICRO-DRILL SOLUTIONS

### SOLUTION 1 — Scope map

**Task:** Map object, qualifier, geography, time, actor and scale for the climate-resilient-India prompt.

**Model response:** Lock resilience, alternative technologies and India; then identify actors and scales without turning each into a compulsory heading.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 2 — Demand type

**Task:** Distinguish causal from evaluative handling in the media-bias prompt.

**Model response:** Causal analysis explains mechanisms of democratic harm; evaluation specifies the threshold at which viewpoint becomes systematic distortion.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 3 — Exclusion box

**Task:** List material that must not enter a scoped FOMO essay.

**Model response:** Exclude a general history of the internet, unrelated cybercrime and unsupported prevalence statistics.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 4 — Thesis correction

**Task:** Repair: 'Technology will solve climate change in India.'

**Model response:** Locally appropriate technology can strengthen resilience when joined to institutions, ecology and equitable access; it is an enabler, not a substitute.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 5 — Evidence selection

**Task:** Choose evidence for a paragraph on heat resilience.

**Model response:** Use the Ahmedabad Heat Action Plan to connect forecast, communication and health response; do not invent impact figures.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 6 — Introduction

**Task:** Write a scoped opening for biased media and democracy.

**Model response:** Define systematic distortion, democratic information and the free-expression boundary before stating a qualified threat thesis.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 7 — Paragraph repair

**Task:** Convert a policy catalogue into one analytical paragraph.

**Model response:** Group technologies by one mechanism, add a named illustration, explain distributional effect and state the implementation limit.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 8 — Transition

**Task:** Bridge from platform design to family and institutional responsibility in the FOMO essay.

**Model response:** Because design amplifies rather than creates every vulnerability, the argument must now move from the screen to the support systems around the user.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.

### SOLUTION 9 — Outline

**Task:** Build a six-paragraph issue-essay outline with one counter-view.

**Model response:** Sequence definition, mechanism, unequal effects, constructive capability, counter-view and qualified response.

**Self-check:** demand fidelity; one controlling claim; safe evidence; mechanism; qualification; no invented attribution or statistic.
