### Constructed-response practice and models

### Transfer 1. Classify every underlined word in: “Those two careful officers spoke unusually calmly.”

**Model answer:** Those—determiner; two—determiner/numeral; careful—adjective; officers—noun; spoke—verb; unusually—adverb; calmly—adverb.

### Transfer 2. Correct: “The equipments were delivered, but one furniture was damaged.”

**Model answer:** The equipment was delivered, but one piece of furniture was damaged.

### Transfer 3. Remove ambiguity: “When Anita spoke to Kavita, she was anxious.”

**Model answer:** State the intended referent, e.g. “Anita was anxious when she spoke to Kavita.”

### Transfer 4. Give both standard readings of “The committee has/have reached a decision.”

**Model answer:** Singular presents the committee as one unit; plural presents members acting individually in British-style usage.

### Transfer 5. Correct only if needed: “Every candidate should bring their admit card.”

**Model answer:** No correction is required; singular their is accepted modern formal English. A plural recast is also safe.

### Transfer 6. Explain why “The accident was happened yesterday” fails.

**Model answer:** Happen is intransitive in this use and has no object to promote into a passive subject.

### Transfer 7. Distinguish “work hard” from “hardly work.”

**Model answer:** Hard means with effort; hardly means scarcely.
