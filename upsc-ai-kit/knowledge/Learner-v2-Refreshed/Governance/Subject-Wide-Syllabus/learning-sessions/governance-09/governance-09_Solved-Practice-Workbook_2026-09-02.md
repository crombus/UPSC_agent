# Civil Services and Mission Karmayogi — Solved Practice Workbook

## BASIC MCQS / REMEDIATION

### Q1. Which statement correctly identifies Civil services as a structurally distinctive democratic institution?

A. The civil service occupies a structurally distinctive place in a democracy because it is the permanent, neutral and expert executant of decisions taken by a changing elected political executive, and its three classical traits of political neutrality, permanence and anonymity were designed as one package in which each trait supports the others, so an answer that recites a list of civil-service duties has already missed what the demand is testing.
B. Anonymity is the convention that civil servants advise confidentially while ministers take public credit or blame, and it must be described as accountability displacement rather than accountability absence, because internal accountability through service rules, vigilance mechanisms, departmental hierarchy and performance appraisal continues even where public attributive accountability sits with the political executive.
C. Permanence means the civil service continues in office across changes of political government, supplying the institutional memory and continuity that budget execution, law and order and service delivery require and that a purely political appointee system would lack, while its cost is that permanence can shelter sustained non-performance wherever appraisal carries no consequence.
D. Political neutrality is the obligation to serve any elected government impartially and to implement lawful policy irrespective of personal political preference, which is the classical basis of the service's legitimacy across changes of government; it does not mean the absence of policy influence, because civil servants can and do shape technical and expert advice, and treating neutrality as silence is the commonest conceptual error in this whole area.
