# UPSC INDICES AND REPORTS - QUICK REVISION INDEX

> **Cut-off:** 13 September 2026.
> Memorise the **publisher, dimensions, frequency and use**, not a rank that changes every
> edition. Rankings and India values should be refreshed from the latest release before an exam.

## Status legend

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Annual / biennial** | Regular publication cycle, though release delays are possible |
| **Periodic** | No dependable annual cycle |
| **Database** | Continuously or frequently updated rather than one annual report |
| **Legacy** | Discontinued, paused or replaced; useful mainly as an exam trap |

## 1. Human development, poverty and inequality

| Index / report | Publisher | Frequency | Core dimensions / method | India use and trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Human Development Report and HDI** | UNDP | Annual/periodic release | Life expectancy, expected/mean schooling and GNI per capita; geometric mean | HDI is broader than income but does not directly measure inequality |
| **Inequality-adjusted HDI** | UNDP | With HDR | Discounts each HDI dimension for inequality in distribution | IHDI equals HDI only under perfect equality |
| **Gender Development Index** | UNDP | With HDR | Female-to-male ratio of sex-disaggregated HDI | GDI compares achievement gaps; do not confuse with GII |
| **Gender Inequality Index** | UNDP | With HDR | Reproductive health, empowerment and labour-market participation | Higher GII means greater inequality |
| **Global Multidimensional Poverty Index** | UNDP and OPHI | Annual | Health, education and living standards; incidence multiplied by intensity | Uses 10 indicators; not India's 12-indicator National MPI |
| **Human Capital Index** | World Bank | Periodic/database | Survival, schooling quantity/quality and health as future-worker productivity | A productivity potential index, not a complete welfare measure |
| **Poverty and Shared Prosperity Report** | World Bank | Biennial/periodic | Global poverty lines, shared prosperity and distribution | International poverty lines differ from national poverty lines |
| **World Inequality Report** | World Inequality Lab | Periodic | Income and wealth distribution using national accounts, surveys and tax data | Not a UN or World Bank publication |
| **Commitment to Reducing Inequality Index** | Oxfam and Development Finance International | Periodic | Public services, progressive taxation and labour rights | Measures policy commitment, not only realised inequality |
| **World Social Report** | UN DESA | Periodic | Thematic analysis of inequality, social inclusion and demographic change | A report, not a country-ranking series every year |
| **World Happiness Report** | Oxford Wellbeing Research Centre with Gallup and UN SDSN partners | Annual | Life evaluation using Gallup World Poll; explanatory variables include income, support and health | Ranking is based mainly on self-reported life evaluation |
| **Global Social Mobility Index** | World Economic Forum | Legacy/irregular | Health, education, technology access, work conditions and social protection | Last flagship edition was 2020; do not treat it as a current annual index |
| **Global Wealth Report** | UBS | Annual | Household wealth levels and distribution | Previously associated with Credit Suisse; publisher changed after acquisition |

## 14. Publisher traps and comparison pairs

| Do not confuse | Correct distinction |
|---|---|
| HDI vs Human Capital Index | UNDP welfare capability vs World Bank future-worker productivity |
| GDI vs GII vs Global Gender Gap | UNDP development ratio vs UNDP inequality loss vs WEF gender gaps |
| Global MPI vs National MPI | UNDP-OPHI 10 indicators vs NITI Aayog 12 indicators |
| Global Hunger Index vs SOFI | NGO composite index vs UN-agency food-security estimates |
| WEO vs Global Economic Prospects | IMF vs World Bank |
| World Development Report vs HDR | World Bank thematic development report vs UNDP human development |
| CPI vs WGI control of corruption | Transparency International single perception index vs World Bank governance dimension |
| Global Peace vs Global Terrorism | Both IEP, but broad peacefulness vs terrorism impact |
| Doing Business vs B-READY | Discontinued legacy index vs replacement World Bank project |
| GCI vs Global Innovation Index | Paused WEF competitiveness ranking vs active WIPO innovation index |
| EPI vs CCPI vs Climate Risk Index | Environmental outcomes vs climate-policy performance vs extreme-weather impacts |
| Living Planet Index vs IUCN Red List | Population-abundance trend vs species extinction risk |
| ISFR forest cover vs legal forest | Satellite canopy class vs land's legal status |
| PLFS vs ASI | Household labour survey vs registered-factory establishment survey |
| SRS vs CRS vs Census | Sample demographic estimates vs registered events vs decennial enumeration |
| NFHS vs National Health Accounts | Household health survey vs health-expenditure accounting |
| LEADS vs LPI | Indian State logistics index vs World Bank cross-country logistics index |
| NIRF vs accreditation | Relative ranking vs threshold quality assurance |

## Verification hierarchy

1. Publisher's latest report, methodology note and dataset.
2. Government ministry or intergovernmental organisation release.
3. Latest edition year and country coverage before quoting a rank.
4. Methodology break, publisher change or discontinued status before comparing time series.
5. Use rankings as supporting evidence, never as the sole basis of a Mains argument.
