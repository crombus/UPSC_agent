#### 2. Nobel architecture

| Category | Core subject link | Frequent trap |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Physics | Matter, energy, quantum science, astronomy, electronics | A technological application may rest on a much older physical discovery. |
| ✅ Chemistry | Molecules, materials, catalysis, biochemistry | Chemistry prizes often overlap biology but remain Chemistry awards. |
| ✅ Physiology or Medicine | Biological mechanism, disease and therapy | Institution or nationality does not identify the scientific contribution. |
| ✅ Literature | Literary body of work | Not a science category. |
| ✅ Peace | Peace, rights, humanitarian or institutional contribution | Can be awarded to an organisation as well as a person. |
| ✅ Economic Sciences | Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel | It was established later and is not one of Alfred Nobel's original five prizes. |

✅ The awarding institutions differ by category. Do not infer a category merely from the word "Nobel" or from the laureate's employer.

- ✅ Born in **Cambridge, United Kingdom**, in 1942.
- ✅ Affiliated at the time of the award with the **University of California, Berkeley, USA**.
- ✅ Shared the prize with **Michel H. Devoret** and **John M. Martinis**.
- ✅ Official motivation: discovery of **macroscopic quantum mechanical tunnelling and energy quantisation in an electric circuit**.

- ⚠️ Exam link: connect the biography to superconductivity, Josephson-junction circuits, quantum tunnelling and quantum-computing hardware; do not stop at the name.

#### 6. Must-know elimination rules

- ✅ **Birthplace, citizenship and award-time affiliation are different facts.**
- ✅ A laureate may work in a country other than the country of birth.
- ✅ A prize can be shared; one option may name a co-laureate whose biography does not match the question.
- ✅ Match the **year** before matching a remembered discovery.
- ✅ Match the official **motivation**, not a broad popular label.
- ❌ A university affiliation proves nationality. -> It proves only the stated institutional association.
- ❌ Every famous scientist connected with a field won that year's Nobel. -> Use year plus citation plus biography.
- ❌ A current-affairs name list is sufficient. -> The underlying mechanism remains examinable after the name becomes stale.

#### Core answer architecture — discovery attribution and mechanism-first current affairs

**Thesis choice.** A discovery/prize question is an evidence-matching task: official citation, year, laureate, award-time affiliation and mechanism must align; popularity, nationality or a company announcement cannot fill a gap.

**10-mark spine.** Start with the exact official prize/discovery fact; explain the underlying mechanism in two lines; connect it to the appropriate Physics/Chemistry/Biology/technology owner; state the status boundary between finding and application.

**15/20-mark spine.** Use **problem and method → named discoverer/team and evidence → mechanism → downstream applications and Indian relevance → uncertainty, replication, maturity and ethics**. Do not turn a scientist biography into a technology policy answer.

**Evidence units.**
- **Claim:** prize attribution is narrower than field attribution → **official Nobel category, year and motivation** → identifies what was recognised rather than a broad public label → **qualification:** affiliation at award is not nationality, and an employer does not prove authorship of every prior contribution.
- **Claim:** a scientific discovery matters through a mechanism → **the 2025 Physics citation on macroscopic quantum tunnelling and energy quantisation in an electric circuit** → routes a biography clue to superconducting circuits, tunnelling and quantum hardware → **qualification:** recognition of fundamental science does not establish a commercial quantum computer or national deployment.
- **Claim:** current discovery evidence has a maturity ladder → **finding → replication/engineering → regulated application → scale** → prevents a company claim, preprint or demonstration from being written as settled societal capability → **qualification:** do not invent discovery dates, prize details, efficacy or future uses absent the official source.

**Verdict.** Use current names as entry points to durable scientific mechanisms and retain source/year/status discipline in every attribution.

#### 1. Analytical lens

⚠️ Major prizes reveal how science moves from a counter-intuitive mechanism to an experimental demonstration and then to a technology platform. The useful sequence is:

```text
theory -> measurable phenomenon -> reproducible experiment
       -> enabling instrument/device -> engineering platform -> social application
```

For the 2025 Physics case, quantum tunnelling and energy quantisation were not merely abstract ideas: superconducting circuits made macroscopic quantum behaviour measurable and helped establish a route toward superconducting qubits and precision sensors.

#### 2. Deeper distinctions

| Distinction | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Discovery vs invention | A discovery establishes a phenomenon; an invention creates a device or process. Prize citations may recognise either kind of contribution. |
| Foundational science vs commercial technology | Scientific proof does not establish scalability, affordability or deployment. |
| Laureate vs institution | The person receives the prize; the institution supplies the research environment but is not automatically a co-recipient. |
| Citation vs later application | The official citation names the awarded work; later applications should be labelled as consequences, not rewritten into the citation. |
| Individual prize vs organisational prize | Peace prizes may recognise organisations; science prizes are awarded to individuals under the applicable rules. |

#### 3. Mains-ready use

- ⚠️ Use laureate examples to illustrate long-horizon public research, instrumentation, international mobility and the path from basic science to strategic technology.
- ⚠️ Avoid "Nobel count" nationalism. A stronger answer examines research funding, universities, laboratory autonomy, interdisciplinary teams and translation capacity.

#### 4. Advanced trap

❌ **A prize proves that the resulting technology is mature.**  
✅ It proves recognition of the cited contribution; engineering readiness, manufacturing scale, cost and regulation remain separate tests.
