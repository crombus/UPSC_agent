## 2. Nobel architecture

| Category | Core subject link | Frequent trap |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Physics | Matter, energy, quantum science, astronomy, electronics | A technological application may rest on a much older physical discovery. |
| ✅ Chemistry | Molecules, materials, catalysis, biochemistry | Chemistry prizes often overlap biology but remain Chemistry awards. |
| ✅ Physiology or Medicine | Biological mechanism, disease and therapy | Institution or nationality does not identify the scientific contribution. |
| ✅ Literature | Literary body of work | Not a science category. |
| ✅ Peace | Peace, rights, humanitarian or institutional contribution | Can be awarded to an organisation as well as a person. |
| ✅ Economic Sciences | Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel | It was established later and is not one of Alfred Nobel's original five prizes. |

✅ The awarding institutions differ by category. Do not infer a category merely from the word "Nobel" or from the laureate's employer.

## 4. 2025 Physics case tested in 2026

📰 **John Clarke — Nobel Prize in Physics 2025**

- ✅ Born in **Cambridge, United Kingdom**, in 1942.
- ✅ Affiliated at the time of the award with the **University of California, Berkeley, USA**.
- ✅ Shared the prize with **Michel H. Devoret** and **John M. Martinis**.
- ✅ Official motivation: discovery of **macroscopic quantum mechanical tunnelling and energy quantisation in an electric circuit**.
- ✅ Their superconducting electrical circuit demonstrated quantum behaviour at a macroscopic scale.
- ⚠️ Exam link: connect the biography to superconductivity, Josephson-junction circuits, quantum tunnelling and quantum-computing hardware; do not stop at the name.

## 6. Must-know elimination rules

- ✅ **Birthplace, citizenship and award-time affiliation are different facts.**
- ✅ A laureate may work in a country other than the country of birth.
- ✅ A prize can be shared; one option may name a co-laureate whose biography does not match the question.
- ✅ Match the **year** before matching a remembered discovery.
- ✅ Match the official **motivation**, not a broad popular label.
- ❌ A university affiliation proves nationality. -> It proves only the stated institutional association.
- ❌ Every famous scientist connected with a field won that year's Nobel. -> Use year plus citation plus biography.
- ❌ A current-affairs name list is sufficient. -> The underlying mechanism remains examinable after the name becomes stale.
