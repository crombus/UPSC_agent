# UPSC INTERNATIONAL ORGANISATIONS AND GROUPINGS - QUICK REVISION INDEX

> **Origin and importance companion:** [`INTERNATIONAL-ORGANISATIONS-ORIGIN-AND-IMPORTANCE.md`](INTERNATIONAL-ORGANISATIONS-ORIGIN-AND-IMPORTANCE.md)
> records the founding problem, predecessor chain and UPSC significance of every body below.

> **Cut-off:** 14 September 2026.
> For large universal organisations, learn the membership category rather than every country.
> For small groupings, the members are listed. "Headquarters" is not invented for informal
> forums that only have a rotating presidency or no permanent secretariat.

## Status legend

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **IGO** | Treaty- or charter-based intergovernmental organisation |
| **UN body** | Principal organ, specialised agency, fund, programme or related organisation |
| **Forum** | Informal or political grouping without a constitutive treaty |
| **Regime** | Coordinated rules/guidelines without a universal treaty organisation |
| **India: partner/observer** | India participates but is not a full member |

## 1. United Nations principal organs and justice bodies

| Organisation / body | Membership | Headquarters / secretariat | Mandate | India's role / trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **United Nations** | 193 Member States | New York | Peace, security, development, rights and international cooperation | India is a founding member and major peacekeeping contributor |
| **UN General Assembly** | All UN members, one vote each | New York | Deliberation, budget, elections and recommendations | Resolutions are generally non-binding except internal/budgetary effects |
| **UN Security Council** | 5 permanent plus 10 elected members | New York | Binding peace-and-security decisions, sanctions and mandates | India seeks permanent membership; it has no veto |
| **UN Economic and Social Council** | 54 elected members | New York | Coordinates economic, social and development work | India is periodically elected; membership is not permanent |
| **International Court of Justice** | 15 independent judges; all UN members parties to ICJ Statute | The Hague | Disputes between States and advisory opinions | Individuals cannot file cases; jurisdiction requires a legal basis/consent |
| **UN Secretariat** | Secretary-General and international civil service | New York with global offices | Implements mandates and supports UN organs | Secretary-General appointed by GA on UNSC recommendation |
| **Trusteeship Council** | UN principal organ, operations suspended | New York | Former supervision of trust territories | Suspended after Palau's independence; not abolished |
| **International Criminal Court** | States Parties to Rome Statute | The Hague | Tries individuals for genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes and aggression | India is not a Rome Statute party |
| **Permanent Court of Arbitration** | Contracting parties to Hague Conventions | The Hague | Administrative framework for arbitration and fact-finding | Not a standing court with permanent judges |
| **International Tribunal for the Law of the Sea** | 21 judges elected by UNCLOS parties | Hamburg | UNCLOS disputes, prompt release and seabed matters | India is an UNCLOS party and may use treaty dispute mechanisms |

## Verification hierarchy

1. Founding treaty/charter and official membership page.
2. Latest summit declaration for new members, partners and observers.
3. Official secretariat page for headquarters and mandate.
4. MEA brief for India's status and initiatives.
5. Never treat an invitation, accession negotiation or dialogue partnership as full membership.
