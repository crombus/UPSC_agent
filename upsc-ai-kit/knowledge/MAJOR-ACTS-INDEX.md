# UPSC MAJOR ACTS - QUICK REVISION INDEX

> **Cut-off:** 13 September 2026.
> **Priority A** means direct Prelims recall plus frequent Mains use. **Priority B** means
> selective but syllabus-relevant. Commencement, repeal and judicial stays are stated
> separately from enactment.

## Status legend

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In force** | Operative law, subject to later amendments and rules |
| **Phased** | Only notified provisions are operative |
| **Legacy** | Repealed/replaced, but may govern older transactions or proceedings |
| **Not fully commenced** | Enacted but one or more central provisions await notification |

## 14. High-yield traps

1. **Enactment is not commencement:** always check the Gazette notification.
2. **An amendment Act is not the principal Act:** cite the principal law "as amended".
3. **Rules and notifications are subordinate legislation:** EIA, CRZ, IT Rules and Fertiliser
   Control Order are not standalone Acts.
4. **Judicial stay is not repeal:** stayed clauses remain enacted but temporarily inoperative.
5. **Repeal does not erase transition:** older offences, tax years and proceedings may continue
   under saved legacy law.
6. **Central law is not universal State adoption:** Art 252 pollution law, Clinical
   Establishments Act and State APMC laws require special attention.
7. **A regulator's Act and sector Act differ:** SEBI/SCRA, IRDAI/Insurance Act and
   PNGRB/petroleum laws operate together.
8. **A statutory entitlement may be qualified:** resource, eligibility, notification and
   procedural conditions still matter.
9. **Do not convert a Bill into law:** use only enacted and commenced text for present status.
10. **Do not memorise obsolete section numbers:** especially after criminal and income-tax
    replacements.

## Verification hierarchy

1. Gazette of India and India Code for enactment, text and commencement.
2. Official ministry/regulator rules and notifications for implementation.
3. Supreme Court final/interim orders for stayed or read-down provisions.
4. PRS Legislative Research for concise legislative history and amendment comparison.
5. Current-affairs sources only for implementation examples, never as a substitute for the law.
