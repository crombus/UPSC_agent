### Eight-year trend correction ⚠️

1. **Highest primary load:** Soul–Immortality–Rebirth–Liberation (**17**) is first; Religious Language (**14**) and Notions of God (**13**) follow.
2. **Every year:** Religious Experience owns exactly one part in every paper. Soul, Proofs, Pluralism and Language recur heavily but not with a mechanically fixed annual count.
3. **Problem of Evil:** absent as a primary owner in 2018, then present in every paper from 2019 through 2025.
4. **Religion without God:** absent as a primary owner only in 2022; Notions of God has no primary-owned part in 2025.
5. **Older-paper depth:** 2018–2021 add sacred/holy as object, public-discourse tests for experience, prayer/worship, moral argument, science–religion, freedom–omnipotence, Jain proofs, Buddhist anti-creator arguments, secular ethics and Indian paths of liberation.
