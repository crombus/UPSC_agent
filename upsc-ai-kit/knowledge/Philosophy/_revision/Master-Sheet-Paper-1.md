## A. WESTERN (items 1–11)

| # | Thinker/School | ONE-LINE THESIS | Key term | Exam fault-line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [**Plato**](../paper-1/western/Plato-Aristotle.md) | Reality = eternal **Forms**; particulars merely *participate* | Form / Idea | Third Man regress |
| 1 | [**Aristotle**](../paper-1/western/Plato-Aristotle.md) | Form is **in** matter; substance = form+matter compound; 4 causes; potentiality→actuality | Hylomorphism, substance | vs Plato's separate Forms |
| 2 | **Descartes** | Method of **doubt** → *cogito*; mind & body two substances | Cogito, dualism | interaction problem |
| 2 | **Spinoza** | **One** substance = God/Nature; mind & body two attributes | Monism, parallelism | determinism vs freedom |
| 2 | **Leibniz** | Infinite **monads**, windowless, pre-established harmony | Monad | no real interaction |
| 3 | **Locke** | Mind a *tabula rasa*; ideas from experience; primary/secondary qualities | Tabula rasa | substance = "something I know not what" |
| 3 | **Berkeley** | *Esse est percipi* — no material substance, only minds & ideas | Immaterialism | God perceives all |
| 3 | **Hume** | No impression → no idea; self = **bundle**; causation = habit | Bundle, constant conjunction | scepticism, induction |
| 4 | **Kant** | **Synthetic a priori** possible; mind imposes space/time & categories | Copernican turn, categories | phenomena vs noumena |
| 5 | **Hegel** | Reality becomes intelligible through **immanent contradiction, determinate negation and sublation** toward the Absolute | Dialectic, *Aufhebung* | not a mechanical thesis–antithesis–synthesis formula |
| 6 | **Moore** | **Common sense** defence; refutation of idealism | Common sense | vs Berkeley/Hegel |
| 6 | **Russell** | **Logical atomism**; logical constructions; incomplete symbols (theory of descriptions) | Definite description | reduce to atomic facts |
| 6 | **Early Wittgenstein** | **Picture theory**: proposition pictures fact; saying vs showing | Picture theory | mystical = shown not said |
| 7 | **Logical Positivism** | Meaning = **verifiability**; metaphysics = nonsense; necessary truths = analytic | Verification principle | principle self-refuting |
| 8 | **Later Wittgenstein** | Meaning = **use**; language-games; no private language | Language-game | anti-private-language argument |
| 9 | **Husserl** | **Phenomenology**: bracket (epoché) & describe essences; anti-psychologism | Epoché, intentionality | pure consciousness |
| 10 | **Kierkegaard** | Truth is **subjectivity** — advanced by the pseudonym *Climacus* in the *Concluding Unscientific Postscript*, so attribute it as "Kierkegaard, through Climacus", not as his flat assertion; leap of faith; the individual | Leap of faith | vs Hegel's system |
| 10 | **Sartre** | **Existence precedes essence**; radical freedom; bad faith | Radical freedom | condemned to be free |
| 10 | **Heidegger** | **Dasein** = being-in-the-world; being-towards-death; temporality | Dasein | fundamental ontology |
| 11 | **Quine** | Attacks **two dogmas** (analytic/synthetic; reductionism); holism | Two dogmas, web of belief | no a priori |
| 11 | **Strawson** | **Descriptive metaphysics**; basic particulars = bodies; person is primitive | Basic particulars, person | vs Cartesian ego |

|---|---|---|---|---|

| 19 | **Wider Vedānta** | Nimbārka: difference-and-non-difference; Vallabha: pure non-dual real manifestation; Caitanya: inconceivable difference-and-non-difference | *dvaitādvaita*, *śuddhādvaita*, *acintyabhedābheda* | matter and comparative mokṣa |
| 20 | **Aurobindo** | **Evolution & involution**; Supermind; integral yoga | Supermind, integral yoga | spiritual evolution |

> 🔑 **Paper-I spine:** Western = *substance & the crisis of knowledge* (Aristotle→Hume→**Kant the hinge**→analysts dissolve metaphysics). Indian = *pramāṇa (how we know) + causation (satkārya vs asatkārya) + self/liberation (ātma vs anātma)*.

### C.1 Western

| # | Owner | Repaired unit | The one line that must be reproducible | Fault-line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Plato–Aristotle](../paper-1/western/Plato-Aristotle.md) | Sun–Line–Cave as **one** argument; *dianoia* vs *noēsis* | What separates *dianoia* from *noēsis* is **method**, not difficulty: mathematics reasons *down* from unexamined hypotheses using images; dialectic reasons *up* to an unhypothetical principle without them | Retelling the Cave story with no epistemology and no *return* (*katabasis*) |
| 1 | [Plato–Aristotle](../paper-1/western/Plato-Aristotle.md) | ***Categories*** vs ***Metaphysics*** Z | The *Categories* identifies **which things** are substances (the individual); *Metaphysics* Z asks **what substance-hood is** (form/essence, sought as a *cause*) | Writing "primary substance = the individual" and stopping |
| 4 | [Kant](../paper-1/western/Kant.md) | **Schematism** | The schema is the **transcendental time-determination** that lets a pure category apply to sensible intuition; without it categories are empty and intuitions blind in practice, not merely in slogan | Treating the categories as applying to intuition automatically |
| 7 | [Logical Positivism](../paper-1/western/Logical-Positivism.md) | **Protocol-sentence debate** | Schlick wanted incorrigible observation reports; Neurath denied any statement is beyond revision — so verification lost its bedrock **from inside the Circle** | Presenting positivism as collapsing only under external attack |
| 8 | [Later Wittgenstein](../paper-1/western/Later-Wittgenstein.md) | **Rule-following** | No interpretation can fix a rule, since every interpretation itself needs interpreting; the regress stops in trained practice, not in a further mental item | Merging rule-following into the private-language argument without stating the regress |
| 9 | [Husserl](../paper-1/western/Phenomenology-Husserl.md) | **Noesis–noema**; lifeworld | Every act (*noesis*) has a correlated intended-sense (*noema*); the *Lebenswelt* is the pre-theoretical ground the natural attitude conceals | Equating *noema* with a mental picture |
| 10 | [Existentialism](../paper-1/western/Existentialism.md) | **Pseudonymity / indirect communication** | Kierkegaard writes through masks because existential truth must be *appropriated*, not received as a finished Hegelian system — this is why "truth is subjectivity" is not relativism | Quoting a pseudonym as Kierkegaard's flat assertion |
| 11 | [Quine–Strawson](../paper-1/western/Quine-Strawson.md) | **Radical translation / *gavagai*** | Rival translation manuals fit all behavioural evidence; the identity-predicate cannot be checked without already being translated — so there is **no fact of the matter** about reference | Treating indeterminacy as mere human ignorance |

### C.2 Indian

| # | Owner | Repaired unit | The one line that must be reproducible | Fault-line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | [Cārvāka](../paper-1/indian/Carvaka.md) | Hostile sources; **Jayarāśi Bhaṭṭa** | The materialist corpus is lost, so the school is reconstructed from opponents; Jayarāśi is a Cārvāka "of an extreme type" who attacks **perception** too — a limit-case, never the standard doctrine | Using the *Tattvopaplavasiṃha* to state Cārvāka epistemology |

| 18 | [Mīmāṃsā](../paper-1/indian/Mimamsa.md) | ***Apūrva***; *bhāvanā* vs *niyoga*; sentence-meaning | *Apūrva* is the unseen potency linking act to later fruit without God; **Bhāṭṭa** = *bhāvanā-vāda* + *abhihitānvaya* (words denote, then connect); **Prābhākara** = *niyoga-vāda* + *anvitābhidhāna* (words denote only as connected) | Writing "Mīmāṃsā" generically when the question names a sub-school |
| 19 | [Vedānta](../paper-1/indian/Vedanta.md) | **Wider Vedānta** | Nimbārka *dvaitādvaita*; Vallabha *śuddhādvaita*; Caitanya *acintyabhedābheda* — "Schools of Vedanta" is printed in the plural and is wider than three | Forcing all schools into the Śaṅkara–Rāmānuja–Madhva grid |
| 20 | [Aurobindo](../paper-1/indian/Aurobindo.md) | Planes of consciousness; **psychic being** | The psychic being's emergence begins *conscious* evolution and reorients nature toward the Divine; it is not yet supramental transformation | Treating "life divine" as an automatic outcome for everyone |
