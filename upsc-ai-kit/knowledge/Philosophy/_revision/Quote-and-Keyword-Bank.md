| Russell | On theory of descriptions: logic as "the essence of philosophy." | ⚠️ |

| Logical Positivism | Ayer: a statement is meaningful only if **analytic or empirically verifiable**. | ⚠️ |
| Later Wittgenstein | ⚠️ *PI* §43 is **qualified**: "For a large class of cases… the meaning of a word is its use in the language." Quoting it without the qualifier misstates the doctrine. "Don't ask for the meaning, ask for the use" is a popular summary, not safe verbatim wording. | ⚠️ |
| Later Wittgenstein | **family resemblance** · rule-following · regress of interpretations · private-diary case · beetle-in-a-box | ✅ keywords |

## SOURCE SPINE AND QUOTATION CONTROL

- Plato/Aristotle: standard translations of *Republic*, *Phaedo*, *Metaphysics* and *De Anima*; W. T. Stace for orientation.
- Descartes to Hegel: primary works where available; Y. Masih and Anthony Kenny for location and doctrinal context.
- Wittgenstein: *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus* and *Philosophical Investigations*; do not convert popular summaries into quotations.
- Indian traditions: Upaniṣads, *Yoga Sūtra* and relevant school texts through C. D. Sharma and Chatterjee-Datta; hostile-source transmission must be flagged.
- Socio-political philosophy: Rawls, Berlin, Ambedkar and Gandhi primary works; O. P. Gauba and Bhargava-Acharya for context.
- Philosophy of religion: Anselm, Aquinas, Hick, Tillich and Braithwaite through the owner files and their listed editions.
- Before final memorisation, record edition/page details in the owner file. This bank deliberately omits page numbers that have not been checked.
