| 9 | **Religious Pluralism** | Many paths true? **E-I-P** (Exclusivism/Inclusivism/Pluralism) | Hick (the transcategorial Real), Vivekananda, Radhakrishnan, Gandhi, Jain anekāntavāda | absolute truth problem; the self-reference objection to pluralism |
| 10 | **Religious Language** | Analogy (Aquinas), symbol (Tillich); cognitive vs non-cognitive | Aquinas, Tillich, **Braithwaite** (conative/moral-policy account), Ayer, Wittgensteinian fideism, Cupitt | meaningful? factual? and can a symbol still make a truth-claim? |

| 3 | [Individual & State](../paper-2/socio-political/Individual-and-State.md) | **Hohfeld's four incidents** | "A right" resolves into claim, liberty, power and immunity, each with its own correlative — only the claim-right strictly correlates with a duty |
| 4 | [Forms of Government](../paper-2/socio-political/Forms-of-Government.md) | **Deliberative** justification of democracy | Legitimacy comes from reason-giving and revisability, not from counting preferences — this is the answer to the majoritarian objection |

| 7 | [Crime & Punishment](../paper-2/socio-political/Crime-and-Punishment.md) | **Expressive → communicative (Duff) → restorative** family; reintegrative shaming | Punishment addresses the offender as a citizen: it communicates censure and calls for repentance, and restorative practice keeps condemnation while refusing vengeance |

| 3 | [Problem of Evil](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Problem-of-Evil.md) | **Horrendous evils** (Adams) | Some evils destroy rather than build a person; **balancing off** is not **defeat**, and goodness must be goodness *to the sufferer* |
| 5 | [Reason, Revelation, Faith](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Reason-Revelation-Faith.md) | **Reformed epistemology vs evidentialism** | Belief in God can be *properly basic* — not groundless, but non-inferential and defeasible by defeaters |

| 8 | [Religion & Morality](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Religion-and-Morality.md) | **Modified divine command** (R. M. Adams) | Value is grounded in God's *nature*; obligation is generated by God's *command* — the split is what answers Euthyphro |
| 9 | [Religious Pluralism](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Religious-Pluralism.md) | Deep disagreement; self-reference objection | Pluralism must preserve real disagreement; reinterpreting every tradition through "the Real" may override each tradition's self-understanding |
| 10 | [Religious Language](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Religious-Language.md) | Analogy/symbol/non-realism master grid | "Symbolic" and "non-cognitive" answer *different* questions: a symbol may still mediate a truth-claim |
