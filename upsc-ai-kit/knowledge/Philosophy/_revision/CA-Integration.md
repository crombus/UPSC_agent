## PAPER-II SOCIO-POLITICAL — CA hooks

### 1. Equality / Justice / Liberty
- 📰 **UCC (Uttarakhand Act 2024, implemented 27 January 2025)** — test-case for Article 44; uniform rules on marriage, divorce and succession, with registration requirements including for live-in relationships. The implementation date is confirmed by Government of India/Uttarakhand public records; any claim about a pending constitutional challenge must be separately checked against the court record. ⚠️ deploy in liberty-vs-equality, Rawls and secularism answers.
- 📰 **Sub-classification of SCs (SC 7-judge bench, 2024)** — states may sub-categorise SC quotas → Rawlsian "difference principle" for the most disadvantaged *within* a group. ⚠️ justice-as-fairness illustration.

### 2. Sovereignty
- 📰 **Governor's assent and federalism (2025 Presidential Reference)** — the Supreme Court rejected court-created fixed timelines and "deemed assent", while leaving prolonged, unexplained inaction open to limited judicial scrutiny. Illustrates **Laski's pluralist sovereignty** vs **Austin's monistic** command theory. ⚠️

### 3. Individual & State
- 📰 **Right to privacy (Puttaswamy, 2017) applied to UCC live-in registration & data** — rights vs state accountability. ⚠️
- ✅ **Aadhaar / DPDP Act 2023** — individual vs state; duties & accountability. ⚠️

### 4. Forms of Government
- ✅ **Electoral bonds struck down (SC, Feb 2024)** — transparency & the health of **democracy** (procedural vs substantive). ⚠️ deploy in majoritarianism/democracy answers.

### 5. Political Ideologies (Anarchism, Marxism, Socialism)
- ✅ **Welfare-state schemes vs market reforms** debate — socialism vs liberalisation. ⚠️
- ✅ Left-wing extremism (Naxalism) decline — Marxist praxis vs constitutional democracy. ⚠️

### 6. Humanism / Secularism / Multiculturalism
- 📰 **UCC debate** — secularism as **"principled distance" (Bhargava)** vs uniformity; multiculturalism (Kymlicka/Taylor) vs common civil code. ⚠️ **highest-value CA hook for Sec-A.**
- 📰 **Places of Worship Act litigation** — constitutional challenges remain pending. The Supreme Court's December 2024 order barred registration of fresh religious-character suits and, in pending suits, barred effective interim/final orders (including survey directions) until further orders. Use for secularism, historical justice and pluralism. ⚠️

### 7. Crime & Punishment
- 📰 **Capital punishment:** mitigation and the possibility of reform remain central to death-sentence review. Law Commission Report No. 262 is the official policy reference for the abolition/retention debate. Do not assert the current status of litigation over hanging without checking the latest Supreme Court order. ⚠️ **retributive vs reformative** live illustration.
- ✅ **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** replaced the IPC from 1 Jul 2024; Section 152 addresses acts endangering sovereignty, unity and integrity rather than reproducing IPC Section 124A verbatim. ⚠️
- ✅ **Corruption / PMLA rulings, genocide & R2P** (global conflicts) — mass-violence ethics. ⚠️

### 8. Development & Social Progress
- ✅ **Sen's capability approach** vs GDP — deploy with **NITI Aayog Multidimensional Poverty Index**, welfare delivery, JAM trinity. ⚠️
- ✅ Development-vs-displacement (tribal land, environment clearances) — progress vs justice. ⚠️

### 9. Gender Discrimination
- 📰 **Marital rape** — Exception 2 to BNS Section 63 excludes sexual intercourse or sexual acts by a man with his adult wife from the rape definition. No final Supreme Court disposition of the constitutional challenge was located in the official judgment/order portals as of 6 August 2026; describe the litigation status cautiously. ⚠️ **autonomy/consent vs tradition** — top gender hook.
- 📰 **UCC equal inheritance/property rights for women** (Uttarakhand). ⚠️
- ✅ **Women's Reservation Act (Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam, 2023)** — representation and substantive equality; commencement is linked to a post-census delimitation exercise. ⚠️
- ✅ Declining but persistent **sex ratio / foeticide (PC-PNDT Act)**. ⚠️

### 10. Caste — Gandhi vs Ambedkar
- 📰 **Caste enumeration in Census 2027** — the Cabinet Committee on Political Affairs decided on 30 April 2025 to include caste enumeration in the forthcoming Census. The Gazette-notified reference dates are 1 October 2026 for specified snow-bound/non-synchronous areas and 1 March 2027 for the rest of India. Keep the Cabinet decision and the Gazette date notification analytically distinct. ⚠️ **Ambedkar's data-for-justice** logic vs Gandhi's reformism. **Top Sec-A CA hook.**
- ✅ **SC sub-classification (State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh, 2024)** — the seven-judge bench permitted sub-classification within Scheduled Castes to reach the more disadvantaged. Separate opinions discussed a creamy-layer principle, but the judgment did not itself create a uniform exclusion rule. ⚠️

## PAPER-II PHILOSOPHY OF RELIGION — CA hooks

### Problem of Evil / Suffering
- ✅ Natural disasters, pandemics, wars → live "problem of evil"; Hick's soul-making vs karma theodicy. ⚠️

### Religion & Morality / Religion without God
- ✅ **Ethics debates (euthanasia — SC passive euthanasia guidelines, living wills 2018/2023)** — autonomy of morality from religion (Euthyphro). ⚠️
- ✅ Rise of "spiritual but not religious" / secular ethics — **religion without God** (Buddhism, Braithwaite). ⚠️

### Religious Pluralism & Absolute Truth
- ✅ **Communal harmony / inter-faith tension** — Hick's pluralism, *"ekaṃ sat viprā bahudhā vadanti"* as India's constitutional-secular ideal. ⚠️ deploy in pluralism + secularism answers.

### Religious Language / Experience
- ⚠️ Debates on faith vs scientific rationality, "scientific temper" (Art. 51A) — reason/revelation/faith. ⚠️

## REPAIRED-DOCTRINE HOOKS (added 15 August 2026)

The modules promoted into Core on 15 August 2026 changed **which concept a live example is
illustrating**, not which examples are usable. This table re-attaches the anchors already listed
above; it adds **no new dated claim**. ⚠️ Every entry is an analytical bridge.

| Repaired module (now Core) | Anchor to reuse from this file | The philosophical point the example must serve |
|---|---|---|
| **Political obligation & civil disobedience** ([Individual & State](../paper-2/socio-political/Individual-and-State.md)) | rights-vs-accountability items under §3 | Whether the duty to obey rests on consent, fair play, natural duty or associative membership — and why principled disobedience *accepts* the legal consequence |
| **Non-domination** ([Ideals](../paper-2/socio-political/Social-Political-Ideals.md)) | the §1 equality/justice items | Freedom as absence of *vulnerability to arbitrary power*, not merely absence of interference |
| **Recognition vs redistribution** ([Humanism–Secularism–Multiculturalism](../paper-2/socio-political/Humanism-Secularism-Multiculturalism.md)) | the §6 secularism and multiculturalism items | Whether the grievance is misrecognition of identity (Taylor), bivalent status injustice (Fraser) or thwarted recognition (Honneth) — do not let the example decide it for you |
| **Communicative & restorative punishment** ([Crime & Punishment](../paper-2/socio-political/Crime-and-Punishment.md)) | the §7 punishment items, incl. Law Commission Report No. 262 | Whether punishment addresses the offender as a citizen; whether reparation and reintegration can coexist with public censure. ⚠️ If you cite any statutory provision for victim compensation or negotiated pleas, verify the current text before the exam — do not quote a section number from memory |
| **Degrowth / productivism critique** ([Development](../paper-2/socio-political/Development-Social-Progress.md)) | the §8 capability and displacement items | Whether progress is being measured by output at all, and at what ecological and human cost — the target is productivism, not merely distribution |
| **Care ethics & intersectionality** ([Gender](../paper-2/socio-political/Gender-Discrimination.md)) | the §9 gender items | Unpaid care, dependency and relational responsibility as moral categories, read as a corrective *inside* justice rather than a retreat from rights |
| **Skeptical theism & horrendous evils** ([Problem of Evil](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Problem-of-Evil.md)) | disaster/pandemic/war illustrations | Distinguish a **defence** (the *noseeum* inference fails) from a **theodicy** (God's reasons stated); and note that balancing off is not defeat |
| **Religious naturalism & non-realism** ([Religion without God](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Religion-without-God.md)) | the "spiritual but not religious" item | Whether a practice retains ultimacy, discipline, community and soteriology once supernatural assertion is dropped |
| **Stace/Zaehner typologies** ([Religious Experience](../paper-2/philosophy-of-religion/Religious-Experience.md)) | faith-vs-scientific-rationality debates | Classification describes the experience; it does not settle its object — that question is epistemological |

> 🔑 **Subordination rule, restated.** The repaired modules make the *doctrine* denser, not the
> current affairs. One dated illustration, in the assessment paragraph, after the argument has
> been made. An answer that leads with the example and reaches the doctrine last has inverted the
> marks.
> ⚠️ **Volatile items** (marital-rape litigation, Census rollout, UCC litigation, death-penalty method and Places of Worship Act cases) change — re-run a web check before the exam and update dates.
