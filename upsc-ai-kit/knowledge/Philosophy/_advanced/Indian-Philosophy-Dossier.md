## 1. Cārvāka

- **Owner:** [Cārvāka](../paper-1/indian/Carvaka.md)
- **Core prerequisite:** ✅ Master pratyakṣa as sole pramāṇa, the vyāpti/upādhi attack on anumāna, rejection of śabda, four-element materialism, anti-ātman/karma/rebirth, and all routed 2018–2025 Cārvāka PYQs.
- **Safe 15/20-marker use:** ⚠️ Add only one enrichment move in the evaluative paragraph—usually transmission-caution, Jayarāśi as a radical edge-case, or svabhāvavāda as a limited naturalist gesture.

| Optional depth module | Precise doctrinal / interpretive issue | Rival school / commentarial readings | Strongest extra objection → counter-reply | Credible source trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ Transmission and reconstruction problem | Can “Cārvāka” be reconstructed as one stable system when most evidence comes from opponents? | Doxographical minimalism: keep only convergent theses from hostile summaries; stronger reconstruction: infer a broader Lokāyata naturalism from scattered reports | **Obj.:** hostile transmission makes the school unusable. **Reply:** treat only the stable anti-inferential and anti-transcendent core as examinable; use reconstruction merely to avoid caricature | Mādhavācārya, *Sarva-darśana-saṃgraha*; Chatterjee & Datta; C.D. Sharma |
| ⚠️ Jayarāśi and radical scepticism | Is Jayarāśi Bhāṭṭa’s *Tattvopaplavasiṃha* a Cārvāka text or a freer sceptical explosion adjacent to Lokāyata? | Continuity reading: Jayarāśi radicalises anti-pramāṇa pressure; discontinuity reading: he exceeds normal Cārvāka and attacks even perception-stability | **Obj.:** importing Jayarāśi makes the owner’s compact Cārvāka unstable. **Reply:** exactly—so mention him only as an optional radical flank, never as the default UPSC reconstruction | Jayarāśi, *Tattvopaplavasiṃha*; Debiprasad Chattopadhyaya, *Lokāyata*; S. Radhakrishnan, *Indian Philosophy* |
| ⚠️ Svabhāvavāda as thin naturalism | Does svabhāvavāda provide positive explanation, or does it merely block theological and karmic explanations? | Positive naturalist reading: things behave by intrinsic nature; deflationary reading: it is mainly a polemical refusal of adṛṣṭa/Īśvara | **Obj.:** “things act by nature” sounds like an explanatory stop-gap. **Reply:** fair criticism; its safe value is negative—Cārvāka prefers immanent explanation to supersensible postulates | Chatterjee & Datta; C.D. Sharma; Radhakrishnan |

- **What not to over-study:** ⚠️ verse-attribution catalogues, every hostile fragment, or full modern debates over whether Lokāyata counts as “positivism” once the 2024 core answer is already secure.
- **Promotion trigger:** ✅ Promote any of the above if a future PYQ directly asks hostile-source reconstruction, Jayarāśi, or svabhāvavāda as a positive metaphysical thesis, or if the owner file cannot safely answer a “naturalism/positivism” question without it.

## 2. Jainism

- **Owner:** [Jainism](../paper-1/indian/Jainism.md)
- **Core prerequisite:** ✅ Master anekāntavāda, dravya-guṇa-paryāya, six dravyas, syādvāda/saptabhaṅgī, karma as subtle pudgala, bhāvabandha/dravyabandha, saṃvara/nirjarā/mokṣa, and all 2018–2025 Jainism PYQs.
- **Safe 15/20-marker use:** ⚠️ Use optional depth only to sharpen evaluation of relativism, realism, or bondage—never instead of the sevenfold judgement or the bondage sequence.

| Optional depth module | Precise doctrinal / interpretive issue | Rival school / commentarial readings | Strongest extra objection → counter-reply | Credible source trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ Syādvāda as logic or disciplined predication | Is saptabhaṅgī best read as many-valued logic, modal logic, or context-indexed qualified assertion? | Modern formalising reading: proto-many-valued / modal sophistication; classical reading: standpoint-conditioned speech disciplined by ontological many-sidedness | **Obj.:** seven predications collapse into contradiction. **Reply:** Jaina defence is that predications differ by standpoint, time, respect, or mode; they are not brute simultaneous contradictories | Umāsvāti, *Tattvārtha-sūtra*; Padmanabh Jaini, *The Jaina Path of Purification*; Chatterjee & Datta |
| ⚠️ Jīva’s expansion and contraction | How can an immaterial conscious substance fit bodies of different size while remaining numerically the same jīva? | Literal ontological reading: jīva really contracts/expands with embodiment; restrained reading: the doctrine marks embodiment-capacity without materialising the soul | **Obj.:** non-material reality should not have size-properties. **Reply:** Jainism treats extension here as a mode of embodied individuality, not gross physical magnitude in the Cārvāka sense | Umāsvāti, *Tattvārtha-sūtra*; Kundakunda, *Pañcāstikāyasāra*; Radhakrishnan |
| ⚠️ Anekāntavāda versus Madhyamaka anti-essentialism | Does Jain non-absolutism resemble Buddhist emptiness, or is it a distinct realist pluralism? | Jaina reading: real dravyas persist through modes; Madhyamaka foil: all dharmas are empty of svabhāva | **Obj.:** if every statement is conditional, Jainism secretly needs an absolute standpoint. **Reply:** the Jaina can concede an ontological real while denying that finite judgements exhaust it unconditionally | Umāsvāti, *Tattvārtha-sūtra*; Nāgārjuna, *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*; Padmanabh Jaini |

- **What not to over-study:** ⚠️ full sub-classification trees of naya, cosmographic enumerations, or sectarian numerology once the owner’s realism-relativism-bondage triad is secure.
- **Promotion trigger:** ✅ Promote if a future PYQ asks whether syādvāda is a logic, asks directly about jīva’s spatiality, or requires a formal contrast with Madhyamaka beyond present owner-level comparisons.

## 3. Buddhism

- **Owner:** [Buddhism](../paper-1/indian/Buddhism.md)
- **Core prerequisite:** ✅ Master pratītyasamutpāda, kṣaṇikavāda, nairātmyavāda, pañcaskandha, karma-memory-continuity replies, nirvāṇa, Triratna, Yogācāra and Mādhyamika basics, and all 2018–2025 Buddhism PYQs.
- **Safe 15/20-marker use:** ⚠️ Add one optional dispute only after the core derivation is complete—best used in the critical paragraph of a 15/20-marker on śūnyatā, vijñānavāda, or nirvāṇa.

| Optional depth module | Precise doctrinal / interpretive issue | Rival school / commentarial readings | Strongest extra objection → counter-reply | Credible source trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ Mādhyamika interpretation dispute | Is Nāgārjuna best read as quietist/dialectical only, or as an anti-essentialist philosopher with a robust conventional realism? | Prāsaṅgika-style stress on reductio and anti-thesis; more assertive readings allow carefully stated conventional philosophical claims | **Obj.:** emptiness destroys all assertion, including Buddhist doctrine. **Reply:** the standard counter is emptiness of svabhāva, not abolition of dependent conventional discourse; “emptiness of emptiness” blocks reification | Nāgārjuna, *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*; Chandrakīrti, *Prasannapadā*; Radhakrishnan |
| ⚠️ Yogācāra “idealism” debate | Does vijñaptimātratā deny external objects metaphysically, or mainly expose how experience is mind-structured? | Strong idealist reading: external objects denied; phenomenological/epistemic reading: objecthood is critique of representation, not solipsism | **Obj.:** mind-only makes intersubjective experience mysterious. **Reply:** Yogācāra answers through beginningless vāsanā/ālaya-vijñāna and shared karmic conditioning | Vasubandhu, *Viṃśatikā* and *Triṃśikā*; Chatterjee & Datta; C.D. Sharma |
| ⚠️ Pudgalavāda as a pressure-point | Can Buddhist continuity be saved better by a “person” theory than by bare skandha-stream language? | Orthodox no-self lines reject pudgala; Pudgalavādin move tries to preserve responsibility without a permanent self | **Obj.:** “person” smuggles soul back in. **Reply:** Pudgalavādins say the pudgala is neither identical with nor wholly different from the aggregates; orthodox schools still treat this as too concessive | *Milindapañha* (for continuity analogies); Kathāvatthu tradition; Radhakrishnan |
| ⚠️ Two truths and ethics | If ultimate analysis dissolves self and substance, what secures moral seriousness at the conventional level? | Minimalist reading: convention is merely practical; stronger Mahāyāna reading: compassion and karma are intelligible precisely at the saṃvṛti level | **Obj.:** ultimate truth empties ethics of force. **Reply:** Buddhist reply is that liberation and compassion operate within dependent conventional life, not outside it | Nāgārjuna, *Mūlamadhyamakakārikā*; *Majjhima Nikāya*; Chatterjee & Datta |

- **What not to over-study:** ⚠️ full scholastic taxonomies of all Buddhist schools, all Abhidharma lists, or the Prāsaṅgika/Svātantrika divide as if it were a compulsory UPSC sub-question.
- **Promotion trigger:** ✅ Promote if a future PYQ directly asks whether Yogācāra is idealist, whether emptiness is self-refuting, whether pudgala is compatible with no-self, or whether the two truths are ethically destabilising.

## 4. Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika

- **Owner:** [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](../paper-1/indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md)
- **Core prerequisite:** ✅ Master padārthas, pramāṇas, Gautama’s perception, inference, fallacies, sāmānya, samavāya, abhāva, anyathākhyāti, self, apavarga, God-proofs, causation, atomism, and all 2018–2025 Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika PYQs.
- **Safe 15/20-marker use:** ⚠️ Optional depth works best in evaluation: one line on Navya precision, one line on samavāya/viśeṣa pressure, or one line on Udayana as cumulative theist.

| Optional depth module | Precise doctrinal / interpretive issue | Rival school / commentarial readings | Strongest extra objection → counter-reply | Credible source trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ Navya-Nyāya technical language | Does later Navya formalism merely complicate Nyāya, or does it supply the finest available tools for analysing cognition, absence and relation? | Classical-expository reading: old Nyāya is enough for UPSC doctrine; Navya reading: later precision clarifies exactly what is being cognised and how | **Obj.:** Navya terminology is scholastic excess. **Reply:** true for exam volume, but it shows why Nyāya remained philosophically alive and analytically exact | Gaṅgeśa, *Tattvacintāmaṇi*; B.K. Matilal, *Perception*; Karl Potter, *Encyclopedia of Indian Philosophies* |
| ⚠️ Samavāya as a relation-theory stress test | Does samavāya explain inseparable relatedness, or merely name the problem of relation? | Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika realism: inherence is a unique irreducible relation; Buddhist/Advaita pressure: positing a special relation invites regress or duplication | **Obj.:** if relation itself must relate relata, regress follows. **Reply:** Vaiśeṣika treats samavāya as sui generis and non-relationally mediated; critics find this primitive but not obviously incoherent | Kaṇāda, *Vaiśeṣika-sūtra*; Praśastapāda tradition; Chatterjee & Datta |
| ⚠️ Viśeṣa as primitive “thisness” | Is viśeṣa a necessary ontological differentiator of eternal reals, or an ad hoc entity added to save plurality? | Realist reading: eternal atoms and selves need ultimate differentiators; rival pressure from Buddhists/Advaita: plurality may be conceptual or derivative, not ontologically basic | **Obj.:** viśeṣa triggers a regress of differentiators. **Reply:** the school stops regress by treating viśeṣa as self-differentiating, primitive particularity | Kaṇāda, *Vaiśeṣika-sūtra*; Udayana; Radhakrishnan |
| ⚠️ Udayana as cumulative rational theist | Are Nyāya proofs strongest singly or cumulatively as a web of causal, teleological, moral and scriptural considerations? | Narrow exam reading: list proofs; broader reading: Udayana builds mutually reinforcing realism and theism | **Obj.:** accumulation hides weakness of each proof. **Reply:** Nyāya can answer that metaphysical explanation often proceeds cumulatively rather than through one demonstrative inference | Udayana, *Nyāyakusumāñjali*; Gautama, *Nyāya-sūtra*; Chatterjee & Datta |

- **What not to over-study:** ⚠️ full Navya symbolising habits, endless lists of later sub-fallacies, or every later theistic proof variant once the routed PYQs are answerable.
- **Promotion trigger:** ✅ Promote if a future PYQ explicitly asks Navya-Nyāya, presses the regress against samavāya or viśeṣa, or asks whether Nyāya theism is cumulative rather than proof-by-proof.

## 5. Sāṃkhya

- **Owner:** [Sāṃkhya](../paper-1/indian/Samkhya.md)
- **Core prerequisite:** ✅ Master one Prakṛti, many Puruṣas, guṇa-traya and guṇa-pariṇāma, satkāryavāda/pariṇāmavāda, tattva-evolution, proofs for Puruṣa, kaivalya, and the Śaṅkara critique as routed by 2018–2025 PYQs.
- **Safe 15/20-marker use:** ⚠️ Use one optional issue only after the standard proof chain is done—usually the “contact problem,” teleology without God, or the charge that plurality of Puruṣas is partly defensive.

| Optional depth module | Precise doctrinal / interpretive issue | Rival school / commentarial readings | Strongest extra objection → counter-reply | Credible source trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ The “contact” of Puruṣa and Prakṛti | If the two are utterly different and one is inactive, what does their “proximity” really mean? | Literal-contact language in summaries; subtler reading: epistemic/reflective conjunction, not physical contact | **Obj.:** without real relation, no experience begins. **Reply:** Sāṃkhya typically relies on presence-for-the-sake-of-experience, not mechanical interaction; critics still find the relation under-explained | Īśvarakṛṣṇa, *Sāṃkhya-kārikā*; Vācaspati Miśra, *Tattvakaumudī*; Gerald James Larson, *Classical Sāṃkhya* |
| ⚠️ Teleology without Īśvara | Can unconscious Prakṛti evolve “for” Puruṣa’s experience and liberation without smuggling in purpose from elsewhere? | Sāṃkhya’s internal teleology: Prakṛti serves Puruṣa; Nyāya/Vedānta pressure: purposive order points beyond unconscious matter | **Obj.:** unconscious matter cannot pursue ends. **Reply:** Sāṃkhya treats teleology as structural, not intentional—like milk flowing for the calf in traditional analogies | Īśvarakṛṣṇa, *Sāṃkhya-kārikā*; Chatterjee & Datta; Radhakrishnan |
| ⚠️ Is plurality of Puruṣas fully demonstrated? | Are the arguments for many Puruṣas decisive, or partly introduced to preserve individual bondage and liberation? | Classical defence: diversity of births, deaths and moral trajectories implies plurality; Advaita pressure: one consciousness can appear many through upādhis | **Obj.:** plurality is inferentially weaker than Sāṃkhya claims. **Reply:** the school can answer that moral individuality and non-simultaneous liberation become unintelligible on strict one-self models | *Sāṃkhya-kārikā*; Vācaspati Miśra; C.D. Sharma |

- **What not to over-study:** ⚠️ long textual-strata debates on every tattva list, or specialist debates on commentarial chronology once the owner’s causation-proofs-liberation sequence is solid.
- **Promotion trigger:** ✅ Promote if a future PYQ directly asks whether Puruṣa–Prakṛti relation is coherent, whether Sāṃkhya teleology presupposes God, or whether many Puruṣas are proved rather than postulated.

## 6. Yoga

- **Owner:** [Yoga](../paper-1/indian/Yoga.md)
- **Core prerequisite:** ✅ Master citta, citta-vṛtti-nirodha, citta-bhūmis, kleśas, aṣṭāṅga and kriyā-yoga, samprajñāta/asamprajñāta/dharmamegha samādhi, Īśvara as puruṣa-viśeṣa, kaivalya, and all 2019–2025 Yoga PYQs.
- **Safe 15/20-marker use:** ⚠️ Add optional depth only in the critical paragraph—typically on whether Īśvara is necessary, whether samādhi itself liberates, or whether dharmamegha/asamprajñāta should be read more strictly.

| Optional depth module | Precise doctrinal / interpretive issue | Rival school / commentarial readings | Strongest extra objection → counter-reply | Credible source trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ Is Īśvara philosophically necessary? | Does Yoga need Īśvara to complete its philosophy, or is Īśvara mainly a privileged practical aid? | Stronger theistic reading: Īśvara is central to Yoga’s distinctiveness; pragmatic reading: ontology is basically Sāṃkhyan and Īśvara primarily aids concentration | **Obj.:** if liberation finally depends on viveka-khyāti, Īśvara is dispensable. **Reply:** Yoga can concede that Īśvara is instrumental rather than constitutive while still insisting that the instrument is uniquely powerful | Patañjali, *Yoga-sūtra*; Vyāsa, *Yoga-bhāṣya*; Edwin Bryant, *The Yoga Sūtras of Patañjali* |
| ⚠️ Asamprajñāta and dharmamegha disputes | Is asamprajñāta simply nirbīja cessation, and how sharply should it be distinguished from dharmamegha-samādhi? | Some commentarial readings compress the upper stages; others keep fine distinctions between seedless cessation, residual saṃskāra, and the final cloud-of-dharma culmination | **Obj.:** the refinements are scholastic overgrowth. **Reply:** for UPSC they are optional, but they help explain why “seedless” does not always mean “already final” in one flat sense | Patañjali, *Yoga-sūtra*; Vyāsa, *Yoga-bhāṣya*; Vācaspati Miśra, *Tattvavaiśāradī* |
| ⚠️ Knowledge versus absorption | What exactly liberates in Yoga: samādhi itself, or discriminative knowledge matured through samādhi? | Absorption-heavy reading: meditative cessation is decisive; cognition-heavy reading: samādhi serves viveka-khyāti, which does the liberating work | **Obj.:** if knowledge liberates, Yoga collapses into Sāṃkhya. **Reply:** Yoga answers that its distinctive psychology explains how such discriminative knowledge is actually stabilised and existentially efficacious | Patañjali, *Yoga-sūtra*; Vyāsa, *Yoga-bhāṣya*; Chatterjee & Datta |

- **What not to over-study:** ⚠️ full commentarial enumerations of samādhi sub-levels, every siddhi passage, or sectarian devotional overlays that turn Yoga into a bhakti system.
- **Promotion trigger:** ✅ Promote if a future PYQ asks whether Īśvara is necessary, whether dharmamegha differs from asamprajñāta in a philosophically weighty way, or whether knowledge or samādhi is primary in liberation.

Mohanty, studies in Indian epistemology |
