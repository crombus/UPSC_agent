# CORE COMPLETENESS AND ADVANCED COVERAGE MATRIX

> **Meaning of PASS:** The owner file remains the complete exam unit. Its named syllabus terms,
> available PYQ demands, standard argument structure, objections, comparisons, traps and answer
> architecture remain in Core. Advanced work may deepen judgement but is not required to attempt
> the paper.
> **Last full re-audit of all forty rows: 15 August 2026.** Every row was re-checked against the
> *repaired* owner file, not against the earlier draft. Where a doctrine has since been promoted
> into Core, the Optional trail has been rewritten so that it no longer advertises Core content
> as Advanced-only.
> **Firewall unchanged:** exactly forty owners; one primary owner per PYQ part; no question is
> ever routed to `_advanced`.

## Audit gates

| Gate | Core must contain | Advanced may contain |
|---|---|---|
| Syllabus | Every printed term as an explicit, searchable treatment | No new standalone syllabus topic |
| PYQ | Exact routing and all knowledge needed by the demand | Further debate after the demand is answerable |
| Doctrine | Statement, argument, presupposition, distinction and example | Primary-text and interpretive nuance |
| Criticism | At least one strong objection, reply and graded evaluation | Counter-objections and specialist disputes |
| Comparison | Every comparison directly required by syllabus/PYQ | Additional cross-tradition parallels |
| Answer conversion | 10/15/20-mark architecture, traps and keywords | Extra evaluative lines and alternative conclusions |

## How to read the two right-hand columns

- **Promoted into Core (this audit)** = the doctrine now has an explicit, labelled treatment in
  the owner file. It must **not** be described anywhere as an Advanced-only trail, and an answer
  that needs it must be writable without opening `_advanced`.
- **Optional Advanced trail** = a second-order dispute, a specialist literature, or a
  counter-objection that *presupposes* the Core account. A row is marked **PASS** only where the
  current Core text supports it; nothing below is passed on the strength of a dossier.

## Re-audit trigger

Change a row from **PASS** to **OPEN** if a new paper, corrected source or owner-file review
reveals that Core lacks any knowledge necessary to answer a syllabus term or PYQ. The missing
material must be added to Core before Advanced work continues.

Change a row's **Optional trail** whenever a promotion fires: the promoted doctrine moves into
the "Promoted into Core" column, and the corresponding dossier entry must be **recast as
second-order enrichment** — stating that the doctrine now lives in Core and keeping only the
counter-objection, the interpretive dispute or the extension. Useful nuance is never deleted
merely because the headline doctrine was promoted.
