# ADVANCED LAYER - Philosophy Optional

> **Status:** Optional enrichment only.
> **Non-negotiable rule:** Skipping this folder must not prevent a well-prepared candidate from
> answering any syllabus term or any available 2018-2025 PYQ.

## 1. Core-completeness firewall

Material belongs in the forty syllabus-owner files, not here, if **any** of the following is
true:

1. it is a term explicitly named in the official syllabus;
2. it is required to explain an available PYQ's exact demand;
3. it is a standard argument, presupposition, distinction, example, objection, reply or
   evaluation needed for a 10-, 15- or 20-mark answer;
4. it is needed to distinguish two syllabus thinkers or schools;
5. omitting it could make an otherwise prepared candidate unable to attempt a compulsory
   short-note question safely.

The Advanced layer may contain:

- a second or third scholarly interpretation after the examiner-safe interpretation is already
  complete in Core;
- primary-text nuance beyond what is needed to reconstruct the doctrine;
- specialist disputes about attribution, chronology or terminology;
- extended comparisons not demanded by the syllabus or the held PYQs;
- contemporary extensions that sharpen evaluation but are not needed to complete the answer;
- difficult counter-objections after the standard objection and reply are already in Core.

## 2. Promotion rule

Advanced material is immediately **promoted to the owner file** when:

- a PYQ asks it directly or presupposes it;
- repeated PYQs show that it is a stable answer requirement;
- it is necessary to repair a doctrinal gap or misleading simplification in Core;
- it becomes the shortest reliable way to explain a named syllabus term.

No question may be routed primarily to an Advanced file. Every PYQ has exactly one syllabus
owner.

## 3. How to study

1. Finish the owner file and its answer architectures.
2. Write at least one relevant PYQ without notes.
3. Use [`Coverage-Matrix.md`](Coverage-Matrix.md) to confirm that the Core gate is closed.
4. Open [`Depth-Map.md`](Depth-Map.md) only for evaluation, comparison and higher-quality
   philosophical judgement.
5. Add at most one or two Advanced insights to an answer. Do not replace exposition with
   scholarship display.

## 4. Scoring purpose

Advanced study should improve:

- precision of evaluation;
- ability to handle unfamiliar wording;
- quality of inter-school comparison;
- sophistication of objections and replies;
- philosophical closure in 15- and 20-mark answers.

It must not create a second syllabus, a second set of compulsory notes, or anxiety-driven
collection of unlimited philosophy.
