## 1. What qualifies as useful depth

An Advanced addition should do at least one of four things:

1. expose a hidden presupposition in a Core argument;
2. supply a stronger objection and a defensible reply;
3. distinguish two credible interpretations of the same philosopher;
4. create a controlled comparison that improves evaluation.

If it merely adds biography, terminology, quotations, names or historical detail without
improving an answer, it does not belong here.

## 2. Western trails

| Owner | High-value enrichment question |
|---|---|
| Plato-Aristotle | Does Aristotle reject separate Forms or transform their explanatory role into immanent form? |
| Rationalism | Can clear and distinct perception escape the Cartesian circle, and can rationalism reconcile necessity with freedom? |
| Empiricism | Does Hume destroy reason or reconstruct it as natural human practice? |
| Kant | Is transcendental idealism a two-world ontology or a two-aspect account of one reality? |
| Hegel | Is the Absolute a metaphysical super-entity or the achieved intelligibility of experience? |
| Moore/Russell/Early Wittgenstein | Does logical analysis reveal reality or impose an artificial logical form on ordinary language? |
| Logical Positivism | Can verification be formulated without becoming either self-refuting or too permissive? |
| Later Wittgenstein | Does rule-following require a community, or only public criteria? |
| Husserl | Can phenomenological reduction avoid idealism while suspending the natural attitude? |
| Existentialism | Can radical freedom coexist with facticity, social oppression and historical conditions? |
| Quine/Strawson | Does conceptual revision defeat descriptive metaphysics, or presuppose the framework it revises? |

## 3. Indian trails

| Owner | High-value enrichment question |
|---|---|
| Carvaka | Can a strict perceptualism state or defend itself without relying on inference? |
| Jainism | Does anekantavada avoid contradiction, or relocate truth to conditioned standpoints? |
| Buddhism | Can causal and moral continuity survive without substance or self? |
| Nyaya-Vaisesika | Does realist categorisation explain experience better than rival process and non-dual ontologies? |
| Samkhya | Is the plurality of purusas demonstrated, or added to protect individual bondage and liberation? |
| Yoga | Is Isvara philosophically necessary to Yoga or a privileged practical aid? |
| Mimamsa | Is intrinsic validity a better response to scepticism than Nyaya's extrinsic validation? |
| Vedanta | Which account of difference best preserves liberation without making world-experience unintelligible? |
| Aurobindo | Is spiritual evolution a philosophical explanation, a metaphysical vision, or both? |

## 4. Socio-political trails

| Owner | High-value enrichment question |
|---|---|
| Equality, Justice, Liberty | Should justice equalise resources, welfare, capabilities, status or power? |
| Sovereignty | Can authority remain supreme in a constitutionally dispersed and globally interdependent order? |
| Individual and State | When do duties and accountability become domination rather than legitimate coordination? |
| Forms of Government | Is democracy justified by equal status, good outcomes, public reason or collective autonomy? |
| Political Ideologies | Can socialism secure substantive freedom without creating bureaucratic domination? |
| Humanism/Secularism/Multiculturalism | How can equal citizenship recognise difference without freezing communities or internal hierarchy? |
| Crime and Punishment | Can punishment communicate censure without reproducing vengeance and exclusion? |
| Development and Social Progress | Is development expansion of freedom, satisfaction of needs, growth of capability or transformation of power? |
| Gender Discrimination | How should structural injustice be assigned when no single agent causes the whole wrong? |
| Caste: Gandhi and Ambedkar | Can moral reform dismantle graded inequality without structural, legal and political reconstruction? |

## 5. Philosophy-of-religion trails

| Owner | High-value enrichment question |
|---|---|
| Notions of God | Are omnipotence, omniscience, perfection and human freedom jointly coherent? |
| Proofs for God | Do cumulative arguments rationally outperform isolated demonstrations? |
| Problem of Evil | Does evil disprove God, lower the probability of God, or expose limits of human judgement? |
| Soul, Immortality, Rebirth | What kind of identity is needed for responsibility, survival and liberation? |
| Reason, Revelation, Faith | Can faith be rational without evidence, and can revelation be public evidence? |
| Religious Experience | Is religious experience perceptual knowledge, interpretation or transformation? |
| Religion without God | Which features make a non-theistic practice genuinely religious? |
| Religion and Morality | Does morality need divine authority, metaphysical objectivity, communal practice or none of these? |
| Religious Pluralism | Can incompatible truth claims be equally valid without weakening truth into perspective? |
| Religious Language | How can language be cognitively meaningful when its object is said to transcend literal predication? |

## 6. Use in answers

For a 15- or 20-mark answer, choose no more than:

- one interpretation dispute;
- one stronger objection;
- one comparison;
- one graded final judgement.

Core exposition must remain the majority of the answer. Advanced material is valuable only when
it directly sharpens the directive word.
