## 2026 — SECTION A ✅

### Q1 — compulsory, answer in about 150 words each (10 × 5 = 50)

- **Q1(a) · 10 marks · [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md):** How does Spinoza explain the relationship between states of mind and bodily processes?
- **Q1(b) · 10 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** Explain the distinction between Intuitive knowledge and Demonstrative knowledge as propounded by Locke. → owner block: `Empiricism.md` §1.1, "Degrees of knowledge".
- **Q1(c) · 10 marks · [Hegel](./western/Hegel.md):** Explain Hegel's idea that the concept of the Absolute as being is the concept of the Absolute as becoming as a process of self-development.
- **Q1(d) · 10 marks · [Plato–Aristotle](./western/Plato-Aristotle.md):** Compare and contrast the notion of entelechy as presented in Aristotle's and Leibniz's philosophy. → owner block: `Plato-Aristotle.md` §6.6; Leibniz half cross-linked from [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md) §2.3.
- **Q1(e) · 10 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** "I can prove now, for instance, that two human hands exist." How does Moore use this illustration to prove the existence of an external world? Critically discuss.

### Q2 (50)

- **Q2(a) · 20 marks · [Plato–Aristotle](./western/Plato-Aristotle.md):** What, according to Aristotle, is the fundamental problem of metaphysics? Discuss this in relation with Aristotle's account of the essence of the world, where he rejects the views of both the Atomists and Plato. → owner block: `Plato-Aristotle.md` §2A.
- **Q2(b) · 15 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** How does Hume account for ideas of reason? How does Kant respond to Hume's views in this regard? Critically discuss. → Kant half cross-linked to [Kant](./western/Kant.md).
- **Q2(c) · 15 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** Describe Sartre's notion of consciousness as 'nothing'. How is it different from Husserl's notion of consciousness? Discuss. → owner block: `Existentialism.md` §2.4A; Husserl half cross-linked from [Phenomenology (Husserl)](./western/Phenomenology-Husserl.md) §1.5.

### Q3 (50)

- **Q3(a) · 20 marks · [Quine–Strawson](./western/Quine-Strawson.md):** Why are states of consciousness ascribed to anything at all and why are they ascribed to the same things to which we also ascribe physical properties? How does Strawson answer these questions in his work *Individuals*?
- **Q3(b) · 15 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** Explain Heidegger's conception of authenticity and inauthenticity as modes of Dasein's being.
- **Q3(c) · 15 marks · [Later Wittgenstein](./western/Later-Wittgenstein.md):** Can words be viewed as the ideas in the mind of the person who uses them? Answer with reference to the philosophy of later Wittgenstein.

### Q4 (50)

- **Q4(a) · 20 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** "Wittgenstein's difficulty about not being able to say anything within a given language about the structure of that language can be met by the idea of a hierarchy of languages." Do you agree with this statement? Give reasons and justifications for your answer. → owner block: `Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md` §6.7; metalanguage/unified-science link cross-noted in [Logical Positivism](./western/Logical-Positivism.md) §3.6.
- **Q4(b) · 15 marks · [Kant](./western/Kant.md):** Analyze and discuss the different ways in which Kant and Hegel conceived the relationship between reality and our thinking about reality. → Hegel half cross-linked to [Hegel](./western/Hegel.md). ⚠️ Routed to Kant on the 2018 Q2(b) precedent: the question moves *from* the Kantian settlement *to* Hegel's correction of it.
- **Q4(c) · 15 marks · [Logical Positivism](./western/Logical-Positivism.md):** How did the logical positivists try to make philosophy a handmaid to science and establish a unified language of science? Why did their project fail? Discuss.

## TREND NOTE, 2026 ⚠️

1. **Comparison is now the default long-question form.** Four of the fourteen parts (Q1(d), Q2(b), Q2(c), Q4(b)) are explicitly two-philosopher comparisons, and Q2(a) is a three-way one (Aristotle against Atomists *and* Plato). Single-thinker exposition survives mainly at 10 marks.
2. **Thinkers previously examined thematically are now examined textually.** Q1(b) asks for Locke's own *degrees of knowledge* taxonomy, not for empiricism in general; Q3(a) names *Individuals*; Q1(e) quotes "Proof of an External World"; Q4(a) quotes a proposal made in Russell's Introduction to the *Tractatus*.
3. **Continuity of owners.** Plato–Aristotle, Empiricism, Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein and Existentialism again own two parts each, sustaining their 2018–2025 dominance; Phenomenology (Husserl) appears only as a cross-owned comparison half, which is the first such year in the local corpus.
4. **The failure question returns.** Q4(c) asks not only for the positivist programme but for **why it failed** — the same critical-verdict demand seen in 2018 Q3(b) and 2025 Q3(b).
5. **Routing discipline.** Two routing calls were judgement calls: Q4(b) → Kant (not Hegel) and Q2(b) → Empiricism (not Kant). In both, the owner is the philosopher whose position the question asks to be *established* before the response is assessed.
