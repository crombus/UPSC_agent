## 2018 ✅

- **Q5(a) · 10 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** How do the Vaiśeṣika philosophers explain the difference of the relationships in the two cases—(i) the brown colour of the table and (ii) the book on the table? Discuss.
- **Q5(b) · 10 marks · [Buddhism](./indian/Buddhism.md):** How do the Mādhyamika Buddhists apply the notion of Pratītyasamutpāda to establish their doctrine of Śūnyatā? Discuss.
- **Q5(c) · 10 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** What is the difference between the indescribability (Anirvacanīyatā) of Brahman and the indescribability (Anirvacanīyatā) of Māyā in the Advaita Vedānta system? Discuss.
- **Q5(d) · 10 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** How do the Buddhists and the Nyāya philosophers explain our knowledge of 'the absence of the jar on the table'? Answer in detail.
- **Q5(e) · 10 marks · [Sāṃkhya](./indian/Samkhya.md):** Is Puruṣa one or many? Explain the Sāṃkhya position in this regard and give arguments in support of your answer.
- **Q6(a) · 20 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** How do the Naiyāyikas prove the existence of God? Do the Yoga philosophers prove God in the same way? If yes, how? And if no, why? Give reasons for your answer.
- **Q6(b) · 15 marks · [Buddhism](./indian/Buddhism.md):** Is it consistent for the Buddhists to admit the theory of Nairātmyavāda and the doctrine of Nirvāṇa simultaneously? Give reasons in favour of your answer.
- **Q6(c) · 15 marks · [Jainism](./indian/Jainism.md):** How do the Jaina philosophers explain 'bondage'? What, according to them, is the distinction between 'liberated soul' and 'bound soul'? What do the Jainas think about the condition of the 'liberated soul'? Discuss.
- **Q7(a) · 20 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** Give a comparative exposition of the concept of Mokṣa as found in the systems of Viśiṣṭādvaita, Dvaita, Śuddhādvaita and Acintyabhedābheda.
- **Q7(b) · 15 marks · [Sāṃkhya](./indian/Samkhya.md):** How do the Advaita Vedāntins react to the Prakṛtipariṇāmavāda of the Sāṃkhya philosophy? How do the Sāṃkhyas defend their own position in this regard? Discuss.
- **Q7(c) · 15 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** How does Rāmānuja refute the doctrine of Māyā as propounded by Śaṅkara? Why is Māyā needed by both Rāmānuja and Śaṅkara to establish their doctrines? Discuss. 📝 **Printed wording:** the philosophically unusual second sentence is printed this way and is retained. The Sanskrit terms are not uncertain: Māyā (**माया**), Rāmānuja (**रामानुज**) and Śaṅkara (**शङ्कर**).
- **Q8(a) · 20 marks · [Mīmāṃsā](./indian/Mimamsa.md):** Does the admission of Svayaṃprakāśavāda necessarily lead to the admission of Svataḥprāmāṇyavāda? Discuss after the Naiyāyikas, the Mīmāṃsakas and the Advaita Vedāntins.
- **Q8(b) · 15 marks · [Cārvāka](./indian/Carvaka.md):** Is Cārvāka rejection of inference acceptable to the other systems of Indian philosophy? If not, why? Do you think the views of other systems to be justified? Give reasons for your answer.
- **Q8(c) · 15 marks · [Aurobindo](./indian/Aurobindo.md):** What is Evolution according to Sri Aurobindo? Describe the process of triple transformation and the nature of gnostic being in his philosophy.

- **Q8(a) · 20 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** Explain the logical consequences of the metaphysical absolutism of Advaitism.
- **Q8(b) · 15 marks · [Aurobindo](./indian/Aurobindo.md):** Explain the unique position of the Supermind in the metaphysical scheme of Aurobindo’s philosophy.
- **Q8(c) · 15 marks · [Sāṃkhya](./indian/Samkhya.md):** Which Sāṃkhya proof for the existence of Prakṛti actually shows that there can be only one Prakṛti? Justify your answer.

## 2022 ✅

- **Q6(c) · 15 marks · [Mīmāṃsā](./indian/Mimamsa.md):** How do Mīmāṃsakas refute the Nyāya view that Implication (arthāpatti) is reducible to Inference (anumāna) and establish Implication as an independent means of valid knowledge (pramāṇa)? Critically discuss.
- **Q7(a) · 20 marks · [Mīmāṃsā](./indian/Mimamsa.md):** Inspite of accepting the intrinsic validity of knowledge, why and how Prabhākara and Kumārila differ in their interpretation of erroneous cognition? Discuss.
- **Q7(b) · 15 marks · [Buddhism](./indian/Buddhism.md):** Explain Buddhist concept of Triratna and their internal relation. Critically examine the consistency of Triratnas with the Buddhist concept of No-soul (Nairātmyavāda).
- **Q7(c) · 15 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** How do Naiyāyikas respond to Cārvāka’s objections against inference (anumāna) and establish inference as an independent means of knowledge? Critically discuss.
- **Q8(a) · 20 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** ‘Brahma satyam jaganmithyā, Jīvo Brahmaiva nāparaḥ’. In the light of this statement explain the ontological status of Īśvara, Jīva and Sākṣī as elucidated in Advaita Vedānta.
- **Q8(b) · 15 marks · [Aurobindo](./indian/Aurobindo.md):** Explain and evaluate the role of integral yoga in the process of triple transformation for individual evolution as expounded by Sri Aurobindo.
- **Q8(c) · 15 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** How does the concept of Liberation (Mokṣa) of Madhvācārya differ from that of Rāmānujācārya? Explain.

## 2023 ✅

- **Q5(a) · 10 marks · [Jainism](./indian/Jainism.md):** “All human knowledge is empirical and therefore relative.” Critically examine Jaina theory of sevenfold judgement (saptabhaṅgīnaya) in the light of above statement.

- **Q5(d) · 10 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** Present an account of Vaiśeṣika’s view of negation in the light of their statement — “Negation always has a counterpositive and absolute negation is an impossibility.”
- **Q5(e) · 10 marks · [Aurobindo](./indian/Aurobindo.md):** Explain the nature and role of Supermind in evolution as per Aurobindo’s philosophy.
- **Q6(a) · 20 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** Discuss Rāmānuja’s criticism of Śaṅkara’s conception of Brahman and Īśvara (God).
- **Q6(b) · 15 marks · [Mīmāṃsā](./indian/Mimamsa.md):** Present Bhaṭṭa’s view of anupalabdhi (non-cognition) as a valid means of knowledge.
- **Q6(c) · 15 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** Elucidate Naiyāyikas view of ordinary and extraordinary perception. Are they justified in accepting that universals are perceived? Discuss.
- **Q7(a) · 20 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** Elucidate Naiyāyikas account of fallacies of the middle term in relation to five characteristics of valid middle term.

- **Q8(a) · 20 marks · [Buddhism](./indian/Buddhism.md):** “Ignorance of dependent origination is suffering while its knowledge is cessation of suffering.” Present an account of Buddhist soteriology in the light of above statement.

### Trend correction ⚠️

1. Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika (**22**) and Vedānta (**20**) dominate the complete corpus, followed by Mīmāṃsā (**14**) and Buddhism (**13**).
2. Jainism, Buddhism, Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika, Sāṃkhya, Mīmāṃsā, Vedānta and Aurobindo own at least one part in every year; Yoga is absent only as a primary owner in 2018, while Cārvāka has no primary-owned part in 2022 or 2023.
3. The restored years materially deepen samavāya versus saṃyoga, absence-cognition, Nyāya/Yoga proofs of God, Sāṃkhya causation, intra-Vedānta mokṣa and Māyā disputes, svayaṃprakāśa–svataḥprāmāṇya, Nimbārka, Viśeṣa and Jīva–sākṣī.
4. Application and controversy remain the dominant form: table/book relations, mango seed, cross-school absence, inference, self, liberation and causation must be argued rather than merely defined.
