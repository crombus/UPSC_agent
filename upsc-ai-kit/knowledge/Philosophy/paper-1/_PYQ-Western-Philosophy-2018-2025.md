## 2018 ✅

- **Q1(a) · 10 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** Are the two truths—'The Sun will rise tomorrow' and '2+2=4' of the same necessity for Hume? Give reasons in favour of your answer.
- **Q1(b) · 10 marks · [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md):** Is there any place for freedom in Leibniz's philosophy, when he speaks of 'pre-established harmony'? Discuss.
- **Q1(c) · 10 marks · [Later Wittgenstein](./western/Later-Wittgenstein.md):** How does the notion of 'family resemblance' help Wittgenstein to uphold that 'Language is a game'? Discuss.
- **Q1(d) · 10 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** How does Sartre connect inauthenticity with bad faith? Why does Sartre show that inauthenticity and bad faith lead to alienation? Discuss.
- **Q1(e) · 10 marks · [Quine–Strawson](./western/Quine-Strawson.md):** How does Strawson explain the concept of person in his philosophy? Discuss.
- **Q2(a) · 20 marks · [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md):** What is the reason for the difference in the definitions and classifications of substances made by Descartes, Spinoza and Leibniz in spite of the fact that they all belonged to the rationalist school of thought? Discuss.
- **Q2(b) · 15 marks · [Kant](./western/Kant.md):** Explain the significance of the Kantian dictum, 'Understanding makes Nature'. How far do you agree that Hegel's Absolutism is the culmination of the Kantian Dualism? Discuss. Give reasons in favour of your answer.
- **Q2(c) · 15 marks · [Quine–Strawson](./western/Quine-Strawson.md):** How does Quine show that the notion of a priori knowledge as discussed by Kant is 'a metaphysical article of faith'? Give reasons for your answer.
- **Q3(a) · 20 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** How does Berkeley establish that Mind and its ideas alone are real? How do Moore and Russell react to Berkeley's view in this regard? Do you find any difference between Moore's reaction and Russell's one? Discuss.
- **Q3(b) · 15 marks · [Logical Positivism](./western/Logical-Positivism.md):** How do the logical positivists show that metaphysical sentences are meaningless? Can their verification theory of meaning account for the meaningfulness of all scientific sentences? Discuss.
- **Q3(c) · 15 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** How does Wittgenstein apply the distinction between 'saying' and 'showing' to point to a single way of apprehending the most decisive problems of philosophy? Is he justified? Give reasons for your answer.
- **Q4(a) · 20 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** What is 'Epoché'? How does Heidegger reject this method of phenomenological reduction? Explain Heidegger's concept of 'being in the world' as opposed to the concept of a transcendental ego.
- **Q4(b) · 15 marks · [Plato–Aristotle](./western/Plato-Aristotle.md):** Is the relation between the Idea and the World as discussed by Plato logically consistent? Discuss Aristotle's views regarding this and also give arguments in favour of your answer.
- **Q4(c) · 15 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** How is Russell's theory of definite description related to his Logical Atomism? Discuss and give reasons for your answer.

- **Q4(a) · 20 marks · [Kant](./western/Kant.md):** How does Kant argue for the transcendence of Space and Time? Discuss.

- **Q4(c) · 15 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** How does Sartre look at the problem of freedom of choice and determinism? Explain.

## 2020 ✅

- **Q1(a) · 10 marks · [Plato–Aristotle](./western/Plato-Aristotle.md):** How does Aristotle argue for the priority of Form over Matter and Actuality over Potentiality? Critically discuss.
- **Q1(b) · 10 marks · [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md):** How does Leibniz's conception of monads bear upon his views on determinism and freedom? Discuss with your own comments.
- **Q1(c) · 10 marks · [Phenomenology (Husserl)](./western/Phenomenology-Husserl.md):** What, according to Husserl, is wrong with psychologism? How does Husserl address the problems with psychologism in his phenomenological method?
- **Q1(d) · 10 marks · [Hegel](./western/Hegel.md):** Examine the reality of the phenomenal world in the light of Hegel's Absolute Idealism.
- **Q1(e) · 10 marks · [Logical Positivism](./western/Logical-Positivism.md):** “The Soul of Superman is Good.” Critically examine the above statement in the light of logical positivism.
- **Q2(a) · 20 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** “I never can catch myself at any time without perception, and never can observe anything but the perception.” How does this statement by Hume problematize the philosophical notion of personal identity? How does Kant deal with this problem in his Critique of Pure Reason?
- **Q2(b) · 15 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** Critically discuss the following statement by Moore: “If anyone tells us that to say 'Blue exists' is the same thing as to say that 'Both blue and consciousness exist', he makes a mistake and a self-contradictory mistake.”
- **Q2(c) · 15 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** “The reason that I call my doctrine logical atomism is because the atoms that I wish to arrive at as the sort of last residue in analysis are logical atoms and not physical atoms.” Write a note on the nature of atomic facts according to Russell in the light of the above statement.
- **Q3(a) · 20 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** What does Kierkegaard mean by saying “Subjectivity is the truth” in the context of the problem of 'the single individual'?
- **Q3(b) · 15 marks · [Quine–Strawson](./western/Quine-Strawson.md):** Evaluate the role of spatio-temporal thinking in objective thinking with reference to Strawson's theory of basic particulars.
- **Q3(c) · 15 marks · [Kant](./western/Kant.md):** When does Pure Reason enter into the realm of Antinomies according to Kant? Is Kant's notion of Antinomies of Pure Reason a natural culmination of his distinction between Phenomena and Noumena? Give reasons in favour of your answer.
- **Q4(a) · 20 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** “You can always make something out of what you have been made into.” Critically discuss this statement by Sartre with reference to his views on existentialism.
- **Q4(b) · 15 marks · [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md):** “From the necessity of the divine nature there must follow infinitely many things in infinitely many ways.” Explain this statement by Spinoza along with some possible criticisms.
- **Q4(c) · 15 marks · [Later Wittgenstein](./western/Later-Wittgenstein.md):** “But could we also imagine a language in which a person could write down or give vocal expression to his inner experiences—his feelings, moods and the rest—for his private use?” Critically discuss the answer offered by Wittgenstein to this question.

## 2021 ✅

- **Q1(a) · 10 marks · [Plato–Aristotle](./western/Plato-Aristotle.md):** “There is a red chair.” How would Plato explain this statement with the use of his theory of forms? Examine.
- **Q1(b) · 10 marks · [Plato–Aristotle](./western/Plato-Aristotle.md):** “Potentiality is indefinable” according to Aristotle. Explain the relationship between potentiality and actuality with reference to the above philosophical position by taking the example of a “wooden table”.
- **Q1(c) · 10 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** “Sensible things are those only which are immediately perceived by sense.” Explain Berkeley’s theory of knowledge with reference to the above statement.
- **Q1(d) · 10 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** Examine the concept of personal identity by Locke.
- **Q1(e) · 10 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** “The relation between cause and effect is one of constant conjunction”. Examine Hume’s criticism of causation in the light of the above statement.
- **Q2(a) · 20 marks · [Hegel](./western/Hegel.md):** Discuss Hegel’s Dialectical method. Explain how his dialectical method leads him to the Absolute Idealism.
- **Q2(b) · 15 marks · [Logical Positivism](./western/Logical-Positivism.md):** What according to Logical Positivists are “pseudostatements”? How does one identify “pseudostatements”? Critically discuss with examples.
- **Q2(c) · 15 marks · [Kant](./western/Kant.md):** Explain how Cartesian formulation of ontological argument is criticised by Kant.
- **Q3(a) · 20 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** What are the main arguments put forward by Moore in his paper “A Defence of Common Sense” to prove that there are possible propositions about the world that are known to be true with certainty? Do you think Moore’s arguments provide a sufficient response to objections presented by the sceptic against the possibility of knowledge? Give reasons in support of your answer.
- **Q3(b) · 15 marks · [Quine–Strawson](./western/Quine-Strawson.md):** What according to Strawson are basic particulars? What reasons does Strawson offer to believe that ‘material bodies’ and ‘persons’ are basic particulars? Critically discuss.
- **Q3(c) · 15 marks · [Quine–Strawson](./western/Quine-Strawson.md):** Critically examine Quine’s postulate of empiricism without the dogmas with reference to his ‘Two Dogmas of Empiricism’.
- **Q4(a) · 20 marks · [Phenomenology (Husserl)](./western/Phenomenology-Husserl.md):** Present a critical exposition of Husserl’s criticism of ‘natural attitude’. How does Husserl propose to address the problems involved in natural attitude through his phenomenological method?
- **Q4(b) · 15 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** “I can always choose, but I ought to know that if I do not choose, I am still choosing”. Critically discuss Sartre’s conception of choice and responsibility in the light of above statement.
- **Q4(c) · 15 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** What does Wittgenstein mean by the statement — “Whereof one cannot speak, thereof one must be silent?” Critically discuss.

## 2022 ✅

- **Q1(b) · 10 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** What is Bertrand Russell’s method of logical analysis? How does it ultimately end in establishing atomic theory of meaning? Discuss.
- **Q1(c) · 10 marks · [Later Wittgenstein](./western/Later-Wittgenstein.md):** Establish the tenability of later Wittgenstein’s motion of language as form of life. 📝 **Printed wording:** the official English line itself prints **“motion”**; it is retained rather than silently changed to “notion”.

- **Q1(e) · 10 marks · [Kant](./western/Kant.md):** What is apperception, according to Immanuel Kant? Discuss with reference to his transcendental exposition of space and time.
- **Q2(a) · 20 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** Provide a critical account of Heidegger’s Being-in-the-world and discuss the problem of ‘authenticity’ in the context of Dasein.
- **Q2(b) · 15 marks · [Plato–Aristotle](./western/Plato-Aristotle.md):** Is Aristotle’s view of nature of identity in consonance with his metaphysical view of causes as processes? Discuss giving suitable examples.
- **Q2(c) · 15 marks · [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md):** Discuss the concept of substance according to Spinoza. Does his discussion on substance lead to pantheism? Substantiate your view.
- **Q3(a) · 20 marks · [Kant](./western/Kant.md):** How does Kant construct antinomies to illustrate the illusory tendencies of pure reason? Explain and examine the antinomies presented by Kant.
- **Q3(b) · 15 marks · [Hegel](./western/Hegel.md):** What is the dialectical method in the philosophy of Georg Wilhelm Hegel? How does this method help in realizing the Absolute? Discuss.
- **Q3(c) · 15 marks · [Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein](./western/Moore-Russell-EarlyWittgenstein.md):** Is there any difference between pictorial form and logical form in Ludwig Wittgenstein’s picture theory of language? How does the logical form define the relation between language and reality? Explain.
- **Q4(a) · 20 marks · [Existentialism](./western/Existentialism.md):** How does Soren Kierkegaard define the notion of ‘subjectivity’? Explain it with reference to three stages of existence as propounded by him.
- **Q4(b) · 15 marks · [Rationalism](./western/Rationalism.md):** How does Rene Descartes explain the notion of certainty with reference to knowledge of the self? Critically discuss the way it differs from the knowledge of the world.
- **Q4(c) · 15 marks · [Empiricism](./western/Empiricism.md):** Why and how does John Locke refute the innate ideas? Elucidate the nature and source of knowledge in Locke’s epistemology.

### Trend correction ⚠️

1. Rationalism, Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein and Existentialism jointly lead with **14** primary-owned parts; Plato–Aristotle and Empiricism follow with **12** each.
2. Plato–Aristotle, Empiricism, Moore–Russell–Early Wittgenstein and Existentialism own at least one part in every year of the complete eight-year corpus.
3. The restored papers add family resemblance, Leibnizian freedom, Kant–Hegel and Berkeley–Moore–Russell comparisons, scientific-sentence pressure on verification, private language, Hume on personal identity and Strawson on spatio-temporal identification.
4. Long questions increasingly demand a bridge between systems, but primary ownership remains singular and is determined by the principal argumentative burden.
