> **Type:** Cross-thinker theme file (Paper-I, Section A). Spans items 4, 6–8, 11.
> **Why this file exists:** A recurring UPSC thread is **"the attack on / rejection of metaphysics."** Four distinct assaults — Hume, Kant, Logical Positivism, Later Wittgenstein (with Quine reinforcing) — each on *different grounds*. Knowing *how they differ* converts a vague answer into a top-scoring one.

## 1. THE FOUR CRITIQUES ✅

### A. HUME — the empiricist bonfire
- **Principle:** every genuine idea must derive from an **impression**. Metaphysical notions — **substance, the self, causal necessity, God** — have **no corresponding impression** → they are illegitimate or fictions. ✅
- **The famous fork test:** any book (metaphysics/divinity) that contains *neither* abstract reasoning about quantity/number *nor* experimental reasoning about matters of fact — *"commit it then to the flames, for it can contain nothing but sophistry and illusion."* ✅
- **Character:** metaphysics fails a **content** test — its terms lack empirical meaning.

### B. KANT — the critical limitation
- **Not rejection but boundary-drawing:** the categories (substance, cause) are valid **only within possible experience** (phenomena). When reason applies them **beyond experience** to the unconditioned (soul, cosmos, God), it generates **illusion**. ✅
- **Symptoms:** the **Antinomies** (equally valid proofs of contradictory theses, e.g. world is/ isn't finite in time) show reason contradicting itself when it oversteps; the **Paralogisms** (fallacies about the soul-substance); and the **demolition of the proofs for God's existence** (ontological, cosmological, teleological). ✅
- **Character:** metaphysics-as-science of the transcendent is impossible, but the *illusion is explained* and a "metaphysics of experience" survives. A **critical**, not dismissive, verdict.

### C. LOGICAL POSITIVISM — the verificationist execution
- **Verification principle:** a statement is cognitively meaningful only if analytic or empirically verifiable. Metaphysical statements are neither → **literally meaningless / pseudo-propositions** (not false — *nonsense*). ✅
- **Diagnosis:** metaphysicians are deceived by **surface grammar** ("the Absolute is perfect"; Heidegger's *das Nichts nichtet* — correctly rendered "**Nothing noths**" or "the nothing nothings", ❌ **never** "Nothing noughts"). Ethics & theology fall too (emotivism; "God exists" = meaningless).
- **Carnap's precise charge** (*"The Elimination of Metaphysics through Logical Analysis of Language,"* 1932): the sentence commits a **double** violation — it treats the syncategorematic quantifier "nothing" as a **name**, and predicates of it a **pseudo-verb**. ⚠️ **Heidegger's available reply, which a critical answer must record:** *das Nichts* is disclosed in the mood of *Angst*, and the breach of logical grammar is deliberate, since logic itself presupposes the prior disclosure of beings as a whole. Reporting the reply is what turns ridicule into assessment. See [`../western/Existentialism.md`](../western/Existentialism.md) §2.9.
- **Carnap's strongest version is not the meaninglessness verdict but the Principle of Tolerance** (*Logical Syntax of Language*, 1934/1937, §17) and the **internal/external** distinction (1950): metaphysical disputes are **framework-choices misread as discoveries**. This explains their irresolubility instead of merely condemning them — and it is what Quine dismantles. See [`../western/Logical-Positivism.md`](../western/Logical-Positivism.md) §C.
- **Character:** a **meaning** test, sharper than Hume — metaphysics doesn't lose the game, it isn't *playing*. ⚠️ (Weakness: the principle is itself unverifiable → self-refuting.)

### D. LATER WITTGENSTEIN — the therapeutic dissolution
- **Meaning = use in a form of life.** Philosophical/metaphysical problems arise when words are wrenched from their ordinary use — *"language goes on holiday"*; philosophy is *"a battle against the bewitchment of our intelligence by means of language."* ✅
- **Method:** not to *refute* metaphysics with a counter-theory but to **dissolve** the confusion by returning words to everyday use — *therapy*. *"Philosophy leaves everything as it is."*
- **Character:** metaphysics is neither false nor meaningless-by-a-criterion but a **muddle** to be cured. Gentler, diagnostic — rejects the positivists' blunt criterion too.

### E. QUINE (reinforcement) — no privileged a priori
- By demolishing the **analytic/synthetic distinction** and adopting **holism/naturalism**, Quine denies metaphysics (or logic) any special a priori standing — but, more moderately, **allows a naturalised ontology** ("to be is to be the value of a bound variable"). ⚠️
- ⚠️ **The sharper point:** Quine's decisive blow lands on **Carnap**, not on metaphysics. The internal/external distinction presupposes the analytic/synthetic distinction; remove the latter and there is only **one** kind of ontological question, answered inside our best total theory. **Positivism's most sophisticated anti-metaphysical instrument is destroyed by another empiricist, and metaphysics returns — chastened and naturalised — through the breach.**

### F. STRAWSON — the reinstatement (the assault that turns into a rescue) ⚠️
- **This entry is what most theme-answers lack.** Strawson does not attack metaphysics; he **redefines** it and thereby saves it: **descriptive metaphysics** describes the actual structure of our conceptual scheme (Aristotle, Kant), while **revisionary metaphysics** proposes a better one (Descartes, Leibniz, Berkeley). *Individuals* (1959).
- The critiques above all target **revisionary/transcendent** metaphysics. Descriptive metaphysics is immune to every one of them: it makes no claim beyond experience (so Kant's limit does not bite), asserts nothing unverifiable about a supersensible realm (so verification does not bite), and describes ordinary use rather than departing from it (so Wittgenstein's therapy does not bite).
- ∴ The trajectory in §3 has **five** stages, not four, and the last one reverses direction.

## 1A. TWO CROSS-THINKER BRIDGES WORTH BUILDING ⚠️ (rarely written; high differentiation)

### Bridge 1 — HEGEL ↔ STRAWSON: two roads to the same verdict on the thing-in-itself

| Step | **Hegel** (1807–31) | **Strawson** (1966) |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Kant's noumenon | Kant's noumenon |
| Charge | To **posit** a limit to thought is already to **think beyond** it; the thing-in-itself is thought's own product | The doctrine of a supersensible reality **affecting** sensibility is **incoherent** — it uses the very concepts (existence, cause, object) whose application beyond experience it forbids (*The Bounds of Sense*) |
| Method | dialectical: the concept destroys itself from within | analytic: the doctrine violates its own restriction |
| Positive residue | reality **is** rational; no unknowable remainder — **Absolute Idealism** | Kant's **austere** core survives once transcendental idealism is stripped away — **descriptive metaphysics** |

> ⚠️ **What to say:** two traditions that despise each other's methods converge on one verdict — the noumenon cannot be both posited and declared unthinkable. They part on the remedy: Hegel abolishes the limit by expanding thought to the Absolute; Strawson abolishes it by shrinking metaphysics to the description of our actual scheme. In Strawson's own vocabulary Hegel's Absolute is **revisionary** metaphysics — and Strawson's list of revisionary metaphysicians (Descartes, Leibniz, Berkeley) is the same list Hegel rejected from the opposite direction. See [`../western/Hegel.md`](../western/Hegel.md) §3.1 and [`../western/Quine-Strawson.md`](../western/Quine-Strawson.md) §3.6.

### Bridge 2 — QUINE ↔ LATER WITTGENSTEIN: holism and use

| Axis | **Quine** | **Later Wittgenstein** |
|---|---|---|
| Target | the **museum myth** — words as labels on determinate mental meanings | the **Augustinian picture** — words as names of objects |
| Positive account | meaning is **dispositions to verbal behaviour** within a total theory | meaning is **use** in a language-game and a form of life |
| Holism | the unit of empirical significance is **the whole of science** | a word has meaning only within a practice; "to understand a sentence is to understand a language" |
| Anti-foundationalism | **Neurath's boat** — rebuild at sea, no dry dock (the epigraph of *Word and Object*) | bedrock where "**my spade is turned**" (PI §217) |
| Against the private | radical translation permits nothing beyond public behaviour to fix meaning | the private-language argument shows nothing private *can* fix meaning |
| Consequence for metaphysics | metaphysics is naturalised, not expelled: ontology is read off our best theory's quantifiers | metaphysics is dissolved: the questions lapse when words return to their home |
| **Where they part** | Quine **regiments** ordinary language into canonical notation and wants a scientific theory of it | Wittgenstein forbids theory-construction: ordinary language "is in order as it is" (PI §98) |

> ⚠️ **What to say:** both reach the impossibility of determinate private meanings from opposite directions — Quine by asking what evidence *could* fix meaning, Wittgenstein by asking what could count as a *rule*. That two philosophers with no shared method and no direct influence converge on the same negative result is the strongest evidence that what fails is the **referential/mentalistic theory of meaning itself**, and not one particular argument against it. The historical link is real, not merely thematic: **Neurath's anti-foundationalism** in the protocol-sentence debate feeds Quine directly. See [`../western/Quine-Strawson.md`](../western/Quine-Strawson.md) §1.8 and [`../western/Logical-Positivism.md`](../western/Logical-Positivism.md) §P.

## 3. HOW THEY DIFFER — the crucial discriminators ⚠️
- **Hume vs Positivists:** both empiricist, but Hume attacks **content** (no impression), positivists attack **meaning** (unverifiable → *nonsense*). Positivism is Hume "logicised".
- **Kant vs the rest:** Kant alone **saves** a chastened metaphysics (of experience) and *explains why* the illusion arises — he limits, he doesn't behead.
- **Positivists vs Later Wittgenstein:** both target language, but positivists wield a **single sharp criterion** (verification); Witt rejects any such criterion — dissolves case-by-case via **use**. Witt even turns against the *Tractatus* (his own earlier attempt to draw limits, item 6).
- **Positivism's internal collapse:** ⚠️ the movement fails from within before it fails from without. The **protocol-sentence debate** (Schlick's incorrigible *Konstatierungen* vs Neurath's fallible, physicalist protocols) leaves verification **without a specified base**; Carnap concedes that the form of protocol sentences is a matter of **linguistic choice**. Say this rather than leading with self-refutation, which is the easiest objection and the least interesting.
- **Quine vs Carnap:** Quine's blow lands on the **internal/external** distinction, not on metaphysics — and lands *because* it presupposes analyticity.
- **Strawson reverses the trajectory:** the four assaults all target **transcendent/revisionary** metaphysics; **descriptive** metaphysics is untouched by every one of them.
- **The trajectory:** *content-attack (Hume) → limit-drawing (Kant) → meaning-criterion (Positivism) → therapeutic dissolution (Witt) → naturalised ontology (Quine) → **descriptive reinstatement (Strawson)***. Increasingly **linguistic** — and then, at the end, **rehabilitative**.

> 🔑 In an answer, **name the ground of each attack** (impression / limit of categories / verifiability / use) — that specificity is what separates a 7 from a 14.

## 4. STATEMENT / QUOTE BANK ⚠️ — with provenance discipline
1. ✅ **Hume:** *"Commit it then to the flames, for it can contain nothing but sophistry and illusion."* — *Enquiry concerning Human Understanding*, **Section XII**, closing paragraph. Aimed at "divinity or school metaphysics."
2. ✅ **Kant:** reason falls into *"antinomies"* when it transcends possible experience — *Critique of Pure Reason*, Transcendental Dialectic (A405/B432 ff.). ⚠️ Cite by **A/B pagination**, which is identical in Kemp Smith, Guyer–Wood and Pluhar. "I had to deny knowledge in order to make room for faith" is **Bxxx**, and *Glaube* is rendered "faith" or "belief" — say which.
3. ✅ **Ayer:** *"A metaphysical sentence… purports to express a genuine proposition, but does not."* — *Language, Truth and Logic* (**1936**); ⚠️ the revised verifiability criterion is in the **1946 Introduction**, and **Church's refutation of it is 1949**.
4. ✅ **Wittgenstein:** *"Philosophy is a battle against the bewitchment of our intelligence by means of language."* — *Philosophical Investigations* **§109**; and *"language goes on holiday"* is **§38** (⚠️ the German *feiert* is closer to "idles"). Cite by § — the numbering is identical across editions and translations.
5. ✅ **Carnap:** *"In logic, there are no morals."* — *The Logical Syntax of Language*, **§17** (German 1934, English 1937).
6. ⚠️ **Heidegger:** *das Nichts nichtet*, *Was ist Metaphysik?* (**1929**) — render "**Nothing noths**" / "the nothing nothings". ❌ **Not** "Nothing noughts."
7. ✅ **Quine:** *"Any statement can be held true come what may, if we make drastic enough adjustments elsewhere in the system."* — "Two Dogmas of Empiricism" (**1951**), §6. ❌ Never truncate before the conditional clause. ⚠️ "To be is to be the value of a bound variable" is from **"On What There Is" (1948)**, a different paper.
8. ✅ **Strawson:** *Individuals: An Essay in Descriptive Metaphysics* (**1959**) — the subtitle is itself the citable phrase.
9. ⚠️ **Popper:** falsifiability (*Logik der Forschung*, **1934**) is a criterion of **demarcation**, not of **meaning**; Popper held metaphysics to be **meaningful** though unscientific, and was never a member of the Vienna Circle. Conflating demarcation with meaning is the commonest error in this theme.

## 6. PYQ-MAPPED SKELETON ⚠️

### "Critically examine the critiques of metaphysics in modern Western philosophy." (recurring)
```
Frame : five assaults on different grounds, and one reinstatement (F-L-N-T + Q, then S).
Body  : Hume (no impression → flames) → Kant (categories overreach → antinomies, limited to phenomena)
        → Positivism (unverifiable → meaningless; and Carnap's stronger tolerance version:
          metaphysical disputes are framework-choices misread as discoveries)
        → Later Witt (misuse of language → dissolve/therapy)
        → Quine (no analytic/synthetic base; ontology naturalised).
Link  : increasingly LINGUISTIC; Kant alone among the first four saves a metaphysics of experience.
Assess: positivism fails FROM WITHIN first — the protocol-sentence debate leaves verification with
        no specified base — before the self-refutation objection is even needed; Quine then removes
        the analyticity on which Carnap's internal/external line depended.
Turn  : Strawson reverses direction — descriptive metaphysics survives every one of these tests
        because it claims nothing transcendent, unverifiable or extraordinary.
Concl : metaphysics survives not as a science of the transcendent but as (a) the critical study of
        the limits of thought (Kant), and (b) the description of the conceptual scheme within which
        anything at all can be said (Strawson). The century's attack destroyed the object it named
        and vindicated a discipline under the same name.
```

### "Compare Hegel's and Strawson's objections to Kant's thing-in-itself." (constructible; see §1A)
```
Frame : one verdict, two methods.
Body A: Hegel — to posit a limit is to think beyond it; the noumenon is thought's own product;
        dualism between an unknowable Real and merely subjective Knowledge is unstable.
Body B: Strawson — the doctrine is incoherent by its own rule: it says the supersensible AFFECTS
        sensibility, applying causality and existence beyond the bounds it has just drawn.
Assess: identical target and verdict; opposite remedies — expand thought to the Absolute (revisionary)
        vs contract metaphysics to description of the scheme (descriptive).
Close : Kant's best reply is available to neither reading of him fully: the noumenon as a purely
        NEGATIVE limiting concept escapes Hegel, but then it cannot "affect" anything, which is
        what Strawson's objection requires it to do. The doctrine survives only by giving up a role
        Kant himself assigned it.
```
