> The papers were OCR-extracted into `knowledge-export/Mains PYQ/` and checked
> against the existing Markdown knowledge base. This is an answer-readiness audit,
> not a claim that the questions have already been propagated into owner files.
>
> **Status definitions:** **FULL** means the present Markdown can support a strong,
> directive-faithful answer. **PARTIAL** means the core answer is possible but an
> important question-specific argument, example, comparison or current fact is
> missing. **GAP** means the central demand lacks adequate coverage.

> machine OCR. The Hindi paper's task structure is legible, but individual Hindi

> `PYQ-MAINS-CA-CROSSCHECK-2026.md` for the distinction between missing static
> concepts, missing current updates, and updates already available in the
