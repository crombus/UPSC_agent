# UPSC INSTITUTIONS AND REGULATORS - QUICK REVISION INDEX

> **Cut-off:** 14 September 2026.
> The legal source matters: a constitutional, statutory, executive, tribunal, registered-society
> or government-company body does not acquire the powers of another category merely because it
> performs an important public function.

## Status legend

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Constitutional** | Created or expressly provided by the Constitution |
| **Statutory** | Created by an Act of Parliament or State legislature |
| **Executive** | Created by resolution, order, notification or allocation-of-business framework |
| **Tribunal** | Adjudicatory body created by statute |
| **Society / company** | Separate registered legal entity owned, controlled or supported by government |

## 1. Constitutional and federal institutions

| Institution | Status / basis | Composition, appointment and tenure | Core powers | Accountability / trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Election Commission of India** | Constitutional, Art 324; appointment law of 2023 | CEC plus ECs fixed by President; appointed by President on committee advice of PM, nominated Cabinet Minister and Lok Sabha LoP; 6 years or age 65 | Superintendence, direction and control of elections | CEC removal resembles SC judge; other ECs removable on CEC recommendation |
| **Union Public Service Commission** | Constitutional, Arts 315-323 | Chair and members appointed by President; roughly half should have 10 years government service; 6 years or age 65 | Recruitment exams, selections, promotions and disciplinary advice | Advice is generally not binding; annual report goes to President and Parliament |
| **State Public Service Commission** | Constitutional, Arts 315-323 | Chair and members appointed by Governor; removal by President; 6 years or age 62 | State recruitment and service advice | Governor appoints but cannot remove on ordinary grounds |
| **Joint State Public Service Commission** | Statutory under Art 315(2) through parliamentary law | Members appointed by President; term aligned with constitutional scheme | Recruitment/advice for participating States | Not itself directly created by Constitution |
| **Finance Commission** | Constitutional, Art 280; Finance Commission Act 1951 | Chair plus four members appointed by President, ordinarily every five years | Tax devolution, grants and fiscal recommendations | Periodic, not permanent; recommendations are advisory |
| **Comptroller and Auditor General of India** | Constitutional, Arts 148-151 | Appointed by President; 6 years or age 65; removal like SC judge | Audits Union/States and bodies under law; reports on public finance | Audits and reports but does not itself recover money or prosecute |
| **Attorney General for India** | Constitutional, Art 76 | Appointed by President; qualified as SC judge; holds office during President's pleasure | Chief legal advice and right of audience; may participate in Parliament | No fixed tenure; not a government servant and cannot vote in Parliament |
| **Advocate General for a State** | Constitutional, Art 165 | Appointed by Governor; qualified as High Court judge; pleasure tenure | State's chief legal adviser | Can participate but not vote in State legislature |
| **GST Council** | Constitutional, Art 279A | Union Finance Minister chair; Union MoS Finance and State finance/tax ministers; ex officio | Recommends GST rates, exemptions, model laws and special provisions | Recommendations have persuasive value; they do not legislate by themselves |
| **National Commission for Scheduled Castes** | Constitutional, Art 338 | Chair, vice-chair and three members appointed by President under rules; fixed rule-based term | Safeguards, complaints, monitoring and advice | Reports to President; civil-court powers do not make it a court |
| **National Commission for Scheduled Tribes** | Constitutional, Art 338A | Chair, vice-chair and three members appointed by President | Safeguards, tribal development monitoring and complaints | Reports to President; distinct from Ministry of Tribal Affairs |
| **National Commission for Backward Classes** | Constitutional, Art 338B | Chair, vice-chair and three members appointed by President | Safeguards and complaints for socially/educationally backward classes | Consultation on policy is constitutionally required; Central List is under Art 342A framework |
| **Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities** | Constitutional, Art 350B | Commissioner appointed by President; service conditions executive/rule based | Investigates safeguards for linguistic minorities | Reports to President, who lays reports before Parliament and States |
| **Inter-State Council** | Constitutional enabling basis, Art 263; Presidential Order 1990 | PM chair; Chief Ministers and specified Union ministers; ex officio | Inquiry, discussion and coordination on inter-State issues | Constitutional enabling body but created by presidential order, not automatically permanent from 1950 |
| **Supreme Court of India** | Constitutional, Arts 124-147 | CJI and judges appointed by President under collegium practice; retirement age 65 | Constitutional, appellate, advisory and writ jurisdiction | Judicial independence is basic structure; not a regulator or legislative chamber |
| **High Courts** | Constitutional, Arts 214-231 | Chief Justice/judges appointed by President under collegium practice; retirement age 62 | Writ, appellate, supervisory and original jurisdictions | Art 226 is wider in subject than Art 32 |

## 2. Federal, planning and inter-governmental bodies

| Institution | Status / basis | Composition, appointment and tenure | Core powers | Accountability / trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **NITI Aayog** | Executive, Union Cabinet resolution 2015 | PM chair; Governing Council of CMs/LGs; vice-chair and members appointed by government | Cooperative federalism, policy strategy, monitoring and knowledge support | Replaced Planning Commission; does not allocate plan funds as the old Commission did |
| **Zonal Councils** | Statutory, States Reorganisation Act 1956 | Union Home Minister chair; CMs, ministers and advisers of member States/UTs | Regional coordination and dispute discussion | Five statutory councils; North Eastern Council is separate |
| **North Eastern Council** | Statutory, NEC Act 1971 | Governors and CMs of eight NE States plus nominated members; Union Home Minister ex officio chair under current framework | Regional planning, coordination and security-related discussion | Not one of the five Zonal Councils |
| **National Development Council** | Executive, 1952 resolution | PM, Union ministers, CMs and administrators | Former national planning consultation | Functionally dormant after Planning Commission's replacement |
| **National Statistical Commission** | Executive, 2005 resolution | Part-time chair and members plus NITI/MoSPI linkage; appointed by government | Advises on statistical standards, coordination and credibility | Not yet a constitutional or fully statutory commission |
| **Registrar General and Census Commissioner of India** | Statutory functions under Census Act and Registration of Births and Deaths Act; office under MHA | Registrar General appointed by Central Government | Census, SRS, CRS and demographic administration | Census and civil registration are different systems |
| **Delimitation Commission** | Statutory, constituted under a Delimitation Act | Usually retired SC judge chair, CEC/nominee and State ECs as ex officio members | Final constituency delimitation orders | Periodic body; orders generally cannot be questioned in court |
| **River-water dispute tribunals** | Statutory, Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956 | Tribunal members are senior judges appointed under statute | Adjudicate notified inter-State river disputes | Not permanent constitutional courts; award framework is Act-specific |

## 3. Transparency, integrity and human-rights bodies

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Central Information Commission** | Statutory, RTI Act 2005 | Chief IC plus up to ten ICs; President appoints on PM-LoP-nominated minister committee; 3 years under 2019 Rules, age ceiling 65 | Second appeals, complaints, disclosure directions and penalties | Does not create records or override every statutory exemption |
| **State Information Commission** | Statutory, RTI Act 2005 | State Chief IC plus up to ten ICs; Governor appoints on CM-LoP-nominated minister committee | State-public-authority appeals and complaints | State body, not subordinate bench of CIC |

| **Lokpal of India** | Statutory, Lokpal Act 2013 | Chair plus up to eight members; half judicial and diversity requirement; selection by PM, Speaker, LoP, CJI/nominee and jurist; 5 years or age 70 | Preliminary inquiry, investigation/prosecution directions and asset-related powers | Lokpal is not a constitutional body and State Lokayuktas follow State laws |
| **National Human Rights Commission** | Statutory, PHRA 1993 | Chair, judicial and expert members plus ex officio commission heads; President appoints on six-member political committee; 3 years or age 70 | Inquiries, interventions, jail visits, research and recommendations | Recommendations are generally not binding; armed-forces procedure is specially limited |
| **State Human Rights Commissions** | Statutory, PHRA 1993 | Chair and members appointed by Governor on statutory committee recommendation | Human-rights inquiries within State List/Concurrent matters | Cannot inquire into matters already before NHRC/another competent commission |
| **National Commission for Women** | Statutory, NCW Act 1990 | Chair, five members and member-secretary appointed by Central Government; normally three years | Review safeguards, complaints, inspections and policy advice | Recommendatory, not a court |
| **National Commission for Minorities** | Statutory, NCM Act 1992 | Chair, vice-chair and five members appointed by Central Government; three years | Safeguards, complaints, studies and advice for notified minorities | Minority notification and commission membership are central-government functions |
| **National Commission for Protection of Child Rights** | Statutory, CPCR Act 2005 | Chair plus six members, at least two women; Central appointment; three-year term with age ceilings | Child-rights monitoring, complaints, inspections and law review | Also monitors POCSO/RTE functions assigned by law |
| **National Commission for Safai Karamcharis** | Executive/non-statutory after original 1993 Act lapsed | Chair, vice-chair and members appointed for government-notified tenure | Monitors safeguards, rehabilitation and grievances | Frequently mislabelled statutory; current continuation is by executive resolutions |
| **Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities** | Statutory, RPwD Act 2016 | Chief Commissioner and commissioners appointed by Central Government under rules | Rights monitoring, complaints, accessibility and recommendations | Distinct from the constitutional commissions |
| **Rehabilitation Council of India** | Statutory, RCI Act 1992 | Chair and members appointed/nominated under Act; terms prescribed | Regulates rehabilitation-professional training and central register | Not the disability-rights grievance commission |
| **National Commission for Minority Educational Institutions** | Statutory, NCMEI Act 2004 | Chair must have been High Court judge plus three members; Central appointment | Minority-status and affiliation-related adjudicatory functions | Distinct from National Commission for Minorities |
| **Press Council of India** | Statutory, Press Council Act 1978 | Chair plus 28 members representing press, Parliament and public fields; three-year council | Press standards, censure and press-freedom protection | Cannot impose criminal punishment or monetary damages |
| **Law Commission of India** | Executive, periodically reconstituted | Chair, members and ex officio officials appointed for notified term | Research and recommend law reform | Reports are advisory; body is neither permanent nor statutory |

## 4. Investigation, enforcement and national-security institutions

| Institution | Status / basis | Composition, appointment and tenure | Core powers | Accountability / trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Central Bureau of Investigation** | Executive organisation deriving police powers from DSPE Act 1946 | Director selected by PM-LoP-CJI/nominee committee; minimum two years, extendable annually up to five | Anti-corruption, economic and special-crime investigation with jurisdictional consent rules | No standalone CBI Act; State consent is not needed when constitutional courts direct investigation |
| **Enforcement Directorate** | Executive department under Department of Revenue; powers under PMLA/FEMA/FEOA | Director appointed by Central Government through statutory committee process; two years extendable up to five | Money-laundering investigation, attachment and foreign-exchange enforcement | ED is not the predicate-offence police agency in every case |
| **Financial Intelligence Unit-India** | Executive office under Department of Revenue | Director and specialised staff appointed by government | Receives, analyses and disseminates suspicious-transaction information | Intelligence unit, not a prosecution court |
| **Serious Fraud Investigation Office** | Statutory recognition under Companies Act 2013; executive office under MCA | Director and multidisciplinary experts appointed by Central Government | Investigates serious corporate fraud assigned by government | Jurisdiction begins through statutory assignment; distinct from CBI/ED |
| **National Investigation Agency** | Statutory, NIA Act 2008 | Director-General appointed by Central Government; police organisation with Special Courts | Investigates scheduled national-security offences | Can take over scheduled cases under statutory process; not an intelligence agency |
| **National Crime Records Bureau** | Executive attached office under MHA | Director and police/statistical staff appointed by government | Crime databases, CCTNS/ICJS support and annual crime statistics | Registered-crime data are administrative, not prevalence surveys |
| **Narcotics Control Bureau** | Statutory authority constituted under NDPS Act 1985; MHA organisation | Director-General and zonal structure appointed by government | Coordinates drug-law enforcement and international cooperation | NCB, DRI, police and other agencies have overlapping statutory roles |
| **Intelligence Bureau** | Executive intelligence agency under MHA | Director appointed by government; no public statutory tenure framework | Domestic intelligence and counter-intelligence | No dedicated parliamentary statute |
| **Research and Analysis Wing** | Executive external-intelligence agency under Cabinet Secretariat | Secretary (Research) appointed by government | External intelligence | Not created by an Act of Parliament |
| **National Intelligence Grid** | Executive/attached-office framework under MHA | CEO and technical/administrative structure appointed by government | Secure federated access to authorised databases for counter-terror investigations | It links data access; it is not itself a general arrest/investigation agency |
| **Multi Agency Centre** | Executive intelligence-coordination platform under IB | Member agencies share intelligence through central/subsidiary MACs | Counter-terror intelligence fusion | Coordination mechanism, not statutory regulator |
| **National Security Council** | Executive, established 1998 | PM chair; ministers and senior officials; serviced by NSCS and NSA | Strategic security coordination | Not a constitutional council |
| **National Security Adviser** | Executive office | Appointed by government; tenure at pleasure/appointment order | Advises PM and coordinates national-security structures | Not a constitutional office |
| **Strategic Policy Group** | Executive part of NSC system | Senior civil, military and intelligence leadership under notified chair | Strategic policy coordination | Composition can change by executive order |
| **Chief of Defence Staff** | Statutory service-office framework plus executive rules | Four-star officer appointed by Central Government; age/service rules apply | Principal military adviser, permanent chair of Chiefs of Staff Committee and head of DMA | Not operational commander of all theatre forces merely by office |
| **Department of Military Affairs** | Executive department in Ministry of Defence | Headed by CDS as Secretary with military/civil staff | Jointness, service matters, procurement categories and reforms | Separate from Department of Defence |
| **Defence Research and Development Organisation** | Executive department/organisation under Defence Ministry | Secretary DDR&D and DRDO chair appointed by government | Defence R&D laboratories and technology development | Not a statutory regulator |
| **Border Security Force, CRPF, CISF, ITBP and SSB** | Statutory central armed police forces under respective Acts | Directors-General appointed by Central Government; service tenures/rules | Border guarding, internal security and specialised protection roles | They are CAPFs under MHA, not the armed forces under Defence Ministry |
| **National Disaster Response Force** | Statutory force, DM Act 2005 | Battalions from CAPFs under Director-General appointed by Central Government | Specialised disaster response and capacity support | NDRF force is distinct from National Disaster Response Fund |

## 5. Fiscal, monetary and financial regulators

| Institution | Status / basis | Composition, appointment and tenure | Core powers | Accountability / trap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reserve Bank of India** | Statutory central bank, RBI Act 1934 | Central Board; Governor and up to four Deputy Governors appointed by Central Government, terms up to five years and reappointment possible | Monetary policy, currency, banking/NBFC/payment regulation and reserves | Operational autonomy exists within statute and government framework |
| **Monetary Policy Committee** | Statutory, RBI Act | RBI Governor, Deputy Governor, one RBI officer and three Central nominees; external members four years, no reappointment | Sets policy repo rate for inflation target; majority vote with Governor casting vote | RBI Central Board does not vote on repo rate |
| **Securities and Exchange Board of India** | Statutory, SEBI Act 1992 | Chair, Finance/RBI nominees and other members appointed by Central Government; tenure under rules | Securities-market regulation, intermediaries, investor protection and enforcement | Quasi-legislative, executive and quasi-judicial functions remain reviewable |
| **Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India** | Statutory, IRDA Act 1999 | Chair, up to five whole-time and four part-time members appointed by Central Government; terms/age under Act | Licenses and regulates insurers/intermediaries and develops insurance market | Insurance Act and IRDA Act operate together |
| **Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority** | Statutory, PFRDA Act 2013 | Chair plus up to six members, at least three whole-time; Central appointment; five-year term with age limits | Regulates NPS and pension intermediaries | Does not administer every government pension scheme |

| **Competition Commission of India** | Statutory, Competition Act 2002 | Chair and two to six members appointed by Central Government on selection process; five years or age 65 | Antitrust enforcement, combinations and market studies | Dominance is not illegal; abuse is |
| **National Financial Reporting Authority** | Statutory, Companies Act 2013 | Chair plus prescribed full/part-time members appointed by Central Government | Accounting/auditing standards advice, inspections and auditor discipline for covered entities | ICAI continues professional role outside/alongside NFRA jurisdiction |

| **Export-Import Bank of India** | Statutory corporation, EXIM Bank Act 1981 | Board headed by government-appointed managing director/chair structure | Export finance, overseas investment and lines of credit support | Commercial/development finance institution, not export regulator |

| **GST Network** | Government-controlled not-for-profit company | Union and States collectively own; professional board/management | Common GST technology platform | Not the constitutional GST Council and not the tax adjudicator |
| **GST Appellate Tribunal** | Statutory tribunal under GST laws | Principal Bench and State Benches with judicial and technical members selected under statute | Second appellate forum for GST disputes | Appeals from adjudicating/appellate authorities; constitutional review remains with courts |
| **Appellate Tribunal under PMLA/SAFEMA framework** | Statutory tribunal | Chair and members appointed under tribunal service rules | Appeals against attachment/adjudication orders under specified laws | Distinct from criminal trial by Special Court |

## Verification hierarchy

1. Constitution or parent Act for status, composition and powers.
2. Current rules/Gazette notification for tenure and service conditions.
3. Official body website for present office-holders and organisational changes.
4. Supreme Court judgments for independence, appointment and jurisdictional limits.
5. Never infer statutory status merely from the words "authority", "commission" or "national".
