## Applicability at a glance

- **Direct official ownership:** **Prelims GS-I** and **Mains Paper II (GS-I)**.
- **Support only:** selected **GS-III** resource and disaster questions where geographic process/background is required.
- **Not claimed here:** institutional disaster-management doctrine, environmental-regulatory design, and macroeconomic policy logic remain owned elsewhere.

### Topic 01 geophysical-phenomena provenance

Topic 01's ownership in the geophysical-phenomena row is deliberately narrow:
**solar-terrestrial coupling, auroras and associated space-weather consequences**.
It is proved by the official 2024 GS-I paper preserved at
`books\mains\UPSC Mains 2024 GS Paper I.pdf`, Question 15:
“What are aurora australis and aurora borealis? How are these triggered?”
The route is independently recorded in
`upsc-ai-kit\knowledge\_PYQ-ROUTING-MAINS-GS1-GS2-ESSAY-2024-2025.md`.
Earthquakes, tsunami, volcanism and cyclones remain with their listed owners;
this addition does not broaden Topic 01 into general hazard ownership.

### Topic 02 structural-geography provenance

Topic 02 directly supports the Mains clause through rock genesis, continental drift,
sea-floor spreading, plate boundaries, isostasy and changing continents/ocean basins.
Its India-applied companion supports the Prelims physical-geography clause through
cratons, mobile belts, sedimentary basins, Deccan volcanics, Himalayan/foreland
structure and Peninsular hill-range placement.

Verified direct demands include:

- `books\more_previous_papers\QP-CSM-22-GENERAL-STUDIES-PAPER I-190922.pdf`,
  GS-I Q4, characteristics and types of primary rocks;
- `books\mains\UPSC Mains 2025 GS Paper 1.pdf`, GS-I Q16, changes in continents
  and ocean basins due to tectonic movement;
- `books\more_previous_papers\QP_CS_Pre_Exam_2023_280523.pdf`, Prelims Q9,
  Amarkantak, Biligirirangan and Seshachalam;
- `books\prelima_question_paper_answers\2026-GS1-Set A.pdf`, provisional
  Prelims Q34, Peninsular-Block features.

The older Archaean/Purana/Dravidian/Aryan ladder is historiographic evidence, not the
controlling modern time scale. Current ownership follows named geological provinces
and the ICS/GSI chronology recorded in the paired Topic-02 owners.

## Ownership boundary

This folder is the direct owner for the printed Prelims and GS-I geography clauses. Its honest GS-III value is supportive: hazards, spatial distribution, and locational logic. It does **not** replace dedicated disaster-management, governance, or environmental-regulation coverage.
