## 2. Executive verdict

**Status: ✅ Complete — after three repair passes, two of which overturned a prior certification.**

The folder began the audit looking strong: exact Core/Advanced pairing, an explicit source-tier
lock, a ✅/⚠️/📰 tagging discipline, a substantial revision chart, an honest syllabus map that
refuses to claim GS-III disaster-management ownership, and dated current anchors. **All of that was
true and none of it made the folder answer-worthy.**

Three findings decided the verdict.

**F1 — A folder-wide systemic gap.** **None of the 37 Core files contained any answer
architecture.** Criteria 2 (direct thesis), 3 (logical structure), 4 (evidence-led analysis) and
9 (reasoned conclusion) scored **0 in every single Core file**. What each file called `Mains angles`
was a one- or two-line topic prompt — for example `basic/23`'s complete Mains provision was *"Why
the taiga is confined to the Northern Hemisphere"* and *"Boreal forests and permafrost as a carbon
sink turning into a source"*. A prompt is not a spine, a thesis, an evidence unit or a conclusion.

**F2 — Twelve demand families were absent from Core altogether, several of them routed.** These were
found by searching for **absences**, not by reading headings. The most serious:

| Absent family | Evidence of the gap |
|---|---|
| **Plate tectonics** | `basic/02` is the routed owner of the 2025 GS-I 15-mark demand *change in continents and ocean basins due to crustal tectonics* and of the 2025 Prelims demand *evidences of continental drift*, and taught **only petrology**. `sea-floor spreading`, `isostasy`, `geosyncline` and `orogen` returned **zero** occurrences across all 74 Core and Advanced files |
| **Tropical cyclones** | The syllabus names *cyclone* verbatim. Core contained one table cell. `cyclogenesis` = 0 hits |
| **Tornadoes** | `tornado` = **0 occurrences** in the entire folder, while `basic/13` listed *"Twisters and their concentration around the Gulf of Mexico"* (2024 GS-I, 15 marks) as a demand it "must now support" |
| **Cloudbursts** | Appeared only inside `basic/13`'s PYQ table (2024 GS-I, 10 marks) and once incidentally in `basic/18` |
| **Auroras** | Appeared **only** in `basic/01`'s PYQ table (2024 GS-I, 15 marks). No magnetosphere, solar wind or geomagnetic-storm content anywhere |
| **ENSO / Walker / La Niña / IOD** | `La Niña` and `Walker circulation` = **0 hits in Core**; concentrated in `advanced/12` and `advanced/16` — paper-essential world climatology trapped in the optional tier |
| **Soil geography** | **No file in the folder taught soil formation or any soil type**, although `basic/04` is routed *black cotton soil formation from volcanic rock*, `basic/30` used "chernozem", "black soil" and "alluvial" as evidence **without defining them**, and `basic/15` is routed a demand on rainforest soil nutrient status |
| **Regional disparity vs diversity** | The exact distinction demanded by a 2024 GS-I 15-marker existed **nowhere in the folder**. `advanced/29` instructed the reader to *"Route the definition/distinction here"* and then did not supply it |
| **Non-farm primary activities** | `basic/30` answered the routed 2025 GS-I 10-marker with a pointer to `../README.md` instead of content |
| **Mantle plumes / hot spots** | Routed 10-mark demand with no teaching content |
| **Glacier mass balance and GLOF** | No accumulation/ablation logic, no ice-mass typology, no outburst-flood chain |
| **Lake shrinkage** | `basic/09` is routed a demand on human-induced lake shrinkage and contained no Aral Sea or Lake Chad material |

**F3 — the first pass certified itself, and a hostile independent stress test overturned it.**
After F1 and F2 were repaired, a 30-question adversarial test was run **on the repaired folder by an
independent reviewer instructed to write the questions before reading the files**. It returned
**18 PASS / 7 PARTIAL / 5 FAIL** and exposed a category the first pass had entirely missed: the
folder had **no Core owner for the physical geography of India**. `Godavari` and `Cauvery` returned
**zero** hits in Core; `Northern Plain` returned three. India's physiographic divisions and drainage
systems — a **direct-owner** syllabus clause, *"Indian and World Geography — Physical … Geography of
India"* — had been deferred wholesale to the optional Advanced tier. A second corrective pass
repaired this and the seven PARTIALs.

**F4 — the second pass was also rejected, and a third pass was required.** A further hostile
review of the twice-repaired folder returned **12 PASS / 5 PARTIAL / 0 FAIL** and explicitly
recommended a *qualified* certification, on the ground that three comparative and critical demands
were supported on one side only: the delta comparison had a method but no second delta, the
regional comparison had a method but no second region, and the Northern Plains could not withstand a
`Critically examine` directive challenging their uniformity. Those three, plus the two
delegation-related PARTIALs, were repaired in a third pass rather than certified around.

**Additionally, the README was materially wrong.** Its "Direct 2024-2025 Geography Mains routing"
table **conflicted with the authoritative ledger on four rows** (groundwater, tsunami, solar,
seawater intrusion) and **omitted eight of the eleven demands the ledger actually routes into this
folder**, including every 2024 question. It has been rebuilt against the ledger.

**After three repair passes:** all 37 Core files carry topic-specific answer architecture; the
twelve absent demand families, the India physiography/drainage gap, and all twelve stress-test
FAILs and PARTIALs are closed in Core. A final independent verification, instructed to detect
superficial patching and fabricated figures, returned **5 PASS / 0 PARTIAL / 0 FAIL** on the last
five contested questions and found **no factual-discipline violation**. Cumulative hostile-test
position: **17 PASS / 0 PARTIAL / 0 FAIL**. Core leakage after repair: none paper-essential.

were read, specifically to attack absences rather than confirm headings, and answer skeletons were
attempted **using Core only** — Advanced was excluded as a source, because Core must stand alone.

- **Test A (during audit, pre-repair diagnosis):** absence-hunting probes across 37 demand terms.
- **Test B (post-repair, independent reviewer):** 30 questions across ten demand families
  (a)–(j) required by the standard, including at least eight on topics the reviewer did *not*
  expect a Geography folder to have prepared for, mixed across 10-, 15- and 20-mark demands.
- **Test C (after the second corrective pass):** re-run of Test B plus five new questions written
  against the second pass's own repairs.
