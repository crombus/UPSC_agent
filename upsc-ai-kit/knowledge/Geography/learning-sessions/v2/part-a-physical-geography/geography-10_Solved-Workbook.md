#### ➕ Storm Surge (coastal-hazard PYQ gap)

> ✅ Grounded in D.R. Khullar + ⚠️ standard geography. Added to close a UPSC gap.

- ✅ D.R. Khullar lists cyclone dangers as gales/strong winds, torrential rain and high tidal waves, also called **storm surges**.
- ✅ Khullar notes most casualties from cyclones are caused by coastal inundation by tidal waves/storm surges, with severe surges penetrating inland from the coast.
- ⚠️ Mechanism: low pressure raises sea level slightly, but the main driver is strong onshore cyclone winds piling seawater against a shallow coast.
- ⚠️ India link: the shallow, funnel-shaped Bay of Bengal and deltaic east coast make Odisha-West Bengal-Bangladesh especially surge-prone.

| Factor | Surge effect | India link |
|---|---|---|
| ✅/⚠️ Strong onshore winds | Piles water at coast | Cyclone landfall side |

| ✅ Deltaic lowlands | More inundation | East-coast deltas |

> 🔑 Trap: Storm surge is **not the same as heavy rainfall flooding**; it is abnormal sea-water inundation pushed ashore by a cyclone.
