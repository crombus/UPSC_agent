# MAJOR ECONOMY COMMITTEES, COMMISSIONS AND TASK FORCES

> **Purpose:** rapid UPSC revision of the panel, its assigned task, the reform idea associated
> with it, and the topic where it should be used. A committee recommendation is advisory:
> never describe it as enacted policy unless a later law, rule or programme implemented it.
>
> **Year convention:** the year shown is normally the constitution or report period. Where a
> panel ran across years, both are shown. Verify the original report before quoting a numerical
> target because later policy may differ.

## 1. Priority map

## 11. Answer-use template

```text
Committee -> assigned problem -> one reform principle -> present policy link -> limitation
```

Example:

> The N. K. Singh FRBM Review Committee shifted fiscal debate toward a debt anchor,
> operational deficit glide paths and escape clauses. It is best used as a reform framework,
> not as proof that every recommendation was enacted unchanged.

## 12. Source and update discipline

- RBI committee and working-group reports control monetary, banking, payments and forex entries.
- Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs and Department of Revenue reports control
  fiscal, tax, disinvestment and financial-sector entries.
- NITI Aayog and former Planning Commission reports control poverty and development-method entries.
- Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Department of Food and Public Distribution and
  NABARD records control agriculture, food-management and rural-credit entries.
- SEBI, Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Ministry of Railways and infrastructure ministries control
  their sectoral panels.
- Committee names and historical tasks are static; implementation status, legal provisions,
  numerical targets and institutional names may change and require current official verification.
