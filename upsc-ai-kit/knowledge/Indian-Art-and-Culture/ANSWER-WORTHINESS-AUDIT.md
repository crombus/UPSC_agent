### 2.1 What the first pass had found and fixed (unchanged, and still true)

The folder had strong syllabus ownership, honest source tagging, an
unusually disciplined current-data protocol and 15 exact Core/Advanced
pairs — but **no Core file carried any answer architecture**. Each
`basic/NN` ended with a five-step "Mains framework" that was a *procedure*
("name the school", "use precise vocabulary") rather than an *answer
engine*: there were no directive maps, no thesis options, no mark-scaled
structures, no demand-family evidence banks, no verdict scaffolds and no
per-topic factual-risk controls. On the standard's scoring, criteria
**2 (direct thesis)** and **9 (reasoned conclusion)** therefore scored
**0 or 1 in all fifteen topics** before repair, and criterion **5
(adequate examples)** failed by *allocation*: a file could contain many
names in total while holding two or fewer selectable examples for a
specific 15- or 20-mark demand.

**Three structural defects were also found and repaired in the first pass:**

1. **Paper-essential material trapped in Advanced.** The README explicitly
   stated that Advanced held "a Mains-ready answer architecture" and that
   `advanced/03` held the "complete Chandella case dossier". That conflicts
   with the rule that Core must be answer-complete. Forty-plus promotable
   items were identified across all 15 Advanced files (§9), and every one
   that could affect a paper outcome has been written into Core.
2. **Core files with unanswerable direct demands.** `basic/03` carried the
   Pallava and Chola PYQs with a four-line and a six-line hint respectively
   — no phased development, no named monument set, no bronze/painting/
   inscription evidence, no limitations. `basic/15` (cinema) had **no
   history at all**, an **empty `## 7. PYQ application` heading**, and an
   Advanced companion that is entirely source-ungrounded at page level, so
   the topic had no reliable factual owner anywhere in the folder.
3. **Coverage gaps inside otherwise strong files.** Core named **no folk
   music form** (topic 08), **no modern theatre figure, institution or
   statute** (topic 10), only two modern painters (topic 07), and no Urdu
   or Hindi literary stream (topic 11) — all four are explicit syllabus
   sub-areas.
