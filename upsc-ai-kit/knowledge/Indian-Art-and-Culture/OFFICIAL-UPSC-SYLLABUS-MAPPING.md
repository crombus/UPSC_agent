## Applicability at a glance

- **Direct official ownership:** **Prelims GS-I** (through the umbrella history clause) and **Mains Paper II (GS-I)**.
- **Ownership tier rule:** **Core (`basic/`) is the owner of every official clause in this folder, and Basic is answer-complete.** Each `basic/NN` file carries an `Answer architecture (10/15/20-mark support)` section and is written to be answer-complete on its own. `advanced/` is **optional enrichment only** — historiography, micro-detail and extended argument. ❌ **No official clause is jointly owned by Core and Advanced, and no clause requires an Advanced file to be answerable.** Skipping `advanced/` cannot cost marks.
- **Support only:** chronology and political narrative are supplied by the History folders where needed.
- **Not claimed here:** detailed dynastic/political history remains owned by the History folders.
