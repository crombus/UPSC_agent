## Applicability at a glance

- **Direct official ownership:** **Prelims GS-I** (through the umbrella history clause) and **Mains Paper II (GS-I)**.
- **Support only:** chronology and political narrative are supplied by the History folders where needed.
- **Not claimed here:** detailed dynastic/political history remains owned by the History folders.

## Official clauses quoted verbatim from the root source

> - "History of India and Indian National Movement."
> - "Indian culture will cover the salient aspects of Art Forms, literature and Architecture from ancient to modern times."
