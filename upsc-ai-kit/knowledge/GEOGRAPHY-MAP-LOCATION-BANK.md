# Geography Map-Location Bank

> **Purpose:** rapid Prelims map elimination and named-location support for GS-I, GS-II and
> GS-III answers. This is the canonical cross-syllabus owner for location recall; subject files
> retain process, policy and narrative depth.
>
> **Currency:** stable physical-location facts checked to **14 September 2026**. The final
> current-affairs watchlist is date-bounded and must be refreshed before a later exam cycle.

## How to use this bank

1. Locate the feature by **neighbours**, not by an isolated dot.
2. Attach one **physical relation** and one **strategic, ecological or economic use**.
3. Revise close pairs together: strait versus canal, source versus mouth, pass versus tunnel,
   reserve versus biosphere reserve, mineral belt versus producing State.
4. On a blank map, mark only the bold canonical entries. Backticks in summary tables are
   cross-references, not additional owners.
