# MAJOR ETHICS, INTEGRITY AND ANTI-CORRUPTION COMMITTEES

> **Use:** cite the panel only after stating the ethical principle or governance failure.
> Recommendations remain advisory unless incorporated into law or institutional practice.

## Core integrity panels

| Priority | Panel/report and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **Committee on Prevention of Corruption - K. Santhanam** | 1962-64 | Examine corruption and improve vigilance administration | Recommended an independent Central Vigilance Commission, preventive vigilance and conduct/disciplinary reform |
| A | **First Administrative Reforms Commission** | 1966-70 | Reform public administration and grievance/integrity institutions | Proposed Lokpal and Lokayuktas; detailed administration is owned by Governance |
| A | **Second ARC Fourth Report - Ethics in Governance** | 2007 | Examine corruption, standards in public life and institutional integrity | Ethics codes, conflict-of-interest controls, Lokpal, whistle-blower protection, citizen charters and process reform |
| A | **H. D. Shourie Working Group** | 1997 | Prepare a right-to-information framework | Linked openness and accountable government to a statutory information right |
| A | **Law Commission Report 179 - Justice B. P. Jeevan Reddy** | 2001 | Design public-interest disclosure and informer protection | Confidential disclosures, competent authorities and protection against victimisation |
| B | **Law Commission Report 166 - Justice B. P. Jeevan Reddy** | 1999 | Design forfeiture law for illicit assets of corrupt public servants | Proposed special machinery to confiscate illegally acquired property |
| A | **Hota Committee on Civil Service Reforms** | 2004 | Improve civil-service performance and accountability | Civil-service code, tenure, appraisal, disciplinary and professionalisation reforms |
| A | **Vohra Committee - N. N. Vohra** | 1993 | Examine links among criminals, politicians, officials and enforcement agencies | Demonstrated systemic integrity risks; detailed security treatment is owned by Internal Security |

## Comparative public-ethics framework

| Priority | Panel | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **UK Committee on Standards in Public Life / Nolan Committee - Lord Nolan** | 1994-95 | Recommend standards of conduct in public life | Seven principles: selflessness, integrity, objectivity, accountability, openness, honesty and leadership |

## High-yield distinctions

| Do not confuse | Correct distinction |
|---|---|
| Santhanam Committee and CVC Act | Santhanam led to the executive CVC in 1964; statutory status came through the CVC Act, 2003 |
| First ARC and Second ARC | First ARC proposed Lokpal/Lokayuktas; Second ARC developed a wider ethics-and-governance framework |
| RTI and whistle-blowing | RTI enables public access to records; whistle-blower protection safeguards insiders reporting wrongdoing |
| Code of ethics and code of conduct | Ethics states guiding values; conduct supplies enforceable behavioural rules |
| Nolan principles and GS-IV values | Nolan's seven principles overlap with, but are not identical to, the UPSC values list |

## Answer-use template

```text
ethical failure -> public value breached -> committee principle -> legal/institutional status
-> implementation gap -> proportionate reform
```
