# UPSC Mains GS-IV 2026 — Section-B Case-Study Routing Supplement

> ## Scope and honesty rules
>
> **Subpart-level routing supplement for the 2026 GS-IV Section B (Q7-Q12).** It is a companion to,
> not a replacement for, the whole-paper routing ledgers in `../_PYQ-ROUTING-MAINS-GS3-GS4-*.md`,
> which route questions at question level. Section B needs finer resolution because **every 2026
> case carried two subparts with different demands**, and a candidate who answers (b) by restating
> (a) forfeits roughly half the available credit.
>
> **A row is a pointer, not an answer key.** The worked answers live in the owning modules;
> this file records *what each subpart asks*, *who owns the substance*, and *where the boundary
> between owners falls*.
>
> **Wording provenance.** Every phrase in quotation marks below is the printed English wording read
> from the local official-paper OCR text (`QP-CSM-26-010926-GENERAL-STUDIES-PAPER-IV`). Nothing has
> been reconstructed from memory, coaching material or aggregators. Where OCR could not be read with
> confidence, the wording is left out rather than guessed.
>
> **Twelve subparts, each routed exactly once.** Q7(a) through Q12(b). No subpart appears in two
> rows; no subpart is omitted.
>
> ✅ = paper-verified or source-grounded | ⚠️ = analytical inference | 📰 = current anchor.

## 5. Current-affairs discipline for this bank

- ⚠️ **All six 2026 cases are fully decidable from static ethics plus settled law.** None requires a
  current-affairs item to reach a defensible decision. Treat 📰 material strictly as **optional
  enrichment or a legal-status caveat**, never as the anchor.
- ✅ Where a 📰 item legitimately belongs, it is as a **status caution** — for example, the pending
  status of the Section 17A question recorded at [`basic/19_Corruption-Legal-Framework.md`](basic/19_Corruption-Legal-Framework.md)
  Sec. 2 and Sec. 5, which a Q9 answer should reflect rather than assert a settled outcome.
- ⚠️ **Q7 requires no current affairs whatsoever.** It is decided entirely on the consent-validity
  test and *Samira Kohli*.
- ⚠️ Two standing cautions carry into every module in this bank: (i) never let a 📰 item substitute
  for the static anchor, and in particular never treat non-statutory guidelines as enacted law;
  (ii) re-verify renumbered statutory provisions (BNS/BNSS, and the medical-regulation framework)
  before citing section numbers in a live answer.
