## How this folder works

- **Two tiers per topic:** `basic/NN` builds the must-do concept, definitions and usable
  examples; `advanced/NN` adds philosophical/administrative debates, legal nuance, institutional
  constraints, case analysis and answer-writing depth. **Same number = same topic** in both tiers.
- **Tagging discipline:** ✅ = directly grounded in a cited source (ARC Report, statute, official
  body, verified PYQ); ⚠️ = analytical inference, standard linkage or illustrative framing;
  📰 = a dated, verifiable current-affairs or status anchor.
- **Dated-source discipline:** The ARC's 4th Report is a **2007 document**. Its data, case
  vintage (e.g., conviction-rate tables) and *recommendations* (e.g., Lokpal, whistleblower law,
  collusive-bribery offence) are presented as **dated source material**, clearly separated from
  **current enacted-law status verified as of 4 August 2026**. Distinguish four different things
  whenever a statute is cited: **enactment** (assent), **rules** made under it, **commencement**
  (being brought into force) and **operation** (whether the machinery actually functions). Live
  examples used throughout this folder:
  - Prevention of Corruption (Amendment) Act, 2018 — assent **26 July 2018**, brought into force
    the same day (S.O. 3662(E)); enacted *and* operative.
  - Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013 — assent 1 January 2014, in force **16 January 2014**, but the
    first Chairperson took office only in **March 2019**: enactment ≠ operation.
  - Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014 — assent **9 May 2014**, but **no commencement
    notification traced as of 4 August 2026**: enacted but *not in force*; the CVC's non-statutory
    PIDPI Resolution (21 April 2004) remains the operative mechanism.
  - DSPE Act s.6A "single directive" — enacted through the CVC Act, 2003 but **struck down** in
    *Subramanian Swamy v. Director, CBI* (2014): enacted then judicially voided.
  - Prohibition of Benami Property Transactions Act, 1988 (as amended 2016, in force 1 November
    2016) — *Union of India v. Ganpati Dealcom* (23 August 2022) was **recalled in full** by the
    Supreme Court on **18 October 2024** and the appeal restored, so the retrospectivity question
    is open, not settled.
  - RTI Act, 2005 s.8(1)(j) — **substituted** by s.44(3) of the Digital Personal Data Protection
    Act, 2023, that substitution being in force from **13 November 2025**.
- **No duplication of constitutional-body detail:** composition, appointment and removal
  procedure of CVC, CBI, Lokpal/Lokayukta, CIC/SIC and the Public Service Commissions are taught
  in `Polity/advanced/37_CVC-and-CBI.md`, `Polity/advanced/38_Lokpal-and-Lokayuktas.md`,
  `Polity/advanced/36_CIC-and-SIC.md` and `Polity/advanced/41_Public-Services.md`. This folder links to
  them and teaches only the **ethics-specific** angle: jurisdiction debates, ethical dilemmas,
  probity and dilemma analysis.
- **No duplication of optional-depth philosophy:** classical positions of Aristotle, Kant, Mill,
  the Buddha and Indian darshanas are taught in depth in `Philosophy/paper-1/western/` and
  `Philosophy/paper-1/indian/`. This folder links to them and teaches only what GS-IV actually
  tests — usable definitions, thinker-to-administration translation and PYQ-quotation analysis.

## Study sequence

1. **Foundations (01-05):** what ethics is, how values/attitude/aptitude/EQ shape conduct.
2. **Thought traditions (06-08):** Indian and Western thinkers, then applied moral theories.
3. **Public-service core (09-12):** values, sources of guidance, accountability, corporate/global ethics.
4. **Contemporary and administrative machinery (13-18):** emerging ethics, probity, transparency,
   codes, service delivery, public-money integrity.
5. **Anti-corruption architecture (19-21):** law, institutions, protection of the honest.
6. **Application (22-23):** case-study architecture, then named/comparative cases for the Mains
   answer bank.

## Source and accuracy notes

- ✅ Primary anchor: 2nd ARC, 4th Report, *Ethics in Governance* (2007) — read directly from the
  local PDF (`books/ethics4.pdf`), with paragraphs cited by number (e.g. `ARC 3.2.2`), boxes by
  box number (e.g. `Box 2.8`) and colloquium material by annexure (e.g. `Annexure-I(2)`).
- ✅ Recent complete PYQ anchors: UPSC GS-IV Mains 2024 and 2025 papers, and the 2024/2025 Essay
  papers, read directly from `books/mains/`. **Section A of GS-IV is Q1-Q6; Section B (case
  studies) is Q7-Q12 in both papers** — case studies are cited by their real paper numbers.
- ⚠️ Older direct questions are quoted only where verified against the official previous-paper
  record on upsc.gov.in; no PYQ number or year is invented.
- ⚠️ **Quotation discipline:** a UPSC paper proves that UPSC printed an attribution, not that the
  attribution is authentic. Where a set quotation cannot be traced to a primary source (e.g. the
  Sardar Patel and William James lines in the 2024/2025 papers, and the Vivekananda "strength of a
  society" line in 2025), the file says so explicitly and answers the *idea* rather than asserting
  the provenance.
- 📰 Current-law status (PC Act 2018, Lokpal, Whistle Blowers Protection Act, Benami, RTI/DPDP,
  Companies Act s.135, Mission Karmayogi/NPCSCB, India AI Governance Guidelines) verified against
  first-party sources (India Code, Supreme Court, DoPT/CVC/Lokpal, MCA, MeitY, UNODC/UN Treaty
  Collection) and date-stamped in each relevant file.
- ⚠️ Not usable as a source: the local `books/Ethics,_Integrity_&_Aptitude_S_Ansari_Sir...pdf` is a
  421-page scan of *handwritten* class notes with no text layer; OCR of every page yields
  unreliable text, so nothing in this folder is sourced to it.
