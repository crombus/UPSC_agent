# MAJOR SOCIAL JUSTICE COMMITTEES, COMMISSIONS AND REPORTS

> **Use:** identify the social-sector problem, attach the correct panel and then state whether
> its recommendation became policy. Poverty-line committees remain owned by Economy and are
> cross-linked rather than duplicated here.

## Education

| Priority | Panel and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **University Education Commission - S. Radhakrishnan** | 1948-49 | Review university education after Independence | University autonomy, general education and the institutional route to UGC |
| A | **Secondary Education Commission - A. Lakshmanaswami Mudaliar** | 1952-53 | Reorganise secondary education | Diversified curriculum, multipurpose schools, guidance and vocational education |
| A | **Education Commission - D. S. Kothari** | 1964-66 | Review the entire education system | 10+2+3 structure, Common School System, three-language formula and the 6% spending benchmark |
| A | **Yash Pal Committee - Learning Without Burden** | 1992-93 | Examine curricular overload and joyless learning | Child-centred pedagogy, understanding over rote learning and reduced textbook burden |
| A | **National Knowledge Commission - Sam Pitroda** | 2005-09 | Build institutions for a knowledge economy | Expansion, excellence, access and regulatory reform in higher education and knowledge systems |
| A | **Yash Pal Committee on Renovation and Rejuvenation of Higher Education** | 2008-09 | Review fragmentation and standards in higher education | Multidisciplinary universities and an overarching higher-education regulator |
| A | **T. S. R. Subramanian Committee for Evolution of New Education Policy** | 2015-16 | Recommend inputs for a new education policy | Learning outcomes, teacher accountability, governance, digital and vocational reform |
| A | **K. Kasturirangan Committee for Draft National Education Policy** | 2017-19 | Draft a comprehensive education policy | 5+3+3+4 structure, foundational literacy and multidisciplinary higher education |

## Public health

| Priority | Panel and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **Health Survey and Development Committee - Joseph Bhore** | 1943-46 | Survey health conditions and design a national health service | Integrated preventive-curative care and a three-tier system centred on primary health centres |
| A | **Health Survey and Planning Committee - A. Lakshmanaswami Mudaliar** | 1959-62 | Review implementation of the Bhore model | Consolidation of existing PHCs, stronger district hospitals and specialist services |
| B | **Mukherjee Committee** | 1963 | Review family-planning and health-field arrangements | Separate staff for family planning and clearer field responsibility |
| A | **Kartar Singh Committee** | 1972-73 | Rationalise multiple single-purpose health workers | Multipurpose male/female health workers and the PHC-sub-centre staffing structure |
| A | **Srivastava Committee** | 1974-75 | Align medical education and health manpower with rural needs | Community health workers, referral-service complexes and reoriented medical education |
| B | **Bajaj Committee on Health Manpower** | 1985-87 | Develop coordinated health-manpower planning | Planning councils, continuing education and linkage between training and service requirements |
| A | **High Level Expert Group on Universal Health Coverage - K. Srinath Reddy** | 2010-11 | Prepare a framework for universal health coverage | Tax-financed essential services, stronger primary care, free essential medicines and higher public spending |

## Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes

| Priority | Panel and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **First Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes Commission - U. N. Dhebar** | 1960-61 | Review Scheduled Area administration and tribal welfare under Article 339 | Land/forest protection, indebtedness reform and culturally sensitive development |
| A | **Lokur Committee - B. N. Lokur** | 1965 | Review the lists and identification criteria for SCs and STs | Influential classification criteria, some of whose terminology is now considered outdated |
| B | **Elayaperumal Committee** | 1965-69 | Examine untouchability and SC economic/educational conditions | Documented continuing discrimination and called for stronger enforcement and development support |
| A | **Bhuria Committee on Panchayati Raj in Scheduled Areas - Dilip Singh Bhuria** | 1994-95 | Extend self-government to Scheduled Areas | Gram Sabha-centred tribal self-rule and the main policy foundation of PESA, 1996 |
| A | **Second Scheduled Areas and Scheduled Tribes Commission - Dilip Singh Bhuria** | 2002-04 | Review safeguards and tribal development since the Dhebar Commission | Land alienation, forest rights, self-governance and implementation of PESA/tribal planning |
| A | **High Level Committee on Tribal Communities - Virginius Xaxa** | 2013-14 | Assess socio-economic, health and educational status of tribal communities | Detailed displacement, land, forest, education and health diagnosis with Gram Sabha-centred reform |
| B | **K. G. Balakrishnan Commission** | 2022-present | Examine SC-status claims of converts to religions not covered by the Presidential Orders | Use only for the constitutional issue under Article 341; do not attribute findings before an official report |

## Other Backward Classes

| Priority | Panel and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **First Backward Classes Commission - Kaka Kalelkar** | 1953-55 | Identify socially and educationally backward classes under Article 340 | First all-India backward-class listing and educational/reservation recommendations |
| A | **Second Backward Classes Commission - B. P. Mandal** | 1979-80 | Identify SEBCs and recommend advancement measures | Eleven indicators and recommendation of 27% reservation for OBCs in Union services |
| B | **Ram Nandan Prasad Expert Committee** | 1993 | Operationalise exclusion of the creamy layer after *Indra Sawhney* | Administrative criteria for identifying socially advanced OBC sections |
| A | **Rohini Commission on OBC Sub-Categorisation - Justice G. Rohini** | 2017-23 | Examine unequal distribution of Central OBC reservation benefits | Analysed concentration of benefits and submitted a sub-categorisation framework |

## Minorities

| Priority | Panel and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **Sachar Committee - Justice Rajinder Sachar** | 2005-06 | Assess the social, economic and educational status of Indian Muslims | Evidence on education, employment, credit and infrastructure; proposed data and equal-opportunity reforms |
| A | **National Commission for Religious and Linguistic Minorities - Justice Ranganath Misra** | 2004-07 | Identify backward sections among minorities and recommend welfare/reservation measures | Minority-reservation proposals and recommendation to delink Scheduled Caste status from religion |

## Women, children, disability and social protection

| Priority | Panel and chair | Year | Assigned task | Major contribution |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| A | **Committee on the Status of Women in India - Phulrenu Guha** | 1971-74 | Assess women's constitutional, social, economic and political status | *Towards Equality* exposed structural inequality and shaped modern women's policy |
| A | **Justice Verma Committee** | 2012-13 | Recommend criminal-law and institutional reforms after the Delhi gang rape | Wider sexual-offence framework, police accountability and gender-sensitive institutional reform |
| A | **High Level Committee on the Status of Women - Pam Rajput** | 2013-15 | Review progress and remaining gender inequalities | Intersectional policy, representation, stronger institutions and gender-responsive budgeting |
| A | **Gurupadswamy Committee on Child Labour** | 1979 | Examine child labour and the feasibility of prohibition | Phased elimination beginning with hazardous work plus rehabilitation and poverty reduction |
| A | **Sudha Kaul Committee on Disability Legislation** | 2010-11 | Draft rights-based disability law aligned with UNCRPD | Shift from welfare to equality, accessibility and enforceable rights |
| B | **Central Vigilance Committee on PDS - Justice D. P. Wadhwa** | 2006-12 | Examine corruption, leakages and delivery failures in PDS | End-to-end computerisation, transparent records, vigilance and supply-chain accountability |

## High-yield distinctions

| Do not confuse | Correct distinction |
|---|---|
| Radhakrishnan, Mudaliar and Kothari | University education; secondary education; whole education system |
| Yash Pal 1993 and Yash Pal 2009 | School-learning burden; higher-education renovation |
| Bhore and Mudaliar health committees | Bhore designed the system; Mudaliar reviewed and consolidated it |
| Dhebar, Bhuria and Xaxa | Constitutional tribal administration; Scheduled-Area self-government; contemporary tribal status review |
| Kalelkar and Mandal | First and second Article 340 Backward Classes Commissions |
| Sachar and Ranganath Misra | Status diagnosis of Muslims; reservation/welfare proposals for religious and linguistic minorities |
| Phulrenu Guha and Pam Rajput | Foundational *Towards Equality* review; later high-level status review |
