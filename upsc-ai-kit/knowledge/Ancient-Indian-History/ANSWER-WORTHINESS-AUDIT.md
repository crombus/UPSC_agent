Ancient Indian History is now structurally much stronger for unfamiliar future questions than it was in the previous audit. The Core layer now supports all 27 topics with:
- qualified thesis options,
- question-appropriate answer structures,
- named evidence banks with significance and limitation,

The earlier architecture gap is therefore **closed at the section-design level across the whole subject**. The existing 9 stress tests still pass, and demand-thesis plus structure-analysis readiness is now uniformly at score 3 across all 27 topics.

audit was not credible. The first exposed Core leakage and missing answer
architecture; the second exposed two factual errors (`kshetra-patni` source and
Sanghabhuti/Sanghabhadra conflation). Both sets of blockers are now repaired.
