## Applicability at a glance

- **Direct official ownership:** **Prelims GS-I**.
- **Support only:** **Mains Paper II (GS-I)** only through the printed culture clause quoted below.

## Official clauses quoted verbatim from the root source

> - "Indian culture will cover the salient aspects of Art Forms, literature and Architecture from ancient to modern times."

## Ownership boundary

Ancient India is directly inside Preliminary history preparation, but this folder does **not** claim the printed GS-I Mains history clauses because those clauses explicitly name modern India, the freedom struggle, post-independence consolidation, and world history. Its honest Mains use is therefore culture-adjacent support, not primary ownership.
