## Applicability at a glance

- **Direct official ownership:** **Mains Paper II (GS-I)**.
- **Support only:** selected **Prelims GS-I** social-development coverage where the prelims line overlaps with poverty, inclusion, and demographics.
- **Not claimed here:** welfare-scheme design, legal doctrine, and macroeconomic measurement remain owned elsewhere.

## Official clauses quoted verbatim from the root source

> - "Salient features of Indian Society, Diversity of India."
> - "Role of women and women's organization, population and associated issues, poverty and developmental issues, urbanization, their problems and their remedies."
> - "Effects of globalization on Indian society."
> - "Social empowerment, communalism, regionalism and secularism."
> - "Economic and Social Development-Sustainable Development, Poverty, Inclusion, Demographics, Social Sector Initiatives, etc."

## Ownership boundary

This folder is the direct GS-I owner for society questions, but its prelims role is only overlapping support where the prelims social-development line touches demographics, poverty, or inclusion. It does **not** claim full ownership of welfare schemes, constitutional doctrine, or macroeconomic measurement.
