## 2. Executive verdict

The original Core had sound concepts, PYQ labels and factual cautions, but Topics 04-15
had no executable answer architecture. More importantly, several **demand families** were
missing rather than merely under-explained: population education; remote work, marriage and
digital child socialisation; gig work and safe suicide-analysis; COVID/class poverty; Tier-2
urban consumption/segregation; technology-resource society effects; post-liberal
communalism; cultural assertiveness; and tolerance/assimilation/pluralism.

All are now answerable from Core through topic-specific directive maps, thesis banks,
10/15/20-mark spines, evidence units, balance tests and verdict scaffolds. Advanced remains
optional enrichment. The one post-repair hostile-review FAIL—formal versus substantive
mobility in Topic 08—was repaired and the review was rerun to **30 PASS / 0 PARTIAL /
0 FAIL**.

## 8. Core gaps that could reduce marks

All paper-affecting gaps found were repaired in Core:

1. **Architecture gap:** Topics 04-15 lacked directive, thesis, mark scaling, evidence,
   balance and conclusion support.
2. **Historical-PYQ leakage:** older ledgers routed 2018-2023 demands to Advanced despite
   Core being presented as Must-Do.
3. **Absent demand families:** family technology; population education; women’s lower-strata,
   gig and suicide demands; pandemic poverty; Tier-2/segregation; globalisation-resource
   effects; crypto/social change; economic communalism; cultural regionalism; pluralism.
4. **Factual status gap:** stale “NFHS-5 is latest” language and an unsupported UCC Amendment
   Act assertion were unsafe.
5. **Hostile-review gap:** Topic 08 initially lacked an executable formal-mobility versus
   substantive-empowerment route.
