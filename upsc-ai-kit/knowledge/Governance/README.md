## How this folder works

- **Core is the answer layer; Advanced is optional.** `basic/NN` is **independently
  answer-complete**: it carries the concepts, the Indian applications, the evidence banks
  and a topic-specific `Answer architecture (10/15/20-mark support)` section sufficient for
  every routed 2018–2026 demand and for unfamiliar questions. `advanced/NN` adds optional
  institutional-design depth, boundary cases and further trade-off analysis. **No mark in
  any 10, 15 or 20-mark answer depends on opening an `advanced/` file.** The same number
  denotes the same topic; all 16 pairs match exactly.
- **Core routing supersedes older ledger pointers.** The central 2018–2023 Mains ledger
  routes **19** Governance demands to an `advanced/` file. All of them are now answered in
  the corresponding **Core** file's `§13.0 Direct Mains demands owned by this Core file`
  block. Where a ledger row still points at `advanced/`, the Core owner controls.
- **Tagging:** ✅ = directly grounded in an official source or verified PYQ;
  ⚠️ = analytical inference or standard conceptual linkage; 📰 = a dated current
  policy, institutional or legal anchor; ❌ = a prohibited or trap claim.
- **Source discipline:** ARC reports are dated reform documents. Their recommendations
  are not presented as current law unless separately verified. Thematic resemblance between
  a recommendation and a later programme is **not** evidence of implementation.
- **Legal-status discipline:** distinguish a proposal, enacted Act, commencement
  notification, notified rule, constituted body, appointed body and operational
  implementation. These stages are not interchangeable.
- **Dynamic facts:** rankings, office-holders, platform-user counts, DBT savings,
  grievance-disposal figures and scheme coverage change frequently. Always retain the
  reporting date and prefer the latest official dashboard or report.

## Boundaries with other subjects

- **Polity owns constitutional/statutory composition and appointment structure.**
  Governance still owns how CIC/RTI, CVC, Lokpal, CAG, local bodies, public services
  and pressure groups produce transparency, answerability, participation, audit and
  service-delivery outcomes. Cross-link; do not omit the mechanism merely because its
  legal anatomy is studied in Polity.
- **Ethics owns the probity and personal-conduct angle:** integrity, corruption,
  detailed RTI ethics, codes and the ethical design of Citizens' Charters. Governance
  studies administrative systems, statutory service guarantees and accountability
  mechanisms.
- **Social Justice owns vulnerable-group schemes, health, education, poverty and
  hunger.** Governance covers the cross-sector policy and implementation tools used
  to deliver and evaluate such interventions.

## Study sequence

1. **Foundation:** `01-03` — define governance, understand policy implementation
   and locate non-government development actors.
2. **Citizen-state interface:** `04-08` — civil society, e-governance, DPI,
   citizen-centric administration and accountability.
3. **Administrative capacity:** `09-11` — civil services, administrative reform and
   regulatory governance.
4. **Delivery and feedback:** `12-15` — local delivery, financial flows,
   participation and outcome evaluation.
5. **Adjacent institutional application:** `16` — sports federations,
   tournament rules, athlete rights, anti-doping and dispute resolution.

Earlier versions of `basic/06` and `basic/08` stated the opposite and are now corrected. Constitutional challenges to the amendment are filed; **no final judgment located** — assert no outcome. |
| CPGRAMS | DARPG's **Comprehensive Guidelines for Handling Public Grievances, August 2024** use a **21-day** redress benchmark with **30 days** for appeals. No later revision located. CPGRAMS remains **administrative, not adjudicatory**. |
| SNA fund flow | **SNA-SPARSH** is the current just-in-time CSS architecture, integrating PFMS, State IFMIS and RBI **e-Kuber**; distinguish it from the earlier bank-account-based SNA model. Introduced by Department of Expenditure **OM No. 1(27)/PFMS/2020 dated 13 July 2023**; a **10 June 2025** OM extended 37 further CSSs from **1 July 2025**, and from **1 November 2025** it applies to all CSSs in States/UTs with legislatures. ⚠️ Applicability by notification ≠ verified operational onboarding everywhere. |
| Whistle-blower statute | The **Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014** (assent 9 May 2014) **has still not been brought into force** by notification. The Whistle Blowers Protection (Amendment) Bill, 2015 was passed by the Lok Sabha in 2015 and **lapsed**. Statutory whistle-blower protection therefore remains **enacted but not commenced**. |
| Sports governance law | The **National Sports Governance Act, 2025 (Act No. 25 of 2025)** received assent on **18 August 2025** and is in phased commencement: select provisions from **1 January 2026**, with a second tranche from **12 May 2026** under **S.O. 2406(E)**. The **National Sports Board** and **National Sports Tribunal** are statutory creations, but uncommenced recognition/jurisdiction provisions must not be treated as operational. As of this cut-off, NSF recognition continues through the **National Sports Development Code of India, 2011** and statutory deeming continuity; no Tribunal case jurisdiction is asserted. **NADA** is statutory under the **National Anti-Doping Act, 2022**; **SAI remains a registered society**. See `basic/16` §6. |
| NeSDA | Latest completed national assessment located: **NeSDA 2021**. DARPG launched the **NeSDA 2025 assessment portal on 25 May 2026**; portal launch is not publication of completed results. The **56 identified mandatory e-services** tracked under NeSDA Way Forward is a dated implementation-set figure, not a parameter count. |
| Mission Karmayogi | The Capacity Building Commission remains functioning and iGOT Karmayogi remains the delivery platform. ❌ No learner, course, completion or MoU figure is asserted — all are continuously updated dashboard values. |
| AI governance | **India AI Governance Guidelines**, released by **MeitY on 5 November 2025** under the title *Enabling Safe and Trusted AI Innovation*. They are a **non-statutory, voluntary-compliance framework**, not an AI law. Core uses the seven-sutra principle set for explainability, human review, audit and bias testing in public services. |
