## 2. Executive verdict

**The README's claim was false when this audit opened, and is true now.**

Three findings, each independently sufficient to reject the pre-audit claim:

**B1 — Folder-wide systemic gap: no answer architecture anywhere.** **None** of the 16 Core
files carried any answer-architecture section. Files contained a "Mains angles" bullet list
and a single "Answer thesis" blockquote, which is not a demand map, not a mark-scaled
structure, not an evidence bank and not a verdict scaffold. Criteria **1 (demand fidelity)**,
**3 (logical structure)** and **9 (reasoned conclusion)** therefore scored **0 or 1 in every
topic**. Repaired: a topic-specific `Answer architecture (10/15/20-mark support)` section is
now written into **all 16** Core files.

**B2 — Ownership blocker: 19 routed Mains demands had no Core owner.** The controlling
2018–2023 Mains ledger contains **22** Governance-routed rows. Only **3** name a
`Governance/basic/` file. The other **19** point **exclusively** at `advanced/` — and were
not *answered* even there, appearing only as generated metadata tables. A candidate relying
on Core alone could not have answered them. Repaired: all 19 are now promoted into their Core
owners as executable `§13.0 Direct Mains demands owned by this Core file` routes, each
carrying an explicit **"Core routing supersedes the older Advanced pointer"** statement.

**B3 — Current-status failures, including unsupported or incomplete corrections.**
Independent live verification found the folder's 21 July 2026 snapshot materially wrong in
several places. The first repair correctly fixed the DPDP/RTI section error but introduced
an unsupported **GGI 2025 release** claim, overstated the operational transition under the
National Sports Governance Act, 2025 and left the old RTI error live in two Advanced files.
See §8 and §14.

**Post-repair position.** The original 73-question internal test is retained as cycle-1
evidence only. An independent 57-question hostile pass rejected it on urban governance,
behavioural insights, PPP, GGI, NeSDA and sports-law stage discipline. Those failures are
now repaired in Core; no exact or routed PYQ requires an `advanced/` file.
