# Official UPSC Syllabus Mapping — Governance

> **Applicable papers:** Preliminary Examination - Paper I (GS-I); Main Examination -
> Paper III (General Studies II).
>
> **Official source text:** [`../OFFICIAL-UPSC-CSE-SYLLABUS-VERBATIM.md`](../OFFICIAL-UPSC-CSE-SYLLABUS-VERBATIM.md)
>
> **Status legend:** **COVERED** = clearly owned in current files; **PARTIAL** = mixed/shared
> or supplementary; **GAP** = no current direct owner file.
>
> **Basic-first rule.** The owner files named below are **Core (`basic/`) files only**, and
> each is independently answer-complete for its clause. Every Core file carries an
> `Answer architecture (10/15/20-mark support)` section supplying the demand map, qualified
> theses, mark-scaled structures, evidence banks, counter-arguments, verdict scaffolds and
> factual/current-status controls. The matching `advanced/NN` companion is **optional
> enrichment**; **no mark in any 10, 15 or 20-mark answer depends on it**, and it is
> therefore not listed as an owner.
>
> **PYQ note:** 2018-2023 coverage is routed centrally via
> [`../_PYQ-INDEX.md`](../_PYQ-INDEX.md). ⚠️ **Core routing supersedes older ledger
> pointers:** the central 2018-2023 Mains ledger routes **19** Governance demands to an
> `advanced/` file; all are now answered in the corresponding Core file's
> `§13.0 Direct Mains demands owned by this Core file` block. Audited 2024-2026
> applications are embedded in their Core owners. The complete routed-demand ledger is in
> [`ANSWER-WORTHINESS-AUDIT.md`](ANSWER-WORTHINESS-AUDIT.md).
