## BASIC MCQS / REMEDIATION

### Learning MCQ 01

Which is the safest historiographical phrase for this topic?

- A. connected histories of interaction and localisation
- B. Greater India as overseas empire
- C. civilisational ownership of Asia
- D. Indianization without qualification

**Answer: A.** It recognises transmission but preserves local agency, hybridisation and return flows.

### Learning MCQ 02

Why is 'monsoon route' a better phrase than 'open sea empire' for this topic?

- A. because monsoon winds created fixed empires
- B. because timing, currents and seasonality shaped navigation without proving political control
- C. because all trade was coastal and local only
- D. because overland routes disappeared

**Answer: B.** Seasonal sailing windows explain movement but do not prove territorial rule.

### Learning MCQ 03

Which carrier best explains why a script or prestige title could travel farther than any single ruler?

- A. complete social uniformity
- B. direct annexation alone
- C. portable scribal and institutional practice
- D. village self-sufficiency

**Answer: C.** Scripts, titulature and documentary habits often outlasted the regime that first sponsored them.

### Learning MCQ 04

What is the strongest way to describe Gandhara art in Topic 25?

- A. a Chinese translation school
- B. a Tamil coastal style
- C. pure Greek art transplanted into India
- D. a hybrid Buddhist frontier idiom shaped by multiple cultural worlds

**Answer: D.** Hybrid frontier formation is the repository's safest formulation.

### Learning MCQ 05

Which figure most clearly ties Srivijaya to Nalanda through a maritime monastic route?

- A. Yijing
- B. Kumarajiva
- C. Mahinda
- D. Ravikirti

**Answer: A.** Yijing travelled by sea, stayed in Srivijaya and then studied at Nalanda.

### Learning MCQ 06

What is the safest generalisation about Tibet in Topic 25?

- A. Tibet merely copied India without change
- B. Tibetan translation culture was itself an act of historical remaking
- C. Tibet had no role in textual transmission
- D. Tibet belongs only to modern diplomacy, not historical study

**Answer: B.** Translation teams and monastic institutionalisation were creative, not mechanical, acts.

### Learning MCQ 07

What is the correct way to handle the Mahinda-Sanghamitta story in an exam answer?

- A. as a direct quotation from Ashoka's rock edicts
- B. as later Chola evidence
- C. as a chronicle tradition that should be used with source criticism
- D. as a legend with no historical value

**Answer: C.** The chronicle tradition is valuable but must not be conflated with the emperor's own inscriptions.

### Learning MCQ 08

Which site best illustrates a distinct Cham adaptation of Shaiva temple culture?

- A. Sarnath
- B. Amaravati
- C. Sanchi
- D. My Son

**Answer: D.** My Son is the cleanest Champa example in this topic's evidence set.

### Learning MCQ 09

Why is the Balaputradeva-Nalanda copperplate so valuable for Topic 25?

- A. it shows a Southeast Asian ruler patronising an Indian monastery
- B. it is a Chola battle report
- C. it proves permanent Pala rule in Java
- D. it is the first Harappan seal from Mesopotamia

**Answer: A.** It demonstrates reciprocity, patronage and institutional prestige across the Bay of Bengal.

### Learning MCQ 10

What is the best inference from the spread of Brahmi-derived scripts into Southeast Asia?

- A. Indian political annexation is certain
- B. scribal and prestige-cultural linkages spread through local adaptation
- C. all local languages disappeared
- D. Buddhism became the only religion

**Answer: B.** Script spread is strong evidence of linkage, but not of total cultural replacement.

### Learning MCQ 11

What is the safest claim about religious transmission in Topic 25?

- A. all Buddhist regions followed the same chronology and doctrine
- B. local cults vanished wherever Buddhism arrived
- C. religious plurality and local integration were common outcomes
- D. Shaiva and Vaishnava traditions never travelled outside India

**Answer: C.** Plurality and adaptation are stronger historical patterns than uniform replacement.

### Learning MCQ 12

Why is Ramayana evidence useful in this topic?

- A. it proves Angkor was politically ruled from India
- B. it belongs only to medieval Europe
- C. it proves a Sanskrit text was always copied word for word
- D. it shows how shared narratives could be localised in relief, court culture and performance

**Answer: D.** Epic transmission is valuable precisely because it shows adaptation rather than simple copying.

### Learning MCQ 13

What is the best description of Angkor for Topic 25?

- A. a Khmer monumental complex that adapted Indian roots into distinct regional architecture
- B. a Buddhist cave in Afghanistan
- C. a Gupta monastery in Bihar
- D. an Indian colony in temple form

**Answer: A.** UNESCO's formulation itself emphasises Indian roots and distinct Khmer development together.

### Learning MCQ 14

Why should 'devaraja' be handled cautiously?

- A. because it has no connection with kingship
- B. because it is a region-specific interpretive label, not a universal formula for all Asian polities
- C. because it refers only to Roman trade
- D. because it is a modern UNESCO slogan

**Answer: B.** It is useful only when bounded by local context and evidence.

### Learning MCQ 15

Which statement is safest on the economics of Asian interaction?

- A. trade volume can be precisely estimated from a few finds
- B. only luxury goods moved
- C. commodity flows were multi-nodal and should be described without invented statistics
- D. economics had no link with culture

**Answer: C.** Commodity types and nodal redistribution are safer than volume claims.

### Learning MCQ 16

Which evidence most clearly demonstrates a return flow into India?

- A. the existence of monsoon winds
- B. the Kalinga war
- C. the Ashokan officer list
- D. Balaputradeva's request for support of a Nalanda monastery

**Answer: D.** A Southeast Asian ruler's patronage of Nalanda is reciprocity in documentary form.

### Broad topic coverage MCQs

> Programmatic answer rotation for this section is strict A -> A -> B -> C with no consecutive repeats.

#### Practice MCQ 01

Which framing best corrects the older 'Greater India' narrative?

- A. interaction, localisation and connected histories
- B. complete absence of Indian influence
- C. unchanged cultural colonisation
- D. exclusive political conquest

**Answer: A.** It preserves real links while avoiding triumphalism and passivity.

#### Practice MCQ 02

The strongest reason to discuss routes ecologically is that

- A. all routes were equally open all year
- B. movement depended on season, chokepoints, passes, ports and institutions
- C. ecology matters only in modern shipping
- D. religious movements ignored physical geography

**Answer: B.** Historical routes were seasonal and infrastructural, not abstract lines on a map.

#### Practice MCQ 03

What did monsoon knowledge mainly provide to historical actors?

- A. instant cultural homogenisation
- B. the disappearance of overland trade
- C. a seasonal navigation window rather than proof of empire
- D. a guarantee against piracy

**Answer: C.** Timing and reversals mattered for sailing, but not as a sovereign claim.

#### Practice MCQ 04

Which set lists carriers most accurately for Topic 25?

- A. only pilgrims and relics
- B. only emperors and armies
- C. only merchants and coins
- D. merchants, monks, diplomats, artisans, texts and objects

**Answer: D.** The topic is multi-carrier by design.

#### Practice MCQ 05

Gandhara should be treated primarily as

- A. a hybrid Buddhist frontier zone shaped by multiple cultural worlds
- B. pure Athenian sculpture in exile
- C. an untouched indigenous style with no foreign contact
- D. a Tamil devotional school

**Answer: A.** Hybrid frontier formation is the exam-safe formulation.

#### Practice MCQ 06

### Remedial MCQs for common confusions

> These target habitual overclaims: Indianisation-as-colonisation, passive-recipient stories, script-language confusion and trade-volume exaggeration.

#### Remedial MCQ 01

Which statement is wrong in an answer on Southeast Asia?

- A. Angkor and Borobudur were Indian monuments abroad
- B. local agency reshaped imported materials
- C. Indian influence can coexist with adaptation
- D. architecture must be read with political ecology

**Answer: A.** That statement is the classic overclaim; the others are exam-safe corrections.

#### Remedial MCQ 02

What is the weakest sentence on Gandhara?

- A. coin iconography and sculpture need triangulation
- B. Gandhara art was simply Greek art in India
- C. Mathura remained a distinct parallel idiom
- D. Gandhara belonged to a connected frontier world

**Answer: B.** 'Simply Greek' ignores frontier hybridity and Buddhist patronage.

#### Remedial MCQ 03

Which sentence mishandles Tibet?

- A. 8th-century institutionalisation can be noted cautiously
- B. translation teams mattered
- C. Tibet passively received finished Indian Buddhism without translation or institutional agency
- D. later Atisha belongs to a bounded legacy

**Answer: C.** The passive-recipient story is precisely what this topic rejects.

#### Remedial MCQ 04

Which line is least defensible on Sri Lanka?

- A. the story survives above all in Sri Lankan chronicles
- B. Sri Lanka also preserved and recirculated Buddhist traditions
- C. Brahmi inscriptions support early Buddhist institutionalisation
- D. Ashoka's own edicts narrate the full Mahinda-Sanghamitta story in detail

**Answer: D.** Chronicle tradition must not be turned into direct edict text.

#### Remedial MCQ 05

Which statement confuses script and language?

- A. A Brahmi-derived script in a region proves a total change in everyday spoken language
- B. scripts can travel across different speech communities
- C. script spread and political conquest are not identical
- D. prestige languages need local scribes

**Answer: A.** Script adoption is not the same as total linguistic replacement.

#### Remedial MCQ 06

Which conclusion from the 2026 place-value question is excessive?

- A. inscriptions reveal prestige and knowledge transfer
- B. technical notation automatically proves full cultural assimilation
- C. key status must be labelled carefully when only provisional
- D. scribal practices could move across regions

**Answer: B.** Technical borrowing is important but not civilisational totalisation.

#### Remedial MCQ 07

Which trade claim should be rejected?

- A. goods lists are safer than volume claims
- B. trade also carried texts and ritual objects
- C. because trade moved, exact ancient volumes can be stated confidently from a handful of finds
- D. Sri Lanka and straits nodes mattered for redistribution

**Answer: C.** Sparse evidence rarely permits precise trade-volume claims.

#### Remedial MCQ 08

Which concluding sentence is weakest?

- A. Asian societies selectively adapted incoming materials
- B. reciprocity and return flows must appear in the final verdict
- C. India influenced Asia through routes and institutions
- D. India civilised passive Asian societies through one-way cultural diffusion

**Answer: D.** The rejected sentence revives the very triumphalism the topic is designed to avoid.
