# MCQ 053

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Justice, liberty, equality and fraternity  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements regarding the Preamble:

1. Justice is expressed in social, economic and political dimensions.
2. Liberty includes thought, expression, belief, faith and worship.
3. Equality refers only to formal equality before law and does not include equality of
   opportunity.
4. Fraternity is connected with the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the
   nation.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 4 only  
B. 1 and 3 only  
C. 2, 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2, 3 and 4

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1, 2 and 4 are correct.

### Meaning of the four objectives

- **Justice:** a social order free from status-based domination, economic deprivation and
  political exclusion.
- **Liberty:** protected space for thought, expression, belief, faith and worship.
- **Equality:** equality of status and opportunity; it is wider than merely identical treatment
  under law.
- **Fraternity:** a shared civic bond that protects individual dignity while sustaining national
  unity and integrity.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The Preamble expressly names social, economic and political justice.  
   **Examples:** abolition of untouchability supports social justice; welfare-oriented Directive
   Principles advance economic justice; universal adult franchise advances political justice.
2. **Correct.** These five forms of liberty are expressly listed.  
   **Examples:** Article 19 protects specified freedoms of citizens, while Article 25 protects
   freedom of conscience and religion subject to constitutional limits.
3. **Incorrect.** The Preamble expressly promises equality of **status and opportunity**.  
   **Examples:** equality before law is reflected in Article 14, while equality of opportunity in
   public employment is addressed by Article 16.
4. **Correct.** The Preamble links fraternity with dignity and national unity and integrity.  
   **Example:** fraternity rejects the treatment of caste, religion, language or region as reasons
   for denying equal civic membership.

### Timeline and context

- **26 November 1949:** the Preamble was adopted with unity of the nation.
- **Forty-second Amendment, 1976:** “integrity” was added, producing “unity and integrity of the
  Nation.”

> **Core insight:** liberty and equality can conflict in particular policies; fraternity and
> justice provide the social conditions within which both can become meaningful.
