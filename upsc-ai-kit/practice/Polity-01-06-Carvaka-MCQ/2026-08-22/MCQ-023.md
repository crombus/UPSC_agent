# MCQ 023

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Coverage point:** Adoption and commencement dates  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement is correct?

A. The Constitution was adopted and fully commenced on 15 August 1947.

B. It was adopted on 26 January 1950 and commenced on 26 November 1950.

C. It was adopted on 26 November 1949 and commenced on 26 January 1950.

D. It was adopted on 9 December 1946 and commenced on 15 August 1947.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

The Constituent Assembly adopted the Constitution on **26 November 1949**. Most provisions came
into force on **26 January 1950**, a date chosen to honour the Purna Swaraj declaration associated
with 26 January 1930.

The Assembly's first meeting was held on 9 December 1946. These dates represent different stages
and must not be merged.

> **Memory:** November = adoption; January = commencement.
