# MCQ 066

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Borrowed constitutional features  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements regarding constitutional sources:

1. The system of constitutional writs is associated with the British constitutional tradition.
2. Judicial review and the Fundamental Rights model were substantially influenced by the United
   States.
3. Directive Principles of State Policy were borrowed from Australia.
4. The procedure for constitutional amendment was influenced by South Africa.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 1, 2 and 4 only  
C. 3 and 4 only  
D. 1 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1, 2 and 4 are correct.

### Meaning of the borrowed features

- **Writs:** judicial commands protecting liberty and controlling unlawful public action, such as
  habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari and quo warranto.
- **Judicial review:** power of courts to test legislation or executive action against the
  Constitution.
- **Directive Principles:** non-justiciable governance principles directing the State toward
  social and economic welfare.
- **Amendment procedure:** constitutionally prescribed method for formally changing the
  Constitution.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The writ tradition is associated with British common law and prerogative writs.  
   **Example:** Habeas corpus protects against unlawful detention.
2. **Correct.** The American Constitution strongly influenced Fundamental Rights and judicial
   review.  
   **Example:** Courts may invalidate legislation violating constitutionally protected rights,
   although India's rights and restrictions were adapted to local conditions.
3. **Incorrect.** Directive Principles came from the Irish Constitution.  
   **Example:** They guide welfare legislation but ordinarily cannot be directly enforced like
   Fundamental Rights.
4. **Correct.** South Africa influenced India's amendment procedure.  
   **Example:** Article 368 uses special parliamentary majorities and, for specified federal
   provisions, state ratification.

### Source map

| Source | Illustrative borrowing |
|---|---|
| Britain | Parliamentary government, rule of law, writs, single citizenship |
| United States | Fundamental Rights, judicial review, Vice-President |
| Ireland | DPSP, Rajya Sabha nomination, presidential-election method |
| Australia | Concurrent List, joint sitting |
| Canada | Strong-Centre federation, residuary powers |
| South Africa | Amendment procedure |

> **Core caution:** India borrowed constitutional devices but adapted their operation; ancestry
> does not mean exact copying.
