# MCQ 005

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Coverage point:** Article 3 procedure  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Regarding a bill under Article 3 of the Constitution, which statement is correct?

A. The President refers it to the affected State Legislature for its views, but Parliament is
not bound by those views.

B. It may be introduced without the President's recommendation if the affected State consents.

C. It must be passed as a constitutional amendment under Article 368.

D. Parliament cannot alter the name of a state without that state's consent.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

An Article 3 bill requires the President's recommendation. The President refers it to the
affected State Legislature for its views within a specified period, but those views are
consultative and **do not bind Parliament**.

Such a law is ordinarily passed by a simple majority and, under Article 4, is not treated as an
Article 368 constitutional amendment.

> **Trap:** Consultation with a State Legislature is compulsory; obtaining its consent is not.
