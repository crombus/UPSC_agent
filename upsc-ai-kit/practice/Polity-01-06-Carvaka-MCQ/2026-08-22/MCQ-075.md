# MCQ 075

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Territory of India, Union of India and Article 3 procedure  
**Status:** Correct

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The territory of India includes the territories of the states, Union Territories and any
   territories that India may acquire.
2. The expression “Union of India” includes states, Union Territories and acquired territories
   equally as constituent members of the federal system.
3. Before the introduction of an Article 3 bill affecting a state, the President must obtain that
   state's consent and is bound by the views of its legislature.
4. A law reorganising states under Articles 2 or 3 may make supplemental changes to the First and
   Fourth Schedules and is not treated as an amendment under Article 368.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 2 and 4 only  
C. 1 and 4 only  
D. 1, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Learner's answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C - Statements 1 and 4 only  
**Result:** Correct

### Statement-by-statement analysis

1. **Correct.** Article 1 provides that the territory of India comprises the territories of the
   states, the Union Territories specified in the First Schedule and territories that India may
   acquire.
2. **Incorrect.** The **territory of India** is wider than the **Union of India**. The Union
   consists of the states as constituent federal units; Union Territories and acquired
   territories belong to India's territory but are not states on an equal federal footing.
3. **Incorrect.** An Article 3 bill requires the President's prior recommendation. When a state is
   affected, the President refers it to that state's legislature for its views, but neither the
   President nor Parliament is bound by those views. Consultation is required; consent is not.
4. **Correct.** Under Article 4, a law made under Article 2 or 3 may amend the First Schedule
   (states and territories) and Fourth Schedule (Rajya Sabha seat allocation) and include
   supplemental or consequential provisions. It is not deemed an Article 368 constitutional
   amendment and can be enacted by a simple majority.

### Technical terms

- **Territory of India:** the full geographical domain under Indian sovereignty, including states,
  Union Territories and acquired territories.
- **Union of India:** the federation's constituent states; it is narrower than the territory of
  India.
- **Prior recommendation:** presidential authorisation required before an Article 3 bill may be
  introduced in Parliament.
- **Consultation:** seeking the state legislature's views without transferring final
  decision-making authority to it.
- **Consequential provision:** an ancillary change needed to make reorganisation operational,
  such as reallocating Rajya Sabha seats.

### Procedure under Articles 3 and 4

```text
Reorganisation proposal
        |
President's prior recommendation
        |
Reference to affected state legislature
        |
State gives views within specified period
        |
Parliament may accept or reject those views
        |
Simple-majority law under Articles 3 and 4
```

### Wider context and examples

- **Telangana, 2014:** Andhra Pradesh's legislature opposed the proposed reorganisation, but its
  consent was not constitutionally necessary.
- **Cession to a foreign country:** Article 3 is insufficient because sovereignty leaves India.
  *Berubari Union* (1960) required an Article 368 amendment; this differs from internal
  reorganisation.

> **Core distinction:** states are constitutionally consulted about reorganisation, but they have
> neither a veto over their boundaries nor a right to secede.
