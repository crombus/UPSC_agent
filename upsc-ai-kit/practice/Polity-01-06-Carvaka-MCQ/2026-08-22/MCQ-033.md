## Question

Following the 2020 merger of Dadra and Nagar Haveli with Daman and Diu into a single Union
Territory, India consists of:

A. 28 states and 8 Union Territories;

B. 29 states and 7 Union Territories;

C. 28 states and 9 Union Territories;

D. 29 states and 8 Union Territories.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

India has **28 states and 8 Union Territories**. The number of Union Territories became eight
after Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu were merged into one Union Territory in January
2020.

The former state of Jammu and Kashmir had earlier been reorganised in 2019 into the Union
Territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.

> **Memory:** Current count = 28 + 8.
