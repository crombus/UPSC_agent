# MCQ 018

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Coverage point:** Intellectual origins of Preamble ideals  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which pair is correctly matched?

A. Justice - French Revolution

B. Liberty, Equality and Fraternity - French Revolution

C. Republic - Russian Revolution

D. Secularism - American Revolution

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Incorrect

The ideals of **Liberty, Equality and Fraternity** are associated with the French Revolution.
The Preamble's conception of social, economic and political **Justice** is associated with the
Russian Revolution.

“Republic” describes a polity with an elected head of state; it is not sourced from the Russian
Revolution in this standard constitutional-origin mapping.

> **Memory:** Justice = Russian; Liberty-Equality-Fraternity = French.
