# MCQ 045

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Length, amendability, judiciary and citizenship  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Constitution's length is partly explained by India's diversity and the inclusion of
   detailed administrative provisions.
2. It combines rigidity and flexibility because different constitutional changes require
   different procedures.
3. An integrated judiciary means that the Union and each State possess entirely separate
   judicial hierarchies.
4. India follows the American model of separate Union and State citizenship.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 1, 2 and 3 only  
C. 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1 and 2 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Rigid Constitution:** important provisions require a special amendment process rather than
  an ordinary legislative majority.
- **Flexible Constitution:** provisions can be changed through a comparatively easier process.
- **Integrated judiciary:** one connected judicial hierarchy headed by the Supreme Court, followed
  by High Courts and subordinate courts; there are not separate federal and state court systems
  like those found in the United States.
- **Single citizenship:** a person possesses Indian citizenship, not separate constitutional
  citizenship of India and an individual state.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The Constitution regulates a large, diverse federation and contains detailed
   administrative, institutional and rights provisions.  
   **Example:** It includes separate Parts and Schedules for legislative distribution, Scheduled
   Areas, languages and local government.
2. **Correct.** Different provisions follow different amendment routes.  
   **Example:** Some matters change by ordinary law; most Article 368 amendments need a special
   parliamentary majority; federal provisions additionally require ratification by at least half
   the states.
3. **Incorrect.** India has a single integrated hierarchy, not two independent judicial systems.  
   **Example:** A case may move from a subordinate court to a High Court and ultimately to the
   Supreme Court.
4. **Incorrect.** India follows single citizenship.  
   **Example:** A resident of Maharashtra is constitutionally an Indian citizen, not separately a
   citizen of Maharashtra in the American dual-citizenship sense.

> **Core distinction:** federalism divides governmental powers, but it does not necessarily
> require dual citizenship or dual court systems.
