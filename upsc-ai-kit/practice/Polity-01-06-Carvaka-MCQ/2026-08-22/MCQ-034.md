# MCQ 034

**Topic:** Polity 06 - Citizenship  
**Coverage point:** Fundamental Rights restricted to citizens  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which Fundamental Right is constitutionally available only to citizens and not to every person?

A. Equality before law under Article 14

B. Freedoms guaranteed by Article 19

C. Protection of life and personal liberty under Article 21

D. Freedom of conscience under Article 25

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Incorrect

Article 19 expressly guarantees its freedoms to **citizens**. Articles 14 and 21 use the wider
language of “person,” while Article 25 extends freedom of conscience and religion to all persons,
subject to constitutional restrictions.

Other citizen-specific rights include Articles 15, 16, 29 and 30, along with voting and
eligibility for specified public offices.

> **Trap:** Article 14 protects both citizens and foreigners; Article 19 does not.
