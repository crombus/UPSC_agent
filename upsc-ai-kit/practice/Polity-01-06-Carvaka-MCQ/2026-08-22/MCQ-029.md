# MCQ 029

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Coverage point:** Government of India Act, 1858  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement correctly describes the Government of India Act, 1858?

A. It abolished Company rule, transferred Indian administration to the Crown and created the
office of the Secretary of State for India.

B. It ended the Company's trade monopoly while retaining its political authority.

C. It introduced dyarchy in the provinces and direct elections.

D. It proposed an All-India Federation and provincial autonomy.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

The Government of India Act, 1858 ended East India Company rule and transferred the government
of India to the British Crown. It created the office of the Secretary of State for India,
assisted by a Council of India. The Governor-General also came to be known as the Viceroy;
Lord Canning was the first Viceroy.

The other options describe the Charter Act, 1813; the 1919 Act; and the 1935 Act respectively.

> **Memory:** 1858 = Company out, Crown in.
