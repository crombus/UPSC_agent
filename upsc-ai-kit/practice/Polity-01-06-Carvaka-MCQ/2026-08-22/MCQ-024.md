# MCQ 024

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Coverage point:** Sources of India's constitutional provisions  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which pair is correctly matched?

A. Directive Principles of State Policy - United States

B. Fundamental Rights - Ireland

C. Joint sitting of the two Houses - Canada

D. Residuary powers vested in the Centre - Canada

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

India borrowed the strong-Centre federal model and the vesting of residuary powers in the Centre
from Canada.

- Directive Principles - Ireland;
- Fundamental Rights - United States;
- joint sitting - Australia.

> **Trap:** Canada and Australia are frequently interchanged: Canada = strong Centre; Australia
> = Concurrent List and joint sitting.
