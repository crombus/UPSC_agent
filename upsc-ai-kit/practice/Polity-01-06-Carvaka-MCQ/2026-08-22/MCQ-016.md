# MCQ 016

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Coverage point:** Key personnel of the Constituent Assembly  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Who served as the Constitutional Adviser to the Constituent Assembly?

A. K.M. Munshi

B. Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar

C. T.T. Krishnamachari

D. Sir B.N. Rau

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Incorrect

Sir B.N. Rau served as the Constitutional Adviser to the Constituent Assembly. He prepared the
initial constitutional draft and advised the Assembly on comparative constitutional design.

K.M. Munshi and Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar were original members of the Drafting Committee.
T.T. Krishnamachari later replaced D.P. Khaitan.

> **Memory:** Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee; B.N. Rau advised the Assembly.
