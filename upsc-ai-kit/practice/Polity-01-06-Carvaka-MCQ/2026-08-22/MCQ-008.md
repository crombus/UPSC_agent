# MCQ 008

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Coverage point:** Distinguishing the constitutional reforms of 1773, 1833, 1909 and 1919  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?

A. Regulating Act, 1773 - Created the office of Governor-General of India

B. Charter Act, 1833 - Introduced open competition for the civil services

C. Indian Councils Act, 1909 - Introduced dyarchy in the provinces

D. Government of India Act, 1919 - Introduced bicameralism and direct elections at the Centre

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Incorrect

The Government of India Act, 1919 introduced bicameralism and direct elections at the Centre,
along with dyarchy in the provinces.

- The Regulating Act, 1773 created the **Governor-General of Bengal**, not India.
- The Charter Act, 1833 created the **Governor-General of India**; open competition is associated
  with the Charter Act, 1853.
- The Indian Councils Act, 1909 introduced separate electorates for Muslims, not dyarchy.

> **Memory line:** 1909 = separate electorate; 1919 = dyarchy; 1935 = provincial autonomy.
