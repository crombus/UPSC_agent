# MCQ 028

**Topic:** Philosophy - Carvaka  
**Coverage point:** Emergent consciousness and dehatmavada  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

How does the classical Carvaka explain consciousness?

A. It is the eternal property of an immaterial atman.

B. It is a quality transmitted to the body by akasa.

C. It is temporarily implanted in matter by God.

D. It emerges from the organised combination of material elements, although the separate
elements are not themselves conscious.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Incorrect

Cārvāka rejects a separate, eternal ātman. Consciousness is understood as an **emergent property**
of the organised living body, arising from combinations of material elements even though the
elements separately are not conscious.

Traditional illustrations compare this emergence to intoxicating power arising when
non-intoxicating ingredients are combined.

> **Doctrine:** Dehātmavāda identifies the person/self with the living conscious body rather than
> an immaterial substance surviving death.
