# MCQ 036

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Coverage point:** Charter Act, 1853  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which development is associated with the Charter Act, 1853?

A. Introduction of separate electorates for Muslims

B. Transfer of Indian government from the Company to the Crown

C. Introduction of provincial autonomy

D. Separation of the legislative and executive functions of the Governor-General's Council and
the opening of civil-service recruitment to competition

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

The Charter Act, 1853 separated the legislative and executive functions of the
Governor-General's Council and opened the way for competitive recruitment to the civil
services.

- Separate electorates: Indian Councils Act, 1909;
- transfer to Crown: Government of India Act, 1858;
- provincial autonomy: Government of India Act, 1935.

> **Memory:** 1853 = council separation and civil-service competition; 1858 = Crown rule.
