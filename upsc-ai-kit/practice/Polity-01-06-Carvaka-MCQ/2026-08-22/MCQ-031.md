# MCQ 031

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Coverage point:** The Mini-Constitution  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which constitutional amendment is commonly called the “Mini-Constitution” because of the
scale and range of its changes?

A. Forty-fourth Amendment

B. Sixty-first Amendment

C. Forty-second Amendment

D. Seventy-third Amendment

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

The Forty-second Amendment Act, 1976 is called the “Mini-Constitution” because it made extensive
changes across numerous constitutional provisions. Among other changes, it inserted Socialist,
Secular and Integrity into the Preamble and added Fundamental Duties.

The Forty-fourth Amendment later reversed or modified several Emergency-era changes, but it is
not called the Mini-Constitution.

> **Memory:** 42nd = Mini-Constitution; 44th = corrective post-Emergency amendment.
