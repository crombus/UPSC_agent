# MCQ 047

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Articles 3 and 4 and foreign cession  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. Parliament may alter the area, boundaries or name of an existing state under Article 3.
2. The President must obtain the affected State Legislature's consent before recommending an
   Article 3 bill.
3. A law made under Articles 2 or 3 may contain supplemental changes to the First and Fourth
   Schedules without being treated as an Article 368 amendment.
4. Article 3 by itself is sufficient to cede Indian territory to a foreign country.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 2 and 4 only  
C. 1 and 3 only  
D. 1, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1 and 3 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Consultation:** seeking a State Legislature's views; the opinion need not be accepted.
- **Consent:** legally binding approval without which action cannot proceed. Article 3 does not
  require state consent.
- **First Schedule:** lists states and Union Territories and their territorial descriptions.
- **Fourth Schedule:** allocates Rajya Sabha seats.
- **Cession:** transfer of Indian territory to a foreign state.

### Constitutional mechanism

```text
Article 3 proposal
      ↓
President's recommendation
      ↓
Reference to affected State Legislature for views
      ↓
Parliament may accept or reject those views
      ↓
Ordinary legislative majority
      ↓
Article 4 makes consequential Schedule changes
```

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Article 3 covers formation of states and alteration of area, boundaries or names.  
   **Example:** Parliament created Telangana in 2014 by reorganising Andhra Pradesh.
2. **Incorrect.** Referral for views is compulsory, but consent is not.  
   **Example:** A State Legislature may oppose a reorganisation bill, yet Parliament retains the
   constitutional power to enact it.
3. **Correct.** Article 4 permits consequential changes to the First and Fourth Schedules and
   declares that such legislation is not an Article 368 amendment.  
   **Example:** Creation of a new state requires updating its territorial entry and Rajya Sabha
   representation.
4. **Incorrect.** The *Berubari Union* position requires an Article 368 amendment for foreign
   cession.  
   **Example:** The Ninth Amendment was enacted for the relevant India-Pakistan territorial
   arrangement.

> **Core distinction:** Article 3 reorganises territory within India; Article 368 is required
> when Indian territory is legally ceded outside India.
