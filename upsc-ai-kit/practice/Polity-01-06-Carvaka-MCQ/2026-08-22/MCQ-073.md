# MCQ 073

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Parts of the Constitution and decentralised government  
**Status:** Correct

## Question

Consider the following pairs:

1. Part IX - Panchayats
2. Part IX-A - Municipalities
3. Part IX-B - Co-operative societies
4. Part XVIII - Emergency provisions

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?

A. All four  
B. Only three  
C. Only two  
D. Only one

## Response and explanation

**Learner's answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A - All four  
**Result:** Correct

### Pair-by-pair analysis

1. **Part IX - Panchayats: Correct.** Part IX contains Articles 243 to 243-O and was inserted by
   the 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act 1992.
2. **Part IX-A - Municipalities: Correct.** Part IX-A contains Articles 243-P to 243-ZG and was
   inserted by the 74th Constitutional Amendment Act 1992.
3. **Part IX-B - Co-operative societies: Correct.** Part IX-B was inserted by the 97th
   Constitutional Amendment Act 2011 and contains constitutional provisions concerning
   co-operative societies.
4. **Part XVIII - Emergency provisions: Correct.** Part XVIII contains Articles 352 to 360,
   covering national, state and financial emergencies.

### Technical terms

- **Part:** a major thematic division of the Constitution containing related Articles.
- **Panchayat:** an institution of rural local self-government.
- **Municipality:** an institution of urban local self-government.
- **Co-operative society:** a voluntary, democratically controlled association formed to advance
  members' common economic or social interests.
- **Emergency provision:** a constitutional mechanism permitting exceptional changes in the
  ordinary distribution or exercise of governmental power during specified crises.

### Timeline

| Year | Development |
|---:|---|
| 1950 | Constitution commenced without constitutionally mandatory three-tier local government |
| 1992 | 73rd and 74th Amendments enacted |
| 1993 | Parts IX and IX-A came into force |
| 2011-12 | 97th Amendment added Part IX-B and strengthened the constitutional status of co-operatives |

### Wider context

Parts IX and IX-A constitutionalised local democracy by prescribing regular elections,
reservations, five-year terms and State Finance Commissions. They did not convert local bodies into
sovereign units: state legislatures continue to determine much of their powers and functions.

For emergency provisions, remember the threefold scheme:

| Emergency | Article | Basic trigger |
|---|---:|---|
| National Emergency | 352 | War, external aggression or armed rebellion |
| President's Rule | 356 | Failure of constitutional machinery in a state |
| Financial Emergency | 360 | Threat to India's financial stability or credit |

> **Part sequence:** **IX = rural; IX-A = urban; IX-B = co-operatives; XVIII = emergencies;
> XX = constitutional amendment.**
