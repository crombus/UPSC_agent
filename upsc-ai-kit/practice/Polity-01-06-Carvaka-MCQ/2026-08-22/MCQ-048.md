# MCQ 048

**Topic:** Polity 06 - Citizenship  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Changing statutory rules of citizenship by birth  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements regarding citizenship by birth:

1. A person born in India between 26 January 1950 and 1 July 1987 was generally a citizen by
   birth irrespective of the parents' nationality.
2. For a person born in India from 1 July 1987 but before 3 December 2004, at least one parent
   had to be an Indian citizen at the time of birth.
3. For a person born in India on or after 3 December 2004, both parents must be citizens, or one
   must be a citizen and the other must not be an illegal migrant.
4. Registration as an OCI cardholder satisfies the requirement of Indian citizenship of a
   parent for these rules.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2, 3 and 4 only  
C. 1 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1, 2 and 3 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Citizenship by birth:** citizenship acquired because a person is born within India while
  satisfying the statutory conditions applicable on the date of birth.
- **Jus soli:** citizenship based principally on place of birth.
- **Illegal migrant:** under the statutory scheme, a foreigner entering without valid travel
  documents or remaining beyond the permitted period, subject to applicable legal exceptions.
- **OCI:** Overseas Citizen of India status; it is not Indian citizenship or dual citizenship.

### Timeline and tightening of the rule

| Birth period | Basic statutory requirement |
|---|---|
| 26 Jan 1950 to before 1 Jul 1987 | Birth in India generally sufficient |
| 1 Jul 1987 to before 3 Dec 2004 | Either parent must be an Indian citizen |
| On/after 3 Dec 2004 | Both parents citizens, or one citizen + other not an illegal migrant |

The movement is from a broad place-of-birth rule toward a rule combining birthplace and parental
status.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The initial regime was predominantly jus soli.  
   **Example:** A person born in India in 1975 generally acquired citizenship by birth regardless
   of parental nationality, subject to statutory exceptions such as children of foreign
   diplomats.
2. **Correct.** The 1987-2004 period required one citizen parent.  
   **Example:** For a child born in India in 1995, citizenship of either the mother or father was
   sufficient.
3. **Correct.** The post-2004 rule adds the illegal-migrant condition.  
   **Example:** If a child is born in India in 2010 and only one parent is an Indian citizen, the
   other parent's legal status becomes relevant.
4. **Incorrect.** OCI does not make the parent an Indian citizen.  
   **Example:** An OCI cardholder may receive residence and travel benefits but cannot vote and
   does not satisfy a statutory requirement specifically demanding an “Indian citizen” parent.

> **Hard trap:** The applicable citizenship rule depends on the date of birth; never apply the
> current rule retrospectively to every person born after 1950.
