# MCQ 064

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Indian Independence Act, 1947  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements regarding the Indian Independence Act, 1947:

1. It created the two independent dominions of India and Pakistan.
2. British paramountcy over the princely states lapsed.
3. The Constituent Assemblies could legislate for their dominions as well as frame constitutions.
4. The Government of India Act, 1935 immediately ceased to provide the adapted framework of
   governance before the new constitutions commenced.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2, 3 and 4 only  
C. 1 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1, 2 and 3 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Dominion:** a self-governing political entity within the then Commonwealth framework, with
  constitutional ties to the Crown during the transitional period.
- **Paramountcy:** the British Crown's superior political relationship over princely states,
  exercised through treaties and indirect control.
- **Lapse of paramountcy:** termination of that British relationship rather than its automatic
  transfer to either India or Pakistan.
- **Adapted framework:** continued use of the Government of India Act, 1935 with modifications
  until replacement by the new Constitution.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The Act partitioned British India into the dominions of India and Pakistan from
   15 August 1947.  
   **Example:** Each dominion received its own Governor-General and Constituent Assembly.
2. **Correct.** British paramountcy over princely states lapsed.  
   **Example:** States such as Hyderabad, Junagadh and Jammu and Kashmir faced decisions concerning
   accession rather than automatically becoming part of India through a transfer of paramountcy.
3. **Correct.** The Constituent Assemblies exercised both constituent and legislative authority.  
   **Example:** India's Assembly framed the Constitution while also functioning as the Dominion
   Legislature.
4. **Incorrect.** The adapted 1935 Act continued as the working constitutional framework during
   the transition.  
   **Example:** India did not face a legal vacuum between 15 August 1947 and 26 January 1950.

### Timeline

```text
18 July 1947: Independence Act receives assent
15 August 1947: two dominions created; paramountcy lapses
26 November 1949: Indian Constitution adopted
26 January 1950: Constitution replaces adapted 1935 framework
```

> **Hard distinction:** independence ended British supremacy, but administrative continuity was
> maintained through the adapted 1935 Act until the Constitution commenced.
