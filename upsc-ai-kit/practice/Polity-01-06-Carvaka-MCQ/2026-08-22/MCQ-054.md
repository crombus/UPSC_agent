# MCQ 054

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Article 1 and the nature of the Union  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The territory of India includes the territories of the states, Union Territories and any
   territories that may be acquired.
2. The expression “Union of States” means that the Indian federation resulted from a revocable
   agreement among sovereign states.
3. States possess no constitutional right to secede from the Union.
4. Article 1 by itself authorises the Union executive to cede Indian territory to a foreign
   country.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 1 and 3 only  
C. 2 and 4 only  
D. 1, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1 and 3 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Territory of India:** the complete geographical domain under Indian sovereignty, comprising
  state territories, Union Territories and territories that may be acquired.
- **Union of States:** Article 1's description indicating a permanent Union whose states do not
  possess a unilateral right of withdrawal.
- **Secession:** withdrawal of a state or territory from the Union to become independent.
- **Acquired territory:** territory lawfully brought under Indian sovereignty after the
  Constitution's commencement.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Article 1 expressly includes states, Union Territories and acquired territories.  
   **Example:** A territory acquired through a valid international settlement would enter the
   third category until constitutionally organised.
2. **Incorrect.** India's federation did not arise from a compact among previously sovereign
   states that retained a right to leave.  
   **Example:** Princely states integrated into India during 1947-50, but today's states do not
   hold contractual sovereignty against the Union.
3. **Correct.** States have no constitutional right to secede.  
   **Example:** Parliament may change a state's boundaries under Article 3, illustrating the
   phrase “indestructible Union of destructible states.”
4. **Incorrect.** Article 1 describes territory; it does not independently authorise foreign
   cession.  
   **Example:** The *Berubari Union* position required an Article 368 amendment, leading to the
   Ninth Amendment.

### Historical context

At independence, British Indian provinces and hundreds of princely states had to be politically
integrated. The Constitution deliberately used “Union” to emphasise that this integration was
final and that no constituent state possessed a secession right.

> **Core distinction:** Parliament can reorganise a state's internal place in the Union under
> Article 3, but transferring sovereign territory outside India requires a higher constitutional
> process.
