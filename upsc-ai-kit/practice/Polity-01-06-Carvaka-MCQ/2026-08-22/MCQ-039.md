# MCQ 039

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Coverage point:** Meaning of republic  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

In the Preamble, the term “Republic” principally signifies that:

A. every law must be approved through a referendum;

B. India has no written Constitution;

C. the head of the State is elected, directly or indirectly, rather than holding hereditary
office;

D. the executive is independent of the legislature.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

A republic has a non-hereditary head of state. India's President is indirectly elected for a
fixed term, unlike a hereditary monarch.

The term also expresses political equality by denying any person's hereditary claim to the
highest public office.

> **Trap:** Republic concerns the character of the head of state, not whether the system is
> parliamentary or presidential.
