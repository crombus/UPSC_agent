# MCQ 051

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Drafting Committee composition and replacements  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements regarding the Drafting Committee:

1. B.R. Ambedkar was its Chairman.
2. Sir B.N. Rau was one of its original seven members.
3. N. Madhava Rau replaced B.L. Mitter.
4. T.T. Krishnamachari replaced D.P. Khaitan.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2, 3 and 4 only  
C. 1, 3 and 4 only  
D. 1 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1, 3 and 4 are correct.

### Original Drafting Committee

The Constituent Assembly appointed the seven-member Drafting Committee on 29 August 1947:

1. **B.R. Ambedkar** - Chairman;
2. N. Gopalaswamy Ayyangar;
3. Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar;
4. K.M. Munshi;
5. Syed Mohammad Saadullah;
6. B.L. Mitter;
7. D.P. Khaitan.

### Replacements

- **N. Madhava Rau** replaced B.L. Mitter.
- **T.T. Krishnamachari** replaced D.P. Khaitan after Khaitan's death.

### Meaning of the roles

- **Drafting Committee member:** directly participated as a member of the committee responsible
  for converting constitutional decisions into a legally organised draft.
- **Constitutional Adviser:** supplied comparative research, technical advice and an initial draft,
  but was not thereby a committee member.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Ambedkar chaired the Committee and defended the Draft Constitution before the
   Assembly.  
   **Example:** His role concerned legal architecture and explanation, while the Constitution
   remained a collective product of the Assembly.
2. **Incorrect.** Sir B.N. Rau was Constitutional Adviser, not one of the seven Committee members.  
   **Example:** Rau studied foreign constitutional systems and prepared an initial draft used by
   the Drafting Committee.
3. **Correct.** N. Madhava Rau replaced B.L. Mitter.
4. **Correct.** T.T. Krishnamachari replaced D.P. Khaitan.

> **Memory:** B.N. Rau advised; B.R. Ambedkar chaired and drafted with the seven-member Committee.
