# MCQ 022

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Coverage point:** Evolution of legislative oversight  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which enactment first allowed members of legislative councils to discuss the budget and ask
questions of the executive?

A. Indian Councils Act, 1861

B. Indian Councils Act, 1892

C. Indian Councils Act, 1909

D. Government of India Act, 1919

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Incorrect

The Indian Councils Act, 1892 enlarged the legislative councils and allowed members to discuss
the budget and ask questions of the executive. It also introduced a limited form of indirect
election, although the statute avoided using the word “election.”

- 1861: Indian non-official participation and portfolio system;
- 1909: separate electorates for Muslims;
- 1919: dyarchy, bicameralism and direct elections.

> **Trap:** Budget discussion began before the introduction of direct elections.
