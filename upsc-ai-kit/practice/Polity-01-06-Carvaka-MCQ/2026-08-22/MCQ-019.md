# MCQ 019

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Coverage point:** Commissions on linguistic reorganisation  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which is the correct chronological order?

A. JVP Committee - Dhar Commission - Fazl Ali Commission

B. Fazl Ali Commission - Dhar Commission - JVP Committee

C. Dhar Commission - JVP Committee - Fazl Ali Commission

D. Dhar Commission - Fazl Ali Commission - JVP Committee

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

The correct sequence is:

1. **Dhar Commission (1948)**;
2. **JVP Committee (1948-49)**;
3. **Fazl Ali Commission (1953-55)**.

### Committee and purpose

- **Dhar Commission (1948):** The Linguistic Provinces Commission under **S.K. Dhar** examined
  whether states should be reorganised primarily on a linguistic basis. It preferred
  administrative convenience and national interest over language alone.
- **JVP Committee (1948-49):** Named after **Jawaharlal Nehru, Vallabhbhai Patel and Pattabhi
  Sitaramayya**. It reconsidered the linguistic-state demand and initially advised postponement,
  prioritising national unity, security and economic development.
- **Fazl Ali Commission (1953-55):** The States Reorganisation Commission was chaired by
  **Fazl Ali**, with **K.M. Panikkar** and **H.N. Kunzru** as members. It accepted language as an
  important basis but balanced it with national unity, administrative efficiency and economic
  viability. Its report led to the States Reorganisation Act, 1956 and the Seventh Amendment.

> **Mnemonic:** Dhar Just Fell = Dhar → JVP → Fazl Ali.
