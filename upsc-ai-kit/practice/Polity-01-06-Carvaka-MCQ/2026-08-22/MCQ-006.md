# MCQ 006

**Topic:** Polity 06 - Citizenship  
**Coverage point:** Overseas Citizen of India status  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement concerning an Overseas Citizen of India cardholder is correct?

A. OCI status amounts to constitutionally protected dual citizenship.

B. An OCI cardholder cannot vote in Indian elections or become a member of Parliament.

C. An OCI cardholder enjoys every political right reserved for Indian citizens.

D. OCI status prevents the Central Government from cancelling registration on statutory grounds.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

OCI is **not dual citizenship**. An OCI cardholder cannot vote, contest elections, become a
member of Parliament or a State Legislature, or hold specified constitutional offices such as
President or Vice-President.

OCI provides a statutory immigration and residence status with certain benefits, not the full
political membership attached to Indian citizenship.

> **Trap:** The word “citizen” in OCI's title does not confer constitutional citizenship.
