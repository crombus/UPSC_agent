# MCQ 007

**Topic:** Philosophy - Carvaka  
**Coverage point:** Rejection of inference and the problem of vyapti  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Why does Carvaka deny that inference can provide philosophically certain knowledge?

A. Inference can operate only upon scriptural statements.

B. Every inference is necessarily contradicted by direct perception.

C. The universal and exceptionless relation required for inference cannot be established by
limited perception without circularity or regress.

D. Carvaka believes that smoke is never associated with fire.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

Inference requires **vyapti**, an exceptionless universal relation such as “wherever there is
smoke, there is fire.” Limited perception cannot inspect every past, present, future, near and
distant instance.

Using inference to establish vyapti would make the proof circular or generate a regress, because
that further inference would itself require another established vyapti.

> **Nuance:** Moderate reconstructions allow useful everyday inference while denying that it
> proves transcendent entities with certainty.
