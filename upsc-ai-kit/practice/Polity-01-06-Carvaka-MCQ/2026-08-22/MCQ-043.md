# MCQ 043

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Constitutional-reform timeline from 1833 to 1919  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Charter Act, 1833 created the office of Governor-General of India and centralised
   legislative authority.
2. The Charter Act, 1853 abolished the East India Company's rule and transferred administration
   to the Crown.
3. The Indian Councils Act, 1909 introduced separate electorates for Muslims.
4. The Government of India Act, 1919 replaced provincial dyarchy with full provincial autonomy.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 2 and 4 only  
C. 1 and 3 only  
D. 1, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1 and 3 are correct.

### Timeline and context

| Year | Measure | Main constitutional development |
|---:|---|---|
| 1833 | Charter Act | Governor-General of India; legislative centralisation |
| 1853 | Charter Act | Legislative-executive separation in the Council; competition for civil service |
| 1858 | Government of India Act | Company rule abolished; administration transferred to the Crown |
| 1909 | Indian Councils Act | Separate electorates for Muslims |
| 1919 | Government of India Act | Dyarchy in provinces; bicameralism and direct elections |
| 1935 | Government of India Act | Provincial autonomy; proposed federation |

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The Charter Act, 1833 made the Governor-General of Bengal the
   **Governor-General of India**; Lord William Bentinck was the first holder. It also concentrated
   legislative authority at the Centre.  
   **Example:** Bombay and Madras lost their independent legislative powers under this
   centralising arrangement.
2. **Incorrect.** The 1853 Act separated legislative and executive work in the
   Governor-General's Council and opened civil-service recruitment to competition. The transfer
   from Company to Crown occurred under the **Government of India Act, 1858**, following the
   Revolt of 1857.  
   **Example:** Lord Canning became the first Viceroy after the Crown takeover.
3. **Correct.** The Morley-Minto reforms of 1909 introduced separate electorates for Muslims.  
   **Example:** Muslim voters in a separate electoral category elected Muslim representatives,
   institutionalising communal representation.
4. **Incorrect.** The 1919 Act **introduced dyarchy** in provinces rather than abolishing it.  
   **Example:** transferred subjects such as education were administered by ministers, while
   reserved subjects such as police remained under the Governor and executive councillors.
   Provincial autonomy came under the 1935 Act.

> **Memory chain:** 1833 centralisation → 1853 council/civil service → 1858 Crown → 1909
> electorate → 1919 dyarchy → 1935 autonomy.
