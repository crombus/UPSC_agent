# MCQ 013

**Topic:** Polity 06 - Citizenship  
**Coverage point:** Acquisition of citizenship under the Citizenship Act, 1955  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which of the following is a statutory mode of acquiring Indian citizenship?

A. Incorporation of territory

B. Appointment to a constitutional office

C. Permanent residence in any Indian State for one year

D. Registration as an Overseas Citizen of India

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

The Citizenship Act, 1955 recognizes acquisition by birth, descent, registration,
naturalisation and **incorporation of territory**.

OCI registration does not confer Indian citizenship or dual citizenship. It creates a separate
statutory status with limited benefits and without citizens' political rights.

> **Mnemonic:** BeD-RaIN = Birth, Descent, Registration, Naturalisation, Incorporation.
