# MCQ 074

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Justice, liberty, equality and fraternity  
**Status:** Incorrect

## Question

Consider the following statements regarding the ideals expressed in the Preamble:

1. The Preamble's conception of social, economic and political justice is commonly associated
   with the ideals of the Russian Revolution.
2. Liberty, equality and fraternity are associated with the French Revolution.
3. Constitutional equality rejects special privilege but does not prohibit every form of
   reasonable classification or affirmative action.
4. Fraternity in the Preamble is concerned only with the territorial unity of India and has no
   relationship with individual dignity.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 1, 2 and 3 only  
C. 2, 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Learner's answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** B - Statements 1, 2 and 3 only  
**Result:** Incorrect

### Statement-by-statement analysis

1. **Correct.** The Preamble's threefold promise of **social, economic and political justice** is
   conventionally linked to the ideals associated with the Russian Revolution.
2. **Correct.** **Liberty, equality and fraternity** are historically associated with the French
   Revolution. This is distinct from the origin conventionally assigned to justice.
3. **Correct.** Constitutional equality does not demand mechanically identical treatment in every
   situation. Article 14 permits reasonable classification, while Articles 15 and 16 expressly
   support constitutionally authorised affirmative action for disadvantaged groups.
4. **Incorrect.** Fraternity expressly assures both **the dignity of the individual** and **the
   unity and integrity of the nation**. It links personal dignity with social solidarity and
   national cohesion.

### Why option C fails

Option C includes Statement 4, which wrongly removes individual dignity from fraternity. It also
excludes Statement 1, although the justice-Russian Revolution association is correct.

### Technical terms

- **Social justice:** removal of entrenched discrimination and unequal social status.
- **Economic justice:** reduction of unjust economic inequality and prevention of exploitation.
- **Political justice:** equal opportunity to participate in political institutions and processes.
- **Reasonable classification:** treating distinguishable groups differently when the
  classification rests on an intelligible differentia connected rationally to the law's purpose.
- **Affirmative action:** constitutionally authorised measures intended to secure substantive
  equality for disadvantaged groups.
- **Fraternity:** a sense of common brotherhood that protects dignity and strengthens national
  unity.

### Timeline and textual context

| Year | Development |
|---:|---|
| 1789 | French Revolution popularised liberty, equality and fraternity |
| 1917 | Russian Revolution became associated with ideals of social, economic and political justice |
| 1947 | Objectives Resolution adopted, supplying the Preamble's philosophical foundation |
| 1949 | Preamble adopted with justice, liberty, equality and fraternity |
| 1976 | 42nd Amendment added “socialist”, “secular” and “integrity” |

### Concrete examples

- Reserving educational opportunities for socially and educationally backward classes does not
  necessarily violate equality; it may promote **substantive equality**.
- Protection of a person's dignity against caste humiliation advances fraternity at the
  individual level while also reducing social divisions that threaten national unity.

> **Memory rule:** **Justice = Russian Revolution; liberty-equality-fraternity = French
> Revolution; fraternity = dignity + unity and integrity.**
