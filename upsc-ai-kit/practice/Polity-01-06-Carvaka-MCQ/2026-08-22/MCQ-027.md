# MCQ 027

**Topic:** Polity 06 - Citizenship  
**Coverage point:** Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2019  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement correctly describes the Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2019?

A. It automatically grants citizenship to every migrant from India's neighbouring countries.

B. It applies equally to persons of every religious community from every neighbouring country.

C. It creates a facilitated application route for six specified communities from Afghanistan,
Bangladesh and Pakistan who entered India by 31 December 2014, subject to statutory scrutiny.

D. It converts every Overseas Citizen of India cardholder into a full Indian citizen.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

The Citizenship (Amendment) Act, 2019 creates a facilitated statutory route for Hindus, Sikhs,
Buddhists, Jains, Parsis and Christians from Afghanistan, Bangladesh and Pakistan who entered
India by 31 December 2014 and meet the statutory conditions.

It does not automatically confer citizenship. Eligible persons must apply and undergo scrutiny.
Its rules were notified in March 2024.

> **Trap:** Eligibility for a facilitated process is not automatic citizenship.
