# MCQ 026

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Coverage point:** Distinction between Articles 2 and 3  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement correctly distinguishes Articles 2 and 3?

A. Article 2 renames existing states, whereas Article 3 admits foreign territories.

B. Article 2 concerns admission or establishment of new states, while Article 3 reorganises
existing states of the Union.

C. Article 2 requires state consent, whereas Article 3 requires approval by every state.

D. Article 2 is an ordinary legislative power, whereas Article 3 always requires Article 368.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

Article 2 authorises Parliament to admit into the Union or establish new states. Article 3
concerns the internal reorganisation of existing states, including forming new states from
existing territory and altering areas, boundaries or names.

Laws under Articles 2 and 3 may contain supplemental changes under Article 4 and are not treated
as Article 368 amendments.

> **Memory:** Article 2 = new from outside; Article 3 = rearrange inside.
