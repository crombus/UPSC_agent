# MCQ 010

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Coverage point:** Parliamentary sovereignty and judicial supremacy  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement best describes the Indian constitutional position?

A. Parliament possesses unlimited sovereignty identical to the British Parliament.

B. India combines elements of parliamentary sovereignty with judicial supremacy.

C. Courts may amend the Constitution without parliamentary action.

D. Judicial review exists only during a national emergency.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

India follows a middle position. Parliament possesses substantial legislative and constituent
power, but its enactments remain subject to the written Constitution, Fundamental Rights,
judicial review and the basic-structure limitation.

Thus, India combines elements of **parliamentary sovereignty** with **judicial supremacy** rather
than adopting either in an absolute form.

> **Trap:** The Indian Parliament is constitutionally limited, unlike the traditional British
> model of unlimited parliamentary sovereignty.
