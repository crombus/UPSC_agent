# MCQ 041

**Topic:** Polity 06 - Citizenship  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Constitutional and statutory citizenship scheme  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Constitution contains a complete and permanent code governing every future mode of
   acquiring and losing Indian citizenship.
2. Article 11 empowers Parliament to regulate citizenship by law.
3. Registration as an Overseas Citizen of India creates constitutionally protected dual
   citizenship.
4. Voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship may result in termination of Indian citizenship
   under the Citizenship Act.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 2 and 4 only  
B. 1, 2 and 4 only  
C. 2 and 3 only  
D. 1 and 3 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 2 and 4 are correct.

- **Statement 1 is incorrect:** Articles 5-11 primarily settled citizenship at the Constitution's
  commencement. They do not contain a complete permanent code for all future cases.
- **Statement 2 is correct:** Article 11 authorises Parliament to regulate citizenship, leading to
  the Citizenship Act, 1955.
- **Statement 3 is incorrect:** OCI is a statutory status, not constitutionally protected dual
  citizenship.
- **Statement 4 is correct:** Voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship may produce
  termination under the statutory scheme.

> **Hard trap:** Articles 5-11 and the Citizenship Act perform different temporal functions.
