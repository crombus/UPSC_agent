# MCQ 032

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Coverage point:** Source of constitutional authority  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

According to the Preamble, the Constitution derives its ultimate authority from:

A. Parliament;

B. the Constituent Assembly;

C. the President of India;

D. the people of India.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Incorrect

The opening words **“We, the People of India”** declare popular sovereignty: the Constitution
derives its ultimate authority from the people.

The Constituent Assembly enacted the document on the people's behalf, while Parliament and the
President exercise only constitutionally conferred powers.

> **Trap:** The institution that drafted or administers the Constitution is not its ultimate
> source of authority.
