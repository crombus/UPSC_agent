# MCQ 004

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Coverage point:** Legal status and amendability of the Preamble  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements concerning the Preamble:

1. It is a part of the Constitution.
2. It is independently enforceable by courts.
3. It can be amended under Article 368, subject to the basic-structure limitation.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1 only  
D. 1 and 3 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

The Supreme Court held in *Kesavananda Bharati* that the Preamble is part of the Constitution.
It may be amended under Article 368, but an amendment cannot damage the Constitution's basic
structure.

The Preamble is **non-justiciable**: it cannot independently create an enforceable claim in
court. It assists constitutional interpretation but is not an autonomous source of legal power.

> **Trap:** “Part of the Constitution” does not mean “independently enforceable.”
