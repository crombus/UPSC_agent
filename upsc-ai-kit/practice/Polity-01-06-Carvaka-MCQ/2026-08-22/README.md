## Method

- One MCQ at a time.
- After each response, record the user's answer, correct answer, result, and explanation.
- Every explanation must include option/statement analysis plus definitions of technical terms,
  relevant timeline, concrete examples and wider constitutional or philosophical context.
- Rotate across all seven topics and progressively cover their complete Core knowledge files.
- Correct-option sequence rotates A, B, C, D.
- Questions 1-40: foundation and medium difficulty.
- Questions 41 onward: hard UPSC-style statements, close options and inter-topic distinctions.
