# MCQ 003

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Coverage point:** Sources of constitutional provisions  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?

A. Fundamental Duties - Ireland  
B. Concurrent List - Canada  
C. Procedure established by law - Japan  
D. Constitutional amendment procedure - Australia

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

India borrowed **“procedure established by law” from Japan**.

The other correct source associations are:

- Fundamental Duties - USSR;
- Concurrent List - Australia;
- constitutional amendment procedure - South Africa.

> **Trap:** Canada supplied the strong-Centre federal model and residuary powers, not the
> Concurrent List.
