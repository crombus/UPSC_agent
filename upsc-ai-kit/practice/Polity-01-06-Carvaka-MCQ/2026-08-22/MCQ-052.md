# MCQ 052

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Independent bodies, local government, co-operatives and emergency design  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Election Commission, Comptroller and Auditor General and Union Public Service Commission
   are constitutional bodies intended to operate with institutional independence.
2. The Seventy-third and Seventy-fourth Amendments constitutionalised rural and urban local
   government respectively.
3. The Ninety-seventh Amendment converted every co-operative society into a subject exclusively
   controlled by Parliament.
4. Emergency provisions illustrate the Constitution's capacity to acquire stronger unitary
   features during exceptional conditions.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2, 3 and 4 only  
C. 1, 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1, 2 and 4 are correct.

### Meaning of the institutions and amendments

- **Constitutional body:** an institution created or expressly recognised by the Constitution,
  whose basic status cannot be removed by an ordinary statute.
- **Institutional independence:** safeguards concerning appointment, tenure, removal, expenditure
  or reporting that reduce day-to-day political control.
- **Third tier:** constitutionally recognised local government below Union and State governments.
- **Unitary tendency:** concentration or expansion of effective power at the Union level.

### Amendment timeline

| Amendment | Year | Principal relevance |
|---|---:|---|
| 73rd | 1992 | Panchayats; Part IX and Eleventh Schedule |
| 74th | 1992 | Municipalities; Part IXA and Twelfth Schedule |
| 97th | 2011 | Co-operative societies; Article 43B, Article 19(1)(c) change and Part IXB |

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** ECI, CAG and UPSC are constitutional institutions with safeguards intended to
   support impartial functioning.  
   **Examples:** ECI supervises elections; CAG audits public finances; UPSC conducts recruitment
   and advises on service matters.
2. **Correct.** The amendments constitutionalised Panchayats and Municipalities.  
   **Example:** They established constitutional frameworks for regular elections, reservations
   and State Finance Commissions.
3. **Incorrect.** The amendment did not transform every co-operative into an exclusively Union
   subject. Co-operative societies remain substantially within the federal distribution of
   legislative power.  
   **Example:** State co-operative societies continue to be regulated under state legislation;
   multi-state co-operatives have a distinct Union-law framework.
4. **Correct.** Emergency provisions can strengthen Union authority.  
   **Example:** During a national emergency, Parliament's legislative reach and the Union's
   executive influence over states may expand under constitutional conditions.

> **Core distinction:** constitutional recognition of an institution does not automatically
> transfer its entire legislative field to Parliament.
