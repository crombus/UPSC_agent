# MCQ 030

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Coverage point:** First meeting and presiding officers  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Who served as the temporary President of the Constituent Assembly at its first meeting?

A. Dr. Rajendra Prasad

B. Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha

C. H.C. Mookherjee

D. Jawaharlal Nehru

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha, the oldest member, served as temporary President at the Constituent
Assembly's first meeting on 9 December 1946. Dr. Rajendra Prasad was subsequently elected its
permanent President.

> **Memory:** Sinha = temporary; Prasad = permanent.
