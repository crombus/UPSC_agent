# MCQ 035

**Topic:** Philosophy - Carvaka  
**Coverage point:** Self-refutation objection  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

What is the central self-refutation objection against the statement “inference is not a valid
source of knowledge”?

A. The statement proves the existence of an eternal soul.

B. The statement is contradicted by every individual perception.

C. It is itself a general claim extending beyond one present perception and therefore appears
to rely upon reasoning or generalisation.

D. It necessarily accepts the infallibility of the Vedas.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

The proposition “all inference is invalid” is not merely a report of one perceived event. It is
a universal claim about every inferential act. Critics argue that Cārvāka must generalise or
reason to defend it and therefore uses the very method being rejected.

A moderate reply is that Cārvāka need not deny every practical inference; it may deny only that
inference supplies exceptionless certainty, especially regarding transcendent entities.

> **Nuance:** The objection is strongest against dogmatic Cārvāka and weaker against Jayarāśi's
> sceptical, opponent-directed criticism.
