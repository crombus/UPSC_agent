# MCQ 072

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Constituent Assembly composition, sovereignty and commencement provisions  
**Status:** Correct

## Question

Consider the following statements regarding the Constituent Assembly and the commencement of the
Constitution:

1. All members of the Constituent Assembly were directly elected through universal adult
   franchise.
2. Representatives of the princely states were to be nominated by their rulers.
3. The Indian Independence Act 1947 made the Constituent Assembly a fully sovereign body capable
   of repealing British laws as applied to India.
4. Article 394 brought certain constitutional provisions into force on 26 November 1949 and the
   remaining provisions into force on 26 January 1950.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 1, 3 and 4 only  
C. 2 and 3 only  
D. 2, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Learner's answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D - Statements 2, 3 and 4 only  
**Result:** Correct

### Statement-by-statement analysis

1. **Incorrect.** The Constituent Assembly was not directly elected through universal adult
   franchise. Provincial representatives were indirectly elected by the provincial legislative
   assemblies through proportional representation by means of the single transferable vote.
   Representatives of princely states were nominated.
2. **Correct.** Under the Cabinet Mission Plan, the princely states were allotted 93 seats and
   their representatives were to be selected through nomination by the rulers rather than through
   direct popular election.
3. **Correct.** Although the Assembly originated under the Cabinet Mission Plan, the Indian
   Independence Act 1947 removed its subordinate status. It could frame any constitution and
   repeal or modify British parliamentary laws applicable to India.
4. **Correct.** Article 394 brought specified transitional provisions into force immediately on
   **26 November 1949**. The remaining provisions commenced on **26 January 1950**.

### Technical terms

- **Universal adult franchise:** every adult citizen has the right to vote subject to lawful
  qualifications; it was not the method used to elect the Constituent Assembly.
- **Proportional representation:** seats are distributed to reflect the relative voting strength
  of groups rather than simply awarding every seat to the largest group.
- **Single transferable vote:** voters rank candidates and votes may transfer according to later
  preferences until seats are filled.
- **Sovereign constituent body:** an assembly possessing final authority to create the
  constitutional order without requiring approval from an external power.
- **Commencement:** the date on which a legal provision becomes operational, distinct from its
  adoption or enactment.

### Timeline and composition

| Date or stage | Development |
|---|---|
| 1946 | Assembly constituted under the Cabinet Mission Plan; original strength 389 |
| 9 December 1946 | First meeting; Sachchidananda Sinha served as temporary President |
| 15 August 1947 | Independence Act made the Assembly sovereign; it also functioned legislatively |
| After Partition | Strength reduced from 389 to 299 |
| 26 November 1949 | Constitution adopted; specified provisions commenced immediately |
| 26 January 1950 | Remaining Constitution commenced; India became a republic |

### Wider constitutional context

The Assembly's democratic legitimacy did not come from direct adult-franchise election. It rested
on its broad deliberations, inclusion of varied groups and the Constitution's commitment to
universal adult franchise for the new republic. Its **origin under a British plan** and its
**sovereign status after 1947** must therefore be kept analytically separate.

> **Article sequence:** **393 = short title; 394 = commencement; 395 = repeal of the Government of
> India Act 1935 and Indian Independence Act 1947.**
