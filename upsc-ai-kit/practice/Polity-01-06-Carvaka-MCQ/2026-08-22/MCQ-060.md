# MCQ 060

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Sovereign, socialist and secular character  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The word “Socialist” was inserted into the Preamble by the Forty-second Amendment.
2. Indian constitutional socialism is generally understood as democratic socialism and does not
   necessarily require complete state ownership of every means of production.
3. Secularism is compatible with state action aimed at social reform and equal religious
   citizenship, subject to constitutional rights.
4. Sovereignty signifies freedom from external control as well as supreme constitutional
   authority within India's legal order.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 2 and 4 only  
C. 1, 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2, 3 and 4

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Incorrect

All four statements are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Sovereign:** India is not legally subordinate to an external power and exercises supreme
  constitutional authority within its territory.
- **Democratic socialism:** pursuit of social and economic justice through constitutional,
  electoral and mixed institutional methods rather than compulsory abolition of all private
  property.
- **Secularism:** equal citizenship and freedom of religion without an official religion, combined
  with constitutionally permissible reform and regulation.
- **Mixed economy:** coexistence of public and private economic activity under regulation and
  welfare commitments.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** “Socialist” was inserted by the Forty-second Amendment Act, 1976.  
   **Example:** The same amendment also inserted “Secular” and “Integrity.”
2. **Correct.** Indian socialism has generally been interpreted as democratic and mixed rather
   than requiring complete state ownership.  
   **Example:** Public-sector enterprises, private businesses, welfare schemes and market
   regulation can coexist.
3. **Correct.** Secularism does not immunise every religiously associated practice from reform.  
   **Example:** The State may pursue equality-oriented social reform or regulate secular
   administration while respecting freedom of conscience and religious practice.
4. **Correct.** Sovereignty has external and internal dimensions.  
   **Example:** Externally, India makes treaties in its own right; internally, every institution,
   including Parliament, functions under India's supreme Constitution rather than foreign law.

### Timeline

```text

1950: Constitution commenced
1976: Socialist + Secular + Integrity inserted
```

> **Hard trap:** constitutional socialism is not identical to complete economic nationalisation,
> and secularism is not identical to absolute state-religion separation.
