# MCQ 011

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Coverage point:** The Forty-second Amendment and the Preamble  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which set of words was inserted into the Preamble by the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment)
Act, 1976?

A. Democratic, Republic and Justice

B. Sovereign, Socialist and Fraternity

C. Socialist, Secular and Integrity

D. Liberty, Equality and Integrity

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

The Forty-second Amendment inserted **Socialist** and **Secular** into the description of India
and changed “unity of the Nation” to “unity and **integrity** of the Nation.”

The Preamble has been amended only once.

> **Memory line:** Forty-second Amendment = Socialist + Secular + Integrity.
