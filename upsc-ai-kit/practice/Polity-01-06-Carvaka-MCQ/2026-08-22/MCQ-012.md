# MCQ 012

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Coverage point:** Cession of Indian territory  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

According to the constitutional position associated with the Berubari Union case, the cession
of Indian territory to a foreign country requires:

A. an executive order of the Union Government alone;

B. an ordinary law under Article 3;

C. approval of the affected State Legislature alone;

D. a constitutional amendment under Article 368.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

The *Berubari Union* advisory opinion distinguished the internal reorganisation of states under
Article 3 from the **cession of Indian territory to a foreign state**. Cession requires a
constitutional amendment under Article 368.

The Ninth Amendment was enacted to implement the relevant India-Pakistan territorial agreement.

> **Trap:** Article 3 changes India's internal political map; it is not sufficient for foreign
> cession.
