# MCQ 044

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Composition, changing status and enactment of the Constituent Assembly  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Constituent Assembly was formed under the Cabinet Mission Plan and combined indirectly
   elected provincial representatives with nominated princely-state representatives.
2. After the Indian Independence Act, 1947, the Assembly became a sovereign constitution-making
   body and also performed legislative functions.
3. Every provision of the Constitution commenced immediately upon its adoption on
   26 November 1949.
4. The original Drafting Committee consisted of seven members.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 3 only  
B. 2, 3 and 4 only  
C. 1, 2 and 3 only  
D. 1, 2 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1, 2 and 4 are correct.

### Meaning and institutional context

- **Indirect election:** Provincial representatives were chosen by members of the provincial
  legislative assemblies rather than directly by the entire adult population.
- **Nomination:** Princely states were allotted representatives who were to be nominated rather
  than elected through the provincial method.
- **Sovereign Constituent Assembly:** After the Indian Independence Act, 1947, the Assembly no
  longer worked under the limitations of the Cabinet Mission framework or British parliamentary
  authority. It could frame any constitution and repeal or modify inherited laws.
- **Dual role:** It framed the Constitution and also functioned as the Dominion Legislature.

### Timeline

| Date | Development |
|---|---|
| 1946 | Constituted under the Cabinet Mission Plan; original strength 389 |
| 9 December 1946 | First meeting |
| 15 August 1947 | Became sovereign after independence; strength after Partition 299 |
| 26 November 1949 | Constitution adopted |
| 26 January 1950 | Most provisions commenced |

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Provincial members were indirectly elected and princely-state representatives
   were nominated.  
   **Example:** This is why the Assembly was not based on universal adult franchise despite later
   creating universal adult franchise for independent India.
2. **Correct.** After independence it combined constitution-making and ordinary legislative work.  
   **Example:** Dr. Rajendra Prasad presided over constitution-making, while legislative business
   was handled separately under the Assembly's legislative arrangement associated with
   G.V. Mavalankar.
3. **Incorrect.** The Constitution was adopted on 26 November 1949, but most provisions commenced
   on 26 January 1950. Certain citizenship, election and transitional provisions came into force
   immediately to prepare for the new constitutional order.  
   **Example:** Adoption created the document; commencement made the new republic fully operative.
4. **Correct.** The original Drafting Committee had seven members, chaired by B.R. Ambedkar.  
   **Example:** N. Madhava Rau later replaced B.L. Mitter, and T.T. Krishnamachari replaced
   D.P. Khaitan.

> **Hard distinction:** constitution-making authority, legislative authority, adoption and
> commencement are four related but separate ideas.
