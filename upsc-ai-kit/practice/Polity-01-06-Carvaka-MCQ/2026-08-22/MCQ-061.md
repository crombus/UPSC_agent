# MCQ 061

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Territorial reorganisation milestones and enclave exchange  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Constitution (One Hundredth Amendment) Act, 2015 facilitated the India-Bangladesh land
   boundary and enclave exchange.
2. In 2019, the former State of Jammu and Kashmir was reorganised into the Union Territories of
   Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh.
3. Telangana was created in 2000 along with Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand and Jharkhand.
4. Maharashtra and Gujarat were created by the States Reorganisation Act, 1956.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 1, 2 and 3 only  
C. 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1 and 2 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Enclave:** a piece of one state's territory completely surrounded by another state's
  territory.
- **Territorial reorganisation:** creation, merger, division, boundary alteration or status
  change of states and Union Territories.
- **Constitutional amendment for boundary settlement:** amendment used when implementing an
  international territorial arrangement affects India's constitutional territory.

### Timeline

| Year | Development |
|---:|---|
| 1953 | Andhra State created |
| 1956 | States Reorganisation Act and Seventh Amendment |
| 1960 | Bombay State divided into Maharashtra and Gujarat |
| 2000 | Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand and Jharkhand created |
| 2014 | Telangana created |
| 2015 | One Hundredth Amendment; India-Bangladesh land-boundary/enclave arrangement |
| 2019 | J&K reorganised into J&K and Ladakh Union Territories |
| 2020 | Dadra and Nagar Haveli merged with Daman and Diu into one UT |

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The One Hundredth Amendment enabled implementation of the India-Bangladesh land
   boundary arrangement, including enclave exchange.  
   **Example:** Residents of formerly isolated enclaves could become territorially connected to
   the surrounding state after the exchange.
2. **Correct.** The former state became two Union Territories in 2019.  
   **Example:** Jammu and Kashmir has a legislature under its statutory arrangement, while Ladakh
   is a Union Territory without one.
3. **Incorrect.** Telangana was created in 2014. The three states created in 2000 were
   Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand and Jharkhand.  
   **Example:** Telangana was separated from Andhra Pradesh.
4. **Incorrect.** Maharashtra and Gujarat were created in 1960 through the Bombay Reorganisation
   Act, not the 1956 Act.  
   **Example:** The bilingual Bombay State was divided mainly along Marathi- and Gujarati-speaking
   lines.

> **Hard trap:** 1956 was the general reorganisation; major later state formations have their own
> dates and enactments.
