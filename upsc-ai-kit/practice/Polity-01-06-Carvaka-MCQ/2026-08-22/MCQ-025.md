# MCQ 025

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Coverage point:** Supreme Court decisions on the Preamble  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement correctly reflects the ruling in *Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala*
(1973)?

A. The Preamble is part of the Constitution and can be amended subject to the basic-structure
limitation.

B. The Preamble is not part of the Constitution and can never be used in interpretation.

C. The Preamble is independently enforceable as a source of Fundamental Rights.

D. Parliament may amend the Preamble without any constitutional limitation.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

*Kesavananda Bharati* established that the Preamble is part of the Constitution. Parliament may
amend it under Article 368, but cannot damage the Constitution's basic structure.

The decision departed from the earlier *Berubari Union* view that the Preamble was not part of
the Constitution. The Preamble remains non-justiciable and does not independently create
enforceable rights.

> **Trap:** Interpretive importance does not make the Preamble independently enforceable.
