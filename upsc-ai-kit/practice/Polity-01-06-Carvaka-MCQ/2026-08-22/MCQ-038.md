# MCQ 038

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Coverage point:** Federal structure with unitary bias  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which description most accurately captures the structural character of the Indian Constitution?

A. A confederation formed by a revocable agreement among sovereign states

B. A federal system with a strong unitary or centralising bias

C. A completely unitary system with no constitutional division of powers

D. A presidential federation modelled exactly on the United States

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

India possesses core federal features, including a written and supreme Constitution, a
constitutional division of legislative powers and an independent judiciary. At the same time,
the Centre is comparatively strong through residuary powers, emergency provisions, a single
Constitution and Parliament's power to reorganise states.

It is therefore commonly described as **federal with a unitary bias** or quasi-federal.

> **Trap:** A strong Centre does not eliminate the Constitution's genuine federal division of
> authority.
