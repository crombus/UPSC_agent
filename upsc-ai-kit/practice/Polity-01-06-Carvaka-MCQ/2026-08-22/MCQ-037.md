# MCQ 037

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Coverage point:** Origin of the Constituent Assembly proposal  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Who first advanced the idea of a Constituent Assembly for India in 1934?

A. M.N. Roy

B. Jawaharlal Nehru

C. B.R. Ambedkar

D. Mahatma Gandhi

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

M.N. Roy first proposed the idea of a Constituent Assembly for India in 1934. The Indian
National Congress officially demanded such an Assembly later, and it was eventually constituted
under the Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946.

> **Trap:** Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution; he was not the first person to propose the
> Constituent Assembly.
