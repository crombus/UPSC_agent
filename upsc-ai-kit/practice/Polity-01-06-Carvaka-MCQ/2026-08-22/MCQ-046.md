# MCQ 046

**Topic:** Polity 04 - Preamble  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Interpretation, enforceability and amendment  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. Courts may use the Preamble to interpret an ambiguous constitutional provision.
2. The Preamble independently grants legislative power to Parliament.
3. Because the Preamble is non-justiciable, its values have no constitutional relevance.
4. The Preamble can be amended, but an amendment cannot destroy the Constitution's basic
   structure.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 4 only  
B. 1 and 4 only  
C. 2 and 3 only  
D. 1, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1 and 4 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Interpretive aid:** a constitutional value or principle used to choose between competing
  meanings when legal text is uncertain.
- **Non-justiciable:** not independently enforceable as a freestanding claim in court.
- **Legislative competence:** constitutional authority empowering a legislature to enact a law.
- **Basic structure:** foundational features that Parliament cannot destroy even through an
  Article 368 amendment.

### Case-law timeline

| Year | Case | Position |
|---:|---|---|
| 1960 | *Berubari Union* | Preamble not treated as part of the Constitution |
| 1973 | *Kesavananda Bharati* | Preamble is part; amendable subject to basic structure |
| 1995 | *LIC of India* | Reaffirmed its integral constitutional status |

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Courts may consult the Preamble where constitutional language is ambiguous.  
   **Example:** The values of justice, liberty, equality and fraternity help illuminate the
   Constitution's overall purpose when two interpretations are textually possible.
2. **Incorrect.** Parliament's powers come from substantive constitutional provisions, not from
   the Preamble.  
   **Example:** Parliament legislates through Articles 245-246 and the legislative lists; it
   cannot cite the word “socialist” alone as an independent source of legislative competence.
3. **Incorrect.** Non-justiciability removes independent enforceability, not constitutional
   significance.  
   **Example:** A person cannot sue merely for “fraternity,” but courts can use fraternity and
   dignity as interpretive values.
4. **Correct.** The Forty-second Amendment proves the Preamble is amendable, but
   *Kesavananda Bharati* prevents destruction of its basic features.  
   **Example:** Parliament inserted Socialist, Secular and Integrity in 1976; it could not
   constitutionally replace democratic republic with hereditary monarchy.

> **Core distinction:** the Preamble guides and limits interpretation, but does not independently
> create legislative powers or enforceable causes of action.
