# MCQ 015

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Coverage point:** Residuary powers under the Government of India Act, 1935  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Under the Government of India Act, 1935, residuary legislative powers were vested in:

A. the federal legislature;

B. the provincial legislatures;

C. the Governor-General;

D. the British Parliament exclusively.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

The Government of India Act, 1935 created Federal, Provincial and Concurrent Lists, but vested
the residuary legislative power in the **Governor-General**.

This differs from the present Constitution, under which Parliament ordinarily possesses
residuary legislative competence.

> **Trap:** Do not project the present constitutional distribution backward onto the 1935 Act.
