# MCQ 009

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Coverage point:** Objectives Resolution  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement concerning the Objectives Resolution is correct?

A. Jawaharlal Nehru moved it on 13 December 1946; it was adopted on 22 January 1947, and
its modified form informed the Preamble.

B. B.R. Ambedkar moved it after becoming Chairman of the Drafting Committee.

C. It was adopted only after the Constitution had been enacted on 26 November 1949.

D. It primarily prescribed the detailed membership of the Drafting Committee.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

Jawaharlal Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution on 13 December 1946. The Constituent Assembly
adopted it on 22 January 1947, and its modified form supplied the philosophical foundation of
the Preamble.

B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee, but he did not move the Objectives Resolution.

> **Trap:** Nehru = Objectives Resolution; Ambedkar = Drafting Committee.
