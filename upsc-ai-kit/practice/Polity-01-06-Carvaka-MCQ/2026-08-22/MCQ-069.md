# MCQ 069

**Topic:** Polity 06 - Citizenship  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Renunciation, termination, deprivation and OCI cancellation  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. Renunciation is a voluntary mode of giving up Indian citizenship.
2. Termination may follow the voluntary acquisition of citizenship of another country.
3. Deprivation means only that a citizen voluntarily returns a citizenship certificate.
4. OCI registration is constitutionally permanent and cannot be cancelled on statutory grounds.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 1, 2 and 3 only  
C. 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

Statements 1 and 2 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Renunciation:** voluntary declaration by an Indian citizen giving up citizenship.
- **Termination:** statutory loss resulting from voluntary acquisition of another country's
  citizenship, subject to the governing law and factual determination.
- **Deprivation:** compulsory cancellation by the Central Government on specified statutory
  grounds; it is not voluntary surrender.
- **OCI cancellation:** withdrawal of Overseas Citizen of India registration under statutory
  grounds; OCI is not constitutionally permanent citizenship.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Renunciation begins with the citizen's voluntary act.  
   **Example:** An adult acquiring a settled legal status abroad may formally declare
   renunciation in the prescribed manner.
2. **Correct.** Voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship may terminate Indian citizenship.  
   **Example:** India does not generally recognise full dual citizenship under the Citizenship
   Act.
3. **Incorrect.** Deprivation is an involuntary governmental measure.  
   **Examples:** Citizenship acquired through fraud or concealment, serious disloyalty, or other
   specified statutory conduct may trigger deprivation proceedings, subject to legal procedure.
4. **Incorrect.** OCI is a statutory registration and may be cancelled on statutory grounds.  
   **Example:** Fraud in obtaining OCI registration can support cancellation; the person cannot
   argue that OCI is an irrevocable constitutional citizenship.

### Comparison

```text
Renunciation = citizen chooses
Termination  = foreign citizenship triggers statutory loss
Deprivation  = government compulsorily removes on specified grounds
OCI cancellation = statutory status withdrawn; not loss of full Indian citizenship
```

> **Core trap:** citizenship deprivation and OCI cancellation concern different legal statuses.
