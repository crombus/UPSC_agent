# MCQ 002

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Coverage point:** Composition of the Constituent Assembly  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which statement correctly describes the original method of constituting India's Constituent
Assembly?

A. All its members were directly elected through universal adult franchise.  
B. Provincial representatives were indirectly elected, while princely-state representatives were nominated.  
C. It was created by the Indian Independence Act, 1947, with an original strength of 299.  
D. All its members were nominated by the British Government.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

The Constituent Assembly was constituted under the Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946. Representatives
of British Indian provinces were **indirectly elected by the provincial legislative assemblies**,
while princely-state representatives were nominated.

The Assembly was not elected through universal adult franchise. Its original strength was 389,
which fell to 299 after Partition.

> **Trap:** The Indian Independence Act made the Assembly sovereign, but it did not originally
> create the Assembly.
