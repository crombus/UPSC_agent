## Question

Which constitutional amendment reduced the voting age for Lok Sabha and State Assembly
elections from 21 years to 18 years?

A. Sixty-first Amendment

B. Forty-second Amendment

C. Seventy-third Amendment

D. Ninety-seventh Amendment

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

The Constitution (Sixty-first Amendment) Act, 1988 reduced the minimum voting age from 21 to
18 years.

- Forty-second Amendment: major constitutional changes; added Socialist, Secular and Integrity.
- Seventy-third Amendment: constitutional status for Panchayats.
- Ninety-seventh Amendment: co-operative societies.

> **Memory:** 61st = voting age 21 to 18.
