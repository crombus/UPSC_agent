# MCQ 059

**Topic:** Polity 03 - Salient Features  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Parliamentary government, secularism, judicial independence and franchise  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. In India's parliamentary system, the Council of Ministers is collectively responsible to the
   Lok Sabha.
2. Indian secularism constitutionally requires the State to have no engagement whatsoever with
   any religious institution or practice.
3. Security of tenure and protected service conditions contribute to judicial independence.
4. The Constitution originally fixed the voting age at eighteen years.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 2 and 4 only  
C. 1 and 3 only  
D. 1, 3 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1 and 3 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Collective responsibility:** the Council of Ministers acts as a unit and remains in office
  only while it enjoys the confidence of the Lok Sabha.
- **Parliamentary executive:** the nominal and real executive are distinguished; the President is
  constitutional head while the Prime Minister and Council of Ministers exercise real executive
  authority.
- **Secularism:** equal citizenship and religious freedom without an official state religion;
  it does not always require an absolute wall preventing every state-religion interaction.
- **Judicial independence:** institutional ability of judges to decide according to law without
  improper executive, legislative or private pressure.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Article 75(3) makes the Council collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha.  
   **Example:** Loss of a confidence vote ordinarily requires the ministry to resign.
2. **Incorrect.** Indian secularism permits constitutionally justified engagement to protect
   freedom, equality and reform.  
   **Example:** The State may regulate secular administration associated with religious
   institutions or enact social-reform laws while remaining bound by religious-freedom guarantees.
3. **Correct.** Tenure, difficult removal procedures and protection of service conditions reduce
   political retaliation.  
   **Example:** A Supreme Court judge cannot be removed through an ordinary executive order.
4. **Incorrect.** The original voting age was twenty-one. The Sixty-first Amendment Act, 1988
   reduced it to eighteen.  
   **Example:** The change expanded the electorate to younger adult citizens.

### Timeline

```text
1950: Voting age 21
1976: Socialist and Secular inserted in Preamble
1988: 61st Amendment lowers voting age to 18
```

> **Hard trap:** secular government may engage with religion for reform or equality; what it
> cannot do is establish religious domination or deny equal citizenship.
