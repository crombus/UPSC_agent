# MCQ 068

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Princely-state integration and territorial facts  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. At independence, the territorial-integration problem involved more than five hundred princely
   states.
2. Hyderabad, Junagadh and Jammu and Kashmir presented especially difficult accession questions.
3. United Provinces was renamed Uttar Pradesh in 1950.
4. The One Hundredth Amendment concerned implementation of an India-Bangladesh territorial
   arrangement.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 2 and 4 only  
C. 1, 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2, 3 and 4

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Incorrect

All four statements are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Princely state:** an internally governed territory under a ruler but subject to British
  paramountcy before independence.
- **Accession:** formal act by which a princely state joined a dominion on specified subjects,
  followed by deeper constitutional integration.
- **Territorial amendment:** constitutional change required to implement certain international
  boundary arrangements.

### Integration context and examples

The standard account records **552 princely states**. Most joined India through Instruments of
Accession and later merger/integration arrangements.

- **Junagadh:** its ruler opted for Pakistan despite geographical circumstances and a largely
  non-Muslim population; subsequent developments included Indian administration and a plebiscite.
- **Hyderabad:** the Nizam sought independence; integration followed the 1948 police action known
  as Operation Polo.
- **Jammu and Kashmir:** the ruler signed the Instrument of Accession in October 1947 amid invasion
  by tribal forces supported from Pakistan.

### Statement-by-statement analysis

1. **Correct.** The problem involved more than five hundred princely states.
2. **Correct.** The three named states produced distinct and difficult accession disputes.
3. **Correct.** United Provinces became Uttar Pradesh in 1950.  
   **Example:** This is identified as the first state-renaming milestone in the Core file.
4. **Correct.** The One Hundredth Amendment enabled the India-Bangladesh land-boundary and enclave
   arrangement.  
   **Example:** It regularised territories that were geographically surrounded by the other
   country.

> **Core distinction:** accession integrated formerly princely political units; later Article 3
> reorganisation rearranged states already within the constitutional Union.
