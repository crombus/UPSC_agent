# MCQ 065

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Duration, sessions, Drafting Committee work and original structure  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Constituent Assembly took approximately 2 years, 11 months and 18 days to complete the
   Constitution.
2. It conducted its work across 11 sessions.
3. The Drafting Committee sat for 141 separate sessions.
4. The Constitution as originally adopted contained 448 Articles and 12 Schedules.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 1, 2 and 3 only  
C. 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1 and 2 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Assembly session:** a period during which the full Constituent Assembly met for deliberation.
- **Drafting Committee sitting day:** a day on which the Committee worked; this is not the same as
  a separate full Assembly session.
- **Original constitutional structure:** the Parts, Articles and Schedules contained in the
  Constitution at adoption, before later amendments inserted, omitted or renumbered provisions.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** The process lasted 2 years, 11 months and 18 days from the first meeting to
   adoption.  
   **Example:** It began on 9 December 1946 and culminated in adoption on 26 November 1949.
2. **Correct.** The Assembly worked through 11 sessions.  
   **Example:** Different sessions addressed objectives, committee reports, the draft and
   clause-by-clause debate.
3. **Incorrect.** The Drafting Committee sat for **141 days**, not 141 separate Assembly-style
   sessions.  
   **Example:** Multiple working days could belong to the same broader phase of drafting.
4. **Incorrect.** The original Constitution contained **395 Articles, 22 Parts and 8 Schedules**.  
   **Example:** Twelve Schedules reflect later constitutional development, not the 1949 document.

### Compact fact box

| Item | Correct fact |
|---|---|
| Assembly duration | 2 years, 11 months, 18 days |
| Full sessions | 11 |
| Drafting Committee work | 141 days |
| Original structure | 395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules |

> **Hard trap:** Similar-looking units—sessions, days, Articles and present-day Schedules—must not
> be mixed.
