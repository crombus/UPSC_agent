# MCQ 040

**Topic:** Polity 05 - Union and Territory  
**Coverage point:** First linguistic state  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Which was the first state created primarily on a linguistic basis after independence?

A. Gujarat

B. Maharashtra

C. Kerala

D. Andhra State

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

Andhra State was created in 1953 following the movement associated with Potti Sriramulu and
became the first state formed primarily on a linguistic basis after independence.

The broader reorganisation of states followed the Fazl Ali Commission and the States
Reorganisation Act, 1956.

> **Trap:** Andhra State was created in 1953; the general linguistic reorganisation occurred in
> 1956.
