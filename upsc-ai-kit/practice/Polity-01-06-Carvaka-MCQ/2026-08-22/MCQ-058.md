# MCQ 058

**Topic:** Polity 02 - Making of the Constitution  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Coverage point:** Criticisms and changing authority of the Constituent Assembly  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Consider the following statements:

1. The Constituent Assembly was criticised because it was not directly elected through universal
   adult franchise.
2. The dominance of the Indian National Congress was cited by critics questioning its
   representativeness.
3. Representatives of the princely states were directly elected by their adult populations.
4. The Cabinet Mission Plan made the Assembly legally sovereign and completely independent of
   British authority from its first meeting.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3 only  
B. 1 and 2 only  
C. 3 and 4 only  
D. 1, 2 and 4 only

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statements 1 and 2 are correct.

### Meaning of the terms

- **Representative body:** an institution whose membership reflects relevant sections of the
  population and whose authority can plausibly be traced to them.
- **Universal adult franchise:** direct voting rights for all eligible adult citizens without
  property, education or similar qualifications.
- **Nomination:** selection without an electoral contest.
- **Sovereign constitution-making body:** a body legally free to determine the Constitution
  without requiring approval from an external superior authority.

### Statement-by-statement analysis with examples

1. **Correct.** Provincial representatives were indirectly elected by legislatures elected under
   a restricted franchise, not by the entire adult population.  
   **Example:** The Assembly nevertheless chose universal adult franchise for the Republic,
   extending political participation far beyond the method used to constitute itself.
2. **Correct.** Congress possessed an overwhelming presence.  
   **Example:** Critics called the Assembly a Congress-dominated body, although Congress itself
   contained ideological, regional and social diversity and the Assembly also included minority,
   Scheduled Caste and women members.
3. **Incorrect.** Princely-state representatives were nominated.  
   **Example:** They were not selected through direct elections conducted among all adults of the
   princely states.
4. **Incorrect.** Under the Cabinet Mission framework, the Assembly was not initially a fully
   sovereign body independent of British legal authority. It became sovereign after the Indian
   Independence Act, 1947.  
   **Example:** After 15 August 1947, it could alter inherited constitutional laws without British
   parliamentary approval.

### Criticism and reply

| Criticism | Reply |
|---|---|
| Not directly elected | Direct nationwide adult-franchise elections were institutionally unavailable; provincial election supplied an indirect link |
| Congress dominated | Congress contained diverse tendencies and extensive debate occurred |
| Partly nominated | Princely-state nomination was a genuine representational weakness |
| Took too long | Comparative reply: drafting a detailed constitution for a vast, diverse country required deliberation |

> **Balanced verdict:** The Assembly was not democratically perfect in origin, but its public
> deliberations, diversity and adoption of universal franchise strengthened the democratic
> legitimacy of its product.
