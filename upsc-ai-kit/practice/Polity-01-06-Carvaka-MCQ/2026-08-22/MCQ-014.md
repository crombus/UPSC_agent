# MCQ 014

**Topic:** Philosophy - Carvaka  
**Coverage point:** Rejection of verbal testimony  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

Why does Carvaka refuse to treat verbal testimony as an independent pramana?

A. Words can refer only to objects that do not exist.

B. Moving from a speaker's words to their truth requires an unverified judgment about the
speaker's competence and honesty.

C. Carvaka denies that human beings communicate through language.

D. All testimony is necessarily contradicted by later perception.

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

Testimony directly establishes only that certain words were spoken. To accept those words as
true, one must judge that the speaker is knowledgeable, honest and reliable. Cārvāka argues
that this judgment depends upon inference, whose certainty is already disputed.

Cārvāka can still use language practically; the objection concerns testimony's status as an
independent and truth-guaranteeing pramāṇa.

> **Trap:** Rejecting testimony as a pramāṇa is not the same as denying the possibility of
> communication.
