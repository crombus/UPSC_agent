# MCQ 001

**Topic:** Polity 01 - Historical Background  
**Coverage point:** Government of India Act, 1935 - proposed federation and reserved subjects  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

With reference to the Government of India Act, 1935, consider the following statements:

1. It provided for an All-India Federation consisting of British Indian provinces and princely states.
2. Under its federal scheme, defence and external affairs were placed under the control of the federal legislature.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

A. 1 only  
B. 2 only  
C. Both 1 and 2  
D. Neither 1 nor 2

## Response and explanation

**Your answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

Statement 1 is correct. The Government of India Act, 1935 **provided for** an All-India
Federation comprising British Indian provinces and princely states. The federation never
actually commenced because the required princely states did not accede.

Statement 2 is incorrect. Defence and external affairs were **reserved subjects** administered
by the Governor-General with councillors. They were not placed under the responsible control
of the federal legislature.

> **Trap:** A provision may be legally present in an Act even if it never becomes operational.
