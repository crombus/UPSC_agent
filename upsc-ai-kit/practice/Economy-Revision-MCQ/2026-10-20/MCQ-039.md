# Polity 15 — President and Vice-President — Diagnostic Question 1

**Learner answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

Which statement correctly distinguishes the electoral colleges of the President and Vice-President of India?

A. Both are elected by elected MPs and elected MLAs, with nominated members excluded.
B. The President is elected by elected MPs and elected MLAs; the Vice-President is elected by members of both Houses of Parliament, elected and nominated, with MLAs excluded.
C. The President is elected by all MPs and all MLAs, including nominated members; the Vice-President is elected only by elected MPs.
D. The President is elected only by elected MPs; the Vice-President is elected by elected MPs and elected MLAs.

**Source:** `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Polity/basic/President-and-Vice-President.md`, snapshot; compare Articles 54 and 66.

**Explanation**
- **A — Incorrect:** The President's electoral college includes elected MLAs, but the Vice-President's does not; nominated MPs may vote in the Vice-Presidential election.
- **B — Correct:** Under Article 54, the President is elected by elected MPs and elected MLAs (including the relevant Union Territories). Under Article 66, the Vice-President is elected by members of both Houses of Parliament, elected and nominated; State legislators are excluded.
- **C — Incorrect:** Nominated MPs and MLAs do not participate in electing the President; the Vice-President's electoral college includes nominated MPs.
- **D — Incorrect:** It excludes elected MLAs from the President's electoral college and wrongly includes MLAs in the Vice-President's.

**Relevant terms:** The President's election uses an electoral college of elected representatives of Parliament and the States; the Vice-President is elected by the parliamentary members of both Houses. The two electoral colleges are not identical.

**Examiner trap:** “Nominated members excluded” is true for the Presidential electoral college, but not for the Vice-Presidential one. Also distinguish election from impeachment/removal: nominated MPs may participate in the latter.
