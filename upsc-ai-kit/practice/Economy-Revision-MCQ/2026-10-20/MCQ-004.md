# Economy 01 — Question 4

**Learner answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

Suppose gross domestic product is 500 and consumption of fixed capital is 40. Which conclusion follows without foreign-income information?

A. GNP equals 460  
B. GNI equals 540  
C. NDP equals 460  
D. NNP equals 460

**Explanation:** Net domestic product (NDP) = GDP − consumption of fixed capital = 500 − 40 = 460. Net national product (NNP) also requires net primary income from abroad, which is not supplied.

- **A:** Incorrect; GNP requires the national-income adjustment, not depreciation.
- **B:** Incorrect; the given figures do not determine GNI.
- **C:** Correct; this follows directly from GDP less depreciation.
- **D:** Incorrect; NNP needs both depreciation and net primary income from abroad.

**Examiner trap:** Gross/net and domestic/national are separate distinctions.
