# Polity 12 — Federal System — Diagnostic Question 1

**Learner answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

Which statement best describes India's federal structure?

A. The Constitution divides powers between the Union and States, but the system is Union-weighted; India is described as a “Union of States.”
B. The Constitution uses the word “federation” and treats States as parties to a compact with a right to secede.
C. The Union and States have identical powers, and residuary legislative powers belong to the States.
D. India is a unitary state because the Constitution contains no division of legislative powers.

**Source:** `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Polity/basic/Federal-System.md`, snapshot and core idea; compare Article 1 and the Seventh Schedule.

**Explanation**
- **A — Correct:** India has a federal division of powers in the Constitution, but several features give the Union a stronger position. Article 1 calls India a “Union of States”; residuary legislative powers belong to Parliament, and States have no right to secede.
- **B — Incorrect:** The Constitution does not use the word “federation” in Article 1, and Indian States are not parties to a compact carrying a right to secede.
- **C — Incorrect:** Union and State powers are not identical. Residuary legislative powers belong to the Union, unlike the United States model.
- **D — Incorrect:** The Constitution expressly divides legislative powers through the Union, State and Concurrent Lists in the Seventh Schedule. India is federal in structure, though Union-weighted.

**Relevant terms:** **Federalism** constitutionally divides authority between levels of government. **Union-weighted** describes the centralising features within India's federal structure; it does not mean the States are mere administrative appendages. In *S.R. Bommai v. Union of India* (1994), federalism was recognised as a basic feature of the Constitution.

**Examiner trap:** Avoid the false binary “fully symmetric federation or unitary state.” India combines a constitutional division of powers with a strong Union.
