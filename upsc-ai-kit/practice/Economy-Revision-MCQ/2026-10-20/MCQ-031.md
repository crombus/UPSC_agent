Under which Article is the Union Government's Annual Financial Statement laid before Parliament?

A. Article 112
B. Article 280
C. Article 151
D. Article 110

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Economy/09-Union-Budget-Fiscal-Policy-and-Deficit-Indicators/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and `ANSWERS.md`, Q1; the constitutional provision and the topic's canonical Basic owner.

**Explanation**
- **A — Correct:** Article 112 requires the President to cause the Annual Financial Statement (AFS) to be laid before both Houses of Parliament for each financial year. It presents estimated receipts and expenditure.
- **B — Incorrect:** Article 280 provides for the Finance Commission.
- **C — Incorrect:** Article 151 concerns the reports of the Comptroller and Auditor General.
- **D — Incorrect:** Article 110 defines a Money Bill; it does not prescribe the AFS.

**Relevant terms:** The AFS is commonly called the Union Budget, though the Budget includes other statements and proposals as well. The AFS is a constitutional financial statement, not the same as an Appropriation Account or the Finance Commission's report.

**Examiner trap:** Distinguish the constitutional Budget document (Article 112) from related fiscal documents and oversight reports.
