# Polity 05 — Union and its Territory — Diagnostic Question 1

**Learner answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Correct

Which statement best reflects the constitutional distinction between the territory of India and the Union of India?

A. They are interchangeable terms, and both include only the States listed in the First Schedule.
B. The territory of India includes States, Union territories and territories that may be acquired; the Union of India refers to the federal union of States.
C. The Union of India includes Union territories, while the territory of India excludes them.
D. The territory of India includes only land governed directly by the Union.

**Source:** `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Polity/basic/Union-and-Territory.md`, Article 1 snapshot and core explanation.

**Explanation**
- **A — Incorrect:** The terms are not interchangeable. The Union of India refers to the federal union of States; the territory of India is broader.
- **B — Correct:** Article 1 describes the territory of India as comprising the States, Union territories and any territories that may be acquired. “Union of India” refers to the federation of States.
- **C — Incorrect:** It reverses the distinction: Union territories are included in the territory of India.
- **D — Incorrect:** The territory of India is not limited to land administered directly by the Union; it includes States as well as Union territories and acquired territories.

**Relevant context:** Article 1 names India as a “Union of States.” Article 2 concerns admission or establishment of new States, Article 3 enables Parliament to reorganise existing States, and Article 4 provides that laws under Articles 2 and 3 are not constitutional amendments under Article 368.

**Examiner trap:** Keep the geographical scope of “territory of India” distinct from the federal composition denoted by “Union of India.”
