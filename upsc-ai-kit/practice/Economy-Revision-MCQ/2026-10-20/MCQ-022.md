Which description of the Reserve Bank of India is most accurate?

A. A private company regulated by SEBI
B. A department of the Ministry of Finance
C. A constitutional authority created by the Constitution
D. A statutory central bank created under the RBI Act, 1934

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Economy/04-RBI-Monetary-Policy-and-Liquidity-Management/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and `ANSWERS.md`, Q1; `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Economy/basic/04_RBI-Monetary-Policy-and-Liquidity-Management.md` §§4, 6.

**Explanation**
- **A — Incorrect:** The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regulates securities markets; it does not own or regulate the RBI as a private company.
- **B — Incorrect:** The RBI is not a department of the Ministry of Finance. It is India's central bank, with statutory responsibilities and institutional functions.
- **C — Incorrect:** The Constitution does not create the RBI. Its statutory basis is the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.
- **D — Correct:** The RBI is a statutory central bank established under the RBI Act, 1934. It began operations on 1 April 1935 and was nationalised on 1 January 1949.

**Relevant terms and context:** “Statutory” means established and governed by legislation; “constitutional” means created or directly provided for by the Constitution. The RBI's central-banking functions include monetary policy, currency management and acting as banker to government and banks, within its legal mandate.

**Examiner trap:** Do not confuse statutory status with constitutional status, or mistake a central bank's governmental role for being a ministry department.
