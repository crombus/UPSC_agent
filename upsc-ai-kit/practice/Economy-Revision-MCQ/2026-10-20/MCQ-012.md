# Economy 01 — Question 12

**Learner answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Incorrect

Which pairing correctly classifies a weaver's productive resources?

A. Yarn—fixed capital; loom—working capital  
B. Yarn—fixed capital; loom—fixed capital  
C. Yarn—working capital; loom—fixed capital  
D. Yarn—working capital; loom—working capital

**Explanation:** Yarn is used up or transformed during production, so it is working capital. A loom is a durable asset used repeatedly across production cycles, so it is fixed capital.

- **A:** Incorrect; both classifications are reversed.
- **B:** Incorrect; yarn is not fixed capital.
- **C:** Correct; yarn is working capital and the loom is fixed capital.
- **D:** Incorrect; the loom is a durable fixed asset, not working capital.

**Examiner trap:** Distinguish inputs consumed in production from durable assets used repeatedly.
