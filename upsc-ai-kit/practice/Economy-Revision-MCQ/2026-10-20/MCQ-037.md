# Polity 13 — Centre-State and Inter-State Relations — Diagnostic Question 1

**Learner answer:** C
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

Which statement correctly describes the status and establishment of NITI Aayog?

A. It is a constitutional body established by Article 263 to adjudicate inter-State disputes.
B. It is a statutory body established by Parliament in 2015 to allocate tax devolution.
C. It was established by an executive resolution in 2015 as an advisory think tank and is neither constitutional nor statutory.
D. It is a constitutional body under Article 280 that replaced the Finance Commission.

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Polity/13-Centre-State-and-Inter-State-Relations/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and `ANSWERS.md`, Q4; canonical Centre-State relations owner. Options reordered to vary the key position in this mixed session.

**Explanation**
- **A — Incorrect:** Article 263 provides for an Inter-State Council; it does not establish NITI Aayog or make it an adjudicatory body.
- **B — Incorrect:** NITI Aayog was not created by an Act of Parliament and does not allocate tax devolution. That is among the Finance Commission's roles under Article 280.
- **C — Correct:** NITI Aayog was established by an executive resolution in 2015 as an advisory think tank. It is neither a constitutional nor statutory body.
- **D — Incorrect:** Article 280 establishes the Finance Commission. NITI Aayog did not replace it.

**Relevant terms:** **Constitutional body** is created or provided for by the Constitution; **statutory body** is established by legislation; **executive-resolution body** is created by government decision. The Inter-State Council under Article 263 and Finance Commission under Article 280 have distinct constitutional bases and functions.

**Examiner trap:** Do not mistake NITI Aayog's policy-advisory role for the constitutional fiscal-transfer function of the Finance Commission.
