# Polity 02 — Making of the Constitution — Question 1

**Learner answer:** B  
**Correct answer:** B
**Result:** Correct

Which statement best describes the Constituent Assembly's role in making the Constitution?

A. It was a directly elected body chosen by universal adult suffrage in 1946.  
B. It both framed the Constitution and functioned as the Dominion Legislature after independence, in distinct capacities.  
C. It was constituted only after 15 August 1947 and had no pre-independence sittings.  
D. It was a sovereign legislature before independence, entirely detached from the Cabinet Mission Plan.

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Polity/02-Making-of-the-Constitution/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and its separate answer key, relevant Constituent Assembly composition, chronology and dual-function material; independently worded.

**Explanation:** After independence, the Constituent Assembly worked in two distinct capacities: it continued framing the Constitution and also served as the Dominion Legislature. Before independence, it had met as a constitution-making body under the Cabinet Mission Plan framework; its members were indirectly elected by provincial legislatures, with princely-state representatives nominated.

- **A:** Incorrect; it was not directly elected by universal adult suffrage.
- **B:** Correct; the two functions existed in distinct capacities after independence.
- **C:** Incorrect; the Assembly first met in December 1946, before independence.
- **D:** Incorrect; its formation arose from the Cabinet Mission Plan framework; before independence it was not simply a fully sovereign legislature detached from that context.

**Examiner trap:** Keep constitution-making distinct from legislative sessions, and distinguish the Assembly's status before and after independence.
