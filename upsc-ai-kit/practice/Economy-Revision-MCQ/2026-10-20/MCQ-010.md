# Economy 01 — Question 10

**Learner answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

Which item can enter measured GDP although no rent is paid in cash?

A. Unpaid care of one's own child  
B. Capital gain on an existing house  
C. An inheritance transferred to a relative  
D. Imputed rent on an owner-occupied home

**Explanation:** National accounts impute a rental value for the housing services owner-occupiers receive from living in their own homes. This maintains comparability with households that rent.

- **A:** Incorrect; unpaid household care of one's own child is generally outside the conventional production boundary.
- **B:** Incorrect; a capital gain on an existing asset is not current production.
- **C:** Incorrect; inheritance is a transfer, not current production.
- **D:** Correct; imputed rent represents current housing services.

**Examiner trap:** The absence of a cash transaction does not exclude every service; some output is imputed under national-accounting rules.
