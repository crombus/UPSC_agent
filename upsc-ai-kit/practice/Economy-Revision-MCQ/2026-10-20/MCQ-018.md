# Polity 06 — Citizenship — Question 1

**Learner answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A
**Result:** Correct

Under Article 11 of the Constitution, which statement best describes Parliament's power regarding citizenship?

A. Parliament may regulate the acquisition and termination of citizenship and related matters by law.  
B. Parliament may alter citizenship only through a constitutional amendment under Article 368.  
C. Parliament may regulate acquisition but has no authority over termination of citizenship.  
D. Parliament may regulate citizenship only with prior approval from every State Legislature.

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Polity/06-Citizenship/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and separate `ANSWERS.md`, concept check on Article 11; independently worded for this mixed diagnostic.

**Explanation:** Article 11 preserves Parliament's power to make laws on the acquisition and termination of citizenship and all other matters relating to citizenship, notwithstanding the preceding constitutional provisions on citizenship at commencement.

- **A:** Correct; this states the breadth of Parliament's Article 11 authority.
- **B:** Incorrect; ordinary legislation can regulate citizenship; a constitutional amendment is not the exclusive route.
- **C:** Incorrect; Parliament's authority includes termination, as reflected in citizenship legislation.
- **D:** Incorrect; Article 11 does not require prior approval from every State Legislature.

**Examiner trap:** Article 11 is a law-making power concerning citizenship, not an Article 368-only amendment power.
