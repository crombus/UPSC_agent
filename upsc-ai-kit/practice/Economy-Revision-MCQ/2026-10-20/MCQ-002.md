# Economy 01 — Question 2

**Learner answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

At basic prices, producers' GVA is 900; taxes on products are 100 and subsidies on products are 30. GDP at market prices equals:

A. 900  
B. 830  
C. 970  
D. 1,030

**Explanation:** GDP at market prices = GVA at basic prices + taxes on products − subsidies on products = 900 + 100 − 30 = 970.

- **A:** Incorrect; it omits the product-tax adjustment.
- **B:** Incorrect; it reverses the adjustments.
- **C:** Correct; the calculation gives 970.
- **D:** Incorrect; subsidies are subtracted, not added.

**Examiner trap:** This bridge uses net taxes on products, not all production taxes.
