# Polity 14 — Emergency Provisions — Diagnostic Question 1

**Learner answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

Which statement best distinguishes a National Emergency under Article 352 from President's Rule under Article 356?

A. Article 352 concerns failure of constitutional machinery in a State; Article 356 concerns war or external aggression.
B. Both Articles can be invoked only after a Supreme Court finding of constitutional breakdown.
C. Article 352 concerns war, external aggression or armed rebellion; Article 356 concerns failure of constitutional machinery in a State.
D. Article 356 automatically dissolves Parliament, while Article 352 applies only to financial emergencies.

**Source:** `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Polity/basic/Emergency-Provisions.md`, snapshot and National Emergency / President's Rule contrast.

**Explanation**
- **A — Incorrect:** It reverses the two grounds. Article 352 concerns war, external aggression or armed rebellion; State constitutional machinery failure is Article 356.
- **B — Incorrect:** Neither provision requires a prior Supreme Court finding. The President acts under the Constitution's stated conditions and safeguards; proclamations are subject to judicial review.
- **C — Correct:** Article 352 is for war, external aggression or armed rebellion. Article 356 applies where the government of a State cannot be carried on in accordance with the Constitution.
- **D — Incorrect:** Article 356 concerns State-level President's Rule, not automatic dissolution of Parliament. Financial Emergency is provided under Article 360, not Article 352.

**Relevant terms:** **National Emergency** is declared under Article 352; **President's Rule** under Article 356 addresses failure of constitutional machinery in a State. The 44th Amendment replaced “internal disturbance” with “armed rebellion” as an Article 352 ground.

**Examiner trap:** Distinguish the emergency grounds precisely and do not confuse the National, State and Financial Emergency provisions (Articles 352, 356 and 360).
