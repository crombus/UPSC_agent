Which statement most accurately identifies recognition of a non-performing asset (NPA)?

A. Any borrower with a fall in market value
B. Any account with a low credit score
C. Any loan with a single delayed instalment
D. Interest or principal remains overdue for more than 90 days

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Economy/06-NPAs-Basel-Norms-Resolution-and-Financial-Inclusion/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and `ANSWERS.md`, Q1; canonical Economy 06 Basic owner §2.

**Explanation**
- **A — Incorrect:** A fall in a borrower's market value alone is not the standard recognition test for a term-loan NPA.
- **B — Incorrect:** A low credit score may signal risk, but it does not by itself classify a loan as an NPA.
- **C — Incorrect:** A single delayed instalment does not automatically satisfy the standard term-loan NPA test.
- **D — Correct:** Under RBI prudential norms, a term loan is generally classified as an NPA when interest or principal remains overdue for more than 90 days.

**Relevant terms:** NPA means non-performing asset. “Overdue” refers to an amount due but not paid by the borrower. Recognition identifies impaired credit; provisioning is a separate step in which the lender charges income to absorb expected losses.

**Examiner trap:** Do not replace the specific overdue-period rule with general indicators of borrower or account risk.
