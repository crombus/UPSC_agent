# Economy 01 — Question 1

**Learner answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Incorrect

An Indian resident firm earns interest on a foreign bond; a foreign-controlled resident firm produces services inside India. Which bridge makes the domestic total a national-income total?

A. Add residents' net primary income from abroad  
B. Deduct all foreign-controlled domestic output  
C. Deduct imports of services from domestic output  
D. Add all remittances received from abroad

**Explanation:** GDP measures production within domestic territory. To obtain gross national income (GNI), add net primary income from abroad: residents' primary income earned abroad minus non-residents' primary income earned domestically. Ownership does not remove domestic production from GDP. Personal remittances are generally current transfers, not primary income.

- **A:** Correct; this is the residence-based national-income adjustment.
- **B:** Incorrect; foreign control or ownership alone does not remove output produced domestically.
- **C:** Incorrect; imports are subtracted in the expenditure identity to isolate domestic production, not to convert GDP to GNI.
- **D:** Incorrect; remittances are not the primary-income bridge.

**Examiner trap:** Do not confuse territory, residence, ownership, citizenship and current transfers.
