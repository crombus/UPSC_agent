# Economy 01 — Question 11

**Learner answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C  
**Result:** Correct

A government builds a new public hospital; it also pays employees to run an existing hospital. Which distinction is sound?

A. Neither outlay can contribute to measured output  
B. Both outlays are necessarily GFCE  
C. New building forms capital; current services enter GFCE  
D. Construction is a pure transfer; salaries are capital formation

**Explanation:** A newly constructed hospital is a produced fixed asset and contributes to gross fixed capital formation (GFCF). Government's current non-market services, such as operating the hospital, are generally valued through production costs and recorded as government final consumption expenditure (GFCE).

- **A:** Incorrect; both capital construction and current services contribute to measured production.
- **B:** Incorrect; the new building is capital formation, not GFCE.
- **C:** Correct; it distinguishes new capital from current government services.
- **D:** Incorrect; construction is not a transfer, and current salaries do not make the service capital formation.

**Examiner trap:** Classify expenditure by whether it creates a produced asset or funds current services.
