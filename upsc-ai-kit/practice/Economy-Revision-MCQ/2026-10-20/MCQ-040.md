# Polity 16 — Prime Minister and Council of Ministers — Diagnostic Question 1

**Learner answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct

Under Article 75(1A), what is the constitutional limit on the total number of Union ministers, including the Prime Minister?

A. It must not exceed 10% of the total membership of the Lok Sabha, excluding the Prime Minister.
B. It must not exceed 15% of the combined membership of both Houses of Parliament.
C. It must not exceed 15% of the total membership of Parliament, including the Prime Minister.
D. It must not exceed 15% of the total membership of the Lok Sabha, including the Prime Minister.

**Source:** `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Polity/basic/PM-and-Council-of-Ministers.md`, snapshot and Must-Know Facts; 91st Amendment, 2003.

**Explanation**
- **A — Incorrect:** The limit is not 10%, and the Prime Minister is included in the total.
- **B — Incorrect:** The denominator is not the combined membership of both Houses.
- **C — Incorrect:** The denominator is not the total membership of Parliament.
- **D — Correct:** Article 75(1A), inserted by the 91st Amendment Act, 2003, caps the total number of Union ministers, including the Prime Minister, at 15% of the total membership of the Lok Sabha.

**Relevant terms:** The constitutional cap counts the full Council of Ministers, including the Prime Minister, against Lok Sabha membership. Separately, the same amendment bars a defector disqualified under the Tenth Schedule from appointment as a minister for the specified period.

**Examiner trap:** Keep the denominator precise—total Lok Sabha membership, not Parliament's combined membership—and remember the Prime Minister is counted within the cap.
