# Polity 09 — Fundamental Duties — Diagnostic Question 1

**Learner answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A  
**Result:** Correct

Which statement correctly locates Fundamental Duties in the Constitution?

A. Part IV-A, Article 51A; inserted by the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976, with the 86th Amendment Act, 2002 adding the duty of a parent or guardian regarding education.
B. Part IV, Article 37; inserted by the 44th Amendment Act, 1978, with the 86th Amendment Act removing the duty.
C. Part III, Article 32; inserted by the 42nd Amendment Act, 1976, as directly enforceable individual rights.
D. Part V, Article 51; inserted by the 86th Amendment Act, 2002, with all duties made enforceable by courts.

**Source:** `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Polity/basic/Fundamental-Duties.md`; compare Article 51A and the 42nd and 86th Amendment Acts.

**Explanation**
- **A — Correct:** Fundamental Duties are in Part IV-A, in Article 51A. The 42nd Amendment Act, 1976 inserted ten duties; the 86th Amendment Act, 2002 added the duty of a parent or guardian to provide educational opportunities to a child or ward aged 6–14.
- **B — Incorrect:** Part IV, Article 37 concerns Directive Principles, not Fundamental Duties. The 44th Amendment did not create the duties, and the 86th Amendment added rather than removed one.
- **C — Incorrect:** Article 32 is the Right to Constitutional Remedies. Fundamental Duties are not in Part III and are non-justiciable rather than directly enforceable as individual rights.
- **D — Incorrect:** Article 51 is about international peace and security in the Directive Principles; Fundamental Duties are in Part IV-A, Article 51A.

**Relevant context:** The Swaran Singh Committee (1976) recommended including Fundamental Duties. The 42nd Amendment inserted ten duties, while the 86th Amendment added an eleventh related to education and Article 21A.

**Examiner trap:** Keep Article 51 (DPSP) separate from Article 51A (Fundamental Duties), and remember that the later 86th Amendment added—not removed—the education duty.
