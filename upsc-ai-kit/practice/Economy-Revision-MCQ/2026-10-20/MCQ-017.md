Which description best captures a scheduled bank?

A. A bank included in the Second Schedule to the RBI Act subject to conditions  
B. A financial company that cannot take deposits  
C. A company registered for insurance business  
D. A government department handling public debt

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Economy/05-Banking-Structure-NBFCs-and-Financial-Regulation/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and `ANSWERS.md`, Q1; the answer key gives A.

**Explanation:** A scheduled bank is included in the Second Schedule to the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, subject to statutory eligibility conditions.

- **A:** Correct; it identifies the legal basis for scheduled-bank status.
- **B:** Incorrect; it describes a broad feature of some financial companies, not scheduled status.
- **C:** Incorrect; insurance registration is unrelated to the Second Schedule.
- **D:** Incorrect; public-debt management is not what defines a scheduled bank.

**Examiner trap:** “Scheduled” here refers to inclusion in the RBI Act's Second Schedule, not merely appearing on any government schedule.
