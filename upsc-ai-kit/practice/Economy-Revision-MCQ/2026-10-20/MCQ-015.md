# Economy 02 — Growth, Development, HDI, IHDI and MPI — Question 1

**Learner answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** A
**Result:** Incorrect

An economy reports an increase in the *money value* of output. Which additional observation establishes that real output also increased, assuming comparable output and price measures?

A. The output-price index increased more slowly than money-value output.  
B. The output-price index increased as rapidly as money-value output.  
C. The output-price index increased more rapidly than money-value output.  
D. The output-price index increased, but its growth rate is unknown.

**Source:** `final-mcq/subjects/Economy/02-Growth-Development-HDI-IHDI-and-MPI/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md` and `ANSWERS.md`, E02-R1-Q01; `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Economy/basic/02_Growth-Development-HDI-IHDI-and-MPI.md` §2.

**Explanation:** Real output increased only if nominal (money-value) output grew faster than the corresponding output-price index. The real-volume change is approximately nominal growth less price growth; exactly, the nominal index is divided by the price index.

- **A:** Correct; the deflated quantity rises when nominal output grows faster than prices.
- **B:** Incorrect; equal nominal and price growth implies no real output increase.
- **C:** Incorrect; faster price growth than nominal growth implies falling real output.
- **D:** Incorrect; without comparing the rates, the direction of real output is unknown.

**Examiner trap:** A rising nominal figure alone does not establish real growth; compare matching nominal and price measures.
