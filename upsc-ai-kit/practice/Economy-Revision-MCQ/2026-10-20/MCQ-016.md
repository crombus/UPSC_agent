# Polity 10 — Amendment and Basic Structure — Question 1

**Learner answer:** C  
**Correct answer:** C
**Result:** Correct

Which statement best distinguishes the Supreme Court's decision in *Minerva Mills* (1980)?

A. It upheld Parliament's power to make the Constitution legally unlimited.  
B. It held that every amendment to the Constitution is automatically invalid.  
C. It treated judicial review and limited amending power as basic features, rejecting the claim of unlimited amending power.  
D. It ruled that Directive Principles have no constitutional status.

**Source:** Adapted from the concept tested by `final-mcq/subjects/Polity/10-Amendment-and-Basic-Structure/revision-1/QUESTIONS.md`, Q1, and its separate answer key. Question wording/options here are independently authored.

**Explanation:** In *Minerva Mills v. Union of India* (1980), the Supreme Court reaffirmed that limited amending power and judicial review are features of the Constitution's basic structure. It struck down the 42nd Amendment's attempt to make Parliament's amending power unlimited and amendments immune from judicial review.

- **A:** Incorrect; the Court rejected unlimited amending power.
- **B:** Incorrect; the ruling did not invalidate every constitutional amendment. Parliament retains a limited amending power under Article 368.
- **C:** Correct; it captures the limited-power and judicial-review holdings.
- **D:** Incorrect; the ruling did not deny Directive Principles constitutional status. It addressed their relationship with Fundamental Rights while rejecting an unlimited power to remove that balance.

**Examiner trap:** Basic-structure review limits amendment power; it does not abolish Parliament's amendment power or invalidate all amendments.
