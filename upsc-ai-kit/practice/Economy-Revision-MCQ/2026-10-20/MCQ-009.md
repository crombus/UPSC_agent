# Economy 01 — Question 9

**Learner answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Incorrect

An unsold finished product is manufactured this year. In expenditure-side accounts it is normally recorded as:

A. A transfer to the manufacturer  
B. A change in inventories  
C. A household consumption purchase  
D. An intermediate input purchase

**Explanation:** Production in the current period counts even if the finished good has not yet been sold. The unsold output is recorded as an addition to inventories, a component of investment.

- **A:** Incorrect; inventory accumulation is production/investment, not a transfer payment.
- **B:** Correct; the unsold finished good adds to inventories.
- **C:** Incorrect; no household purchased or consumed it in the period.
- **D:** Incorrect; the item is finished output, not an intermediate input used in further production.

**Examiner trap:** No sale does not mean no current production; inventories reconcile output with expenditure.
