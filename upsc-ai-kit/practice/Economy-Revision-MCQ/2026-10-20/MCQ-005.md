# Economy 01 — Question 5

**Learner answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** B  
**Result:** Incorrect

Which observation most directly explains why three theoretically identical national-account approaches can produce different published estimates?

A. Net foreign income changes domestic to national  
B. Data sources, timing and revisions differ  
C. Product taxes bridge basic GVA and market GDP  
D. CPI and GDP deflators use different baskets

**Explanation:** Production, income and expenditure approaches should theoretically converge, but measured estimates can differ because their source data, timing and revisions differ.

- **A:** Incorrect; this bridges domestic and national aggregates.
- **B:** Correct; source, timing and revision differences explain route-specific statistical discrepancies.
- **C:** Incorrect; this is a valuation bridge between GVA and GDP.
- **D:** Incorrect; CPI and GDP-deflator coverage differs, but that does not explain disagreement among the three measurement approaches.

**Examiner trap:** A statistical discrepancy is not itself a producing industry or conceptual adjustment.
