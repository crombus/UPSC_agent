## Challenge

Consider the following statements:

1. The 42nd Constitutional Amendment added the words `socialist`, `secular` and `integrity` to the
   Preamble.
2. A republic requires a non-hereditary head of state, but it does not necessarily require that
   the head be directly elected by citizens.
3. The word `democratic` in the Preamble refers exclusively to political democracy and has no
   connection with social or economic democracy.
4. India's sovereignty is incompatible with voluntary membership of organisations such as the
   United Nations or the Commonwealth.

Which statements are correct?

**A.** 1 and 2 only  
**B.** 1, 2 and 3 only  
**C.** 2 and 4 only  
**D.** 1 and 4 only

## Learner response and evaluation

**Learner's answer:** A  
**Correct answer:** A - Statements 1 and 2 only  
**Result:** Correct without hint  
**XP earned:** 10

### Statement analysis

1. **Correct.** The 42nd Amendment Act 1976 inserted `socialist` and `secular` before
   `democratic republic` and changed `unity of the nation` to `unity and integrity of the nation`.
2. **Correct.** A republic rejects hereditary succession to the headship of the state. India's
   President is indirectly elected, which fully satisfies republican government.
3. **Incorrect.** Political democracy must be supported by social and economic democracy. Voting
   equality alone does not fulfil the Preamble's commitments to justice, equality and dignity.
4. **Incorrect.** Sovereignty means that India has final legal authority over its internal and
   external decisions. It may voluntarily accept international obligations or join organisations
   without surrendering sovereignty.

### Concept map

```text
Sovereign  -> independent decision-making
Socialist  -> democratic welfare orientation, not one rigid economic model
Secular    -> no state religion; principled equality among faiths
Democratic -> political + social + economic democratic aspiration
Republic   -> non-hereditary head of state
```
