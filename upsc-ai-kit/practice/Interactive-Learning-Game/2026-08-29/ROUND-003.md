## Challenge

Consider the following statements:

1. Social, economic and political justice in the Preamble is conventionally associated with the
   ideals of the Russian Revolution.
2. Liberty, equality and fraternity in the Preamble are conventionally associated with the
   Russian Revolution.
3. Fraternity assures both the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the
   nation.
4. Constitutional equality requires identical treatment in every situation and therefore
   prohibits affirmative action.

Which statements are correct?

**A.** 1, 2 and 3 only  
**B.** 2 and 4 only  
**C.** 1 and 3 only  
**D.** 1, 3 and 4 only

## Learner response and evaluation

### First attempt

**Learner's answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** C - Statements 1 and 3 only  
**Result:** Incorrect  
**XP earned so far:** 0

### Statement analysis

1. **Correct.** Social, economic and political justice is conventionally linked with the ideals
   of the Russian Revolution.
2. **Incorrect.** Liberty, equality and fraternity are associated with the **French Revolution**,
   not the Russian Revolution.
3. **Correct.** The Preamble links fraternity to both **dignity of the individual** and **unity and
   integrity of the nation**.
4. **Incorrect.** Equality does not always demand identical treatment. The Constitution permits
   reasonable classification and expressly enables affirmative action to remedy structural
   disadvantage.

### Why D fails

Option D includes Statement 4. Treating differently situated groups identically may preserve
existing inequality. Constitutional equality therefore includes:

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Formal equality | Same rule for similarly situated persons | Equal application of a general law |
| Substantive equality | Corrective treatment to overcome disadvantage | Constitutionally authorised reservation |

## Remedial challenge

Which statement best expresses constitutional equality?

**A.** Every person must receive identical treatment regardless of relevant differences.

**B.** Any reservation or protective discrimination necessarily violates equality.

**C.** Equality is only a political ideal and has no enforceable constitutional expression.

**D.** Equality permits reasonable classification and constitutionally authorised affirmative
action when they pursue legitimate corrective purposes.

## Remedial response

**Learner's answer:** D  
**Correct answer:** D  
**Result:** Correct after remedial retry  
**XP earned:** 5

### Concept restored

Article 14 permits classification when:

1. It rests on an **intelligible differentia** distinguishing the included group from others.
2. That differentia has a **rational nexus** with the law's objective.

The Constitution also expressly enables affirmative action through provisions such as Articles
15(3), 15(4), 15(5), 15(6), 16(4) and 16(6). These measures pursue **substantive equality** by
addressing real disadvantage rather than mechanically treating unequal situations alike.

> **Exam trap:** equality forbids class legislation, not every reasonable classification.
