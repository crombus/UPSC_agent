## Challenge

In no more than **four lines**, distinguish `democratic` from `republic` in the Preamble.

Your answer must explain:

1. Why India's indirectly elected President is consistent with being a republic.
2. One way in which constitutional democracy extends beyond merely conducting elections.

## Evaluation criteria

| Element | Expected idea |
|---|---|
| Democratic | Government ultimately derives authority from the people |
| Republic | Head of state is non-hereditary and holds office for a fixed term |
| Indirect election | Direct popular election is not necessary for republican status |
| Deeper democracy | Social/economic democracy, rights, equality, accountability or rule of law |

### What was done well

- Correctly contrasted India with the United Kingdom's hereditary monarchy.

  republic.

### Precision improvement

Use **head of state**, not merely “leader in the executive.” A republic concerns how the state's
formal head obtains office. The Prime Minister is the real executive head in India's parliamentary
system, whereas the President is the constitutional head of state.

### Missing dimension

Elections establish political democracy, but constitutional democracy also requires limits on
power and conditions for meaningful equality. Relevant examples include:

- Fundamental Rights and rule of law.
- Governmental accountability.
- Social equality and individual dignity.
- Economic justice and fair opportunity.

## One-line completion

Complete only this sentence:

> Constitutional democracy extends beyond elections because ________________________________.

Use at least **two** of these ideas: rights, rule of law, accountability, social equality,
economic justice or dignity.

## Completion response

> Constitutional democracy extends beyond elections because of freedom, dignity, justice and
> fundamental rights.

**Result:** Correct after one focused prompt  
**XP earned:** 7

### Final answer model

> Democracy means that governmental authority ultimately derives from the people, while a
> republic requires a non-hereditary head of state; therefore, India's indirectly elected
> President is republican. Constitutional democracy extends beyond elections through Fundamental
> Rights, rule of law, accountability, dignity and commitments to social and economic justice.
