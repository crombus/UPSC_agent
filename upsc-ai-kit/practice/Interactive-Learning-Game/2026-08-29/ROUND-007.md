## Question

> “The Preamble is an interpretive charter, but not an independently enforceable source of
> constitutional power.” Discuss.

## Challenge

Write only the **introduction** in no more than **two sentences or 45 words**.

The introduction should:

1. Establish the Preamble's constitutional status.
2. State a thesis that distinguishes interpretive importance from enforceability.

Do not narrate the full case-law history yet.

## Evaluation criteria

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Directness | Addresses the quotation immediately |
| Legal accuracy | Part of Constitution, but non-justiciable/not a source of power |
| Thesis | Explains that its force operates mainly through interpretation |
| Economy | Two sentences or 45 words maximum |

## Learner response and evaluation

### First attempt

> Preamble is the begninng of constitution objective and goals for evry citizen of india. It is
> not enfoceable buy law but court use it to interpret the consitution for justice.

**Assessment:** Core idea understood, but introduction not yet exam-ready  
**XP earned so far:** 0

### What was done well

- Stayed within the required length.
- Recognised that the Preamble is not independently enforceable.
- Recognised its role in constitutional interpretation.

### What must improve

1. State that the Preamble is **part of the Constitution**.
2. It expresses the Constitution's **source of authority, character of the State and governing
   objectives**; it does not prescribe personal objectives for every citizen.
3. Replace “not enforceable by law” with the precise term **non-justiciable**.
4. State that it is neither a standalone source of rights nor a source of governmental power.
5. Courts use it to resolve ambiguity and illuminate constitutional purpose, not merely “for
   justice.”

## Structured rewrite

Write two sentences by completing this logical structure in your own words:

> The Preamble is part of the Constitution and expresses ________________________________.
> Although it is non-justiciable and does not independently confer ______________________,
> courts use it to _________________________________________________________________.

Remain within 45 words.

## Rewrite response and final evaluation

> The Preamble is part of the Constitution and expresses authority, State character and personal
> goals for citizens. Although it is non-justiciable and does not independently confer right or
> power, courts use it to interpret consitution.

**Result:** Cleared after guided rewrite  
**XP earned:** 7  
**Precision bonus:** Not awarded

### Final correction

Replace **“personal goals for citizens”** with **“the constitutional order's objectives.”** The
Preamble promises justice, liberty, equality and fraternity as commitments of the polity; it does
not assign personal ambitions to citizens.

### Examiner-ready version

> The Preamble is part of the Constitution and expresses popular authority, the character of the
> Indian State and the constitutional order's objectives. Although non-justiciable and not an
> independent source of rights or power, it guides courts in interpreting ambiguous provisions.

### Construction lesson

An effective introduction performs three tasks:

```text
status -> limitation -> thesis answering the question
```
