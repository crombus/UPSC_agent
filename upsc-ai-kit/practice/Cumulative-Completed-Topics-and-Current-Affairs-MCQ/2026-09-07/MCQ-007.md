## Question

Consider the following statements concerning causation and God in Indian philosophy:

1. Nyāya-Vaiśeṣika regards God as both the material substance of the world and the efficient
   arranger of its eternal atoms.
2. Classical Sāṃkhya treats prakṛti as the material source of cosmic evolution and does not need
   a creator God to initiate that evolution.
3. Advaita accepts Īśvara as cosmically significant at the empirical level while maintaining
   nirguṇa Brahman as the non-dual ultimate reality.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1 and 3 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3
