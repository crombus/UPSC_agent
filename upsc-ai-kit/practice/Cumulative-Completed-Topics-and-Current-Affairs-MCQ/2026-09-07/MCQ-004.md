# MCQ 004 — I4C and Cybercrime Coordination

**Area:** Current Affairs + Internal Security  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Status:** Completed

## Source anchor

- Ministry of Home Affairs and PIB material on the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre,
  cyber-fraud reporting and interstate coordination, accessed on 2026-09-07.
- Official reference: https://www.mha.gov.in/en/divisionofmha/cyber-and-information-security-cis-division
- PIB reference: https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2223095

## Question

With reference to recent developments concerning the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre
(I4C), consider the following statements:

1. I4C became an attached office of the Ministry of Home Affairs in 2024.
2. The Suspect Registry used for countering financial cyber fraud is maintained by CERT-In under
   the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
3. The Samanvaya platform supports data sharing and coordination in cybercrime cases having
   interstate linkages.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 1 and 3 only  
C. 2 and 3 only  
D. 1, 2 and 3

## Learner response

**D. 1, 2 and 3**

## Evaluation and explanation

**Result:** Incorrect  
**Correct answer:** B. 1 and 3 only

### Statement analysis

1. **Correct.** The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre became an attached office of the
   Ministry of Home Affairs with effect from 1 July 2024.
2. **Incorrect.** The Suspect Registry is an I4C initiative developed with banks and financial
   institutions. It is not maintained by CERT-In under MeitY. The registry consolidates
   identifiers linked with suspected cybercriminals and financial-fraud networks.
3. **Correct.** Samanvaya is an I4C coordination and analytics platform that assists law
   enforcement agencies in connecting cybercrime complaints and investigations across States and
   Union Territories.

### Full forms, objectives and timelines

#### NTRO — National Technical Research Organisation

**Objective and functions**

- A technical-intelligence organisation supporting India's national-security system.
- Develops and supplies intelligence from technical capabilities, including geospatial,
  communications, electronic and cyber domains.
- Provides technical intelligence support to authorised security and intelligence agencies.

**Timeline**

| Year | Development |
|---|---|
| 1999-2001 | The post-Kargil security review and Group of Ministers process highlighted gaps in technical intelligence coordination. |
| 2004 | NTRO was established as a specialised technical-intelligence organisation. |
| 2014 | NCIIPC under NTRO was designated the national nodal agency for CII protection. |

#### NCIIPC — National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre

**Objective and functions**

- Protects India's Critical Information Infrastructure (CII).
- Identifies critical information-infrastructure elements and advises them on risk reduction,
  controls, standards, threat intelligence and incident preparedness.
- Coordinates with sectoral organisations and CERT-In where CII incidents overlap with the wider
  national cyber-incident-response system.

**Legal meaning of CII**

Section 70 of the Information Technology Act describes CII as a computer resource whose
incapacitation or destruction would have a debilitating impact on national security, the economy,
public health or safety.

**Timeline**

| Year | Development |
|---|---|
| 2008-2009 | The IT (Amendment) framework inserted Section 70A, enabling designation of a national nodal agency for CII protection. |
| 2013 | Rules governing NCIIPC's functions and duties were notified. |
| 16 January 2014 | The Union government designated NCIIPC under NTRO as the national nodal agency under Section 70A. |

#### I4C — Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre

**Objective and functions**

- Creates a comprehensive and coordinated national response to cybercrime.
- Supports prevention, detection, investigation and prosecution.
- Strengthens State and Union Territory law-enforcement capacity, interstate coordination,
  cybercrime analytics, training, forensics and public reporting.
- Supports the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, helpline 1930, the Citizen Financial Cyber
  Fraud Reporting and Management System, the Suspect Registry and Samanvaya.

**Timeline**

| Date | Development |
|---|---|
| 2018 | I4C was initiated as an MHA scheme for coordinated cybercrime response. |
| 10 January 2020 | The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre was formally inaugurated. |
| 1 July 2024 | I4C acquired the status of an attached office of the Ministry of Home Affairs. |
| September 2024 | The Suspect Registry was launched with financial-sector participation. |

### Institutional relationship

```
NTRO ── technical-intelligence organisation
  └── NCIIPC ── protects Critical Information Infrastructure

MHA
  └── I4C ── coordinates cybercrime prevention, reporting and investigation

MeitY
  └── CERT-In ── coordinates national cyber-incident response
```

### UPSC trap

Cyber institutions often cooperate, but cooperation does not transfer institutional ownership.
CERT-In responds to cyber incidents; NCIIPC focuses on CII; I4C coordinates cybercrime and
law-enforcement mechanisms. Therefore, statement 2 made option D incorrect.
