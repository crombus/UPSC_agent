# MCQ 001 — Preventive Detention Safeguards

**Area:** Polity + Internal Security  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

With reference to preventive detention under the Constitution of India, consider the following
statements:

1. The safeguards of being informed of the grounds of arrest and consulting a legal practitioner
   under Article 22(1) do not apply to a person detained under a preventive-detention law.
2. Ordinarily, preventive detention cannot continue beyond three months unless an Advisory Board
   reports sufficient cause for continued detention.
3. The Advisory Board must consist of persons who are, have been, or are qualified to be appointed
   as judges of a High Court.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1, 2 and 3  
B. 1 and 2 only  
C. 2 and 3 only  
D. 1 and 3 only

## Learner response

**B. 1 and 2 only**

## Evaluation and explanation

**Result:** Incorrect  
**Correct answer:** A. 1, 2 and 3

### Statement analysis

1. **Correct.** Article 22(3) expressly excludes a person detained under a preventive-detention
   law from the protections in Article 22(1) and 22(2). Therefore, the constitutional guarantees
   of being informed immediately of the grounds of arrest, consulting and being defended by a
   legal practitioner of one's choice, and production before a magistrate within 24 hours do not
   apply in the ordinary Article 22(1)-(2) form.
2. **Correct.** Under the operative text of Article 22(4), detention ordinarily cannot exceed
   three months unless an Advisory Board reports sufficient cause for continued detention.
   Parliament may prescribe exceptional classes or circumstances under Article 22(7).
3. **Correct.** Article 22(4)(a) requires the Advisory Board to consist of persons who are, have
   been, or are qualified to be appointed as judges of a High Court.

### Important distinction

Preventive detention aims to prevent a person from committing a future prejudicial act; punitive
detention follows an alleged offence and criminal process. Preventive detention does not eliminate
all safeguards. Article 22(5) requires communication of the grounds as soon as possible and the
earliest opportunity to make a representation, subject to the public-interest exception in
Article 22(6).

### Constitutional timeline

- The Forty-fourth Amendment Act, 1978 proposed reducing the initial period in Article 22(4) from
  three months to two months and changing the Board's composition.
- The relevant amendment has not been brought into force; the operative constitutional period
  remains three months.

### UPSC trap

The phrase "qualified to be appointed as a High Court judge" may appear weaker than requiring
sitting judges, but it is the Constitution's express wording. Statement 3 was therefore correct.
