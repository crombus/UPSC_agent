# MCQ 003 — India's Cyber-Security Architecture

**Area:** Internal Security - Cyber Security, CII and Cybercrime  
**Difficulty:** Medium-High  
**Status:** Completed

## Question

With reference to India's cyber-security architecture, consider the following statements:

1. CERT-In derives its statutory status from Section 70B of the Information Technology Act, 2000.
2. The National Critical Information Infrastructure Protection Centre is the national nodal
   agency for protecting critical information infrastructure and operates under Section 70A of
   the Information Technology Act.
3. The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre functions under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

A. 1 and 2 only  
B. 2 and 3 only  
C. 1, 2 and 3  
D. 1 and 3 only

## Learner response

**C. 1, 2 and 3**

## Evaluation and explanation

**Result:** Correct  
**Correct answer:** C. 1, 2 and 3

### Statement analysis

1. **Correct.** The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) is the national agency for
   cyber-incident response under Section 70B of the Information Technology Act, 2000. Its
   functions include collecting and analysing incident information, issuing alerts and
   advisories, coordinating responses and prescribing incident-reporting directions.
2. **Correct.** Section 70A enables the designation of a national nodal agency for protecting
   Critical Information Infrastructure (CII). The National Critical Information Infrastructure
   Protection Centre (NCIIPC), functioning under the National Technical Research Organisation,
   performs this role.
3. **Correct.** The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) functions under the Ministry of
   Home Affairs. It coordinates the law-enforcement response to cybercrime rather than serving as
   the technical incident-response body for all cyber incidents.

### Institutional distinction

| Institution | Primary role | Administrative location / legal anchor |
|---|---|---|
| CERT-In | National cyber-incident response | MeitY; IT Act, Section 70B |
| NCIIPC | Protection of designated CII | NTRO; IT Act, Section 70A |
| I4C | Cybercrime coordination and law-enforcement support | Ministry of Home Affairs |

### What qualifies as CII?

Under the IT Act, Critical Information Infrastructure is a computer resource whose incapacitation
or destruction would have a debilitating impact on national security, the economy, public health
or safety. Banking, power, telecom, transport and strategic-government systems may therefore
require sector-specific protection.

### Concrete example

A ransomware attack on an ordinary private workstation may require incident reporting and
CERT-In coordination. An attack threatening a designated national power-grid control system also
engages the CII-protection architecture associated with NCIIPC. Investigation of the offenders,
money trail and interstate victim network may involve I4C-supported law-enforcement coordination.

### UPSC trap

The three bodies overlap in the broad cyber-security ecosystem but are not interchangeable.
UPSC can switch their ministries, statutory sections or primary functions to create plausible
distractors.
