## 2026 — SECTION B ✅

### Q5 — compulsory, answer in about 150 words each (10 × 5 = 50)

- **Q5(a) · 10 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** According to Nyaya view of inference (Anumana), what are the characteristics that ascertain the validity of inferential sign (Hetu)? Discuss. 📝 **Printed wording:** the English line prints "Anumdna" for *anumāna*. → owner block: `Nyaya-Vaisesika.md` §3.3, "Five characteristics of a valid hetu".
- **Q5(b) · 10 marks · [Buddhism](./indian/Buddhism.md):** How does the Buddhist theory of momentariness problematize the issue of human action and responsibility? Critically discuss.
- **Q5(c) · 10 marks · [Sāṃkhya](./indian/Samkhya.md):** Is Purusa one or many? Explain and examine the Sankhya position in this context. 📝 **Printed wording:** "Sarnkhya" for *Sāṃkhya*.
- **Q5(d) · 10 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** Critically explain the nature of Samavaya (inherence) as propounded in Vaisesika philosophy. → owner block: `Nyaya-Vaisesika.md` §2.9.
- **Q5(e) · 10 marks · [Yoga](./indian/Yoga.md):** Explain, as per Yoga philosophy, the nature of Samadhi with special reference to the difference between Samprajnata Samadhi and Asamprajnata Samadhi.

### Q6 (50)

- **Q6(a) · 20 marks · [Sāṃkhya](./indian/Samkhya.md):** "A mango tree is grown out of a mango seed." How do the Sankhya philosophers explain this process through their theory of causation by rejecting their rival perspectives? → *satkāryavāda* against *asatkāryavāda* (Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika) and *vivartavāda* (Advaita); rivals cross-linked to [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md) and [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md), and to [Causation across schools](./_themes/Causation-across-schools.md).
- **Q6(b) · 15 marks · [Aurobindo](./indian/Aurobindo.md):** Explain the basic characteristics of Sri Aurobindo's Integral Yoga for the realization of supramental consciousness through triple transformation.
- **Q6(c) · 15 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** Bring out the main points of contestation between Naiyayikas and Prabhakara Mimamsakas with reference to knowledge through words (Sabdabodha). 📝 **Printed wording:** "Naiydyikas", "Mimarhsakas", "Sabdabodha". → owner block: `Nyaya-Vaisesika.md` §3.6A; Prābhākara half cross-linked from [Mīmāṃsā](./indian/Mimamsa.md) §8.2, §8.4.

### Q7 (50)

- **Q7(a) · 20 marks · [Mīmāṃsā](./indian/Mimamsa.md):** Critically explain the arguments presented by the Akhyativadins for explaining erroneous cognition. Evaluate the arguments against Akhyativadins by the Anyathakhyativadins. → *akhyāti* is Prābhākara Mīmāṃsā (`Mimamsa.md` §6.2–§6.4); the *anyathākhyāti* counter-arguments are cross-linked to [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md) §4.1–§4.4. ⚠️ Routed to Mīmāṃsā because the burden of exposition falls on the *akhyātivādin*, whom the Naiyāyika then attacks.
- **Q7(b) · 15 marks · [Jainism](./indian/Jainism.md):** Explain the meaning of seven steps of judgement (Saptabhanginaya) and its application in Jaina's Syadvada for advocating a philosophy of non-absolutism (Anekantavada).
- **Q7(c) · 15 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** Do you agree with Nimbarka's explanation of the nature of God, Souls and Matter? Justify your answer. → *dvaitādvaita / bhedābheda*.

### Q8 (50)

- **Q8(a) · 20 marks · [Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika](./indian/Nyaya-Vaisesika.md):** Present an account of Upamana as an instrument of knowledge (Pramana). How is Nyaya view of Upamana different from Mimamsa view? Discuss. → owner block: `Nyaya-Vaisesika.md` §3.5A; Mīmāṃsā half cross-linked from [Mīmāṃsā](./indian/Mimamsa.md) §3.7.
- **Q8(b) · 15 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** Critically explain Ramanuja's conception of Brahman with special reference to the difficulties in explaining the relation of Brahman to Spirit (Cit) and Matter (Acit). → *viśiṣṭādvaita*, the *aprthaksiddhi* / body-soul analogy and its objections.
- **Q8(c) · 15 marks · [Vedānta](./indian/Vedanta.md):** Why does the Dualistic School of Vedanta consider that differences (Bhedas) have separate existence and constitute the unique nature of things? Answer with reference to the theory of five-fold difference (Pancavidhabheda). → Madhva's *dvaita*.

## TREND NOTE, 2026 ⚠️

1. **Epistemology dominates Section B.** Four parts (Q5(a), Q6(c), Q7(a), Q8(a) — 65 marks) are *pramāṇa*-and-*khyāti* questions. A candidate who prepares Nyāya epistemology with its Mīmāṃsā counterparts can carry the whole of Q6 to Q8.
2. **Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika is the single heaviest owner** at four parts and 55 marks, its strongest showing in the local corpus; Vedānta follows with three parts, all on **non-Advaita** ācāryas — Nimbārka, Rāmānuja and Madhva — with **no Śaṅkara part at all**. Preparing only Advaita would have cost 45 marks.
3. **Inter-school contestation is asked explicitly.** Q6(c) ("points of contestation"), Q7(a) ("evaluate the arguments against") and Q8(a) ("how is the Nyāya view different") all require *both* positions plus the objections; a one-school exposition cannot score.
4. **Cārvāka is absent**, and Buddhism and Jainism appear once each — the heterodox block contributes only 25 marks, against 75 for the Nyāya–Mīmāṃsā axis.
5. **Applied/ethical framing continues:** Q5(b) asks momentariness as a problem for **action and responsibility**, and Q6(b) asks Aurobindo through **triple transformation** — both demand the doctrine be put to work, not merely stated.
