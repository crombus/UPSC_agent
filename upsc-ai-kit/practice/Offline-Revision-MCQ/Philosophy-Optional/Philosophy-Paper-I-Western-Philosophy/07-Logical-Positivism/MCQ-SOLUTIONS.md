## MCQ 1

Which description correctly identifies the 1929 Vienna Circle manifesto?

A. Schlick alone authored the manifesto as the Circle's binding verificationist creed.
B. Ayer issued the manifesto in Britain after publishing Language, Truth and Logic.
C. Hahn, Neurath and Carnap wrote the manifesto and dedicated it to Moritz Schlick.
D. Wittgenstein and Popper jointly issued the manifesto as a programme of falsification.

**Answer: C.**

**Option explanations:**
- **A:** False proposition: “Schlick alone authored the manifesto as the Circle's binding verificationist creed.” Correct doctrine: Schlick led the Circle but did not author the manifesto, which did not impose one frozen criterion. Correct replacement: “Hahn, Neurath and Carnap wrote the manifesto and dedicated it to Moritz Schlick.”
- **B:** False proposition: “Ayer issued the manifesto in Britain after publishing Language, Truth and Logic.” Correct doctrine: Ayer's 1936 book popularised the movement in English; it was not the 1929 manifesto. Correct replacement: “Hahn, Neurath and Carnap wrote the manifesto and dedicated it to Moritz Schlick.”
- **C:** The manifesto was a collective scientific-worldview statement by Hahn, Neurath and Carnap, dedicated to Schlick.
- **D:** False proposition: “Wittgenstein and Popper jointly issued the manifesto as a programme of falsification.” Correct doctrine: Neither was a Circle member, and falsifiability is Popper's distinct proposal. Correct replacement: “Hahn, Neurath and Carnap wrote the manifesto and dedicated it to Moritz Schlick.”

**Examiner trap 1:** Confusing leadership, authorship and later popularisation.

**Repair action:** Attach 1929 to Hahn–Neurath–Carnap, dedicated to Schlick.

**Coverage mapping:** `C01`.

**PYQ linkage:** 2026 Q4(c); historical frame.

## MCQ 3

**Examiner trap 3:** Reading 'handmaid' as subordinate empirical research.

## MCQ 5
