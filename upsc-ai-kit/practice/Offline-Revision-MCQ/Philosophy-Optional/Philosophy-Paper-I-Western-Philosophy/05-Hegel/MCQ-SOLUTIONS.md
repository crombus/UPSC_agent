## MCQ 1
For Hegel, the dialectical method is best described as:

A. a formal calculus that treats every contradiction as a defect in reasoning

B. an externally imposed debate that alternates assertions with their simple denials

C. an empirical pattern inferred from recurring stages in political and cultural history

D. an immanent movement in which finite concepts expose, negate and overcome their own limits

**Answer: D.**

**Option explanations:**
- **A:** Incorrect: Formal consistency belongs to understanding, but Hegelian dialectic investigates contradictions generated within finite determinations.
- **B:** Incorrect: This makes dialectic an external argumentative technique; Hegel presents the movement as arising from the content itself.
- **C:** Incorrect: History exhibits dialectical movement, but the method is not an induction from historical sequences.
- **D:** Correct: Dialectic is the self-movement of a determination through contradiction, determinate negation and a richer mediated form.

**Repair action:** Use abstract/negative/speculative moments and replace an external recipe with immanent development.

**Coverage mapping:** `C02` — Dialectical method, logical moments and T-A-S caution.

## MCQ 4

A. cancelling a determination, preserving its truth, and raising it into a richer mediated unity

B. rejecting a determination completely and replacing it with an unrelated category
