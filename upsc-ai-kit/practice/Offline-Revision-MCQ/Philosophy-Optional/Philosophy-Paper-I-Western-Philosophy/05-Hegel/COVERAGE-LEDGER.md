## Genuine source gaps and cautions

  PYQ sources; none is fabricated.
- “Thesis–antithesis–synthesis” is retained only as qualified shorthand associated with
  Fichte/Chalybaeus; Hegel's safer vocabulary is moment, determinate negation, mediation and
  sublation.
- Posthumously edited lecture material on history is used with source and Eurocentrism cautions.
- Later Marxist, feminist, anti-colonial and recognition-theoretic uses are labelled receptions,
  not silently attributed to Hegel's own text.
