### SESSION 1 — Crisis, Rigorous Philosophy and the Phenomenological Project

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** Husserl asks philosophy to stop beginning with ready-made theories about mind or world and to describe carefully how anything becomes present, meaningful and evident in experience.

**Technical definition:** Phenomenology is a disciplined description of the structures and modes of givenness of objects to consciousness. Husserl's demand for philosophy as a rigorous science responds to naturalism, which reduces meaning and reason to natural processes; historicism, which makes truth relative to an age; and scepticism, which follows when objective validity cannot be grounded. The slogan 'to the things themselves' directs inquiry to subject-matters as given, not merely to physical things or private mental images.

#### VISUAL — Project map: crisis to rigorous phenomenology

```text

NATURALISM -> meaning and logic reduced to natural processes

HISTORICISM -> truth reduced to a historical worldview

SCEPTICISM -> objective validity lacks a secure account

                    |

                    v

PHILOSOPHY AS RIGOROUS SCIENCE

                    |

                    v

DESCRIBE HOW OBJECTS ARE GIVEN -> CLARIFY MEANING, EVIDENCE, OBJECTIVITY

```

*The project answers threatened validity by returning to disciplined givenness.*

#### VISUAL — What phenomenology studies

```text

NOT: private image inside a mental container

NOT: causal psychology of how a brain produces a state

NOT: decorative description of how something seems

YES: OBJECT <-> MODE OF GIVENNESS <-> INTENTIONAL EXPERIENCE

QUESTION: how can the same object be meant, presented, confirmed or corrected?

```

*The object remains the focus, but it is examined through its modes of givenness.*

#### ANSWER-GRABBING OPENING — WRITE/ADAPT IN THE EXAM

> Husserlian phenomenology is neither vague introspection nor a catalogue of appearances, but a method for describing givenness so that meaning, evidence and objectivity can be grounded.

#### MUST-WRITE KEYWORDS

- **scepticism**
- **givenness**

**How to use them:** Open with naturalism, historicism and scepticism, define phenomenology through givenness, and state how rigorous science seeks objectivity without reducing objects to private images.

#### CORE OBJECTION, REPLY AND RESIDUAL LIMIT

**Objection:** The promise of presuppositionlessness may be impossible because every description already uses language, concepts and historical habits.

**Best reply:** Husserl treats presuppositionlessness as a regulative discipline: suspend unexamined posits and make every claim answer to how its object is given.

**Residual limit:** The reply does not eliminate inherited concepts or historical situatedness, so complete presuppositionlessness remains an aspiration rather than an accomplished state.

#### EXAM USE AND CONCISE REVISION

**Answer architecture:** Use crisis -> project -> method -> objectivity -> presupposition objection -> qualified verdict; then forecast intentionality, reduction, essences and anti-psychologism.

- Phenomenology describes modes and structures of givenness.
- It is directed to objects-as-given, not private images.
- Naturalism reduces validity to nature; historicism relativises it to an age.
- Rigour requires evidence and clarified structures, not a borrowed scientific model.

### SESSION 2 — Natural Attitude, World-Positing, Profiles and Horizons

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** Ordinary life simply takes the world and its objects as there; Husserl calls this useful, unexamined stance the natural attitude rather than stupidity or a false doctrine.

**Technical definition:** The natural attitude is the pre-reflective world-positing in which objects, persons and the world are accepted as existing. Its general thesis is operative rather than usually stated. Perceptual objects are transcendent to any single experience because they exceed what is presently given: a table appears through changing profiles or adumbrations, yet is intended as one identical table. Every presentation also has a horizon, the background of anticipated sides, uses, contexts and possible further appearances.

**Plain-language start:** Right now you assume the world is simply there - the chair holds you up, the street exists whether or not you look. You cannot study the water while you are the fish swimming in it unreflectively.

#### 1.1 The Natural Attitude and Its General Thesis (PYQ 2021 — 20m) ✅

- In everyday life we live in the **natural attitude** (*natürliche Einstellung*): we unreflectively *posit* the world as existing, as "there," as containing objects with determinate properties independent of our awareness.
- This attitude has a **general thesis** (*Generalthesis*): the world exists, has always existed, and would continue to exist without me. ✅ - The natural attitude encompasses not only naïve common sense but also the natural sciences — physics, biology, psychology all *presuppose* that they investigate an independently existing domain.
- **Problem:** the natural attitude is not *wrong*, but it is **philosophically uncritical** — it takes for granted precisely what philosophy must examine: the *relationship between consciousness and world*. So long as we remain within it, we cannot ask the foundational question: *how does the world come to have meaning for us?* ⚠️

### SESSION 3 — Epoché and the Phenomenological, Transcendental and Eidetic Reductions

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** Epoché means putting the ordinary existence-commitment out of play so that experience can be examined without either denying the world or trying to prove it.

**Technical definition:** Epoché or bracketing suspends the existential posit of the natural attitude for the purpose of phenomenological inquiry. It differs from Cartesian doubt: doubt questions or withholds belief because existence may be false, whereas epoché neutralises the posit without affirming, denying or destroying the world. Epoché and phenomenological reduction are related moves: suspension clears the existence-commitment, while phenomenological reduction redirects inquiry from naïve object-focus to the correlation between intended object and modes of givenness. Transcendental reduction can radicalise that redirection by disclosing transcendental subjectivity as the field in which sense and objectivity are constituted. Eidetic reduction has a distinct fact-to-essence task: it begins from factual or imagined examples and varies them freely to disclose invariant structures, without logically awaiting a completed transcendental reduction. None is ordinary introspection or psychological self-observation.

#### CORE OBJECTION, REPLY AND RESIDUAL LIMIT

**Objection:** The epoché may be circular, impossible to execute completely, or a concealed route to transcendental idealism and world-denial.

**Best reply:** Husserl answers that no object-content is removed, only the unexamined existential posit; the method changes the question from whether the world exists to how world-sense is possible.

**Residual limit:** The distinction blocks crude world-denial but does not settle whether transcendental constitution gives subjectivity excessive philosophical priority.

#### EXAM USE AND CONCISE REVISION

**Answer architecture:** Use exact contrasts, a functional map of the reductions, one table example, the circularity objection and the qualified claim that the world is recovered as constituted meaning. If you give a pedagogical order, label it as an expository route rather than a claim of strict logical dependence.

- Epoché is methodological suspension, not sceptical denial.
- Phenomenological reduction redirects attention to correlation.
- Transcendental reduction reaches the constituting field, not a mental substance.
- Eidetic reduction moves from contingent fact to invariant possibility-condition.

**Plain-language start:** The **epoche** (Greek for "suspension") is one deliberate move: stop *banking on* the world's independent existence. Crucially you do **not** deny it - you put the existence-question in brackets and set it aside. The tree still looks exactly as green and solid as before; you simply stop asserting "and it exists out there." What is left over - the **residue** - is your whole stream of experience exactly as lived. The **reduction** is the positive payoff: by switching off the existence-posit you are *led back* to consciousness as the place where anything can mean anything. It is a change of *attitude*, not a change of *scenery*.

#### 1.2 The Epoché / Phenomenological Reduction ✅

- **Epoché** (Greek ἐποχή = "suspension," "withholding of assent"): we **bracket** (*einklammern*) the existence-positing of the natural attitude.
- **What is bracketed:** the general thesis — i.e., the positing that the world (and its objects) *exists* independently. Crucially, we bracket the *existence-claim*, **not the content of experience**. The tree I see is still described as presenting itself with its full richness of colour, shape, spatial placement — but I no longer *assert* or *deny* that it exists beyond my experience of it. ✅
- **What is NOT bracketed:** the stream of consciousness itself, with all its intentional contents. The phenomenological residue (*Residuum*) is the entire field of **pure experience** as lived — phenomena in their self-givenness. - **Epoché ≠ Cartesian doubt:** Descartes *denies* existence to reach the indubitable cogito; Husserl neither affirms nor denies — he **suspends** (*enthält sich*). The epoché is not scepticism but a *methodological neutralisation* that frees the philosopher to describe without prejudice. ✅ (This distinction is a favourite UPSC trap — see §5.)
- **The phenomenological reduction** is commonly presented as the positive redirection enabled by the epoché: inquiry turns from the object simply posited to the correlation between object and mode of givenness. The two functions must still be distinguished, and this relation does not make eidetic or transcendental reduction later rungs of one logically compulsory ladder.

#### 1.7 Why Bracketing and Reduction Matter (PYQ 2023 — 15m) ⚠️

The epoché and reductions are not arbitrary methodological quirks; they serve three interrelated purposes:
1. **Secure a domain of indubitable givenness** — after the epoché, the stream is apodictically unavoidable as experienced, without making every self-description adequate or infallible in the same way that the independent existence of the world can. ✅ 2. **Avoid the Cartesian slide into substance-dualism** — unlike Descartes, Husserl does not *conclude* to a soul-substance over against a body-substance. The reduction reveals consciousness not as a *thing* but as a *relational field* (intentionality). The reduction reformulates rather than automatically eliminates every mind-body problem. ⚠️
3. **Support a science of essences** — phenomenological suspension can free inquiry from factual existence-commitments, while eidetic variation independently varies factual or imagined examples to disclose invariants. Eidetic variation can operate in descriptive phenomenology and does not logically await a completed transcendental reduction. ⚠️

### SESSION 4 — Intentionality, Fulfilment, Immanence, Transcendence and Horizon

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** Intentionality means that consciousness is always directed to something; it does not mean having a plan, and it does not imprison us among private representations.

**Technical definition:** Intentionality is the directedness of consciousness: perceiving, remembering, imagining and judging are always of or about an object. The act and object correlate without becoming two separable things. An empty or signitive intention means an object without intuitive presence, as when a name or proposition is understood; intuitive fulfilment occurs when the object is given in a way that confirms, disappoints or enriches the intention. An experience is immanent as lived in the stream, while a physical object is transcendent because no finite series of profiles exhausts it. Horizon names the open field of possible fulfilments, corrections and further appearances.

### SESSION 5 — Noesis, Noema and Constitution without the Intermediary-Picture Error

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** Noesis names the intentional act or process; noema names the object as intended or its sense within that act, not a private picture placed between consciousness and the world.

**Technical definition:** Noesis is the intentional act-side, including the mode of perceiving, judging, remembering or valuing. Noema is the object-as-intended or noematic sense within the correlation. It must be distinguished from an inner image, a psychological content and an intermediary entity that blocks worldly access. Constitution is the achievement or disclosure of stable sense and objectivity through syntheses of profiles, fulfilments and horizons; it does not mean that a mind causally creates physical objects. Evidence is the fulfilled givenness through which a claim is justified, whereas truth is not identical with a subject's present feeling of evidence.

#### HYLE and MORPHE — the internal structure of the intentional act ✅ (*Ideas I* §85)

Noesis/noema is the *correlation* between act and object. **Hyle/morphe is the analysis of the noetic side itself**, and it is what stops phenomenology from being a bare assertion that "consciousness is directed."

| | **HYLE** (matter, *stuff*) | **MORPHE** (form) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The non-intentional **sensory content** actually lived through — colour-data, sound-data, tactile-data, the felt "material" of the experience | The **animating apprehension** that interprets the hyletic stuff as a presentation *of* an object |
| Is it itself intentional? | **No.** Hyletic data are not *of* anything; they do not point beyond themselves. This is their whole point. | **Yes.** *Morphe* is what confers directedness — it "animates" (*beseelt*) the material |
| Is it perceived? | **No** — I do not perceive my sensation-data; I perceive *the house*. They are lived (*erlebt*), not observed | Also not observed in the natural attitude; both are disclosed only under the reduction |

3. Husserl's later genetic phenomenology largely absorbs the hyle into passive synthesis, which is a tacit concession.

### SESSION 6 — Temporal Synthesis, Retention, Primal Impression and Protention

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** A melody is heard as one unfolding whole because consciousness holds the just-past and anticipates the just-coming within a structured living present.

**Technical definition:** Internal time-consciousness analyses the living present through retention, primal impression and protention. Primal impression is the now-phase; retention is the just-past still held within present experience and must not be confused with recollection; protention is the open anticipation of what is about to occur. These moments form a passive temporal synthesis that supports identity across phases before explicit judgment. Active synthesis later includes deliberate identification, predication and confirmation. The absolute flow is not another object in time, which creates a reflexive difficulty.

```text
DISAPPOINTMENT: the melody takes an unexpected turn

```

*The present is thick: it includes the just-past and just-coming as structured moments.*

#### INNER TIME-CONSCIOUSNESS — RETENTION · PRIMAL IMPRESSION · PROTENTION ✅

**The problem.** If consciousness were a succession of momentary now-points, we could never experience a **melody** — only an isolated tone, replaced by another isolated tone, with no experience of sequence at all. Yet we plainly hear a melody, a sentence, a gesture. **How is the experience of temporal extension possible within a present?**

**Husserl's answer — the three-fold structure of the living present ✅:**

```
        │                                                    │
   RETENTION   ←──────  PRIMAL IMPRESSION  ──────→   PROTENTION

  still held in           the now-phase             to-come, already
```

|---|---|---|---|
| **Primal impression** | *Urimpression* | The "source-point," the strictly now-phase of what is experienced | Not an instant *in* time but the origin of time-constitution |
| **Retention** | *Retention* / **primary memory** | The just-elapsed phase **still held** within the present, sinking away but not yet gone | ⚠️ **Retention is NOT recollection.** Retention is *part of the present perception*; recollection (*Wiedererinnerung*, **secondary memory**) is a *re-presentation* (*Vergegenwärtigung*) of something no longer present. **Canonical example ✅ — hearing a melody.** As each tone sounds it is a primal impression; the tones already heard are retained (with progressively "sinking" clarity, the *Ablaufsphänomene* or "running-off phenomena," pictured by Husserl in the well-known time-diagram); and the next tone is protentively anticipated. What is heard is therefore *a melody*, not a series of tones. Secondary example: hearing a **spoken sentence** — the earlier words must be retained for the sentence to have sense.

**Consequences worth stating ⚠️:**
1. **Objective temporal identity is constituted through the living flow; worldly time is not denied.** Husserl reaches a level he calls the "absolute time-constituting flow of consciousness," which he says we lack names for — a candid limit. 2. 3. It grounds **identity and habituality**: the ego's sedimented history is possible only because the past is retained rather than merely recorded. 4. It is the doctrine **Heidegger takes over and transforms** into ecstatic temporality (having-been / making-present / coming-towards) in *Being and Time* — see `Existentialism.md` §3.
5. ❓ **Objection:** the account risks regress — if the flow constitutes time, what constitutes the flow? Husserl's answer, that the flow constitutes itself ("the flow of consciousness constitutes its own unity"), is either a genuine self-grounding or a stipulation, and commentators divide.

### SESSION 7 — Eidetic Science, Free Imaginative Variation and Invariant Essence

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** Eidetic science uses free imaginative variation to identify the invariant essence that must remain possible for something to count as a phenomenon of a given kind.

**Technical definition:** An essence or eidos is an invariant structure or possibility-condition disclosed across possible cases. Factual sciences investigate actual individuals and causal regularities; eidetic sciences investigate necessary structures of possible objects and experiences. Eidetic reduction employs free imaginative variation: alter accidental features of an example until removing a feature would change its kind. A triangle may vary in size and colour but not in three-sidedness; a promise may vary in wording but not lose the undertaking that makes it a promise; perception may vary in content but retains perspectival givenness. Categorial intuition gives categorial forms and states of affairs beyond simple sensuous intuition; eidetic intuition, by contrast, apprehends essences disclosed through free imaginative variation. They are related extensions of intuitive givenness, not identical achievements, and neither should be reduced to induction, dictionary definition or separated Platonism.

#### 1.4 The Eidetic Reduction (from Fact to Essence) ✅

- Eidetic inquiry distinguishes *particular* or imagined examples (this red, that sound) from the *essential structures* they instantiate (redness-as-such, sound-as-such). It is often combined with phenomenological suspension, but free imaginative variation does not logically depend on a completed transcendental reduction.
- The **eidetic reduction** moves from the factual instance to its **eidos** (essence) via the technique of **free imaginative variation** (§2). - Purpose: phenomenology aims to be not an empirical chronicle of experiences but a science of the **essential structures** of consciousness and its objects. ⚠️

#### 2. THEORY OF ESSENCES ✅

#### 2.1 Eidos / Wesen — What Essences Are ✅

- **Essence** (*Wesen*, *eidos*): the invariant structure or "whatness" of a phenomenon — that which makes it the kind of thing it is and without which it would cease to be that kind.
- Essences are **ideal** — not spatio-temporal, not causally efficacious, not generated by mental acts. - Essences are **non-psychological** — they are not "ideas in the mind" (that would be psychologism); they are the *meanings* that intentional acts *grasp*, not produce.
- Essences are **not Platonic Forms in the separated sense** — they do not inhabit a *χωρισμός* (separate realm). Husserl holds that essences are given *in and through* particular instances, not in a detached heaven of Forms. There is no "two-world" theory. ✅
- Essences are **objective** — they are intersubjectively accessible; different subjects can intuit the same essence.

#### 2.2 Free Imaginative Variation — the Technique ✅

1. Begin with a factual instance (e.g. a perceived physical object, a melody, a judgement).
2. Vary it freely in imagination — change its colour, size, material, duration, context.
3. Note at each step: does the phenomenon *survive* the variation, or does it collapse into something else?
4. That which **cannot be varied away without destroying the phenomenon** is its essence. ✅
- Example: vary "triangle" — change its angles, side lengths, material (drawn, imagined, physical) — within Euclidean geometry, closed three-sided plane-figure structure remains; angle-sum depends on the geometrical framework. These are the *essential* (eidetic) features. - The technique is *not* induction (which generalises from many observed cases); it is an *a priori* procedure conducted in imagination. A single instance suffices to launch variation. ✅

#### 2.3 Wesensschau (Eidetic Intuition) and Categorial Intuition ✅

- Husserl claims that essences are intuitively disclosed rather than inductively inferred. **Wesensschau** means intuition of essences or **eidetic intuition**: apprehension of an essence disclosed through free imaginative variation. - **Categorial intuition** (*kategoriale Anschauung*), introduced in the Sixth *Logical Investigation*, concerns the givenness of non-sensuous categorial forms and states of affairs beyond simple sensuous intuition, including forms such as "and," "or" and predicative being. ✅ - The two doctrines are related in extending intuition beyond simple sensation, but they are not identical: categorial intuition fulfils categorial intentions, whereas eidetic intuition apprehends essences through variation.
- **Presupposition:** Husserl must maintain that there is a form of *intellectual* seeing that is as legitimate and self-grounding as sense-perception. This is a strong claim and the source of much criticism (see §4). ⚠️

#### 2.4 Regional Ontologies — Material and Formal Essences ✅

- Essences fall into **regions** — each region of objects (nature, consciousness, culture, mathematics) has its own domain of essential truths. - A **regional ontology** articulates the essential structures governing a particular region: e.g. the ontology of the physical thing (extension, duration, causality), the ontology of the psyche (intentionality, temporal flow), the ontology of the social (intersubjectivity, norms). ✅ - **Formal ontology** vs **material (regional) ontology:**
  - *Formal ontology* deals with the most general categorical forms (object, property, relation, state of affairs, whole-part) that apply to *every* region. It is analogous to formal logic. - *Material ontology* deals with the specific essential structures of *each* region. - Husserl envisions phenomenology as the *foundational science* that grounds all regional ontologies by clarifying how their objects are constituted in consciousness. ⚠️

#### 2.5 Essences as Exhibiting Continuity Between Consciousness and Being (PYQ 2024 — 15m) ✅

This is a subtle and exam-critical point:
- For Husserl, there is a **correlational a priori** — the essential structure of the *object* (noema) is correlated with the essential structure of the *act* that intends it (noesis). ✅ - "Object-as-experienced" and "experiencing-act" are not two independently existing entities that happen to meet; they are *essentially* correlated — you cannot describe the one without the other.
- **Consequence:** essences are not located exclusively "in" consciousness (idealism in the subjective sense) nor exclusively "in" being (naïve realism); their role is articulated through the intentional correlation rather than location in a third realm. The essence of "perception of a spatial thing" simultaneously tells us about the *structure of perceiving* and the *structure of the perceived thing*. ✅ - This is how essences "exhibit a continuity between consciousness and being" — they bridge the gap because they *are* the a priori structure of the consciousness–world correlation. Neither term is intelligible without the other. ⚠️
- **Implication:** phenomenology does not choose between idealism and realism in the traditional sense; it reframes the realism/idealism opposition through correlation, while transcendental idealism remains disputed — the level of constituting intentionality. ⚠️

  - (c) the empirical psyche studied by psychology. ✅
- **PYQ 2025 (20m) — Husserl's "I think" vs Descartes' cogito:**
  - *Agreement:* both start with the indubitable self-givenness of the thinking act. Both seek a presuppositionless beginning. - *Divergence:* (i) Descartes *infers* a substantial soul (*res cogitans*) — he slides from "I think" to "I am a thing that thinks." ### SESSION 9 — Psychologism, Ideal Meaning and the Bridge to Transcendental Phenomenology

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

**Plain-language definition:** Psychologism mistakes facts about how minds happen to think for standards that determine whether a judgment is logically valid or true.

**Technical definition:** Psychologism reduces logical laws, meanings, numbers or validity to empirical psychological facts and processes. This differs from descriptive psychology, which legitimately studies real acts. In the Logical Investigations, Husserl argues that logical laws are ideal, normative, universally valid and necessary, whereas psychological laws are empirical, causal, contingent and species-dependent. Psychologism confuses the act of judging with judgment-content or proposition, truth and evidence; it cannot explain error or normative correctness and tends toward relativism or scepticism. Ideal meanings remain grasped in psychological acts without being reduced to those acts.

#### 3. AVOIDANCE OF PSYCHOLOGISM ✅

#### 3.1 Psychologism Defined ✅

**Psychologism** is the doctrine that the laws of logic (e.g. the principle of non-contradiction, modus ponens, the laws of valid inference) are **empirical generalisations about how human beings in fact think** — that logic is a branch, or at least a consequence, of descriptive psychology.

Representatives: John Stuart Mill, Theodor Lipps, Christoph Sigwart, and (in his earlier phase) Husserl himself.

Specific claims of psychologism: ✅
- "The principle of non-contradiction describes how we *cannot* think" — it reports a psychological impossibility.
- "Logical laws hold because of the constitution of the human mind" — they would be different for differently constituted beings.
- "Truth is what we are compelled to believe" — truth reduces to a psychological fact.

#### 3.2 Husserl's Own Early Position — and Frege's Review ✅❓

- In the *Philosophie der Arithmetik* (1891), Husserl attempted to ground the concept of number in the psychological acts of collecting and counting — a project his later self classed as psychologistic.
- **Gottlob Frege** reviewed the book severely in 1894 (*Zeitschrift für Philosophie und philosophische Kritik*). ⚠️ **Paraphrase, not quotation** — Frege's central charge is that Husserl slides from the objective *content* of thought to the subjective *act* of thinking it, so that everything in logic and arithmetic is turned into a *Vorstellung* (mental presentation) belonging to a particular mind at a particular time; on such a view, objects "become blurred" and arithmetic collapses into a description of mental processes.
  > ❌ **Do not write** the widely circulated sentence "one might as well try to explain astronomy by the psychology of astronomers" as a Frege **quotation**. It is a textbook paraphrase of the objection, not a line from the 1894 review. ✅ **Safe form:** "Frege's objection, in substance, is that explaining arithmetic by the mental operations of the arithmetician confuses the content thought with the act of thinking it — a confusion no more acceptable in logic than it would be in astronomy."
- By the *Logical Investigations* (1900–01), Husserl had reversed himself entirely and devoted the whole of the *Prolegomena to Pure Logic* (Vol. I) to demolishing psychologism.
- ❓ **Contested causal claim — do not assert it flatly.** Whether Frege's review *caused* the reversal is disputed. **Føllesdal** (1958) argued for Frege's decisive influence; **J. N. Mohanty** (*Husserl and Frege*, 1982) argued from the manuscripts that Husserl's anti-psychologism was already developing independently, on grounds arising from Bolzano and Lotze and from his own difficulties with the *Philosophie der Arithmetik*. ✅ **Safe form for an answer:** "Frege's 1894 review coincided with, and probably sharpened, a reversal whose sources scholars still dispute."

#### 3.3 Husserl's Four Arguments Against Psychologism (*Prolegomena*) ✅

| # | Argument | Logic |
|---|---|---|
| **(i)** | **Necessary → merely probable** | Psychological laws are inductive generalisations — at best *highly probable* but never strictly *necessary*. Logical laws (non-contradiction, excluded middle) are *necessarily* true. You cannot derive the necessary from the merely probable. ✅ |
| **(ii)** | **Confusion of ideal content and real act** | The *content* of the judgment "2+2=4" is an ideal, atemporal *meaning* (a proposition); the *act* of judging it is a real, datable psychic event (occurring at 3pm on Tuesday in my brain). Psychologism confuses these — it treats the timeless proposition as a feature of the temporal act. ✅ |
| **(iii)** | **Relativism and self-refutation** | If logical laws hold only "for beings constituted like us," then truth is **species-relative** (*Anthropologismus*). But the assertion "truth is relative" is itself offered as *absolutely* true — so it contradicts itself. Psychologism therefore leads to a self-refuting relativism. ✅ |
| **(iv)** | **Conditions of thinking ≠ grounds of validity** | That we *must* think in accordance with the law of non-contradiction (a *causal* necessity of our psychology) does not *explain* why the law *holds* (its *logical* validity). The cause of our believing something is not the *reason* for its truth. To conflate them is the *genetic fallacy*. ✅ |

#### 3.4 Positive Consequence: Ideal Objectivity ✅

- Logical and mathematical truths belong to a domain of **ideal objectivity** — they are neither physical objects nor mental events but *meanings* (*Bedeutungen*) that subsist independently of any act of thinking them. - Phenomenology studies consciousness *as the site where such ideal meanings are grasped* — without *reducing* the meanings to the acts that grasp them. The acts are real; the contents are ideal. ✅ - This dual recognition — real acts, ideal contents — is Husserl's decisive move against both psychologism (which denied the ideal) and Platonism-in-the-naïve-sense (which denied that the ideal is *accessible* to consciousness).

#### 3.5 The Objection: Does the Transcendental Turn Re-introduce Psychologism? (PYQ 2022 — 10m) ❓

- **The charge:** after the *Ideas* (1913), Husserl locates all meaning-constitution in the **transcendental ego**. If truth is "constituted in transcendental subjectivity," have we not made it subject-dependent again — i.e., re-introduced psychologism at the transcendental level? - **Husserl's standard reply:**
  1. The transcendental ego is NOT the empirical psyche. The empirical psyche is a mundane object *within* the world; the transcendental ego is the *condition* under which world (and psyche) have meaning at all. Reducing to it is therefore not psychology. ✅ 2. "Constitution" does not mean "creation" or "fabrication." To say that the meaning of "2+2=4" is constituted in transcendental subjectivity is to say that its *sense* is *disclosed* there — not that the subject *makes it up*. The ideal content remains atemporal and objective; subjectivity is the *medium of access*, not the *source* of validity. ⚠️ 3. The transcendental ego is *intersubjective* (the community of transcendental egos — "transcendental intersubjectivity") — so meanings are constituted not for *one* ego but for *any possible* rational subject. ⚠️
- **Assessment:** Whether this reply fully succeeds is contested. Critics (including later Heidegger and Merleau-Ponty) hold that the notion of a "transcendental ego" remains too close to the idealist subject of the tradition. ❓

#### DEFINITION / WHAT THIS IS CALLED

#### 3A. THE FIFTH *CARTESIAN MEDITATION* — INTERSUBJECTIVITY ✅ (the answer to the solipsism charge)

**Source and status:** lectures delivered in **Paris, February 1929**; published first in **French** (tr. Levinas and Peiffer, **1931**) as *Méditations cartésiennes*; the German *Cartesianische Meditationen* appeared posthumously in **1950** (*Husserliana* I). ⚠️ **The French text is the first publication** — worth one clause, and it explains Levinas's early and intimate engagement.

**The problem, stated exactly.** After the transcendental reduction, everything — including other people — has the status of *sense constituted for my ego*. If so, the "other" is a moment of my own constituting life, and phenomenology ends in **transcendental solipsism**. Husserl regards this as the most serious objection to his own programme and devotes the longest Meditation to it.

**⚠️ First, correct the question.** Husserl is **not** trying to *prove* that other minds exist — that would be a natural-attitude question, and the epoché has suspended existence-positing altogether. He is asking: **how does the sense "another subject, for whom the world is also there" get constituted in my experience, with the evidence proper to it?** Answers that treat the Fifth Meditation as a failed proof of other minds have misidentified the task, and saying so is the strongest single sentence available on this sub-topic.

**The argument, numbered ✅:**
1. **A further reduction — the sphere of ownness (*Eigenheitssphäre*).** Abstract from everything in my experience that owes its sense to other subjectivity: all cultural predicates ("hammer," "book," "road"), all objectivity-for-anyone. What remains is my **primordial sphere** — the world reduced to what is constituted purely within my own ego. 2. **Within it, my body is unique.** My **lived body** (*Leib*) is the only physical thing that is also an organ of will, a field of sensings, and the absolute "**here**" from which everything else is "there." Every other body is a mere *Körper* (physical body).
3. **In the primordial sphere another body appears** which resembles mine in appearance and, crucially, in *behaviour* — it moves in a governed, expressive way.
4. **Pairing (*Paarung*).** A **passive synthesis of association** occurs: my body and that body are constituted as a pair, an original coupling that happens before any judgement or inference. 5. **Appresentation (*Appräsentation*).** On the basis of the pairing, the other's subjectivity is **appresented** — co-intended without being itself presented, exactly as the unseen back of a table is appresented in seeing its front. ⚠️ **This is analogy in the sense of an analogising transfer of sense, not an argument from analogy.** Husserl explicitly denies that it is an inference. 6. **The "as if I were there."** The other is constituted as an alter ego: another "I" who is at *his* here — which for me is a *there* — and for whom the world is given in the aspects I would have if I were there. The other's ego is my ego "as if I were over there," an **intentional modification** of my own. 7. **Non-originality is a necessity, not a defect.** ✅ If the other's experiences were given to me **originally** (in person, as my own are), the other would not be an other — he would be **me**. The *inaccessibility* of the other's stream is therefore part of the very *sense* "other," and it is verified rather than violated by experience.
8. **Verification is harmonious behaviour.** The appresentation is confirmed, or disappointed, by the continuing concordance of the other's conduct — a fulfilment structure exactly like that of ordinary perception. 9. **Result — objectivity itself is constituted.** Once other egos are constituted, the world is constituted as **there for everyone**: objectivity just *is* intersubjective validity. The transcendental field is therefore not one ego but a **community of monads** (Husserl adopts Leibniz's word — but these monads, unlike Leibniz's, have windows: see `Rationalism.md` §2.3).

**Presuppositions ⚠️:**
- **P1** The reduction to ownness is coherent — that one *can* strip away all sense derived from others and be left with a self-contained sphere.
- **P2** My own body is originally given to me as a lived body prior to any experience of others. - **P3** Pairing is genuinely passive and pre-predicative, not a disguised inference. - **P4** The order of constitution is asymmetrical: I am constituted first, others through me.

**Strongest objections → replies ❓:**
| Objection | Source | Reply available to Husserl | Residual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Circularity:** the sphere of ownness is supposed to be free of all reference to others, yet my body is recognised as a *body like others'* only because I already possess the sense "other." The argument presupposes what it constitutes. | **Alfred Schutz**, "The Problem of Transcendental Intersubjectivity in Husserl" (1957) — the classic critique | The ownness sphere is an *abstractive layer*, not a temporal stage; the analysis is of validity-founding, not of chronology. | ⚠️ **The most serious objection**; most commentators think Schutz identifies a real strain even if the reply is available. |
| **The Other is not known by appresentation but encountered in the Look**: the other reveals himself by making *me* an object, in shame — an experience of my being-for-others, not a constitution of his being-for-me. | **Sartre**, *Being and Nothingness* (1943), Part III | Sartre describes a *modality* of encountering the other that itself presupposes the constituted sense "other subject." | ⚠️ Strong and genuinely rival; see `Existentialism.md`. |
| **Reducing the Other to an alter ego is the erasure of otherness.** Constituted as "another me," the Other loses precisely what makes him Other; ethics, not epistemology, is first philosophy. | **Levinas**, *Totality and Infinity* (1961) — and Levinas was the *Meditations*' first translator | Husserl can reply that the analysis is of *sense-constitution*, which is silent on ethical priority. | ⚠️ The reply concedes Levinas's point rather than answering it. |
| **There is no problem to solve:** Dasein is *Mitsein* — being-with — from the outset, so a solitary ego that must reach others is a pseudo-starting-point. | **Heidegger**, *Being and Time* (1927) §26 | Husserl: Heidegger's "always already with others" is a description, not a clarification of how that sense is legitimated. | ⚠️ The dispute is about whether legitimation is needed at all. |

**Executable verdict:** "The Fifth Meditation directly confronts the programme's strongest intersubjectivity pressure. Its permanent achievement is the demonstration that the *inaccessibility* of the other's experience is constitutive of the sense 'other,' not an epistemic deficiency to be repaired — which reframes rather than simply disposes of the traditional other-minds problem. Its ownness-first structure remains contested: an analysis that must *begin* from a sphere purified of others can reach the other only as a modification of the self, so Levinas's objection is not answered but conceded. Husserl links objectivity to intersubjectivity, while ownness-first method remains vulnerable to circularity and reduction of alterity."

#### Development and owner scope

#### Method and intentionality

- Suspension (*epoché*) withholds the existence-posit; phenomenological reduction redirects to givenness; transcendental reduction asks about constituting subjectivity; eidetic reduction varies invariants.

#### Essences and psychologism

- Transcendental subjectivity is not a Cartesian substance or merely Kant's “I think plus content.”

### RAPID REVISION 10 — Intersubjectivity - The Fifth Cartesian Meditation, Pairing, Appresentation and the Answer to Solipsism



#### Rapid recall

- **Problem:** after the reduction, the Other risks being a mere moment of my ego = **transcendental solipsism** (Husserl's gravest self-objection). - **First correct the question:** not "prove other minds exist" (epoche suspended existence) but "how is the *sense* 'another subject' constituted?"
- **Machinery:** **sphere of ownness** -> **lived body** (*Leib*, absolute "here") -> another body resembling mine in **behaviour** -> **pairing** (*Paarung*, passive) -> **appresentation** (co-intended, not presented) -> **alter ego** ("as if I were there"). - **Appresentation = analogising *transfer of sense*, NOT an argument from analogy/inference.**
- **Non-originality is necessary:** if I lived the other's stream originally, he would be *me*; inaccessibility = the sense "other." Objectivity = intersubjective validity; a **community of monads** (with windows). - **Objections:** **Schutz** (circularity - gravest), **Sartre** (the Look), **Levinas** (erases otherness), **Heidegger** (Dasein is *Mitsein*).
- **Provenance:** Paris lectures **1929**; French **1931** (Levinas/Peiffer); German posthumous **1950** (*Husserliana* I).

### ADVANCED SESSION 1 — The Programme - Presuppositionless Rigorous Science, 'To the Things Themselves' and the One-Screen Map



#### What "presuppositionless" and "rigorous science" actually commit Husserl to

- **Presuppositionless** does not mean "with no beliefs"; it means *positing nothing that has not shown itself with evidence*. The one thing philosophy may not borrow uncritically is the very thing the natural attitude takes for granted - the independent existence of the world (subtopic 2).
- **Rigorous science** (*strenge Wissenschaft*, from the 1911 manifesto *Philosophie als strenge Wissenschaft*) is a claim *against* two rivals at once: against **naturalism/psychologism**, which would make philosophy a branch of empirical psychology (subtopic 9), and against **historicism/worldview-philosophy**, which would reduce it to the spirit of an age. Phenomenology claims a footing as firm as mathematics but about *meaning* rather than number.
- **"Zu den Sachen selbst!"** appears in the Introduction to Volume II of the *Logical Investigations*. Translate it carefully: *Sachen* means "matters" or "subject-matters," **not physical things**. The slogan says "back to how the matters themselves are given," which is the opposite of naive realism - a favourite misreading.

> ANALYSIS: the strongest opening you can write on any Husserl question states the *architecture* first - method (reductions) yields the field, the theory of essences describes its structures, and the war on psychologism guarantees the results are objective rather than merely mental. An answer that defines epoche and stops has missed that the three terms are one movement.

**Provenance caution (worth a mark):** name the 1911 *Logos* essay for the "rigorous science" claim and the *Logical Investigations* (1900-01) Introduction for the motto; do not attribute the slogan to *Ideas I*.



### ADVANCED SESSION 2 — The Natural Attitude and Its General Thesis - Why the World Must Be Bracketed, Not Denied



#### The subtle point examiners reward: not false, but uncritical

- **The natural attitude is not a mistake to be corrected.** Husserl never says the world does not exist. He says the *general thesis* is philosophically **naive** - it functions before any question is asked, so it smuggles an un-examined assumption into every inquiry that rests on it. - **The natural sciences are inside it, not above it.** Physics and psychology *presuppose* an independently existing domain; that is exactly why they cannot ground themselves. Only a stance *outside* the general thesis can ask how any domain gets constituted as meaningful. This is the seed of the *Crisis* critique of scientific objectivism (subtopic 8).
- **This sets up the epoche precisely.** Because the thesis is a *positing* of existence, the corrective is not to deny existence (that would just be another positing, the sceptic's) but to *suspend* the positing altogether (subtopic 3).

> ANALYSIS: the 2021 question asks for a *critical exposition* of Husserl's criticism of the natural attitude. The examiner-strong move is to insist on the two-sided verdict: the natural attitude is (i) indispensable and true enough for life and science, yet (ii) disqualifying for philosophy until it is bracketed. Miss either side and the answer collapses into "Husserl thinks common sense is wrong," which is exactly wrong.

**Comparison to keep labelled:** Heidegger will object that you cannot step outside being-in-the-world at all (subtopic 8/10); Merleau-Ponty that the return to the natural attitude is inevitable and enriching. These are *sympathetic radicalisations*, not refutations - say so.



### ADVANCED SESSION 3 — Epoche, Bracketing and the Reduction - Suspension (Not Doubt), the Residue and Why It Matters



#### Epoche is NOT Cartesian doubt - state the contrast exactly

- **Descartes doubts; Husserl suspends.** Descartes *denies* existence to whatever can be doubted, hunting for the one thing that *cannot* be doubted (the cogito). Husserl neither affirms nor denies - he *withholds assent* (*enthalt sich*). The epoche is not scepticism; it is a **methodological neutralisation** that frees description from prejudice. - **What survives is not a substance but a field.** Descartes' method yields a *thing that thinks* (subtopic 8's warning); Husserl's yields the *stream of intentional experience*, given with apodictic self-evidence but not hypostatised into a soul.
- **The three purposes (the 2023 answer already in seed form):** the epoche and phenomenological/transcendental reductions (i) **secure a domain of givenness**, and (ii) **avoid the Cartesian slide into substance-dualism** by disclosing consciousness as a relational field. (iii) **Eidetic inquiry supplies a science of essences** by freely varying factual or imagined examples. This third task can be coordinated with suspension but is not logically dependent on completing the transcendental reduction.

> ANALYSIS: notice that only the third purpose is *peculiar* to Husserl; the first two he shares in ambition with Descartes but reaches without Descartes' costs. That single observation lifts a 15-marker from description to assessment.

**Provenance caution:** cite *Ideas I* (1913) by section - the epoche is developed around sections 31-32, the principle of principles at section 24. Section numbers are edition-invariant; the Boyce Gibson (1931) and Kersten (1982) translations differ in wording, so paraphrase or name the translation.

> WRONG: never write that the epoche "denies the existence of the world" or "proves the world is only in the mind." It suspends a *positing*; it decides nothing about existence.



### ADVANCED SESSION 4 — Intentionality - Consciousness OF Something, Noesis-Noema, and Why Hallucination Is a Genuine Act



#### From Brentano, but transformed - and the hallucination pay-off

- **Source and transformation.** The directedness thesis comes from **Franz Brentano** (*intentionale Inexistenz*), but Husserl transforms it: intentionality is not a psychological mark of the mental but the *structure of sense* itself, disclosed under the reduction. The noema is not a mental picture inside a box; it is the object *as meant*, so there is no inner image to be matched against an outer thing.
- **Why representationalism dissolves.** Because the intended object just *is* the object-as-meant, no inference from an inner representation to an outer thing is required. This is what separates Husserl at a stroke from Locke's "veil of ideas" and Russell's sense-data - the point returns forcefully under hyle/morphe (subtopic 5).
- **Hallucination (the 2019 demand), stated for full marks.** A hallucination has (i) a noesis - a genuine perceptual-seeming act - and (ii) a noema - the percept-as-meant, with determinate shape and colour. It is therefore *paradigmatically intentional*. Intentionality requires **directedness, not existence**; the mistake is to think an intentional object must be a real referent. - **The principle of principles** (*Ideas I* section 24) grounds all of this: originary intuitive givenness is the source of legitimacy - "accept what is given as it gives itself, but only within the limits in which it is given."

> ANALYSIS: contemporary scholarship anchor (labelled, not doctrinal proof) - Chang Liu, "How the Phenomenal Determines the Intentional - A Husserlian Approach," *Husserl Studies*, vol. 42 (2026), reopens exactly this act-object relation, arguing that the *phenomenal character* of an experience **grounds** its intentional content. Cite it only as evidence that the noesis-noema problem is live research (it engages the analytic "phenomenal intentionality" debate), never as proof of a doctrinal claim.

> WRONG: "intentionality means the object must exist." False - intentional objects may be non-existent (hallucinations, fantasies); directedness is the point.



### ADVANCED SESSION 5 — Hyle and Morphe - Non-Intentional Sense-Matter, Animation, and the Anti-Sense-Datum Argument



#### The anti-sense-datum argument, and the Merleau-Ponty / Sartre objections

- **The polemical point.** British empiricism (Locke, Hume, and later Russell's sense-data) treats the raw sensory element as *itself an object* that we perceive and then infer the world behind it - the "veil of ideas." Husserl's hyle/morphe blocks this: the hyle is **lived through, never looked at**. We perceive the *tomato*, not a red patch from which we deduce a tomato. There is no inner object, hence no sceptical gap to bridge. This continues the anti-representationalism of intentionality (subtopic 4).
- **Objection 1 - Merleau-Ponty.** To keep a layer of formless *hyle* at all is to smuggle back the very empiricist "sensation" Husserl claims to defeat. In perception there is no raw uninterpreted matter; the body already gives perception its form. So the hyle/morphe scheme is an unstable half-measure.
- **Objection 2 - Sartre.** Consciousness is pure translucent intentionality with *no* contents in it at all; positing hyletic *data inside* consciousness re-introduces exactly the inner "stuff" phenomenology should have expelled. For Sartre consciousness has no inside.
- **Reply available to Husserl.** The hyle is not a hidden object but a *dependent moment* abstractable only in reflection; naming it records a real phenomenal difference (the *sensuous fullness* of perception versus the emptiness of mere thought) without hypostatising it.

> ANALYSIS: the examiner-strong verdict is even-handed: hyle/morphe *destroys* the sense-datum picture (a genuine achievement) yet *itself* looks like a residue of the empiricism it attacks - which is precisely why Husserl's successors abolished the hyle. Say both.

**Provenance caution:** the fully worked hyle/morphe doctrine is *Ideas I* (1913, around sections 85-86); Merleau-Ponty's critique is the *Phenomenology of Perception* (1945), Sartre's the *Transcendence of the Ego* (1936-37) and *Being and Nothingness* (1943).



### ADVANCED SESSION 6 — Inner Time-Consciousness - Retention, Primal Impression, Protention and the Absolute Flow



#### The examinable heart: retention is NOT recollection; and the absolute flow

- **Retention vs recollection - the single most testable distinction.** *Retention* (primary memory) is *part of the present perception*: I do not remember the note just played, I still *hear* it as just-past. *Recollection* (*Wiedererinnerung*, secondary memory) is a **re-presentation** (*Vergegenwartigung*) of something no longer present - a fresh act that makes the past present again. Retention is *passive and continuous*; recollection is *active and reproductive*.
- **Protention is falsifiable in experience:** when a melody takes an unexpected turn, the protention is *disappointed* - which is exactly what shows it was operative.
- **The absolute flow and the regress worry.** Beneath constituted time Husserl reaches the "absolute time-constituting flow of consciousness," for which, he candidly says, "we lack names." Objection: if the flow constitutes time, what constitutes the flow - a regress? Husserl's reply is that **the flow constitutes its own unity** ("the flow of consciousness constitutes its own unity"); critics judge this either a deep insight into self-manifesting subjectivity or an unstable stopping-point. - **What it accomplishes.** (i) It answers **Hume**: Hume found only discrete impressions and no self because he looked for the self *among* the contents rather than in the constituting flow. (ii) It grounds **identity and habituality** (subtopic 8): a sedimented history is possible only because the past is *retained*, not merely recorded. (iii) **Heidegger transforms** it into ecstatic temporality (having-been / making-present / coming-towards) in *Being and Time*.

> CAUTION: provenance is itself examinable here. *On the Phenomenology of the Consciousness of Internal Time* gathers lectures of **1893-1917**, was edited by **Edith Stein**, and was published by **Heidegger in 1928**. Always flag this editorial history when you cite it - the text you quote was shaped by two other philosophers.



### ADVANCED SESSION 7 — Essences - Eidetic Reduction, Free Imaginative Variation, and the Continuity of Consciousness and Being



#### Categorial intuition, regional ontologies, and the continuity of consciousness and being

- **Categorial and eidetic intuition are related but distinct.** In the *Logical Investigations* VI, categorial intuition explains the givenness of categorial forms and states of affairs beyond simple sensuous intuition. In Husserl's developed eidetic method, **Wesensschau** or eidetic intuition apprehends an essence disclosed through free imaginative variation. The earlier doctrine helps make non-sensuous givenness intelligible, but it must not be equated with the later method-specific act.
- **Material vs formal essences; regional ontologies.** *Formal* essences belong to any object whatever (formal ontology - unity, plurality, relation); *material* (regional) essences belong to a domain - physical thing, living body, mind, number - each governed by a **regional ontology** with its own a priori laws. Phenomenology maps these regions. - **Not Platonism, not psychologism.** The eidos is **not** a Form in a separate heaven (no *chorismos*, no separate existence) and **not** a psychological idea in a mind. It is the *necessary structure of the object as such*, given in intuition. Holding this middle line is the whole point - lose it toward Plato and you re-mythologise; lose it toward psychology and you collapse into the psychologism subtopic 9 forbids.
- **The continuity of consciousness and being (the 2024 demand).** Because every essence is disclosed only as the correlate of a corresponding mode of consciousness (perceived thing <-> perceiving, number <-> counting), Husserl asserts a **correlational a priori**: being and consciousness are not two separate realms to be bridged but *two sides of one intentional structure*. Consciousness is not sealed off from being; it is *of* being throughout.

> CAUTION: this continuity must **not** be read as **subjective idealism** ("only my mind exists" / "things are bundles of my ideas"). Husserl's claim is transcendental - the *sense* "being" is constituted in and for consciousness - not the Berkeleyan claim that objects are mental. State this guard explicitly or a 2024-type answer slides into the very idealism Husserl rejected.

> ANALYSIS: the sharpest 15-mark line links the two halves - the eidetic method (how essences are grasped) *is* the evidence for the continuity thesis (that being shows itself only in correlation with consciousness). Method and metaphysics are one movement.



### ADVANCED SESSION 8 — The Transcendental Ego and the Lebenswelt - Constituting Field vs Res Cogitans, and the Crisis of the Sciences



#### The three-point contrast, the Kant guard, and Lebenswelt provenance

- **Husserl vs Descartes - three divergences (the 2025 core).** (i) **No substance-inference:** Descartes slides from "I think" to "I am a *res cogitans*"; Husserl refuses - the transcendental ego is a *constituting function*, not a mental thing left inside the brackets. (ii) **No God-bridge:** Descartes needs divine benevolence to guarantee the external world; Husserl needs none, because the world was never *lost*, only *bracketed* - meaning-constitution already contains the world as its intentional correlate. (iii) **Not worldless:** Descartes' ego could in principle exist alone; Husserl's is *essentially* world-constituting - without the world-as-meant there is nothing for it to be conscious *of*.
- **The Kant guard.** The transcendental ego is also **not** Kant's merely formal, logical "I think" that must accompany all representations. Husserl's ego has **content** - **habitualities** and **sedimentations**, a *history* of constituting (grounded in retention, subtopic 6). It is a concrete transcendental life, not a bare logical point.
- **The Lebenswelt and the Crisis.** Natural science *abstracts* from the life-world and substitutes **Galilean mathematisation** for lived quality; the "crisis" is that science has **forgotten its own origin** in the life-world and mistakes its idealisations for the real. The life-world is a pre-given horizon of evidence presupposed by scientific idealisation, the pre-given horizon within which all theory has meaning - and the bridge to Heidegger's "being-in-the-world."

> CAUTION: state the *Crisis* provenance exactly. **Parts I-II appeared in the journal *Philosophia* (Belgrade) in 1936**; Husserl **died in 1938** leaving it unfinished; **Part III and the full text were published posthumously only in 1954** as *Husserliana* Vol. VI, edited by **Walter Biemel**; the English translation is **David Carr's (1970)**. Safe form: "the *Crisis* (Parts I-II 1936; full text posthumously 1954)." Never write "the *Crisis* (1936)" as though the whole book appeared then, and do not treat the unfinished later sections as settled doctrine.

> ANALYSIS: the transcendental *solipsism* worry - if the world is constituted by my ego, how are there *others*? - is raised here and *answered* in the Fifth Meditation (subtopic 10). Flag it here, resolve it there.



### ADVANCED SESSION 9 — Avoidance of Psychologism - Four Prolegomena Arguments, Frege, Ideal Objectivity and the Transcendental-Turn Charge



#### Frege, the early Husserl, and the boomerang charge (2022)

- **The early Husserl and Frege's review - handle the provenance with care.** In the *Philosophie der Arithmetik* (1891) Husserl tried to ground *number* in the psychological acts of collecting and counting - a project his later self classed as psychologistic. **Gottlob Frege** reviewed it severely in **1894**; his charge, *in substance*, is that Husserl slides from the objective *content* of thought to the subjective *act* of thinking, turning arithmetic into a description of mental processes.
- **Two quotation/causation cautions (each a mark):** (1) do **not** attribute the popular line "one might as well explain astronomy by the psychology of astronomers" to Frege as a *quotation* - it is a textbook *paraphrase*. (2) Do **not** assert flatly that Frege's review *caused* Husserl's reversal: **Follesdal** (1958) argued for Frege's decisive influence, but **J. N. Mohanty** (*Husserl and Frege*, 1982) showed from the manuscripts that Husserl's anti-psychologism was already developing (via Bolzano and Lotze). Safe form: "Frege's 1894 review coincided with, and probably sharpened, a reversal whose sources scholars still dispute."
- **Ideal objectivity is the positive core.** Real acts grasp ideal, atemporal *meanings* (*Bedeutungen*). This dual recognition - **real acts, ideal contents** - defeats *both* psychologism (which denied the ideal) *and* naive Platonism (which denied that the ideal is accessible to consciousness).
- **The boomerang charge (2022 Q1(d)).** After *Ideas* (1913), all meaning-constitution is located in the **transcendental ego**. Does "truth constituted in transcendental subjectivity" re-introduce psychologism at a higher level? Husserl's three-part reply: (1) the transcendental ego is **not** the empirical psyche (a mundane object) but the *condition* for any world/psyche having sense; (2) **"constitution" is disclosure, not creation** - the ideal content stays atemporal and objective; subjectivity is the *medium of access*, not the *source* of validity; (3) the ego is **intersubjective** - meanings are constituted for *any possible* rational subject, not one mind.

> ANALYSIS: the examiner-strong verdict on 2022 is graded, not flat: the reply *succeeds* against the *literal* charge (empirical psychologism) because the transcendental ego is not empirical; whether it *fully* escapes a subtler transcendental idealism is contested (Heidegger, Merleau-Ponty think the "transcendental ego" stays too close to the idealist subject). Award yourself the qualification.



### ADVANCED SESSION 10 — Intersubjectivity - The Fifth Cartesian Meditation, Pairing, Appresentation and the Answer to Solipsism



#### Analogising transfer (not an inference), and the four great objections

- **Appresentation is an *analogising transfer of sense*, not an *argument from analogy*.** Husserl explicitly denies an inference "that body is like mine, so it probably has a mind." The pairing is a **passive synthesis of association** that happens *before* any judgement, exactly as the unseen back of a table is co-given in seeing its front. Getting this right is worth a mark: it is analogy in the sense of *sense-transfer*, not in the sense of *inductive reasoning*.
- **Why non-originality is a feature, not a bug.** If the other's experiences were given to me *originally* (as my own are), the other would not be an *other* - he would be *me*. The **inaccessibility** of the other's stream is therefore *constitutive of the very sense "other"* and is *verified* (not violated) by the harmonious continuation of the other's behaviour. This single point **dissolves the traditional problem of other minds** as usually posed. - **Result:** once other egos are constituted, the world is constituted as **there-for-everyone**; **objectivity just is intersubjective validity**. The transcendental field is not one ego but a **community of monads** (Leibniz's word - but these monads "have windows"). - **The four rival objections (label each; keep separate from the phenomenological *method* they share):**
  - **Schutz** (1957): **circularity** - the "ownness" sphere claims to be free of all reference to others, yet I recognise the other's body *as a body like others'* only because I already have the sense "other." *Reply:* ownness is an *abstractive layer*, not a temporal stage. *Residual:* most think Schutz finds a real strain - the most serious objection.
  - **Sartre** (1943): the Other is met not by appresentation but in **the Look** (shame) - my being-*for*-others, not my constituting *his* being-for-me. *Reply:* the Look presupposes the already-constituted sense "other subject."
  - **Levinas** (1961): reducing the Other to an **alter ego erases otherness**; ethics, not epistemology, is first philosophy. *Reply* concedes rather than answers.
  - **Heidegger** (1927, section 26): Dasein is **Mitsein** (being-with) from the start, so a solitary ego that must *reach* others is a pseudo-problem. *Dispute:* whether legitimation is needed at all.

> CAUTION: provenance. The Meditations were **lectures in Paris, February 1929**; **first published in French** (tr. Levinas and Peiffer, **1931**); the German *Cartesianische Meditationen* appeared **posthumously in 1950** (*Husserliana* I). The French text is the *first* publication - which is why Levinas engaged Husserl so early and so intimately.

> ANALYSIS: executable verdict - "the Fifth Meditation directly confronts Husserl's strongest intersubjectivity pressure": it reframes the traditional other-minds problem (inaccessibility is constitutive of "other"), yet an analysis that must *begin* from a sphere purified of others can reach the other only as a *modification of the self*, so Levinas's objection is conceded, not answered. Husserl links objectivity to intersubjectivity, while the ownness-first method remains circularity-prone.

### Register 1 - The Programme
- **Aim:** a *presuppositionless, rigorous science of consciousness* (*strenge Wissenschaft*, 1911 Logos manifesto).
- **Motto:** "Zu den Sachen selbst!" = *to the things themselves* (matters/subject-matters, not physical objects; motto is from the *Logical Investigations* introduction).
- **Three syllabus terms = one movement:** Method (how to look) -> Theory of Essences (what is seen) -> Avoidance of Psychologism (what is barred). - **Two enemies of "rigorous science":** naturalism/psychologism and historicism. - **Ownership:** 7 owned PYQ parts, 2019-2025; 2018 has none.

### Register 2 - The Natural Attitude
- **Natural attitude** (*naturliche Einstellung*): the default stance that *posits* the world; its **general thesis** (*Generalthesis*) = "the world exists, independent of me, always has." - Covers common sense *and* the natural sciences (which therefore cannot self-ground).
- **Criticism:** not false but *uncritical* - blocks the constitution question. Not a denial of the world.
- **Remedy:** epoche + phenomenological reduction.

### Register 3 - Epoche and the Reductions
- **Epoche/bracketing** (*Einklammerung*): suspend the natural attitude's existence-posit - neither affirm nor deny. **Not** Cartesian doubt (doubt denies to find the indubitable). - **Residue:** the whole stream of intentional experience, self-given. - Distinguish the operations: *phenomenological* reduction redirects inquiry from the posited object to its modes of givenness; *eidetic* reduction uses free imaginative variation to disclose essence; *transcendental* reduction asks after constituting subjectivity.
- **Three purposes (2023):** indubitable field; no slide to substance-dualism; a science of essences becomes possible (only the third is uniquely Husserl's).

### Register 4 - Intentionality
- Consciousness is always *of* something (from **Brentano**, transformed). **Noesis** (act) / **noema** (object-as-meant). - Intentional objects **need not exist**; the noema is not an inner picture. - **Hallucination (2019):** genuine noesis + full noema -> paradigmatically intentional. Directedness, not reference. - **Principle of principles** (*Ideas I* section 24): originary intuitive givenness legitimates knowledge. - *Contemporary anchor (labelled):* Chang Liu, *Husserl Studies* 42 (2026), on phenomenal character grounding intentional content.

### Register 5 - Hyle and Morphe
- **Hyle** = non-intentional sensory matter; **morphe** = intentional form that animates it (**apprehension**, *Auffassung*).
- **Anti-sense-datum:** hyle is lived through, not looked at -> no veil of ideas, no sceptical gap. - **Objections:** Merleau-Ponty (formless hyle re-imports empiricism); Sartre (consciousness has no contents). Provenance: *Ideas I* c. sections 85-86.

### Register 6 - Inner Time-Consciousness
- Living present = **retention + primal impression (Urimpression) + protention**. - **Retention is NOT recollection:** retention = primary memory *within* the perception; recollection = secondary memory, a *re-presentation* (*Vergegenwartigung*). Most examinable point.
- Examples: **melody**, **spoken sentence**. **Absolute flow** "constitutes its own unity" (regress worry). - Answers **Hume**; grounds habituality; **Heidegger** transforms into ecstatic temporality. - Provenance: lectures 1893-1917, ed. **Edith Stein**, pub. **Heidegger 1928**.

### Register 7 - Essences and the Continuity of Consciousness and Being
- **Eidetic reduction:** fact -> essence via **free imaginative variation**; the invariant = **eidos/Wesen**, apprehended in **Wesensschau** or eidetic intuition. **Categorial intuition** (*LI* VI) concerns the givenness of categorial forms and states of affairs beyond simple sensuous intuition; it is a related developmental basis, not another name for eidetic intuition.
- **Formal** vs **material/regional** essences; **regional ontologies**. - **Not Platonic**, **not psychological** - the necessary structure of the object as such. - **Continuity (2024):** the **correlational a priori** - being is given only as correlate of consciousness. Guard against **subjective idealism**.

### Register 8 - Transcendental Ego and Lebenswelt
- **Transcendental ego:** the constituting field of sense; **not** the empirical psyche, **not** a *res cogitans*, **not** Kant's formal "I think" (it has **habituality/sedimentation**, a history). - **vs Descartes (2025):** agree on the indubitable "I think"; diverge on function vs substance, no God-bridge (world bracketed not lost), intentional/temporal/intersubjective subjectivity vs thinking substance. - **Lebenswelt:** pre-scientific lived world, the *ground of evidence*; science idealises it (Galileo) and forgets its origin = the **crisis**.
- Provenance: *Crisis* Parts I-II **1936**; Husserl died **1938**; full text posthumous **1954** (*Husserliana* VI, ed. **Biemel**); Eng. tr. **David Carr, 1970**.

### Register 9 - Avoidance of Psychologism
- **Psychologism:** logical laws = empirical facts about how we think (Mill, Lipps, Sigwart; early Husserl, *Philosophie der Arithmetik* 1891).
- **Four Prolegomena (1900) arguments:** (i) necessary vs merely probable; (ii) ideal content vs real act; (iii) relativism self-refutes; (iv) cause of thinking is not ground of validity. - **Frege 1894** review (content/act confusion). *Cautions:* "astronomy/astronomers" is a **paraphrase, not a quote**; causation of the reversal is **disputed** (Follesdal vs **Mohanty** 1982). - **Positive result:** **ideal objectivity** - real acts grasp ideal, atemporal meanings. - **2022 boomerang:** transcendental turn re-imports psychologism? Reply: not empirical psyche; constitution = **disclosure not creation**; **transcendental intersubjectivity**. Verdict: beats the literal charge; subtler idealism worry persists.

### Register 10 - Intersubjectivity (Fifth Cartesian Meditation)
- **Problem:** the Other risks being a mere moment of my ego = **transcendental solipsism**. - **First correct the question:** not "prove other minds exist" but "how is the *sense* 'other subject' constituted?"
- **Machinery:** sphere of ownness -> **lived body** (*Leib*) -> another body resembling mine in behaviour -> **pairing** (*Paarung*, passive) -> **appresentation** (co-intended, an analogising *transfer of sense*, not an inference) -> **alter ego**. - Non-originality is necessary; **objectivity = intersubjective validity**; a **community of monads** (with windows). - **Objections:** **Schutz** (circularity - gravest), **Sartre** (the Look), **Levinas** (erases otherness), **Heidegger** (Dasein is *Mitsein*).
- Provenance: Paris lectures **1929**; French **1931** (Levinas/Peiffer); German posthumous **1950** (*Husserliana* I).

### REVIEW-PROMOTED HUSSERL CONTROLS

- Distinguish early descriptive, mature transcendental and late life-world phases.
- Suspension, phenomenological reduction, eidetic variation and transcendental reduction are distinct operations.
- Noema is not a private image; hallucination is intentional without worldly fulfilment.
- Profiles/horizons/synthesis explain identity; evidence is graded through fulfilment.
- Categorial and eidetic intuition are related but not timelessly identical.
- Transcendental ego is intentional/temporal/intersubjective, not a Cartesian substance.
- Fifth-Meditation appresentation reframes other minds but remains circularity-prone.
- Time-consciousness and life-world/sedimentation are bounded enrichment.
- Use English concepts first; existential heirs retain separate ownership.

```text
     entities that HAPPEN TO MEET; they are ESSENTIALLY CORRELATED, so neither
     can be described without the other.
   ===========================================================================
        ONE ESSENCE: 'PERCEPTION OF A SPATIAL THING' - READ EITHER WAY
   NOETIC SIDE - how the ACT must     |  NOEMATIC SIDE - what the OBJECT must be
   proceed                            |
   perspectival                       |  three-dimensional
   through PROFILES (Abschattungen)   |  given in profiles, NEVER EXHAUSTED by
   unseen sides CO-INTENDED           |  one appearance
   held together by a TEMPORAL        |  possessing an INTERNAL HORIZON of
   SYNTHESIS OF IDENTIFICATION        |  further profiles
   open to FULFILMENT or              |  IDENTICAL through the series;
   DISAPPOINTMENT                     |  determinable, correctable, never
                                      |  adequately given in one act
   ===========================================================================
   STATE THREE-DIMENSIONALITY AND YOU HAVE SPECIFIED PERSPECTIVAL GIVENNESS.
   STATE PERSPECTIVAL GIVENNESS AND YOU HAVE SPECIFIED A HORIZONED OBJECT.
   THAT MUTUAL ENTAILMENT *IS* THE CONTINUITY.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   WHAT THE CLAIM IS NOT
   X a MERGER of subject and object into one substance
   X a THIRD ENTITY ('the noema') located between mind and world
   X the claim that consciousness MANUFACTURES being
   WHAT IT IS  a STRUCTURAL CORRELATION. Essence is the A PRIORI FORM OF THEIR
   TOGETHERNESS.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE CONSEQUENCE THAT MAKES THIS CONTESTED
   Phenomenology REFRAMES realism/idealism: 'does the world exist
   independently?' is replaced by 'HOW IS WORLD-SENSE CONSTITUTED?'
   ? Husserl's mature position is an avowed TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM. Whether the
   correlation AVOIDS idealism or simply ARTICULATES one remains DISPUTED - and
   the honest 15/20-mark verdict SAYS SO.
   NOT BERKELEY  the noema is an OBJECTIVE CORRELATE accessible to all subjects,
   and objectivity is completed only by TRANSCENDENTAL INTERSUBJECTIVITY.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 26/30: PSYCHOLOGISM - DEFINITION, HOLDERS, REVERSAL
==============================================================================
   PSYCHOLOGISM  the doctrine that the laws of logic - non-contradiction,
   excluded middle, modus ponens, the laws of valid inference - are EMPIRICAL
   GENERALISATIONS ABOUT HOW HUMAN BEINGS IN FACT THINK; that logic is a branch,
   or at least a consequence, of DESCRIPTIVE PSYCHOLOGY.
   REPRESENTATIVES  JOHN STUART MILL | THEODOR LIPPS | CHRISTOPH SIGWART -
   AND, IN HIS EARLIER PHASE, HUSSERL HIMSELF
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ITS THREE CHARACTERISTIC CLAIMS
   1  'the principle of non-contradiction describes how we CANNOT think' - a
      psychological impossibility
   2  'logical laws hold BECAUSE OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE HUMAN MIND' - they
      would be DIFFERENT for differently constituted beings
   3  'truth is what we are COMPELLED TO BELIEVE' - truth reduces to a
      psychological fact
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE ONE CONFUSION, SHOWN ONCE
   THE ACT                              |  THE CONTENT
   'I judged that 2+2=4 at 3 p.m. on    |  '2+2=4'
   Tuesday, in this brain, while tired' |  not at 3 p.m., not in this brain,
   a REAL, DATABLE psychic event        |  not tired
                                        |  an IDEAL, ATEMPORAL meaning
   PSYCHOLOGISM READS THE RIGHT-HAND COLUMN AS A FEATURE OF THE LEFT-HAND ONE.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE REVERSAL  1891 Philosophie der Arithmetik (number grounded in acts of
   COLLECTING and COUNTING) -> 1894 FREGE'S REVIEW (paraphrase only; the causal
   claim is DISPUTED - Foellesdal yes, Mohanty 1982 no) -> 1900-01 PROLEGOMENA
   TO PURE LOGIC, VOL. I: the demolition
   ! NAMING 1891 CONVERTS 'Husserl opposed psychologism' INTO 'Husserl REVERSED
     HIMSELF' - truer, more interesting, and worth a mark on its own.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 27/30: THE FOUR PROLEGOMENA ARGUMENTS AND IDEAL OBJECTIVITY
==============================================================================
   (i)  NECESSARY vs MERELY PROBABLE
        psychological laws are INDUCTIVE - at best HIGHLY PROBABLE, never
        strictly NECESSARY. Logical laws are NECESSARILY true.
        YOU CANNOT DERIVE THE NECESSARY FROM THE MERELY PROBABLE.
   (ii) CONFUSION OF IDEAL CONTENT AND REAL ACT        <-- THE ROOT CONFUSION
        the CONTENT of '2+2=4' is an ideal, ATEMPORAL MEANING; the ACT of
        judging it is a REAL, DATABLE psychic event. Psychologism treats the
        TIMELESS PROPOSITION as a feature of the TEMPORAL ACT.
        ! THE OTHER THREE ARE THIS ONE, DRAWN IN DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS
   (iii) RELATIVISM AND SELF-REFUTATION               <-- STRONGEST AT 15/20
        if logical laws hold only 'for beings constituted like us', truth is
        SPECIES-RELATIVE (Anthropologismus). But 'truth is relative' is offered
        as ABSOLUTELY TRUE. SELF-CONTRADICTION.
        THE SHARP FORM  if logical laws were empirical generalisations about
        thinking, THE PSYCHOLOGIST'S OWN REASONING WOULD BE ONLY PROBABLE, and
        his conclusion would UNDERMINE ITS OWN SUPPORT
   (iv) CONDITIONS OF THINKING vs GROUNDS OF VALIDITY
        that we MUST think in accordance with non-contradiction is a CAUSAL
        necessity of our psychology; it does not EXPLAIN WHY THE LAW HOLDS,
        which is a question of LOGICAL VALIDITY. The CAUSE of believing is not
        the REASON for truth. -> THE GENETIC FALLACY.
   ===========================================================================
   THE POSITIVE RESULT: IDEAL OBJECTIVITY
   logical and mathematical truths have validity that is NOT IDENTICAL WITH ANY
   DATABLE PSYCHOLOGICAL ACT
   ! AVOID 'SUBSIST' if it suggests a separately located Platonic realm
   PHENOMENOLOGY STUDIES CONSCIOUSNESS AS THE SITE WHERE IDEAL MEANINGS ARE
   GRASPED - WITHOUT REDUCING THE MEANINGS TO THE ACTS THAT GRASP THEM.
   THE ACTS ARE REAL; THE CONTENTS ARE IDEAL.
   -> this DUAL recognition defeats BOTH psychologism (which denied the ideal)
      AND naive Platonism (which denied that the ideal is ACCESSIBLE)
   BOUNDARY CORRECTION  avoiding psychologism does NOT mean EXCLUDING
   CONSCIOUSNESS. Husserl studies intentional acts throughout; he refuses only
   to make LOGICAL VALIDITY DEPEND ON EMPIRICAL MENTAL LAWS.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 28/30: THE TRANSCENDENTAL-TURN WORRY - THE 2020/2022 ROUTE
==============================================================================
   THE CHARGE  after IDEAS (1913) all meaning-constitution is located in the
   TRANSCENDENTAL EGO. If truth is 'constituted in transcendental subjectivity',
   has psychologism RETURNED AT A HIGHER LEVEL?
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE THREE-PART REPLY
   1  THE TRANSCENDENTAL EGO IS NOT THE EMPIRICAL PSYCHE
      the psyche is a MUNDANE OBJECT WITHIN the world and is bracketed with it;
      the transcendental ego is the CONDITION under which world and psyche have
      meaning at all. Reducing to it is therefore NOT PSYCHOLOGY.
      ! the objection trades on treating 'subject' UNIVOCALLY
   2  CONSTITUTION IS NOT CREATION OR FABRICATION
      to say the sense of '2+2=4' is constituted in transcendental subjectivity
      is to say its SENSE IS DISCLOSED there - not that the subject MAKES IT UP.
      The ideal content remains ATEMPORAL and OBJECTIVE.
      SUBJECTIVITY IS THE MEDIUM OF ACCESS, NOT THE SOURCE OF VALIDITY.
   3  OBJECTIVITY IS GROUNDED IN TRANSCENDENTAL INTERSUBJECTIVITY
      not in the private preferences of one empirical subject. HOW SUCCESSFULLY
      HE REACHES OTHER EGOS IS THE FIFTH MEDITATION'S PROBLEM (Panel 29)
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ? WHAT SURVIVES OF THE CHARGE  limb 3 TRANSFERS THE BURDEN. If
   intersubjectivity is not independently secured, limb 3 cannot carry the
   weight limb 2 leaves it - and SCHUTZ'S CIRCULARITY OBJECTION (1957) is
   exactly a challenge to that security.
   VERDICT  PSYCHOLOGISM PROPER IS ESCAPED: nothing in the transcendental
   account makes logical validity depend on how any mind happens to work. WHAT
   IS NOT ESCAPED IS TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM - a DIFFERENT and WEAKER charge.
   The reply is COHERENT BUT NOT DECISIVE; neo-Kantians, the analytic tradition,
   and later HEIDEGGER and MERLEAU-PONTY remain unconvinced.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   TWO QUESTIONS, ONE DIFFERENCE
   2020 Q1(c) 10m  what is WRONG with psychologism + how the METHOD addresses it
                   -> the four arguments PLUS the method-link
   2022 Q1(d) 10m  what IS psychologism + how Husserl avoids it IN THE DISCOURSE
                   OF TRANSCENDENTAL PHENOMENOLOGY
                   -> the four arguments PLUS this panel. STOPPING AT THE FOUR
                      ARGUMENTS ANSWERS HALF.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 29/30: THE TRANSCENDENTAL EGO, DESCARTES, AND THE FIFTH MEDITATION
==============================================================================
   THE EGO  IS  the FIELD OF SENSE-CONSTITUTION - that FOR WHICH all meaning is
   constituted; INTENTIONAL, TEMPORAL, HABITUAL (sedimented history), and
   ultimately INTERSUBJECTIVE
            NOT (a) a CARTESIAN RES COGITANS - a mental substance, a bit of the
                    world left inside the brackets
                (b) identical WITHOUT QUALIFICATION to KANT's formal 'I think' -
                    Husserl analyses HABITUALITIES, SEDIMENTATION and TEMPORAL
                    CONSTITUTION
                (c) the EMPIRICAL PSYCHE studied by psychology
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   2025 Q4(a) 20m  AGREEMENT FIRST, THEN THREE DIVERGENCES
   AGREEMENT     both start from the indubitable SELF-GIVENNESS of the thinking
                 act; both seek a PRESUPPOSITIONLESS BEGINNING
                 ! DENYING THIS REMOVES THE AXIS AND THE ANSWER BECOMES TWO
                   PORTRAITS
   DIVERGENCE 1  SUBSTANCE. Descartes concludes to a THINKING SUBSTANCE.
                 Husserl SUSPENDS that mundane ontological inference.
   DIVERGENCE 2  WORLD-ACCESS. Descartes invokes DIVINE NON-DECEPTION to rebuild
                 world-knowledge. Husserl NEVER DENIES the world; he brackets
                 its POSITING.  NO GOD-BRIDGE IS NEEDED BECAUSE NO CHASM WAS
                 OPENED.
   DIVERGENCE 3  SCOPE. Husserl's ego is INTENTIONAL, TEMPORAL and ultimately
                 INTERSUBJECTIVE - not a sealed inner thing.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE COST: TRANSCENDENTAL SOLIPSISM. Husserl treats it as the MOST SERIOUS
   objection to his own programme and gives it the LONGEST Meditation.
   ! FIRST, CORRECT THE QUESTION. He is NOT trying to PROVE that other minds
     exist - a natural-attitude demand the epoche has suspended. He asks HOW THE
     SENSE 'another subject, for whom the world is also there' IS CONSTITUTED,
     with the evidence proper to it.   <-- STRONGEST SINGLE SENTENCE AVAILABLE
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE FIFTH CARTESIAN MEDITATION - NINE STEPS
   1 REDUCTION TO OWNNESS (Eigenheitssphaere): abstract from all sense owing to
     OTHER subjectivity - cultural predicates ('hammer', 'book', 'road') and all
     objectivity-for-anyone. What remains is my PRIMORDIAL SPHERE
   2 MY BODY IS UNIQUE IN IT: the LIVED BODY (Leib) is the only physical thing
     that is also an ORGAN OF WILL, a FIELD OF SENSINGS, and the absolute HERE
     from which all else is THERE. Every other body is a mere KOERPER
   3 ANOTHER BODY APPEARS resembling mine in appearance and, crucially, in
     GOVERNED EXPRESSIVE BEHAVIOUR
   4 PAIRING (Paarung): a PASSIVE SYNTHESIS OF ASSOCIATION couples the two
     bodies - BEFORE any judgement or inference
   5 APPRESENTATION (Appraesentation): the other's subjectivity is CO-INTENDED
     without being itself presented - exactly as the UNSEEN BACK OF A TABLE is
     appresented in seeing its front
     ! AN ANALOGISING TRANSFER OF SENSE, *NOT* AN ARGUMENT FROM ANALOGY.
       HUSSERL EXPLICITLY DENIES THAT IT IS AN INFERENCE
   6 THE 'AS IF I WERE THERE': the other is constituted as an ALTER EGO - an 'I'
     at HIS here, which for me is a THERE. An INTENTIONAL MODIFICATION of my own
   7 NON-ORIGINALITY IS A NECESSITY, NOT A DEFECT: if the other's experiences
     were given to me ORIGINALLY, as my own are, HE WOULD NOT BE AN OTHER - HE
     WOULD BE ME. Inaccessibility belongs to the very SENSE 'other'
   8 VERIFICATION IS HARMONIOUS BEHAVIOUR: confirmed or disappointed by
     CONTINUING CONCORDANCE - the same fulfilment structure as ordinary
     perception
   9 RESULT: the world is constituted as available to PERSPECTIVES OTHER THAN
     MINE -> OBJECTIVITY. 'COMMUNITY OF MONADS' is a LEIBNIZIAN TERM; the
     CONTENT is embodiment, empathy and appresentation
   FOUR PRESUPPOSITIONS  P1 ownness is coherent | P2 my body is originally given
   as LIVED | P3 pairing is genuinely PASSIVE | P4 constitution is ASYMMETRICAL
   PROVENANCE  Paris lectures FEB 1929 -> FRENCH 1931 (Levinas and Peiffer) ->
   GERMAN 1950 (Husserliana I) -> Eng. tr. CAIRNS 1960

   QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
   Husserl's achievement is a METHOD that makes the constitution of sense a
   describable subject-matter, and a THEORY OF ESSENCES that secures ideal
   validity WITHOUT a separate realm and WITHOUT reduction to psychic events.
   The programme's own limits are equally clear and should be stated rather than
   hidden: COMPLETE PRESUPPOSITIONLESSNESS remains a regulative aspiration;
   the HYLE is absorbed into passive synthesis rather than defended; the
   SELF-CONSTITUTING FLOW is reflexively difficult; TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM is
   avowed rather than avoided; and the OWNNESS-FIRST route to the other remains
   exposed to circularity. PSYCHOLOGISM IS DEFEATED; IDEALISM IS NOT - and that
   distinction, held firmly, is the most defensible verdict on the clause.
```
