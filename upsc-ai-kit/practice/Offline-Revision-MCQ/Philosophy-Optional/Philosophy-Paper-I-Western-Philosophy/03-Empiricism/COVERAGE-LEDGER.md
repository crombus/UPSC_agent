## Independent sizing audit

The bank was re-audited from Empiricism's coverage cells rather than extended to a preset number.
Q1–32 were retained because they remain useful, including the remedial drills. The independent
audit found exactly nine uncovered discriminations; one genuinely non-duplicate question was
added for each. The resulting count of 41 is therefore an audit outcome, not a target.

| Added MCQ | Previously uncovered cell | Why the earlier bank did not close it |
|---|---|---|
| 33 | Dispositional innatism and Locke's “in mind means perceived” premise | Earlier items stated Locke's grounds but did not test whether his decisive premise begs the question against a dispositional Cartesian. |
| 34 | Ordered Humean causal components | Earlier items tested necessity and the two definitions but not why priority, contiguity and conjunction must be ordered before the mental transition. |
| 35 | Structural isomorphism versus literal resemblance | Earlier items raised Berkeley's likeness objection without testing the strongest qualified rescue of representative realism. |
| 36 | Berkeley's God as guarantor of public objectivity | Earlier coverage derived God but did not force the distinction between stable intersubjective order and private subjectivity. |
| 37 | Atomic perceptions and the Appendix unity problem | Earlier bundle questions stated the confession but did not test whether atomism removes the resources needed to explain unity. |
| 38 | Psychological explanation versus rational justification of induction | Earlier items located the dilemma but did not isolate the explanatory/justificatory distinction in application. |
| 39 | Limits of the simple-content origin rule | Earlier items tested Hume's fork but not why empirical concept-origin alone cannot explain a priori necessity. |
| 40 | Berkeley versus Hume on scientific order and necessity | Earlier comparisons did not distinguish their two ways of preserving prediction while denying ordinary efficient necessity. |
| 41 | Humean custom versus Kantian synthetic-a-priori necessity | Earlier routed questions named Kant's response but did not test whether custom answers or merely relocates the demand for objective necessity. |
