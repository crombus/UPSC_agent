operations); Q34–Q36 (duplicates of King-of-France, tautology and atomism comparisons); Q38–Q40

The **19 replacement operations** test: chronology; the status of truisms; all three proof

## Genuine source gaps and cautions

- No direct current-affairs event is established; none is fabricated.
- The official syllabus typo “Sying and Showing” may be quoted, but the doctrine is Saying and Showing.
- Moore's 1925 defence and 1939 proof must not be merged.
- Russell's phase-specific sense-data commitments must not be projected unchanged across his career.
- *Tractatus* numbering and translations require caution; paraphrases are not presented as quotations.
- Tarski is later independent corroboration, not Wittgenstein's own solution.
