## Formal-session reconciliation and validation

The authoritative completed `Learning-Session.md` and `Solved-Practice-Workbook.md` are enumerated
as full canonical blocks. `FORMAL-COVERAGE-REVIEW.json` is a separately authored decision layer:
refreshing the audit never creates, changes or approves a decision. Structural umbrellas require
both their direct parent prose and the complete recursive child union; large leaves require
distributed beginning/middle/end evidence; the 28-panel master flow requires panel-specific
hashes, propositions and destinations. Source hashes and semantic-label, heading and ancestor
context mutations invalidate prior decisions.

The validator checks source identity, route closure, body-level evidence, randomized MCQ
distribution and visible cues, exact PYQ wording, timed bands and four current PDFs. These
mechanical checks do not prove philosophical truth or semantic completeness.
