# How to Study and Practise Offline

## Previously studied topic

```text
closed-book structure recall -> redraw visuals -> targeted revision -> hard MCQs
-> inspect full solutions -> log error and repair -> answer outline -> delayed retest
```

## New or severely forgotten topic

Read the complete canonical learning session first. Then reconstruct the roadmap, use the Revision Guide for retrieval, attempt MCQs without notes, and transfer learning into one answer outline.

## One-question MCQ protocol

1. Open only `MCQ-QUESTIONS.md`; attempt one numbered item.
2. Record option, brief reasoning and confidence (1–5) in `PRACTICE-LOG.md`.
3. Open the same number in `MCQ-SOLUTIONS.md`; analyse every option and examiner trap.
4. Classify errors using the codes in `START-HERE.md`. A correct guess is `GUESS-C`, not mastery.
5. Repair from the mapped Revision Guide section and schedule D1–2, D7, D21 and D45 retests.

## Completion test

Do not close a topic until every ledger cell has been sampled, high-confidence errors are repaired, an unseen transfer task succeeds, and delayed recall confirms retention.
