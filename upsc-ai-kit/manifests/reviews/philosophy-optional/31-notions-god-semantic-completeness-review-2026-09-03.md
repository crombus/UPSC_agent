## 7. Canonical and cross-owner boundaries

- This owner defines and tests God-concepts; it does not prove existence.
- Ontological, cosmological, teleological, moral and Nyāya existence arguments
  remain with **Proofs for the Existence of God**.
- The full logical/evidential suffering challenge remains with
  **Problem of Evil**.
- Full Buddhist/Jain creator critiques remain with **Religion without God**;
  only the classification boundary is retained here.
- Experience, faith/revelation, morality and immortality keep their own owners.
- Plural deity concepts inside a tradition are distinguished here; conflicting
  interreligious truth-claims remain with **Religious Pluralism**.
- Analogy, symbol and gendered reference are minimal controls here; semantic
  theories remain with **Nature of Religious Language**.
- Brahman, classical personal God, Spinoza's substance and Nyāya Lord are never
  treated as interchangeable.
