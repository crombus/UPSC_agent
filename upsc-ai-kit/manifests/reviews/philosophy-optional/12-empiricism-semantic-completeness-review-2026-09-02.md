## 7. Answer architecture and factual control

- The owner supplies directive-specific 10-, 15- and 20-mark structures, reconstructed arguments, objections, replies and verdicts.
- Locke's blank slate denies innate content, not every faculty or operation.
- Locke's personal identity is continuity of consciousness; memory is its chief appropriation mechanism, not an unproblematic substance.
- Berkeley denies material substratum, not experienced tables, science, all minds or the external order.
- *Esse est percipi* applies to ideas; spirits are active perceivers.
- Hume does not deny causal practice or prove that no world exists; he denies rationally perceived objective necessity/substance.
- Missing shade qualifies rather than simply destroys the Copy Principle.
- Kant's unity is formal, not an observed soul; his causal category governs phenomena, not things in themselves.
- Treatise and Enquiry claims remain textually separated.
