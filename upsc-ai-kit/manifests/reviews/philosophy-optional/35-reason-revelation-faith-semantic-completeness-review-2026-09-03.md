## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every answer defines reason, revelation and faith separately.
- General/special and propositional/non-propositional distinctions are not conflated.
- Reason is distinguished as regulative or constitutive.
- Faith is more than belief-that but is not belief without evidence.
- Revelation requires interpretation; scripture cannot authenticate itself
  non-circularly without independent support.
- Objective revelation-content is distinguished from revelation-for-a-recipient.
- The 2019 answer chooses faith for reception and reason for regulation.
- Aquinas' preambles/mysteries structure remains precise.
- Kierkegaard is framed through appropriation and objective uncertainty.
- Pascal supplies prudential reasons, not evidence; James permits only living,
  forced and momentous options.
- Proper basicality remains grounded, defeasible and exposed to Great-Pumpkin
  and diversity objections.
- Miracles/testimony are defeasible and do not validate one interpretation automatically.
- Indian testimony and authorless scripture are not collapsed into creator revelation.
