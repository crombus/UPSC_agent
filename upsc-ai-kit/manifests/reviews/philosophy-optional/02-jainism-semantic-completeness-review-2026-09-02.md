## 6. Canonical and cross-owner boundaries

- Jainism owns pluralist realism, many-sided reality, standpoints, conditional and sevenfold predication, material karma, bondage and liberation.
- Cārvāka owns materialism and anti-transcendence; Jainism contains only the comparison required for the 2020 PYQ.
- Buddhism owns dependent origination, momentariness, no-self and its school traditions; Jainism contains only bounded comparisons concerning continuity, self and relational language.
- Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika owns its distinct atomism, substance taxonomy and new-production causation; Jainism contains only contrasts needed to explain qualitatively similar atoms and substance–mode causation.
- Sāṃkhya owns strong pre-existence of the effect; Jainism contains only the causal comparison.
- Vedānta owns non-dual or qualified-non-dual accounts of reality and liberation; Jainism contains only objections to its enduring pluralism.
- Formal reconstructions of sevenfold predication and second-order absolutism debates remain in the advanced dossier.
