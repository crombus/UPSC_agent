## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every routed PYQ retains an exact model and timed-paper upgrade.
- Growth, development, human development, capability and social progress are
  never used as synonyms.
- Indicator families are identified by informational space and blind spot; no
  current figure is required.
- Modernisation is a contested theory, not neutral inevitability.
- Sen's capability is a real opportunity, not an achieved functioning or
  welfare scheme.
- Economic development is treated as threshold-enabling and clearly
  insufficient, not simply necessary-and-sufficient.
- Technology expands power without supplying ethical ends.
- Skill education remains conditional on access, decent work, adaptability,
  dignity and critical citizenship.
- Participation and empowerment are distinguished by actual decision-power.
- Social progress is directional but non-automatic and plural within a dignity
  and anti-domination floor.
- Gandhian concepts are English-first and tested against Ambedkar's village
  objection.
- Tribal communities are neither romanticised nor treated as obstacles.
- Degrowth is asymmetric; it is not prescribed uniformly below deprivation
  thresholds.
- Current statistics, targets and policy claims are excluded.
