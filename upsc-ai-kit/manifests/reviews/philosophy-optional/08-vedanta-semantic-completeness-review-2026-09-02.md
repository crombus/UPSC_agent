## 6. Canonical and cross-owner boundaries

- Vedānta owns Brahman-centred interpretation of the Upaniṣads and the rival identity/qualification/difference architectures.
- Mīmāṃsā owns injunction-centred hermeneutics; Vedānta borrows epistemic tools but redirects scripture toward accomplished reality.
- Sāṃkhya owns primordial-nature transformation; Advaita criticizes it, Rāmānuja adopts real transformation only through dependent modes, and Madhva retains dependent matter.
- Nyāya owns atomism, God as efficient cause and misplacement error; Vedānta includes these only as contrasts.
- Buddhism owns no-self, dependent origination and emptiness; the crypto-Buddhist charge does not erase Advaita's positive Brahman.
- Advaita owns appearance, superimposition and identity; Viśiṣṭādvaita owns inseparable qualification/body–soul relation; Dvaita owns five real differences.
- Nimbārka, Vallabha and Caitanya receive only the depth directly required by verified PYQs and standard taxonomy.
- Aurobindo remains unopened and outside this review.
