## 6. Canonical and cross-owner boundaries

- Sāṃkhya owns primordial material nature, conscious witnesses, three qualities, twenty-five principles, pre-existent effect, real transformation, subtle body and discriminative liberation.
- Yoga shares much of this metaphysics but owns mind-field, modifications, afflictions, meditative absorption, eight-limbed discipline and special-Puruṣa theism.
- Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika owns new-production causation, atomism, active self and developed creator proofs; Sāṃkhya retains only the causal and theistic contrasts.
- Advaita owns apparent transformation and non-dual Brahman; Sāṃkhya contains Śaṃkara's criticism because it is directly routed in 2018, 2023 and 2025.
- Buddhism owns momentariness, no-self and dependent origination; Sāṃkhya contains only the permanent-witness and material-root contrast.
- Later theistic Sāṃkhya and detailed commentarial chronology remain source-qualified enrichment, not the classical default.
