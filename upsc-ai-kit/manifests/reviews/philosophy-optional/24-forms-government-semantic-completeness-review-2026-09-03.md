## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every routed PYQ retains an exact model and timed-paper upgrade.
- Plato is reconstructed as a knowledge/competence critic, not a hereditary monarchist.
- Aristotle's “democracy” is identified as a classical deviant form; polity is not equated with universal-suffrage liberal democracy.
- Monarchy's freedom verdict is conditional on constitutional limitation.
- Theocracy is distinguished from religious influence or religiously informed public reasoning.
- Weberian legitimacy is sociological belief in rightfulness, not moral certification.
- Schumpeter supplies a minimum competitive method, not a complete democratic ideal.
- Michels's iron law is treated as a tendency vulnerable to institutional countermeasures.
- Populism remains contested across Mudde, Müller and Laclau; anti-elitism is not automatically anti-democratic, whereas exclusive moral representation is anti-pluralist.
- Majority rule is not constitutional democracy; minorities, opposition and future alternation remain essential.
- Parliamentary/presidential and unitary/federal forms are not themselves tests of democracy.
- Fusion of powers is distinguished from absence of checks; separation is distinguished from institutional isolation.
- Tocqueville's tyranny-of-majority diagnosis is paired with associations, local liberty and press.
- Bureaucratic expertise remains necessary but answerable.
- India is described as parliamentary and federal under constitutional supremacy and judicial review, never as exercising UK-style parliamentary sovereignty.
- Constitutional provisions/statutes are legal illustrations, not philosophical proof.
