## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every routed PYQ retains an exact model and timed-paper upgrade.
- Wrongdoing, criminalisation and punishment are three separate gates.
- An external act/omission and fault are prerequisites; thought alone is not
  punished.
- Justification defends conduct; excuse reduces blame.
- Compatibilist responsibility uses reasons-responsive understanding/control
  without claiming metaphysical freedom as a courtroom fact.
- Retribution is distinguished from revenge.
- Pure deterrence faces the punishment-of-innocents objection.
- Six theory families are compared by direction, test and danger.
- Restitution is one possible outcome within broader restorative justice.
- Denunciation supplies meaning but not an independent justification for hard
  treatment.
- Hart's Q1 aim, Q2 liability and Q3 amount structure controls mixed theories.
- Foucault remains genealogy, not normative justification.
- Restorative justice remains conditional on voluntariness, safety and power
  balance.
- Juvenile seriousness is relevant but not conclusive; developing agency
  affects culpability.
- Deterrent effect and reoffending remain empirical questions; no unsupported
  rate is stated.
- Genocide requires specific group-destruction intent and is not inferred from
  scale alone.
- *Bachan Singh* is a judgment; Report No. 262 is advisory; statutes remain
  statutes. None is philosophical proof.
