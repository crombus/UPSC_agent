## 7. Answer architecture and factual control

- Each owned PYQ has an explicit answer route, not merely a question citation.
- Dialectic is not debate, a mnemonic or arbitrary inconsistency; each transition must be immanently demonstrated.
- Sublation (*Aufhebung*) cancels isolated sufficiency, preserves valid content and reorganises it at a more concrete level.
- Pure Being and pure Nothing are indistinguishable at pure indeterminacy; Becoming is their passage, not temporal creation.
- Identity-in-difference is not numerical identity of opposites.
- Absolute Spirit is not a supernatural person; competing metaphysical and post-Kantian readings are acknowledged.
- Hegel's Kant critique is strongest against reified two-world/affection readings and does not by itself prove the complete system.
- Lordship–bondage does not yet complete reciprocal recognition.
- “The rational is actual” is not “whatever exists is justified.”
- The history scheme is Hegel's dated Eurocentric retrospective teleology, not a predictive deterministic law.
- Moore/Russell are not made owners of every criticism attributed to British Idealism.
