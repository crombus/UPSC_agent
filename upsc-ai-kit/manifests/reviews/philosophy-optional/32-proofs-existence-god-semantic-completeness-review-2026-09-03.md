## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every proof is classified before evaluation.
- Arguments are reconstructed as premises and conclusion rather than listed.
- Validity, soundness and divine adequacy are tested separately.
- A proponent must defend the starting premise, inferential bridge and
  theological identification.
- Anselm is not Descartes; Gaunilo is not Kant; a modal version shifts the
  burden to possibility.
- Aquinas' first cause is explanatory, not automatically temporal.
- Aquinas, Leibniz and the temporal-beginning argument remain distinct.
- Way 5 is not Paley's watch analogy.
- Hume attacks inference strength and divine attributes, not the bare
  possibility of every designer.
- Natural selection is restricted to biological adaptation; fine-tuning
  remains probabilistic and model-dependent.
- Kant postulates God, freedom and immortality for practical reason; he does
  not theoretically prove God.
- Nyāya God is the intelligent efficient cause, not the material cause.
- Jainism is not assigned creator-proofs; Buddhism is not said to deny all deities.
- Sanskrit proof terms are followed by one clause of content and qualified
  where enumerations vary.
- All 12 PYQs retain exact year, marks, directive and answer route.
- All ten Basic sessions now have aligned definitions, keyword spines,
  executable guidance and readable `STARTING CONCEPT` closures.
