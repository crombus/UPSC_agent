## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every routed PYQ retains an exact model and demand-specific timed-paper upgrade.
- “How far” questions receive conditional degree judgments; comparisons use shared axes; “improvement” names the criterion first.
- Core models follow claim → named thinker/text/example → analysis → qualification.
- Equality is not sameness; capability is not income or identical functioning.
- Berlin's positive liberty is distinguished from Green's enabling freedom.
- Non-interference is not non-domination; Pettit supplies the normative theory and Skinner the historical recovery.

- Rawls's first principle is equal basic liberty; the opportunity principle precedes the difference principle.
- Nozick's acquisition, transfer and rectification are all retained.
- Sen's realised-justice critique supplements rather than simply supersedes institutional design.
- Indian constitutional/statutory examples are illustrations, never philosophical proof.
