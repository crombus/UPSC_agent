## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every routed PYQ retains an exact model and timed-paper upgrade.
- Comparison answers run parallel shared axes, not three doctrine summaries.
- Anarchism is a theory of non-coercive order, not disorder.
- Philosophical anarchism does not entail immediate insurrection.
- Gandhi is qualified as anarchist in ideal and pragmatic reformer in method;
  he is not assimilated to Bakunin.
- Marx's dialectic is not reduced to the later textbook
  thesis–antithesis–synthesis formula.
- Base/superstructure is not presented as a mechanical one-way cause.
- Alienation is distinguished from low wages; exploitation from unequal
  distribution.
- Dictatorship of the proletariat is not treated as textual permission for
  permanent one-party dictatorship.
- Humanist and structural Marxism receive separate agency/structure grammars.
- “Scientific” socialism marks an explanatory claim, not demonstrated
  infallibility; “utopian” is not used as a synonym for foolishness.
- Nationalisation is not treated as socialisation.
- Pandemic disorder, expanded state power and mutual aid may coexist; none
  alone establishes anarchism as an adopted ideology.
- Current relevance is judged separately as diagnosis, prediction and
  institution.
- Constitutional provisions remain legal-status illustrations, never
  philosophical proof.
