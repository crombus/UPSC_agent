## 8. Answer architecture and factual/source control

- Every routed PYQ retains an exact model and demand-specific timed-paper upgrade.
- Rights/duties answers distinguish claim-right correlativity from liberties; “two sides of one coin” is not treated as universal logic.
- Priority questions name a criterion before ranking rights, duty or accountability.
- Social-contract answers show what changes from Hobbes to Locke to Rousseau rather than presenting three biographies.
- Liberalism is not reduced to atomism; welfare liberalism and neutrality can recognise social formation and public goods.
- Hegel's rational ethical state is distinguished from every actual state and from totalitarian command.
- Green treats the state as a limited means to common good and positive freedom.
- Marx's class-state critique includes the eventual withering of the state and does not become simple statism.
- Mill's harm principle and soft/hard paternalism discipline coercion for a person's own good.
- Neutrality is not value-free; perfectionism remains vulnerable to conformity.
- Privacy protects autonomy but cannot legalise harm or domination.
- Aristotle's 2024 advantage rests on criticism of excessive Platonic unity and preservation of plurality, household and private property—not on an unargued appeal to the ethical doctrine of the mean.
- Aristotle is only a comparative middle; slavery, gender exclusion and restricted citizenship prevent a modern-liberal verdict.
- Accountability requires explanation, judgment/consequence and correction.
- Article 19 and Article 51A are illustrations, not philosophical proof; Fundamental Duties remain non-justiciable.
- Civil disobedience, conscientious objection, resistance and revolution remain graded rather than interchangeable.
- Concepts remain English-first; right conduct and moral duty precede *dharma*, self-rule precedes *swaraj*, and truth-force/non-violent resistance precedes *satyāgraha* where used.
