## 7. Answer architecture and factual control

- The owner supplies reconstructed transcendental arguments, objections, replies, directives and graded verdicts.
- Objects as possible experiences conform to a priori cognitive conditions; private minds do not create things in themselves.
- Space/time are transcendentally ideal and empirically real, not unreal fantasies.
- Apperception is a formal unity of synthesis, not an empirical ego or immortal soul.
- The transcendental exposition does not itself deduce apperception; Aesthetic, Deduction and Schematism have distinct jobs.
- Negative noumenon limits sensible cognition; positive noumenon would require intellectual intuition humans lack.
- The affection problem remains a genuine tension rather than a solved noumenal causal story.
- Antinomy arises from treating the unconditioned world-series as a completed object in itself.
- Dynamical resolutions distinguish standpoints without producing theoretical knowledge of freedom or a necessary being.
- Kant rejects theoretical proof of God, not God's existence.
- Historical quotations and A/B edition-specific claims are explicitly controlled.
