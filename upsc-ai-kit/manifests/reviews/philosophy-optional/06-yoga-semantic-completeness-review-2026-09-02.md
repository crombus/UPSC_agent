## 6. Canonical and cross-owner boundaries

- Yoga owns mind-field, modifications, afflictions, practice/dispassion, eight limbs, absorption, Īśvara and isolation.
- Sāṃkhya owns primordial nature, conscious witness, qualities, twenty-five principles and core real-transformation metaphysics; Yoga includes only the bridge required for practice.
- Nyāya owns creator/efficient-cause proofs; Yoga's real Īśvara is primarily special witness, teacher and contemplative aid.
- Buddhism owns no-self, dependent origination and its meditation framework; Yoga retains plural witnesses and a continuing mind substrate.
- Advaita owns non-dual Brahman and identity-liberation; Yoga isolation is not merger.
- Public-health or wellness Yoga is contextual only and cannot replace Pātañjala doctrine.
- Detailed siddhi catalogues and specialist commentarial controversies remain optional enrichment.
