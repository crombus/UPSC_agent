## 6. Canonical and cross-owner boundaries

- Aurobindo owns Supermind, involution/evolution, psychic being, triple transformation, Integral Yoga and divine life.
- Advaita owns world-sublation and identity-liberation; Aurobindo accepts non-dual reality but rejects world-negation as final.
- Patañjali owns cessation, eight limbs and isolation; Aurobindo transforms nature through ascent and descent.
- Sāṃkhya owns two independent ultimates and real material transformation; Aurobindo redeploys witness/nature language without accepting ultimate dualism.
- Darwinian theory owns biological mechanisms; Aurobindo offers a metaphysical interpretation rather than replacement science.
- Western process/evolutionary parallels remain illustrative and cannot establish influence or identity.
- Human unity and social evolution remain bounded consequences, not hidden syllabus expansion.
- Western Philosophy was not opened.
