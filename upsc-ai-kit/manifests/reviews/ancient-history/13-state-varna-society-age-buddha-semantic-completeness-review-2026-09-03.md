## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- The “age” was not made a sudden pan-Indian revolution at 600 BCE.
- Pali, Jain and Brahmanical texts were not treated as neutral contemporaneous
  surveys.
- NBPW was not equated with a city, state, people or one universal start date.
- Iron was not made a self-acting cause.
- Coinage was not made universally state-minted or economy-wide.
- Land was not described as either uniformly private or wholly royal.
- Kings were not treated as absolute; oligarchies were not called democracies.
- Later Mauryan offices were not projected backward.
- Tax fractions and army sizes were not made exact uniform facts.
- Gahapati and setthi were not treated as synonyms.
- Varna was not equated wholesale with later caste, occupation or class.
- Vaishya did not become every merchant; Shudra did not become every labourer
  or slave.
- Dāsa terminology was not mapped to one modern slavery model.
- Marginal-group labels were not treated as one timeless pan-Indian list.
- Women were neither erased nor described as enjoying uncomplicated equality.
- Buddhism/Jainism were not reduced to merchant or modern anti-caste movements.
- Ashokan dhamma was not projected into the Buddha's lifetime.
- No current-affairs claim was required.
