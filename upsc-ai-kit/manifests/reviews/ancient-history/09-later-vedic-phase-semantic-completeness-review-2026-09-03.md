## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- Later Vedic change was not treated as sudden or pan-Indian.
- PGW was not equated with a people, language or all Vedic communities.
- Iron was not made the sole cause of agriculture or state formation.
- Agriculture was described as intensifying, not beginning.
- Cattle remained economically/ritually important.
- Ratnins were not called a modern cabinet.
- Revenue formation was not made a Mauryan bureaucracy.
- Assemblies did not disappear uniformly.
- Royal sacrifices were claims/performances, not exact maps of sovereignty.
- Varna consolidation was not equated with the fully later jati system.
- Textual prescriptions were not treated as universal social practice.
- Women's status was not reduced to one uniform decline.
- Gotra and four-ashrama systems were treated as developing.
- Upanishads were presented as internal transformation, not simple anti-Vedic
  rejection.
- No new current-affairs claim was required.
