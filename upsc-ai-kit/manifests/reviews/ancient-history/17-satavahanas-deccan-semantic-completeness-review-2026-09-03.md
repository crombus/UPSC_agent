## 4. Key factual and historiographical controls

- Andhra and Satavahana were not treated as an uncontested ethnic identity.
- Founder, ruler and dynasty dates were not presented as exact.
- Pratishthana was not made the permanent sole capital.
- Gautamiputra's prashasti was separated from coin-supported inference.
- Nahapana overstrikes did not become destruction of every Shaka formation.
- Rudradaman's kinship/war claim remained a rival eulogy.
- Aharas and Mauryan-looking titles did not prove uniform centralization.
- Matronymics did not become matriliny or matriarchy.
- Land grants did not become developed medieval feudalism.
- Brahmadeya terminology was not projected without inscriptional support.
- Royal varna-restoration claims were not treated as social census.
- Roman trade was not made the sole source of prosperity.
- Lead coinage was not equated with poverty.
- Satavahana religion was not reduced to one tradition.
- Nagarjunakonda's major Ikshvaku phase remained distinct.
- Dynastic contraction was not made synchronized Deccan collapse.
