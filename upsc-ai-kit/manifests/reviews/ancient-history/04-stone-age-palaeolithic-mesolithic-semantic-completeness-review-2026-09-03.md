## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- R.S. Sharma's date bands were labelled textbook scaffolding, not final
  pan-Indian chronology.
- Attirampakkam's **385 ± 64 ka** transition was treated as a site-specific
  luminescence range.
- Acheulian was not treated as a race or species.
- Levallois was identified as a reduction method, not a finished tool.
- Microliths were not confined rigidly to the Holocene Mesolithic.
- Hathnora was not assigned one settled taxon or exact age.
- No Denisovan fossil was claimed for India.
- Plant-food and gathering evidence was kept visible despite preservation bias.
- Grinding stones and pottery were not converted automatically into farming.
- Bagor and Adamgarh domestication evidence was marked disputed.
- Ganga-valley faunal and residence interpretations were attributed cautiously.
- Grave goods were not treated as proof of afterlife or hereditary rank.
- Bhimbetka's paintings were not collapsed into one Mesolithic phase.
- No new current-affairs claim was required for this review.
