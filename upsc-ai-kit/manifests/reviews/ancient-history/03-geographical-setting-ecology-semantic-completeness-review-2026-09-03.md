## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- The Himalayas were treated as barriers and corridors, not an absolute wall.
- Modern political borders were not projected backward as ancient regions.
- River courses and coastlines were treated as historically changeable.
- The Ghaggar-Hakra palaeochannel was not equated automatically with a named
  Vedic river.
- Present Thar aridity was not treated as timeless.
- Resource presence was separated from extraction, skill, fuel, labour,
  transport, demand and control.
- River fertility and iron did not become sole causes of Ganga expansion or
  Magadhan power.
- Forests were treated as inhabited resource zones, not empty obstacles.
- Environmental proxies were tied to catchment, sample, chronology and
  preservation.
- Correlation was not upgraded to causation.
- Older textbook palaeoclimate propositions were not promoted as settled
  facts where modern verification was absent.
- No new live current-affairs claim was required for this review.
