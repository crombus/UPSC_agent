## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- Traditional founder dates were not presented as uncontested.
- Later biographies were not treated as contemporary diaries.
- Jain and Buddhist selves, karma and liberation were not conflated.
- Pali was not claimed as a verbatim record of the Buddha's personal dialect.
- Textual prescriptions were not treated as complete social practice.
- Śramaṇa did not become a synonym for one religion or a high-status priest.
- Merchant and royal patronage were not made exclusive explanations.
- Social critique was not converted into a claim of instant caste abolition.
- Nuns were neither romanticized as fully equal nor erased.
- Digambara-Śvetāmbara division was not reduced to one famine or council.
- Buddhist councils were labelled as school-specific transmission traditions.
- Theravada was not equated with Hinayana.
- Mahayana was not assigned one birth date, council or inventor.
- Images and rituals were not treated as exclusively Mahayana.
- Ashoka was not made founder or sole globalizer of Buddhism.
- Buddhist decline was not reduced to Turkish raids, Brahmanical hostility,
  tantra, or lost patronage.
- Jain persistence was not presented as unchanged survival.
- No current-affairs claim was required.
