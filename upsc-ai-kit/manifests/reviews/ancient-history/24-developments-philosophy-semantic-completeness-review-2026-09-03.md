## 7. Factual and interpretive controls

- Upanishads were not described as uniformly anti-ritual.
- Astika was not equated with theism or nastika with atheism.
- Later six-system doxography was not projected into the Vedic period.
- Traditional founders were not assigned secure biographies.
- Earliest texts were separated from later systematization.
- Buddhism was not described as nihilism.
- Jainism was not reduced to non-violence.
- Samkhya was not made necessarily theistic.
- Vedanta was not represented as one doctrine.
- Charvaka quotations were not presented as intact primary scripture.
- Gargi and Maitreyi were not used to claim universal gender equality.
- Sanskrit archive dominance was not made a Sanskrit-only intellectual history.
- Kumarila was placed before Shankara; later meeting stories remained
  hagiographic.
- Ramanuja and mature medieval Vedanta remained outside the core.
