## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- Brahmi left-to-right and Kharoshthi right-to-left were checked.
- Prinsep's decisive **1837** step was retained as cumulative decipherment, not
  lone discovery.
- Vikrama **58/57 BCE**, Shaka **78 CE** and Gupta **319–320 CE** were stated as
  broad era epochs; conversion still requires calendar conventions.
- Scientific dates were stated as ranges tied to samples.
- OSL was limited to last sufficient light exposure of mineral grains.
- Archaeobotany, archaeozoology, palaeoenvironmental, isotope and aDNA claims
  were restricted to their physical evidentiary reach.
- aDNA was not allowed to identify language, caste, religion or polity.
- Text, manuscript and event dates were separated.
- Coin absence was not treated as proof of absent trade.
- Inscriptions were not treated as unbiased or representative social surveys.
- No new current-affairs item was required for the semantic repair.
