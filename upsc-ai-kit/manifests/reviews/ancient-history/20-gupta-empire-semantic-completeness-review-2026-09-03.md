- Gupta origin, caste and homeland remained uncertain.
- Prashasti campaigns were not mapped as annexed provinces.
- Southern reinstatement, tribute and frontier categories remained distinct.
- The empire was not described as pan-Indian.
- Lichchhavi/Vakataka marriages did not become automatic annexation.
- Mehrauli's “Chandra” attribution remained qualified.
- Land grants did not prove complete feudalism.
- Coin circulation did not prove direct rule.
- Gold coin quality/quantity did not prove universal prosperity.
- Religion was not presented as uniformly Vaishnava.
- Faxian was treated as a selective Buddhist pilgrim.
- “Golden Age” remained a bounded, unequal florescence.
- Hunas were not made the sole cause of decline.
