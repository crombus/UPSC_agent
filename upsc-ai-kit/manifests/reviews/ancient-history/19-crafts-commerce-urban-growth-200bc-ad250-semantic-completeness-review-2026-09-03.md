## 4. Factual controls

- No single pan-Indian boom model.
- No craft cluster automatically became a guild.
- Guild deposits were not modern banking.
- Coin finds did not automatically prove markets, prosperity or rule.
- Roman coins could be bullion, hoards, ornaments or currency.
- Imported ceramics did not prove colonies.
- Roman trade did not dominate the whole economy.
- Ports depended on agrarian/craft hinterlands.
- Religious patronage was not reduced to commercial motives.
- Urbanization was not detached from villages and labour.
- State influence was not assumed uniformly.
- Third-century change was not made synchronized urban collapse.
- Araghatta was corrected to the official pot-wheel description without
  imposing one motive-power mechanism.
