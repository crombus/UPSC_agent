## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- Sixteen was treated as a conventional textual list, not a fixed simultaneous
  geopolitical map.
- Ancient zones were not equated with present state borders.
- Political forms of Kuru, Panchala and Kamboja were not made uniformly fixed.
- Vajji and Malla were not called modern democracies.
- NBPW was not equated with a city, people, ruler or state.
- Iron was not made the sole cause of agriculture, urbanization or state
  formation.
- Punch-marked coins were not all assigned to Magadha.
- Coin absence was not used as proof of no trade.
- Guild detail from later textual layers was not projected uncritically.
- Tax fractions were not presented as uniform actual rates.
- Bimbisara-Ajatashatru-Udayin dates were labelled conventions.
- Marriage and weapon traditions were not treated as neutral archives or
  excavated technology.
- Rajagriha walls were not automatically dated to one ruler.
- Mauryan structures at Kumrahar were not back-projected into Haryanka times.
- Nanda origin stories and army figures were treated as conflicting/rhetorical.
- Magadha's river, iron and elephant advantages were compared with rivals.
- No current-affairs claim was required.
