## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- Mehrgarh was located in present-day Pakistan while retained inside the
  historical subcontinental frame.
- Mehrgarh was not made a universal diffusion template.
- Koldihwa/Mahagara rice was not treated as automatically domesticated.
- Lahuradewa did not become proof of one independent domestication origin.
- North-eastern similarities were not converted into migration or ethnicity.
- Ash mounds were not assigned one universal ritual or practical function.
- Chalcolithic did not become exclusive copper technology or automatic
  urbanism.
- Black-and-red ware was not equated with one culture or people.
- Ganeshwar, OCP and copper hoards were kept distinct.
- Broad Kayatha/Malwa/Jorwe bands were identified as textbook scaffolding.
- Storage, grave goods and rare copper were not upgraded automatically to
  state, caste or hereditary class.
- Late Jorwe change was treated as multi-causal reorganisation.
