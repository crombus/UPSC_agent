## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- Arthashastra was not treated as a transparent Mauryan manual.
- Megasthenes fragments were not treated as neutral or complete.
- Nanda-overthrow legends were separated from contemporary evidence.
- Seleucid territorial clauses and epigamia were kept debated.
- Bindusara's expansion was not mapped with false precision.
- Kalinga casualty/deportation figures were treated as royal claims.
- Ashoka was not portrayed as abolishing coercion or the army.
- Edict findspots were not turned into a uniform border.
- Tamil polities and Tambapanni were not called Mauryan provinces.
- Provincial structures were not made identical everywhere.
- Officer titles were not equated with modern posts.
- Megasthenes' committees/army figures were not treated as audited data.
- Arthashastra monopolies were not converted into an empire-wide command
  economy.
- Punch-marked coins were not all assigned to Mauryan royal issue.
- Megasthenes' “no slavery” claim was tested against other sources.
- Dhamma was neither equated wholly with Buddhism nor modern secularism.
- Animal policy was not called a total meat/war ban.
- Third Council and mission narratives were not called fully inscriptional.
- Mauryan art was not reduced to Persian copying.
- Decline was not reduced to pacifism, Brahmanical reaction, economic collapse
  or weak successors.
