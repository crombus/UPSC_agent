- Praśasti claims were not converted into audited campaign records.
- Campaign zones were not treated as permanent provinces.
- Modern linguistic states were not projected backward.
- Brahmanization was not equated with total cultural homogenization.
- Grant formulas did not automatically prove feudalism or actual enforcement.
- Bhakti was not reduced to anti-caste protest.

- Maritime contact was not converted into overseas colonization.
- Elite female donors were not used to claim universal gender equality.
- Eastern Chalukya continuation was separated from Badami Chalukya collapse.
- Rajaraja I's Vengi intervention was not used to claim the dynasty simply ended
