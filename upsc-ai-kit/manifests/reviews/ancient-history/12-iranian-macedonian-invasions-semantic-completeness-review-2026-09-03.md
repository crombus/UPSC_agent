## 9. Factual, chronological and source controls

- “First foreign invasion” was rejected.
- Darius's 516 BCE date was labelled a convention, not exact proof.
- Gandhara and Hindush were not expanded to all Punjab, Sindh or India.
- Royal inscription lists were not treated as administrative maps.
- Herodotean tribute, army and ethnographic figures were not used as exact
  statistics.
- Achaemenid control was not made uniformly direct.
- Kharosthi was not assigned a known Persian inventor or exact introduction
  event.
- Punch-marked coinage was not derived automatically from Persia.
- Mauryan art/administration was not called a Persian copy.
- Aornos identification was kept debated.
- Alexander was not said to have conquered India or the Ganga plain.
- Ambhi's alliance and local resistance were both retained.
- Porus was neither made an unequivocal victor nor erased after a simple
  defeat.
- The Hyphasis halt was not reduced to Nanda army rumours.
- Surviving classical narratives were not called neutral eyewitness sources.
- Indian silence was not treated as proof of no event or impact.
- Alexander's settlements were not equated with durable pan-regional rule.
- Mauryan unification was not made a direct result of the campaign.
- Later Gandhara art and Indo-Greek coinage were not projected into 326 BCE.
- No current-affairs claim was required.
