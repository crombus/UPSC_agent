**1 — B.** (A) sixteen were not provinces under a single ruler; (B) the transmitted list must be compared with changing regional histories; (C) lists do not supply one year's exact map; (D) urban centres do not fix political frontiers. Trap: textual map reflects variable horizons. [B §§9.1–9.2, 11; W Q1–2]

**2 — D.** (A) kin identities survived territorial development; (B) dynastic succession was not a necessary common outcome; (C) royal institutions did not appear uniformly; (D) territoriality could increase without one predetermined political path. Trap: avoid teleology from tribe to empire. [B §§9.1, 10; W Q3–4]

**3 — D.** (A) even the older list needs archaeological comparison; (B) later variants may reflect different political horizons; (C) list size does not establish fixed borders; (D) compare textual layers with archaeological horizons. Trap: *Mahāvastu*/*Bhagavatī* variation matters. [B §9.2; A §§1–2; W Q2]

**4 — C.** (A) Kaushambi/Yamuna belongs to Vatsa; (B) Shravasti/Sarayu belongs to Kosala; (C) Avanti belongs to Malwa–Narmada, while Ashmaka remains Godavari-linked; (D) Champa/Ganga belongs to Anga. Trap: a nearby corridor is not the same polity's core. [B §§11, 21; W Q33 and verified PYQ Q17]

**5 — B.** (A) upper doab belongs more closely to Kuru, not Kamboja; (B) Taxila/Gandhara and the Kamboja north-west highland frontier fit; (C) Mathura is Shurasena's, not Gandhara's; (D) lower Yamuna belongs to Vatsa's corridor. Trap: Kamboja's precise limits are uncertain. [B §11; W Q33]

**6 — B.** (A) Vaishali belongs to Vajji, not Malla; (B) Kaushambi/Vatsa, Pava/Malla and Champa/Anga all fit; (C) Vaishali is not Anga's; (D) Ujjayini is Avanti's, not Vatsa's. Trap: keep proximate eastern cities politically distinct. [B §11; W Q41]

**7 — D.** (A) Anga is important but not in the conventional four; (B) Kāśi was absorbed by Kosala; (C) Gandhara is not in that four-power competition; (D) Vatsa joins Magadha, Kosala and Avanti. Trap: a listed polity need not be part of this quartet. [B §§1, 11.1–12; W Q8]

**8 — C.** (A) Mathura belongs to Shurasena, not Matsya; (B) Kaushambi belongs to Vatsa, not Kuru; (C) Kuru/Indraprastha and Matsya/Viratanagara fit tradition; (D) Ujjayini is Avanti's, not Matsya's. Trap: use approximate zones, not exact modern borders. [B §11; A §2]

**9 — A.** (A) all four traditional matches hold; (B) Vaishali belongs to Vajji, not Kosala; (C) Kaushambi is Vatsa's, not Shurasena's; (D) Champa is Anga's, not Kāśi's. Trap: Panchala also has the southern Kampilya centre. [B §11; T Q10–11]

**10 — C.** (A) Taxila is firmly linked to Gandhara; (B) Rajagriha is Magadha's early centre; (C) Rajapura is a later uncertain Kamboja association, not a secure excavated capital; (D) Kaushambi is Vatsa's. Trap: a transmitted candidate is not an excavated capital. [B §11]

**11 — B.** (A) elite deliberation is plausible; (B) a hall does not establish universal suffrage; (C) restricted political membership is plausible; (D) chiefly executives and assemblies could coexist. Trap: a structure does not reveal a complete electorate. [B §§9.1, 12; W Q11–12]

**12 — C.** (A) cohesion alone did not settle logistics or warfare; (B) revenues alone did not settle coalition dynamics; (C) both cohesion and Magadhan mobilization mattered; (D) narrative devices alone do not settle outcome. Trap: Vassakara story is not a complete war chronology. [B §§12, 14; W Q21, Q31]

**13 — A.** (A) assembly and later voting-token descriptions require contextual dating; (B) identical sixth-century franchise is not documented; (C) procedures were not uniform across all *gaṇas*; (D) terms do not independently date Vajji elections. Trap: do not backdate later procedural terminology. [B §12; T Q15–16]

**14 — A.** (A) context and independent textual/material correlation are needed; (B) a parallel pottery assemblage helps dating, not naming a ruler; (C) a later nearby king story does not fix this site's identity; (D) regional ware distribution does not identify a capital. Trap: NBPW ≠ king. [B §§9.2, 13.1; W Q13–14]

**15 — D.** (A) coins may enter a hoard at different times; (B) symbols may not identify a ruler; (C) monetary and in-kind exchange could coexist; (D) burial does not give the minting year of every coin. Trap: burial chronology ≠ mint chronology. [B §§9.2, 13.1–13.3; W Q15]

**16 — C.** (A) business/credit is more typical of *seṭṭhi*; (B) substantial householder more typical of *gahapati*; (C) *gahapati* and northern *Uttarāpatha* both fit; (D) *Dakṣiṇāpatha* denotes southern, not northern corridors. Trap: related economic roles are not interchangeable. [B §13.3; T Q24–26]

**17 — A.** (A) organization is suggested, while later institutional detail cannot be backdated wholesale; (B) uniform guild forms are unproved; (C) a group name alone does not date a charter; (D) membership does not imply royal appointment. Trap: textual redaction controls dating. [B §13.3; T Q23, Q54]

**18 — B.** (A) storage without collection is not enough; (B) labour, extraction, storage and transport enable sustained force; (C) markets alone cannot replace extraction and coercion; (D) ore and land control do not eliminate storage. Trap: surplus does not automatically fund armies. [B §§13.2, 17; W Q17–18]

**19 — D.** (A) Harappan urbanism was not the middle Ganga phase; (B) NBPW horizons postdate Harappan cities; (C) first urbanism predates Magadha; (D) renewed early historic/Gangetic growth is called second. Trap: first and second urban phases are distinct. [B §§1, 13.1; W Q13, Q31]

**20 — C.** (A) Vajji is Ajatashatru's war, Avanti is linked to Shishunaga; (B) Vajji was not Shishunaga's conventional conquest; (C) Bimbisara/Anga, Ajatashatru/Vajji, Shishunaga/Avanti fit; (D) transfers Vajji to Udayin and Avanti to Ajatashatru. Trap: cumulative expansion was not one reign's achievement. [B §§14–15; W Q19, Q21, Q24]

**21 — A.** (A) diplomacy is recalled but subsequent Kosalan conflict complicates it; (B) no exact extant treaty terms; (C) later war contradicts perpetual peace; (D) the marriage did not equal Bimbisara's conquest of Kosala. Trap: do not mistake retrospective story for diplomatic archive. [B §§11, 14; W Q20]

**22 — C.** (A) walls cannot securely be attributed to Bimbisara in both places; (B) early phases were not identical; (C) Rajagriha's hill/wall setting and Pataliputra's stratigraphy require separate phasing; (D) excavated fifth-century palace plans for both are unavailable. Trap: Mauryan architecture ≠ Haryanka construction. [B §§9.2, 16.1; W Q27, Q39]

**23 — B.** (A) reverses the traditional roles; (B) Ajatashatru's frontier fort and Udayin's capital role fit qualified traditions; (C) Bimbisara is associated with Rajagriha; (D) Shishunaga is better linked to Avanti's defeat. Trap: exact founding chronology varies. [B §§14–16; T Q35–36]

**24 — C.** (A) Shishunaga preceded Nanda; (B) Haryanka preceded Shishunaga; (C) Haryanka, Shishunaga and Nanda lead to the Mauryan threshold; (D) Mauryas follow rather than precede Nanda rule. Trap: broad succession is safer than exact reign dates. [B §§10, 15, 18; W Q38]

**25 — A.** (A) reports indicate perception of strength, not independently audited figures; (B) changing units do not certify exact headcounts; (C) reports are not regimental censuses; (D) Greek numbers cannot all be assigned precisely to Mahapadma. Trap: outsider force figures are not payrolls. [B §§9.2, 15; W Q26, Q44]

**26 — A.** (A) geography offered opportunities that political organization exploited amid real rivals; (B) Avanti's resource access rules out sole Magadhan ore control; (C) Kosala's fertile Sarayu zone mattered; (D) Avanti's commercial routes remained a competitive advantage. Trap: no single factor guaranteed victory. [B §§1–2, 17; A §§1, 5; T Q43]

**27 — B.** (A) later construction cannot date early ceramics; (B) early occupation and later Mauryan hall are distinct phases; (C) earlier ware does not name a later hall's builder; (D) proximity does not prove a single building phase. Trap: pottery and architecture have different chronologies. [B §§13.1, 16.1; A §1; W Q39]

**28 — C.** (A) Mauryan departments must not be assigned unchanged to Nandas; (B) Ashokan officers cannot be backdated as Haryanka offices; (C) Nanda consolidation preceded Mauryan unification; (D) Shishunaga's offices are not known as identical to Ashoka's. Trap: stop before Mauryan imperial apparatus. [B §§16.2, 18; W Q32]

**29 — A.** (A) mobility and patronage shaped audiences, not doctrinal reasoning; (B) donations alone cannot explain specific teachings; (C) crafts did not mechanically determine arguments; (D) nonroyal donors and mobile audiences existed. Trap: markets do not author doctrine. [B §§13.3–13.4; W Q31; T Q32]

**30 — B.** (A) Shuktimati is not a securely excavated capital; (B) approximate Chedi and Malla zones fit, though Chedi's traditional centre is less certain; (C) Malla's centres do not fix political borders; (D) ancient zones do not coincide precisely with modern districts. Trap: locate without inventing cadastral precision. [B §§9.2, 11; T Q10]

**31 — B.** (A) Kāśi's absorption predates later Kosala–Magadha war; (B) Kosala absorbed Kāśi before the subsequent rivalry; (C) reverses absorption; (D) Kāśi did not remain a separate equal power into Nanda times. Trap: Magadha was not the first expansionist kingdom. [B §§11, 14; T Q11]

**32 — D.** (A) identical functions in every state are unproved; (B) Mauryan duties cannot be projected backward; (C) fragmentary terms are not a full palace register; (D) senior/local roles are remembered but exact institutional duties remain unclear. Trap: a title is not a uniform organogram. [B §16.2; W Q29]

**33 — A.** (A) the device name survives, not a precise archaeological blueprint; (B) no exact battlefield follows from a name; (C) no mechanism or projectile dimensions are securely established; (D) a named machine does not fix a war year. Trap: transmitted war technology is not excavated engineering evidence. [B §15; W Q22]

**34 — D.** (A) Avanti's defeat belongs to Shishunaga, not Mahapadma; (B) Vajji's conquest belongs to Ajatashatru, not Dhana; (C) Shishunaga is credited with Avanti, not Kalashoka; (D) Kalashoka/council, Mahapadma/Nanda expansion, Dhana/last Nanda are transmitted associations. Trap: these traditions do not certify exact dates. [B §§10, 15; W Q25–26]

**35 — C.** (A) Kaushambi, not Potana, is Vatsa's centre; (B) Avanti's centres are Malwa/Narmada-linked; (C) Potana is a traditional Ashmaka centre in the Godavari region, precise site uncertain; (D) Chedi's traditional centre is Shuktimati. Trap: a literary centre is not an exact excavated coordinate. [B §11; W Q5]

**36 — A.** (A) a prescribed produce share can coexist with other dues and variable collections; (B) prescription is not observed uniform practice; (C) tolls and tribute also appear; (D) there is no uniform Nanda treasury audit. Trap: fiscal extraction is not an automatic rate. [B §§13.2, 16.2; T Q30, Q53]

**37 — D.** (A) supply does not replace military organization; (B) capture does not replace logistical transport; (C) rivals had other resource bases; (D) gathering, provisioning and deployment required political organization. Trap: military ecology is an enabling condition, not destiny. [B §§2, 17; T Q44]
