**19 — C.** (A) Recurring proportions support a modular convention. (B) Shared ratios can support technical coordination. (C) A 1:2:4 proportion does not fix absolute brick dimensions at every site. **20 — A.** (A) Household facilities and shared drains operate at compatible scales; drains require maintenance. **21 — A.** (A) Widespread wheat/barley and regionally important millet/rice fit ecological diversity. (B) Rice/millets were not equally important in every region. **22 — D.** (A) Cotton evidence is relevant, but seals do not establish regulation of spinners. (B) Household whorls support production, not a decoded quota system. **23 — B.** (A) Seal motifs cannot resolve osteological identity or prove transport use.
