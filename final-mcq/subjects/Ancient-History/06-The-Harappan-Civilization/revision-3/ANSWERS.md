**21 — A.** (A) Regionally varied crops, animals and aquatic resources fit the broad urban-rural economy. (B) Coastal millet does not prove dependence on imported Indus-core grain. **22 — D.** (A) Spinning can be inferred from whorls. (B) Domestic craft context can be examined. (C) Cotton cultivation and craft may coexist. **24 — C.** (A) Finished goods alone do not locate manufacture at the city. (B) Waste on the small site's floor supports local production, not imported waste. **25 — B.** (A) Association in a context does not make faience and stone necessarily earlier.
