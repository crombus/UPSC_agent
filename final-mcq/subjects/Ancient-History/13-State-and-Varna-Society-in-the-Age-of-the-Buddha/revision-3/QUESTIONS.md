# Revision 3 — State & Varna Society in the Age of the Buddha

Thirty original, single-best-answer questions. Select one of A–D for each. This paper does not contain answers.

1. Consider the following source uses:
   1. Vinaya rules reveal concerns of an institution rather than a population census.
   2. Coins securely identify the ruler who issued each early series.
   3. Settlement surveys can help test the scale of town–village linkages.

   Which statements are defensible?
   - A. 1 and 2 only.
   - B. 2 and 3 only.
   - C. 1 and 3 only.
   - D. 1, 2 and 3.
