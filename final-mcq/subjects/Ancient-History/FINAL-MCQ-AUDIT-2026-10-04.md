# Ancient-History final-MCQ compliance audit — 2026-10-04

## Scope

Audited all 27 catalogue topics, all 81 revision folders, their `QUESTIONS.md`,
`ANSWERS.md`, topic ledgers and the Ancient-History index. No other subject was
edited.

## Pre-repair findings

- All three revisions existed for every topic, but the hostile pass found answer
  detail gaps: missing explicit examiner-trap blocks in 392 answer sections and
  incomplete explicit option-labelled reasoning in Topic 01 Revision 1 Q1–Q12.
- Keyed option-length extremes were concentrated in several banks. Examples:
  Topic 04 had 21/34, 17/32 and 23/34 uniquely longest keyed choices; Topic 05
  had 14/36, 21/34 and 27/36 uniquely shortest keyed choices; Topic 06 had
  18/45, 10/47 and 10/47 uniquely longest keyed choices; Topic 11 had
  20/42, 14/37 and 18/37 uniquely longest keyed choices.
- Existing ledgers correctly held unresolved or rights-restricted PYQs; no
  unresolved candidate was promoted to an active solved item.

## Repairs

- Normalised visible option lengths without changing the keyed proposition,
  option order, question numbering or answer key.
- Added explicit examiner-trap blocks to every answer entry that lacked one.
- Added explicit missing option-labelled rationale lines in Topic 01 Revision 1.
- Preserved all source references, PYQ holds, rights holds and catalogue
  completion claims. No exact PYQ wording was added.
