# Revision 2 — cited answers

Original questions only. Sources `B`, `A`, `N` defined in `../COVERAGE.md`. Each alternative is independently evaluated.

1. **B.** A describes direct fabric; **B** recognises “granary” as a modern, function-based suggestion, not a translated Harappan designation; C describes measurable built elevation. 2. **C.** A correctly keeps domestic houses and halls but replaces the public-bath *building class* with a household supply well. 3. **D.** A wrongly treats grid variation as absence of planning. B mistakes variation for lack of shared norms.
