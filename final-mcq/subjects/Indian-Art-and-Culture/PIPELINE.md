# Indian Art and Culture MCQ continuation pipeline

**Queue:** All 15 catalogue topics are released with three independent original banks. Re-run the repository-wide audit after any repair; do not mark a bank complete without its per-bank ledger and hostile report.

At the start of every continuation:

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `AGENT_MEMORY.md`, `final-mcq/INSTRUCTIONS.md`, this file, the subject-root `COVERAGE.md`, and the active topic's coverage ledger.
2. Run `python final-mcq/tools/indian_art_culture_pipeline.py`; obey its `NEXT` topic and structural findings.
3. Read the active topic's complete Basic owner, Advanced owner, solved workbook/PYQ records and mandated OCR sources before authoring.
4. Build three independent original banks. Each bank needs an answer-free `QUESTIONS.md`, a detailed `ANSWERS.md`, and its own `COVERAGE.md`.
5. Audit source grounding, owner boundaries, four-option balance, key mapping, cross-bank distinctness, leakage, PYQ provenance/key/rights and hostile distractors. Keep unresolved PYQs explicitly held.
6. Update coverage ledgers and `INDEX.md` only after the audit is clean. Run the subject pipeline last.

The pipeline is a read-only structural gate. It cannot authenticate a UPSC key, grant reproduction permission or replace substantive source review.
