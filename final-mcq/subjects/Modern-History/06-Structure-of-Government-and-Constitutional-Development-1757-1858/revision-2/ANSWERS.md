### R2-002 — C

✅ **Source:** `B§1; Bipan p.94` (Diwani grant, nominal nawab's Nizamat and Company fiscal control); Basic = `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Modern-Indian-History/basic/06_Government-Structure-and-Constitutional-Development-1757-1858.md`; Bipan = `books/modern india/MODERN INDIA -- BIPIN CHANDRA -- ENGLISH ##.pdf`. ### R2-044 — A
