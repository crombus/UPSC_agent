## 1813 primary-text follow-up — R2-016 distinctness, not topic release

✅ Read the **1817 printed statutory collection's period OCR** for [53 Geo. III c.155, preamble, §I and §XCV](https://archive.org/download/10225491bsb/10225491bsb_djvu.txt), [scan volume record](https://archive.org/details/10225491bsb).
