**Source:** Cabinet Mission Statement, 16 May 1946, paras. 3-23, UK Parliament Historic Hansard; Bipan Chandra et al., *India's Struggle for Independence*, PDF pp. 522-524; Topic 26 Basic, §§8.7-8.8.

- **A:** Incorrect. Those provinces belonged to Section A.

- **B:** Correct. The arrangement placed the north-western and north-eastern Muslim-majority zones in separate sections within one Union.

- **C:** Incorrect. This swaps the two sections.

- **D:** Incorrect. They were made up of British Indian provinces.

**Examiner trap:** Swapping the north-western and north-eastern sections.

### Q47. [Original]

**Answer: C**

**Source:** Cabinet Mission Statement, 16 May 1946, paras. 3-23, UK Parliament Historic Hansard; Bipan Chandra et al., *India's Struggle for Independence*, PDF pp. 522-524; Topic 26 Basic, §§8.7-8.8.

- **A:** Incorrect. The plan stressed immediate formation.

- **B:** Incorrect. The Mission was temporary and advisory.

- **C:** Correct. The transitional executive was intended to carry day-to-day administration with popular support.

- **D:** Incorrect. The statement proposed Indian control of all portfolios.

**Examiner trap:** Assuming constitutional negotiations postponed all executive Indianisation.
