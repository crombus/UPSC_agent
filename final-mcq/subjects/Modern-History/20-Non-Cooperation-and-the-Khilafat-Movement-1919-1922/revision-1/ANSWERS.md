### R1-001 — C

**Source:** `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Modern-Indian-History/basic/20_Non-Cooperation-and-Khilafat-Movement.md` §§1-2, 4; Bipan Chandra, *Modern India*, printed pp.269-270; Sekhar Bandyopadhyay, *From Plassey to Partition*, printed p.299.

- **B — incorrect:** Separate electorates under the 1919 reforms were a constitutional issue, not the original object of the Khilafat agitation.
- **C — correct:** Correct. The Khilafat agitation arose from anxiety that the Ottoman Sultan-Caliph would lose authority over Muslim holy places and essential territorial standing after the war.
- **D — incorrect:** Indian Khilafat leaders sought preservation of the Ottoman Caliphate; they did not demand its immediate abolition.

**Examiner trap:** Keep the Ottoman post-war issue separate from Indian constitutional representation.
