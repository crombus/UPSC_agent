2. The 1 August formal launch and the distinction between launch, Calcutta adoption and Nagpur ratification.

4. Surrender of titles; schools and national education; courts and panchayats; councils; official functions and conditional service resignation.
5. Foreign-cloth boycott, khadi, charkha, swadeshi, temperance and the constructive/negative programme distinction.
6. Conditional escalation to mass civil disobedience and tax refusal at Bardoli, announced but never launched.
7. Students, lawyers, traders/business divisions, women and workers.
8. The rule of local translation rather than uniform central direction.
9. Oudh Kisan Sabha and Eka distinctions; Eka grievance, oath, social base, autonomy and repression.
10. Midnapur, Andhra/Guntur, Assam labour, Akali, tribal and forest-law strands without overclaim.
11. Malabar's agrarian origins, Khilafat-tenant fusion, repression, changing communalised trajectory and source caution.
12. Prince of Wales boycott, Bombay violence, Volunteer Corps, arrests and repression.
13. Chauri Chaura: verified 4 February 1922 date, Gorakhpur, twenty-two policemen; withdrawal on 12 February through the Bardoli resolution.
14. Gandhi's arrest and six-year sentence; bounded legal-defence PYQ route.
15. Achievements, limitations, mass character, Congress transformation, unity and strains.
16. Abolition of the Caliphate in 1924 as consequence; Topic 21 receives only a handoff.
