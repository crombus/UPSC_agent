## Holds and source variances

- Swaraj Party formation: December 1922 is used by compressed accounts; repository working date is 1 January 1923. No live key forces the disputed single date.
- Jatin Das: 63/64-day counting variance. Live items test the demand and secure death date, not disputed duration.
- HRA/HSRA: `Association` and `Army` are expansion variants in sources, not separate organisations.
- Vithalbhai Patel: official Sansad date is 22 August 1925; Bipan prints March 1925. Live banks key only the secure year/office unless the official-date variance is explicitly stated.
- Exact seat/vote data: only the supported 42/101 central elected-seat figure is used. No unsupported all-India vote share is keyed.
- *The Philosophy of the Bomb*: strongest wording attributes drafting/authorship to Bhagwati Charan Vohra; possible consultation is not converted into equal co-authorship.
- `Lost Leader`: propaganda tactic is covered; unsupported printing counts, verbatim text and distributor rosters remain held.
- Exact official PYQs: held absent authenticated same-series paper/key pairing plus documented reproduction permission.
