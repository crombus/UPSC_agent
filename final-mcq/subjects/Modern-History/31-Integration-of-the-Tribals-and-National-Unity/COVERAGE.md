## Factual repairs and holds

- “Tribal Panchsheel” is treated as a later label for five guidelines; no unsupported proclamation year is assigned.
- Nagaland: 1955 insurgency; early-1956 army deployment; mid-1957 containment; July 1960 Sixteen-Point Agreement; 1962 Act; statehood **1 December 1963**.
- Meghalaya: APHLC 1960; 1969 Act; autonomous state operational **2 April 1970**; full statehood **21 January 1972**.
- NEFA: the claim “created as a Union Territory in 1948” is held and never keyed. Live chronology uses the **1954 NEFA name**, Arunachal Pradesh Union Territory on **21 January 1972**, and statehood on **20 February 1987**.
- Mizoram: the source's “Union Territory in 1973” is held and never keyed. Live chronology uses **21 January 1972**, Mizo Accord **30 June 1986**, and statehood **20 February 1987**.
- Manipur and Tripura statehood is used only for the comparative **21 January 1972** accommodation map.
- Jharkhand broadening is described as partial coalition expansion, not complete conversion from tribal to class politics.

## Constitutional precision

- TAC: mandatory in each state with Scheduled Areas; possible by Presidential direction in an ST state without Scheduled Areas.
- Fifth Schedule governor powers are always tied to Scheduled Areas and presidential oversight.
- Sixth Schedule: Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and Mizoram; autonomous districts/regions, district/regional councils and enumerated legislative, administrative, judicial and fiscal powers under state/gubernatorial oversight.
- Original Article 338 is the presidential Special Officer/Commissioner arrangement, not the later separate NCST.
