## Chronology control

- 1948: Congress dual-membership rule and Socialist exit.
- 1949: DMK founded under C.N. Annadurai.
- 1950-51: Tandon contest and resolution of the party-government issue.
- 1951: Jana Sangh and KMPP formed; CPI adopts electoral participation near year-end.
- 1952: KMPP and Socialist Party merge into PSP.
- 1953, 1956, 1958, 1961: CPI's Madurai, Palghat, Amritsar and Vijayawada position changes.
- 1955: Congress Avadi turn; Lohia's separate Socialist Party phase begins.
- 1957: elected Communist ministry in Kerala.
- 1959: Kerala ministry dismissed; Swatantra founded; Nagpur agrarian resolution.
- 1963: Kamaraj Plan, strictly bounded to party revitalisation.
- 1964: CPI splits into CPI and CPI(M).
- 1967: transition to wider state-level non-Congress government formation; no exact state count asserted.
