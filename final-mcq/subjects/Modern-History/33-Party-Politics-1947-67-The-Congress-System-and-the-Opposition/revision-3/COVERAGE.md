## Chronology control

- **1948:** Congress rule against dual membership; Socialist exit; CPI insurrectionary line active.
- **1951:** Jana Sangh and KMPP formation; CPI's turn towards the approaching election.
- **1951-52:** first general election under universal adult franchise and plurality voting.
- **1952:** KMPP-Socialist merger into the PSP.
- **1957:** elected CPI ministry in Kerala.
- **1959:** Kerala dismissal and President's Rule; Swatantra formation.
- **1962:** third general election retained only as part of the first-three-election pattern; no unsafe exact figures are used.
- **1963:** Kamaraj Plan appears only as an organisational-revival attempt.
- **1964:** CPI split into CPI and CPI(M).
- **1967:** terminal state-level transition; no later ministry or organisational narrative is imported.
