## Coverage ledger

- December 1984 record mandate: Congress 404 of 514 elected seats; deferred Assam/Punjab polling.
- Rajiv modernisation: telecom, computerisation, science missions, education and panchayati-raj initiative; constitutionalisation caution.
- 1985 Punjab (Rajiv–Longowal), Assam and Mizoram accords.
- Anti-Defection Act and 52nd Constitutional Amendment (1985).
- Shah Bano reversal (1985–86) and Ayodhya shrine opening as twin identity concessions.
- Bofors (1987), V.P. Singh's Jan Morcha (1987), Janata Dal/National Front (1988).
- 1989 fragmentation: Congress about 197, BJP 2 to 86, National Front minority government with BJP and Left outside support.
- Mandal implementation in 1990: 27% OBC reservation in central government employment and nationwide agitation.
- Advani's rath yatra (1990), Babri Masjid demolition on 6 December 1992; chronology kept distinct.
- 1991 Rao–Manmohan liberalisation triggered by a balance-of-payments crisis; detailed mechanics remain Economy-owned.
- Rajiv Gandhi's 1991 assassination.
- 1996 short BJP government, United Front under Deve Gowda and Gujral, NDA under Vajpayee in 1998–99, consolidation in 1999.
- Advanced synthesis: Mandal, Mandir and Market; three simultaneous transitions and the democratic-deepening counter-reading.
