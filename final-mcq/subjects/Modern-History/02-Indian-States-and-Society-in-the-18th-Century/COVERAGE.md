Panipat and the Company feature here only for regional-state outcome and chronological comparison; no Topic01 original stem or direct owner is repeated.

**Classification caution:** B/A's three-type teaching schema places Marathas among mobilised/new states, and Mysore among older independent kingdoms. Chandra's OCR chapter can call the Maratha kingdom a powerful “succession” state in a *broader* post-imperial sense (PDF p. 37). These are different levels of classification, **not** evidence that the Maratha confederacy was a Mughal provincial successor of the Bengal/Awadh/Hyderabad type. Coin, khutba, title and sanad show legitimacy or *claim*, not universal collection/compliance. Twelve major misls is a conventional classification, not an immutable administrative grid. Regional commercial vigour cannot be used as a peasant-welfare statistic. No unsupported demographic or revenue percentage has been introduced.
