## Coverage ledger

Every bank independently tests:

- Janata's January 1977 merger of Congress(O), Jana Sangh, Bharatiya Lok Dal and Socialists.
- March 1977 mandate: 330 of 542 seats; Morarji Desai; first non-Congress Union government.
- Janata's structural weakness, the 1979 dual-membership controversy over RSS affiliation, and Charan Singh's July 1979 caretaker phase without a confidence vote.
- The 1978 Congress split and Congress(I); Indira Gandhi's January 1980 return with 353 of 529 seats, interpreted as Janata's collapse rather than a fresh Emergency mandate.
- Sanjay Gandhi's June 1980 death and Rajiv Gandhi's entry into active politics.
- Telugu Desam: N.T. Rama Rao, founding on 29 March 1982, 1983 Andhra breakthrough, Telugu self-respect and resentment of central interference.
- Left Front: CPI(M), Jyoti Basu and West Bengal from 1977.
- Assam Movement: AASU, migration and the “foreigners” question.
- Jammu and Kashmir: Sheikh/Farooq Abdullah pattern and recurring central interference with elected governments.
- Anandpur Sahib Resolution: Chandigarh, river-water shares and greater state autonomy.
- Bhindranwale, militancy, Operation Blue Star in June 1984, Golden Temple complex at Amritsar, and Indira Gandhi's assassination on 31 October 1984.
- Comparative federalism: electoral accommodation versus delayed negotiation and escalation; regional assertion as a consequence of integration rather than automatic national disintegration.
- Political-management, national-security and federalist readings of Punjab; Chandra's qualified preference for political management.
- Evidence restraint, topic boundaries and safe use of the 1985 accords as a following-topic boundary marker.
