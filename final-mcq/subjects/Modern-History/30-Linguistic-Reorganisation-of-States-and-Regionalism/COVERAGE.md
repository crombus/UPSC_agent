## Factual repairs and holds

- The official SRC resolution/report dates, **29 December 1953** and **30 September 1955**, replace the package/OCR shorthand of August 1953 and October 1955 in live questions.
- The States Reorganisation Act is tested as effective on **1 November 1956**, not loosely as an Act first passed in November.
- The Act's territorial work is distinguished from the Seventh Amendment's constitutional restructuring.
- The 1956 Bombay settlement is enlargement of bilingual Bombay; bifurcation belongs to **1 May 1960**.
- Haryana is a state; Chandigarh is a Union territory and joint capital; Himachal's 1966 change is a territorial transfer to the then Union territory, not statehood.
- Nagaland's **1 December 1963** statehood is used only for classification; substantive tribal integration is held for Topic 31.
- English is not described as a constitutionally designated “associate official language”.
- No casualty figure, boundary detail or commission recommendation lacking source support is used as a live key.
