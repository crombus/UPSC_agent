## Factual and interpretive holds

- The source's interpretation of 1962 emphasising Chinese compulsions is never keyed as uncontested fact.
- Forward Policy causation is treated as disputed; no monocausal verdict is live.
- Panchsheel is not represented as a completed border settlement.
- Bandung is a precursor to NAM; Belgrade 1961 is the founding summit.
- Non-alignment is active independent judgement, not neutrality, isolation, arithmetic equidistance or pacifism.
- Goa demonstrates bounded use of force in territorial decolonisation, not entry into a bloc.
- Socialistic pattern means a public-sector-led mixed economy, not abolition of private enterprise.
- Defence restraint, force expansion and the specific 1962 planning failure are kept analytically distinct.
