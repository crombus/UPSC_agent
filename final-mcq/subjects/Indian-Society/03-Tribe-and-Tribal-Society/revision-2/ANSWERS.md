# Revision 2 — Tribe and Tribal Society: explained answers

Each item is original. The answer-free question release is separate from this explained key.

### Q1. B
- **A — Incorrect:** Scheduled Tribes are internally diverse across territory, language, livelihood and religion.
- **B — Correct:** Article 342 supplies the legal notification route; sociological variation cannot be used to invalidate a notified group.
- **C — Incorrect:** Interaction with caste society is a sociological observation, not the constitutional trigger.
- **D — Incorrect:** Forest livelihood is neither a universal sociological test nor the constitutional rule for notification.

**Sources:** Basic §2; Basic semantic-completeness block; Constitution of India, Article 342 via India Code: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/
**Examiner trap:** Confusing an administrative constitutional notification with a universal anthropological checklist.
