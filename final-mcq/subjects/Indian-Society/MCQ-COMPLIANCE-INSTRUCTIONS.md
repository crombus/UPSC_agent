# Indian Society Final-MCQ Compliance Instructions

Read this file immediately before and after every Indian Society topic pass.

## Non-negotiable requirements

1. Follow the catalogue order exactly. Do not skip, merge, compress, or open a later topic.
2. Each topic requires `revision-1`, `revision-2`, and `revision-3`.
3. Every revision must independently cover the complete Basic/Advanced owner boundary.
4. Every revision must contain answer-free `QUESTIONS.md`, separately explained `ANSWERS.md`,
   and an auditable `COVERAGE.md`.
5. Questions are original unless exact PYQ wording, same-paper official key, and reproduction
   permission are all documented. Otherwise keep the PYQ as an explicit hold and test its
   concept independently.
6. Questions must not reveal answers through option length, grammar, specificity, formatting,
   terminology, or repeated keyed patterns. Options must be plausible, distinct and balanced.
7. Do not reuse generic stems or near-duplicate wording within or across revisions. Revision 1
   establishes the foundation; Revision 2 uses counterexamples and close distinctions; Revision
   3 uses applied, institutional and evidence-based framing.
8. Do not hallucinate facts, dates, schemes, reports, funding figures, institutional mandates,
   sources, PYQ status or outcomes. Date every live statistic or programme fact.
9. For tribal institutions/schemes, include the founding/legal instrument or policy origin,
   mandate, implementing institutions, timeline, reporting/monitoring route, funding/outlay
   where authoritatively available, and India-specific social consequence. Keep Polity and
   Social-Justice doctrine routed to their owners rather than duplicating it.
10. Each answer must give the correct option, evidence, a rationale for all four options, and an
    examiner trap. Every unresolved source/PYQ/rights issue remains visible in the topic ledger.
11. Run structural, option-balance, leakage, cross-revision distinctness, source and hostile
    audits. Repair every actionable finding before changing the subject index.

## Required release checks

- All three revisions and all required files exist.
- Every question maps to a named coverage unit in that revision.
- Question/key mapping is one-to-one and contiguous.
- Questions contain no answer, key, rationale, source cue or explanatory suffix.
- Each answer contains four option-specific rationales, sources and a trap.
- Correct positions are independently shuffled and reasonably balanced.
- No material owner detail is only a distractor; each revision has affirmative coverage.
- Exact PYQ wording/options remain withheld unless the provenance/key/rights gate is documented.
- The subject pipeline passes after the index update.
