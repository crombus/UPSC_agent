# Indian Society 01 — Revision 2: original cross-link practice

### Q1. A district table records several languages, but its neighbourhoods differ in composition and use of shared facilities. Which evidence best tests whether district diversity entails recognised contact *within* neighbourhoods?

B. Compare neighbourhood composition and observed intergroup use of shared sites, while checking participation in decisions.
C. Compare distances between language-group settlements and their ward totals, treating proximity as evidence of recognition.

### Q2. Which research design best investigates whether class position modifies a language minority's access to schooling?

B. Compare remote and connected minority settlements without separating class positions within either.
