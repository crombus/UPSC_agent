## Explicit holds

- **Exact Prelims PYQs:** 0 included. A candidate can be released only after a visually checked official paper, matching official same-paper/Series-A key, upstream provenance, and documented reproduction permission. The 2019 and 2024 GS-I items in the owner are Mains themes, not objective Prelims PYQs.
- **2025–2027 Census evidence:** the 30 April 2025 Cabinet decision and 16 June 2025 Gazette reference dates are decisions/planning evidence, not completed caste-enumeration results. No released question asserts a completed Census 2027 table.
- **PVTG PDF date:** the Ministry-hosted PDF has 9 July 2024 file-creation metadata, but no printed dated notification or list-update event. That metadata is not treated as an event and is not used as affirmative evidence.
- **Dynamic facts:** PVTG counts and the legacy criteria remain dated to their named PIB/MoTA sources; later official revisions must be checked before reuse.
- **Institutional scope:** detailed constitutional/statutory doctrine, full caste-system theory, tribal-policy debates, welfare instruments and regionalism/secularism doctrine remain routed to their owning topics.
