## Source decisions and outstanding holds

- Read the complete Basic and Advanced owner, the existing solved workbook and practice atlas, and Satish Chandra, *A History of Medieval India*, OCR pp. 127–128 (print 116–117), 186–189 (print 175–178). Each new item cites its page/section in `ANSWERS.md`; each A–D rationale is separately written and checked against its question's cited source. Q50 additionally uses Chandra, *Medieval India: From Sultanat to the Mughals*, Part II, OCR p. 27.
- **Correction, not an invented patron:** Chandra OCR p. 187 only says Jaisi *lived* at Jaunpur. Rekhta's [Jaisi profile](https://www.rekhta.org/poets/malik-muhammad-jayasi/profile) dates *Padmavat* to 1540 and records its praise of Sher Shah; a Sharqi commission for that work would be anachronistic. Q51 tests the evidence limit, not an alleged named Sharqi patron.
- **Chronology conflict:** Chandra OCR p. 187 says Bahlul was formally crowned (1451) “when the ruler of Delhi died in exile”; its sequencing of Alam's death conflicts with [Britannica's Alam Shah entry](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Ala-al-Din-Alam-Shah), which dates retirement to Badaun to 1451 and death to 1478. Q46 retains the secure accession/withdrawal sequence and deliberately **does not** infer a death in 1451. The root's “formal crown after death” detail is held, not endorsed.
- Babur's gunners are cross-owned by the Mughal transition; Q50 asks only their verified names at Panipat, not later campaigns. Q38/48/49 distinguish Ranthambhor **attempted** allegiance transfer, Nagaur protection, and Dholpur/Gwalior conquest without conflating them.
- The separate root/AUDIT and revisions 1–2 are intentionally untouched. Their unchecked affirmative-detail gaps remain open. No directly owned exact official Prelims Series-A paper/final-key pair was independently established; do not label any question a PYQ or invent a key.
