## Source and correction ledger

- Rechecked Satish Chandra, *A History of Medieval India*, printed pp. 116–117 / OCR pp. 127–128 (Firuz succession, Timur) and printed pp. 175–178 / OCR pp. 186–189 (Sharqi, Sayyid–Lodi, Sikandar, frontier); Basic §§9–19, Advanced §§1–5, complete local solved workbook and practice atlas.
- **Correction, not an added attribution:** Chandra says Malik Muhammad Jayasi *lived at Jaunpur*, but does **not** say a Sharqi sultan personally patronised him. `basic/04_Khaljis-Alauddin.md` describes *Padmavat* as a sixteenth-century literary work, after Bahlul's 1484 annexation. R1-Q42 tests this distinction; the parent-level claim of Sharqi patronage remains unsupported and must not migrate into R2/R3. Sharqi architectural and collective literary patronage remain supported (OCR pp. 186–187; R1-Q16, Q36).
- Named gun specialists are sourced to `basic/12_Babur-and-Central-Asian-Backdrop.md`, not the OCR pages of this topic. R1-Q48 deliberately asks only Ustad Ali and Mustafa; later Babur campaigns remain cross-owned.
- Chandra, OCR p. 187, describes Bahlul's men taking Delhi and links the **formal crowning** to the Delhi ruler's death in exile; R1-Q43 retains the source's wording rather than assigning an unsupported separate death date.
- Chandra, OCR pp. 188–189, distinguishes harsh corruption punishments, **conquest** of Dholpur/Gwalior, **protection** of Nagaur's khan and an **attempt** to shift Ranthambhor's allegiance. R1-Q38 and Q44–Q47 test all four relations without assuming an accomplished annexation of Ranthambhor.
- Original questions have independently positioned keys: A/B/C/D = **12/12/12/12**, irregular ordering. Unique keyed longest = **12/48**; unique keyed shortest = **12/48** by character count; remaining 24 are intermediate or tied. Neither extreme is a dependable heuristic.
