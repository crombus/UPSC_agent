## Fact-check and publication holds

- **Exact PYQs:** the 2019 amil/iqta/Mir Bakhshi and 2022 fanam items in the solved workbook do not qualify as exact solved Prelims items until the printed official Series-A paper **and matching FINAL key** have been visually compared. They are covered by original questions only. The 2020 Persian-sources and 2023 technology questions are Mains, not Prelims. Official year-by-year census remains `in_progress`; do not label this bank `complete` pending that step.
- **Risalat:** Chandra ch. 8 p. 121 assigns religious matters and pious foundations under the chief sadr; other reconstructions use diplomacy. R1-Q10 asks specifically for Chandra’s account; official correspondence is securely insha in the same page.
- **Coin context:** Chandra p. 124 uses generic copper *dirham*, and elsewhere discusses *jital*; Basic §4 distinguishes the Delhi silver tanka/copper jital pair from southern/coastal gold *fanam*. Do not turn the routed 2022 fanam demand into a guessed exact PYQ.
- **Taxes:** Chandra p. 135 notes practical Brahman exemption before Firuz separated jizya from land revenue and applied it to Brahmans. Jizya, kharaj, ushr, zakat, khams and canal dues have different bases and are not interchangeable.
- **Women/property:** Chandra p. 132 reports commentators giving a widow rights to *dispose* of the sonless husband’s divided, non-joint property; neither a universal widow right over joint estates nor a census of real-life inheritance follows.
- **Numerical clause resolved, limited scope:** IGNOU Unit 19 PDF p. 15 independently says Firuz's water tax on villages using his canals was *one tenth of produce*; IGNOU *BHIC-107* Unit 9, printed pp. 184 and 190, identifies the canal-user tax as *haqq-i-sharb*. R1-Q86 now tests both basis and rate rather than confusing ten per cent of produce with a universal cash/land tax. **Held assertion:** Satish Chandra ch. 8 pp. 118–137 does not independently state that rate; no rate for private-well users, nonbeneficiaries or all Sultanate districts is inferred. These official IGNOU PDFs were read directly, not inferred from search snippets.
- **Office provenance resolved, qualified chronology:** IGNOU *BHIC-107* Unit 4, printed p. 95, calls mushrif-i-mumalik accountant-general and mustaufi-i-mumalik auditor-general and specifies the **Firuz-era** income/expenditure division; printed p. 104 identifies kotwal with town order and defence. IGNOU Unit 19 PDF pp. 9–10 independently places the finance posts under wizarat and the kotwal in policing. R1-Q45 now affirmatively distinguishes all three with three same-era, close function swaps. **Held assertion:** Chandra ch. 8 pp. 118–137 does not itself identify these individual offices by name, nor do these IGNOU passages establish identical duties in every reign. Diwan-i-riyasat is explicitly named by the cross-owned Advanced Topic 04, not Chandra ch. 8.
- **Period control:** Mir Bakhshi and mansab belong to later Mughal arrangements, not Khalji military bureaucracy. Q72 expressly warns that *qanungo* cannot be projected as a universal early Mamluk office. Sufi and court sources are not treated as interchangeable evidence.
- **Audit boundaries:** All 90 items and all 360 explanations were checked for one-to-one IDs, four distinct choices, one marked key, correct/incorrect flags, source coordinates, absence of key text in QUESTIONS.md, option punctuation and conditional length cue. Scope is the examinable topic owner and named cross-owners, not a claim that all possible minor medieval terms are settled.
