## Source-caution decisions preserved

- Panipat II is securely keyed to 5 November 1556.
- No exact day is keyed for Haldighati because the available OCR record contains unsafe precision.
- Kashmir is treated as a qualified 1586–87 campaign/annexation process, not a single effortless date.
- Sindh is treated as a qualified 1590–91 process.
- Qandahar's 1595 transfer is not converted into a complete Topic 19 foreign-policy treatment.
- Haldighati did not terminate Mewar resistance, and battlefield outcome is not equated with immediate durable control.
- Rajput policy is not reduced to either marriage alliance or coercion.
- Akbar's expansion is not explained by battlefield victory alone; mobility, logistics, incorporation and repeated reconquest remain necessary.
