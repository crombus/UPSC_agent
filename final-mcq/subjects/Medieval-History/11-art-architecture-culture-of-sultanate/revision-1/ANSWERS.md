# Revision 1 — Answers

### Q1. A
**Source:** Basic owner §10.
- **A:** Trabeate construction uses posts/columns and horizontal lintels or beams.
- **B:** Radiating voussoirs define a true arch.
- **C:** A shell and pendentive description belongs to dome support.
- **D:** Inward-projecting courses describe corbelling.
**Examiner trap:** “Post-and-lintel” is structural vocabulary, not a religious label.

### Q2. B
**Source:** Basic owner §10; Advanced owner §§1, 4.
- **A:** Material alone does not distinguish the systems.
- **B:** Radial wedge action differs from horizontally projecting corbel courses.
- **C:** A true arch is structural rather than merely decorative.
- **D:** True arches commonly require centring during construction.
**Examiner trap:** A pointed outline does not prove radial voussoir construction.

### Q3. C
**Source:** Basic owner §§2, 8.2, 11, 24; Advanced owner §§1, 4.
- **A:** Alai Darwaza is later and is not the early reused prayer screen.
- **B:** Balban's ruin is not a Tughlaq garden tomb.
- **C:** This preserves the source-bounded milestone/maturity distinction.
- **D:** Neither pair is wholly attributable to Iltutmish.
**Examiner trap:** “First surviving marker” and “mature accomplished example” are different claims.

### Q4. D
