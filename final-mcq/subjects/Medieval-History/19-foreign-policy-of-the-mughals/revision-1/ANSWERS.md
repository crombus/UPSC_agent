## Foreign Policy of the Mughals

### Q1. C

- **A:** The topic includes embassies, trade and frontier management, not warfare alone.
- **B:** Mughal missions were episodic; no permanent resident embassy system existed.
- **C:** The label is useful if it does not import modern ministries, borders or nation-state doctrine.
- **D:** Objectives and methods changed across reigns even when some interests endured.
