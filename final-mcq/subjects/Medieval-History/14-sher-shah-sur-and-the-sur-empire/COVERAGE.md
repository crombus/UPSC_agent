## Source-caution decisions preserved

- Sarwani is retrospective and idealizing; praise and dramatic totals are not audited statistics.
- Sher Shah standardized coinage; he did not invent money or the rupee.
- Older roads and `dagh`/`chehra` precedents are retained.
- `Patta`/`qabuliyat` is not British ryotwari; intermediaries remained.
- Annual measurement and the one-third norm are not universalized; Multan and wider implementation controversy are tested.
- Sur territorial terms are not converted into later Mughal subas.
- Rohtas and Rohtasgarh are distinct; Purana Qila is multi-phase.
- Raisen retains its political, religious and violent dimensions.
- Sur collapse is explained contingently, not as inherent Afghan incapacity.
