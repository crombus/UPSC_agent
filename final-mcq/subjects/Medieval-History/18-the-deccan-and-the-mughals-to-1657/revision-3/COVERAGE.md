- the canonical Basic owner identifies 1626 as standard chronology;
- `books/medival_history/Satish Chandra History of Medieval India.pdf`, PDF p. 280, prints 1626.

It explicitly records that:

- `books/medival_history/Medieval-History-Satish-Chandra-1526-1748-Part-2.pdf`, PDF p. 179, prints 1627.

Q20 tests correct source handling. The bank neither silently reproduces 1627 nor obscures the disagreement with an invented range.
