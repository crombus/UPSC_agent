1. **Malik Ambar death:** standard chronology and the shorter Satish Chandra history give **1626**; the assigned Part II OCR narrative prints **1627**. Q20 records the discrepancy without keying the OCR variant as uncontested.
2. **1597 “Sonepat”:** retained only as a Deccan–Berar campaign reference; not identified with Sonipat in Haryana.
3. **Revenue policy:** later Marathi attribution supports measurement and zabti-type reconstruction, not a proven uniform one-third rate or exact Todar Mal copy.
4. **Treaty figures:** rupees and huns remain in their stated source units; OCR rupee conversions are not independently asserted.
5. **Khutba/sikka:** treated as sovereign recognition, not automatic evidence of effective territorial administration.
6. **Jahangir painting:** treated as imperial representation, not literal event reportage.
7. **Dara's role:** no question asserts a proven decisive Dara intervention in the 1656–57 policy reversal.
