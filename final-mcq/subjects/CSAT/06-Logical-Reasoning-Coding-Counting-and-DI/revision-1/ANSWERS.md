**R1-Q01 — C.** I follows by chaining attend -> badge -> locker. III is the contrapositive of that chain (no locker -> no badge -> did not attend). II fails: statement 3 links trainees to lockers, not to badges, so a trainee with a locker and no badge satisfies every statement.

- B: Rejects I although it follows by chaining the first two statements.
