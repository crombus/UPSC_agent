### R1-Q01

Read the three statements and decide which conclusions follow. A conclusion follows only if it is true in every case that satisfies all the statements.

**Statements**
1. Every member who attends the induction is issued a badge.
2. Every member who is issued a badge is given a locker.
3. Some members who have lockers are trainees.

**Conclusions**
I. Every member who attends the induction is given a locker.
II. Some trainees are issued badges.
III. Every member who is not given a locker did not attend the induction.

Which of the conclusions follow?

A. I and II only
B. II and III only
C. I and III only
D. I, II and III
