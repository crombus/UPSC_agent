# CSAT 06 — Revision 3: application and mixed framing

All items are original practice questions, not UPSC PYQs. No UPSC wording or options are reproduced.

**Set size and time:** 62 questions. Budget about 72 seconds per question, about 75 minutes for the set. Each block below carries its own time budget; the eight budgets add to 75 minutes. Items in Block 8 are timed sprints.

**Scoring:** +2.5 for a correct answer, -0.83 for a wrong answer, 0 for a skipped question.

## Block 1 — Statement and syllogism logic (Q01–Q08) — 9 minutes

### R3-Q01

Four officers, Asha, Bilal, Chitra and Dev, are being considered for postings abroad. The table records two facts about each officer.

| Officer | Trained | Cleared |
|---|---|---|
| Asha | Yes | Yes |
| Bilal | No | No |
| Chitra | Yes | Yes |
| Dev | No | Yes |

Rule 1: an officer posted abroad must be trained. Rule 2: an officer who is not cleared cannot be posted abroad. Exactly two officers will be posted abroad, and both rules apply to every posting.

Which pair of officers could be the two posted abroad?

A. Asha and Chitra
B. Asha and Dev
C. Bilal and Chitra
D. Chitra and Dev

### R3-Q02

Premises:
(1) Every auditor is a graduate.
(2) Some graduates are tax consultants.
(3) No tax consultant is a salaried employee.

Which conclusion necessarily follows?

A. Every tax consultant is an auditor.
B. Some graduates are not salaried employees.
C. Some auditors are salaried employees.
D. No graduate is a salaried employee.

### R3-Q03

Premises:
(1) Some reviewers attended the March workshop.
(2) Every person who attended the March workshop passed the test.
(3) No one who passed the test was dismissed.

Which statement necessarily follows?

A. Some reviewers were dismissed after the March workshop.
B. All reviewers in the panel had passed the test.
C. Some reviewers who attended the March workshop were not dismissed.
D. Every person who attended the workshop is a reviewer.

### R3-Q04

Premises:

(2) Some people who passed a medical test are instructors.
(3) No instructor is a licensed pilot.

Which statement must be true?

B. Every instructor has passed a medical test.
C. Not every medical-test passer must be an instructor.
D. Some medical-test passers are not licensed pilots.

### R3-Q05

(i) If the proposal is approved, the committee has met.
(ii) If the committee has met, the minutes are published.
(iii) The minutes were not published.

Which statement must be true?

A. The proposal has not been approved so far.
B. The committee met without approving it.
C. The committee met, and minutes were published.
D. Approval of the proposal remains possible.

### R3-Q06

Statements:
Some pencils are erasers.
All erasers are sharpeners.

I. Some pencils are sharpeners.
II. All sharpeners are erasers.

Which of the following is correct?

A. Neither I nor II follows from the statements.
B. Only conclusion I follows from the statements.
C. Only conclusion II follows from the statements.
D. Both conclusions I and II follow from the statements.

### R3-Q07

Which statement is correct?

### R3-Q08
