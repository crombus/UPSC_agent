# csat-07 — Revision 2: answers and explanations

All items are original judgement items; none is a UPSC PYQ. Keys apply the owner principles in `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/CSAT/basic/07_Interpersonal-and-Communication-Skills.md` and `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/CSAT/advanced/07_Interpersonal-Communication-Negotiation-and-Public-Dealing.md`. Each entry states the principle behind the key, the error pattern in every option, the examiner trap, a shortcut and a time target.

**R2-Q01 — B.**
- **A:** A memorandum through the routine file system is too slow for a danger that must be acted on tonight.
- **B (key):** A call for immediate action, followed by a written note the same night, gives both speed and the record.
- **C:** A broadcast to an unscoped group does not confirm who received the message or who acted.
- **D:** Delays a dangerous report until a meeting ends.
- **Trap:** the record requirement does not decide the channel alone; the urgency sets the first channel.
- **Shortcut:** pair speed for action with writing for proof.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q02 — D.**
- **A:** Treats polite agreement as commitment, which mistakes compliance language for agreement.
- **B:** Forces public repetition, which exposes the subordinate and does not reveal the objection.
- **C:** Assumes disloyalty without evidence.
- **D (key):** Meets privately, paraphrases what the subordinate seems to accept and asks which part will not work and why.
- **Trap:** "yes, sir" is compliance language, not a statement of agreement.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that asks for the objection privately.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q03 — A.**
- **A (key):** Raises the error before issue, cites the clause and figures and proposes the corrected wording.
- **B:** Exposes the senior in a group meeting.
- **C:** Allows a known error to reach an applicant.
- **D:** Is general and does not name the error, so it cannot be acted on.
- **Trap:** a private route is not a route for silence; it must still correct the fact.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that is specific and before issue.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q04 — C.**
- **A:** Physical barrier; nothing in the stem suggests that the setting of the training is at fault.
- **B:** Organisational barrier; a hierarchy chart does not address the belief that the form is unchanged.
- **C (key):** Selective perception; asking officers to paraphrase the rules and check them against the form tests the old assumption.
- **D:** Technological barrier; the content is clear, and a digital form does not correct the wrong assumption.
- **Trap:** a barrier is diagnosed from the symptom and the stated belief, not from the most visible tool.
- **Shortcut:** identify what the people believe before choosing the channel.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q05 — D.**
- **A:** A second channel for every circular adds volume to an overloaded system.
- **B:** Marking everything urgent destroys the signal.
- **C:** Shifts the filing burden to each officer and does not prioritise.
- **D (key):** Sorts by action and recipient, publishes a weekly decision list and reserves urgency for deadlines.
- **Trap:** marking everything urgent destroys the signal.
- **Shortcut:** reject any remedy that adds messages to an overloaded system.
- **Time target:** 45 seconds.

**R2-Q06 — B.**
- **A:** Decides on emotion rather than on the verifiable ground.
- **B (key):** Expresses sympathy, asks for the hospital record and the guideline ground, and ties the outcome to verification.
- **C:** Refuses without any hearing, which is rigid and fails the communication-quality step.
- **D:** Passes responsibility to a sponsor who is not the decision-maker.
- **Trap:** a rule can be applied fairly and still be applied with empathy.
- **Shortcut:** the answer keeps both the rule and the dignity.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q07 — A.**
- **A (key):** Cites the specific order and deadline, uses the accident data and offers phased payments within the rules.
- **B:** Threatens a licence outcome beyond the stated facts.
- **C:** Applies social pressure through an unverified claim about other companies.
- **D:** Promises leniency, which is deception or favouritism.
- **Trap:** phased payment is acceptable only within the rules, so check that the option says so.
- **Shortcut:** reject pressure, threats and promised leniency.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q08 — C.**
- **A:** Keeps a possible public hazard secret because the employee asked for it.
- **B:** Delays the report for a week while the possible contamination continues.
- **C (key):** Protects the source where possible but reports the possible contamination to the competent authority, since safety comes first.
- **D:** Publishes an unverified concern to all staff without the authority that can test it.
- **Trap:** the request for secrecy does not override immediate safety.
- **Shortcut:** if the risk is to others, the option must reach the authority.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q09 — B.**
- **A:** Treats the existing price as the alternative, which is the current position, not a fallback.
- **B (key):** The lawful re-tender, with its estimated cost and timing, gives the body its walk-away point on price.
- **C:** A full cancellation is not the only lawful alternative and is presented as a certainty.
- **D:** Treats the contractor's reservation point as the body's own.
- **Trap:** the reservation point is the boundary beyond which the alternative is better, not the other side's minimum.
- **Shortcut:** identify the lawful alternative, then the walk-away from it.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q10 — D.**
- **A:** Splits a fixed grant, which is distributive and creates no value.
- **B:** Awards everything to one party and ignores the other's need.
- **C:** Divides the difference between stated minimums, which is distributive.
- **D (key):** Explores sharing resources and checks whether a joint bid still meets the criteria, which creates value without changing the rules.
- **Trap:** a fair split is still distributive; value creation needs an interest-based option.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that tests a shared resource without changing the rules.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q11 — C.**
- **A:** A joint meeting under threat turns intimidation into a negotiation.
- **B:** A private meeting without the officer leaves the threatened party exposed.
- **C (key):** Mediation is unsuitable where threats coerce a party, so the formal process applies and the family is protected.
- **D:** Pressures the weaker party to accept less because the other side holds the stronger position.
- **Trap:** a meeting is not a mediation if one party is intimidating the other.
- **Shortcut:** threats cancel the voluntary route.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q12 — A.**
- **A (key):** States the measured fact, the uncertainty and its reason, and a specific action for low-lying residents.
- **B:** Claims certainty without showing the data, which causes unsupported alarm.
- **C:** Offers false reassurance based on history.
- **D:** Withholds information while the risk is rising.
- **Trap:** uncertainty is not a reason to remain silent; it is a reason to say what is uncertain.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that gives measured fact, stated uncertainty and an action.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q13 — D.**
- **A:** A quiet reissue hides the earlier error from those who acted on it.
- **B:** Delays correction until the next scheduled bulletin while the error is live.
- **C:** Corrects one clinic privately and leaves the public uninformed.
- **D (key):** Corrects through the original channels, states the wrong and right figures and tells clinics what to check now.
- **Trap:** a silent correction does not reach the people who acted on the error.
- **Shortcut:** an error that reached the public must be corrected through the same channel.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q14 — B.**
- **A:** Fatigue is not a design fix and will not change the agreement bias.
- **B (key):** Mixes positive and negative items, uses balanced choices and includes a reverse check, which exposes agreement by default.
- **C:** Selective reporting drops the weak agreement and biases the result.
- **D:** Removes the structure and makes the results hard to compare.
- **Trap:** an open-ended format is not a cure for bias.
- **Shortcut:** look for a design fix that changes the wording balance.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q15 — C.**
- **A:** Treats a self-selected 22 per cent as representative.
- **B:** Adds burden and does not address who is missing.
- **C (key):** Checks respondents against non-respondents using records, and follows up a sample of non-respondents before reporting.
- **D:** Replaces the sample with the most aggrieved people, which biases the result in the other direction.
- **Trap:** a low response rate with a skewed profile cannot support a general satisfaction claim.
- **Shortcut:** look for a check of who did not respond.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q16 — A.**
- **A (key):** Independent written views before discussion, and an invitation to object to the leading proposal before a vote.
- **B:** Adopts the first proposal without a test.
- **C:** Makes silent members commit to the first speaker's view.
- **D:** Postpones indefinitely, which is delay without a method.
- **Trap:** quick agreement is not evidence of a sound decision.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that gets independent views first.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q17 — B.**
- **A:** Waits for a formal complaint that the authority gradient is already preventing.
- **B (key):** Creates a confidential risk route, invites dissent in review meetings and protects the technician from retaliation.
- **C:** Gives the inspection to the engineer whose dismissal of concerns is the problem.
- **D:** Routes a safety concern to a contractor with no authority over the department.
- **Trap:** the most senior person is not the most reliable checker of a concern about their own dismissal.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that changes the reporting route and protects the reporter.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q18 — D.**
- **A:** Collects only supportive views, so the belief is confirmed by construction.
- **B:** Relies on experience as proof.
- **C:** Asks the contractor only to confirm the belief.
- **D (key):** Names the evidence that would disprove apathy and checks payment dates against the timeline.
- **Trap:** an explanation that no observation could contradict is not a finding.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that names the disconfirming evidence.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q19 — A.**
- **A (key):** An assisted route with trained staff, a paper checklist and a receipt keeps the service open to people without internet access.
- **B:** Pushes applicants to depend on relatives, which is burdensome and exposes personal data.
- **C:** Restores paper for everyone, which abandons the digital system without need.
- **D:** Assigns a decision that belongs to the scheme to a local body.
- **Trap:** an accessible route is not one that requires someone else to act for you.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that keeps the service open to the person.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q20 — C.**
- **A:** Ignores a group with a direct interest.
- **B:** Relies on a single venue that the households may not reach.
- **C (key):** Consults the association and separately seeks the households' views in their language and place, with information on access effects.
- **D:** Routes consultation through a party that may not represent the households.
- **Trap:** high power does not erase the need to hear the highly affected low-power group.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that consults each group in a way it can use.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q21 — B.**
- **A:** Puts the requirement first and the observation later, so the colleague cannot respond to the specific facts.
- **B (key):** Describes the returned files, states their impact, invites an explanation, sets the signature standard, then agrees an action and review.
- **C:** Opens with reputation and a judgement of carelessness, which ends the conversation before the facts are shared.
- **D:** Omits any agreed action or review.
- **Trap:** the order matters, because an early judgement blocks the other person's explanation.
- **Shortcut:** check whether the observation comes before any judgement, and whether the option ends with an agreed action and review.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q22 — D.**
- **A:** Delays a public-health result for an unrelated motive question.
- **B:** Publishes an unverified result as final.
- **C:** Removes the record of a labelling error by correcting it privately.
- **D (key):** Holds the result, records the concern, re-tests under the authorised procedure and reviews the motive question separately.
- **Trap:** a motive allegation is separate from whether the sample was labelled correctly.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that verifies first and separates the motive question.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q23 — C.**
- **A:** Makes the custom a bar on everyone else and silences younger residents and women.
- **B:** Excludes people through language.
- **C (key):** Lets the elders open, then holds a separate session where women and younger residents can speak, and records all views.
- **D:** Avoids the group on a stereotype.
- **Trap:** respect for elders does not require that others be silenced.
- **Shortcut:** reject any option that excludes a group or relies on a stereotype.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q24 — A.**
- **A (key):** States the rule, offers a visit within two days and asks for the instruction in writing if it stands.
- **B:** Complies and shares blame privately, which is passive.
- **C:** Refuses with a raised voice and a dismissive tone, which is aggressive.
- **D:** Complies silently and leaves the issue unrecorded.
- **Trap:** a refusal can be firm and still respectful.
- **Shortcut:** a strong assertive response contains the boundary and a specific next step.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R2-Q25 — D.**
- **A:** A neutral factual question about travel time.
- **B:** A neutral question about which services were used.
- **C:** A neutral question about friendliness on a scale.
- **D (key):** "Given how poorly the centre has been run" presupposes a conclusion before asking satisfaction.
- **Trap:** a question can be neutral in form and still lead through a premise.
- **Shortcut:** look for an adjective or premise that the respondent did not supply.
- **Time target:** 40 seconds.

**R2-Q26 — B.**
- **A:** Depends on a headman, which biases the answers, and uses closed questions that cannot probe.
- **B (key):** Trained private interviews with open questions and follow-up probes suit low literacy and a sensitive topic.
- **C:** Assumes online access and literacy, which the hamlet may not have.
- **D:** Produces social-desirability pressure in front of others.
- **Trap:** a questionnaire is standardised but cannot probe, and a group setting suppresses sensitive answers.
- **Shortcut:** choose the method that probes privately.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R2-Q27 — C.**
- **A:** Delays protection for consultation, which the owner's advanced rule rejects.
- **B:** Treats consultation as unnecessary, which ignores the community's knowledge afterwards.
- **C (key):** Orders immediate protection for those at risk, then informs and consults the community on next steps and the plan's review.
- **D:** Waits for objections while people remain exposed.
- **Trap:** consultation can be sequenced after protection, not skipped.
- **Shortcut:** reject any option that delays immediate protection for consultation.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q28 — A.**
- **A (key):** Keeps the personal complaint confidential, refuses to suppress the inspection record and reports the instruction to the authority.
- **B:** Hides the failure in a private notebook, which keeps it off the official register.
- **C:** Holds the failure off the register for a week, which hides a public-safety record.
- **D:** Creates an uncontrolled public alarm without verification.
- **Trap:** confidentiality and secrecy are different.
- **Shortcut:** a public-safety record must remain on the official register.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q29 — D.**
- **A:** Reassigns the work without speaking to the member, which is unfair and ignores the cause.
- **B:** Keeps the workload unchanged and adds reminders, which ignores the bereavement.
- **C:** Adds daily written reporting, which increases pressure after a bereavement.
- **D (key):** Speaks privately, expresses concern without pressure, agrees which tasks can shift and reviews the plan with the team.
- **Trap:** monitoring is not support.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that talks to the person before changing the plan.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R2-Q30 — B.**
- **A:** Reads the reason aloud in a public room, which discloses personal case information to others.
- **B (key):** Gives the reason in a private room, in plain language, with a secure written copy.
- **C:** Wrongly states that the rules forbid disclosure to the applicant, who is entitled to the reason for a decision on their own case.
- **D:** Exposes other applicants' data on a public board.
- **Trap:** privacy does not mean withholding the reason from the person it concerns.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that is private and gives the reason.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.
