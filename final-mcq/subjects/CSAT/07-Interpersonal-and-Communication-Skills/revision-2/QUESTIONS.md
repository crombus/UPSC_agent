# csat-07 — Revision 2: Interpersonal and Communication Skills

All scenarios and options are original. Each item presents four courses of action or statements; choose the single most appropriate one for the public servant described. Several items combine two principles, so test each option against every fact given.

Practice budget: 30 questions in 36 minutes (about 72 seconds per question). Practice scoring: +2.5 for a correct answer, −0.83 for a wrong answer, 0 for a skipped question.

### R2-Q01
A flood-control engineer must tell the district magistrate that an embankment shows dangerous seepage. The message is urgent, action must be taken tonight, and a written record will be needed later. Which channel is most appropriate?

A. A written memorandum sent through the routine file system, so that the record is complete before any action is taken on it tonight.
B. A direct telephone call to the magistrate reporting the seepage and the immediate action, followed by a written note the same night.
C. A message posted in the district emergency group chat, so that all officers with flood duties are alerted at the same moment.
D. A detailed email sent to the magistrate's office, to be read when the magistrate has finished the current review meeting.

### R2-Q02
A subordinate repeatedly says "yes, sir" in meetings but then does not implement the decisions. The officer wants to know whether the subordinate actually disagrees. What is the best step?

A. Treat the repeated agreement as a commitment and hold the subordinate to every decision in the next performance review.
B. Ask the subordinate to repeat each decision in front of the group, so that the agreement is visible and any gap can be identified.
C. Conclude that the subordinate is disloyal, and move the subordinate's duties to another officer before the next review.
D. Meet privately, paraphrase what the subordinate seems to accept, and ask which part they think will not work and why.

### R2-Q03
A deputy must tell a senior officer that a draft order the senior has signed contains a factual error that affects eligibility. Which approach is best?

A. Raise it privately before the order is issued, cite the clause and the specific figures, and propose the corrected wording.
B. Mention it at the next full staff meeting, so that the error is discussed openly and the senior cannot place the blame on the deputy alone.
C. Keep quiet, since the senior has more experience, and correct the order later if an applicant complains about it.
D. Send a general note stating that the department should review its drafting standards, without naming the order or the error.

### R2-Q04
After a training session on a new reporting form, most officers agree that the form is clear, yet it continues to be filled in incorrectly. A supervisor finds that officers rarely read the instruction box and assume the new form is the same as the old one. Which barrier is most directly involved, and what remedy fits it?

A. Physical barrier; change the room in which the training was held, so that officers can hear the instructions more clearly.
B. Organisational barrier; issue a new hierarchy chart showing which officer approves each form and who may sign it.
C. Selective perception; ask officers to paraphrase the new rules and check them against the form, testing the old assumption.
D. Technological barrier; replace the paper form with a digital version that blocks incomplete entries and shows the instructions.

### R2-Q05

A. Send every circular to every officer through a second channel, so that a missed notice is at least received twice.
B. Mark all circulars as urgent in one bulk message, so that officers read every item with greater attention and care.
C. Ask each officer to keep a personal file of all circulars, so that no item is lost when the next one arrives.
D. Sort circulars by action and recipient, publish a weekly decision list, and reserve urgency for items with deadlines.

### R2-Q06
An applicant for a scholarship says she missed the income-certificate deadline because her mother was hospitalised, and asks the officer to accept a late certificate. The scheme's guidelines allow late certificates only with a verified ground that they list. What is the best response?

A. Accept the certificate because the hardship is genuine and the officer can see that the applicant is distressed by her situation.
B. Express sympathy, ask for the hospital record and the guideline ground, and say the decision depends on verifying that ground.
C. Refuse the request without discussion, since the scholarship is limited and the rules apply equally to all applicants without exception.
D. Refer the applicant to the scholarship's sponsor, so that a senior officer can decide the case with the sponsor's agreement.

### R2-Q07

A. Explain the specific order and its deadline, show the accident data for the route, and discuss phased payments within the rules.

### R2-Q08
An employee tells a colleague that a storage tank at the plant is leaking into a stream and asks her to keep it secret. The colleague believes the employee may be exaggerating. What should the colleague do?

A. Keep the confidence entirely, because the employee asked for it and the colleague cannot be sure that the reported leak is real.
B. Discuss the matter only with the employee, and ask the employee to correct the storage problem and confirm the repair within one week.
C. Keep the source private where possible, but report the possible contamination to the competent authority, since safety comes first.
D. Post the concern on the plant's internal noticeboard, so that all employees can take precautions until the leak is checked.

### R2-Q09
A municipal body negotiates with a contractor over a delayed road project. The contractor wants a price increase. The body has a lawful option to re-tender the remaining work. Which option should the negotiator treat as the alternative that sets its walk-away point?

A. The contractor's current price, since the contract already exists and any change to it would count as a concession by the body.
B. Re-tendering the remaining work lawfully, with its estimated cost and timing, which gives the body its walk-away point on price.
C. A full cancellation of the road project, which is the only way the body can be certain of avoiding any price increase at all.
D. The contractor's stated minimum price, since a rational negotiator should accept the rival's reservation point as its own.

### R2-Q10
Two NGOs apply for the same district grant for different activities. One needs the money for a training centre; the other needs a monitoring team that the first could also use. Which approach creates the most value without changing the grant rules?

A. Split the grant equally between the two NGOs, since equal shares are the most objective and the least likely to be challenged.
B. Award the entire grant to the NGO with the stronger application, since the rules prefer the highest-scored proposal in every case.
C. Ask each NGO to state its lowest acceptable amount, and then divide the difference between those two amounts equally.
D. Explore sharing the monitoring team and training centre resources, and check whether a joint bid still meets the grant criteria.

### R2-Q11
A tenant and a landlord dispute a deposit. The landlord has threatened the tenant's family and refuses to attend a joint meeting unless the tenant withdraws a complaint to the police. Which is the most appropriate step?

A. Arrange a mediated meeting at the district office, with both parties seated together, so the deposit can be settled quickly and cheaply.
B. Ask both parties to meet privately, without the officer, so that they can agree on a deposit figure that each finds acceptable.
C. Decline voluntary mediation because of the threat and coercion, and proceed under the formal process while protecting the family.
D. Ask the tenant to accept a smaller refund to avoid further conflict, since the landlord holds the stronger position here.

### R2-Q12

A. State the gauge reading, say the forecast is uncertain and why, and list the actions residents in low-lying areas should take now.

C. Say that there is no reason to worry, because levels have historically risen and fallen in the area without causing damage.

### R2-Q13

B. Wait for the next scheduled bulletin to correct the figure, so that all corrections are issued together in a single notice.
C. Tell the clinic privately to check its storage and say nothing publicly, to avoid undermining public trust in the department.

### R2-Q14
A satisfaction survey uses only positively worded agree-or-disagree statements, and the results show that almost every respondent agrees with almost every statement. What is the best design correction?

A. Add a second page of agree-or-disagree statements, so that respondents who agree become tired and stop agreeing.
B. Mix positively and negatively worded statements, use balanced response choices, and include a reverse-check item.
C. Ask respondents to rate only the statement they feel strongly about, so that weak agreement is dropped from the results.
D. Remove all statements and ask respondents to write their own opinion, so that no response is influenced by the wording.

### R2-Q15

C. Compare respondents with non-respondents using records such as processing time, and follow up a sample of non-respondents.

### R2-Q16
