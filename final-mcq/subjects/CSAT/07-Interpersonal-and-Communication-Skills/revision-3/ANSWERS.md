# csat-07 — Revision 3: answers and explanations

All items are original judgement items; none is a UPSC PYQ. Keys apply the owner principles in `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/CSAT/basic/07_Interpersonal-and-Communication-Skills.md` and `upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/CSAT/advanced/07_Interpersonal-Communication-Negotiation-and-Public-Dealing.md`. Each entry states the principle behind the key, the error pattern in every option, the examiner trap, a shortcut and a time target.

**R3-Q01 — D.**
- **A:** Apologises in front of the class before the facts are established, which exposes the pupil.
- **B:** Favours the teacher on status, before any evidence.
- **C:** Suspends the teacher before any finding.
- **D (key):** Records both accounts separately, checks whether others heard the remark and applies the complaint procedure.
- **Trap:** a parent's credibility is not established by the teacher's status.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that verifies and uses the procedure.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q02 — C.**
- **A:** Calls the reply a legitimate boundary, but it gives no explanation and no route for the clerk to learn.
- **B:** Names a physical barrier that the stem does not describe, and the reply made it no quieter.
- **C (key):** A psychological barrier, because the reply signals impatience and makes the clerk reluctant to ask again.
- **D:** Relabels a dismissive reply as a recognised test, which the owner does not support.
- **Trap:** a rebuke is a communication event; it changes whether the listener will ask again.
- **Shortcut:** identify what the listener will do next after the reply.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q03 — B.**
- **A:** Gives the lead role by size, which is not a published criterion.
- **B (key):** Applies the published criteria, shares the scores and gives each NGO a defined role.
- **C:** Splits the lead role without a rule and creates joint control, which the owner warns against.
- **D:** Delegates the decision to the parties themselves.
- **Trap:** equal treatment requires a transparent criterion, not a compromise between the parties.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that uses the published criteria.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q04 — A.**
- **A (key):** Fixes the period and the frequency categories, so the question has one meaning for every respondent.
- **B:** A "not sure" option does not define the period, so the ambiguity remains.
- **C:** "A lot" is undefined, so the revised question is still ambiguous and asks each respondent to judge usage on a private scale.
- **D:** Changes the construct to attitude, which the survey did not ask about.
- **Trap:** a question can look improved by shortening while its undefined term remains.
- **Shortcut:** check whether every term that a respondent must interpret has a fixed scope.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q05 — C.**
- **A:** Repeats the rumour's claims and so amplifies them.
- **B:** Waits for publication and loses the chance to correct early.
- **C (key):** States what is verified and undecided, names the official source and gives the next update date.
- **D:** Uses a legal threat in place of a factual response.
- **Trap:** a denial without detail, or a threat, does not correct a rumour.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that is factual, dated and source-named.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q06 — D.**
- **A:** Micromanages every draft and creates a bottleneck.
- **B:** Gives no oversight until the end, which risks the deadline.
- **C:** Asks for daily status without clear outcomes, so the status reports measure activity rather than results.
- **D (key):** Sets outcomes and owners, agrees check-in points and leaves methods to the team.
- **Trap:** monitoring is not the same as checking every draft.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option with outcome, owner and check-in.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q07 — A.**
- **A (key):** Acknowledges the stress, restates the concern in the citizen's terms and asks which document was returned.
- **B:** Blames the citizen and shifts responsibility to the portal.
- **C:** Makes the citizen's calm a precondition for discussion.
- **D:** Sets an unjustified condition (reading a leaflet) before any discussion.
- **Trap:** a fact-based reply that ignores emotion is still a poor first response.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that acknowledges, restates and asks.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q08 — B.**
- **A:** Threatens counter-action and defamation, which escalates the conflict.
- **B (key):** Keeps to the facts, explains the lawful review process and its timeline and invites a written review request.
- **C:** Reverses a rule-based decision under threat, which the owner's rule against threats and unsafe bargaining rejects.
- **D:** Accepts a failure that the facts do not establish.
- **Trap:** a threat to post is not a reason to change a decision.
- **Shortcut:** a decision made under the rules is changed only through the review route.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q09 — C.**
- **A:** Generic praise to the whole team.
- **B:** Praise mixed with an unrelated criticism that dilutes the feedback.
- **C (key):** Names the method and its effect, and asks the person what made it work.
- **D:** Flattery that gives all credit to one person and ignores the team.
- **Trap:** a sandwich of praise and criticism is not automatically effective feedback.
- **Shortcut:** look for a named behaviour and a question.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q10 — A.**
- **A (key):** Replaces the legal terms with plain words, adds a worked example and a contact, and checks comprehension.
- **B:** Adds legal citations, which deepen the barrier.
- **C:** Reads the same text aloud, so the barrier remains.
- **D:** Records a signature, which does not show understanding.
- **Trap:** a signed statement is a record, not comprehension.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that changes the wording and checks understanding.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q11 — D.**
- **A:** Imposes a rota without the facts, so the dispute is not resolved.
- **B:** Settles by seniority, which is positional.
- **C:** Ends the working relationship without evidence.
- **D (key):** Sets ground rules, hears each account separately, identifies the needs and records a schedule with a review date.
- **Trap:** a rota can be a fair result, but only after the facts have been heard.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that asks for both accounts before a rule.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q12 — B.**
- **A:** Accepts a first demand without testing the lawful alternative.
- **B (key):** Sets the limit at the second quote's price and delivery package and trades delivery timing for price where the supplier gains.
- **C:** Ignores the available alternative.
- **D:** Refuses to engage and uses a budget figure that the facts do not connect to the market.
- **Trap:** a higher quote is not a worse package if faster delivery is part of the value.
- **Shortcut:** identify the lawful alternative first, then test the trades.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R3-Q13 — C.**
- **A:** Reveals the identity to the accused, which exposes the complainant.
- **B:** Announces the complaint and name in a staff meeting.
- **C (key):** Shares the identity only with the investigating authority, on a need-to-know basis.
- **D:** Withholds the identity from the investigators, who cannot test the allegation.
- **Trap:** confidentiality is not secrecy from those who must investigate.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that limits disclosure to the investigating authority.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q14 — A.**
- **A (key):** States that backdating is not permitted, explains the record concern, proposes a lawful late-approval note and asks for it in writing.
- **B:** Complies with a backdated record and complains only privately, which is passive.
- **C:** Refuses loudly and calls the request corrupt in front of staff, which is aggressive.
- **D:** Says nothing and hides the matter from the senior officer, which is passive.
- **Trap:** a firm refusal can still be respectful, and the lawful alternative is what keeps the working relationship.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option with the boundary and the lawful alternative.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q15 — D.**
- **A:** Apologises for a decision that followed the rules, which is inaccurate and undermines the process.
- **B:** Ignores the request.
- **C:** Avoids the citizen, which leaves the matter unresolved.
- **D (key):** Apologises for the raised voice, restates the decision with its reasons and explains the appeal route.
- **Trap:** an apology for tone is not an apology for the decision.
- **Shortcut:** separate the conduct from the decision.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q16 — B.**
- **A:** Makes the applicant depend on a companion.
- **B (key):** Provides the notice in an accessible format, reads its key points aloud and checks understanding.
- **C:** Moves the same printed text, which remains inaccessible without a magnifier.
- **D:** Routes the applicant to a helpline that does not give the notice itself.
- **Trap:** moving the same inaccessible text does not remove the barrier.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that changes the format.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q17 — C.**
- **A:** Replies after ninety days, which gives the acknowledgement no value.
- **B:** Leaves the citizen with no timeline.
- **C (key):** Acknowledges in writing with a reference number, a timeline and a named contact.
- **D:** Leaves no written record.
- **Trap:** a verbal acknowledgement is not a record.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option with reference, timeline and contact.
- **Time target:** 45 seconds.

**R3-Q18 — A.**
- **A (key):** Collects attendance by household and transport distance and checks whether absences rise on bus-breakdown days, which could disconfirm the indifference explanation.
- **B:** Relies on two visits and experience, which the owner's test does not accept.
- **C:** Treats two visits as an established cause.
- **D:** Collects photographs of absent parents, which only documents the belief.
- **Trap:** a few observed absences do not establish a cause.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that could show the belief to be wrong.
- **Time target:** 60 seconds.

**R3-Q19 — B.**
- **A:** Concludes hostility from a posture and ends the meeting.
- **B (key):** Weighs the context, notes the mismatch between words and posture and clarifies verbally.
- **C:** Dismisses non-verbal cues altogether.
- **D:** Treats the posture as evidence of concealment.
- **Trap:** non-verbal cues are contextual, not decisive.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that checks the context and clarifies verbally.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q20 — D.**
- **A:** Acts on an unverified informal account.
- **B:** Spreads the unverified news to colleagues.
- **C:** Waits without checking the official source.
- **D (key):** Confirms the change through the official channel and records the source.
- **Trap:** an informal account can be accurate and still not be an official decision.
- **Shortcut:** confirm through the official channel before acting.
- **Time target:** 45 seconds.

**R3-Q21 — C.**
- **A:** Sends the full file through an unsecured messaging app.
- **B:** Publishes personal details on a public board.
- **C (key):** Shares the minimum needed through the official secure system, with the urgency stated.
- **D:** Leaves the full file on a desk where others can see it.
- **Trap:** urgency does not justify an unsecured or excessive transfer.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that shares the minimum through an approved channel.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q22 — B.**
- **A:** Varies the questions, which makes candidates impossible to compare fairly.
- **B (key):** Asks the same core questions, probes with consistent follow-ups and scores against the same criteria.
- **C:** Asks about family arrangements, which are irrelevant and may be discriminatory.
- **D:** Forms an impression first and then seeks confirmation.
- **Trap:** consistency is not rigid scripting; the probes must be consistent across candidates.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that is consistent across candidates.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q23 — A.**
- **A (key):** Checks whether the rule permits the skip, since unanimous agreement cannot change a requirement that the rule sets.
- **B:** Treats unanimity and the minute as authority.
- **C:** Defers the rule to a future panel.
- **D:** Formalises the skip without checking the rule.
- **Trap:** unanimous agreement is not a legal basis for skipping a requirement.
- **Shortcut:** check the rule before the agreement.
- **Time target:** 45 seconds.

**R3-Q24 — D.**
- **A:** Escalates an ordinary dispute at once to the district head.
- **B:** Leaves the quarrel alone indefinitely, which lets an ordinary conflict harden.
- **C:** Ignores the quarrel and gives no structure.
- **D (key):** Starts with a facts-based conversation and escalates only if that fails or safety requires it.
- **Trap:** escalation is necessary in some cases but is not the first move in an ordinary staff dispute.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that starts with a facts-based conversation.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q25 — A.**
- **A (key):** Sets ground rules, lets each society speak without interruption and identifies shared facts and underlying interests.
- **B:** Proposes an equal split before the interests are known.
- **C:** Decides alone, which removes the mediator's role.
- **D:** Uses public pressure, which breaks the neutral process.
- **Trap:** an equal split is a position, not a solution derived from interests.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that starts with the process.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.

**R3-Q26 — C.**
- **A:** Keeps the instruction verbal, which leaves no record of a change to a file.
- **B:** Records every conversation in full without a method.
- **C (key):** Confirms the verbal instruction in writing when it changes a file, creating a record.
- **D:** Destroys personal notes, which removes the trace of the instruction.
- **Trap:** a written record does not need to be lengthy; it needs to exist for a change that matters.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that creates a record of a change.
- **Time target:** 45 seconds.

**R3-Q27 — B.**
- **A:** Consults only the best-organised economic group.
- **B (key):** Invites the daily-wage workers through accessible settings and times, and records their concerns.
- **C:** Holds one meeting in the capital, which excludes the people affected.
- **D:** Relies on a summary prepared by an interested party.
- **Trap:** the most organised group is not a representative sample of the affected public.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that consults the group that cannot attend.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q28 — D.**
- **A:** Keeps quiet about a broken promise.
- **B:** Delegates the response to the team without addressing the commitment.
- **C:** Blames the data source without the facts.
- **D (key):** Shares the report on the promised date, or explains the delay now and gives a firm new date.
- **Trap:** silence about a delay is not a promise kept.
- **Shortcut:** choose the option that corrects the commitment openly.
- **Time target:** 45 seconds.

**R3-Q29 — A.**
- **A (key):** Refuses to suspend a statutory safety requirement and offers a written review of implementation with the union.
- **B:** Suspends the rule for half the month, which still waives a statutory requirement.
- **C:** Suspends it for the full month, which waives it entirely.
- **D:** Puts a statutory requirement to a members' vote, which cannot waive it.
- **Trap:** a compromise is not automatically correct, and an unlawful term cannot be split.
- **Shortcut:** reject any option that suspends a statutory requirement.
- **Time target:** 55 seconds.

**R3-Q30 — C.**
- **A:** Chooses a positive frame to influence behaviour, which uses framing against informed choice.
- **B:** Accurate but one-sided; a single frame still steers the reader, and the owner's remedy is parallel wording.
- **C (key):** Neutral parallel wording presents detection and miss rates on the same basis, which removes the framing effect.
- **D:** Selects a frame by its persuasive effect, which is manipulation rather than informed communication.
- **Trap:** both drafts are accurate; accuracy does not neutralise framing.
- **Shortcut:** if the option keeps one frame only, it is still framed.
- **Time target:** 50 seconds.
