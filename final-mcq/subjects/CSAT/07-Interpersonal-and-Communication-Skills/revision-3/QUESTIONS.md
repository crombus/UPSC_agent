# csat-07 — Revision 3: Interpersonal and Communication Skills

All scenarios and options are original. The settings vary across schools, offices, field work, consultations and counters. Each item presents four courses of action or statements; choose the single most appropriate one. Use only the facts given in the item.

Practice budget: 30 questions in 36 minutes (about 72 seconds per question). Practice scoring: +2.5 for a correct answer, −0.83 for a wrong answer, 0 for a skipped question.

### R3-Q01
A school principal receives a parent's complaint that a teacher used humiliating language about a pupil in class. The teacher says the parent misunderstood a joke. What should the principal do first?

A. Ask the teacher to apologise to the class in front of the children, so that the matter is settled openly and quickly.
B. Tell the parent that the teacher's account is more credible, since teachers are trained to handle classroom remarks.
C. Suspend the teacher from classes until the complaint is resolved, so that no further remarks of that kind can be made.
D. Record both accounts separately, check whether other pupils heard the remark, and then apply the school's complaint procedure.

### R3-Q02

A. A legitimate boundary, since the clerk should have read the manual before asking the question, and repeated questions waste time.
B. A physical barrier, since the room was too noisy for the clerk to hear the explanation clearly, and the reply made that worse.
C. A psychological barrier, because the reply signals impatience and is likely to make the clerk reluctant to ask again.
D. A test of the clerk's knowledge through the authority gradient, which is a recognised method of checking competence.

### R3-Q03

B. Run the selection by the published criteria, share the scores with both NGOs, and offer each a defined support role.
C. Split the lead role equally between the two NGOs, with joint signatures required on every decision taken under the programme.

### R3-Q04
A customer survey asks: "How often have you used the new online system recently?" Which revision best removes the defect in this question?

A. Replace "recently" with a stated period, such as the last four weeks, and give bands such as never, one to three times, and four or more times.
B. Keep the wording and add a "not sure" option, so that respondents who cannot recall the period can still answer the question.
C. Replace it with "Do you use the new online system a lot?", so that respondents rate their use on a scale of their own choosing.
D. Replace it with "Do you like the new online system?", so that the question is shorter and respondents can answer it quickly.

### R3-Q05

B. Wait until the reporter publishes the story, and then respond to the specific article once it has appeared in print.
C. State what has been verified and what is undecided, name the official source for updates, and give the date of the next update.
D. Warn the originator of the rumour that legal action will follow, and say nothing further until the matter has been fully investigated.

### R3-Q06

### R3-Q10

### R3-Q11

### R3-Q12
