**R2-Q01 — C.** The passage says most new e-book borrowers had not used the library before, which supports the claim that digital borrowing draws in new readers.

- **A. Total loans rose because most e-book borrowers were already regular print borrowers.** — Contradicted: the passage says most new e-book borrowers had not used the library before.
- **B. Digital licences are the cheaper option per loan once the full life of a printed copy is counted.** — Contradicted: a digital licence costs about four times as much per loan, even after the printed copy's many circulations.
- **D. Printed collections will become redundant in this library within a few years, as the trend suggests.** — Over-reach: the passage says the redundancy conclusion depends on whether digital borrowing replaces or adds to print.
- **Trap:** Treating a manager's conclusion as established by the data, or reading a per-copy life as a per-loan cost.
- **Shortcut:** Check each option against the two facts that matter: who the new borrowers were and the cost per loan.
- **Time target:** 75 seconds
