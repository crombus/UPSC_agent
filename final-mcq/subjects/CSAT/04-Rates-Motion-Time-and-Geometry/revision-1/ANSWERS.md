# CSAT 04 — Revision 1: answers and explanations

Each entry gives the key, the reason, why each other option fails, the examiner trap, a shortcut and a time target. Every key was verified by computation; see `AUDIT.md`.

**R1-Q01 — B.**

- **A.** Gives the speed in metres per second (1500 ÷ 40 = 37.5 m/s), left unconverted; the km/h conversion needs ×18/5.
- **B.** Correct. 1500 m ÷ 40 s = 37.5 m/s; × 18/5 = 135 km/h.
- **C.** Results from a digit slip, using 1.2 km for 1.5 km (1200 ÷ 40 = 30 m/s, × 3.6 = 108).
- **D.** Divides by 36 s instead of 40 s (a transcription slip); 1500 ÷ 36 × 3.6 = 150.

Trap: Leaving the answer in m/s, or using the wrong conversion factor (÷ 3.6 instead of × 3.6).

Shortcut: m/s × 3.6 = km/h. 37.5 × 3.6 = 135 in one step.

Time target: 60 s.

**R1-Q02 — D.**

- **A.** Plain arithmetic mean (20 + 30) ÷ 2. Valid only when the two legs take equal time, which they do not here.
- **B.** Time-weighted error: weights each speed by the other leg's time, giving (20 × 2/3 + 30 × 1) ÷ 5/3 = 26.
- **C.** Reports the speed of the final leg only, which is not an average over the journey.
- **D.** Correct. Total distance 40 km ÷ total time (1 h + 2/3 h) = 24 km/h; equal legs make this the harmonic mean 2 × 20 × 30 ÷ 50 = 24.

Trap: Averaging the two speeds arithmetically. The plain mean is right only for equal times.

Shortcut: Equal distances: average = 2·s1·s2/(s1+s2) = 2·600/50 = 24.

Time target: 75 s.

**R1-Q03 — C.**

- **C.** Correct. Equal times (2 h each) make the plain mean valid: distance 60 km ÷ 4 h = 15 km/h.
- **D.** Reports the slower leg speed, ignoring the faster second leg.

Shortcut: Equal times: arithmetic mean. Equal distances: harmonic mean. Check which is equal first.
