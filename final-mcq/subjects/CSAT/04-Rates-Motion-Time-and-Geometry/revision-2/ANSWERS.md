**R2-Q01 — C.**

- **A.** Adds the two speeds, as for opposite directions (108 km/h = 30 m/s, 300 ÷ 30 = 10 s).
- **B.** Uses only Y's length (120 ÷ 10 = 12 s), ignoring X's own length.
- **C.** Correct. Same direction: relative speed 36 km/h = 10 m/s; the total to cover is 180 + 120 = 300 m; 300 ÷ 10 = 30 s.
- **D.** Uses only X's length (180 ÷ 10 = 18 s), ignoring the length of Y.

Trap: Adding speeds for same-direction motion, or counting only one of the two lengths.

Shortcut: Same direction: subtract speeds; total distance is both lengths.

**R2-Q02 — D.**

- **A.** The difference of the two speeds (9 − 6 = 3), without halving it.
- **B.** Half the downstream speed (9 ÷ 2), which is not a relation between the two legs.
- **C.** The still-water speed b = (9 + 6) ÷ 2 = 7.5 km/h, not the stream speed.
- **D.** Correct. Downstream speed 36 ÷ 4 = 9 = b + s; upstream speed 36 ÷ 6 = 6 = b − s; so s = (9 − 6) ÷ 2 = 1.5 km/h.

Trap: Stopping at b + s − (b − s), or confusing still-water speed with stream speed.

Shortcut: s = (downstream − upstream)/2.
