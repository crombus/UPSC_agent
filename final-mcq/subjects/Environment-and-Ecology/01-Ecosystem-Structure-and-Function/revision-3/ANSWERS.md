1. **A.** A: correct: the practical system boundary can be drawn for analysis while water and nutrients cross it (L session 1; B§2). B: closed nutrient storage is not a requirement. 3. **C.** A: scavenging redistributes organic matter already made. B: mineral release during decay does not itself show new fixation. 4. **D.** A: secondary applies to the grazer meal, not algae; fish is no producer. B: fish never becomes a producer on the algae meal. C: neither trophic label matches the relevant meals. 5. **D.** A: litter mass/moisture can affect decomposition but neither alone separates the physical and chemical steps. B: microbial abundance/respiration omit the earthworms' particle-size effect. 6. **D.** A: phosphate can cycle but cannot supply the radiant energy of photosynthesis. B: consumer metabolic heat dissipates; soil roots do not return it as usable light. C: decomposer respiration releases energy rather than supplying radiant energy.
