# Polity Final-MCQ Compliance Instructions

These instructions must be read immediately before and after every Polity topic is built or repaired.

## Non-negotiable requirements

1. Process topics strictly in catalogue order. Do not skip, merge, compress, or work ahead.
2. Each topic must contain `revision-1`, `revision-2`, and `revision-3`.
3. Each revision must independently cover the complete topic scope. A declaration-only ledger is insufficient.
4. Each revision must include:
   - `QUESTIONS.md` with answer-free questions and options.
   - `ANSWERS.md` with the key, evidence, four option-specific rationales, and traps.
   - `COVERAGE.md` with auditable question-level coverage mapping.
5. Questions must be original unless exact PYQ provenance, official key, and reproduction rights are verified. Otherwise record an explicit hold.
6. No answer may be identifiable from option length, grammar, specificity, formatting, wording, or appended cues.
7. `QUESTIONS.md` must contain no answer keys, rationale markers, source cues, checkmarks, crosses, or explanatory suffixes.
8. Options must be distinct, plausible, and reasonably balanced in length and structure.
9. Do not repeat generic stems or near-duplicate questions within or across revisions.
10. Do not hallucinate facts, dates, cases, articles, statistics, sources, or PYQ status.
11. Run structural and hostile audits after each topic, repair every actionable finding, and only then update the index.
12. Read this file again after completing each topic before beginning the next topic.

## Required release checks

- All three revision folders and required files exist.
- Every question maps to a coverage unit.
- Every answer maps to exactly one question.
- All four options exist and are distinct.
- No answer leakage appears in question files.
- No repeated or near-duplicate stems are actionable.
- Keyed-option length and wording do not create a reliable cue.
- Evidence and rationales are present and option-specific.
- Topic boundaries are respected.
- PYQ provenance, official key, and rights status are explicit.
- The subject pipeline passes with no pending-topic error.
