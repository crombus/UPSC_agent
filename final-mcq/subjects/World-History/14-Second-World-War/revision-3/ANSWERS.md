# Second World War — Revision Bank 3 — Answers and Rationales

> Separate explained key. Every option is analysed; evidence anchors are repository sources, not reproduced protected PYQ text.

### Q1. Consider the proposition about Two regional wars fused in 1941. Which option is correct?
**Correct answer: B.**
**Option A — incorrect:** This option describes **Occupation brutality**, not the asked point. Lowe links racial domination, forced labour, mistreatment of prisoners and harsh rule in Asia to the growth of resistance and the loss of potential allies.
**Correct-option rationale (B):** The Asian war predates the European outbreak; the conflicts fused in 1941 when Germany attacked the USSR and Japan attacked Pearl Harbor, followed by Hitler's declaration against the USA.
**Option C — incorrect:** This option describes **El Alamein and Suez**, not the asked point. Allied reinforcements, air power and attacks on Axis supply shipping created shortages; the victory prevented an Axis link between North Africa and the Middle East.
**Option D — incorrect:** This option describes **Total war and occupied societies**, not the asked point. The social history of the war includes state control, displacement, forced labour and occupied-society choices, not just battlefield movement.
**Evidence:** ✅ Basic §10.4; Complete package §10.4; Lowe OCR pp. 89, 95-98 (lines 6810-9151). The option is original synthesis; no exact UPSC wording or options are reproduced.
**Examiner trap:** Do not describe the Second World War as a Europe-only war beginning only in September 1939.
**Coverage:** Two regional wars fused in 1941

### Q2. Which statement about Poland and Blitzkrieg would survive a hostile fact-check?
**Correct answer: A.**
**Correct-option rationale (A):** Lowe describes Poland's rapid defeat through coordinated motorised thrusts, tanks and air support; Soviet entry in eastern Poland completed the collapse and occupation.
**Option B — incorrect:** This option describes **Postwar order and decolonisation**, not the asked point. The aftermath was structural rather than a simple peace settlement: Europe was weakened, empire delegitimised and new institutions and bipolar power relations emerged.
**Option C — incorrect:** This option describes **Midway and carrier air power**, not the asked point. Lowe identifies Midway as a crucial turning point; code-breaking, Japanese force-splitting and vulnerability during rearming helped the smaller American carrier force prevail.
**Option D — incorrect:** This option describes **Stalingrad and Soviet advance**, not the asked point. Stalingrad protected the Caucasus oil route and prevented the German southern strategy from succeeding; the defeat transformed morale and initiative on the eastern front.
**Evidence:** ✅ Basic §§1,3; Complete package §10.4; Lowe OCR pp. 89-90 (lines 6810-9151). The option is original synthesis; no exact UPSC wording or options are reproduced.
**Examiner trap:** Blitzkrieg is a combined operational method, not simply a synonym for tanks or for cavalry warfare.
**Coverage:** Poland and Blitzkrieg

### Q5. Consider the proposition about Battle of Britain. Which option is correct?

**Coverage:** Battle of Britain
