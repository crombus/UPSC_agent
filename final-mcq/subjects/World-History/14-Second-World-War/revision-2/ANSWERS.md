# Second World War — Revision Bank 2 — Answers and Rationales

> Separate explained key. Every option is analysed; evidence anchors are repository sources, not reproduced protected PYQ text.

### Q1. A historian is testing the following claim about Two regional wars fused in 1941. Which option supports it?
**Correct answer: D.**
**Option A — incorrect:** This option describes **Pearl Harbor and US entry**, not the asked point. The attack opened American participation and Japan's rapid expansion, while Hitler's declaration gave Roosevelt an easier path to a European war declaration.
**Option B — incorrect:** This option describes **Poland and Blitzkrieg**, not the asked point. Lowe describes Poland's rapid defeat through coordinated motorised thrusts, tanks and air support; Soviet entry in eastern Poland completed the collapse and occupation.
**Option C — incorrect:** This option describes **Japan's contested surrender**, not the asked point. Lowe foregrounds the bombings but records the controversy over peace feelers and Soviet intervention, making a single-cause answer analytically weak.
**Correct-option rationale (D):** The Asian war predates the European outbreak; the conflicts fused in 1941 when Germany attacked the USSR and Japan attacked Pearl Harbor, followed by Hitler's declaration against the USA.
**Evidence:** ✅ Basic §10.4; Complete package §10.4; Lowe OCR pp. 89, 95-98 (lines 6810-9151). The option is original synthesis; no exact UPSC wording or options are reproduced.
**Examiner trap:** Do not describe the Second World War as a Europe-only war beginning only in September 1939.
**Coverage:** Two regional wars fused in 1941

**Coverage:** Poland and Blitzkrieg
