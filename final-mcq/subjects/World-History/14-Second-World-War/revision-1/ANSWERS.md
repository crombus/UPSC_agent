# Second World War — Revision Bank 1 — Answers and Rationales

> Separate explained key. Every option is analysed; evidence anchors are repository sources, not reproduced protected PYQ text.

### Q1. Which statement best explains Two regional wars fused in 1941 in the Second World War?
**Correct answer: C.**
**Option A — incorrect:** This option describes **Battle of Britain**, not the asked point. The Battle of Britain was the first major check to German expansion. The London shift relieved pressure on RAF airfields at a critical moment, while radar and range constraints aided Britain.
**Option B — incorrect:** This option describes **Battle of the Atlantic**, not the asked point. The Atlantic was a strategic lifeline: by mid-1943 Allied ship production and anti-submarine measures brought the struggle under control.
**Correct-option rationale (C):** The Asian war predates the European outbreak; the conflicts fused in 1941 when Germany attacked the USSR and Japan attacked Pearl Harbor, followed by Hitler's declaration against the USA.
**Option D — incorrect:** This option describes **Why the Axis lost**, not the asked point. Lowe's explanation is cumulative: early operational success could not sustain a prolonged multi-front war against a larger coalition.
**Evidence:** ✅ Basic §10.4; Complete package §10.4; Lowe OCR pp. 89, 95-98 (lines 6810-9151). The option is original synthesis; no exact UPSC wording or options are reproduced.
**Examiner trap:** Do not describe the Second World War as a Europe-only war beginning only in September 1939.
**Coverage:** Two regional wars fused in 1941

### Q2. Which option correctly identifies Poland and Blitzkrieg as a source-grounded proposition?
**Correct answer: B.**
**Option A — incorrect:** This option describes **Air power and strategic bombing**, not the asked point. Lowe's modified verdict is that early bombing wasted effort, while improved accuracy and attacks on synthetic oil, transport and armaments became materially decisive by 1944-45.
**Correct-option rationale (B):** Lowe describes Poland's rapid defeat through coordinated motorised thrusts, tanks and air support; Soviet entry in eastern Poland completed the collapse and occupation.
**Option C — incorrect:** This option describes **Greece and the Barbarossa delay**, not the asked point. Lowe records the delay argument but presents it as a possibility, not a proven certainty that Moscow would otherwise have fallen.
**Option D — incorrect:** This option describes **Holocaust scope and bounded figure**, not the asked point. The Holocaust was a state project pursued through occupation and wartime machinery, but its Jewish estimate must not be merged with all Nazi victims.
**Evidence:** ✅ Basic §§1,3; Complete package §10.4; Lowe OCR pp. 89-90 (lines 6810-9151). The option is original synthesis; no exact UPSC wording or options are reproduced.
**Examiner trap:** Blitzkrieg is a combined operational method, not simply a synonym for tanks or for cavalry warfare.
**Coverage:** Poland and Blitzkrieg

### Q3. Which inference is most defensible about Phoney war and Norway for UPSC revision?
**Correct answer: A.**
**Correct-option rationale (A):** Norway offered access to iron ore through Narvik and fjords useful for naval operations. The campaign also exposed Allied weaknesses and contributed to Chamberlain's fall.
**Option B — incorrect:** This option describes **Intentionalist-functionalist debate**, not the asked point. Lowe presents both approaches and records the argument that anti-Jewish ideology supplied the core while conquest, occupation and wartime opportunity enabled escalation.
**Option C — incorrect:** This option describes **Barbarossa and overreach**, not the asked point. The eastern invasion was ideologically central to Nazism but also strategically escalatory. Weather, inadequate winter preparation and extended supply lines blocked a quick victory.
**Option D — incorrect:** This option describes **Italy and the route to D-Day**, not the asked point. Italy was not the final theatre, but its elimination contributed to Allied victory by opening bases and diverting German forces before the western second front.
**Evidence:** ✅ Basic §§2,10.4; Complete package §10.4 and §10.6; Lowe OCR pp. 89-90, 92 (lines 6810-9151). The option is original synthesis; no exact UPSC wording or options are reproduced.
**Examiner trap:** Do not treat the phoney war as a separate peace settlement or confuse Quisling with a resistance leader.
**Coverage:** Phoney war and Norway

**Coverage:** France and Dunkirk
