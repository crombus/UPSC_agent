**Corrections and qualifications:** Congress Poland was a separately administered kingdom under the Russian emperor, not Russia's annexation of every Polish land; Austria regained Galicia and Kraków became a free city. Prussia gained roughly two-fifths of Saxony, not the entire kingdom; the German Confederation counted 39 states under Austrian presidency. The Norway transfer originated at Kiel in 1814 and was confirmed in the later settlement. British overseas holdings are described as part of the postwar distribution, not as all first acquired by signing Vienna. Holy Alliance's three monarchies, the four-power defensive/consultative pact and the broader Concert are distinct; France was admitted to the wider consultation at Aix although the four-power pact persisted. Britain disputed general intervention at Troppau and resisted its overseas extension at Verona; do not present intervention as unanimous Concert policy. Aix (1818), Troppau (1820), Laibach (1821), Verona (1822), Belgian independence (1830) and the divided revolutions/Frankfurt effort (1848) remain separate episodes.
