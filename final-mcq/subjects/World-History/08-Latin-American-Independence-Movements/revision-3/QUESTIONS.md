# Revision 3 — Latin American Independence Movements

Original UPSC-style practice. Choose one option for each question.

### Q1. A wealthy American-born white landowner is denied an office awarded to a newly arrived Spaniard. Which explanation best fits colonial hierarchy?

B. Ownership of an estate placed the creole among officials born in the peninsula.
C. Place of birth could limit a creole's office despite his substantial landholding.

B. Indigenous land claims were settled by replacing peninsular governors with creoles.
C. Military participation gave Indigenous communities control over landed creole estates.
D. Participation in independence did not itself secure Indigenous control of land.

### Q3. In Saint-Domingue, abolition and self-government did not occur at the same constitutional moment as independence. Which step marks the break from French sovereignty rather than a prior emancipation or autonomous stage?

B. Louverture's autonomous government still recognising a French connection.
C. Dessalines' declaration after renewed war against the French expedition.
D. The constitution naming Louverture governor of a French-linked colony.

### Q4. Which account distinguishes a persistent grievance from the crisis that made a Spanish-American break politically possible?

C. Juntas ended trade restrictions before creoles challenged metropolitan authority.
D. Napoleon's occupation created colonial trade controls that the juntas later resisted.

### Q5. The constitution adopted under Louverture abolished slavery and established local institutions. Which of its actual provisions most clearly rules out reading it as a declaration of Haitian independence?

C. Every man, regardless of colour, was eligible for public office there.
D. The central assembly voted to accept or reject laws proposed by the governor.

### Q6. A Spanish-American council invokes a monarch whose authority has been disrupted. What is the best description of its initial political claim?

### Q9. San Martín regarded the Spanish position in Peru as a danger to his Argentine base. Which route best describes his response through Chile?

### Q11. Who commanded the patriot field army at Ayacucho?

### Q13. A Brazilian planter supported Pedro's break with Portugal but opposed changing plantation labour. Which outcome best describes what political independence meant for that planter?
