- **Important correction to the owner shorthand:** Ayacucho was **Sucre's** field command, not San Martín's or Bolívar's personal battlefield command. San Martín's earlier Peruvian campaign and Bolivia's subsequent separate founding must not be collapsed. Gran Colombia encompassed the lands of present-day Colombia, Venezuela, Ecuador and Panama, **not Bolivia**; the Central American federation was another polity. Toussaint did not proclaim Haitian independence; Dessalines did. Do not treat the labels “free Black republic” and “republic in every year after 1804” as synonymous: Dessalines later became emperor.
