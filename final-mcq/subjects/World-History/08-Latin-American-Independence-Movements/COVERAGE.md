## Source-risk register

- B§8.4's blanket phrase “emancipation was immediate only in Haiti” is usable solely as a comparative shorthand: avoid claiming that no other emancipation occurred later or that Haiti's revolutionary emancipation had no reversals before final independence.
- B§8.5's Ayacucho row follows San Martín's campaign row but does **not** mean San Martín commanded Ayacucho; questions distinguish endpoint from commander.
- B§8.6 and A§4 refer to Lowe's opening context; Lowe PDF p.625 is retrospective, not a contemporary witness or a source for every named nineteenth-century causal detail.
- No individual caudillo, invented army size, state total, precise fiscal figure, unsupported constitution clause or invented official PYQ is used. Individual answer files cite the accessible full-text historical pages used for Sucre, Gran Colombia, the Central American federation, Mexico's leaders and the 1801 Haitian constitution; inaccessible index snippets are not cited as checked pages.
