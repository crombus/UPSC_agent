# Revision 1 — answer review

Original questions, not official Prelims PYQs. B/A/T/W/L and full-text historical source keys H/S/C/F are defined in [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md). Each option is assessed on its own claim; where a false option contains a true fragment, the explanation identifies the exact failing fragment.

### Q1. D
- **A:** Creoles' landed wealth did not confer preferential top colonial appointments; peninsulares enjoyed that advantage. [B§2,§8.4]
- **B:** Peninsulares were favoured, not excluded, in royal appointments. [B§2]
- **C:** Creole landownership is real, but preferential *leadership of senior viceroyal administration* belonged to peninsulares. [B§2,§8.4]
- **D (correct):** Wealthy American-born creoles resented Spanish-born peninsulares' superior senior access. [B§2; T1–4]
- **Trap:** Senior office access is not synonymous with ownership of land.

### Q2. D
