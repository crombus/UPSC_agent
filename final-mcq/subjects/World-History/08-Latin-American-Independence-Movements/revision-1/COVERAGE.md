Keys: **A 10 / B 10 / C 10 / D 10**, not delivered in A–B–C–D rotation. **Conditional exact-length audit: 7 keyed uniquely longest / 29 items with a unique longest (24.1%); 8 keyed uniquely shortest / 32 items with a unique shortest (25.0%).** Denominators exclude items tied at the corresponding extreme; option text is measured by Unicode character count within each four-choice item. The result is descriptive, not a forced tying rule or a 130% ratio test. Chronology-choice texts contain **no years**. The question file has no answers, rationale, source list or option markers of correctness.
- The analytical frames in B§8 and A§1–5 are **interpretations**, not claims of uniform experience. Haiti's emancipation did not mean every emancipation process elsewhere stopped; Ayacucho did not end every Spanish outpost instantly; Bolívar's wider campaigns did not make Peru/Bolivia members of Gran Colombia.
