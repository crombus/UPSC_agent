## Source and ownership cautions

- Lowe PDF p.37 distinguishes Russian **success against Austria in Galicia** from defeats by **Germany at Tannenberg and the Masurian Lakes**; do not transfer a battle to the wrong adversary or generalise western immobility to the east.
- Lowe PDF pp.39–40 distinguishes Gallipoli's *failed Allied* attempt to open Russia's Black Sea supply line from the later German–Bulgarian overrun of Serbia. Lowe PDF pp.45–47 distinguishes propaganda/war aims (duration), the spring offensive (German failure), Amiens/Hindenburg Line (Allied counterattack) and naval/civilian resource constraints (defeat).
- Lowe PDF pp.55–56 identifies Austrian **St Germain** (Bohemia/Moravia to Czechoslovakia; South Tyrol/Trieste to Italy) separately from Hungarian **Trianon** (Slovakia/Ruthenia to Czechoslovakia; Transylvania to Romania). Do not compress the treaty cluster into merely matching treaty names.
- Basic §2's shorthand “Russia exits in 1917” must be read alongside its own timeline: Bolshevik armistice **December 1917**, Brest-Litovsk **March 1918**. Avoid a question claiming that the March treaty was signed in 1917.
- The 2024 balance-of-power question is Topic 09 **Mains** ownership, not a Topic 10 Prelims PYQ. **Zero exact official PYQs are reproduced**; an exact item remains held until same-paper/series official scoring key, precise question identification **and** documented UPSC reproduction permission.
