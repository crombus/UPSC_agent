## Audit basis and results

- Read Basic 07 including §8, Advanced 07, root `COVERAGE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `AGENT_MEMORY.md` and `final-mcq/INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Scanned **all 80** atlas questions and all their A–D alternatives and generic explanations; OCR inventory of the **entire 29-page** solved-workbook PDF confirmed all 80 question labels and six original solved Mains exercises. Its zero-direct-PYQ routing does **not** establish a historical official-paper census. The atlas's alternative true statements and fixed A–B–C–D rotation are not model distractors for this test.
- Norman Lowe local OCR PDF p. 21 corroborates post-1880 market/raw-material expansion; pp. 25–26 distinguish later Morocco diplomacy and the **1878** European Congress of Berlin from the African **1884–85** conference. Directly fetched 2026-10-01: [Britannica Berlin Conference](https://www.britannica.com/event/Berlin-West-Africa-Conference), [Scramble for Africa](https://www.britannica.com/event/Scramble-for-Africa), [Fashoda](https://www.britannica.com/event/Fashoda-Incident). The latter verifies Marchand's eastward advance from Gabon, Kitchener's southward advance from Egypt, and the **1899** Nile–Congo watershed settlement. Berlin's Act covers new **coastal** occupations; Congo and Niger navigation are separate declarations.
- **403-dependence refinement (2026-10-01):** Re-fetched the previously cited Britannica Congo Free State, Menilek II and French West Africa pages; all returned HTTP 403. Removed **every citation to these three pages** from R2 `ANSWERS.md`; rechecked all 47 keyed choices and all 188 A–D rationales for Congo sovereignty, Congo coercion, French federation and Ethiopian claims, even where a 403 URL had not appeared in an item's source line. No R2 key or rationale now relies solely on an inaccessible page. Eight freshly accessible sources and item-level section/page routes replace them:
  - **Congo origins/recognition/1908:** [U.S. State Department, Office of the Historian, *Congo Free State*, Summary, Recognition and Demise](https://history.state.gov/countries/congo-free-state) directly records Leopold's association, local agreements, his separate recognised sovereignty in 1885 and Belgium's annexation on **15 November 1908**. [AfricaMuseum, *History*, Origin/Construction](https://www.africamuseum.be/en/discover/history) independently identifies Leopold's private Congo profits and the 1908 change to Belgian Congo. R2 Q10–Q13, Q18, Q25, Q32–Q33, Q38, Q43.
  - **Congo rubber/hostages:** [AfricaMuseum, *Mai-Ndombe*, “A province with a grim past”](https://www.africamuseum.be/en/research/discover/news/mai-ndombe) describes Leopold's rubber predation and brutality by agents and auxiliaries. The primary [Roger Casement, *Correspondence and Report ... Congo*, British parliamentary *Africa No. 1 (1904)*, Report No. 3, printed pp. 46, 50; further correspondence, part II p. 61](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/50573/pg50573-images.html) records women/children held until rubber was produced, a local “house of hostages,” armed coercion and punishment. It is a British diplomatic report to read critically, corroborated by the museum's account; no unsupported death total is used. R2 Q06, Q12, Q32.
  - **French federation:** [ASNOM, *French West Africa*, opening section](https://www.asnom.org/french-west-africa-aof) names the **16 June 1895** decree and Senegal, Sudan, Guinea, Ivory Coast as original members; [Columbia Encyclopedia, *French West Africa*](https://www.encyclopedia.com/reference/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/french-west-africa) confirms 1895 and the West African constituent territories. Neither Algeria nor Madagascar is listed. R2 Q16.
  - **Menelik/Adwa:** [Dennis Showalter, “Addis Ababa, Treaty of,” *Encyclopedia of Modern Europe*](https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/addis-ababa-treaty) explicitly documents the two Ucciali/Wuchale texts, Italy's protectorate claim, Menelik's protest and military mobilisation, Adwa, and the October 1896 treaty confirming independence. [U.S. State Department-hosted 1906 tripartite agreement, Articles 1 and 4(b)](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1907p1/d460) explicitly preserves the Ethiopian emperor's sovereign rights while treating Eritrea and Somaliland as Italy's neighbouring interests. R2 Q19–Q21, Q40, Q46. The independent [ASNOM Ivory Coast section](https://www.asnom.org/french-west-africa-aof) records the defeat of Samory by French forces, supporting Q40's non-key distinctions.
