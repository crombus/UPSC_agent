### Q1
During a hostile audit of **Policy cycle**, which qualification should be retained?

A. A bottom-up account should not erase the legal design or the authority against which implementation is evaluated.
B. The cycle is a locating device, not a claim that every policy moves neatly once through a fixed one-way sequence.
C. The block programme cannot be used to supply a district rank, KPI or expenditure figure absent a verified source.
D. Discretion is not automatically illegitimate; the governance task is to guide it with reasons, records and review.

### Q2
During a hostile audit of **Implementation gap**, which qualification should be retained?

A. Not every contradiction is a coordination failure; some are genuine value conflicts that process alone cannot resolve.

D. A specific allegation of creaming needs caseload, staffing or case-distribution evidence rather than a generic assertion.
