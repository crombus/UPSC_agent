## Source discipline

- Markdown owners are the source of truth; the complete package and solved workbook were read for cross-checking.
- Dynamic counts are carried only with their owner verification date and are never treated as timeless.
- No evaluation finding, district rank, indicator value, effect size, completed-evaluation count or undated coverage percentage is asserted.
- Analytical statements are marked by their qualified wording in the owner material; they are not presented as official findings.
