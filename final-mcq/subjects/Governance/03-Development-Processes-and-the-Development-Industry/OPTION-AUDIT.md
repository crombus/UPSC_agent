# Topic 03 Option Audit

- PASS: correct positions are independently shuffled per bank with near-balanced A/B/C/D counts.
- PASS: no bank uses a visible A→B→C→D rotation.
- PASS: every question has four distinct, grammatically complete options.
- PASS: distractors are drawn from neighbouring owner concepts to create close-option discrimination rather than arbitrary falsehoods.
- PASS: no option contains an answer-key label, rationale, source heading or explanatory suffix.
- PASS: option-length screening completed by the release script; any residual difference is substantive legal or analytical content, not a key cue.
