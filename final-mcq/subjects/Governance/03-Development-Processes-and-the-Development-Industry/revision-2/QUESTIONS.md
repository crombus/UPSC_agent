# Governance Topic 03 — Revision 2: Questions

Original UPSC-style Prelims questions. Each item is answer-free; exact PYQ wording and options are withheld under the source/PYQ/rights hold.

### Q1
A State is reviewing a development partnership. Which diagnostic statement is the soundest? The issue under examination is **Development industry as system**.

A. In application, the relevant proposition is that for qualifying companies, the CSR minimum is 2% of average net profits of preceding financial years; rule-based details should not be guessed.
B. In application, the relevant proposition is that federal/local fit asks whether a project converges with district and local plans instead of creating a parallel silo.
C. In application, the relevant proposition is that the development industry is an ecosystem of donors, trusts, foundations, CSR programmes and implementing NGOs delivering development alongside the State.
D. In application, the relevant proposition is that mHA-administered FCRA, 2010 as amended in 2020 gates foreign contributions through registration or prior permission.

### Q2
A district programme is being classified before release. Which proposition best fits the case? The issue under examination is **Funding actors**.

A. In application, the relevant proposition is that voluntary philanthropy has no equivalent statutory spending floor or Schedule VII activity restriction.

D. In application, the relevant proposition is that income-tax provisions such as 12A/12AB and 80G concern tax exemption or donor deduction; they are not incorporation or FCRA permission.

### Q3
An evaluator is tracing a non-state intervention from finance to outcomes. Which inference is most defensible? The issue under examination is **Public charitable trust regime**.

A. In application, the relevant proposition is that analysis must distinguish funding source, legal vehicle and last-mile implementer; calling all three 'charity' collapses different accountability roles.
