# Governance Final-MCQ Compliance Instructions

Read this file immediately before and after every Governance topic continuation.

## Non-negotiable requirements

1. Follow `final-mcq/subjects/Governance/INDEX.md` and the catalogue order exactly. Do not
   skip, merge, compress, or open a later Governance topic while the active topic has
   unresolved release work. Social Justice, Disaster Management and Internal Security are
   separate queues and must not be started by this pass.
2. Every topic requires independent `revision-1`, `revision-2`, and `revision-3` banks.
3. Each revision must cover the complete Basic/Advanced owner boundary affirmatively. A detail
   appearing only in a distractor, source note, or explanation does not count.
4. Every revision must contain answer-free `QUESTIONS.md`, separately explained `ANSWERS.md`,
   and a question-level `COVERAGE.md`. No key, rationale, source cue, or answer-revealing
   heading may appear in `QUESTIONS.md`.
5. Exact PYQs may be included only with the same-paper/set/question provenance, an
   authenticated official key, and documented reproduction permission. Otherwise retain an
   explicit source/PYQ hold and test the concept independently.
6. Options must be plausible, grammatically parallel, distinct, and visually balanced.
   Reject answer cues from length, specificity, terminology, formatting, absolutes, or
   predictable key patterns. Stems must be materially distinct within and across revisions.
7. Revision 1 establishes the full foundation; Revision 2 uses close distinctions,
   counterexamples and institutional routing; Revision 3 uses application, measurement,
   evidence and mixed UPSC framing. Do not reshuffle or paraphrase one bank into another.
8. Do not hallucinate institutional origin, mandate, headquarters, membership, reports or
   frequency, funding, leadership, committees, timeline, India linkage, awards, statistics,
   dates, sources, PYQ keys or outcomes. Mark unavailable or time-sensitive facts as holds.
   For every relevant institution or instrument, record which of those fields are applicable,
   verified, not applicable, or held.
9. Each answer must state the key, explain the correct choice and every alternative, cite
   evidence, and identify an examiner trap. Source and PYQ/rights holds must remain visible.
10. Run structural, option-balance, leakage, cross-revision distinctness, source/date,
    institutional-fact and hostile audits. Repair every actionable finding before changing
    the subject or repository index.

## Release gate

- All three revisions and all required ledgers/audit reports exist.
- Question/key mapping is one-to-one and contiguous.
- Every question has exactly four options and every bank has affirmative owner coverage.
- Questions contain no answers, keys, rationales, source cues, or explanatory suffixes.
- Answers contain four option-specific rationales, sources and a trap.
- Exact PYQ wording/options remain withheld unless the provenance/key/rights gate is complete.
- A topic may be marked `complete` only after all actionable audits pass; explicit dated,
  issuer-access, PYQ and reproduction-rights holds remain recorded rather than guessed away.
