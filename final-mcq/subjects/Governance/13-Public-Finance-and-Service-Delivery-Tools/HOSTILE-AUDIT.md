# Topic 13 Hostile Audit

## Adversarial checks
- PASS — JAM, DBT scope, PFMS ownership, SNA succession, SNA-SPARSH components, e-Kuber and State IFMIS are kept distinct.
- PASS — E1 inclusion error, E2 exclusion error, leakage, diversion, inefficiency and outcome failure are not collapsed into one category.
- PASS — credited account versus usable access, cash versus in-kind, and gross savings versus welfare gain are separately tested.
- PASS — GFR procedure is not presented as proof of procurement quality, value for money or impact.
- PASS — budget, release, agency, procurement, beneficiary and outcome stages are routed to different instruments.
- PASS — Article 280(3)(bb)/(c), tied/untied grants and the three-source local-finance test are institutionally bounded.
- PASS — SNA-SPARSH's notified applicability is not described as universal operational completion.
- PASS — no unsupported savings, counts, rates, thresholds, portal volumes or State utilisation statistics are published.
- PASS — exact objective PYQ wording/options and unauthenticated keys remain withheld.

## Residual holds
- Official objective-key provenance and reproduction-rights holds remain open.
- Current operational onboarding and future scheme-status claims require re-check before reuse.
- Live DBT dashboard and CAG transport/access limitations are recorded rather than filled by inference.
