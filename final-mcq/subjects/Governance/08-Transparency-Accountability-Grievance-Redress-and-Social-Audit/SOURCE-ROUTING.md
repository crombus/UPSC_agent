## Fact controls

- RTI 30-day/48-hour response periods, first-appeal 30 days, second-appeal 90 days, and PIO Rs 250/day up to Rs 25,000 are treated as statutory owner facts.
- MGNREGA section 17, Audit of Schemes Rules 2011, six-monthly audit, independent State Social Audit Unit, Gram Sabha and public-hearing design are treated as source-grounded.
- Meghalaya 2017 is treated as a State-level generalised statutory social-audit model, not a national law.
- CPGRAMS 21-day redress and 30-day appeal figures are dated to DARPG Comprehensive Guidelines, August 2024.
- RTI section 8(1)(j) is treated as substituted by DPDP section 44(3) from 13/14 November 2025; DPDP section 44(2) is separately held for 13 May 2027.
- Whistle Blowers Protection Act, 2014 is treated as enacted but not commenced; the 2015 amendment bill is treated as lapsed.
