# Topic 06 Option Audit

- All three banks contain 45 questions and 180 options per bank; every question has four distinct options.
- Correct positions are independently distributed with counts recorded in each bank coverage ledger.
- Distractors are drawn from adjacent owner concepts and are grammatically parallel statements, not answer-key cues.
- No option uses unsupported transaction, user, coverage, penalty or ranking figures.
- Exact PYQ wording/options are not reproduced.
