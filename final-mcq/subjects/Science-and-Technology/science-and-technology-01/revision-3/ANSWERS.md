**Examiner trap:** Funding/design approval cannot substantiate flight demonstration.

### 35. B — Range safety, strategic dual use, debris, insurance, private indemnity and appeal gaps

### 36. D — Hardware-limited cadence

- A: ❌ Ground support does not fill a missing launch-stage production slot.
- B: ❌ Administrative capacity does not create tested stages for a launch.
- C: ❌ Another launch window does not manufacture flight-qualified hardware.
- D: ⚠️ To fill successive unused pad slots, qualified stage-manufacturing output must feed successive vehicle integrations; pad access alone cannot supply flight-ready hardware.

**Sources:** upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Science-and-Technology/basic/01_Space-Programme-ISRO-Launch-Vehicles.md; upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Science-and-Technology/advanced/01_Space-Programme-ISRO-Launch-Vehicles.md.
**Examiner trap:** Empty pad capacity is not a ready vehicle.

### 37. B — Damage location and treaty standard

- A: ❌ Insurance cannot transfer the international launching-State obligation to an insurer; the two quoted damage standards are otherwise correctly assigned.
- B: ✅ Liability Convention Article II makes the launching State absolutely liable for aircraft-in-flight damage. Article III imposes a fault standard for damage to another launching State's space object elsewhere than on Earth's surface; private insurance does not replace the treaty's State-to-State rules.
- C: ❌ The aircraft-in-flight rule is absolute, while the inter-space-object rule elsewhere than Earth's surface is fault-based, not the reverse.
- D: ❌ The Convention does not impose absolute liability for the described collision between space objects in orbit.

**Sources:** https://www.unoosa.org/oosa/en/ourwork/spacelaw/treaties/liability-convention.html (Articles II–III, UN primary treaty text); upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Science-and-Technology/advanced/01_Space-Programme-ISRO-Launch-Vehicles.md.
**Examiner trap:** Aircraft in flight invokes Article II's absolute rule; a collision with another State's space object in orbit invokes Article III's fault test. Insurance is no treaty exemption.

### 38. B — Small-launcher architecture

- A: ❌ SSLV's main propulsion stages are solid; the trimming module is liquid.
- B: ✅ ISRO specifies three solid stages, small payload class and liquid velocity trimming.
- C: ❌ SSLV does not have four liquid main stages or a solid trimming module.
- D: ❌ SSLV's main stage count is three and its trimming module is not cryogenic.

**Sources:** https://www.isro.gov.in/sslv_CON.html; upsc-ai-kit/knowledge/Science-and-Technology/basic/01_Space-Programme-ISRO-Launch-Vehicles.md.
**Examiner trap:** The terminal liquid trimming module does not turn SSLV into four solid main stages.

### 39. C — Learning sequence
