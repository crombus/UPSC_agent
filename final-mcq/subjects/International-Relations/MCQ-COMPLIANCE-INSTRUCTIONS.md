# International Relations Final-MCQ Compliance Instructions

Read this file immediately before and after every International Relations topic pass.

1. Process the catalogue in order and complete exactly one topic per pass.
2. Each revision requires answer-free `QUESTIONS.md`, separate `ANSWERS.md`, and its own
   auditable `COVERAGE.md`.
3. Every question must be original unless official PYQ wording, same-paper key provenance,
   and reproduction permission are all documented. Otherwise record a hold and author an
   independent concept test.
4. Options must be plausible and balanced in length, grammar, specificity, and formatting.
   No answer may be identifiable from visual or grammatical cues.
5. For institutions, expand the relevant origin or founding instrument, mandate, headquarters,
   membership, reports/publications and frequency, funds or financial instruments,
   leadership/appointment, committees, timeline, and India linkage. Add Nobel or major-award
   facts only when directly authoritative and relevant.
6. Every answer needs the correct option, dated source/evidence, four option-specific
   rationales, and an examiner trap.
7. Record live-fact dates, PYQ provenance, source rights, and unresolved official-status holds.
8. Run structural and hostile audits; repair actionable findings before updating the subject
   index. Read this file again after the topic and before any next topic.
