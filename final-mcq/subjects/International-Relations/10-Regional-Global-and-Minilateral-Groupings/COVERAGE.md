| Quad | 2004 tsunami Core Group; 2007 dialogue; revival 2017; leaders 2021 | No permanent HQ or Secretariat; India, Australia, Japan, US | Leaders' and ministerial meetings; issue initiatives rather than treaty reporting | No Article 5, no collective-defence fund, no permanent committee structure | India uses Quad for maritime, technology, health, infrastructure and critical-minerals cooperation |
| NDB / AIIB | NDB Fortaleza 2014, operational 2015; AIIB Articles effective 2015 | NDB Shanghai; AIIB Beijing; development-bank members and governance organs | Project and annual institutional reporting; not political-grouping summit reports | Infrastructure/sustainable-development finance; shareholding does not equal unilateral control | India is NDB founder and AIIB founder/second-largest shareholder |
