# UN and International Institutions: Global Governance — Revision 1 explained answers

**Release date:** 2026-10-04. Correct answers are separated from the answer-free question release.

### 1. A

**Sources:** UN Charter Chapter V, Articles 23-32: https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/chapter-5 (accessed 2026-10-04).
