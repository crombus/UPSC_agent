# Hostile audit — Topic 12

- **Cue/leakage:** no correct-answer labels or explanatory suffixes in question releases; options are grammatical parallel statements.
- **Template test:** revision stems vary by institutional fact, status distinction and application framing; the three banks are not answer-order reshuffles.
- **Institution test:** origin/mandate, headquarters, membership/composition, leadership/committees, reports/frequency, funding, timeline, India linkage and award facts are represented where relevant.
- **Status test:** adoption, signature, ratification, entry into force, notification and completed withdrawal are separately tested.
- **PYQ test:** exact wording/options remain held; no provisional key is promoted to an answer.
- **Source test:** claims are tied to official URLs or the dated canonical owner; unsupported current claims are not released.
- **Cross-bank distinctness:** stems remain item-unique within the subject release and each revision tests the complete root spine.
