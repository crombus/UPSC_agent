
ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/31: Absolute truth without epistemic absolutism
THE ALLEGED INFERENCE (2022 stem, reconstructed)
  P1  There is exactly ONE Absolute Truth.
  P2  [SUPPRESSED]  A finite formulation CAN EXHAUST it.
  P3  [SUPPRESSED]  MY tradition's formulation is that one.
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  C   Therefore every other tradition is simply mistaken  ==> EXCLUSIVISM
  THE ARGUMENT IS VALID. It is UNSOUND if P2 or P3 fails.
CONCEDE FIRST: if "absolute truth" MEANS "my religion's propositions are the sole truth",
  the inference succeeds - trivially, because exclusivism was built into the premise.
DENY P2 - THREE INDEPENDENT TRADITIONS ALREADY DO
  ADVAITA  nirguna Brahman (WITHOUT QUALITIES) is beyond exhaustive predication, while
           saguna forms (WITH QUALITIES) structure real devotional paths
  JAINISM  syadvada indexes every assertion to a standpoint (Panel 15)
  HICK     the Real an sich is transcategorial (Panel 7)
  ==> ABSOLUTE REFERENT  +  PLURALIST ACCESS. The link is BROKEN.
FOUR THINGS AN ABSOLUTE-TRUTH ANSWER MUST SEPARATE
  1 REFERENT     what the truth is ABOUT            (one)
  2 FORMULATION  how it is STATED                   (many, non-exhaustive)
  3 ACCESS       who can KNOW it, and how well      (partial, corrigible)
  4 SALVATION    who is LIBERATED by it             (a FURTHER question)
THREE THESES THAT DO NOT ENTAIL ONE ANOTHER
  ONTOLOGICAL ABSOLUTISM  |  EPISTEMIC FALLIBILISM  |  POLITICAL TOLERANCE
  ==> an exclusivist may consistently defend religious freedom (Locke); and tolerance
      cannot be inferred from metaphysics without an ADDITIONAL premise.
PRESUPPOSITION THE REPLY ITSELF MAKES: truth permits DEGREES OF ADEQUACY or NON-LITERAL
  MEDIATION without collapsing into relativism. State it - a critic who denies it reinstates
  the inference, and that is where the argument properly continues.
SELF-REFERENCE WORRY: "there is one absolute Truth" is itself a finite formulation.
  REPLY: the claim is FORMAL (a single referent), not substantive - the same move as Hick's,
  with the same cost (thinness). What it does establish is that the position must be held
  CORRIGIBLY, which is what epistemic fallibilism asserts.
TEMPLATE: "Ontological absolutism entails epistemic exclusivism ONLY IF one adds the premise
  that a finite formulation can exhaust the absolute. Deny that premise - as Advaita's nirguna
  and Jain anekantavada both do - and the inference from absolute truth to exclusivism fails."
2024 STEM ("notion of absolute truth"): a NOTION question - the four-way separation IS the
  analysis, and the compatibility result is the note's conclusion.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 17/31: What pluralism cannot dissolve - hard contradiction
APPARENT CONFLICT BETWEEN TRADITIONS - four legitimate reinterpretive moves, and the residue
  +-----------+-----------+------------+----------------+------------------------------+
  | LEVEL     | SYMBOL    | STANDPOINT | SOTERIOLOGICAL | RESIDUE                      |
  |           |           |            | END            |                              |
  | nirguna / | non-      | syat-      | different REAL | SAME question, SAME respect  |
  | saguna;   | literal   | indexed    | ends (Heim):   | ---------------------------- |
  | ultimate /| predi-    | assertion  | communion =/=  | "a personal creator exists"  |
  | conven-   | cation;   | (Jain)     | non-dual =/=   | vs "no such being exists"    |
  | tional    | symbolic  |            | nirvana        | "the self is permanent"      |
  | truth     | reference |            |                | vs "there is no such self"   |
  |-----------+-----------+------------+----------------|------------------------------|
  |     these dissolve APPARENT conflict                | NOT DISSOLVABLE -            |
  |                                                     | ACKNOWLEDGE IT               |
  +-----------------------------------------------------+------------------------------+
THE CONTROL, VERBATIM IN SUBSTANCE: a personal creator cannot both exist and not exist in the
  same respect; the self cannot both be permanent and denied in the same sense. Pluralism may
  reinterpret level, symbol, standpoint or end, BUT IT MUST ACKNOWLEDGE ANY CONTRADICTION
  THAT REMAINS.
WHY CONCEDING THE RESIDUE STRENGTHENS THE ANSWER
  a pluralism that concedes irreducible conflict at some points but maintains authentic plural
  response at others is a DETERMINATE position that can be argued for; a pluralism that
  harmonises everything is UNFALSIFIABLE and, by D'Costa's argument, is quietly imposing its
  own framework on traditions that assert contradictories DELIBERATELY.
OBJECTION: if contradictions remain, at most one tradition is right about them, so pluralism
  has collapsed into exclusivism about those claims.
REPLY: yes - ABOUT THOSE CLAIMS, and that is the correct result, not a defeat. Pluralism is a
  thesis about traditions as wholes and about the availability of truth and transformation
  beyond one's own; it is NOT the thesis that no religious proposition is false. Alethic
  exclusivity on a specific proposition is compatible with soteriological and political
  pluralism - which is exactly what the four axes of Panel 1 were designed to permit.
VERDICT-LADDER NOTE: "explicit acknowledgement of hard contradictions that pluralism cannot
  dissolve" is a TOP-BAND requirement.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 19/31: Peer disagreement - conciliationism against steadfastness
DEFINITION: an EPISTEMIC PEER is one roughly equal in EVIDENCE and REASONING ABILITY.
  +----------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------+
  | CONCILIATIONISM / EQUAL WEIGHT         | STEADFASTNESS                                |
  | Richard Feldman - David Christensen    | Thomas Kelly - Peter van Inwagen             |
  |----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
  | on discovering that a peer disagrees,  | one MAY retain one's belief, since one's OWN |
  | REDUCE confidence, often toward        | reasoning is part of one's evidence          |
  | suspension                             |                                              |
  |----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
  | NEEDS the INDEPENDENCE PRINCIPLE:      | DENIES the independence principle            |
  | assess the disagreement WITHOUT relying|                                              |
  | on the reasoning under dispute         |                                              |
  +----------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------+
VAN INWAGEN'S WELL-KNOWN CLAIM: it is not irrational to retain one's belief even when one
  cannot show that the other party is wrong.
WHY NAMING THE INDEPENDENCE PRINCIPLE MATTERS: it shows WHY the two camps disagree rather than
  merely THAT they do - the whole dispute turns on whether the disputed reasoning may be used
  in assessing the dispute.
CONNECTION TO PANEL 9: Plantinga's third reply - awareness of disagreement REDUCES but does
  not EXTINGUISH warrant - is a steadfast position, and gets its technical backing here.
DISTINCTION TO PROTECT: PEER disagreement assumes a SHARED FRAMEWORK and asks what to do about
  a peer. DEEP disagreement (Panel 20) DENIES the shared framework. Conflating them makes both
  positions unstatable.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 20/31: Deep disagreement - Fogelin, hinges, and four consequences
WHY THE MODULE IS NEEDED: the pluralism debate ASSUMES rival truth-claims could in principle be
  settled by argument. The epistemology of disagreement asks whether that assumption is true -
  and its answer RESHAPES THE WHOLE CLAUSE.
FOGELIN 1985, "The Logic of Deep Disagreement"
  some disagreements are NOT RESOLVABLE BY ARGUMENT IN PRINCIPLE, because the parties do not
  share the FRAMEWORK PROPOSITIONS - Wittgenstein's HINGE commitments from ON CERTAINTY -
  against which evidence would count.
  ==> when backgrounds diverge, THE SAME EVIDENCE IS NOT EVIDENCE FOR THE SAME THING,
      and argument has NO SHARED FULCRUM.
MICHAEL LYNCH extends this to disagreement about EPISTEMIC PRINCIPLES themselves - which
  sources are reliable - where any defence is CIRCULAR.
APPLICATION TO RELIGION - the parties differ over
  * SOURCES of evidence      which scripture, which experience, which inference
  * CRITERIA of adequacy     salvation? liberation? cessation?
  * the FRAMEWORK            within which any datum would count at all
FOUR CONSEQUENCES - WRITE THEM AS FOUR, AND NOTE THAT THEY CUT BOTH WAYS
  1 the failure of rational convergence is PREDICTED, not scandalous
    ==> it is WEAK EVIDENCE FOR RELATIVISM
  2 exclusivism CANNOT BE CONVICTED OF IRRATIONALITY merely for persisting
  3 pluralism CANNOT BE ESTABLISHED merely by pointing at persistence
  4 what CAN be rationally required are PROCEDURAL VIRTUES - fallibilism, intellectual
    humility, epistemic charity, and willingness to MAKE ONE'S FRAMEWORK EXPLICIT
THE INDIAN RESOURCE: anekantavada / nayavada / syadvada is in effect a FORMAL APPARATUS for
  exactly this - it makes the standpoint (naya) explicit and prefixes every assertion with
  syat, so framework-relativity is marked INSIDE the proposition. Saying it that way is worth
  more than describing the elephant parable.
OBJECTION: if deep disagreement is unresolvable, pluralism collapses into relativism.
REPLY: UNRESOLVABILITY IS EPISTEMIC, NOT ALETHIC - one party may still be RIGHT; what fails is
  our ability to DEMONSTRATE which.                          <== THE PAYOFF SENTENCE
SECOND OBJECTION: this licenses complacency.
REPLY: it licenses only the RETENTION of belief, not the refusal of dialogue - and it makes the
  procedural virtues OBLIGATORY rather than optional.
VERDICT FORMULA: "Religious disagreement is deep in Fogelin's technical sense: it lacks a
  shared framework against which evidence could adjudicate. That EXPLAINS the persistence of
  exclusivism WITHOUT VINDICATING it, and supports pluralism as an ETHIC OF ENGAGEMENT rather
  than as a demonstrated metaphysics."
PROMOTION NOTE: the Advanced dossier's ledger of 15 AUGUST 2026 records deep disagreement
  about Hick's Real as PROMOTED TO CORE. It is not optional enrichment.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 21/31: Toleration - three components and one paradox
WHAT TOLERATION IS - three conditions, ALL required
  (i)   OBJECTION   I genuinely disapprove of, or believe false, what is tolerated
  (ii)  ACCEPTANCE  I have reasons that OUTWEIGH but do not CANCEL the objection
  (iii) POWER       I COULD interfere, and deliberately REFRAIN
  remove (i)   ==> INDIFFERENCE or APPROVAL, not toleration
  remove (iii) ==> MERE ENDURANCE, not toleration
  ==> TOLERATION IS NOT INDIFFERENCE.
THE PARADOX OF TOLERATION
  to tolerate is to ALLOW WHAT ONE HOLDS WRONG
     -> looks WEAK-WILLED  if the objection is serious
     -> looks INSINCERE    if it is not
RESOLUTION BY LEVELS
  FIRST-ORDER   : your doctrine is false
  SECOND-ORDER  : coercing belief is wrong, ineffective, or destructive of the very good
                  at stake
  ==> the reasons for ACCEPTANCE sit at a DIFFERENT LEVEL from the objection.
WHY THE DEFINITION DOES THE WORK: the 2020 stem asks for the IMPORTANCE of tolerance and says
  "justify". Define it by the three components first, and the importance FOLLOWS from the
  definition instead of having to be asserted.
CONSEQUENCE OF (i): the objection component is what makes toleration a MORAL ACHIEVEMENT
  rather than mere indifference - so a society of genuine disagreement cannot be built on
  esteem alone.
CONSEQUENCE OF (iii): toleration is always exercised by someone who COULD HAVE DONE OTHERWISE
  - which is why tolerance from the strong to the weak is PERMISSION, NOT EQUALITY (Panel 22).
TRAP: do not treat "tolerance" as one thing. Grade it (Panel 22) before praising any example.

  3 RESPECT      reciprocal recognition as equal moral-political persons DESPITE deep

  ------------   ONLY 3 AND 4 ARE COMPATIBLE WITH EQUAL CITIZENSHIP
  2 COEXISTENCE  mutual, pragmatic modus vivendi to avoid conflict
  1 PERMISSION   the powerful permit a minority to exist on sufferance

THE WESTERN ARGUMENT-STOCK
  LOCKE, A Letter Concerning Toleration (1689) - THREE arguments
     (a) the magistrate has NO COMMISSION over souls
     (b) FORCE CANNOT PRODUCE BELIEF, only profession
     (c) even if it could, coerced conformity WOULD NOT SAVE
     ==> (b) is a claim about the PSYCHOLOGY OF BELIEF, so it is available to an EXCLUSIVIST
  MILL, On Liberty (1859)
     (a) the HARM PRINCIPLE
     (b) the EPISTEMIC argument: suppressing an opinion robs us of either the truth or
         "the clearer perception of truth produced by its collision with error"
  POPPER, The Open Society and Its Enemies, note to ch. 7 - THE PARADOX OF TOLERANCE
     unlimited tolerance leads to the disappearance of tolerance, so a tolerant society must
     retain the right to refuse to tolerate the intolerant
     ==> POPPER'S OWN FORMULATION RESERVES THIS FOR THOSE WHO REFUSE RATIONAL ARGUMENT AND
         ANSWER WITH VIOLENCE. Do NOT state it as a general licence to suppress.
  RAWLS - an OVERLAPPING CONSENSUS on political principles allows citizens with incompatible
     comprehensive doctrines to share a stable polity.
LIMITS MUST BE DRAWN BY HARM AND RECIPROCITY, NOT BY DOCTRINAL CONTENT - otherwise the
  limit-drawing becomes the intolerance it was meant to prevent.
OBJECTION: toleration is second-best; respect and esteem are what a plural society needs, and
  toleration keeps the tolerator in the position of JUDGE.
REPLY: correct as a critique of LEVEL 1 - but the objection component is what makes toleration
  a moral achievement, and a society of genuine disagreement cannot be built on esteem alone.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 24/31: Religious freedom - the bundle and its four justifications
THE BUNDLE - different members, different protection
  CONSCIENCE (the inner forum) ------------------------------- ABSOLUTELY PROTECTED
  PROFESS   (declare)      \
  PRACTISE  (act)           >-- LIMITABLE by PUBLIC ORDER, MORALITY and HEALTH
  PROPAGATE (persuade)     /
  CHANGE OR LEAVE a religion ------------------------------- follows from conscience
  FREEDOM FROM compelled religious observance
  ASSOCIATIONAL right of communities to order their internal affairs
  ==> "possible?" must be answered DIFFERENTLY for different members of the bundle.
WHY IT IS PHILOSOPHICALLY DEFENSIBLE - four INDEPENDENT arguments
  1 LOCKE'S INCOERCIBILITY  belief is not directly voluntary, so coercion produces
                            HYPOCRISY, not faith
  2 AUTONOMY                matters of ultimate concern are the PARADIGM CASE in which a
                            person must judge FOR HERSELF
  3 EPISTEMIC               given deep disagreement and fallibility, NO INSTITUTION is
                            entitled to the certainty that suppression presupposes
  4 SOCIAL                  in a plural society, enforced uniformity is a CAUSE of conflict
                            rather than its cure
OBJECTION: religious freedom is a LIBERAL value being universalised, and traditions that
  reject liberal individualism have not consented to it.
REPLY: the incoercibility argument is NOT DISTINCTIVELY LIBERAL - it is a claim about the
  PSYCHOLOGY OF BELIEF - and traditions from Asoka's edicts to Sufi and Bhakti universalism
  reach comparable conclusions ON THEIR OWN GROUNDS.
SECOND OBJECTION: unlimited freedom of practice makes governance impossible.
REPLY: the limits are REAL; the philosophical task is to justify them by HARM AND RECIPROCITY
  rather than by ORTHODOXY.
OPENING LINE: "Religious freedom is possible in a multireligious society, and is more nearly
  NECESSARY there than in a homogeneous one. What is not possible is an UNLIMITED or a
  PERFECTLY NEUTRAL version of it."

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 25/31: Religious freedom - the five hard problems
  1 PRACTICE / BELIEF ASYMMETRY
      belief ABSOLUTELY protected; PRACTICE is not, because it can HARM
      every constitutional order limits practice by PUBLIC ORDER, MORALITY and HEALTH
      DIFFICULTY: those limits are stated in terms whose CONTENT IS CONTESTED
  2 PROPAGATION AND CONVERSION
      if profession is free, propagation FOLLOWS
      but conversion is experienced by communities as LOSS, and "inducement" is hard to
      distinguish from "persuasion"
      THE PRINCIPLED LINE: COERCION AND FRAUD - not persuasion as such
  3 INTERNAL MINORITIES - AYELET SHACHAR'S "PARADOX OF MULTICULTURAL VULNERABILITY"
      group rights granted to protect a community FROM the majority can ENTRENCH the power of
      that community's DOMINANT members over its own WEAKER members, typically women
      accommodating a GROUP may thus REDUCE the freedom of PERSONS INSIDE IT
      ==> THE SINGLE MOST EXAMINABLE TENSION IN THE CLAUSE
  4 STATE NEUTRALITY - two models
      WALL OF SEPARATION      the state abstains entirely
      PRINCIPLED DISTANCE     RAJEEV BHARGAVA - the Indian model: the state may engage with or
      (the Indian model)      abstain from religions DIFFERENTIALLY, guided by FREEDOM and
                              EQUALITY rather than by a uniform rule of non-engagement
      ==> principled distance PERMITS REFORM (temple-entry, abolition of untouchability)
          that strict separation would FORBID
  5 JUDICIAL DETERMINATION OF WHAT COUNTS AS RELIGION
      the "essential religious practices" doctrine makes the state an ARBITER OF THEOLOGY
      philosophically this sits uneasily with neutrality, whichever way particular cases go
      ==> NOTE THE TENSION; DO NOT LITIGATE CASES
VERDICT FORMULA: "Religious freedom is POSSIBLE in a multireligious society, and is more nearly
  NECESSARY there. What is not possible is an unlimited or perfectly neutral version: every
  regime must limit practice, and every limit embodies a judgement. The defensible position is
  a PRINCIPLED DISTANCE justified by freedom and equality, with the INTERNAL-MINORITIES problem
  treated as A STANDING COST, NOT A SOLVED ONE."
TRAP: do not answer with a list of constitutional articles. The philosophy is the asymmetry,
  the conversion line, the internal minorities, the neutrality models and the arbiter problem.

     2 blind obedience
     3 establishing the "ideal" time

THE FOUR STANDING CRITICISMS AND THEIR REPLIES
  1 RELATIVISM            equal validity erases truth
    REPLY: distinguish EQUAL DIGNITY / SALVIFIC EFFICACY from IDENTICAL PROPOSITIONAL TRUTH
  2 SELF-REFERENCE        pluralism becomes an exclusive meta-truth
    REPLY: hold it as a DEFEASIBLE HYPOTHESIS.  REJOINDER: it still judges first-order traditions
  3 CONFLICTING SALVATION GOALS  communion, non-dual knowledge and nirvana are not obviously
    one end
    REPLY: family resemblance or the transformation criterion.  CRITICS: this is FLATTENING
  4 CONFLICT CLAIM        pluralism may CREATE dispute by relativising commitment
    REPLY: conflict is not caused by plurality alone; POWER, IDENTITY and EXCLUSIVIST CERTAINTY
           mediate it

THE QUALIFIED CONCLUSION, WITH ITS DEFEATER
  DEFENSIBLE: no finite tradition exhaustively possesses the absolute; hard contradictions must
    be acknowledged rather than harmonised; deep disagreement makes the procedural virtues
    OBLIGATORY. That supports pluralism as an ETHIC OF ENGAGEMENT.
  THE DEFEATER THAT MUST BE CARRIED: D'Costa's charge that the pluralist's own second-order
    account is itself a privileged framework. NO POSITION IN THIS CLAUSE IS NEUTRAL - which is
    why the final question is not WHO IS NEUTRAL but WHOSE COMMITMENT IS HELD CORRIGIBLY.
