# UPSC CSE 2013–2018 Source Manifest

> Scope: source validation only. No questions were solved, and no `solved_pyqs` catalogue/status file was edited.

## Result

**PASS** — all 54 merit question-paper slots and all 11 final Prelims answer-key records are present, PDF-readable, hashed, and identity-verified.

- Question papers: **54/54** (45 newly downloaded under `2013`–`2017`; 9 existing held paths reused for 2018).
- Answer keys: **11/11** (9 existing 2013–2017 records; 2 newly downloaded 2018 final keys).
- Image-only PDFs with no embedded text: **43**; these are explicitly marked and were checked with local OCR/visual evidence.
- Actual generic/reused official-URL histories with multiple archived PDF digests: **20**. Every affected record is pinned to an exact Wayback timestamp and digest.
- Wrong-year downloads: **0**. Invalid files removed: **0**. Blockers: **none**.

## Provenance tiers

- `T1_DIRECT_OFFICIAL_UPSC_REPOSITORY_HELD_COPY`: existing 2018 question papers originally obtained from UPSC; local copies have an earlier OCR layer, so local hashes differ from official-source hashes while page rasters remain verified.
- `T2_INTERNET_ARCHIVE_CAPTURE_OF_OFFICIAL_UPSC`: 2013–2017 question papers; exact timestamped Wayback capture of an official UPSC URL, resolved by the local file’s Wayback SHA-1 digest.
- `T3_OFFICIAL_KEY_MIRROR_IAS_EXAM_PORTAL`: Commission final answer-key scans mirrored by IAS Exam Portal; mirror bytes were re-downloaded and SHA-256 matched.

## Collision and identity findings

- Generic UPSC filenames such as `Paper-II.pdf`, `GS1_0.pdf`, `ESSAY.pdf`, and `General Studies-II.pdf` are not safe identifiers by themselves. The manifest uses the exact timestamped capture plus digest and printed/OCR identity evidence.
- No local file proved to be a wrong-year paper. No file was deleted or replaced.
- The nine held 2018 question papers were not duplicated into the new `2018` folder; only the two requested final answer keys were added there.

## Validation method

1. Opened every local PDF with PyMuPDF and recorded page count.
2. Computed SHA-256 for every local file.
3. Used embedded text where present; where absent, recorded `image-only` and used local OCR/visual cover and question evidence.
4. Resolved 2013–2017 official UPSC originals through the Wayback CDX digest index; the returned digest is computed from the local bytes, preventing generic-filename substitution.
5. Re-fetched all answer keys from the IAS Exam Portal PDF URLs and confirmed byte-for-byte SHA-256 equality; downloaded only the two 2018 keys.
6. Compared each held 2018 paper with a timestamped capture of its official UPSC URL; page counts and visual page content match.
