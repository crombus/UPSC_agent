## Card focus per subject (my judgement of the most useful flashcards)

- **Geography** — Landform/feature cards (definition, formation process, India + world examples, location), climate-type cards (distribution, temperature/rainfall regime, vegetation, economy, Köppen letters), ocean current/tide/salinity facts, rock/mineral/soil cards, population/migration/settlement concepts and theories, map-based "where is it" cards, process → landform causal chains, formulas/thresholds, India physical/economic geography facts.
- **Environment and Ecology** — Ecology concepts and formulas (pyramids, cycles, succession), biodiversity hotspots/IUCN categories/species, protected-area and Ramsar/biosphere entries, Acts and Rules (year, schedules, authorities), conventions/protocols (year, venue, key provision), climate-science and negotiation milestones, pollution standards, institutions, schemes, current-affairs trackers.
- **Science and Technology** — Technology cards (what it is, how it works, applications, Indian missions/programmes, institutions), ISRO/DRDO systems and launches, nuclear/defence programme facts, digital/AI/quantum/semiconductor policies, biotech and genetics concepts, IPR, EV/battery/drone/materials, general-science fundamentals (physics, chemistry, biology) as concept + example cards, institutions, Nobel/discoveries.
- **Governance** — Concept and framework cards (good governance, e-governance models, citizen charter, social audit), Acts and schemes, commissions/committees (2nd ARC recommendations), regulators, missions (Karmayogi), DPI/data governance, local governance and service delivery tools, M&E concepts, sports governance institutions.
- **International Relations** — Foreign-policy doctrines, neighbourhood and great-power relations (key agreements, disputes, dates, projects), regional/minilateral groupings (members, year, HQ, purpose), UN/international institutions, connectivity corridors, diaspora and soft-power instruments, trade agreements and frameworks, bilateral fact cards.
- **Social Justice** — Constitutional provisions and Acts per vulnerable group, commissions/committees, schemes (year, ministry, objective, components), indicators and indices, institutions, landmark judgments, concept cards (inclusion, welfare state), targeting/convergence/data-architecture items.
- **Indian Society** — Concept cards (diversity, caste, tribe, family, kinship, globalisation effects), sociologist/thinker cards with core claim, population and census facts, social-movement and women-organisation cards, Acts and policies, comparison pairs (communalism/regionalism/secularism), modernisation theories.
- **Ethics** — Concept definitions (ethics, values, attitude, aptitude, emotional intelligence), thinker–doctrine cards (Indian and Western), moral-theory cards with objections and replies, public-service values, probity/accountability concepts, laws and institutions (RTI, Lokpal, PCA, whistle-blower), codes, case-study method steps and framework cards, quotation-ready lines.
- **Ancient Indian History** — Event/ruler/dynasty/site cards (date, place, cause, course, significance), source/evidence cards (inscriptions, texts), chronology ladders, religion and philosophy movements, society/economy/administration cards, art and architecture by period, close-option comparison pairs.
- **Medieval Indian History** — Dynasty/ruler/battle/treaty cards, administrative and revenue systems (terms, definitions), Bhakti/Sufi cards, architecture and culture cards, regional kingdoms, economy/trade cards, chronology ladders, comparison pairs.
- **Modern Indian History** — Event/Act/movement/personality cards (date, cause, course, outcome, significance), British policy and Acts (year, provisions), revolts and national-movement phases, social-reform movements and leaders, constitutional development milestones, economic-impact cards, chronology ladders, comparison pairs.
- **World History** — Revolution/war/treaty/movement cards (date, cause, course, consequence), ideologies and thinkers, empire/colonialism/decolonisation cards, redrawing-of-boundaries cards, industrial and political change, chronology ladders, comparison pairs.
- **Indian Art and Culture** — Architecture style/monument cards, sculpture and painting schools, dance/music/theatre forms (state, features, exponents), literature and language cards, UNESCO/GI/intangible-heritage entries, institutions and schemes, religion/philosophy cultural cards, festival and craft cards, chronology.
- **Philosophy** — School/thinker/concept cards in English-first terminology with Sanskrit/Pali IAST in parentheses; doctrine → argument → objection → reply chains; Indian vs Western comparison pairs; proofs, problem of evil, religious language, notions of God; socio-political concepts; key texts and quotations from the keyword bank; PYQ-trap cards.
- **Political Theory** — Thinker–idea cards (work, central claim, objection, reply), concept cards (liberty, equality, justice, rights, democracy, state, sovereignty, power), ideologies, theory comparisons, constitutional-theory and global-politics frameworks, key terms and contrasts.
