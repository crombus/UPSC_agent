MUST REMEMBER: Shastri became Prime Minister on 9 June 1964; the 1965 war led to the Tashkent
  Declaration on 10 January 1966 and his death on 11 January; Indira Gandhi took office on 24
  January, devaluation followed on 6 June, the 1967 election weakened Congress, the party
  split and fourteen banks were nationalised in 1969, privy purses ended constitutionally in
  1971, and the Bangladesh war and Simla Agreement followed in 1971-72.
