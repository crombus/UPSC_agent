+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 01 — CHOOSE                                                                |
| Select the prompt whose terms, tension, thesis and examples can be               |
| controlled.                                                                      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 02 — DECODE                                                                |
| Define key terms in context and locate the hidden contrast or relationship.      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 03 — THESIS                                                                |
| State a qualified arguable position by the end of the introduction.              |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 04 — PLAN                                                                  |
| Build distinct reason, mechanism, consequence, limitation and response           |
| moves.                                                                           |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 05 — INTRODUCTION                                                          |
| Enter the issue directly; avoid dictionary openings and decorative               |
| quotations.                                                                      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 06 — PARAGRAPH ARCHITECTURE                                                |
| Use claim, explanation, safe example and explicit link to thesis.                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 07 — COHERENCE                                                             |
| Order paragraphs so each advances rather than repeats the argument.              |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 08 — COUNTERARGUMENT                                                       |
| Present a genuine objection or limit and answer or accommodate it.               |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 09 — EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE                                                   |
| Use defensible illustrations and never invent statistics or quotations.          |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 10 — STYLE                                                                 |
| Prefer plain formal English, controlled sentence length and precise              |
| connectors.                                                                      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 11 — WORD AND TIME CONTROL                                                 |
| Target about 600 words and reserve a final revision window.                      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
        |
        v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 12 — REVISION AND VERDICT                                                  |
| Check thesis, paragraph jobs, grammar, unsupported claims, count and             |
| conclusion.                                                                      |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
