ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Category boundary
TRIBE -> no single universal sociological checklist
SCHEDULED TRIBE -> constitutional notification under Article 342
CONTINUUM -> historical thesis about long caste-society interaction
RULE -> the thesis never overrides the legal category

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Three policy positions
ELWIN -> isolation, shield from market and settler exploitation
GHURYE -> assimilation, absorption as backward Hindus
NEHRU -> integration, development on tribals' own genius
OUTCOME -> none fully implemented, uneven acculturation followed

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Panchsheel content
OWN GENIUS -> development on tribal terms
LAND AND FOREST -> rights retained rather than surrendered
SERVICES -> education, health and administration supplied
REJECTED -> both museum isolation and forced absorption

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Why displacement recurs
RESOURCE GEOGRAPHY -> minerals, forests and hydel in tribal regions
PROJECT SITING -> mining, dams and industrial corridors
LOSS -> subsistence base and identity anchored in that land
EXPERIENCE -> development felt as trade-off, not shared gain

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Two mechanisms of land loss
DISPLACEMENT -> acute, project-driven, single event
LAND ALIENATION -> slow transfer through debt or encroachment
REMEDY 1 -> resettlement design and livelihood restoration
REMEDY 2 -> restriction and restoration of transferred land

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Rehabilitation test
LAND FOR LAND -> was an equivalent land base restored
LIVELIHOOD -> was the earning system rebuilt, not just paid for
COMMUNITY -> was the village relocated intact or scattered
VERDICT -> restoration means gain, compensation alone means loss

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Vulnerability card
SOURCE -> Ministry of Tribal Affairs list dated 9 July 2024
COUNT -> 75 Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups
SPREAD -> 18 States and the UT of Andaman and Nicobar Islands
CRITERIA -> pre-agricultural technology, low literacy, subsistence economy

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Aggregation split
AGGREGATE FOR -> notification, entitlement portability, atrocity protection
DISAGGREGATE FOR -> livelihood design and scheme targeting
VARIATION -> forest-dependent, settled cultivator, urban tribal worker
CONTEXT -> Fifth Schedule belts against Sixth Schedule polities

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Knowledge domains
ECOLOGICAL -> seasonal indicators, fire and fallow practice
MEDICINAL -> plant remedies held in practitioner lineages
GOVERNANCE -> customary rules on forest produce, grazing, water
CRAFT AND COSMOLOGY -> apprenticeship technique and sacred groves

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Mode contrast
EMBEDDED -> held in practice, not abstracted into models
COLLECTIVE -> authored by a community, not an individual
ORAL AND PLACE-SPECIFIC -> transmitted by speech, indexed to one tract
VALIDATION -> long-run use rather than controlled testing

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Erosion and prohibition
DISPLACEMENT -> severs the ecology the knowledge was indexed to
SCHOOLING AND MARKET -> break oral transmission chains
PROHIBITION 1 -> no substitute for public health or formal education
PROHIBITION 2 -> no unverified remedy attributed to a named tribe

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/12: Tribal answer spine
OPEN -> state the opinion and the framing being tested
BODY -> displacement axis, then rehabilitation adequacy
ADD -> what the framing omits: identity, services, self-governance
CLOSE -> graded verdict with the dated official evidence
