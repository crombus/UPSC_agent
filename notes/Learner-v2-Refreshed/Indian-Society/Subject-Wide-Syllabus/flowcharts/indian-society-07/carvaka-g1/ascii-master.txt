ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Organisational layering
REFORM ERA -> elite-led advocacy on widow remarriage and child marriage
EARLY NATIONAL -> Women's Indian Association, then All India Women's Conference 1927
RIGHTS ERA -> National Federation of Indian Women on wages and dowry
ECONOMIC ERA -> Self Employed Women's Association, then federated self-help groups

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Precedence card
EARLIER BODY -> Women's Indian Association
FOUNDED 1927 -> All India Women's Conference, education first
LATER SCOPE -> legal and social reform advocacy
PROHIBITION -> never describe the 1927 body as India's first

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Four organisational models
RIGHTS ADVOCACY -> All India Women's Conference platform work
WAGE AND DOWRY -> National Federation of Indian Women mobilisation
TRADE UNION -> Self Employed Women's Association collective bargaining
SAVINGS FEDERATION -> self-help groups federating upward

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Trichotomy definitions
EQUALITY -> equal rights, status and opportunity as the baseline
EQUITY -> context-responsive measures against unequal starting conditions
EMPOWERMENT -> expanded agency, capability, resources and voice
RULE -> a right or a coverage figure never proves the third term

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Where the chain stalls
INSTRUMENT EXISTS -> is it reachable by this woman at all
REACHED -> was it actually taken up rather than merely offered
USED -> did the use change a recognised decision
STALL POINT -> coverage without uptake, or uptake without follow-through

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Time and space constraints
TIME -> unpaid care and domestic work compress schooling and paid work
SPACE -> mobility limits, harassment and safety condition public presence
WORK -> workplace access and treatment shape continuity of employment
DIGITAL -> internet and device access shape information and market reach

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Access indicator card
SOURCE -> National Family Health Survey round five, 2019-21
ACCOUNT USE -> 78.6 per cent of women aged 15-49 with an account they used
INTERNET -> 33.3 per cent had ever used the internet
STATUS -> dated reach indicators; round six was released 29 May 2026

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Intersectional reach
UNIVERSAL FRAMING -> women treated as one analytical category
CRITIQUE -> caste, class, tribe and religion differentiate constraint
EVIDENCE -> separate Dalit and tribal women's organisations emerged
READING -> a corrective to reach, not a fragmentation of the movement

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Three boundary cases
EQUALITY WITHOUT EQUITY -> full legal rights, no income or mobility support
EQUITY WITHOUT EMPOWERMENT -> credit received, loan use controlled by another
FULL CHAIN -> credit, collective bargaining, then an independent decision
COMPOUNDED -> caste-based wage discrimination added to gender constraint

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Gig-work verdict
GAIN -> wider entry, flexible hours, independent earning opportunity
COST -> income volatility, algorithmic control, thin social protection
TEST -> do earnings, safety and voice improve together
BOUNDARY -> platform legal status is owned by Social Justice

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Causal restraint
REPORTED CATEGORY -> an outcome record, never a proof of cause
STRESSORS -> candidate pathways, examined without attribution
FRAME -> National Suicide Prevention Strategy of 2022, surveillance to resilience
CLOSE -> support access and non-stigmatising discussion

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/12: Women and organisations spine
OPEN -> define the exact term the directive names
BODY -> organisational mechanism, then reach across caste and class
TEST -> instrument reached, taken up, and converted into a decision
CLOSE -> agency verdict with the coverage-is-not-agency caveat
