ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Two measurement questions
WELFARE VIEW -> what income, goods or benefits were received
AGENCY VIEW -> what the person can actually do, choose and be
SEN -> real freedoms to achieve valued functionings
RULE -> receipt is an input and never by itself an outcome
MUST REMEMBER: Social empowerment means expanding capabilities, voice, resources, recognition,
  representation, legal agency and institutional access for groups facing structural
  exclusion; redistribution, recognition, participation and autonomy are complementary but not
  interchangeable routes.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Three-stage chain
ENTITLEMENT -> reserved seat, targeted scheme, statutory right
CONVERSION FACTOR -> schooling, information, mobility, health, stigma
OUTCOME -> mobility, income, recognised voice
DIAGNOSIS -> name the stage before naming the remedy

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Conversion-factor checklist
PRIOR SCHOOLING -> is the entitlement usable at the required standard
INFORMATION -> does the household know the entitlement exists
REACH -> can it be accessed without prohibitive travel or cost
STIGMA -> does social cost deter uptake even when access exists

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Mobility types
OPEN SYSTEM -> position follows achievement
CLOSED SYSTEM -> position follows birth-ascribed status
FORMAL MOBILITY -> documented income, education or occupation change
SUBSTANTIVE MOBILITY -> that change plus recognition and freedom from stigma

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Phule's three axes
CASTE -> critique of Brahminical ritual dominance
GENDER -> girls' education with Savitribai Phule
PEASANT CLASS -> critique of exploitative agrarian relations
ORGANISATION -> Satyashodhak Samaj as the reform vehicle

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Graded inequality
CLAIM -> caste is not benign division of labour
STRUCTURE -> each caste looks down and simultaneously aspires upward
SANCTION -> religious sanction on endogamy reproduces the grade
CONSEQUENCE -> the system supplies defenders at every level

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Reform against annihilation
PIECEMEAL REFORM -> one group improves its own relative position
LIMIT -> the graded structure survives the improvement
ANNIHILATION -> dismantle caste as an institution, not adjust it
BOUNDARY -> constitutional doctrine routed to Polity and Social Justice

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Two diagnoses compared
PHULE -> breadth: caste, gender and peasant class as one structure
AMBEDKAR -> depth: why the structure reproduces itself
SEN -> site: where an intervention must act to change a life
USE -> three distinct tools, never one merged reform narrative

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Locating the gap
ENTITLEMENT EXISTS -> comprehensive equity and social-justice policy
CONVERSION FAILS -> schooling, information, distance, stigma
OUTCOME SHORTFALL -> benefit not fully realised by the target group
REMEDY -> capability building, not entitlement expansion alone

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Estimate card
SOURCE -> NITI Aayog discussion paper of 2024
TITLE -> Multidimensional Poverty in India since 2005-06
ESTIMATE -> 11.28 per cent multidimensional poverty in 2022-23
EXITS -> about 24.8 crore persons between 2013-14 and 2022-23
CLOSE DISTINCTION: Welfare is not empowerment, formal inclusion is not substantive access,
  equality is not uniform treatment, reservation is not the whole of social justice, and
  constitutional rights, statutory commissions, executive schemes and community institutions
  have different mandates.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Three boundary cases
ENTITLEMENT WITHOUT CAPABILITY -> admitted seat, weak prior schooling
CAPABILITY BUILT -> bridge course and mentoring before and during study
FORMAL WITHOUT SUBSTANTIVE -> higher income, continuing social exclusion
READING -> each case fixes one link in the conversion chain
EVIDENCE LIMIT: Analyse SC, ST, OBC, minority, disability, transgender, elderly and other
  experiences without merging them; use Articles 14-17, 21, 38, 46 and relevant
  commissions/laws precisely, separating entitlement, implementation, uptake and outcome.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/12: Empowerment answer spine
OPEN -> define empowerment as capability and agency, not receipt
BODY -> entitlement, conversion factor and outcome named in order
TEST -> boundary cases that break an entitlement-only claim
CLOSE -> capability-building verdict with a dated estimate caveat
