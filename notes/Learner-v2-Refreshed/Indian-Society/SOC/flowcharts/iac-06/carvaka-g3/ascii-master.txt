ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Population as a social process
ENTRY -> schooling, marriage timing, desired family size, son preference
SERVICES -> contraceptive access and maternal and child health provision
EXCLUDED -> stage-based transition model and dividend theory owned by Geography
OUTPUT -> a social explanation, not a model recital
MUST REMEMBER: Population analysis separates size, growth, fertility, mortality, age
  structure, sex composition, density, distribution, migration and human capability;
  demographic transition and dividend are conditional processes shaped by health, education,
  gender agency and employment.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Determinant interaction
EDUCATION -> later marriage plus contraceptive knowledge and wider options
MARRIAGE TIMING -> compressed reproductive window, better maternal outcomes
COST OF CHILDREN -> urban housing, schooling and foregone earnings
RULE -> determinants interact; no single factor is the sole cause

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Attitude and metric
SON PREFERENCE -> attitude rooted in inheritance and old-age expectations
SEX RATIO AT BIRTH -> the measurable demographic outcome
COEXISTENCE -> smaller desired family size with continued son-seeking
OWNERSHIP -> sex-selection law belongs to Social Justice and Polity

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Policy card
DOCUMENT -> National Population Policy, 2000, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
IMMEDIATE -> address unmet contraceptive need
MEDIUM TERM -> bring Total Fertility Rate to replacement level
LONG TERM -> stable population by around 2045 with sustainable development

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Evidence-status ladder
NFHS-6 -> released 29 May 2026, total 2.0, urban 1.6, rural 2.1, provisional
NFHS-5 -> 2019-21 round, Total Fertility Rate 2.0, historical comparator
CENSUS -> 2011 is the last completed full count; Census 2027 is prospective
PROHIBITION -> no survey average may be quoted as a state or current stock

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Aggregate against divergence
NATIONAL -> a weighted summary of states in different transition phases
BELOW REPLACEMENT -> ageing pressure and workforce-shrinkage risk
ABOVE REPLACEMENT -> young structure with an open dividend window
VERDICT -> the honest answer is state-graded, not nation-uniform
CLOSE DISTINCTION: Population growth is not fertility, replacement fertility is not zero
  growth, density is not pressure, sex ratio at birth is not overall sex ratio, Census stock
  is not survey flow, and demographic dividend is an opportunity rather than an automatic
  bonus.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Winter and dividend
DEMOGRAPHIC WINTER -> sustained sub-replacement fertility with ageing and decline
DEMOGRAPHIC DIVIDEND -> largest working-age share, growth-favourable window
RELATION -> near-opposite phases of the same transition
CAUTION -> a large country can display both across its regions

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Ageing double reading
ACHIEVEMENT -> longer life from successful mortality and health transition
BURDEN -> rising old-age dependency and workforce recomposition
SUPPORT -> family-based care weakened by nuclearisation
GAP -> formal old-age security not yet scaled where fertility fell earliest

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Two transition frameworks
FIRST TRANSITION -> mortality decline followed by fertility decline
SECOND TRANSITION -> value and family-formation shift below replacement
ORIGIN -> the second framework is largely European in derivation
STATUS -> an analytical lens, never an established Indian diagnosis

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Migration offset
RECEIVING REGION -> working-age inflow cushions workforce shrinkage
SENDING REGION -> elderly parents left with thinner household support
NET EFFECT -> the ageing problem is redistributed, not removed
ANSWER USE -> state the regional trade-off before recommending mobility

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Population education design
CONTENT -> age-appropriate, scientific and rights-respecting instruction
OBJECTIVES -> health information, gender equality, voluntary choice, resource awareness
DELIVERY -> schools, community outreach and health services
SAFEGUARD -> capability building, never a demographic target

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/12: Population answer spine
OPEN -> define the term precisely and name the social mechanism
BODY -> dated anchor, then immediate disaggregation by state
TEST -> ageing pressure against continuing dividend conditions
CLOSE -> graded verdict with the survey-versus-census caveat
EVIDENCE LIMIT: Census 2011 is the latest completed census baseline as of review, while SRS,
  NFHS-5 and projections have different dates and methods; compare Kerala/Tamil Nadu ageing,
  EAG-state youth and migration destinations without causal claims from state averages alone.
