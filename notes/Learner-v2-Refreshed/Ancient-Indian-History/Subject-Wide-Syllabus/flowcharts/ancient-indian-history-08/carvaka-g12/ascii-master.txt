Do not turn remembered rivers into precise political borders.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/9: Early to Later Vedic transformation
EARLY VEDIC                         LATER VEDIC
north-west/Sapta-Sindhu             upper Ganga-Yamuna and eastward movement
jana and kin leadership             janapada, stronger monarchy and ratnins
cattle-centred mixed economy        plough agriculture, surplus and crafts
fluid differentiation               firmer varna/patriarchy and gotra
deity praise and yajna              royal ritual and Upanishadic questioning
VERDICT: cumulative transformation with regional variation, not abrupt rupture.
