ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Script, seals and religion: evidence ladder
SECURE: short signs, seals/tablets, motifs, ritual installations, burials, figurines
PROBABLE: identity/transaction marking and differentiated ritual practices
DISPUTED: script language; proto-Shiva; mother-goddess system; universal fire altars
REJECT: reading later Hindu doctrine directly into every Harappan image.
                         |
                         v
Undeciphered script blocks direct access to office, belief and political vocabulary.
Use object + context + distribution before analogy.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Evidence ladder and confidence control
EVIDENCE LADDER
1. Separate excavated fact from function label and social interpretation.
2. Anchor every claim in site, phase, context, comparison and regional variation.
3. The undeciphered script limits named claims about language, rulers and doctrine.
VERDICT: Harappan history is strongest on material systems, weakest on named ideas.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/12: Examiner traps and contested boundaries
CLOSE DISTINCTIONS
1. Citadel, granary, dockyard, Priest-King and Pashupati are modern labels.
2. Horse evidence is rare and disputed; standardisation does not prove one empire.
3. Deurbanisation was regional and multi-causal, not one invasion or drought.
VERDICT: State the secure evidence first, then grade each interpretation.
