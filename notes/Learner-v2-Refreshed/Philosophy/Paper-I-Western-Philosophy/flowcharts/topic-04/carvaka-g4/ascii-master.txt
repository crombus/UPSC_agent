ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/10: Critical starting problem and the rationalism-empiricism synthesis
CENTRAL QUESTION: How can cognition be informative, necessary and objectively valid?
RATIONALISM -> a priori necessity, but dogmatic claims about soul, world and God
EMPIRICISM / HUME -> sensory content, but no experienced necessary connection
KANTIAN CRITIQUE -> sensory matter + a priori form, restricted to possible experience
COPERNICAN REVOLUTION -> objects AS EXPERIENCED conform to cognitive conditions
SENSIBILITY -> intuitions in space/time -> UNDERSTANDING -> categories and objects
UNDERSTANDING -> REASON -> Ideas of Soul, World and God; drive to the unconditioned
TRANSCENDENTAL = conditions of possible experience; TRANSCENDENT = beyond experience
RESULT -> empirical realism + transcendental idealism + limit on theoretical reason
ANSWER LINE -> Kant secures objectivity by making its a priori conditions mind-given.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/10: Synthetic a priori judgments and the Copernican explanation
ANALYTIC -> predicate contained in subject; denial contradicts the concept
SYNTHETIC -> predicate adds content; denial is not self-contradictory
A PRIORI -> necessary/universal warrant independent of particular experience
A POSTERIORI -> empirical, informative and contingent warrant
ANALYTIC A PRIORI -> 'Bodies are extended'; ANALYTIC A POSTERIORI -> no Kantian class
SYNTHETIC A POSTERIORI -> 'This table is brown'; informative but contingent
SYNTHETIC A PRIORI -> 7 + 5 = 12; every event has a cause; informative + necessary
HOW POSSIBLE? -> the mind supplies space, time and categories to every appearance
MATHEMATICS / FUNDAMENTAL SCIENCE -> valid for objects of possible experience
LIMIT -> no inference from these forms to things in themselves or transcendent objects

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/10: Transcendental Aesthetic: space and time as forms of intuition
FORM OF INTUITION -> a priori way in which sensory matter can be given
SPACE -> pure form of OUTER sense; coexistence, externality and geometry
TIME -> pure form of INNER sense; succession and all representations
A PRIORI ARGUMENT -> spatial/temporal ordering presupposes, not produces, the forms
NECESSITY ARGUMENT -> empty space/time is representable; objects without them are not
INTUITION ARGUMENT -> one space/time; regions and periods are limitations within it
INFINITY ARGUMENT -> magnitude is represented as a given whole, not instances under a concept
EMPIRICALLY REAL -> objectively valid for every appearance and possible experience
TRANSCENDENTALLY IDEAL -> not properties of things in themselves
OBJECTION -> modern geometry/science pressures examples, not the whole transcendental strategy

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/10: Categories, synthesis and transcendental unity of apperception
QUANTITY -> Unity | Plurality | Totality
QUALITY -> Reality | Negation | Limitation
RELATION -> Inherence/Subsistence | Causality/Dependence | Community/Reciprocity
MODALITY -> Possibility/Impossibility | Existence/Non-existence | Necessity/Contingency
METAPHYSICAL DEDUCTION -> identifies categories from functions of judgment
TRANSCENDENTAL DEDUCTION -> justifies their objective application
MANIFOLD -> SYNTHESIS under rules -> 'I THINK' can accompany each representation
APPERCEPTION -> formal unity of self-consciousness, not empirical ego or soul-substance
OBJECTIVE UNITY -> the same rule unites representations as one experience of objects
LIMIT -> categories constitute appearances only; they do not picture or determine noumena

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/10: Schematism: temporal bridge from pure category to appearance
PROBLEM -> pure intellectual category and sensible particular appearance are heterogeneous
SCHEMA -> rule/procedure of productive imagination; never a determinate mental image
WHY TIME? -> it is a priori like the category and contains every representation
QUANTITY / time-series -> NUMBER through successive homogeneous addition
QUALITY / time-content -> DEGREE or intensive magnitude
RELATION / time-order -> permanence, succession by rule, reciprocal coexistence
MODALITY / time-scope -> some time, determinate time, all times
DEDUCTION proves THAT categories apply; SCHEMATISM explains HOW they apply
UNSCHEMATISED CATEGORY -> logical function without objective sense for appearances
RESIDUAL -> Kant calls the mediating operation an art concealed in the human soul

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/10: Analogies of Experience and Kant's precise answer to Hume
MASTER PREMISE -> time itself cannot be perceived; objective time needs proxies
FIRST ANALOGY / duration -> substance persists while its states alter
SECOND ANALOGY / succession -> every event follows according to a causal rule
THIRD ANALOGY / simultaneity -> substances coexist through reciprocal community
HOUSE -> viewing order is reversible; succession belongs to my apprehension
SHIP DOWNSTREAM -> order is irreversible; an objective event requires a rule
ANTI-HUME MOVE -> causality is a condition of judging objective succession, not habit
QUALIFICATION -> particular causal laws remain empirical and require investigation
STRAWSON OBJECTION -> involuntary order does not automatically prove natural necessity
VERDICT -> Kant secures entitlement to causality more clearly than any particular law

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/10: Phenomena, noumena, empirical realism and the idealism boundary
PHENOMENON -> object as given in space/time and thought through categories
POSSIBLE EXPERIENCE -> knowable, public, causal and scientifically investigable domain
NOUMENON NEGATIVELY -> thing considered apart from sensible intuition; limiting concept
NOUMENON IS THINKABLE, NOT KNOWABLE -> no theoretical predicates from the categories
TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM -> our forms condition appearances
EMPIRICAL REALISM -> external objects are real within experience, not private illusions
REFUTATION -> determinate inner time-awareness requires an empirically external permanent
TWO-ASPECT READING -> one object considered as appearance and independently of our access
HEGEL/JACOBI PRESSURE -> boundary knowledge and affection threaten internal consistency
SAFE VERDICT -> negative limitation is defensible; positive noumenal causation is not

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/10: Ideas of Reason, regulative unity and natural transcendental illusion
UNDERSTANDING -> supplies rules for appearances; REASON -> seeks the unconditioned
SOUL -> projected complete unity of inner conditions
WORLD -> projected complete totality of cosmological conditions
GOD -> projected complete ground of all conditions and reality
REGULATIVE USE -> guide inquiry toward systematic unity; legitimate 'as if'
CONSTITUTIVE USE -> claim the Idea determines a known object; illegitimate
TRANSCENDENTAL ILLUSION -> natural and persistent drive to reify completion
PARALOGISM -> logical 'I think' is misread as simple, substantial, enduring soul
VALUE -> systematic research and explanatory integration
DANGER -> paralogisms, antinomies and speculative theology

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/10: Four cosmological antinomies and their differentiated resolutions
1 THESIS -> beginning in time + spatial limit | ANTITHESIS -> infinite time/space
2 THESIS -> composites end in simple parts | ANTITHESIS -> nothing simple exists
3 THESIS -> freedom besides nature | ANTITHESIS -> universal natural causality
4 THESIS -> absolutely necessary being | ANTITHESIS -> no necessary being
SOURCE OF CONFLICT -> categories applied to the world-series as a completed whole
MATHEMATICAL 1-2 -> reject shared assumption that a finite/infinite whole is given
DYNAMICAL 3-4 -> phenomenal conditions and intelligible standpoint can be distinguished
FREEDOM -> thinkable noumenally while every appearance remains naturally determined
NECESSARY GROUND -> thinkable boundary, not theoretical proof of God
SIGNIFICANCE -> indirect case for idealism and limit of speculative metaphysics

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/10: God-proof critique, evaluation ledger, traps, PYQs and answer spine
ONTOLOGICAL -> perfect concept includes existence | KANT -> existence adds no content
100 possible/actual thalers -> same concept; actuality cannot be extracted analytically
COSMOLOGICAL -> contingent exists, therefore necessary God | illicit causal extension
COSMOLOGICAL DEPENDENCE -> necessary being = ens realissimum repeats ontological proof
DESIGN -> order implies designer | at most an architect, not necessary creator
DESIGN DEPENDENCE -> creator/necessity needs cosmological then ontological steps
BOUNDARY -> theoretical proof fails; this is not a demonstration of atheism
COMPARISON -> rationalist necessity + empiricist content under critical limitation
PYQ ROUTES -> synthesis, space/time, apperception, antinomies, Hume and God proofs
ANSWER SPINE -> thesis -> doctrine -> argument -> objection/reply -> qualified verdict
FINAL VERDICT -> Kant gains phenomenal objectivity at the cost of noumenal agnosticism
