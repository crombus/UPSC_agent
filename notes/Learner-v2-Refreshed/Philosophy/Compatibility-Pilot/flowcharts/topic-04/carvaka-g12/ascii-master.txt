ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/8: 2. Seven padārthas
PADĀRTHA: whatever is nameable and knowable
 1 DRAVYA: earth, water, fire, air, ākāśa, time, space, self, mind
 2 GUṆA: quality; depends on substance, does not itself possess quality/action
 3 KARMA: motion/action in a substance
 4 SĀMĀNYA: real universal, one-in-many
 5 VIŚEṢA: ultimate particularity individuating eternal atoms and selves
 6 SAMAVĀYA: eternal inherence; whole-parts, universal-particular, quality-substance
 7 ABHĀVA: absence — prior, posterior, mutual, absolute
 CAUTION: samavāya is not conjunction (saṃyoga), which is separable.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/8: 3. Four pramāṇas and perception
PRAMĀṆAS
 +-> pratyakṣa: sense-object contact -> nirvikalpaka -> savikalpaka recognition
 +-> anumāna: sign (liṅga/hetu) through vyāpti to sādhya
 +-> upamāna: knowledge through similarity and prior description
 +-> śabda: trustworthy utterance, ultimately āpta testimony
                         |
 PERCEPTION TYPES: external/internal | ordinary/alaukika
 alaukika: sāmānyalakṣaṇa, jñānalakṣaṇa, yogaja
 ERROR is mislocation of a real qualifier: anyathākhyāti.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/8: 4. Inference engine
SVĀRTHĀNUMĀNA: inference for oneself | PARĀRTHĀNUMĀNA: demonstrated to another
 five members:
 1 pratijñā: hill has fire
 2 hetu: because it has smoke
 3 udāharaṇa: wherever smoke, fire — as kitchen
 4 upanaya: hill has such smoke
 5 nigamana: therefore hill has fire
                         |
 pakṣadharmatā + vyāpti + uncontradicted application -> conclusion
 HETVĀBHĀSA: savyabhicāra, viruddha, satpratipakṣa, asiddha, bādhita.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/8: 5. Causation and atomism
ASATKĀRYAVĀDA / ĀRAMBHAVĀDA: effect is a new beginning, not pre-existent as effect
 causes: samavāyi (inherent) | asamavāyi (non-inherent) | nimitta (efficient)
                         |
 earth/water/fire/air paramāṇu -> dyad -> triad -> gross composite
                         |
 pralaya: composites dissolve; eternal atoms, selves, space, time remain
 sṛṣṭi: Īśvara activates atoms according to adṛṣṭa/karma
 TRAP: God orders atoms; God is not the material cause and atoms are not created ex nihilo.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/8: 6. Self, God and liberation
ĀTMAN: eternal, many, all-pervasive substrate of cognition, desire and volition
 MANAS: atomic inner sense linking self to one cognition at a time
                         |
 ĪŚVARA: eternal omniscient efficient cause; author/teacher of Veda;
 coordinates atoms and karmic deserts without abolishing individual agency
                         |
 true knowledge -> doṣa removed -> pravṛtti ceases -> karma exhausted -> apavarga
 apavarga = complete cessation of pain; classical Nyāya does not define it as Brahman-union.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/8: 7. Proof, objection and comparison
UDAYANA ROUTES: kāryāt (effect) | āyojanāt (combination) | dhṛtyādeḥ (support)
 padāt (word-use) | pratyayataḥ (trust) | śruteḥ (Veda) | vākyāt (sentences)
 saṃkhyāviśeṣāt (number) | adṛṣṭāt (moral order)
                         |
 OBJECTION: atoms + karma may make God redundant; bodies expose unequal suffering
 REPLY: unconscious atoms/adṛṣṭa cannot intelligently initiate and allocate order
                         |
 SĀṂKHYA: satkāryavāda, prakṛti material cause | NYĀYA: new effect from atoms
 BUDDHISM: momentary process | NYĀYA: enduring substances and selves.
