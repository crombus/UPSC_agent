ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/8: 2. Knowledge and proof of prakṛti
THREE PRAMĀṆAS: perception | inference | reliable testimony
 prakṛti is inferred, not ordinarily perceived
                         |
 finite effects require cause -> effects share common features
 coordinated world requires common source -> evolutes need an unevolved ground
 pleasure/pain/dullness in effects -> sattva/rajas/tamas in cause
                         |
 PRAKṚTI: beginningless, unmanifest, independent material cause;
 not “matter” alone, but psychophysical source of intellect, senses and elements.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/8: 4. Twenty-five tattvas
PRAKṚTI (1)
   -> MAHAT / BUDDHI (2)
      -> AHAṂKĀRA (3)
         +-> sāttvika -> MANAS (4) + 5 JÑĀNENDRIYAS + 5 KARMENDRIYAS
         +-> tāmasa  -> 5 TANMĀTRAS -> 5 MAHĀBHŪTAS
         +-> rājasa supplies activity to both branches
 PURUṢA (25) stands outside the evolute chain as witness
 COUNT CONTROL: 1 prakṛti + 23 evolutes + puruṣa = 25 tattvas.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/8: 5. Satkāryavāda
EFFECT PRE-EXISTS LATENTLY IN CAUSE
 asadakaraṇāt: non-being cannot be produced
 upādānagrahaṇāt: specific material cause is selected
 sarvasambhavābhāvāt: not everything arises from everything
 śaktasya śakyakaraṇāt: cause produces only what it has power to produce
 kāraṇabhāvāt: effect shares nature of cause
                         |
 pariṇāmavāda: real transformation of prakṛti
 NYĀYA ārambhavāda: new effect | ADVAITA vivarta: apparent transformation.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/8: 6. Why many puruṣas?
PURUṢA EXISTS BECAUSE
 composites serve another | experience needs conscious witness
 objects of three guṇas require non-guṇa experiencer | liberation is conceivable
                         |
 PLURALITY: births/deaths differ; sense capacities and actions are uncoordinated;
 if one puruṣa, one person's liberation or pain would belong to all
                         |
 CONTACT PROBLEM: inactive consciousness cannot literally touch unconscious prakṛti
 analogies: blind-and-lame cooperation | magnet and iron
 pressure remains: how does mere proximity begin purposive evolution?

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/8: 7. Bondage, liberation and critique
AVIVEKA: buddhi's states are appropriated as “I” by reflected consciousness
 experience + karmic dispositions -> repeated embodiment
                         |
 tattva-jñāna / viveka-khyāti: “I am puruṣa, not prakṛti or its evolutes”
                         |
 prakṛti has been seen -> ceases activity for that puruṣa -> KAIVALYA
                         |
 ŚAṂKARA: unconscious prakṛti cannot act for an end; dualism cannot explain contact
 SĀṂKHYA REPLY: teleology is immanent, as milk flows for calf; no creator is needed.
