ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/8: 2. Jīva and ajīva mechanics
JĪVA: consciousness (upayoga) is essential; knowledge is naturally unbounded
 embodied state -> soul expands/contracts to body -> agency + experience
                         |
 AJĪVA FIVEFOLD
 pudgala = matter, including karmic matter | ākāśa = accommodation
 dharma = medium of motion              | adharma = medium of rest
 kāla = condition of change
 TRAP: dharma/adharma here are cosmic media, not moral merit/demerit.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/8: 5. Saptabhaṅgī sevenfold branch
ABOUT OBJECT X, UNDER SPECIFIED STANDPOINT / TIME / MODE:
 1 syād-asti              -> in some respect, X is
 2 syān-nāsti             -> in some respect, X is not
 3 syād-asti-nāsti        -> successively, X is and is not
 4 syād-avaktavyaḥ        -> X is inexpressible under simultaneous demand
 5 syād-asti-avaktavyaḥ   -> X is and is inexpressible
 6 syān-nāsti-avaktavyaḥ  -> X is not and is inexpressible
 7 syād-asti-nāsti-avaktavyaḥ -> X is, is not, and is inexpressible
 CONTRADICTION IS AVOIDED BY DIFFERENT RESPECTS, NOT BY ASSERTING P AND NOT-P SIMPLY.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/8: 7. Objections, replies, comparisons
OBJECTION                         JAIN REPLY
syādvāda is self-refuting           it applies conditionally to itself, not absolutely
seven forms are repetitive          combinations track affirmation, denial, ineffability
anekānta destroys truth             it rejects one-sided absolutism, not determinate truth
karma-matter/soul contact obscure   bondage is beginningless via passions and subtle pudgala
                         |
 BUDDHISM: change without substance | VEDĀNTA: unity beneath plurality
 JAINISM: real substances persist through real modes; plurality is irreducible.
