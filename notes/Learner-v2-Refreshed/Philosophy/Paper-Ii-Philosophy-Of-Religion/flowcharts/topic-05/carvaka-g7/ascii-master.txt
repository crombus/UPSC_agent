+-- SESSION 01: REASON, REVELATION AND FAITH: THE BASIC PROBLEM
|   DEFINITION / TERMS : Reason appeals to publicly assessable inference, revelation to purported divine disclosure, and faith to committed assent or trust that may exceed demonstration.
|   MECHANISM / ARGUMENT: Religious systems combine rational preambles, claims of disclosure and acts of trust in different proportions to justify belief and practice.
|   CONSEQUENCE / CONTRAST: Different weightings produce rationalism, compatibilism, critical faith and fideism, each with a distinct burden of proof.
|   TRAP / ANSWER-USE: Do not equate faith with unsupported belief or revelation with a self-authenticating proof available to every observer.
|   EXAM OPENING: The philosophical problem is not whether religion uses only one source, but how rational inquiry, disclosed authority and committed trust can coexist without contradiction or arbitrariness.

+-- SESSION 03: REVELATION, AUTHORITY AND DISCLOSURE
|   DEFINITION / TERMS : [!IMPORTANT] Revelation is an alleged divine self-disclosure that supplies truth or presence unavailable to unaided reason.
|   MECHANISM / ARGUMENT: Final verdict: A revelation claim becomes philosophically credible only when disclosure, interpretation and public criteria are kept distinct.
|   CONSEQUENCE / CONTRAST: Non-propositional view (Hick): revelation is God's acts/self-presence in history & experience, humanly interpreted — not a set of dictated propositions.
|   TRAP / ANSWER-USE: Do not miss this limiting distinction: epistemic justification of revelation (2024 PYQ).
|   EXAM OPENING: Revelation is an alleged divine self-disclosure that supplies truth or presence unavailable to unaided reason.

+-- SESSION 04: FAITH, BELIEF, TRUST AND COMMITMENT
|   DEFINITION / TERMS : Final verdict: Faith is rationally responsible when it transcends evidence without violating coherence or ignoring defeaters.
|   MECHANISM / ARGUMENT: Core argument/criticism: Aquinas explains faith as intellectual assent commanded by the will, while Kierkegaard stresses passionate commitment under objective uncertainty.
|   CONSEQUENCE / CONTRAST: This reconciles reason & faith — reason establishes the "preambles" (God exists), faith accepts the "mysteries" (Trinity) that exceed but don't contradict reason. ✅ Kierkegaard — faith as the LEAP: faith is a passionate, subjective commitment in "objective uncertainty"; the leap of faith transcends reason (Abraham's "teleological suspension of the ethical").
|   TRAP / ANSWER-USE: Do not miss this limiting distinction: faith is rationally responsible when it transcends evidence without violating coherence or ignoring defeaters.
|   EXAM OPENING: Faith is firm trust and commitment that goes beyond conclusive proof without necessarily opposing reason.
