+-- SESSION 07: OBJECTIONS, COMPARISONS AND EXAM VERDICT
|   DEFINITION / TERMS : A non-theistic religion may retain soteriology, ritual, ethics, authoritative teaching and a sacred orientation while rejecting creator-God theism.
|   MECHANISM / ARGUMENT: Religious functions are supplied by a path, community, authoritative teaching, ethical discipline and liberation goal rather than by divine command or creation.
|   CONSEQUENCE / CONTRAST: The category of religion becomes broader than theism, though critics question whether a non-personal ultimate can sustain worship, dependence and obligation.
|   TRAP / ANSWER-USE: Do not equate non-theism with atheistic materialism or assume that the absence of a creator God removes every sacred or transcendent commitment.
|   EXAM OPENING: God is sufficient for many forms of religion but not conceptually necessary for every religion, as Buddhist traditions demonstrate through non-theistic soteriology and disciplined practice.
