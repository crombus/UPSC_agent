ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/8: 1. Six survival terms
SOUL = alleged enduring subject | SELF = first-person or identity principle
 PERSON = embodied/psychological individual | IDENTITY = later is same person
 IMMORTALITY = survival of death | REBIRTH = renewed conditioned existence
 REINCARNATION = substantial soul re-embodied
 RESURRECTION = divine restoration of embodied person
 LIBERATION = release from bondage or rebirth
                         |
 QUESTIONS MUST BE KEPT DISTINCT
 what survives? -> by what continuity criterion? -> where/when? -> through whose agency?
 -> is survival endless life, renewed embodiment, or freedom from rebirth?
 TERM CONTROL prevents treating Plato, Buddhism, Vedānta and Christianity as one doctrine.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/8: 2. Western immortality arguments
PLATO
 cyclical argument: opposites arise from opposites
 recollection: a priori knowledge suggests prenatal existence
 affinity: invisible rational soul resembles unchanging Forms
 Phaedo final argument: soul essentially bears life and cannot admit death
                         |
 objections: pre-existence != endless survival; harmony analogy; dependence on brain
 DESIRE/JUSTICE ARGUMENTS: fulfilment or moral completion suggests afterlife
 limit: wish and moral need do not by themselves establish metaphysical survival.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/8: 3. Personal identity after death
IDENTITY CANDIDATES
 +-> same immaterial substance
 +-> bodily continuity / resurrection
 +-> memory and psychological continuity (Locke)
 +-> causal narrative continuity
                         |
 REID: brave officer exposes memory transitivity problem
 duplication: one psychology copied twice cannot yield two numerically identical persons
 resurrection requires divine re-identification of this person, not mere replica production
 verdict depends on whether identity needs strict sameness or survival-grade continuity.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/8: 4. Karma, rebirth and Buddhist continuity
INDIAN REBIRTH: karma + saṃskāra/vāsanā -> renewed embodiment in saṃsāra
                         |
 BUDDHISM DENIES ĀTMAN
 five skandhas + dependent origination -> causal stream -> rebirth without transmigrant
 flame-to-flame: neither numerically identical nor wholly unrelated
 intention conditions later fruit; memory is causally produced cognition
                         |
 objection: who receives karma? reply: demand for substance mistakes continuity for identity
 nirvāṇa ends craving and the causal appropriation that renews birth.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/8: 5. Liberation parity bench
SCHOOL             BONDAGE                         LIBERATION
Advaita            avidyā, false identity           jñāna: ātman = Brahman
Viśiṣṭādvaita      karma, dependence obscured       bhakti/prapatti; communion
Sāṃkhya-Yoga       puruṣa-prakṛti confusion         kaivalya through viveka
Nyāya              false knowledge, doṣa, karma     apavarga: cessation of pain
Jainism             karmic pudgala on jīva           kevala-jñāna, siddhahood
Buddhism            craving/ignorance                nirvāṇa
Christianity       sin/death                         resurrection and salvation
 SAME WORD “release” conceals different selves, causes, methods and end-states.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/8: 6. Vedānta and Jain routes
ADVAITA: subtle body carries karma until knowledge; jīvanmukti possible;
 videhamukti ends embodiment, not the ātman's existence
 VIŚIṢṬĀDVAITA: jīva remains real and distinct; grace, bhakti and prapatti lead to God
                         |
 JAINISM: jīva + passions/activity -> āsrava -> bandha
 saṃvara blocks influx -> nirjarā sheds karma -> kevala-jñāna -> mokṣa
                         |
 contrast: Advaita removes ignorance of identity; Rāmānuja perfects dependence;
 Jainism purges material karma from an eternally individual soul.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/8: 7. Paths, objections and ethical meaning
THREE BROAD MEANS: jñāna (knowledge) | bhakti (devotion) | karma (disciplined action)
 Yoga adds meditative discipline; Jainism adds vows/austerity; Buddhism eightfold path
                         |
 objections: disembodied soul lacks individuation | rebirth lacks memory
 liberation as painlessness seems empty | endless survival may be burden, not good
 replies: character/causal continuity can outlast episodic memory;
 liberation is evaluated by freedom from ignorance, craving or sin, not mere duration
 ethical test: doctrine should transform present conduct, not postpone justice to another life.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/8: 8. PYQ answer rail
DEFINE exact survival term -> identify bearer and continuity criterion
 -> explain causal mechanism -> state soteriological end and path
 -> strongest objection (identity, evidence, justice) -> strongest reply
 -> compare one unlike doctrine on same axes -> qualified conclusion
                         |
 ANSWER AXES: self/no-self | body/disembodiment | karma/grace | continuity/identity
 | endless survival/release from rebirth
 CLOSE: survival doctrines are credible only when their ontology of the person,
 continuity mechanism and ethical-liberative purpose support one another.
