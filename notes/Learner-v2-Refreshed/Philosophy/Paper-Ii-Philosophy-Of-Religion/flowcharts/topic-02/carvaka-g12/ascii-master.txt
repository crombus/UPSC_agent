ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/8: 1. Proof architecture
THEISTIC ARGUMENTS START FROM DIFFERENT DATA
 +-> concept of God -> ONTOLOGICAL (a priori)
 +-> contingent/moved/caused world -> COSMOLOGICAL (a posteriori)
 +-> order and purposiveness -> TELEOLOGICAL
 +-> objective obligation -> MORAL
 +-> Indian effect/order/language/karma -> NYĀYA-UDAYANA
                         |
 CONTROL: proofs may be deductive, abductive or cumulative;
 never judge all by the standard of a single demonstrative syllogism.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/8: 2. Ontological line
ANSELM: God = that than which nothing greater can be conceived
 existing in reality > existing only in understanding
 therefore the greatest conceivable being exists in reality
                         |
 DESCARTES: necessary existence belongs to supremely perfect being
 MODAL FORM: possibly necessary God -> necessarily God, if S5 possibility is granted
                         |
 GAUNILO: perfect-island parody | KANT: existence is not a real predicate
 reply: necessary being is unlike contingent islands; pressure shifts to coherent possibility.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/8: 3. Cosmological family
AQUINAS: motion -> unmoved mover | efficient causes -> first cause
 contingency -> necessary being; not “every event has a cause”
 KALĀM: whatever begins has a cause; universe began; therefore a cause
 LEIBNIZ: principle of sufficient reason -> why something rather than nothing?
                         |
 HUME: causal principle may not extend beyond experience; universe may be brute
 RUSSELL: universe is “just there”; composition fallacy
 REPLY: argument concerns contingent totality's explanation, not a member-cause in time.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/8: 4. Design and fine-tuning
PALEY: complex purposive adaptation -> watchmaker analogy -> designer
 FINE-TUNING: life-permitting constants appear within narrow range
                         |
 rival explanations
 +-> design
 +-> physical necessity
 +-> chance / multiverse + selection effect
 +-> unknown deeper law
 HUME: weak analogy, many designers, imperfect designer | DARWIN: adaptation by selection
 verdict: modern design is probabilistic/abductive, not Paley's biological inference unchanged.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/8: 5. Moral argument
KANT: morality does not theoretically prove God
 highest good (virtue proportioned to happiness) -> practical postulates:
 freedom + immortality + God
                         |
 contemporary route: objective moral obligations -> adequate personal ground
 objection: Euthyphro dilemma; secular realism can ground moral facts
 reply: modified divine command grounds obligation in commands flowing from good nature
 limit: the move from normativity to one religiously specified God remains underdetermined.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/8: 6. Nyāya-Udayana proof cluster
UDAYANA'S ROUTES TO ĪŚVARA
 kāryāt: world as effect -> maker        āyojanāt: atomic combination -> arranger
 dhṛtyādeḥ: support/dissolution          padāt: conventional word-use
 pratyayataḥ: trustworthy cognition      śruteḥ/vākyāt: Vedic discourse
 saṃkhyāviśeṣāt: determinate magnitudes  adṛṣṭāt: karmic allocation
                         |
 ĪŚVARA = omniscient efficient cause; eternal atoms = material causes
 not creation ex nihilo; proof aims at intelligent coordination of a realist cosmos.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/8: 7. Indian and Western critiques
CĀRVĀKA: no perception of God; inference overreaches
 BUDDHIST: momentary dependent series needs no permanent creator
 JAINA: beginningless jīva/ajīva and karma; creation makes God responsible for evil
 MĪMĀṂSĀ: Veda is authorless; dharma/apūrva explains ritual fruit without divine author
                         |
 HUME/KANT: causal/design inference exceeds categories or experience
 NYĀYA REPLY: unconscious atoms, apūrva or karma cannot intelligently allocate order
 COUNTER: coordination may show explanatory gap, not uniquely the Nyāya God.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/8: 8. Cumulative verdict and answer plan
DEFINE target God -> classify argument -> formalise premises -> expose hidden bridge
 -> strongest named critic -> best reply -> test what conclusion actually establishes
                         |
 ontological: necessity if possibility | cosmological: ground if PSR
 design: probability, not deduction | moral: practical/normative support
 Nyāya: intelligent order within atomism
                         |
 QUALIFIED VERDICT: no proof compels every rational agent; together they can form a
 cumulative case only if their contested modal, causal, probabilistic and moral premises stand.
