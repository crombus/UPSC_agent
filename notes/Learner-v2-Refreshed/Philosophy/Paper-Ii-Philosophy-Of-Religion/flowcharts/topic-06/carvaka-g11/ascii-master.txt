ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/8: 1. Three-question method
RELIGIOUS EXPERIENCE
 1 PHENOMENOLOGY: what is it like?
 2 OBJECT: what is experienced — God, the Holy, Brahman, emptiness, unity?
 3 EPISTEMIC FORCE: does seeming-to-encounter justify belief?
                         |
 TYPES: mystical unity | numinous encounter | conversion | prayer/worship
 | revelatory vision | non-dual awareness
 CONTROL: intensity and sincerity establish occurrence, not automatically veridical object.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/8: 2. William James
JAMES'S MARKS OF MYSTICAL EXPERIENCE
 INEFFABILITY: cannot be adequately stated
 NOETIC QUALITY: felt as insight or knowledge
 TRANSIENCY: usually brief
 PASSIVITY: received rather than fully produced by will
                         |
 “fruits, not roots”: assess transformation in life, not only origin theory
 authority is strongest for experiencer, not coercively binding on outsiders
 plural experiences support openness, but diverse objects complicate doctrinal proof.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/8: 3. Rudolf Otto and the numinous
THE NUMINOUS = MYSTERIUM TREMENDUM ET FASCINANS
 mysterium: wholly other, beyond ordinary grasp
 tremendum: awe, majesty, creature-feeling, dread
 fascinans: attraction, grace, compelling value
                         |
 “Holy” includes non-rational numinous depth plus moral/rational development
 SACRED != automatically God: an object, place or event may mediate the Holy
 objection: description can reify culturally trained emotion into an external object.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/8: 4. Advaita and Radhakrishnan
ADVAITA: aparokṣānubhūti is immediate knowledge of non-dual Brahman
 not subject seeing divine object; subject-object division is sublated
 śravaṇa -> manana -> nididhyāsana -> liberating knowledge
                         |
 RADHAKRISHNAN: integral intuition discloses spiritual unity and grounds tolerance
 strength: experience is transformative and non-inferential
 pressure: ineffability vs doctrinal description; private certainty vs public adjudication;
 Advaita interpretation may be one conceptual construction among religious traditions.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/8: 5. Object models
MODEL                WHAT EXPERIENCE PRESENTS
personal theism      an addressive “Thou,” grace, command, presence
Otto                 numinous Holy / wholly other
Advaita              non-dual Brahman; no final subject-object split
Buddhism             emptiness, cessation or suchness without creator
naturalism           altered brain/body state interpreted religiously
                         |
 SAME phenomenological report may underdetermine ontology;
 object-claim needs interpretation beyond raw immediacy.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/8: 6. Veridicality tests
SWINBURNE PRINCIPLE OF CREDULITY: accept seeming appearance unless defeated
 PRINCIPLE OF TESTIMONY: generally trust reports unless there is reason not to
                         |
 DEFEATERS: drugs/pathology | contradiction | prior implausibility | unreliable setting
 positive checks: coherence | moral fruit | intersubjective fit | predictive/explanatory value
 ALSTON: socially established doxastic practice may confer prima facie justification
 objection: incompatible religious practices cannot all validate exclusive objects unchanged.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/8: 7. Criticisms, replies and public life
FREUD: wish-fulfilment | MARX: social consolation | neuroscience: neural correlate
 pluralism: contradictory interpretations | ineffability: no communicable evidence
                         |
 replies: causal explanation does not equal debunking; all perception has neural correlates;
 fruits and disciplined practices may distinguish mature experience from fantasy
                         |
 prayer/worship test object-relations: petition, gratitude, surrender, contemplation
 public use must be modest: experience may justify commitment without authorising coercion.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/8: 8. PYQ answer spine
DEFINE type -> describe phenomenology with James/Otto -> specify claimed object
 -> distinguish experience from interpretation -> apply credulity/testimony and defeaters
 -> compare Advaita/theism/Buddhism -> strongest naturalist or pluralist objection
 -> reply -> qualified public verdict
                         |
 CLOSE: religious experience is significant evidence within a life and practice,
 but its object is not read straight off the feeling; interpretation and defeater control remain.
