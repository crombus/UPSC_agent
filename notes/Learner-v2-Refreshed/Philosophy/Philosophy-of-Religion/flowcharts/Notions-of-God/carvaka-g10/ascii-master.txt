#### ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/10: Western models: theism, deism and Spinoza

```ascii-master

                              │

 ▼            ▼            ▼            ▼            ▼
Theism       Deism       Pantheism   Panentheism   Process God
                              │
                              ▼
                     CLASSICAL THEISM
• Personal and necessary Creator
• Distinct from the world
• Creates and continuously sustains it
• Transcendent yet immanent
• Omnipotent, omniscient and perfectly good
• Eternal, immutable, simple and self-existent
                              │
                              ▼
                     GOD–WORLD RELATION
              Creator → Sustainer → Providence
                              │
                              ▼
                      GOD–MAN RELATION
• Human beings are dependent creatures
• Moral responsibility and divine command
• Prayer, worship, grace and providence are intelligible
                              │
                              ▼
                            DEISM
• God creates the universe and its natural laws
• God does not normally intervene afterward
• Miracles and special revelation are rejected
• God resembles a “cosmic clockmaker”
                              │
                              ▼
              CREATION → NATURAL LAW → WITHDRAWAL
                              │
                              ▼
                         CONSEQUENCE
• God remains transcendent
• Divine immanence and providential intervention are weakened
• Man encounters God primarily through reason and natural order
                              │
                              ▼
                          PANTHEISM
                    GOD AND WORLD ARE IDENTICAL
                              │
                              ▼
                      SPINOZA’S GOD
                   “DEUS SIVE NATURA”
                     “God, or Nature”
                              │
                              ▼
                    ONE INFINITE SUBSTANCE
                              │
        ┌─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┐
        ▼                                           ▼
     ATTRIBUTES                                  MODES
• Infinite ways in which                  • Finite things
  substance is expressed                  • Minds and bodies
• Human beings know                       • Depend on the one
  Thought and Extension                     infinite substance
                              │
                              ▼
                GOD DOES NOT CHOOSE TO CREATE
• Everything follows necessarily from divine nature
• God is not a personal ruler outside nature
• God is wholly immanent
• Divine freedom means acting from the necessity of one’s nature
                              │
                              ▼
                   SPINOZA’S CENTRAL PROBLEM
• Personality, providence and free will appear to disappear
                              │
                              ▼
                        SPINOZA’S REPLY
• Freedom is not arbitrary choice
• Freedom is self-determined activity arising from one’s nature
                              │
                              ▼
```

```ascii-master
• Contradictions are not genuine objects of power
• Inability to perform nonsense is not weakness
                              │

                    OMNISCIENCE AND FREEDOM
                              │
        God infallibly knows what a person will choose
                              │
                              ▼
        Can that person genuinely choose otherwise?
                              │
 ┌────────────────┬────────────────┬────────────────┬────────────────┐
 ▼                ▼                ▼                ▼

rather than      remain dependent “middle         are not yet fixed
foreknowing      on free choices   knowledge”
                              │
                              ▼
                      QUALIFIED VERDICT
Compatibility is possible only after clearly defining:
• Divine knowledge
• Human freedom
• Alternative possibilities
• Temporal or timeless eternity
                              │
                              ▼
             GOODNESS + POWER + KNOWLEDGE PROBLEM
                              │
      If God is all-powerful, all-knowing and perfectly good,
                       why does evil exist?
                              │
                              ▼
                       COHERENCE PRESSURE
• Evil challenges the joint compatibility of divine attributes
• The problem concerns the kind of God being affirmed
• Detailed theodicies belong primarily to the Problem of Evil topic
                              │
                              ▼
                      DIVINE SIMPLICITY
• God is not composed of matter and form
• No substance–property composition
• No real division between divine essence and existence
• Wisdom, goodness and power are not separate parts of God
                              │
                              ▼
                         PURPOSE
     Whatever is composite depends on its constituent parts
                              │
                              ▼
       The ultimate independent ground cannot be composite
                              │
                              ▼
                         OBJECTION
If God is identical with every divine attribute, how can:
• Wisdom differ from power?
• God freely relate to changing creatures?
• Personal divine action remain intelligible?
                              │
                              ▼
```

#### ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/10: Answer-writing spine and qualified conclusion

```ascii-master
                    ANSWER-WRITING SPINE
                              │
 Define the precise God-concept
              │
              ▼
 State its metaphysical model
              │
              ▼
 Explain God–world and God–man relations
              │
              ▼
 Introduce exact philosopher/school terminology
              │
              ▼
 Present strongest objection and strongest reply
              │
              ▼
 Compare using the same conceptual axes
              │
              ▼
 Give a qualified philosophical verdict
                              │
                              ▼
                    MODEL CONCLUSION
The notion of God is not one universally identical concept but
a family of metaphysical models. Classical theism emphasises
perfect personal agency and transcendence; Spinoza emphasises
unity, necessity and immanence; Advaita protects non-dual
absoluteness; Rāmānuja reconciles unity with real plurality and
devotion; and Nyāya preserves intelligent causation and moral
governance. Their adequacy depends on whether ultimacy is best
understood as a perfect person, infinite substance, non-dual
consciousness, organic unity or intelligent ordering.

High-yield formula:

NOTION OF GOD
      =
Nature of ultimate reality
      +
Divine attributes
      +
God–world relation
      +
God–man relation
      +
Coherence under objections
```
