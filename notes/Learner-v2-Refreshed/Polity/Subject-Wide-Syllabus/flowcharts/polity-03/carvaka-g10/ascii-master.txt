|   DEFINITION / TERMS : Correct: It has a constitutionally distributed federal structure with centralising features.
|   MECHANISM / ARGUMENT: India is a federation in structure with constitutionally strong Union capacity; its operation also includes cooperative, competitive and asymmetric forms of federalism.
|   CONSEQUENCE / CONTRAST: Article 1 calls India a 'Union of States'; the Constitution does not use 'Federation'.
|   TRAP / ANSWER-USE: The Union was not created by a compact among sovereign States, and States have no right to secede.
|   EXAM OPENING: Indian federalism distributes power to accommodate diversity, but retains centralising, cooperative and asymmetric devices so the Union can coordinate without erasing the States.
