ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Birth, descent and registration: date-and-category drill
BIRTH IN INDIA
1950-before 1 Jul 1987 -> birth generally sufficient, subject to exceptions
1 Jul 1987-before 3 Dec 2004 -> either parent a citizen
from 3 Dec 2004 -> both citizens OR one citizen + other not illegal migrant

DESCENT OUTSIDE INDIA
before 10 Dec 1992 -> paternal rule | later -> either parent
from 3 Dec 2004 -> consular registration plus statutory declaration controls

REGISTRATION -> category first; seven-year formula is 12 months + 6 of prior 8 years.
