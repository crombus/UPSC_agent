  +-- CITIZEN
  |     +-- political membership of India

  |     +-- eligibility for specified constitutional offices
  +-- ALIEN
        +-- person-based guarantees include Articles 14, 20 and 21
        +-- may be foreign national or stateless person

DOMICILE = permanent legal home plus intention
RESIDENCE = factual presence under a specified rule
CITIZENSHIP = legal-political membership

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/9: Articles 5-7: commencement citizenship decision tree
AT COMMENCEMENT, 26 JAN 1950
  +-- ARTICLE 5: domicile in India + one connection
  |     birth in India | parent born in India | five years' ordinary residence
  +-- ARTICLE 6: migrated from Pakistan to India
  |     before 19 Jul 1948 -> ordinary-residence route
  |     on/after 19 Jul 1948 -> registration after permit-era conditions
  +-- ARTICLE 7: migrated to Pakistan after 1 Mar 1947
        generally excluded
        +-- permit for resettlement/permanent return may restore Article 6 route

CONTROL
Five-year residence alone never satisfied Article 5; domicile remained essential.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/9: Five statutory routes to acquire citizenship
CITIZENSHIP ACT 1955
  +-- BY BIRTH: date and parents' status
  +-- BY DESCENT: parent link + date-specific registration conditions
  +-- BY REGISTRATION: listed categories and residence conditions
  +-- BY NATURALISATION: Third Schedule qualifications and discretion
  +-- BY INCORPORATION: Central Government specifies eligible persons

NO ROUTE IS AUTOMATIC MERELY FROM
long residence | ancestry | marriage | property | voter document

PROCESS
statutory category -> exact date -> documents -> oath and decision

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/9: Birth and descent rules change across dates
BY BIRTH IN INDIA
26 Jan 1950-30 Jun 1987   birth generally sufficient; exceptions apply
1 Jul 1987-2 Dec 2004     at least one parent must be Indian citizen
from 3 Dec 2004           one citizen parent + other not illegal migrant,
                           or both parents citizens

BY DESCENT OUTSIDE INDIA
before 10 Dec 1992        paternal transmission under then text
10 Dec 1992-2 Dec 2004    either Indian parent can transmit
from 3 Dec 2004           consular registration and declaration controls

A document proves facts; it does not override the date-specific statutory test.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/9: Three routes of loss and their safeguards
RENUNCIATION
  adult citizen's declaration
  -> minor child may follow, with statutory resumption protection

TERMINATION
  voluntary acquisition of another country's citizenship
  -> India does not recognise full dual citizenship

DEPRIVATION
  Central Government order for specified grounds
  +-- fraud or concealment
  +-- disloyalty or unlawful enemy dealing
  +-- specified conviction or residence conditions
  +-- notice, inquiry and public-good control

renunciation = citizen act | termination = foreign acquisition | deprivation = State order

ASSAM-SPECIFIC SECTION 6A
  +-- entered before 1 Jan 1966
  |     deemed citizens under statutory scheme
  +-- entered 1 Jan 1966-before 25 Mar 1971
  |     detection + registration
  |     ten-year electoral-roll exclusion
  +-- entered on/after 25 Mar 1971

NRC uses 24 March 1971 midnight as Assam's cut-off reference.

IN RE: SECTION 6A (2024) — 17 OCTOBER
4:1 judgment upholds Section 6A; implementation remains crucial.

TENSION: national membership + Assam migration history + cultural protection.
