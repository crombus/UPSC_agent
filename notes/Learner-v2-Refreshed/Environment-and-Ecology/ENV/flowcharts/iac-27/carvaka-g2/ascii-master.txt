ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Institution-type compass
MINISTRY -> policy administration and coordination
STATUTORY BOARD OR AUTHORITY -> Act-defined functions
RESEARCH INSTITUTE -> evidence training and advisory work
ENFORCEMENT BODY -> investigation or operational compliance role
TRIBUNAL -> adjudication within statutory jurisdiction
MUST REMEMBER: Environmental governance distributes policy, standard-setting, consent,...

LAW AND RULE ADMINISTRATION -> subject to statutory allocation
PROGRAMME AND INTERNATIONAL COORDINATION -> ministry function
SUBORDINATE OR AUTONOMOUS BODIES -> separate legal identities
RULE -> ministry is not every field regulator

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: CPCB national rail

COORDINATE STATE BOARDS -> inter-board function

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Standards-consent firewall
CPCB STANDARD OR GUIDELINE -> national regulatory benchmark
SPCB OR PCC CONSENT -> project-specific permission context
INSPECTION AND ENFORCEMENT -> competent territorial authority
CENTRAL DIRECTION OR COORDINATION -> separate legal route
RULE -> one output cannot be substituted for another

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: WII science pipeline

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Adjudication boundary
ADMINISTRATIVE DECISION -> ministry authority board or state body
MONITORING OR ENFORCEMENT -> competent executive institution
CHALLENGE OR DISPUTE -> applicable adjudicatory route
NGT -> tribunal within its statutory jurisdiction
RULE -> adjudication does not erase executive responsibility

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Legal-basis test
CREATED DIRECTLY BY ACT -> statutory body
CONSTITUTED UNDER PARENT ACT -> notified authority
DELEGATED POWER -> scope follows delegation
AUTONOMOUS OR RESEARCH BODY -> institutional mandate
VERDICT -> cite the exact founding instrument
CLOSE DISTINCTION: MoEFCC is not CPCB, CPCB standards are not every SPCB consent...
