ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Digital ecosystem boundary
DIGITAL INDIA -> programme for infrastructure, governance and services
INDIA STACK -> layered architectural idea
DPI -> reusable interoperable rails with public rules
PRIVATE APP -> interface or participant on a rail
RULE -> these labels cannot be substituted

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Modular stack map
IDENTITY -> Aadhaar / UIDAI
PAYMENTS -> UPI / NPCI under RBI regulation
FINANCIAL DATA -> Account Aggregator consent flow
DOCUMENTS / SIGNATURES -> DigiLocker and eSign
OUTPUT -> interoperable services without one super-database

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Aadhaar legal identity card
AADHAAR -> 12-digit resident identity number
UIDAI -> statutory authority under MeitY
AADHAAR ACT -> legal foundation
NOT PROOF -> citizenship, domicile, entitlement or payment
RULE -> identity match answers only the identity question

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Three Aadhaar workflows
AUTHENTICATION -> yes or no match against CIDR
e-KYC -> permitted demographic return with consent
OFFLINE VERIFICATION -> QR or XML without CIDR request
ENTITLEMENT DECISION -> separate legal and administrative step
TRAP -> one workflow cannot be relabelled as another

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: UPI transaction chain
USER APP -> initiates payment instruction
UPI / NPCI -> interoperable messaging and switching rail
BANK OR PERMITTED INSTRUMENT -> funding source
RBI FRAMEWORK -> payment-system regulation
RULE -> rail, app, account and regulator are distinct

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Payment-rail matrix
UPI -> instant retail payment instruction
IMPS / NEFT / RTGS -> distinct interbank settlement modes
AePS -> Aadhaar-authenticated correspondent banking
BBPS / NACH -> bills and recurring bulk mandates
TRAP -> digital payment does not mean UPI alone

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Operator regulator participant
NPCI -> operates specified retail rails
RBI -> regulates payment systems
BANK / PPI ISSUER -> holds or issues funding instrument
APP PROVIDER -> customer interface
UIDAI -> separate Aadhaar authority

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Account Aggregator flow
CUSTOMER -> gives explicit consent
FIP -> holds regulated financial information
AA -> retrieves and transfers as a data blind
FIU -> receives for consented purpose
RULE -> AA does not own or retain the information

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Trust layers
DIGILOCKER -> access, share and verify trusted documents
eSIGN -> online electronic signature under trust framework
AADHAAR -> identity and authentication
UPI -> payment instruction rail
RULE -> document, signature, identity and payment stay separate

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Open-network extensions
ONDC -> open commerce protocol
OCEN -> open credit protocol
NETWORK LOGIC -> unbundle front ends from shared rules
APPLICATIONS -> compete on top of protocol
TRAP -> protocol is not a private marketplace or lender

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Electronic toll status rail
FASTag -> RFID tag-reader architecture
NETC -> clearing and dispute framework
ANPR-FASTag -> barrierless pilot combination
GNSS DISTANCE TOLLING -> proposed or pilot direction
RULE -> do not call a proposal nationwide deployment

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/12: DPI claim firewall
DEFINE -> programme, rail, app, operator and regulator
TRACE -> identity, payment, consent, document or toll flow
SEPARATE -> identity from citizenship and entitlement
PAIR SCALE -> inclusion, fraud, privacy and grievance safeguards
VERIFY -> monthly official transaction and coverage data
