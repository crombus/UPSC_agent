+--------------+----------------------+---------------------------+
| FAMILY       | PROCESS              | FIELD CLUES               |
+--------------+----------------------+---------------------------+
| Igneous      | magma/lava cooling   | crystals, glass, vesicles |
| intrusive    | slow cooling         | coarse texture            |
| extrusive    | fast cooling         | fine or glassy texture    |
| Sedimentary  | deposit + lithify    | beds, fossils, ripples    |

| foliated     | differential stress  | aligned fabric            |
