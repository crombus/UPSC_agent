+-- SESSION 04: WEBER'S LEAST-COST THEORY: LEARN IT IN SIX STEPS
|   DEFINITION / TERMS : Weber's least-cost theory: learn it in six steps: The first task is to find the site with the lowest total cost of moving inputs in and output out.
|   MECHANISM / ARGUMENT: The model gives a disciplined starting mechanism.
|   CONSEQUENCE / CONTRAST: Alfred Weber's 1909 model asks: where can a factory minimise the combined burden of transport cost, labour cost and agglomeration/deglomeration effects?
|   TRAP / ANSWER-USE: It is the starting point, not an automatic final answer.
|   EXAM OPENING: !Weber material index, transport minimum and isodapane
