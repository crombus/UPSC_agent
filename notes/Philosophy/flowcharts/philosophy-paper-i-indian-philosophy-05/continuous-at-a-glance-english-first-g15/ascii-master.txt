ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/9: Textual identity, suffering and subtle continuity
TRADITION: Kapila -> Āsuri -> Pañcaśikha; early works not securely extant
CLASSICAL OWNER: Īśvarakṛṣṇa's Sāṃkhyakārikā; later commentarial layers vary
THREEFOLD SUFFERING: ādhyātmika | ādhibhautika | ādhidaivika
SUBTLE BODY: liṅga-śarīra = 18 principles carrying dispositions between bodies
Conscious witness (puruṣa) neither acts nor migrates; empirical subtle body bears action and moral
  consequence (karma) and rebirth
CLASSICAL POSITION: non-theistic/God-unproved; later Vijñānabhikṣu re-theizes
YOGA BOUNDARY: Yoga owns citta, kleśa, samādhi, eight limbs and special Conscious witness (puruṣa)

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/9: Pre-existence of the effect in the cause (satkāryavāda)
EFFECT PRE-EXISTS LATENTLY IN CAUSE
 asadakaraṇāt: non-being cannot be produced
 upādānagrahaṇāt: specific material cause is selected
 sarvasambhavābhāvāt: not everything arises from everything
 śaktasya śakyakaraṇāt: cause produces only what it has power to produce
 kāraṇabhāvāt: effect shares nature of cause
                         |
 real transformation (pariṇāmavāda): real transformation of primordial material nature (Prakṛti)
 NYĀYA new production (ārambhavāda): new effect | ADVAITA vivarta: apparent transformation.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/9: Why many conscious witness (puruṣas)?
CONSCIOUS WITNESS (puruṣa) EXISTS BECAUSE
 composites serve another | experience needs conscious witness
 objects of three qualities (guṇas) require non-guṇa experiencer | liberation is conceivable
                         |
 PLURALITY: births/deaths differ; sense capacities and actions are uncoordinated;
 if one conscious witness (puruṣa), one person's liberation or pain would belong to all
                         |
 CONTACT PROBLEM: inactive consciousness cannot literally touch unconscious primordial material
   nature (Prakṛti)
 analogies: blind-and-lame cooperation | magnet and iron
 pressure remains: how does mere proximity begin purposive evolution?
Kārikā 18: fixed births/deaths/faculties | non-simultaneous activity | constituent quality (guṇa)
  differences
differential liberation = supplementary plurality argument

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/9: Bondage, liberation and critique
AVIVEKA: determinative intellect (buddhi)'s states are appropriated as “I” by reflected
  consciousness
 experience + karmic dispositions -> repeated embodiment
                         |
 tattva-jñāna / viveka-khyāti: “I am conscious witness (puruṣa), not primordial material nature
   (Prakṛti) or its evolutes”
                         |
 primordial material nature (Prakṛti) has been seen -> ceases activity for that conscious witness
   (puruṣa) -> ISOLATION (kaivalya)
                         |
 ŚAṂKARA: unconscious primordial material nature (Prakṛti) cannot act for an end; dualism cannot
   explain contact
 SĀṂKHYA REPLY: teleology is immanent, as milk flows for calf; no creator is needed.
Conscious witness (puruṣa) is never really bound, liberated or transmigrating
jīvanmukti: knowledge now; body continues like potter's wheel
videhamukti: subtle/gross embodiment finally ceases
