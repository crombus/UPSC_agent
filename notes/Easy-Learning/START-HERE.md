## Four-pass method

`First Pass → Exact Law Pass → Exam Pass → Data Vault`

- **First Pass:** understand the teacher explanation, analogy and visual.
- **Exact Law Pass:** add Articles, statutes, cases and tests.
- **Exam Pass:** practise confusions, PYQ demands and answer spines.
- **Data Vault:** retrieve complete reference and source-preserved detail.
