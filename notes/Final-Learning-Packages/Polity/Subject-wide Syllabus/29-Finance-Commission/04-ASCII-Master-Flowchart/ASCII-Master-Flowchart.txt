ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 1/12: CONCEPT AND TWO FISCAL GAPS
==============================================================================
Union: broader tax capacity ---- States: service-heavy responsibilities
                     -> VERTICAL GAP -> Union-State sharing
Unequal State income/cost/ecology -> HORIZONTAL GAP -> inter se sharing
FC = periodic constitutional equalisation; not a permanent spending ministry.
ANSWER: rule-based transfers reconcile autonomy, equity and Union capacity.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 2/12: DECODED REVENUE SPINE
==============================================================================
Art 269 -> specified inter-State taxes assigned to States.
Art 269A -> inter-State GST levied/collected by Union and apportioned.
Art 270 -> shareable Union taxes + relevant GST distribution.
Art 271 -> Union-purpose surcharge excluded from sharing.
Art 279 -> net proceeds minus collection cost; CAG certificate final.
Art 279A -> GST Council; not the Article 280 devolution body.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 7/12: FC-16 HORIZONTAL FORMULA
==============================================================================
GSDP distance 42.5 | Population 2011 17.5 | Demography 10
| Area 10 | Forest 10 | GDP contribution 10.
Demography = inverse population growth 1971-2011; not current TFR.
Area floor = 1.5%. Forest = 80:20 stock/increase, weighted density.
GDP contribution = square-root GSDP share; no separate tax-effort criterion.
