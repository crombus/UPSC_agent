+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 1/12 - IDENTITY AND SOURCE                                               |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| CONSTITUTIONAL LAW-OFFICER MAP                                                 |
| Article 76 -> Attorney-General for India (AGI) -> Union advice/advocacy        |
| Article 165 -> Advocate-General -> State advice/advocacy                       |
| SG/ASG -> executive/rule-governed assistants; NOT constitutional offices       |
| Law Minister governs policy; courts adjudicate; AGI advises and argues.        |
| TRAP: highest law officer does not mean judge, minister or prosecutor-general. |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 2/12 - ARTICLE 76 COMPLETE SPINE                                            |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 76(1) President appoints + Supreme-Court-Judge qualification                      |
| 76(2) legal advice + assigned duties + functions conferred by Constitution/law    |
| 76(3) right of audience in all courts in India while performing duties            |
| 76(4) President's pleasure + remuneration determined by President                 |
| NO fixed constitutional term, impeachment, fixed salary or binding-opinion power. |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 3/12 - QUALIFICATION                                                      |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Article 124(3) imported through Article 76(1):                                  |
| citizen + 5 years High Court Judge; OR                                          |
| citizen + 10 years High Court advocate; OR                                      |
| citizen + distinguished jurist in President's opinion.                          |
| Qualification does NOT import judgeship, judicial removal or age 65 retirement. |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 4/12 - APPOINTMENT, TENURE AND INDEPENDENCE                                 |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Formal appointment: President.                                                    |
| Tenure: presidential pleasure; Constitution states no fixed term/removal process. |
| Remuneration: President determines; no constitutional fixed salary.               |
| Strength: high qualification + constitutional status + court audience.            |
| Vulnerability: executive-client dependence + pleasure + outside-work conflicts.   |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 5/12 - ADVICE AND LITIGATION                                                 |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| REFERRED LEGAL MATTER -> AGI advice -> Government decides -> Court may review      |
| ASSIGNED CASE -> authorised counsel appears -> Court controls procedure/outcome    |
| AGI opinion is persuasive advice, not judgment, legislation or binding direction.  |
| Article 76 gives no general policy veto or power to initiate criminal prosecution. |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 6/12 - AUDIENCE AND PARLIAMENT                                         |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Article 76(3): audience in ALL Indian courts while performing duties.        |
| Article 88: speak/take part in either House, joint sitting, named committee. |
| NO vote by office; NO membership by office.                                  |
| Article 105(4): relevant clauses extend for proceeding-linked participation. |
| TRAP: audience is not case monopoly; privilege is not blanket immunity.      |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 7/12 - PART-TIME STATUS AND RULE 8                                               |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Not a whole-time government servant; outside professional work not universally barred. |
| BUT court briefs and clients are confined by the current Rule 8 perimeter.             |
| No advice/brief against GoI; no likely future conflict with GoI/covered body.          |
| Criminal defence -> GoI permission where applicable.                                   |
| Company/corporation office -> GoI permission where applicable.                         |
| Formal Ministry/PSU advice -> Department of Legal Affairs reference route.             |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 8/12 - UNION LAW-OFFICER TEAM                                         |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| AGI: Article 76 constitutional head.                                        |
| Solicitor General / Additional Solicitors General: executive/rule-governed. |
| Panel counsel/private advocates: professional engagement only.              |
| Only AGI receives Article 88 participation by virtue of office.             |
| TRAP: courtroom prominence cannot create constitutional status.             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 9/12 - ADVOCATE-GENERAL                                           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Article 165(1): Governor appoints; Article 217(2) qualification.        |
| citizen + 10 years judicial office OR 10 years High Court advocacy.     |
| Article 165(2): State advice + assigned/conferred legal functions.      |
| Article 165(3): Governor's pleasure + Governor-determined remuneration. |
| No express Article 76(3)-style all-India audience clause.               |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 10/12 - STATE LEGISLATURE AND COMPARISON                                 |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Article 177: Advocate-General participates in State House(s)/named committee.  |
| NO vote by office; Article 194(4) gives proceeding-linked privilege extension. |
| AGI / President / Art 124(3) / GoI / express all-court audience / Art 88       |
| AdvG / Governor / Art 217(2) / State / no matching audience text / Art 177     |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 11/12 - CONTEMPT, CASE AND REFORM                                              |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Contempt Act s.15: bounded motion/consent routes; courts retain suo motu power.      |
| Not a universal consent rule; not a power to prosecute offences or decide contempt.  |
| Brijeshwar Singh Chahal (2016): fair/objective State counsel selection context.      |
| Reform: criteria + conflict screening + recusal + permission records + case quality. |
| Do not convert the case into an AGI collegium or fixed-tenure holding.               |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PANEL 12/12 - EXAM ANSWER ROUTE                                                       |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| DEFINE -> constitutional government counsel under Article 76.                         |
| DECODE -> appointment, qualification, duty, audience, pleasure/remuneration.          |
| ENABLE -> Article 88 voice + Article 105(4) proceeding privilege, no vote.            |
| LIMIT -> advice non-binding + no Cabinet/judicial/prosecution power + Rule 8.         |
| COMPARE -> SG/ASG, Advocate-General, Law Minister, CJI, private advocate.             |
| VERDICT -> broad access, bounded authority, professional independence under pressure. |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
