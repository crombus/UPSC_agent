ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 1/12: CONCEPT AND PURPOSE
==============================================================================
Legislature authorises -> executive collects/spends/records -> CAG tests authority, evidence
and results -> President/Governor lays -> legislature scrutinises. LIMIT: evidence, not
executive command.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 2/12: ORIGIN AND AMENDMENTS
==============================================================================
Constitution 1950 constitutional office. DPC Act 56/1971 duties and service. Act 45/1976
amended ss10,11,22 for departmentalisation. Acts 2/1984, 50/1987 and 51/1994 revised
pension/service provisions, not general audit jurisdiction.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 3/12: ARTICLES 148-151 AND 279
==============================================================================
148 office, warrant, oath, SC-judge removal, service protection, post-office bar, IAAD,
charged expenses. 149 law gateway. 150 President on CAG advice; 42nd Amendment 1976 changed
formulation. 151 report routes. 279 final net-proceeds certificate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 4/12: APPOINTMENT AND INDEPENDENCE
==============================================================================
President appoints; Third Schedule oath. DPC s4: six years or age 65. Removal: proved
misbehaviour/incapacity and Art124(4) address. Strength after appointment; selection
transparency, access and follow-up remain issues.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 5/12: ACCOUNTS VERSUS AUDIT
==============================================================================
Accounts record/compile; audit independently tests. 1976 Union departmentalisation moved
much compilation to ministries/CGA. DPC ss10-12 retain residual roles. India is
predominantly ex-post auditor, not routine pre-withdrawal comptroller.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 6/12: DPC ACT MAP
==============================================================================
s13 expenditure/funds; s14 financed bodies; s15 grants; s16 receipts; s17 stores; s18
access; s19/19A companies/corporations; s20 entrustment; s23 scope; s24 limited checks.
Jurisdiction first, access second.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 7/12: ENTITY BOUNDARIES
==============================================================================
Department -> ss13/16/17/18. Government company -> s19 + Companies Act 2(45),139,143.
Corporation -> own law. Grantee -> s14/15. Other body -> s20. Local/private/PPP audit needs
a bounded legal nexus.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 8/12: AUDIT TYPES
==============================================================================
Financial = fair presentation. Compliance = authority. Propriety = prudence/economy.
Performance = economy/efficiency/effectiveness. Finding is not guilt, recovery order,
invalid policy or final adjudication.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 9/12: REPORT TO ACCOUNTABILITY
==============================================================================
CAG report -> President/Governor -> legislature. PAC examines Appropriation/Finance Accounts
and selected reports. CoPU examines undertakings. CAG assists but is not a member. Executive
enforcement needs separate power.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 10/12: CASES AND EXAMPLES
==============================================================================
Arvind Gupta 2012 performance audit valid. CPIL 2012 arbitrary 2G FCFS, 122 licences
quashed, not adoption of audit estimate. Arun Kumar Agrawal 2013 parliamentary scrutiny.
Balaji 2013 ex-post propriety. Telecom 2014 bounded revenue access.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 11/12: COMPARISON, LIMITS, REFORM
==============================================================================
CGA accounts; FC recommends; EC runs elections; UPSC advises; PAC/CoPU scrutinise. Limits:
ex post, data delay, bounded reach. Reform: transparent selection, auditability, digital
access, timely laying/replies, committee capacity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 12/12: TRAPS AND ANSWER SPINE
==============================================================================
TRAPS: tenure source; no pleasure removal; Art150 advice; Art151 route; s18 ancillary;
local/private audit not automatic; report != verdict. ANSWER: define -> 148 -> 149/DPC ->
type/entity -> 151 -> case -> limit -> reform -> verdict.
