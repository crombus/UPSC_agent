ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Citizenship, rights and one national membership
CITIZENSHIP = legally recognised membership of the Indian political community
  +-- citizen-specific text: Articles 15, 16, 19 and 29
  +-- person-based text: Articles 14, 20, 21 and 25
  +-- Article 22(3)(a): enemy alien excluded only from clauses (1)-(2)
  +-- vote: statutory right requiring citizenship plus electoral conditions

CITIZENSHIP = membership | DOMICILE = permanent legal home | RESIDENCE = factual link
ONE INDIAN CITIZENSHIP; constitutionally bounded regional protections are not State citizenship.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Five acquisition routes and exact gates
CITIZENSHIP ACT, 1955
  +-- BIRTH: place + date + parental status + Section 3(2) exceptions
  +-- DESCENT: citizen parent + date + consular/declaration controls
  +-- REGISTRATION: listed category + residence calculation + oath + decision
  +-- NATURALISATION: 12 months + 11/14 aggregate + Third Schedule tests
  +-- INCORPORATION: Central Government order for newly incorporated territory
NO AUTOMATIC CITIZENSHIP from marriage, ancestry, property, residence or an identity card.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Birth, descent, registration and illegal-migrant control
BIRTH IN INDIA
1950-before 1 Jul 1987 -> birth generally sufficient, subject to exceptions
1 Jul 1987-before 3 Dec 2004 -> either parent citizen
from 3 Dec 2004 -> both citizens OR one citizen + other not illegal migrant
Section 3(2) -> diplomat-immunity and enemy-alien/enemy-occupation exceptions

DESCENT: before 10 Dec 1992 father rule; later either parent; from 3 Dec 2004 registration controls.
REGISTRATION: category first; seven years = 12 immediate months + 6 of preceding 8.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Loss and procedural safeguards
RENUNCIATION -> full-age declaration; minor follows, with statutory resumption window
TERMINATION -> voluntary acquisition of foreign citizenship; Section 9
DEPRIVATION -> eligible category + listed ground + public-good finding + procedure
  grounds: fraud | disaffection | enemy dealing | specified conviction | seven years abroad
TRAP: citizenship by birth/descent is outside Section 10(1)'s opening category gate.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: NRI, PIO and OCI — diaspora without dual citizenship
NRI = Indian citizen ordinarily abroad
PIO card = legacy scheme merged into OCI in 2015
OCI = foreign national with statutory card under Sections 7A-7D
  +-- 2021: lifelong multiple-entry visa + notified limited parity
  +-- no vote, Indian passport, legislature or citizen-reserved high office
  +-- special permission for listed research/religious/journalistic/protected-area activity
  +-- 11 Aug 2025 S.O. 3662(E): specified sentence/charge-sheet cancellation ground
OCI is not Indian citizenship and not constitutional dual citizenship.

             from Afghanistan | Bangladesh | Pakistan
             entered on/before 31 Dec 2014 + specified exemption
  +-- application for registration/naturalisation; no automatic citizenship
  +-- aggregate naturalisation limb 11 years -> 5; immediate 12 months remains
  +-- excluded: Sixth Schedule tribal areas in four named States + Inner Line areas
