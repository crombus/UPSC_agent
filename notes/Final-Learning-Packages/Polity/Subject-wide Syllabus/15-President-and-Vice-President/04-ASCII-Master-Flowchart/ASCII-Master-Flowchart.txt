ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Constitutional Design
PRESIDENT
  Article 52 -> office must exist
  Article 53 -> Union executive power formally vested in President
  Articles 74-75 -> binding ministerial advice + Lok Sabha responsibility
VICE-PRESIDENT
  Article 63 -> office must exist
  Article 64 -> ex-officio Rajya Sabha Chairman, not a member
  Article 65 -> temporary acting/discharge role, not automatic succession

ANSWER LINE
President = constitutional sentinel, not rival executive.
Vice-President = parliamentary presiding officer plus continuity bridge.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Election Colleges
ARTICLE 54 — PRESIDENT
  elected Lok Sabha + elected Rajya Sabha
  elected State + Delhi + Puducherry MLAs
  excludes nominated members, MLCs and J&K UT MLAs
ARTICLE 66 — VICE-PRESIDENT
  all elected + nominated MPs of both Houses
  no State or UT legislator

TRAP
Nominated MPs: President election NO; VP election YES; impeachment YES.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Vote Value And Article 71
ARTICLE 55
  MLA value = 1971 population / elected MLAs / 1000, with rounding
  MP value = total MLA vote value / elected MPs of both Houses
  same value for every elected MP
  PR-STV + secret ranked ballot + majority quota
ARTICLE 71
  Supreme Court finally decides both election disputes
  electoral-college vacancy does not invalidate election
  prior official acts survive later invalidation

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Removal And Immunity
ARTICLE 61 — PRESIDENTIAL IMPEACHMENT
  either House; 1/4 signatures; 14 days
  2/3 total membership in both decisive Houses
  President may appear or be represented
ARTICLE 67(b) — VP REMOVAL
  Rajya Sabha initiates; majority of all then members
  Lok Sabha agrees; 14 days; no stated ground
ARTICLE 361
  procedural personal immunity during tenure
  government action remains judicially reviewable

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Veto
ARTICLE 111 ORDINARY BILL
  assent / withhold / return once -> re-passage compels assent
MONEY BILL
  assent / withhold -> cannot return
ARTICLE 368 AMENDMENT BILL
  24th Amendment (1971) -> assent obligatory
ARTICLE 201 RESERVED STATE BILL
  assent / withhold / return non-Money Bill through Governor
  State re-passage does not textually compel presidential assent
POCKET VETO
  arises from no fixed Article 111 deadline

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Ordinance
ARTICLE 123 TRIGGER
  both Houses not simultaneously in session + immediate necessity
FORCE
  Act-like within Parliament competence; may be retrospective
LIMIT
  cannot amend Constitution or abridge Fundamental Rights
EXIT
  lay before both Houses; six weeks after later reassembly
CASES
  R.C. Cooper (1970) -> satisfaction reviewable
  D.C. Wadhwa (1986) -> routine re-promulgation is fraud
  Krishna Kumar Singh (2017) -> laying mandatory; no ordinance rule

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Clemency
ARTICLE 72 FORMS
  pardon / reprieve / respite / remission / commutation
PRESIDENT'S FIELD
  court-martial + Union field + every death sentence
ARTICLE 161 GOVERNOR
  State field; no court-martial; cannot pardon death but may commute
CASES
  Maru Ram (1980) -> binding advice
  Kehar Singh (1988) -> merits may be examined afresh
  Epuru Sudhakar (2006) -> limited review for bad faith/arbitrariness
