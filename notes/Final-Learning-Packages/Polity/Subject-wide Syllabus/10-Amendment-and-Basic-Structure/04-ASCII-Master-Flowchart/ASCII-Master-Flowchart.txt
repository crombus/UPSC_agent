ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 2/12: Three routes and majority distinctions

ROUTE 1: OUTSIDE ARTICLE 368
  Articles 2-4 -> State reorganisation + consequential First/Fourth Schedule change
  Article 11 -> citizenship law
  Article 169 -> special State Assembly resolution, then ordinary parliamentary law
  Article 239A / Fifth para 7 / Sixth para 21 -> express non-368 mechanisms
  Other authorised fields -> quorum, salaries, procedure, privileges, election machinery,
                             delimitation, court strength/jurisdiction, language, Schedule details

ROUTE 2: ARTICLE 368 SPECIAL MAJORITY
  majority of TOTAL MEMBERSHIP + 2/3 PRESENT AND VOTING in EACH House

ROUTE 3: ROUTE 2 + STATES
  ratification by resolutions of >= half State legislatures

DO NOT CONFUSE
  effective majority = then-existing strength after vacancies, used elsewhere
  Article 368 total membership = full sanctioned strength

HELD

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 11/12: Evaluation, transformation and restraint

CRITIQUE
  unwritten limit | open-ended list | uncertainty | counter-majoritarian judicial finality
        |
DEFENCE
  Article 368 grants amendment, not destruction
  democracy includes future elections, opposition, rights, federal voice and review
  Minerva Mills -> limited power cannot erase its own limit
        |
SELF-RESTRAINT TEST
  1 identify feature through text/structure/precedent
  2 show its constitutional function
  3 prove actual disabling damage, not policy disagreement
  4 distinguish regulation/rebalancing from destruction
  5 use severability and a narrow remedy where possible

TRANSFORMATIVE AMENDMENT -> legitimate innovation preserving identity
REPLACEMENT/NEW CONSTITUTION -> original constituent act, not Article 368 amendment
