ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 1/12: Origin, insertion and democratic caution
ORIGINAL CONSTITUTION (1950) -> Part III rights + Part IV State directives
                              -> no separate list of citizen duties
SWARAN SINGH COMMITTEE (1976) -> recommended a separate chapter + eight duties
42ND AMENDMENT (1976) -> inserted Part IVA, Article 51A, clauses (a)-(j): ten duties
J.S. VERMA COMMITTEE (1999) -> education, awareness and existing-law implementation
86TH AMENDMENT (2002) -> inserted clause (k): present total eleven
SOURCE TAG -> erstwhile USSR; LIMIT -> Indian duties remain democratic and rights-bounded

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 2/12: Exact Article 51A clauses (a)-(d)
OPENING -> "It shall be the duty of every citizen of India"
(a) abide by the Constitution and respect its ideals and institutions,
    the National Flag and the National Anthem
(b) cherish and follow the noble ideals which inspired our national struggle for freedom
(c) uphold and protect the sovereignty, unity and integrity of India
(d) defend the country and render national service when called upon to do so
TRAP -> lawful criticism is not disloyalty; clause (d) does not itself create conscription.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 3/12: Exact Article 51A clauses (e)-(h)
(e) promote harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood amongst all the people of India
    transcending religious, linguistic and regional or sectional diversities;
    renounce practices derogatory to the dignity of women
(f) value and preserve the rich heritage of our composite culture
(g) protect and improve the natural environment including forests, lakes, rivers and
    wild life, and have compassion for living creatures
(h) develop scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of inquiry and reform
TRAP -> dignity of women is in (e); compassion is in (g); reform is in (h).

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 4/12: Exact Article 51A clauses (i)-(k)
(i) safeguard public property and abjure violence
(j) strive towards excellence in all spheres of individual and collective activity so
    that the nation constantly rises to higher levels of endeavour and achievement
(k) parent or guardian: provide opportunities for education to his child or ward
    between the age of six and fourteen years
EDUCATION TRIAD -> Article 21A child right, 6-14 | Article 45 State effort, below 6
                  | Article 51A(k) parent/guardian duty, 6-14

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 5/12: Legal status and enforceability firewall
ARTICLE 51A ADDRESSEE -> citizens only; foreigners remain bound by valid ordinary law
DIRECT STATUS -> non-justiciable; no standalone writ merely for breach
CRIMINAL STATUS -> no offence, prosecutor, procedure or punishment in Article 51A itself
MEDIATED EFFECT -> civic education + policy + interpretive aid + valid legislation
VALID LAW -> legislative competence + defined elements + procedure + proof + sanction
REVIEW -> Fundamental Rights + equality + due process + proportionality + judicial review
RULE -> duty may explain purpose; it cannot cure an unconstitutional law.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 6/12: Rights, DPSP and duties without slogans
PART III RIGHTS -> protect liberty, equality, conscience and remedies
PART IV DPSP -> direct State policy toward social and economic justice
PART IVA DUTIES -> orient citizen responsibility for common constitutional goods
BRIDGES -> 48A + 51A(g) environment | 21A + 45 + 51A(k) education
         -> fraternity + 51A(e) harmony/gender dignity
LIMIT -> no Part substitutes for another; duties do not automatically override rights.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 7/12: Constitutional patriotism and national symbols
51A(a) -> Constitution, institutions, Flag and Anthem
BIJOE EMMANUEL (1986) -> respectful non-singing protected by Articles 19 and 25
NAVEEN JINDAL (2004) -> dignified Flag display is Article 19(1)(a) expression
SHYAM NARAYAN CHOUKSEY -> 2016 interim cinema mandate
                              -> final order 9 Jan 2018: playing optional/directory
NATIONAL HONOUR ACT 1971 -> defined insult, prevention and disturbance offences
VERDICT -> respect without compelled conformity; conscience is not automatic disrespect.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 8/12: Civic domains and interpretive cases
51A(e) -> fraternity across difference + renounce practices derogatory to women
51A(f) -> plural, layered composite culture; preservation does not freeze inequality
51A(h) -> scientific temper + humanism + inquiry + reform
ARUNA ROY (2002) -> non-proselytising comparative/value education permissible;
                    no licence for dogma or superstition
51A(j) + AIIMS STUDENTS UNION (2001) -> excellence has interpretive significance
51A(i) -> public property + non-violence; peaceful dissent remains protected

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 9/12: Environmental citizenship and animal compassion
ARTICLE 48A (State) + ARTICLE 51A(g) (citizen) + ARTICLE 21 (right)
  -> shared environmental constitutionalism
M.C. MEHTA (1991) -> environmental education and awareness
STATUTES -> Environment Protection Act 1986 | Wild Life Protection Act 1972
         -> Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam 1980
A. NAGARAJA (2014) -> strong compassion-based statutory/constitutional reasoning
JALLIKATTU CONSTITUTION BENCH (2023) -> amended State regimes upheld with safeguards
LIMIT -> neither case creates a free-standing absolute animal right from clause (g).

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 10/12: Real statutory nexus and committee separation
SYMBOLS -> Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act 1971; Flag Code 2002
SOVEREIGNTY / HARMONY -> UAPA 1967; BNS 196-197; RPA 123(3)/(3A)
DIGNITY -> Protection of Civil Rights Act 1955; SC/ST PoA Act 1989; gender laws
ENVIRONMENT -> environment, wildlife and forest enactments
PUBLIC PROPERTY -> Prevention of Damage to Public Property Act 1984
EDUCATION -> RTE Act 2009, including section 10
SWARAN SINGH -> recommended duties; omitted tax/blanket-penalty proposals stay omitted
J.S. VERMA -> operationalisation through teaching, awareness and existing law
RULE -> illustrative nexus, not an exhaustive or false one-to-one statutory map.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 11/12: Criticism, civic republicanism and graded reform
CRITICISM -> vague/open-textured | no general sanction | citizen-only text
           -> Emergency provenance | selective moral policing | State hypocrisy risk
REPLY -> constitutional visibility | active citizenship | interpretive value
      -> fraternity, environmental citizenship, scientific temper, public ethics
COERCION RISK -> majoritarian definitions of patriotism/culture/harmony
SAFEGUARDS -> constitutional literacy -> institutional example -> enforce existing law
           -> civil/administrative measures -> narrow harm-based legislation
           -> equality, conscience, due process, proportionality and review
