ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: 2015 origin, legal status and power boundary
1 JANUARY 2015 -> Union Cabinet resolution creates NITI Aayog
STATUS -> executive + non-constitutional + non-statutory
ROLE -> advise, convene, strategise, monitor and support capacity
NO OWN POWER -> legislate, tax, allocate divisible pool or impose State plans

LIMIT: recommendation becomes action only through competent government authority.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Mandate: think, convene and learn
THINK -> strategy, policy research and knowledge networks
CONVENE -> Union-State dialogue and inter-sector coordination
LEARN -> monitoring, evaluation, feedback and adaptation
FOUNDATION -> shared vision + bottom-up plans + vulnerable groups + technology

TRAP: an official objective is a mandate, not evidence of achieved outcomes.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Composition and notification-sensitive roles
CHAIRPERSON -> Prime Minister
GOVERNING COUNCIL -> State CMs + relevant UT leadership
EXPERT SIDE -> Vice-Chairperson + full-time and part-time members
UNION LINK -> ex-officio Ministers + special invitees
ADMINISTRATION -> CEO

CONTROL: names, portfolios and invitees require a dated official notification.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Governing Council and Regional Councils
GOVERNING COUNCIL -> continuing PM-chaired development-policy forum
MEMBERS -> State and UT political leadership under current constitution/order
REGIONAL COUNCIL -> temporary, issue-specific, multi-State or regional mechanism
PROCESS -> agenda -> joint diagnosis -> consultation -> agreed follow-up

LIMIT: consensus is political coordination, not binding law or a State veto.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Planning Commission transition
CONTINUITY -> both bodies arose through executive resolution
PLANNING COMMISSION -> Five-Year Plans + plan-resource influence + central negotiation
NITI -> indicative strategy + federal dialogue + monitoring + knowledge
LATER REFORM -> plan/non-plan budget distinction ended separately

VERDICT: directive allocation shifted toward advisory coordination and softer leverage.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Cooperative and competitive federalism
COOPERATIVE -> shared agenda + State voice + joint problem-solving + capacity support
COMPETITIVE -> common indicator -> comparison -> reputation -> administrative response
STATE SUPPORT -> policy and institutional capability partnerships
RISKS -> central agenda power + boycott/non-attendance + unequal capacity + data gaming

BALANCE: participation improves legitimacy only when evidence and follow-up are credible.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Evidence, indicators and evaluation
OUTCOME -> indicator -> baseline -> verified data -> comparison
        -> diagnosis -> intervention -> reassessment
DMEO -> monitoring and evaluation support
SAFE REPORTING -> rank + absolute level + change + uncertainty + field evidence

TRAP: dashboard movement does not by itself establish causation or durable welfare gain.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Aspirational Districts and Blocks
CONVERGENCE -> align schemes and agencies
COLLABORATION -> Centre + State + district/block teams
COMPETITION -> delta indicators and peer comparison
DELIVERY -> remains with competent governments and field administration

LIMIT: NITI coordinates and monitors; it does not directly deliver every service.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: SDGs, DMEO and Atal Innovation Mission
SDG LOCALISATION -> goals -> indicators -> State/local attention
DMEO -> programme monitoring and evaluation
AIM -> innovation initiative under NITI; architecture is date-sensitive
PROGRAMME TEST -> owner + mechanism + implementer + evidence + limit

PYQ 2019: institutional ownership matters more than the programme name.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: NITI and neighbouring institutions
NITI -> executive strategy, dialogue and monitoring
FINANCE COMMISSION -> Article 280 fiscal recommendations
GST COUNCIL -> Article 279A GST recommendations
INTER-STATE COUNCIL -> Article 263 coordination route
ZONAL COUNCILS -> statutory regional cooperation
STATE FINANCE COMMISSIONS -> State-local fiscal recommendations

Never transfer one body’s source or power to another.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Strengths, deficits and accountable reform
STRENGTHS -> flexibility + expertise + convening + cross-sector evidence
DEFICITS -> non-binding advice + central agenda + weak reporting + data risk
REFORM -> State agenda windows + published consultations + independent data audit
        -> action tracking + parliamentary visibility + implementation feedback

GOAL: retain flexibility while making influence transparent and evaluable.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/12: PYQ routes and answer synthesis
PYQ 2018 -> NITI principles versus Planning Commission
PYQ 2019 -> Atal Innovation Mission institutional location
PRELIMS -> executive != unconstitutional | Council != legislature | index != power
MAINS -> origin -> mandate -> composition -> federal mechanism -> programme evidence
        -> fiscal/data limits -> accountable reform -> qualified verdict

NITI succeeds through credible persuasion, State ownership and implementation.
