Repair summary
--------------
The package teaches 36 visual-first Core sessions from the Nanda-Magadha base through rulers,
inscriptional geography, layered administration, political economy, society, Kalinga, dhamma,
Buddhism, scripts, monuments and the centralisation debate. Ashokan inscriptions remain primary;
the Arthashastra, Megasthenes, Puranic lists and Buddhist/Jain traditions are source-qualified.
Far-southern and north-eastern limits, influence-versus-rule, regional diversity and the bounded
post-Ashoka bridge to Topic 15 are explicit.

Practice and validation
-----------------------
Exactly 32 original hard/remedial MCQs precede PYQs; ABCD repeats eight times; all 128 option
explanations and 32 traps are unique. Five direct routed Prelims PYQs and two adjacent cross-owned
questions retain key discipline. Six original solved Mains comprise 2 x 10, 2 x 15 and 2 x 20
markers, all within 150/250-word ceilings.

Graphical master: 4,800 x 15,000 pixels at approximately 300 dpi.
Tiles: rows 0-4050, 3650-7700, 7300-11350 and 10950-15000; 400-pixel overlap.
All PDFs are indexed where applicable, nonblank and free of detected text overflow; poster and
tiles preserve the exact saved master pixels.

Remaining limits
----------------
Exact ruler dates, Seleucid territorial and epigamia clauses, Bindusara's conquests, provincial
boundaries, army totals, Megasthenes' committees, Arthashastra implementation, realised tax rates,
land rights, social prevalence, edict reception and local administrative depth remain incomplete
or debated. Third Council and mission detail is chiefly later chronicle tradition. Detailed decline
belongs to Topic 15; detailed style/iconography belongs to Indian Art and Culture.
