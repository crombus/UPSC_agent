THE HARAPPAN CIVILIZATION — ASCII MASTER FLOW DIAGRAM

[1] NAME + TIME + METHOD
    Harappan/Indus civilisation = Bronze Age archaeological civilisation
    Early c. 3300-2600 BCE -> Mature c. 2600-1900 BCE -> Late c. 1900-1300 BCE
    regionalisation            integration                 localisation
    CAUTION: dates vary by site; phases are processes, not sealed peoples.

[2] DISCOVERY + EVIDENCE CONTROL
    Cunningham seal (1853) -> Sahni at Harappa (1921) -> Banerji at Mohenjo-daro
    -> Marshall's announcement -> later household/landscape/scientific archaeology
    script undeciphered -> strong claims: building/craft/diet/exchange
                         -> weak claims: named rulers/language/doctrine/law
        |
        v
[3] GEOGRAPHICAL SYSTEM
    west Sutkagen-dor | north Manda | east Alamgirpur | south Malvan
    Indus core + Ghaggar-Hakra/Haryana-Rajasthan + Kutch/Gujarat + Shortugai outpost
    Ghaggar-Hakra palaeochannel != automatically the textual Sarasvati.
        |
        v
[4] SETTLEMENT AND LABOUR HIERARCHY
    farms/herds/resource zones -> villages -> craft/route centres -> towns -> cities
    food + labour + copper/stone/shell/timber -> workshops/public works/consumption
    urbanism depended on rural production; labour status is not directly known.
        |
        v
[5] SITE-FEATURE PRECISION
    Harappa: Ravi, R-37, working platforms, 12-unit 'granary' caution
    Mohenjo-daro: Great Bath, wells/drains, bronze Dancing Girl, 'Priest-King' caution
    Dholavira: Khadir, stone, multi-sector plan, reservoirs, signboard
    Rakhigarhi: Haryana, large inland complex, craft/cemetery; one-genome limit
    Kalibangan: ploughed field, fire installations; not automatic Vedic ritual
    Lothal: bead/copper craft, brick basin; 'dockyard' qualified
    Banawali: sequence, moat/fortification, plough model, seals/weights
    Chanhudaro: unfortified craft town | Surkotada: disputed equid
    Shortugai: Afghanistan procurement node, not automatic imperial province
        |
        v
[6] BUILT FORM + WATER
    shared brick/street/courtyard norms + regional materials/layouts
    well/reservoir -> bathing floor -> house drain -> street drain/soak/inspection
    Dholavira water storage != Mohenjo-daro well-drain emphasis
    coordination and maintenance secure; political office unknown.

[7] SUBSISTENCE + PRODUCTION
    wheat/barley + regional millets/rice + pulses/oilseeds + cotton
    cattle/sheep/goat/buffalo/pig + fish/wild resources; horse rare/disputed
    beads + shell + faience + pottery + textiles + copper/bronze metallurgy
    BRONZE AGE: no iron-centred technology, no minted coinage.

[8] MEASUREMENT + INTERNAL/EXTERNAL EXCHANGE
    graded weights + scales + seals + transport -> comparable production/exchange
    internal: food, fibre, metals, stone, shell, timber and finished goods
    external: Meluhha -> Indus-related eastern sphere; Dilmun/Magan -> Gulf links
    contact secure; route, volume, colony and state control remain qualified.
        |
        v
[9] SCRIPT + AUTHORITY
    short inscriptions; many right-to-left; several hundred signs; undeciphered
    standardisation/public works -> strong coordination
    central state vs regional states vs corporate elites -> unresolved
    no secure king-list, dynasty, palace, royal tomb or constitutional text.
        |
        v
[10] SOCIETY + RELIGION
    houses + rare goods + craft zones + burials -> differentiation/occupation
    NOT automatic caste, slavery, matriarchy or complete class map
    horned figure + female figurines + pipal/animals + fire features + Great Bath
    -> possible rituals; NOT secure Shiva/Mother Goddess/Vedic/state religion.

        v
[11] DECLINE -> TRANSFORMATION
    river/flood change + monsoon stress + exchange contraction + reorganisation
    -> uneven post-1900 BCE city contraction, settlement shifts, reduced script/seals
    -> Late Harappan regionalisation, agricultural diversification and continuity
    NOT one invasion, one drought, one river or instant extinction.
