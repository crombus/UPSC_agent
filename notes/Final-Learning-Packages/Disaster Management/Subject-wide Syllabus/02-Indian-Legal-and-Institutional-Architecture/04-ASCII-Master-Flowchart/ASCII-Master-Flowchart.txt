INDIAN LEGAL AND INSTITUTIONAL ARCHITECTURE
==============================================

1. LEGAL EVOLUTION
Relief administration -> DM Act 2005 -> NPDM 2009 -> NDMP 2016/2019 -> 2025 amendment

2. FEDERAL CASCADE
NDMA/NEC -> SDMA/SEC -> DDMA -> local authorities

3. ROLE SEPARATION
Authority: policy and plan direction
Executive committee: coordination, monitoring and implementation
Operational arm: specialist response
Knowledge arm: training, research and capacity building

4. NATIONAL TIER
Prime Minister -> NDMA
Union Home Secretary -> NEC
Cabinet Secretary -> NCMC
HLC -> specified financial assistance

5. STATE TIER
Chief Minister -> SDMA
Chief Secretary -> SEC
State may notify UDMA under s.41A
State may constitute specialist response force under s.44A

6. DISTRICT AND LOCAL TIER
Collector/Magistrate -> DDMA
Elected local representative -> Co-Chair
DDMA -> District Plan -> local execution

7. SPECIALIST CAPACITY
NDRF force -> search, rescue, pre-positioning
NIDM -> training, research, documentation
NPDRR -> multi-stakeholder policy review

8. PLANNING
National Plan -> State Plan -> District Plan -> local/facility plans
2016: first NDMP
2019: latest published revision

9. FINANCE
s.46 response fund
s.47 mitigation fund
s.48 state and district response/mitigation funds
Force != fund

10. 2025 DATA AND ACCOUNTABILITY
s.2(da) database definition
s.6(2)(v) national database
s.18(2)(p) state databases
plan review every 3 years; update at least every 5 years

11. EVIDENCE LADDER
law -> notification -> staff/budget -> plan/drill/expenditure -> output -> outcome

12. ANSWER SPINE
law + tiers + plan + capacity + finance + data + local implementation + measured outcome
