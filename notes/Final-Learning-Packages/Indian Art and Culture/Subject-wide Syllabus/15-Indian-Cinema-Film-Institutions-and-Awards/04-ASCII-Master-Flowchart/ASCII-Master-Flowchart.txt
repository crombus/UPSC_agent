ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/4: MEDIUM AND FILM LANGUAGE
CINEMA AS MODERN ART
    story + performance + image + sound + editing -> reproducible mass medium
    cinema != theatre because camera, editing, recorded sound and repeatability matter

FILM LANGUAGE
    cinematography -> frame / light / movement
    mise-en-scene -> visible arrangement inside frame
    editing -> continuity / rhythm / contrast
    documentary -> non-fiction but framed
    animation -> full moving-image form

REGULATION

    CBFC -> statutory body under MIB
    cuts / refusal powers -> censoring effect
    High Court appeal caution; do not present FCAT as current
    dubbed films need fresh certificate

INSTITUTIONS BY FUNCTION

    NFAI legacy / NFHM / NFDC-linked preservation -> archive and restoration
    NFDC -> development + merged public film architecture
    IFFI -> festival platform
    National Film Awards / Dadasaheb Phalke Award -> recognition
