ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Central thesis
Temple architecture is best read as an evolving regional argument about sacred
space, power, material, sculpture and institutional life - not as a monument list.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Core vocabulary
jagati -> ardhamandapa -> mandapa -> antarala -> garbhagriha.
vertical terms -> jangha / shikhara / urushringa / amalaka / kalasha.
Dravida path -> vimana / gopura / prakara / corridor / tank.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Chronology rail
Gupta structural beginnings -> mature Nagara and early Dravida -> Pallava turn
-> Chola imperial scale -> Deccan experiments -> Vijayanagara and Nayaka.
Keep ancient development separate from modern recovery and UNESCO years.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Nagara family
ODISHA -> rekha deul sanctum + pidha deul hall + enclosure-conscious Kalinga form.
KHAJURAHO -> high jagati + no enclosure wall + clustered shikhara + dense sculpture.
SOLANKI -> Modhera + Surya Kund + distinct western Nagara solution.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Pallava to Chola
Pallava -> rock-cut cave -> monolithic ratha -> structural temple.
Chola -> early shrines -> Brihadishvara climax -> later gopura emphasis.
Rule -> write architecture with bronzes, murals and inscriptions together.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/12: Deccan caution
Aihole = incubator.
Badami = refinement.
Pattadakal = Nagara and Dravida evidence on one site.
Vesara = useful label only after morphology is described.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Hoysala and Kakatiya
HOYSALA -> soapstone + stellate jagati + zig-zag walls + lathe-turned pillars.
KAKATIYA -> Thousand Pillar and Ramappa -> sandbox technique + named architect.
Both prove that material and engineering matter, not style labels alone.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Vijayanagara and Nayaka
Vijayanagara -> chariot streets + mandapas + Raya gopuram + sacred-royal city.
Nayaka -> concentric enclosures + graded gopuras + tanks + prakarams + stucco.
Mechanism -> visual emphasis shifts from vimana to gateway and corridor system.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Temple as institution
ritual + patronage + endowment + festival + administration + craft labour.
Use inscriptions, merchant-guild traces and named artisans.
Do not treat temple evidence as a full social census.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: Chandella and Khajuraho dossier
Jejabhukti / Bundelkhand -> Chandella assertion -> Khajuraho clusters.
Most temples sandstone; Chausath Yogini, Brahma and Mahadeva are granite exceptions.
Khajuraho Chausath Yogini must be kept separate from the circular Morena example.
Kandariya Mahadeva = culmination of tower, wall and sculpture integration.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Khajuraho imagery caution
breadth of life -> deities, worshippers, teachers, dancers, musicians, courtly life.
erotic imagery -> real but only one register among many.
Do not reduce the site to sexuality or invent unsupported percentages.
