Package notes
-------------
- This repaired package uses one corrected conceptual spine across the learning session, workbook, quick-glance chart, ASCII flowchart and graphical flowchart.
- The package teaches Indian music as theory plus performance grammar plus patronage: shruti/swara -> raga/tala -> Hindustani/Carnatic -> forms -> gharana/guru-shishya -> instruments -> folk/devotional/fusion -> institutions and modern continuity.
- Carnatic improvisation is kept explicit through alapana, neraval, kalpana swara and RTP; Hindustani-Carnatic comparison avoids foreign-versus-pure caricature.
- The Aurangzeb cliché is corrected, instrument identification is solved through mechanism first, and the 2026 Mallikarjun Mansur route remains a bounded gap rather than a guessed answer.
- Objective/application PYQs retain answer letters only where a locally verified official key exists; verification-pending and provisional-key items use the exact withholding formula.
