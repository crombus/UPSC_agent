Package notes
-------------
- This repaired package uses one corrected conceptual spine across the learning session, workbook, quick-glance chart, ASCII flowchart and graphical flowchart.
- The package teaches Topic 10 as Natya Shastra grammar -> classical stage vocabulary -> convention/decline -> Kutiyattam -> folk theatre publics -> colonial/modern stage -> puppetry by mechanism -> ritual/martial/public ecology -> safeguarding.
- Mechanism-first puppetry classification is enforced: string, shadow, rod and glove; Bommalattam is retained only as a hybrid caution.
- Objective/application PYQs print no answer letter unless a final official or local-official key was independently verified in-session; both objective PYQs here therefore retain the standard withholding formula.
- Kutiyattam is kept as a living-theatre continuity line and a 2008 UNESCO Representative-List inscription, not as proof that Sanskrit theatre never contracted.
- Topic boundaries remain explicit: theatre owns enacted dramaturgy and stage ecology here; music, dance, literary history and the full institution/UNESCO ledgers are not duplicated.
