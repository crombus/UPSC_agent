ASCII PANEL 2/12 - Natya Shastra frame
----------------------------------------
NATYA SHASTRA
Bharata -> Natya Veda -> text + enactment + music + rasa
                |
                +-- Lokadharmi = realistic depiction of life
                +-- Natyadharmi = stylised symbolic narration

WRITE:
theatre is composite performance, not literature alone.

DO NOT WRITE:
one falsely exact date or unchanged continuity across all later forms.

ASCII PANEL 4/12 - Convention, archaeology and decline
--------------------------------------------------------
SANSKRIT THEATRE
four-to-seven acts
ordered opening -> progression -> pause -> conclusion
rare tragedy / order-restoring closure
Bharata stage prescription: about 400 persons, two levels, curtains

QUALIFICATION
prescription != archaeology
Sitabenga / Jogimara linked to performance by some scholars,
but 'world's oldest amphitheatre' is disputed.

DECLINE
plays -> poems and songs
difficult Sanskrit / wider Pali-Prakrit use
changed patronage

ASCII PANEL 5/12 - Kutiyattam continuity
------------------------------------------
KUTIYATTAM / KOODIYATTAM
Kerala
oldest continuing theatre lineage in the source-owned bank
Sanskrit + Prakrit + Malayalam
Mizhavu + Edakka
Nirvahana before story expansion

UNESCO OFFICIAL PAGE
one of India's oldest living theatrical traditions
synthesis of Sanskrit classicism and local Kerala practice
inscribed in 2008 on the Representative List

ASCII PANEL 7/12 - Regional theatres I
----------------------------------------
YAKSHAGANA  -> Karnataka / parts of Kerala, Vijayanagara memory,
               Vaishnava influence, dance-drama energy
JATRA       -> eastern India, open-air, Chaitanya route,
               later secular/patriotic themes
NAUTANKI    -> north India, offshoot of Swang,
               lyrical dialogue + Nagada
TAMASHA     -> Maharashtra, humour + erotic charge + Lavani
BHAVAI      -> Gujarat, dance-rich small plays, pots/pitchers,
               Nayaka as Sutradhar

ASCII PANEL 11/12 - Puppet ecology
------------------------------------
STRING  = articulated body
SHADOW  = projected silhouette
ROD     = larger standing manipulation
GLOVE   = intimate hand control

PERFORMANCE ECOLOGY

screen or curtain
music and rhythm support
village portability / travelling stage

ARCHIVE VALUE
Mahabharata + Silappadikaram + Bhagavad Gita metaphor

ASCII PANEL 12/12 - Safeguarding and final answer route
---------------------------------------------------------
TWO DECLINE TYPES
Sanskrit theatre = historical contraction with named causes
Puppetry         = ongoing audience/funding vulnerability

THEREFORE
text/documentation alone cannot safeguard a living performance.
Living forms need performers, pedagogy, repertory and audience.
