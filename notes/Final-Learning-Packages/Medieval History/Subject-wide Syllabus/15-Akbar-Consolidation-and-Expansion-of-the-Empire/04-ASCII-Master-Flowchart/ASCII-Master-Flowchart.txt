ASCII MASTER FLOW — Akbar: Consolidation & Expansion of the Empire

PANEL 1: THE WHOLE TOPIC
1556 fragile succession -> Panipat II saves core -> regency ends -> nobles disciplined -> Rajput bargain expands coalition -> Gujarat/Bengal/frontier gains -> Deccan opened but unfinished.

PANEL 2: CHRONOLOGY RAIL
Kalanaur 1556 | Panipat II 1556 | Bairam removed 1560 | Malwa 1561 | Garh-Katanga 1564 | Uzbek challenge broken 1567 | Chittor 1568 | Ranthambhor 1569 | Gujarat 1572-73 | Tukaroi 1575 | Rajmahal + Haldighati 1576 | Kashmir 1586 | Sindh 1591 | Qandahar 1595 | late Deccan opening | 1605 death.

PANEL 3: CORE RECOVERY
Humayun dies -> young Akbar at Kalanaur -> Hemu takes Delhi-Agra -> Panipat II restores Mughal core -> Afghan alternatives survive in the east.

PANEL 4: FROM REGENCY TO PERSONAL RULE
Bairam Khan stabilises minority kingship -> Akbar dismisses regent -> Adham Khan kills Atka Khan -> emperor punishes household violence -> personal sovereignty becomes visible.

PANEL 5: INTERNAL CONSOLIDATION
Household access problem + Uzbek/old-noble challenge -> 1567 major resistance broken -> centre strong enough for larger campaigns.

PANEL 6: EARLY EXPANSION WITH COERCION
Malwa -> Baz Bahadur defeated, later incorporated.
Garh-Katanga -> Rani Durgavati resists; conquest exposes plunder and coercive cost.

PANEL 7: RAJPUT POLICY
Amber alliance -> Bhara Mal, Bhagwant Das, Man Singh.
Mechanism -> honour + office + service + watan security.
Counter-example to marriage-only view -> Surjan Hada.

PANEL 8: CHITTOR TO MEWAR
Chittor 1568 -> strategic fort + massacre.
Ranthambhor 1569 -> settlement route.
Haldighati 1576 -> Mughal field advantage, no final closure because Pratap survives.

PANEL 9: GUJARAT
Ports + urban wealth + Mirza risk -> conquest 1572-73 -> rapid return shows retention mattered as much as victory.

PANEL 10: BENGAL AND THE EAST
Daud Khan Karrani -> Tukaroi -> temporary arrangement -> Rajmahal -> annexation.
Lesson: riverine geography made retention harder than one battle suggests.

PANEL 11: FRONTIER ARC
Mirza Hakim/Kabul -> Lahore focus -> Kashmir 1586 -> Sindh 1591 -> Qandahar 1595.
Rule: frontier gains created strategic depth, not effortless permanence.

PANEL 12: LATE DECCAN AND VERDICT
Ahmadnagar / Chand Bibi / Berar / Khandesh / Asirgarh -> Deccan opening, not full settlement.
Final verdict -> durable but uneven empire; conquest, incorporation and vigilance worked together.
