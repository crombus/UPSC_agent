7. PREVIEWS AND CONTACT SHEET - GENERATION METHOD
   Provenance correction. Earlier builds of this report described the sheet previews and
   the poster preview as "1:1 crops" of the master canvas. That description was wrong and
   is withdrawn. None of the preview images in previews/ is a 1:1 pixel crop. Every one of
   them is a reduced-resolution resample of a larger source, produced as follows.

   previews/page-001.png ... previews/page-006.png
     One per printed sheet. Each is produced by cropping that sheet's region out of the
     4800 px-wide master and then resampling the crop down to 1400 px wide with PIL's
     bicubic filter (Image.resize(..., Image.BICUBIC)), i.e. a scale factor of
     1400 / 4800 = 0.29167. They are downscaled renders, not 1:1 crops.
     page-001 to page-005 are 1400 x 974 px; page-006 is 1400 x 548 px (short tail sheet).

   previews/poster-page-001.png
     652 x 2347 px (measured with PIL after generation). Rendered in this pass directly
     from the regenerated At-a-Glance-Poster.pdf with PyMuPDF, using
     Page.get_pixmap(matrix=fitz.Matrix(z, z)) with z = 652 / 1145.20 = 0.56933 applied to
     the single 1145.20 x 4121.05 pt poster page. This is a full-page rasterisation of the
     whole poster at a reduced zoom - PyMuPDF's own downsampling rasteriser - and it is
     therefore a downscaled whole-page render, not a 1:1 crop of any region.
     Its aspect ratio 1 : 3.60 matches the poster page aspect
     (1145.20 : 4121.05 = 1 : 3.60), which is the arithmetic signature of a whole-page
     reduction rather than a crop. Opened and read by the executor: the reduced image shows
     the document title block, all ten numbered stages 01 to 10 in order and the unbroken
     cyan rail running the full height.

   previews/poster-fit.png
     1141 x 4106 px. Regenerated in this pass by bicubic-resampling the whole 4800 x 17273
     master down to 1141 px wide (scale factor 1141 / 4800 = 0.23771). Also a downscaled
     whole-poster render, not a crop.

   previews/contact-sheet-01.png
     2560 x 902 px. Regenerated in this pass from the six new sheet previews. Each sheet
     preview is further bicubic-thumbnailed into a 620 x 431 px cell and pasted into a
     4-column x 2-row grid (margin 10 px, gap 20 px, two empty cells at the end) on the
     document background colour. Every cell is therefore a twice-downscaled render of a
     sheet region; no cell is a 1:1 crop and no cell contains the poster preview.

   Sheets 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 were opened and read by the executor on the regenerated
   package. Headers fit inside their cards, no text crosses a card boundary, no column
   overruns its neighbour, and both facts added in this pass are legible at reading size.
