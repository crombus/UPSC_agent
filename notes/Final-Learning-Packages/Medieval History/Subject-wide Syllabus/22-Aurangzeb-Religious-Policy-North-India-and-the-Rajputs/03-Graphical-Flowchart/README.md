# Graphical Flowchart — Aurangzeb: Religious Policy, North India & the Rajputs

One continuous at-a-glance rail carries the whole topic from the 1658 accession
result to the graded verdict on North Indian legitimacy.

## What is here

- `High-Resolution-Master.png` — the full rail in one image.
- `At-a-Glance-Poster.pdf` — the same rail on a single tall page for screen revision.
- `Printable-Tiled-Version.pdf` — the same rail cut into overlapping A3 landscape
  sheets. Print all pages, trim nothing, and overlap the repeated strip to join them.
- `previews\` — reduced page images and a contact sheet for quick reference.

## What the rail covers

Chronology and topic boundary; court-symbolic periodisation; law, sovereignty and
the 1679 jizyah; the temple-policy evidence ladder; North Indian unrest and the
Sikh case; the pre-1678 Rajput baseline; differentiated incorporation; the
Marwar-Mewar rupture; historiography and the graded verdict; and Prelims/Mains
application.
