FINAL TRAP CONTROL
- Recommendation != statute != notification != implementation.
- 41% applies to divisible-pool net proceeds, not gross receipts.
- Zero-rated != exempt != nil-rated != non-GST.
- Vertical division is between levels; horizontal division is among States.
