[01 POVERTY TAXONOMY]
  |- absolute -> fixed real minimum
  |- relative -> position in current distribution
  |- transient -> temporary shock
  |- chronic -> persistent deprivation
  `- poverty != inequality != vulnerability

[08 INCLUSIVE-GROWTH ENGINE]

  |- jobs + assets -> market income
  |- services -> capability
  |- taxes + transfers -> redistribution

[09 FISCAL + DELIVERY CHAIN]

  |- fiscal incidence -> net distribution
  |- allocation -> release -> spend
  |- output -> quality -> outcome
