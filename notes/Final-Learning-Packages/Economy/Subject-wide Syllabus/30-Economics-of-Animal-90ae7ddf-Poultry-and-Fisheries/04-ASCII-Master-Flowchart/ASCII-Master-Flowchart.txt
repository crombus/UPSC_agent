ECONOMY TOPIC 30 - ANIMAL REARING, LIVESTOCK, DAIRY, POULTRY AND FISHERIES
CURRENT-STATUS CUTOFF: 10 SEPTEMBER 2026
================================================================================
PANEL 1 BOUNDARY AND STATISTICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
terrestrial livestock/poultry | aquaculture = managed farming | capture = wild stock
Livestock Census = point-in-time stock | ISS = annual flow | BAHS = publication
latest released stock: 20th Census 2019; 21st results not public at cutoff

PANEL 2 ALLIED-SECTOR ROLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
seasonal crop cash + daily/short-cycle animal output + liquid asset
-> income diversification + risk smoothing + nutrition + rural work
inclusion test: ownership | payment control | commons/water | voice | care burden

PANEL 3 PRODUCTION ECONOMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
revenue + by-products + asset change
- feed - labour - health - breeding - housing - finance - mortality - logistics
= net enterprise income
policy target: healthy lifetime productivity and value, not headcount alone

PANEL 4 INPUT COMPLEMENTARITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
suitable genetics x feed/fodder x health/reproduction x management
x heat adaptation x collection/market = realised output and margin
one binding constraint can defeat investment in every other input

PANEL 5 PUBLIC GOODS, ONE HEALTH AND WELFARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
surveillance -> vaccination/biosecurity -> lower transmission
compensation/insurance -> honest reporting -> faster control
animal <-> human <-> environment | AMR/residue/zoonosis
welfare improves ethics, survival, quality and market access

PANEL 6 DAIRY VALUE CHAIN AND MODELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
production -> collection -> fat/SNF test -> chilling -> processing -> market
Operation Flood: 1970-80 | 1981-85 | 1985-96
Anand: village society -> district union -> state federation
compare cooperative | producer company | private | informal on power and services

PANEL 7 LIVESTOCK SCHEMES - DATED STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rashtriya Gokul Mission (RGM): genetics; to 30 Sep 2026; Rs 800 cr
National Livestock Mission (NLM): enterprise + fodder + innovation/insurance
Livestock Health and Disease Control Programme (LHDCP): vaccination/services
NPDD/Dairy Development + IDF/AHIDF: procurement and infrastructure

PANEL 8 POULTRY AND OTHER LIVESTOCK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
broiler = meat | layer = egg | backyard != commercial intensive
integrator supplies chick/feed/vet/market; farmer supplies shed/labour/utilities
risk is reallocated, not removed
sheep/goat -> dryland/commons | piggery -> fast cycle + high disease sensitivity

PANEL 9 FISHERIES PRODUCTION AND FEDERALISM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
marine/inland/brackish x capture/culture x extensive/intensive
0-12 nautical miles: State/UT fisheries regulation
beyond territorial waters: Union competence | coastal aquaculture: CAA Act
2024-25 fish output: 19.775 million tonnes (Economic Survey 2025-26)

PANEL 10 FISHERIES BIOECONOMICS AND VALUE CHAIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
open access -> race to fish -> excess effort -> stock/profit loss
MSY = biological yield | MEY = economic rent | equity/ecosystem targets differ
harvest -> landing/ice -> cold transport -> processing -> domestic/export
RAS: mechanical solids removal + microbial nitrification + oxygen/disinfection

PANEL 11 FISHERIES POLICY - DATED STATUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PMMSY: 2020-21--2024-25; FY 2026-27 BE Rs 2,500 cr, tenure qualified
Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF): to 2025-26
Kisan Credit Card (KCC) 2018 | PM-MKSSY/NFDP: credit/insurance/traceability
