[01 LABOUR-MARKET ACCOUNTING]
  |- specified population -> labour force + outside labour force
  |- LFPR = labour force / population
  |- WPR = employed / population
  |- UR = unemployed / labour force
  |- outside labour force is not unemployed
  `- UPSC rule: preserve definition, date, denominator and legal status
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[02 REFERENCE-PERIOD MAP]
  |- usual status ps+ss -> 365 days
  |- CWS -> seven days; one hour work qualifies
  |- CDS -> day/half-day person-days
  |- annual usual status != monthly CWS
  |- state age group and geography
