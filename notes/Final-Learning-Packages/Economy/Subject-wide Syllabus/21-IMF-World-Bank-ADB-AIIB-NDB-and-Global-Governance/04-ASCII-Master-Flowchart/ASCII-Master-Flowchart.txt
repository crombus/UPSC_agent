PANEL 05: SDR AND RESERVE TRANCHE
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SDR = reserve asset + unit of account; not currency/grant/loan                |
| Reserve tranche = member's liquid IMF reserve claim                           |
| Gold tranche = historical synonym, not another facility                       |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
