ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/20: SCOPE AND GOVERNING PROPOSITION
CENTRAL QUESTION: what did British rule DO to the Indian economy?
GOVERNING PROPOSITION:
  MODERNISATION OF CIRCULATION WITHOUT AUTONOMOUS DEVELOPMENT
        goods, information and people moved faster
        AND
        surplus direction, risk placement and policy control
        were located outside Indian hands
THREE IDEAS KEPT SEPARATE (never merged):
  COMPANY PLUNDER      episodic, conquest-linked extraction
  CONTINUING DRAIN     ongoing, institutional external transfer
  STRUCTURAL           the long-run outcome in land, craft,
  UNDERDEVELOPMENT     trade and industry

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/20: PERMANENT SETTLEMENT, 1793

UNIT the zamindari estate | REVISION fixed in PERPETUITY
        |
        v

        ESTATE ITSELF is sold
        |
        v

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/20: THE 'HOW FAR' ANSWER ARCHITECTURE (2024 GS-I Q13)

       Industrial Revolution -> cheaper machine yarn and cloth

       tariff asymmetry | colonial control of commercial policy
       court decline    | railway access to interior markets

       an economy with TARIFF AUTONOMY could have slowed,

       'substantially responsible, but not sufficient without
        colonial policy'   — NOT yes / no

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 19/20: FAMINE SEQUENCE, POLICY AND VARIATION

      codified -> Famine Code framework

--------------------------------------------------------------

severe famine recur? -> tests the DOCTRINE, not the chronology.
