ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Ballistic versus cruise
BALLISTIC -> powered boost then largely ballistic path

CRUISE DRIVER -> air-breathing propulsion and aerodynamic control
TRAP -> range alone never determines the class
