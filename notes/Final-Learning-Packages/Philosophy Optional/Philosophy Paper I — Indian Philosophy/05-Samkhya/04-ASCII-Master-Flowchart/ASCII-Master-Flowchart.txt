  mūla-prakṛti      ROOT: cause of all, effect of none         fixes its place in the 1/7/16/1 grid

  EXAM-SAFE DEFINITION: prakṛti is the AVYAKTA PRADHĀNA — the unmanifest chief principle
  constituted by the three guṇas.

  WHAT THE RESERVOIR CONTAINS  ! "material" here means UNCONSCIOUS, not physical  [trap 11]
    +-------------+---------------+-----------------+------------------------------+
    | buddhi      | ahaṃkāra      | manas + 10      | 5 tanmātra -> 5 mahābhūta    |
    | intellect   | ego-maker     | capacities      | subtle -> gross elements     |
    +-------------+---------------+-----------------+------------------------------+

  WHY A SECOND PRINCIPLE IS FORCED
    1 the world shows plurality, transformation, dependence, purposiveness, structure
    2 a school accepting REAL effects, REAL bondage and REAL liberation needs a REAL
      material source; it may not dissolve the world into appearance
    3 puruṣa is CHANGELESS and INACTIVE => it cannot itself become the world

  ANSWER LINE: Sāṃkhya's dualism is misdescribed as mind against matter; intellect, ego
  and mind sit firmly inside unconscious nature, so the fault-line runs between everything
  that changes and the one thing that merely watches.
