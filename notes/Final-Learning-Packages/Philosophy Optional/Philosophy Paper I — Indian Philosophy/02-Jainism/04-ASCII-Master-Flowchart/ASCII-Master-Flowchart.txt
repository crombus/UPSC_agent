ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/12: Conditional predication and the sevenfold predication
 THE OPERATOR syat = "in a certain respect"
   a DEFINITE assertion with its condition stated
   NOT "perhaps", NOT probability, NOT doubt, NOT scepticism
                                   |
                                   v
 WHY EXACTLY SEVEN? three primitives, taken singly, in pairs, and all together
   singly    : asti · nasti · avaktavya                        = 3
   in pairs  : asti+nasti · asti+avaktavya · nasti+avaktavya   = 3
   jointly   : asti+nasti+avaktavya                            = 1
                                                        TOTAL  = 7
                                   |
                                   v
 THE SEVEN, IN ORDER
   1 syad asti                          in some respect, it IS
   2 syan nasti                         in some respect, it IS NOT
   3 syad asti ca nasti ca              successively, it is AND is not
   4 syad avaktavyam                    in some respect, it is INEXPRESSIBLE
   5 syad asti ca avaktavyam            it is, and is inexpressible
   6 syan nasti ca avaktavyam           it is not, and is inexpressible
   7 syad asti ca nasti ca avaktavyam   it is, is not, and is inexpressible
                                   |
                                   v
 THE FOUR INDICES THAT BLOCK CONTRADICTION
   dravya  substance -> the jar IS clay, IS NOT gold
   ksetra  place     -> IS on this table, IS NOT in that room
   kala    time      -> IS now, WAS NOT yesterday
   bhava   mode      -> IS jar-shaped, IS NOT lump-shaped
   existence asserted in OWN-FORM (svarupa) · non-existence in OTHER-FORM (pararupa)
   contradiction needs SAME respect, SAME time, SAME sense -> syat guarantees it never holds
                                   |
                                   v
 INEXPRESSIBILITY (avaktavya) = failure of ONE unqualified SIMULTANEOUS utterance
   NOT mystical silence · NOT a third truth-value · NOT suspension of judgement
 BLIND MEN AND THE ELEPHANT -> pedagogical only; one sentence, then return to the indices
 ANSWER LINE -> "syat is an index, not a hedge: the seven forms record conditions of
 truth rather than degrees of doubt."

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Evaluating non-absolutism — three distinct challenges
 CHALLENGE A: SELF-REFUTATION OR TRIVIALITY
   if qualified -> weak;  if unqualified -> inconsistent;  if merely "views differ" -> thin
   JAINA REPLY -> walk through the FIRST horn: the thesis IS asserted syat
                  reflexive qualification is OBEDIENCE, not exemption
                  aspect-conditioned truth is NOT equal truth for all opinions
   [ANALYSIS] the triviality charge is answered only by SHOWING the work the doctrine
   does: holding permanence and change together, and diagnosing rivals as absolutised nayas
                                   |
                                   v
 CHALLENGE B: "RELATIVISM NEEDS ABSOLUTISM"   (2022, Q6(b), 15 marks)
   critic  -> universal scope looks absolute, so the doctrine leans on what it attacks
   reply 1 -> it denies ONE-SIDED EXCLUSIVISM by finite knowers, not comprehensiveness
   reply 2 -> ordinary cognition is ksayopasamika; kevala-jnana is the INTERNAL complete case
   reply 3 -> "relativism" mislabels it: relativism relativises TRUTH, non-absolutism
              relativises EXCLUSIVE CLAIMS
   [ANALYSIS] the charge equivocates on "absolute"; once separated, the contradiction goes
   [RESIDUE] the unconditioned is RELOCATED to omniscience, not abolished -> say so
                                   |
                                   v
 CHALLENGE C: "PLURALISTIC AND REALISTIC?"    (2025, Q7(c), 15 marks)
   PLURALISTIC -> six substances · innumerable souls · many siddhas · no monistic reduction
   REALISTIC   -> substances, qualities and modes objectively real · world not illusion
                  aspects are IN the object, not projected onto it
   QUALIFIER   -> judgement is standpoint-indexed -> QUALIFIED REALISM
   KEY LINE    -> qualification of KNOWLEDGE does not entail unreality of the OBJECT
                                   |
                                   v
 GRADED VERDICT TO CLOSE ANY CRITICAL ANSWER
   STRONG    permanence-and-change; disciplined ontology-to-language sequence;
             causally serious ethics; derived non-violence
   WEAK      soul-matter interaction; unverifiable higher knowledges; heavy taxonomy;
             possible dilution of assertoric force
