  RĀMĀNUJA'S CRITIQUE OF THE QUALITYLESS ABSOLUTE — asked 2023 Q6(a), 20 marks
    an entity WITHOUT attributes cannot be known
    if utterly indeterminate => cannot be the referent of scriptural description
    if no internal intelligibility => cannot cause a richly qualified world
    scriptural passages that NEGATE DEFECTS do not negate ALL qualities
    => Śaṅkara's absolute becomes unknowable emptiness OR an abstraction too thin to
      ground religion, devotion, causality or ethics
  ADVAITA'S REPLY: it is not known as an OBJECT with predicates; it is SELF-REVEALED
    as the ground of all knowing. Denying limiting predicates ≠ denying nature.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/22: Efficient and material causality across the schools

  QUESTION: is ultimate reality the world's MATERIAL cause as well as its EFFICIENT
  cause?   — asked 2025 Q6(b), 15 marks, "with suitable examples"

  DEFINITIONS
    efficient cause (nimitta-kāraṇa) .... the intelligent agency that brings about
    material cause (upādāna-kāraṇa) ..... that OUT OF WHICH the effect is constituted

  ADVAITA ------------------------------------------------------------------------
    premise .... nothing exists independently of ultimate reality
    therefore .. there is NO SECOND MATERIAL out of which the world is made
    so ......... it is BOTH the intelligence behind the world AND the material basis
                 of its APPEARANCE
    image ...... the SPIDER spinning the web out of itself
    qualified by APPARENT TRANSFORMATION (vivarta): it does NOT really become the
    world; the world is an appearance dependent on it
    image ...... the ROPE and the SNAKE — substrate supports what it never becomes
    net ........ "material cause" = SUBSTRATE, not transformed stuff

  VIŚIṢṬĀDVAITA -------------------------------------------------------------------
    both causes, but causation is REAL
    subtle conscious (cit) and non-conscious (acit) modes => gross manifestation
    ultimate reality remains their controlling SUBSTANTIVE WHOLE
    the transformation belongs to it THROUGH the real transformation of the
    NON-CONSCIOUS mode — NOT through corruption of God's auspicious essence
    cause-state = SUBTLE · effect-state = GROSS

  DVAITA --------------------------------------------------------------------------
    God = EFFICIENT cause (nimitta-kāraṇa) ONLY
    primordial matter (prakṛti) = REAL DEPENDENT material cause, energised and
    governed by God
    dependence on God is universal — YET God, souls and matter remain really and
    eternally different
    ! therefore "brahman is both efficient and material cause" is TRUE of Advaita and
      Viśiṣṭādvaita IN DIFFERENT SENSES, and FALSE of Dvaita

  RIVAL SCHOOLS OUTSIDE VEDĀNTA — one-line contrast bank
    Sāṃkhya .... prakṛti is material cause; NO efficient creator-God
    Nyāya ...... God is primarily EFFICIENT; eternal atoms are MATERIAL
    formula .... Sāṃkhya splits material causality from intelligence; Nyāya splits
                 efficient from material causality; Vedānta REUNITES both

  ! TRAP: "all Vedānta schools hold Brahman is both causes" — Madhva denies it
    explicitly.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/22: Six means of valid knowledge and the primacy of testimony

  ADVAITA'S SIX INSTRUMENTS (following the Bhāṭṭa Mīmāṃsā list)
  +------------------+--------------------------------------+------------------------+
  | perception       | immediate cognition through sense-   | "this pot is blue"     |
  | (pratyakṣa)      | contact with a PRESENT object        |                        |
  | inference        | unperceived fact via a perceived     | smoke on the hill =>    |
  | (anumāna)        | mark (liṅga) + concomitance (vyāpti) | fire                   |
  | comparison       | fresh cognition of an ABSENT         | gavaya seen => the      |
  | (upamāna)        | familiar object's similarity         | remembered cow is like |
  | verbal testimony | valid cognition from reliable words, | the great sentence     |
  | (śabda)          | supremely the AUTHORLESS             | teaches self-absolute  |
  |                  | (apauruṣeya) Veda                    | identity               |
  | postulation      | presumption of an unseen fact to     | fat Devadatta does not |
  | (arthāpatti)     | reconcile incompatible known facts   | eat by day => eats at   |
  |                  |                                      | night                  |
  | non-apprehension | cognition of ABSENCE through non-    | "there is no pot on    |
  | (anupalabdhi)    | perception under proper conditions   | the floor"             |
  +------------------+--------------------------------------+------------------------+
                                     |
     EVERY instrument presupposes the triad KNOWER - MEANS - KNOWN
                 (pramātṛ - pramāṇa - prameya)
                                     |
     that triad presupposes DUALITY => all six work ONLY at the EMPIRICAL level
     (vyāvahārika); at the ULTIMATE level (pāramārthika) there is no second object,
     no separate knower, no instrument — the whole apparatus is sublated
                                     |
  REFLEXIVE PUZZLE ─► if all instruments are only empirically valid, how does
                      testimony establish the ULTIMATE truth?
  REPLY ───────────► scripture REMOVES IGNORANCE and then loses operative relevance
                      ALONG WITH the ignorance it removed
  IMAGES ──────────► the CLEARING-NUT (kataka) that settles the mud and settles with
                      it · the STICK that stirs the pyre and is consumed in it
  => scripture is NOT an eternal second reality alongside the absolute; it is the
    EMPIRICAL LADDER by which the non-empirical truth is recognised

  WHY NOT THE OTHER FIVE?
    perception ..... the absolute is qualityless, non-objectifiable, not sensed
    inference ...... no perceived instance, no mark uniquely co-present; and an
                     inferred absolute becomes an inferred OBJECT (prameya)
    comparison, postulation, non-apprehension ..... derivative / empirically
                     parasitic: similarity, presumption and absence all operate
                     INSIDE the field of knowable objects
  => Śaṅkara: the absolute is ŚĀSTRA-YONI / ŚĀSTRA-PRAMĀṆAKA — known through scripture
  => FOUR GREAT SENTENCES: tat tvam asi · ahaṃ brahmāsmi · prajñānaṃ brahma ·
    ayam ātmā brahma

  FAULT-LINE WITH EARLIER MĪMĀṂSĀ
    Mīmāṃsā ..... Vedic authority is primarily authority for INJUNCTION (vidhi) about
                  duty; statements of fact are subordinate to ritual action
    Advaita ..... Upaniṣadic statements about ACCOMPLISHED REALITY (siddha-vastu) are
                  independently and supremely valid; knowledge (jñāna) is NOT an act
                  to be performed (kārya)
    OBJ ......... if the Veda is for action, how can a sentence stating identity
                  produce liberation?
    REPLY ....... bondage is IGNORANCE, not absence of ritual; therefore the direct
                  remover must be KNOWLEDGE
    => the divide is not ritual-versus-metaphysics but whether Vedic sentences can
      independently reveal a NON-ACTIONAL accomplished reality

  ! TRAP: "Advaita rejects perception and inference" — it denies ULTIMACY, not
    empirical validity.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/22: The Lord — three placements and Rāmānuja's critique

  ADVAITA ........ the Lord = ultimate reality CONDITIONED BY illusion
                   (māyā-upahita brahman / māyā-viśiṣṭa brahman)
                   omniscient · omnipotent · moral governor · creator of the empirical
                   world · FULLY REAL at the empirical level (vyāvahārika)
                   YET sublated into the qualityless absolute at the ultimate level
                   => theism is NEITHER false in a crude sense NOR final in the last
  VIŚIṢṬĀDVAITA .. the Lord IS ultimate reality — supreme personal Viṣṇu-Nārāyaṇa
                   no qualityless level above him · qualities REAL and ETERNAL
                   IMMANENT because world and souls are his BODY
                   TRANSCENDENT because he EXCEEDS them
  DVAITA ......... Viṣṇu absolutely supreme, ALONE independent and self-sufficient
                   all selves and the world depend on him ontologically, morally,
                   soteriologically · difference NEVER erased by liberation

  RĀMĀNUJA'S CRITIQUE OF ADVAITA'S LORD — the second half of 2023 Q6(a)
    if the Lord belongs ONLY to the empirical level => devotion becomes MERELY
    PREPARATORY · scriptural descriptions of God become LOWER TRUTH · religious life
    is STRUCTURALLY SUBORDINATED to non-personal absolutism
    => this hollows out devotion and makes scriptural theism PROVISIONAL

  THE 2022 HIERARCHY — Q8(a), 20 marks
    witness (sākṣī) ..... pure witnessing consciousness · unaffected · immediate ·
                          non-objectifiable — CLOSEST to the ultimate standpoint
    individual (jīva) ... consciousness reflected/limited by ignorance — empirical
                          BONDAGE
    the Lord (īśvara) ... consciousness conditioned by illusion — empirical COSMIC
                          ORDER
    => NOT three substances but ONE consciousness under THREE DESCRIPTIONS
    => the printed verse "Brahma satyaṃ jagan-mithyā, jīvo brahmaiva nāparaḥ" is a
      TRADITIONAL ADVAITA SUMMARY, not one of the four Upaniṣadic great sentences

  ! TRAP: "Advaita denies God" ignores that the Lord is FULLY REAL at the empirical
    level; only ULTIMACY is denied.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/22: Original-reflection, and the three models of individuation

  ORIGINAL-REFLECTION MODEL (bimba-pratibimba-vāda) — asked 2025 Q8(a), 20 marks
       ORIGINAL (bimba) = pure consciousness / ultimate reality
                   |
       reflected in v a conditioning medium (internal organ under ignorance)
       REFLECTION (pratibimba) = the empirical self
       * the original is UNAFFECTED by defect, motion or multiplicity in the media,
         as ONE FACE is unchanged though many mirrors — cracked, tilted, moving —
         yield differently conditioned images
       * SOME later formulations: reflection in TOTAL COSMIC appearance = the Lord;
         reflections in INDIVIDUAL ignorance / internal organs = the selves
       ? OTHER formulations make the Lord the ORIGINAL relative to the self, or
         prefer LIMITATION or SEMBLANCE models instead
       ! do NOT write that the Lord is simultaneously "the original" and
         "consciousness reflected in illusion" WITHOUT NAMING the relative or later
         model being used; these are LATER explanatory alternatives, not one uniform
         proposition attributable without qualification to Śaṅkara

  SOTERIOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE — the half of the 2025 question most answers miss
    the reflected image has only DERIVATIVE reality
    => when ignorance is removed the finite self is known NEVER to have possessed
      independent reality apart from original consciousness
    => liberation = RECOGNITION of non-difference, NOT movement, merging or the
      destruction of one substance by another
    => this is why liberation can be ATTAINMENT OF WHAT IS ALREADY ATTAINED
      (prāptasya prāptiḥ) and why LIBERATION WHILE LIVING (jīvanmukti) is possible
    ! LIMIT: a formless original, a formless medium and a non-dual reality make
      LITERAL reflection impossible; reflection also normally needs a spatial gap.
      Use the model for SUGGESTIVE value, not optical physics.
    ! Madhva uses the SAME metaphor for the OPPOSITE conclusion — that the dependent
      image is REALLY DIFFERENT from its original — which shows how far the metaphor
      under-determines the metaphysics.

  THREE MODELS OF ONE PROBLEM
    limitation (avaccheda-vāda) ...... one space appearing as pot-space (ghaṭākāśa);
                                       emphasis on DELIMITATION
                                       associated with VĀCASPATI MIŚRA
    reflection (pratibimba-vāda) ..... consciousness reflected in ignorance;
                                       emphasis on DERIVATIVE APPEARANCE and
                                       phenomenological vividness
                                       associated with PADMAPĀDA and the VIVARAṆA line
    semblance (ābhāsa-vāda) .......... the self is a mere SEMBLANCE (ābhāsa);
                                       emphasis on ONTOLOGICAL THINNESS
    COMMON CONCLUSION: individuality LACKS ULTIMATE REALITY

  ! TRAP: swapping the names — limitation is Vācaspati Miśra with pot-space,
    reflection is Padmapāda with the mirrored face.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/22: The true self, the witness, and the three states

  THE STATE-ANALYSIS ARGUMENT (Advaita)
    waking (jāgrat) ......... an EXTERNAL object-world is presented
    dream (svapna) .......... INTERNALLY generated objects are presented
    deep sleep (suṣupti) .... NO manifest subject-object cognition
                                   |
    report on waking: "I slept peacefully; I knew nothing"
    => presupposes CONTINUITY through deep sleep
    => supports a WITNESS not identical with any particular mental mode
                                   |
    the FOURTH standpoint (turīya) is NOT a fourth episode after the three;
    it is the non-dual consciousness that UNDERLIES and ILLUMINES all three
                                   |
    ! HONEST QUALIFICATION: this is an argument WITHIN Advaita, not a neutral proof.
      Rāmānuja and Nyāya retain an ENDURING SELF while REJECTING its identity with
      the non-dual absolute. The argument establishes an enduring witness more
      securely than it establishes identity.

  WITNESS-CONSCIOUSNESS (sākṣī) — WHAT IT IS AND IS NOT
    + illumines all mental states, never altered by them
    + pleasure, pain, doubt, memory, desire are WITNESSED, not essential to it
    + not a passive blank but the CONDITION for all experience
    + self-luminous (svayaṃ-prakāśa): manifestation belongs to consciousness as its
      very nature
    - NOT one more knower sitting inside the person
    => Advaita's elegant split: CHANGING PSYCHOLOGY vs CHANGELESS CONSCIOUSNESS
    => episodic cognitions, objects and the empirical knower remain INSIDE the
      subject-object structure

  THREE SETTLEMENTS OF ONE ENDURING SELF
  +----------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+
  | Advaita        | pure consciousness        | IDENTICAL in essence          |
  | Viśiṣṭādvaita  | real eternal conscious    | INSEPARABLE MODE, not         |
  |                | self, ATOMIC (aṇu)        | identical                     |
  | Dvaita         | real eternal dependent    | ETERNALLY DIFFERENT           |
  |                | self                      |                               |
  +----------------+---------------------------+-------------------------------+

  RĀMĀNUJA'S MECHANISM — how an ATOMIC self knows a WIDE world
    substantive knower = ATOMIC (aṇu), self-luminous
    ATTRIBUTIVE CONSCIOUSNESS (dharma-bhūta-jñāna) = its quality, which REVEALS
    objects · CONTRACTS under karmic obstruction · EXPANDS without obstruction in
    liberation
    => atomic size does NOT confine cognitive range
    in deep sleep the self PERSISTS, but object-manifesting cognition is UNEXPRESSED
    because no object is presented

  WHY THIS DECIDES EVERYTHING DOWNSTREAM
    self IS the absolute ............ liberation = RECOGNITION
    self is a MODE of it ............ liberation = COMMUNION
    self is permanently DIFFERENT ... liberation = ETERNAL SERVICE

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/22: The individual self — three non-dual models, two realist rivals

  MARK THIS FIRST
    true self (ātman) ..... the self in its ESSENTIAL metaphysical nature
    individual (jīva) ..... the EMBODIED empirical individual in bondage, agency and
                            transmigration
    => many answers improve immediately just by stating this distinction

  ADVAITA — three models, one conclusion (see Panel 8 for the images)
    limitation ....... the absolute appears as individual when DELIMITED by ignorance
                       or internal adjuncts; NOT a real fragment — apparent limitation
                       alone produces plurality
    reflection ....... the individual is the REFLECTION of consciousness in ignorance;
                       explains dependence AND phenomenological individuality
    semblance ........ the individual is a MERE SEMBLANCE; pushes derivative status
                       further than reflection
    => naming all three shows INTERNAL PLURALITY without losing the shared conclusion

  VIŚIṢṬĀDVAITA — real conscious mode
    a REAL conscious MODE (prakāra) of ultimate reality
    NEITHER an illusion NOR a detached independent substance
    body-soul analogy (śarīra-śarīrī): depends entirely on God for EXISTENCE, SUPPORT
    and ACTIVITY
    ! "body" does NOT mean gross physical body; it means THAT WHICH IS CONTROLLED,
      SUSTAINED AND USED by an indwelling self

  DVAITA — real distinct dependent substance
    never reducible to God · never a mere mode · never illusory
    difference AMONG individuals is ALSO real
    taxonomy: eternally free (nitya) · liberated (mukta) · bound (baddha)
    bound souls further described as liberation-eligible (mukti-yogya) · eternal
    transmigrators (nitya-saṃsārin) · darkness-eligible (tamo-yogya)
    ! a REAL Madhva doctrine, but NOT needed in every fivefold-difference answer.
      Use it where hierarchy, grace or differentiated liberation is relevant, and
      EVALUATE its severe ethical implications rather than reporting predestination
      casually.

  COMPACT STATEMENT BANK — use verbatim
    Advaita ......... the individual is the absolute APPEARING FINITE through ignorance
    Viśiṣṭādvaita ... the individual is a REAL CONSCIOUS MODE of the absolute

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/22: The world — three levels of reality and three verdicts

  THE THREE LEVELS OF REALITY (Advaita)
    ULTIMATE (pāramārthika) ..... the absolute alone; NEVER SUBLATED (trikālābādhita)
    EMPIRICAL (vyāvahārika) ..... the world, the Lord and individual selves — VALID,
                                  coherent, PUBLIC, law-governed, INTERSUBJECTIVE;
                                  sublated only by knowledge of the absolute
    APPARENT (prātibhāsika) ..... dream, rope-snake, shell-silver — PRIVATE, cancelled
                                  within empirical experience itself
    => the world is denied ABSOLUTE reality, NOT empirical functioning
    => DEPENDENTLY REAL (mithyā) ≠ WHOLLY NON-EXISTENT (tuccha, e.g. a hare's horn)
    => sublation (bādha) is what GRADES the three levels

  THREE VERDICTS ON THE WORLD
    ADVAITA ......... APPARENT TRANSFORMATION (vivarta); the rope-snake substrate is
                      real, the appearance dependent and sublatable; mithyā not tuccha
    VIŚIṢṬĀDVAITA ... the world is REAL and forms the NON-CONSCIOUS (acit) dimension
                      of the absolute's BODY; creation is a real manifestation
                      involving the non-conscious mode (prakṛti); cause-state SUBTLE,
                      effect-state GROSS; because effects are real, the universe is a
                      MEANINGFUL FIELD of duty, devotion and divine manifestation
    DVAITA .......... the world is REAL; dependence does NOT cancel distinctness;
                      God is its EFFICIENT governor, dependent primordial matter its
                      MATERIAL cause; matter is NOT absorbed into identity by
                      knowledge => the STRONGEST realist posture among the three

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/22: Apparent transformation versus real transformation

  THE CAUSATION FORK — asked 2022 Q5(e), 10 marks:
  "Do you agree that Vivartavāda is the logical development of Pariṇāmavāda?"

  SĀṂKHYA .... REAL transformation (pariṇāma-vāda): the effect is a REAL MODIFICATION
               of the cause .................... MILK => CURD
  ADVAITA .... APPARENT transformation (vivarta): the substrate is UNCHANGED and the
               effect is a SUBLATABLE APPEARANCE .... ROPE => SNAKE

  ARGUMENT FOR "logical development"
    both refuse production FROM NOTHING and both make the effect CAUSE-DEPENDENT
    if the cause is the ABSOLUTE, and the absolute admits NO real modification,
    then the effect cannot be equally real AS TRANSFORMATION
    => transformation must be only APPARENT: not pariṇāma but vivarta
    => vivarta PRESERVES causal dependence while PREVENTING real change in the absolute

  ARGUMENT AGAINST
    real transformation and apparent transformation assign CONTRARY ONTOLOGICAL STATUS
    to the effect
    what real transformation MOST INSISTS ON — genuine change in the cause — is
    precisely what apparent transformation ABANDONS
    => a doctrine that denies the core commitment of another is a REJECTION, not a
      completion

  GRADED VERDICT — the mark-winning line
    apparent transformation DEVELOPS the search for an effect DEPENDENT on its cause,
    but ABANDONS real transformation's central commitment to REAL transformation.
    AGREEMENT IS STRUCTURAL; DISAGREEMENT IS ONTOLOGICAL.

  FIVE-POSITION TRANSFORMATION BANK (one line each, high comparative value)
    Advaita ......... APPARENT transformation — substrate unchanged, effect sublatable
    Sāṃkhya ......... REAL transformation of primordial matter
    Rāmānuja ........ REAL transformation OF MODES; essence uncorrupted
    Nimbārka ........ REAL transformation of the absolute's DEPENDENT POWERS
    Vallabha ........ UNCHANGED REAL MANIFESTATION (avikṛta-pariṇāma) — effect real,
                      cause unaltered; NEITHER vivarta NOR ordinary pariṇāma

  ! TRAP: answering "yes" or "no" flatly. The word in the stem is DEVELOPMENT, and it
    is the graded verdict that is being tested.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 14/22: The seven objections, part one — locus to indefinability

  RĀMĀNUJA'S SEVENFOLD CHARGE (sapta-anupapatti / sapta-vidhā-anupapatti)
  asked 2025 Q5(d), 10 marks · names and sequence VARY slightly across expositions

  1 LOCUS (āśraya-anupapatti) — untenable locus of ignorance
    OBJ ..... in the absolute => it ceases to be pure consciousness;
              in the individual => the individual is itself a PRODUCT of ignorance
              => MUTUAL DEPENDENCE (anyonyāśraya)
    REPLY ... VIVARAṆA: the absolute is the LOCUS (āśraya) WITHOUT being modified or
              tainted, since ignorance is not a real modification of it
    REPLY ... BHĀMATĪ: ignorance is located in the individual, and the
              individual-ignorance relation is BEGINNINGLESS (anāditva); a
              beginningless seed-sprout dependence is not automatically vicious
    RESIDUE . THE HARDEST OBJECTION: two Advaita sub-schools give TWO DIFFERENT
              answers, which is itself a dialectical opening

  2 CONCEALMENT (tirodhāna-anupapatti)
    OBJ ..... the absolute is SELF-LUMINOUS (svayaṃ-prakāśa); to conceal it is to
              destroy its very nature as light / consciousness
    REPLY ... ignorance does not conceal the absolute AS SUCH; it conceals it AS
              RECOGNISED BY the individual, and the concealment is itself only
              apparent from the standpoint of ignorance
    EXAMPLE . a CLOUD does not extinguish the SUN; it obstructs the VIEWER'S vision
    RESIDUE . the analogy helps against literal concealment, but one may still ask
              how ANY obstruction arises when the absolute alone is ultimately real

  3 NATURE (svarūpa-anupapatti)
    OBJ ..... the nature of ignorance is incoherent if it is NEITHER real NOR unreal
    REPLY ... the being / non-being / indescribable TRICHOTOMY: experienced => not
              non-being; sublated => not being; therefore INDESCRIBABLE
    RESIDUE . works ONLY if SUBLATABILITY is accepted as a criterion of degrees of
              reality — which realist schools DENY OUTRIGHT

  4 INDEFINABILITY (anirvacanīyatva-anupapatti)
    OBJ ..... whatever exists must be real or unreal; a THIRD CATEGORY is a refusal
              to answer, and seems to violate the excluded middle
    REPLY ... the contrast is NOT being versus non-being as bare contradictories but
              ONTOLOGICAL GRADES measured by sublatability; and the claim is not that
              illusion lacks a determinate nature altogether, only that it cannot be
              filed under EITHER ultimate head
    REPLY ... TU QUOQUE: Rāmānuja's NON-CONSCIOUS (acit), real-but-dependent BODY of
              the absolute, ALSO occupies a status that is neither independent
              absolutism nor non-being
    RESIDUE . Rāmānuja and Madhva call this VERBAL unless the third grade is
              INDEPENDENTLY GROUNDED by a means of valid knowledge

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/22: The seven objections, part two — and the realist alternative

  5 MEANS OF KNOWING (pramāṇa-anupapatti)
    OBJ ..... perception gives an OBJECT, not a positive ignorance; inference lacks a
              valid mark; scripture does not straightforwardly teach a POSITIVE
              INDEFINABLE ENTITY
    REPLY ... WITNESS-PERCEPTION (sākṣi-pratyakṣa): the immediate experience
              "I DO NOT KNOW" presents ignorance POSITIVELY, not as blank absence
    REPLY ... POSTULATION (arthāpatti): if knowledge REMOVES bondage, there must be
              something POSITIVE to remove
    REPLY ... scripture itself teaches illusion / ignorance as a DEPENDENT POWER
              responsible for appearance
    RESIDUE . Rāmānuja: "I do not know" reports an ABSENCE of knowledge, not a
              mysterious positive entity => with (1), THE MOST DANGEROUS OBJECTION

  6 REMOVER (nivartaka-anupapatti)
    OBJ ..... what KIND of knowledge removes ignorance? Qualityless knowledge seems
              CONTENTLESS; determinate knowledge is itself WITHIN ignorance
    REPLY ... UNDIVIDED-FORM MENTAL MODE (akhaṇḍākāra-vṛtti): the great-sentence-
              generated cognition that takes the absolute as content by SUBLATING all
              limiting adjuncts
    REPLY ... knowledge does NOT act on ignorance as one object acts on another; TRUE
              cognition SUBLATES the false, then the mental mode SUBSIDES like the
              stick consumed in the fire it stirs
    RESIDUE . strong inside Advaita's LADDER-MODEL; a critic still asks how a mental
              mode WITHIN the empirical order can TERMINATE that order
    ! ATTRIBUTION: the undivided-form mental mode is a LATER systematic explanation;
      do not present it as Śaṅkara's verbatim formula

  7 CESSATION (nivṛtti-anupapatti)
    OBJ ..... if ignorance is REAL it cannot be removed; if UNREAL there is nothing to
              remove; and if removed, the status of its CESSATION is obscure
    REPLY ... cessation is NOT a fifth entity produced after ignorance; it is simply
              the absolute REMAINING AS IT ALWAYS WAS once superimposition is sublated
    EXAMPLE . asking where ignorance WENT is like asking where the SNAKE went when the
              ROPE was recognised
    RESIDUE . elegant IF superimposition is granted; QUESTION-BEGGING if the opponent
              rejects the rope-snake model for world-experience

  FORCE OF THE SEVEN — three levels of attack
    (a) the STATUS of ignorance
    (b) the POSSIBILITY of concealment and projection
    (c) the COHERENCE of liberation as removal of ignorance
    => Advaita answers MOST convincingly where SUBLATION-LOGIC applies — CESSATION and
      CONCEALMENT; it is MOST VULNERABLE on LOCUS and MEANS OF KNOWING, because the
      locus and proof of ignorance are not settled without granting Advaita's own
      standpoint
    => the seven are NOT a list; they are a systematic attack on the EXPLANATORY COST
      of making the world indescribable

  RĀMĀNUJA'S POSITIVE ALTERNATIVE — what replaces illusion-doctrine
    INSEPARABLE DEPENDENCE (apṛthaksiddhi) ... conscious (cit) and non-conscious
      (acit) are INSEPARABLE from the absolute yet REALLY DISTINCT as MODES
    BODY-SOUL RELATION (śarīra-śarīrī-bhāva) ... world and selves are the absolute's
      BODY; the absolute is their INDWELLING CONTROLLER
    ERROR-THEORY: APPREHENSION OF THE REAL (satkhyāti) ... cognition is ALWAYS of the
      real; error is mistaking ONE REAL FOR ANOTHER
    MECHANISM: ELEMENTAL INTERMIXTURE (pañcīkaraṇa / trivṛtkaraṇa) — every object
      contains a portion of every element, so a REAL silver-element in the shell can
      be misapprehended

  DVAITA'S POSITION
    does NOT accept illusion in the Advaita sense of ontological illusion; the world
    is REAL and ignorance is REAL ignorance
    error = APPREHENSION OTHERWISE, NEWLY ANALYSED (abhinava-anyathā-khyāti)
    Madhva's critique is SHARPER in one respect: it does not try to absorb plurality
    into qualified unity but makes REAL DIFFERENCE FUNDAMENTAL

  THE 2018 PRINTED ANOMALY — Q7(c), 15 marks
    the paper asks how Rāmānuja REFUTES illusion AND why illusion is "NEEDED BY BOTH".
    The second sentence is philosophically unusual and is RETAINED AS PRINTED.
    ROUTE: FLAG the anomaly · state that Rāmānuja REJECTS Advaitic illusion · read the
    demand as comparing HOW EACH SYSTEM EXPLAINS PLURALITY — Śaṅkara by a sublatable
    indescribable power, Rāmānuja by REAL INSEPARABLE MODES.
    ! do NOT rationalise it into a false doctrine; do NOT skip the printed demand.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/22: Superimposition, the four error-theories, the ledger

  SUPERIMPOSITION (adhyāsa) — Śaṅkara's opening move
    definition ... MUTUAL superimposition of subject and object
    shorthand .... ATASMIN TAD-BUDDHIḤ — cognition of one thing in what is NOT that
    fuller ....... attributes of the OBJECT put on the SUBJECT, and vice versa
    character .... NATURAL (naisargika) and BEGINNINGLESS (anādi)
                   => ordinary life is ALREADY structured by misidentification BEFORE
                     any philosophical reflection
    components ... CONCEALMENT (āvaraṇa) hides the true nature ·
                   PROJECTION (vikṣepa) throws up the false appearance
                   (mirrors the two powers of illusion — Panel 13)
    root of all empirical dealing (vyavahāra):
      "I am tall" · "I am happy" · "I act" · "I know"
      => bodily and mental predicates FALSELY ATTRIBUTED to the self, and consciousness
        falsely attributed to body and mind
      => worldly experience is not OCCASIONALLY mistaken; it is STRUCTURALLY pervaded
    examples ..... ROPE-SNAKE · SHELL-SILVER · DESERT-MIRAGE
    PROCESS vs STATUS: superimposition is the PROCESS; indescribable-error theory
      states the STATUS of the object thereby presented => CONNECT, never CONFUSE
    comparative force: for NYĀYA error is wrong cognition OF OBJECTS; for ADVAITA the
      EGOIC STANDPOINT ITSELF is already infected by primordial superimposition

  THE PROBLEM OF ERROR — asked 2021 Q7(a), 20 marks, "critically discuss"
  +------------------+------------------------------------------------------------+
  | NYĀYA            | APPREHENSION OTHERWISE (anyathā-khyāti): the silver is REAL |
  |                  | silver known ELSEWHERE, wrongly connected with this locus   |
  | ADVAITA REFUTES  | sublation takes the form "THIS IS A SHELL" and cancels the  |
  |                  | silver AT THAT VERY LOCUS; it does NOT relocate it. Had it  |
  |                  | been real silver merely misconnected, correction would      |
  |                  | dissolve a RELATION, not annihilate OBJECTHOOD so radically.|
  |                  | Also, the illusory silver is PRACTICALLY EFFECTIVE — one    |
  |                  | reaches for it — which a bare memory-image should not be.   |
  | VIŚIṢṬĀDVAITA    | APPREHENSION OF THE REAL (satkhyāti) + elemental            |
  |                  | intermixture supplies a REAL silver-element in the shell    |
  | BUDDHIST         | APPREHENSION OF THE NON-EXISTENT (asat-khyāti) — REJECTED:  |
  |                  | the illusory silver APPEARS, and sheer non-being cannot     |
  | ADVAITA          | INDESCRIBABLE-ERROR THEORY (anirvacanīya-khyāti):           |
  |                  | REAL? then it would survive sublation — it does not         |
  |                  | UNREAL? then it could never appear — but it did             |
  |                  | => dependent, provisional, INDESCRIBABLE status              |

  PRESUPPOSITION-LED DEFENCE OF INDESCRIBABILITY — the 20-mark spine
    P1 .. being (sat) / non-being (asat) / indescribable are EXHAUSTIVE and MUTUALLY
          EXCLUSIVE grades within Advaita's scheme
    D  .. sat = NEVER SUBLATED (trikālābādhita) · asat = NEVER PRESENTED at all
          (hare's horn, square circle) · anirvacanīya = PRESENTED, then SUBLATED
    A  .. illusion and world fall in the THIRD grade
    P2 .. SUBLATION (bādha) is the criterion of degrees of reality
    A  .. without bādha the three-tier scheme COLLAPSES into realism or nihilism
    P3 .. experience of an object gives it SOME positive status
    A  .. hence Advaita prefers indescribable-error theory over asat-khyāti
    P4 .. ignorance is POSITIVE IN FORM (bhāva-rūpa), not merely privative
    A  .. a bare absence could not perform BOTH concealment AND projection
    OBJ . "indescribable" seems to violate the excluded middle
    REP . the trichotomy is GRADES of sublatability, not bare contradictories
    RES . Rāmānuja and Madhva call it VERBAL unless the third grade is independently
          established
    NOTE  ŚRĪHARṢA's strategy in the Khaṇḍanakhaṇḍakhādya is DIALECTICAL, not
          constructive: he refuses a positive definition of the real and shows that
          the opponent's definitions FAIL

  CRITICAL LIMIT: the refutation establishes an ANOMALY for Nyāya rather than a PROOF
  of the third grade — the Naiyāyika can reply that cancellation removes only the
  WRONG CONNECTION.

    so'yaṃ devadattaḥ — "THIS is THAT Devadatta"

    => implication (lakṣaṇā) is a RECOGNISED semantic operation; Advaita claims only
      that THIS sentence needs the MIXED variety — neither total abandonment
      (jahal-lakṣaṇā) nor total retention (ajahal-lakṣaṇā)

    for Rāmānuja "thou" names a self whose very nature is to be an INSEPARABLE MODE of
    "that" => the sentence asserts INSEPARABILITY, not sameness

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 19/22: Inseparable dependence and the body-soul relation

  THE PROBLEM RĀMĀNUJA IS SOLVING
    ADVAITA .... unity real, plurality appearance => world and devotion downgraded
    DVAITA ..... plurality real, unity only dependence => non-dual sentences strained
    RĀMĀNUJA ... can plurality be REAL and yet INSIDE unity?

  INSEPARABLE DEPENDENCE (apṛthaksiddhi)
    conscious selves (cit) + non-conscious matter (acit)
      ► exist in an INSEPARABLE relation with ultimate reality
      ► CANNOT exist or be conceived INDEPENDENTLY of it
      ► are NOT IDENTICAL with it
    => the central conceptual instrument by which qualified non-dualism combines UNITY
      and PLURALITY

  INSEPARABLE QUALIFIER (apṛthaksiddha-viśeṣaṇa)
    a qualifier that CANNOT STAND APART from the substance it qualifies
    the attribute is REAL and DISTINCT — but INSEPARABLE
    STRONGER than a mere conceptual relation · WEAKER than identity
    ! a MODE is not a PART: parts can be separated, modes cannot

  BODY-SOUL RELATION (śarīra-śarīrī-bhāva) — asked 2024 Q5(e), 10 marks
    selves and world = the absolute's BODY (śarīra)
    the absolute = their indwelling SOUL (śarīrin / antaryāmin)
    THREE CONDITIONS OF BEING A BODY — state ALL THREE; this is the answer's spine
      1 entirely SUPPORTED by it
      2 entirely CONTROLLED by it
      3 exists FOR ITS PURPOSES
    ! "body" is FUNCTIONAL, not physical: whatever is SUPPORTED, CONTROLLED and USED
      by an indwelling self — which is why CONSCIOUS SELVES can also be called God's
      body
    ! THE ANALOGY IS ASYMMETRIC: an ordinary self is AFFECTED, limited and pained by
      its body's defects; God's essential auspicious nature is UNTOUCHED. The parallel
      holds for SUPPORT-CONTROL-PURPOSE and FAILS for VULNERABILITY.
      => this asymmetry is how "critically discuss" is answered in 2024

  WHAT THE DOCTRINE BUYS
    preserves NON-DUALITY ...... there is only ONE ultimate substantive whole
    preserves REAL DIFFERENCE .. cit and acit are GENUINE MODES, not illusory
    preserves DEVOTION ......... the God-soul relation remains MEANINGFUL
    => in one stroke it answers Advaita's MONISM and Dvaita's PLURALISM

  MADHVA'S REPLY
    calling souls God's BODY already concedes too much: a body is an INSTRUMENT of its
    soul, and a REAL self cannot be another self's instrument without loss of its own
    substantiality => Madhva keeps DEPENDENCE and DROPS the body-relation

  FORMULA FOR QUICK RECALL
    Advaita ......... IDENTITY without real plurality
    Viśiṣṭādvaita ... INSEPARABLE PLURALITY within unity
    Dvaita .......... ETERNAL PLURALITY with dependence

  ! MAJOR TRAP: apṛthaksiddhi is NOT identity. Do not paraphrase it as "a kind of
    oneness"; that silently converts an inseparable qualifier into an identity.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 20/22: Fivefold difference and Dvaita's realism

  FIVEFOLD DIFFERENCE (pañcavidhabheda) — asked 2020 Q5(d), 10 marks
  NOT practical distinctions generated by ignorance — METAPHYSICALLY ULTIMATE

    1  jīva-īśvara-bheda ........ soul   vs  God
    2  jīva-jīva-bheda .......... soul   vs  soul
    3  jīva-jaḍa-bheda .......... soul   vs  matter
    4  īśvara-jaḍa-bheda ........ God    vs  matter
    5  jaḍa-jaḍa-bheda .......... matter vs  matter

  WHAT THE DOCTRINE DOES
    + directly REJECTS Advaita's claim of IDENTITY
    + ALSO rejects Rāmānuja's attempt to retain unity through QUALIFIED INSEPARABILITY
    + difference is NEITHER epistemic NOR merely empirical: it is REAL and ETERNAL

  CONSEQUENCES — where "significance" answers separate from list answers
    from (1) ... the soul remains ETERNALLY DIFFERENT from God => DEVOTION IS NEVER
                 TRANSCENDED; it is the PERMANENT FORM of the relation
    from (2) ... soul-soul difference is real => HIERARCHY AMONG SOULS IS POSSIBLE
                 => gradation (tāratamya) in liberation, and the severe classifications
                   of bound souls
    from (5) ... matter-matter difference is real => the PLURAL WORLD IS ONTOLOGICALLY
                 ROBUST; no reduction to one substance
    overall .... God alone INDEPENDENT (svatantra); everything else DEPENDENT
                 (paratantra) — difference does NOT threaten supremacy, because
                 supremacy is defined by INDEPENDENCE, not by ABSORPTION

  SUPPORTING APPARATUS
    ? UNIQUE DIFFERENTIATOR (viśeṣa) — invoked so that real distinctions, e.g. between
      a substance and its attribute, can be secured WITHOUT requiring a further
      difference AD INFINITUM. Commonly presented this way; EXPOSITIONS VARY. Without
      some such device Dvaita faces a REGRESS problem.
    WITNESS (sākṣin) — the ultimate internal validator of cognition, which for Madhva
      CONFIRMS real difference rather than dissolving it
      ! same word as Advaita's witness, OPPOSED metaphysics

  CRITICISM AND REPLY
    OBJ ..... eternal difference risks ULTIMATE PLURALISM and a hierarchy that offends
              MORAL UNIVERSALITY; and non-dual Upaniṣadic sentences become harder to
              read
    REPLY ... plurality is NOT a defect; it is reality AS CREATED AND SUSTAINED BY GOD,
              and devotion is STRONGEST when ontological distinction is preserved
    RESIDUE . the GRADED DESTINY of souls remains the sharpest normative difficulty and
              should be EVALUATED, not reported casually

  ! TRAP: reducing the doctrine to "difference between God and world" — that much is
    compatible with qualified non-dualism and proves nothing distinctive.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 21/22: The wider Vedānta, and Śaṃkara against Sāṃkhya

  NIMBĀRKA — DUALISTIC NON-DUALISM (dvaitādvaita) · matter asked 2020 Q5(e), 10 marks
    God, conscious selves (cit) and non-conscious reality (acit) are ALL REAL
    selves and matter DIFFER from the absolute because they are LIMITED
    selves and matter are NON-DIFFERENT because they have NO INDEPENDENT EXISTENCE
    => difference and non-difference are EQUALLY REAL ASPECTS OF ONE RELATION
    NON-CONSCIOUS REALITY (acit) — the three types demanded in 2020:
      1 ordinary primordial matter (prākṛta) ..... material nature and its products
      2 non-prakritic divine substance (aprākṛta)  substance associated with the
                                                   divine realm / body
      3 time (kāla)
      ? terminology and the PLACEMENT OF TIME vary across expositions => STATE the
        classification adopted; do NOT invent further subtypes
    matter is NEITHER illusion NOR an independent second absolute: REAL ·
    TRANSFORMABLE · DEPENDENT — related to the absolute as POWER/ATTRIBUTE to POSSESSOR
    creation is a REAL TRANSFORMATION of the absolute's DEPENDENT POWERS
    liberation: knowledge enabled by devotion and grace, WITHOUT destroying
    individuality

  VALLABHA — PURE NON-DUALISM (śuddhādvaita)
    the supreme is KṚṢṆA; souls and world are REAL MANIFESTATIONS, not products of an
    indefinable illusion
    UNCHANGED REAL MANIFESTATION (avikṛta-pariṇāma): NEITHER merely apparent
    transformation NOR an ordinary transformation that ALTERS the absolute — the
    EFFECT IS REAL while the absolute remains UNCHANGED
    the world remains a DIVINE MANIFESTATION; the cycle of possessive bondage
    (saṃsāra) arises from ignorance and EGOIC IDENTIFICATION
    PATH OF GRACE (puṣṭi-mārga): grace, loving devotion and service are CENTRAL, not
    provisional aids to identity-knowledge
    ! liberation = PARTICIPATION in Kṛṣṇa's bliss and loving service. Do NOT describe
      it as the disappearance of the real world or as loss of individuality.
    ! COST: "real effect from an unaltered cause" needs its own account of how
      MANIFESTATION differs from MODIFICATION — the position is named clearly and the
      mechanism is more asserted than argued

  CAITANYA TRADITION — INCONCEIVABLE DIFFERENCE-NON-DIFFERENCE (acintyabhedābheda)
    Kṛṣṇa, souls and world are REALLY NON-DIFFERENT as dependent divine powers and
    REALLY DIFFERENT as distinct manifestations
    "INCONCEIVABLE" (acintya) marks the inability of FINITE REASONING to reduce the
    relation to identity alone or difference alone
    ! it is NOT permission for contradiction without doctrine
    souls remain ATOMIC, REAL and DEPENDENT; the world is a real manifestation of
    divine EXTERNAL POWER, not an illusion
    LOVING DEVOTION (bhakti / prema) is the SOLE DIRECT PATH

  ŚAṂKARA AGAINST SĀṂKHYA — THE CHIEF-WRESTLER (pradhāna-malla) DEBATE
    WHY THE NEAREST RIVAL IS THE MOST DANGEROUS
      + accepts EFFECT-PRE-EXISTENT-IN-CAUSE (satkāryavāda), as Advaita broadly does
        in transformed fashion
      + posits a SINGLE material principle (prakṛti / pradhāna)
      + makes liberation turn on DISCRIMINATIVE KNOWLEDGE, not ritual
      - BUT makes that principle UNCONSCIOUS
    FOUR ARGUMENTS
      1 THE CAUSE MUST BE CONSCIOUS — the Upaniṣads describe the world-cause as being
        (sat), KNOWING, WILLING and PURPOSIVE; an unconscious principle cannot satisfy
        those descriptions
      2 TELEOLOGY REQUIRES INTELLIGENCE — mere equilibrium and disturbance of the
        constituents (guṇa) do not explain MEANINGFUL ORDERED creation
      3 THE WITNESS-MATTER RELATION IS UNINTELLIGIBLE — how does the mere PROXIMITY of
        an inactive conscious subject (puruṣa) set unconscious matter into purposive
        motion?
      4 THE UPANIṢADIC CAUSE IS ONE, NOT DUAL — Sāṃkhya divides the ultimate, so it
        cannot claim full scriptural support
    SĀṂKHYA'S REPLY (state it in a 15/20-marker)
      scriptural appeal is INTERNAL to Vedānta · purposiveness is the CONSTITUTIONAL
      TENDENCY of matter towards the subject's experience and release · proximity-
      causation is no more obscure than an UNCHANGING absolute grounding a world it
      does not enter
    ! OWNERSHIP: the 2018 Q7(b) question on the Advaita reaction to real transformation
      is PRIMARY-OWNED BY SĀṂKHYA; Vedānta supplies only the Advaita side
