THE TWO ILLICIT SLIDES (the whole clause exists to block them):
  SLIDE A (4 -> 1): "we coexist peacefully" ==> "all religions are equally true"      WRONG
  SLIDE B (1 -> 1): "there is one absolute Truth" ==> "therefore I am an exclusivist" WRONG
  Each needs a suppressed premise. NAMING THE PREMISE IS THE ANSWER.
FIREWALL: social tolerance, epistemic humility, alethic pluralism and soteriological

CONVERSELY: tolerance is a civic ethic and is fully available to an EXCLUSIVIST.
THE ONE RULE: name the axis in sentence one. A win on one axis is not a win on another.

    BEFORE (Ptolemaic)                         AFTER (Copernican)

THE ARGUMENT - WRITE IT NUMBERED
 (1) DIVERSITY IS PERSISTENT and strongly correlated with BIRTH CONTEXT   [descriptive]
 (2) MULTIPLE TRADITIONS demonstrably produce moral and spiritual transformation [evaluative]
 (3) EQUAL DIVINE CONCERN resists an arbitrary exclusivism under which salvation
     would track birthplace                                              [theological]
 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 (4) THEREFORE one Real may be AUTHENTICALLY EXPERIENCED through different
     conceptual schemes                                                  [the hypothesis]
NOTE THE SHAPE: three premises from three DIFFERENT domains. That is the argument's breadth
  and its breaking point - premise (3) is unavailable against a NON-THEISTIC exclusivist,
  and a naturalist reads premise (2) differently.
STATUS OF THE CONCLUSION: a POSSIBILITY CLAIM supported by inference to the best explanation.
  Hick does not present it as a demonstration, and overstating it invites easy refutation.
THE REAL IS TRANSCATEGORIAL: personal and non-personal predicates apply to its EXPERIENCED
  MANIFESTATIONS, not literally to the Real as it is independently of human schemes.
  ==> "God is personal" and "the ultimate is beyond personality" are not contradictories
      about the same object under the same description.
WHAT THE HYPOTHESIS IS NOT: a rival first-order creed. It is SECOND-ORDER philosophy - and
  that status is exactly what Hick's reply to the arrogance charge depends on (Panel 10).
SOURCES: An Interpretation of Religion; God and the Universe of Faiths. "Transcategorial"
  is Hick's own later term.
TRAP: describing the position without RECONSTRUCTING the argument caps the script at average.

THE DEPARTURE: Hick treats the noumenal Real as CAUSALLY related to religious experience.
  That is stronger than the analogy licenses, and it is DELIBERATE - without it the Real
  would explain nothing.                                     <== NAME THE DEPARTURE
PRESUPPOSITIONS: (i) the Kantian appearance/reality distinction can be extended religiously;
  (ii) soteriological transformation is COMPARABLE across traditions.
THE NOUMENAL OBJECTION, STATED PRECISELY
  If the Real is beyond ALL categories, then:
      "the Real EXISTS"            <- existence is a category
      "the Real CAUSES experience" <- causality is a category
      "the Real is GOOD"           <- evaluative predication
  ...are all ILLICIT. The hypothesis needs the Real to do work its own transcategoriality
  forbids it from doing.
HICK'S LINE OF REPLY: the predicates apply FORMALLY, not substantively - the Real is that to
  which the traditions respond. Consistency preserved; INFORMATIVENESS reduced.
HONEST VERDICT: the objection THINS the hypothesis rather than refuting it. Say exactly that.
ADVANCED-DOSSIER FORM: "the unknowable Real is too thin to ground determinate religions" ->
  reply: a second-order hypothesis about transformation, not a new creed -> note:
  REVISIONISM REMAINS A LIVE CHARGE.
INDIAN COMPARATOR (structural, not decorative): nirguna Brahman (without qualities) is
  likewise beyond exhaustive predication - but Advaita has a BUILT-IN two-level semantics
  (nirguna / saguna) that Hick must CONSTRUCT.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/31: Hick III - the salvation-and-liberation criterion, and its price
WHY A PRACTICAL CRITERION AT ALL: doctrinal comparison across traditions is DEADLOCKED -
  "God is personal" vs "the ultimate is beyond personality"; "one life then judgement" vs
  "beginningless rebirth" - and there is no neutral adjudicating framework (Panel 20).
THE CRITERION
  +-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  |  SELF-CENTREDNESS  ------------------------->  REALITY-CENTREDNESS       |
  |  (grasping, ego, fear, acquisition)           (compassion, justice,      |
  |                                                detachment, love)         |
  |  measured by: saints, moral fruits, transformed lives across traditions  |
  +-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
WHAT IT SETTLES:      whether a TRADITION is an authentic response
WHAT IT DOES NOT:     which DOCTRINES are true; whether the ENDS are the same
  ==> "IT DOES NOT MAKE CONTRADICTORY DOCTRINES JOINTLY TRUE."  <== WRITE THIS SENTENCE
THREE OBJECTIONS THAT ARRIVE TOGETHER - one complaint at three levels
  (a) METAPHYSICAL   the Real is too thin to be the terminus of transformation (Panel 7)
  (b) EPISTEMIC      SELF-REFERENTIAL / PARITY - pluralism criticises first-order claims to
                     superiority while asserting second-order superiority. Exclusivists ask
                     why Hick's meta-framework escapes the contestability he attributes to all.
  (c) EXISTENTIAL    IDENTITY AND MISSION - traditions object that being redescribed as
                     culturally limited appearances ERASES their self-understanding and
                     weakens witness or mission.
                     ==> DIALOGUE REQUIRES HEARING THAT OBJECTION, NOT DECLARING IT SOLVED.
FURTHER OBJECTION: the criterion is COARSE-GRAINED - it excludes movements whose observable
  fruit is systematic self-assertion, but cannot discriminate between two traditions that
  both produce saints.
CONSEQUENCE OF CONCEDING THE COARSENESS: the criterion supports pluralism as an
  ETHIC OF ENGAGEMENT rather than as a DEMONSTRATED METAPHYSICS.
CONFLICTING ENDS: communion / non-dual knowledge / cessation are not obviously ONE end.
  Hick: family resemblance or transformation. Critics: FLATTENING. Heim builds on this (Panel 11).

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/31: Pluralism accused - D'Costa's covert-exclusivism charge
THE CHARGE, IN ONE MOVE
  PLURALISM -> makes its OWN second-order account UNIQUELY TRUE
            -> REINTERPRETS every tradition AGAINST that tradition's self-understanding
            -> therefore it IS an exclusivism, about a different object
STATUS: the OWNER RECORDS THIS AS THE STRONGEST SINGLE OBJECTION TO HICK.
HICK'S REPLY: "mine is a philosophical HYPOTHESIS, not a rival CREED."
OWNER'S VERDICT ON THE REPLY: it DOES NOT FULLY ESCAPE the charge.
WHY IT DOES NOT ESCAPE: a second-order theory that tells first-order traditions what they are
  REALLY doing exercises exactly the adjudicating authority it denies to each of them.
  The distinction between orders is real, but it RELOCATES the claim to privileged access
  rather than dissolving it.
CONVERGENCE: the charge converges with the IDENTITY AND MISSION concern (Panel 8c) that the
  traditions themselves raise - the same complaint, once from philosophy, once from the pews.
IF PLANTINGA AND D'COSTA ARE BOTH RIGHT, IS IT A STALEMATE?
  No - it is a CHANGE OF QUESTION. What survives is that NO position escapes holding a
  contested commitment. The live question becomes WHICH COMMITMENTS ARE HELD CORRIGIBLY,
  with the procedural virtues that deep disagreement makes obligatory (Panel 20).
D'COSTA'S OWN LIMIT: the charge is DIAGNOSTIC, not CONSTRUCTIVE - it shows Hick has not
  achieved neutrality; it does not show exclusivism is TRUE.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/31: The Vedic verse, and the four disciplines that make it usable
TEXT: "ekam sad vipra bahudha vadanti" - RGVEDA 1.164.46, asya vamasya riddle-hymn,
  ascribed to DIRGHATAMAS. "The one existent, the wise speak of in many ways."
 STEP 1 CITE PRECISELY        RIGHT: "Rgveda 1.164.46"      WRONG: "the Vedas say..."
 STEP 2 STATE THE NATIVE SUBJECT
        the verse names ONE EXISTENT under MANY DIVINE NAMES:
        Indra | Mitra | Varuna | Agni | the celestial Garutman | Yama | Matarisvan
        ==> its own topic is the UNITY OF THE VEDIC PANTHEON,
            NOT the unity of the world's religions
 STEP 3 SUPPLY THE BRIDGING ARGUMENT            <== THIS IS WHERE THE MARKS ARE
        the verse establishes a STRUCTURE: ONE REFERENT / MANY NAMINGS
        the pluralist claim needs that structure applied ACROSS traditions
        the extension is a 19th-20th century development (Vivekananda, Radhakrishnan, Gandhi)
        therefore: ARGUE that the structure generalises - do not assume the ancient text
        already made the modern claim
 STEP 4 KEEP TRANSLITERATION CONSISTENT
        "ekam sad vipra bahudha vadanti" or "ekam sat vipra bahudha vadanti" - both
        acceptable; pick ONE and hold it through the script
WHERE THE EXTENSION IS WEAKEST: inside the pantheon the REFERENT IS AGREED and the dispute is
  nominal. ACROSS traditions the referent itself is in dispute - a personal creator vs a
  non-personal absolute vs no ultimate substance at all. The analogy transfers the FORM of
  one-referent-many-names but NOT the agreement that made the form uncontroversial.
WHY THE DISCIPLINE PAYS: it is precisely BECAUSE the verse separates referent from formulation
  that it supports the absolute-truth answer of Panel 16 - an absolute REFERENT with plural,
  non-exhaustive FORMULATIONS.
DOUBLE FUNCTION: philosophically it supplies the referent/formulation distinction in its
  clearest ancient form; historically it shows the distinction is not a modern liberal import.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/31: Jain many-sidedness as a logic of standpoints
THE THREE-LEVEL APPARATUS
  ANEKANTAVADA   (metaphysical)   reality is MANY-SIDED
       v
  NAYAVADA       (methodological) every judgement is made FROM a standpoint; NAME it
       v
  SYADVADA       (logical)        prefix the assertion with "syat" = "in a certain respect"
       v
  SAPTABHANGI    (formal)         the SEVENFOLD scheme of predication
WHY THIS IS NOT "ANYTHING GOES"
  RELATIVISM: "P is true for you, not-P for me, and there is no fact of the matter."
  SYADVADA:   "P holds in respect R1; not-P holds in respect R2. Both are DETERMINATE claims
               about ONE reality; the respects are STATED and can be CHECKED."
  ==> predication is REGULATED, not abandoned.
PRESUPPOSITION: contradictory-seeming claims may concern different ASPECTS, LEVELS or
  PRACTICAL ORIENTATIONS.
CANONICAL EXAMPLE, CORRECTLY WEIGHTED: the blind persons and the elephant illustrate partial
  access - but THE DOCTRINE IS MORE RIGOROUS THAN THE PARABLE. The parable illustrates;
  the sevenfold scheme REGULATES.
TWO LIVE USES
  (a) ABSOLUTE TRUTH: reality is many-sided, every viewpoint is PARTIALLY true, and no partial
      viewpoint may claim exhaustive absoluteness ==> epistemic humility DISSOLVES dogmatic
      exclusivism while leaving an absolute REFERENT intact (Panel 16).
  (b) DEEP DISAGREEMENT: it makes the standpoint (naya) EXPLICIT and prefixes every assertion
      with syat, so framework-relativity is marked INSIDE the proposition rather than left
      implicit ==> A GENUINE INDIAN CONTRIBUTION TO A LIVE WESTERN PROBLEM, and the owner
      records that saying so is worth MORE than describing the elephant parable.
ADVANCED OBJECTION: if every claim is only conditional, no claim has full authority.
  REPLY: Jain logic REGULATES predication by standpoint and object-aspect; it disciplines
  absolutism rather than abolishing truth.
THE LIMIT: some claims contradict on the SAME question in the SAME respect. There, no index of
  standpoint helps - pluralism must distinguish LITERAL CONFLICT, SYMBOLIC LANGUAGE and
  DIFFERENT SOTERIOLOGICAL AIMS, and acknowledge whatever survives (Panel 17).

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 20/31: Deep disagreement - Fogelin, hinges, and four consequences
WHY THE MODULE IS NEEDED: the pluralism debate ASSUMES rival truth-claims could in principle be
  settled by argument. The epistemology of disagreement asks whether that assumption is true -
  and its answer RESHAPES THE WHOLE CLAUSE.
FOGELIN 1985, "The Logic of Deep Disagreement"
  some disagreements are NOT RESOLVABLE BY ARGUMENT IN PRINCIPLE, because the parties do not
  share the FRAMEWORK PROPOSITIONS - Wittgenstein's HINGE commitments from ON CERTAINTY -
  against which evidence would count.
  ==> when backgrounds diverge, THE SAME EVIDENCE IS NOT EVIDENCE FOR THE SAME THING,
      and argument has NO SHARED FULCRUM.
MICHAEL LYNCH extends this to disagreement about EPISTEMIC PRINCIPLES themselves - which
  sources are reliable - where any defence is CIRCULAR.
APPLICATION TO RELIGION - the parties differ over
  * SOURCES of evidence      which scripture, which experience, which inference
  * CRITERIA of adequacy     salvation? liberation? cessation?
  * the FRAMEWORK            within which any datum would count at all
FOUR CONSEQUENCES - WRITE THEM AS FOUR, AND NOTE THAT THEY CUT BOTH WAYS
  1 the failure of rational convergence is PREDICTED, not scandalous
    ==> it is WEAK EVIDENCE FOR RELATIVISM
  2 exclusivism CANNOT BE CONVICTED OF IRRATIONALITY merely for persisting
  3 pluralism CANNOT BE ESTABLISHED merely by pointing at persistence
  4 what CAN be rationally required are PROCEDURAL VIRTUES - fallibilism, intellectual
    humility, epistemic charity, and willingness to MAKE ONE'S FRAMEWORK EXPLICIT
THE INDIAN RESOURCE: anekantavada / nayavada / syadvada is in effect a FORMAL APPARATUS for
  exactly this - it makes the standpoint (naya) explicit and prefixes every assertion with
  syat, so framework-relativity is marked INSIDE the proposition. Saying it that way is worth
  more than describing the elephant parable.
OBJECTION: if deep disagreement is unresolvable, pluralism collapses into relativism.
REPLY: UNRESOLVABILITY IS EPISTEMIC, NOT ALETHIC - one party may still be RIGHT; what fails is
  our ability to DEMONSTRATE which.                          <== THE PAYOFF SENTENCE
SECOND OBJECTION: this licenses complacency.
REPLY: it licenses only the RETENTION of belief, not the refusal of dialogue - and it makes the
  procedural virtues OBLIGATORY rather than optional.
VERDICT FORMULA: "Religious disagreement is deep in Fogelin's technical sense: it lacks a
  shared framework against which evidence could adjudicate. That EXPLAINS the persistence of
  exclusivism WITHOUT VINDICATING it, and supports pluralism as an ETHIC OF ENGAGEMENT rather
  than as a demonstrated metaphysics."

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 21/31: Toleration - three components and one paradox
WHAT TOLERATION IS - three conditions, ALL required
  (i)   OBJECTION   I genuinely disapprove of, or believe false, what is tolerated
  (ii)  ACCEPTANCE  I have reasons that OUTWEIGH but do not CANCEL the objection
  (iii) POWER       I COULD interfere, and deliberately REFRAIN
  remove (i)   ==> INDIFFERENCE or APPROVAL, not toleration
  remove (iii) ==> MERE ENDURANCE, not toleration
  ==> TOLERATION IS NOT INDIFFERENCE.
THE PARADOX OF TOLERATION
  to tolerate is to ALLOW WHAT ONE HOLDS WRONG
     -> looks WEAK-WILLED  if the objection is serious
     -> looks INSINCERE    if it is not
RESOLUTION BY LEVELS
  FIRST-ORDER   : your doctrine is false
  SECOND-ORDER  : coercing belief is wrong, ineffective, or destructive of the very good
                  at stake
  ==> the reasons for ACCEPTANCE sit at a DIFFERENT LEVEL from the objection.
WHY THE DEFINITION DOES THE WORK: the 2020 stem asks for the IMPORTANCE of tolerance and says
  "justify". Define it by the three components first, and the importance FOLLOWS from the
  definition instead of having to be asserted.
CONSEQUENCE OF (i): the objection component is what makes toleration a MORAL ACHIEVEMENT
  rather than mere indifference - so a society of genuine disagreement cannot be built on
  esteem alone.
CONSEQUENCE OF (iii): toleration is always exercised by someone who COULD HAVE DONE OTHERWISE
  - which is why tolerance from the strong to the weak is PERMISSION, NOT EQUALITY (Panel 22).
TRAP: do not treat "tolerance" as one thing. Grade it (Panel 22) before praising any example.
