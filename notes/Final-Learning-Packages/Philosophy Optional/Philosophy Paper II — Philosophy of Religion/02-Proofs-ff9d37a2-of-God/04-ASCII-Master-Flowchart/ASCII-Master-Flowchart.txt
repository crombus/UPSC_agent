ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/22: THE A PRIORI / A POSTERIORI DIVISION  (2022 Q8(a), 20 marks)
                    A PRIORI              |            A POSTERIORI
 BASIS      concept of God alone          | facts of the world
 EXAMPLE    ontological only              | cosmological, teleological, moral, Nyaya
 STRENGTH   if valid, yields a NECESSARY  | begins from premises the critic concedes
            God                           |
 WEAKNESS   Kant: existence is not a real | reaches at best a probable, finite,
            predicate                     | possibly plural cause
                            |
 CLASSIFICATION RULE: classify by the SOURCE OF THE PREMISES, never by the
 modality of the conclusion.  Nyaya reaches an omniscient being and is still
 a posteriori; Aquinas' Fourth Way starts from OBSERVED degrees and is still
 a posteriori.
                            |
 2022 VERDICT FORMULA: preference depends on the criterion named —
   evidential contact -> a posteriori
   modal strength     -> a priori
   religious adequacy -> neither alone.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/22: ONTOLOGICAL ARGUMENT  (2018 Q6(c) · 2020 Q6(a) · 2024 Q7(c))
 ANSELM, Proslogion 2 (1077-78) — a REDUCTIO
   ASSUME: God exists in the understanding ALONE
   P1: God =def "that than which nothing greater can be conceived"
       (aliquid quo nihil maius cogitari possit); "greater" = MORE PERFECT
   P2: existing in reality is greater than existing in the mind alone
   => a greater than God could be conceived — contradicts P1
   .:. the assumption is false; God exists in reality
                            |
 ANSELM, Proslogion 3 — THE PHILOSOPHICALLY SERIOUS FORM
   a being that CANNOT be thought not to exist is greater
   .:. God exists NECESSARILY — core notion: self-existence (aseity)
                            |
 DESCARTES, Meditations V (1641): existence belongs to the essence of a
   supremely perfect being as three angles to a triangle, a valley to a mountain
   NOT to be fused with Meditations III's CAUSAL proof from the idea of the infinite
                            |

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/22: Ontological argument II: Gaunilo, Kant, the modal repair and the Indian absence
 OBJECTION 1 — GAUNILO, In Behalf of the Fool: "most perfect island" parody
   precision point: he wrote "most perfect OF islands", not "most perfect
   CONCEIVABLE island" — correcting this strengthens the parody
   ANSELM'S REPLY: an island is a contingent piece of the physical world and
   can be thought not to exist; only God is defined with necessary existence
   RESIDUE: protects form 2, exposes form 1, and shifts the burden to whether
   maximal greatness is COHERENT (islands have no intrinsic maximum)
                            |
 OBJECTION 2 — KANT: "existence is not a real predicate" (CPR A598/B626 region)
   100 real thalers contain not one coin more than 100 possible thalers
   Chatterjee-Datta's Indian idiom: 100 rupees imagined in the pocket
   .:. nothing can be defined into existence; only a CONDITIONAL follows
                            |
 OBJECTION 3 — post-Fregean logic: "exists" is a second-order quantificational
   notion; it cannot appear in a list of perfections at all
                            |
 REPLY — MODAL VERSIONS (Malcolm, Hartshorne, Plantinga)
   1 possibly a maximally great being exists
   2 maximal greatness = necessary existence + maximal excellence in every world
   3 if possible, it exists in some possible world
   4 what exists necessarily in some world exists in every world
   .:. it exists in the actual world
   BURDEN MIGRATES to premise 1 - the POSSIBILITY PREMISE; "possibly no such being"
   yields the contrary conclusion by the same modal logic
                            |
 INDIAN NOTE: no classical Indian analogue.  Reason is pramana-theoretic —
   Nyaya's means of knowledge are world-involving and inference needs vyapti
   (invariable concomitance) fixed by observation; Advaita reaches Brahman by
   scripture plus experience, not by concept-analysis.
                            |
 2018 VERDICT: the concept entails existence CONDITIONALLY (if God exists, God
 exists necessarily) — conceptual entailment, never instantiation.

 THE DISTINCTION THE WHOLE ARGUMENT TURNS ON
   ESSENTIALLY ORDERED (per se): hand -> stick -> stone.  Members act
     SIMULTANEOUSLY; intermediates have only DERIVATIVE power; remove the hand
     and the series stops NOW.  This is the series Aquinas terminates.
   ACCIDENTALLY ORDERED (per accidens): grandfather -> father -> son.  Each
     member has power in its own right and may perish.  Aquinas need NOT stop it.
   .:. "FIRST" = EXPLANATORY priority here and now, NOT the earliest event.
   Aquinas holds a temporal beginning is known by FAITH, not demonstration.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/22: Cosmological family II: the Five Ways mapped, and the critics
 THE FIVE WAYS, MAPPED
   Way 1 motion/change -> unmoved mover        (cosmological)
   Way 2 ordered efficient causes -> first cause (cosmological)
   Way 3 contingency -> necessary being         (cosmological)
   Way 4 observed degrees -> maximum/source     (henological)
   Way 5 regular directedness -> intelligence   (teleological)
   Each closes "and this everyone understands to be God."
   Way 3's weak steps: the quantifier shift at "at some time nothing existed",
   and necessary-through-another (ab alio) vs necessary-of-itself (a se).
                            |
 CRITICS
   HUME    : why not an infinite series? explaining each part may explain the
             whole; the cause need not be one, personal, infinite or good
   RUSSELL : the universe is "just there" — a brute fact; composition fallacy
   KANT    : the passage from "a necessary being" to "the most real being"
             is the ONTOLOGICAL argument in disguise — an ADEQUACY objection
   REPLY   : the argument concerns DEPENDENCE, not property-transfer; but
             allowing a brute totality REJECTS sufficient reason rather than
             refuting the argument.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/22: TELEOLOGICAL ARGUMENT  (2025 Q6(a), 20 marks — with Hume)
 THREE TELEOLOGIES THAT MUST NOT BE CONFUSED
   [1] AQUINAS Way 5 : regular directedness of NON-COGNITIVE nature.
       Archer and arrow.  A GOVERNANCE argument, not an artefact analogy.
   [2] PALEY (1802)  : ANALOGY from artefact to organism.  Watch on a heath —
       wheels, cogs, axles, springs, balances adjusted for regular timekeeping;
       a stone is not.  Three riders: the inference survives (i) never having
       seen a watch made, (ii) imperfect working, (iii) parts whose function
       we cannot discover.  Cumulative evidence: eye as self-adjusting optical
       instrument, bird's wing, fish's fin, planetary rotation, the seasons.
   [3] FINE-TUNING   : probabilistic/abductive, pre-biological, about constants.
                            |
 CHRONOLOGY TRAP: Hume's Dialogues (published 1779) PRECEDE Paley (1802).
 Philo — not Cleanthes — carries the objections.
                            |

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/22: Teleological argument II: Hume, Darwin and fine-tuning
 HUME'S FIVE FRONTS   (mnemonic A-A-P-P-E)
   A  ADAPTED     : any persisting universe must look adapted; there could not
                    be birds with wings unable to live in air.  EPICUREAN
                    HYPOTHESIS: finite particles in random motion pass through
                    every combination in unlimited time; one is a stable order.
   A  ANALOGY     : the universe resembles a vegetable or a crustacean at least
                    as much as a machine; only STRONG analogy licenses inference.
   P  PROPORTION  : proportion cause to effect — from a finite world one can
                    never infer an infinite creator (the scales illustration).
   P  PLURALITY   : many designers, an inferior deity, an infant or senile one,
                    or an abandoned first attempt fit the evidence equally.
   E  EVIL        : disorder and suffering count against a good, powerful maker.
   LIMIT: Hume does NOT disprove a designer.  He attacks inference-strength and
   the attributes of the conclusion.
                            |
 DARWIN (1859): random variation + differential survival explains adaptation.
   Ozone illustration: life fitted itself to the filter, not the filter to life.
   EXACT SCOPE: defeats Paley's BIOLOGICAL premise.  Does NOT touch cosmic
   law-governedness or the fine-tuning of constants.
                            |
 FINE-TUNING'S LIVE RIVALS
   +-- design
   +-- physical necessity
   +-- chance / multiverse + observation-selection effect
   +-- an unknown deeper law
   VERDICT: probabilistic inference to the best explanation, never demonstration.
                            |
 INDIAN ANALOGUE: ayojanat — the pot-and-potter inference.  Chatterjee-Datta
 note parallels in Janet, Lotze and Martineau, but stress the divergence:
 Western theists make God the source of EXISTENCE; Nyaya makes God the cause
 of ORDER only.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/22: MORAL ARGUMENT  (2021 Q7(b), 15 marks)
 THREE ROUTES — DO NOT MERGE THEM
   ROUTE 1  MORAL LAW / CONSCIENCE  (Newman, Grammar of Assent)
     binding duty and shame imply One before whom we are responsible
     OBJECTION: moral psychology and social life may explain conscience
     HICK: the route assumes values resist naturalistic explanation — it BEGS
     the question.  Force: an inference to agency, not a demonstration.
   ROUTE 2  OBJECTIVE VALUE AND DUTY
     stance-independent moral truths require a transcendent ground
     OBJECTION: moral realism need not be theistic; EUTHYPHRO presses the
     grounding — good because willed, or willed because good?
     Force: at most an abductive grounding argument.
   ROUTE 3  KANT'S HIGHEST GOOD  (Critique of Practical Reason, 1788)
     KANT'S TWO STOREYS: CPR (1781/87) DESTROYS the ontological, cosmological
     and physico-theological proofs; CPrR REINSTATES God as a POSTULATE.
     1 the moral law obligates pursuit of the highest good (summum bonum:
       virtue with proportionate happiness)
     2 finite agents cannot secure that proportion; nature does not supply it
     3 "ought implies can" licenses postulating its conditions
     4 THREE POSTULATES: FREEDOM, IMMORTALITY, GOD
     A postulate is practically necessary and theoretically undemonstrable —
     rational faith (Vernunftglaube): subjectively sufficient, objectively
     insufficient assent.
                            |
 EUTHYPHRO'S TARGET: divine-command and value-grounding routes — NOT Kant.
 For Kant the moral law is AUTONOMOUS; God guarantees PROPORTION, not obligation.
                            |
 OBJECTIONS: projection / wish-fulfilment; autonomy tension; adequacy.
 HICK'S VERDICT: moral seriousness is "at least a move in the direction of
 belief in God", but values cannot be said to point all the way to the infinite,
 omnipotent, self-existent personal creator.
                            |
 INDIAN COUNTERPART: Nyaya's fruit-giver (karmaphaladata).
 KEY CONTRAST: Kant POSTULATES a God who COULD proportion virtue and happiness;
 Nyaya INFERS a God who DOES — epistemically bolder, correspondingly more exposed.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/22: NYAYA-UDAYANA THEISM  (2018 Q8(a) · 2023 Q7(a) · 2025 Q7(b))
 NATURE OF ISVARA
   first EFFICIENT cause — a builder, NEVER the material cause
   also preserver (the world is kept in existence by God's will) and destroyer
   one, infinite, eternal, omniscient; possesses the six perfections (sad-aisvarya)
   the world relates to God as a body to the self residing in it
   omnipotent YET GUIDED by the merit and demerit of human actions
   eternal intelligence is an INSEPARABLE ATTRIBUTE of God, not His essence
     (the explicit contrast with Advaita)
   THEISM, NOT DEISM: the deist's God withdraws like a clockmaker; Nyaya's God
   maintains a continuous relation as creator, maintainer and destroyer
   God is prayojaka karta — efficient DIRECTIVE cause of human action; man is
   the efficient INSTRUMENTAL cause, relatively free, directed as a wise father
   directs a son
                            |

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/22: Nyaya-Udayana theism II: Udayana's eight collected grounds
 UDAYANA, NYAYAKUSUMANJALI, 5th stabaka (11th c.) — THE COLLECTED GROUNDS
 (a mnemonic verse conventionally unpacked as EIGHT hetus; glosses of the last
  four VARY — give the Sanskrit term plus one clause, and say that they vary)
   1 KARYAT          the composite world is an EFFECT; every effect presupposes
                     an intelligent maker who knows its material, means and end.
                     Composites are effects because they are made of parts
                     (savayava) and have intermediate magnitude (avantara-
                     mahattva); space, time, ether and self are excluded as
                     partless infinities, atoms and manas as simple infinitesimals.
                     The maker must be OMNISCIENT, since only an omniscient being
                     can directly know atoms.
   2 AYOJANAT        inert atoms cannot combine themselves; the initial
                     conjunction (samyoga) requires a conscious agent's volition
   3 DHRTYADEH       support, sustenance and periodic dissolution require a
                     governing will ("adi" carries the maintenance/destruction pair)
   4 PADAT           transmission of language, arts and crafts presupposes an
                     original competent teacher
   5 PRATYAYATAH     the VALIDITY (pramanya) of the Veda requires a trustworthy
                     (apta) author, as a science's authority derives from its
                     founder; the jiva cannot be that author
   6 SRUTEH          the Veda's own testimony to Isvara counts as evidence within
                     the system (Brhadaranyaka, Svetasvatara, Kausitaki, Gita)
   7 VAKYAT          the Veda consists of SENTENCES, and sentences are of the kind
                     produced by persons — hence a personal author
   8 SAMKHYAVISESAT  the "special number": the dyad (dvyanuka) that begins creation
                     requires the numerical cognition (apeksa-buddhi) of a conscious
                     being present at the start, when no finite self is embodied
                     [technically the hardest — state it only if stated correctly]
                            |

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/22: Nyaya-Udayana theism III: adrsta, internal answers and external pressure
 THE ARGUMENT FROM ADRSTA — the philosophical core, running beneath all eight
   differences of lot require a cause -> karma -> the time-gap requires a
   persisting stock of merit and demerit (punya/papa = ADRSTA) -> adrsta is
   UNCONSCIOUS and cannot allot the right kind and degree of joy and sorrow;
   individual selves neither know nor control it
   .:. an eternal, omniscient dispenser — karmadhyaksa / karmaphaladata
                            |
 TWO OBJECTIONS ANSWERED INSIDE THE SYSTEM
   CIRCULARITY : in the ORDER OF EXISTENCE God creates the Veda; in the ORDER OF
                 KNOWLEDGE the Veda leads to God.  Reciprocal dependence across
                 DIFFERENT orders is not a vicious circle.
   BODILESS AGENT : a dilemma — if God's existence is already established by
                 scripture the objection is idle; if not, it presupposes what it
                 disputes.
                            |
 EXTERNAL PRESSURE
   SANKARA'S ATOMISM DILEMMA: atoms active -> permanent creation; inactive -> no
     creation; both -> contradiction; neither -> an outside agency, itself
     problematic.  Advaita grants Isvara only empirical (vyavaharika) standing.
   SAMKHYA: unconscious nature (prakrti) evolves without a creator.
   REDUNDANCY CHARGE (C. D. Sharma): Nyaya's God is "reduced to the status of a
     supervisor" with "hands and feet chained by the shackles of the Law of Karma
     unfolding itself as the Adrsta"; the system "could have easily done away
     with a God".  This is Nyaya's hardest challenge.
   CHATTERJEE-DATTA'S OWN CONCLUSION: no proof can prove God, because God as
     ultimate reality is the highest of all premises and cannot be deduced from
     an anterior one; God must be known through DIRECT EXPERIENCE.
     Lotze: "all proofs that God exists are pleas put forward in justification
     of our faith."

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 13/22: Two senses of "god" in the indian debate
   SENSE A — WORLD-MAKER (jagat-karta / Isvara-karana-vada)
     the cosmological office: creates or arranges, sustains, dissolves, allots
     AFFIRMED by: Nyaya-Vaisesika, theistic Vedanta, Yoga (as a special self)
     DENIED  by: Jainism, Buddhism, Mimamsa, Carvaka, classical Samkhya
                            |
   SENSE B — PERFECTED / OMNISCIENT BEING (achieved divinity)
     the soteriological office: omniscient, passion-free, exemplary, non-creative
     AFFIRMED by: Jainism (arihanta, tirthankara, siddha), Buddhism (buddhas,
       celestial bodhisattvas), and by devotional traditions generally
                            |
   THE RULE THAT SAVES THE ANSWER
     "Denying a creator is not denying divinity."
     A school may deny Sense A completely and affirm Sense B completely.
     Nastika means denier of VEDIC AUTHORITY, not denier of spirit.
     Buddhism denies a CREATOR, not devas — who are long-lived, impermanent
     beings inside the round of rebirth.
                            |
   CONSEQUENCE FOR THE 2019 AND 2018 STEMS: a stem that asks for "the proofs
   propounded in Jainism" or for "arguments against God as creator" is asking
   about SENSE A.  Answer in Sense A, then supply Sense B as what the school
   affirms instead.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 14/22: JAINA CRITIQUE — GODHEAD WITHOUT GOD  (2019 Q5(e), 10 marks)
 READ THE STEM EXACTLY: Jainism propounds NO proof of a creator.  It propounds
 (a) a battery of arguments AGAINST one, and (b) divinity as achieved perfection.
 A script that invents Jain theistic proofs loses the paper; a script that says
 only "Jainism is atheistic" loses half the marks.
                            |
 THE WORLD: uncreated, beginningless, eternal; six substances (jiva, pudgala,
 dharma, adharma, akasa, kala); reality is origination, decay and permanence
 (utpada-vyaya-dhrauvya); change belongs to MODES, not to a divine act.
                            |
 SIX ARGUMENTS AGAINST A CREATOR
   1 MOTIVE (the strongest, and the one examiners want)
     a perfect being is complete — without unfulfilled desire, deficiency or need
     creation is an ACTION, and every action presupposes a want or purpose
     .:. either God is perfect and had no motive, or God had a motive and is not perfect
   2 REGRESS      if everything needs a maker, the maker needs a maker; if the
                  maker can be uncaused, so can the world — the premise self-defeats
   3 INSTRUMENT   before creation there was no matter, no space, no body, no
                  instrument; an immaterial God with nothing to work on cannot act
   4 MORAL        creation from compassion is refuted by suffering, inequality and
                  predation; creation without compassion is not good; creation as
                  sport is childish and cruel.  Karma explains the DISTRIBUTION of
                  fortune without impugning anyone's goodness
   5 REDUNDANCY   karmic matter, the six substances and causal law already explain
                  order, bondage and release — the parsimony (laghava) move
   6 SVABHAVA     things act by their own nature: fire burns, water flows
                  (assembled in Jinasena's Mahapurana and in Hemacandra;
                   also Haribhadra, Akalanka, Vidyanandi)
                            |

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/22: Jaina critique II: achieved divinity, objections and verdict
 WHAT JAINISM AFFIRMS INSTEAD — DO NOT BLUR THESE
   ARIHANTA / KEVALIN : destroyed the four obscuring (ghatiya) karmas and attained
                        omniscience (kevala-jnana) WHILE STILL EMBODIED
   TIRTHANKARA        : a special arihanta who RE-ESTABLISHES the fourfold order
                        (monks, nuns, laymen, laywomen) and preaches the path.
                        24 in each half-cycle; Mahavira is the 24th of the present.
                        Defined by a TEACHING-AND-FOUNDING function, not by rank.
   SIDDHA             : has additionally shed the four non-obscuring (aghatiya)
                        karmas at death; DISEMBODIED, at the siddhasila at the
                        summit of the cosmos, with the four infinitudes
                        (ananta-catustaya: infinite knowledge, perception, bliss, energy)
   .:. EVERY TIRTHANKARA BECOMES A SIDDHA; NOT EVERY SIDDHA WAS A TIRTHANKARA.
   Neither creates, sustains, judges nor answers prayer.  The tirthankara is
   passion-free (vitaraga), so worship is COMMEMORATIVE and ASPIRATIONAL — the
   object does not respond, and cannot, since responsiveness would be a passion.
   The five-fold salutation (arihanta, siddha, acarya, upadhyaya, sadhu) salutes
   QUALITIES AND STAGES, not a deity.
   Every jiva intrinsically possesses the four infinitudes, obscured not absent;
   "we are all potential Jinas" — a religion of self-help.
                            |
 OBJECTION -> REPLY
   NYAYA     : unconscious karmic matter cannot allot determinate fruits to
               determinate persons, nor can inert atoms self-organise
   JAIN REPLY: karmic influx and maturation are LAW-LIKE properties of the
               jiva-matter relation, exactly as poison acts without a poisoner
               supervising it
   COUNTER   : "God has no motive" assumes creation must be motivated as HUMAN
               action is; classical theism replies that creation is a free,
               non-needy diffusion of goodness (bonum diffusivum sui), not the
               satisfaction of a lack.  THIS EXCHANGE IS THE HIGHEST-YIELD
               PARAGRAPH IN THE ANSWER.
   INTERNAL  : Sharma — if ignorance and karma are inseparable from the soul,
               liberation is impossible; if external, bondage is impossible.
                            |
 VERDICT: Jainism supplies no proof OF God; it supplies a proof-structure
 AGAINST a creator and a redefinition of divinity as the soul's achieved
 perfection.  Its motive argument is powerful against an anthropomorphic creator
 and weaker against a classical theism that denies creation is need-driven.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/22: BUDDHIST CRITIQUE — THREE STRATA  (2018 Q8(b), 15 marks)
 STRATUM (a) — CANONICAL / GENETIC
   BRAHMAJALA SUTTA (DN 1): the being first reborn in an empty Brahma-world,
     seeing others appear after his wish, concludes "I am Brahma, the Maker,
     the Creator"; later arrivals remember him and transmit the error
   AGGANNA SUTTA (DN 27): a naturalistic-cyclic account of cosmic and social
     evolution replaces creation
   AVYAKATA (undeclared questions) + the poisoned-arrow parable: speculative
     cosmogony is soteriologically idle
   HONEST LIMIT: these DIAGNOSE the belief; they do not REFUTE the proposition.
                            |
 STRATUM (b) — CAUSAL-METAPHYSICAL  [Dharmakirti; Santaraksita's Tattvasamgraha
   with Kamalasila's Panjika]  — THIS IS WHERE THE EXAMINABLE ARGUMENT LIES
   1 CRITERION OF EXISTENCE: to be real is to be causally efficacious
     (arthakriya-karitva)
   2 THE SUCCESSIVE-OR-SIMULTANEOUS DILEMMA (krama-akrama-virodha):
       a PERMANENT, UNCHANGING cause must produce its effects either
       ALL AT ONCE -> the whole cosmos arises in one instant and nothing follows
       or SUCCESSIVELY -> God acquires at t2 an auxiliary he lacked at t1,
         and is therefore neither permanent nor self-sufficient
   3 AUXILIARY REGRESS: if God needs auxiliaries (material, karma, time) he is
     not the SOLE cause; if he does not, the world's temporal order is unexplained
   4 EVIDENTIAL: the inference "effect -> intelligent maker" rests on a vyapti
     established only for ARTEFACTS; generalising it is the very point at issue
   5 REDUNDANCY: conditioned co-arising (pratityasamutpada) already accounts for
     arising and cessation — a creator is an idle wheel
                            |

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 17/22: Buddhist critique II: moral stratum, distinction, replies and verdict
 STRATUM (c) — MORAL
   a being able to create who ordered THIS quantity of suffering is either not
   benevolent or not omnipotent; and if all is God's will, karma and moral effort
   are pointless.  DEVADAHA SUTTA (MN 101) makes the parallel point against
   determinism generally: a doctrine that makes present effort ineffective
   destroys the moral life.
   [Bhuridatta Jataka anti-creator verses: widely cited, wording
    translation-dependent — PARAPHRASE ONLY.]
                            |
 DISTINCTION: Buddhism is NON-THEISTIC, not merely atheistic.  It rejects a
 CREATOR (isvara-karana-vada), not every divine being; devas are impermanent
 beings within the round of rebirth, and later Buddha-fields and celestial
 bodhisattvas perform religious functions without an omnipotent creator.
                            |
 OBJECTION -> REPLY
   NYAYA     : God's WILLING is eternal while its EFFECTS are timed by the
               readiness of adrsta, so no change in God is required; and to
               demand PERCEPTION of God begs the question against inference
   BUDDHIST  : an eternal will whose efficacy is timed by something else is not
               the sole and sufficient cause the theist claimed
   SECOND    : if there is no self and no creator, what carries karma?
               -> routed to Topic 04, Soul, Immortality, Rebirth and Liberation
                            |
 VERDICT: strongest where METAPHYSICAL — the permanence/production dilemma bites
 on any UNCHANGING creator — and weakest where merely GENETIC, since explaining
 why people believe in Brahma does not show there is none.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 18/22: Mimamsa and carvaka — the remaining two denials
 MIMAMSA — displaces God twice over
   APAURUSEYA : the Veda is eternal, AUTHORLESS and self-valid; no human and NO
     DIVINE author is needed.  Authorlessness protects it from an author's deceit
     or ignorance.  Against Nyaya: proving God, then His omniscience, then His
     truthfulness, is a cumbersome three-stage dependence that authorlessness avoids.
     -> the exact contradictory of Udayana's padat / pratyayatah / sruteh / vakyat
   APURVA     : the unseen potency of a correctly performed ritual matures into
     future fruit WITHOUT a divine dispenser; Prabhakara's niyoga grounds
     obligation in the injunction itself
     -> the exact contradictory of the adrsta / karmadhyaksa argument
   Word-meaning relation is intrinsic (autpattika), not conventionally instituted
   by a person — removing the last need for a divine institutor of language
   Chatterjee-Datta record Max Muller's points that a creator God faces charges of
   cruelty and partiality, and that denying a CREATOR is not denying God
   THEIR CRITICISM: Mimamsa deities are not objects of worship and exist only in
   the mantras describing them — "eternal and self-manifesting concepts"; God is
   relegated to an ambiguous position

 CARVAKA — cuts the ground from every inferential proof
   only perception is a means of valid knowledge; inference cannot reach the
   supersensible; the world is made by the automatic combination of the material
   elements; Vedas and priests are rejected
   -> if this stands, EVERY anumana-based proof, Indian or Western, fails at once

 ADVAITA'S LEVELLING (not a denial, a demotion)
   Nyaya's God is a qualified Lord (saguna Isvara) at the empirical (vyavaharika)
   level, not the quality-transcending absolute (nirguna Brahman); the atomistic
   proof is judged logically weak

   demanding perception of God begs the question against inference

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 19/22: Cumulative-case logic and the comparative bench
   C-INDUCTIVE : premises RAISE the probability of the conclusion
                 P(theism | e) > P(theism), but not necessarily > 0.5
                       |  several INDEPENDENT strands
                       v
   P-INDUCTIVE : premises make the conclusion MORE PROBABLE THAN NOT  (> 0.5)
   [Swinburne, The Existence of God; Mitchell, The Justification of Religious Belief]
                            |
   IMAGES: CHAIN (wrong — the components are not deductions, so no link breaks)
           ROPE  (right — many strands, none end to end, jointly load-bearing)
           JURY  (Mitchell — converging circumstantial evidence, none sufficient alone)
   NOT THE SAME AS inference to the best explanation, which compares rival TOTAL
   hypotheses rather than accumulating confirmation.
   SWINBURNE'S PRIOR: SIMPLICITY — one agent with infinite (hence non-arbitrary)
   degrees of power, knowledge and freedom is simpler than any finite alternative.
                            |
   FLEW, God and Philosophy: TEN LEAKY BUCKETS — buckets that each leak hold no
   more water when stacked; zero plus zero is zero.
   REPLY: correct only if each argument had NO force; a C-inductive argument has
   some by definition.  Rope, not stacked buckets.
   RESIDUAL OBJECTIONS: (i) underdetermination — a finite designer, a committee
   of gods or an impersonal value-principle fit equally; simplicity is the
   proposed selector and is itself contested; (ii) the case must be run as a NET
   balance, with suffering counted on the other side.
   UDAYANA'S 8-FOLD COLLECTION IS STRUCTURALLY THE SAME MOVE, EIGHT CENTURIES
   EARLIER — and Flew's objection applies to both equally.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 21/22: Verdict ladder, traps and the answer spine
 FOUR DEFENSIBLE VERDICTS — CHOOSE ONE, NAME THE CRITERION, PAY ITS COST
   V1 MINIMAL      no proof is demonstrative; each fails on validity, soundness
                   or adequacy  (Chatterjee-Datta, following Kant and Lotze)
   V2 CUMULATIVE   individually weak, jointly they raise probability
                   (Udayana; Swinburne; Mitchell)
   V3 DIVERGENT    different proofs establish different things — necessity,
                   agency, intelligence, moral government — and no single one was
                   ever meant to deliver the whole God of religion
   V4 EXPERIENTIAL proofs are "pleas put forward in justification of our faith"
                   (Lotze); God is known through direct experience

 TWELVE MASTER TRAPS
   1  do not merge the Five Ways into one temporal "first cause" argument
   2  the watch is PALEY'S (1802), never Aquinas'
   3  Kant POSTULATES God for practical reason; he does not prove God by morality
   4  Nyaya's God is the EFFICIENT, never the MATERIAL, cause; atoms are eternal
   5  Hume attacks inference-strength and attributes; he does not disprove a designer
   6  distinguish a proof's VALIDITY from the ADEQUACY of the God it establishes
   7  never omit Anselm's second form; the reply to Gaunilo depends on it
   8  modal versions carry a POSSIBILITY-PREMISE burden; they do not defeat Kant cleanly
   9  Mimamsa's Veda is apauruseya and karma can be self-operating — the exact
      contradictory of Nyaya's scripture and adrsta grounds
  10  "Jainism is atheistic" misreads the 2019 stem: Jainism denies God, not godhead;
      and tirthankara is not siddha
  11  Buddhism denies a CREATOR, not all gods; and the case is not "the Buddha was
      silent" — it is the krama-akrama dilemma
  12  a cumulative case is a ROPE, not a chain; and Udayana's list is not a fixed
      canonical eight with agreed glosses

 QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
 "The proofs fail as demonstrations but succeed as diagnostics.  The ontological
  argument shows that God, if real, cannot be a contingent item in the world; the
  cosmological arguments show that contingent reality does not explain itself; the
  design argument shows that order invites, without compelling, an intentional
  explanation; the moral argument shows what a serious moral agent must hope; and
  Nyaya shows that the same inferential path was walked in India, and met the same
  objections from Mimamsa and Buddhism that Hume and Kant later pressed in Europe.
  What no proof delivers is the passage from the being it establishes to the God
  actually worshipped — which is why every tradition eventually supplements proof
  with experience, testimony or faith."
