ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/25: The clause, the one question, and the four layers
SYLLABUS CLAUSE (verbatim): "Religious Experience : Nature and Object (Indian and Western)."
CENTRAL QUESTION: what does an experience taken to be of the sacred establish, and for whom?
  +-------------+-------------+---------------+-------------+
  | 1 PHENOMEN- | 2 CLAIMED   | 3 INTERPRET-  | 4 EPISTEMIC |
  |   OLOGY     |   OBJECT    |   ATION       |   FORCE     |
  | what it is  | what seems  | how a         | what it     |
  | like        | given       | tradition     | justifies,  |
  |             |             | names it      | and for whom|
  +-------------+-------------+---------------+-------------+
THREE ILLEGITIMATE JUMPS
  A  1 -> 4  "it felt overwhelming, therefore it was veridical"   (intensity is not warrant)
  B  3 -> 2  "my tradition names it God, therefore God was met"   (naming is not meeting)
  C  4 <- x  "it has a neural correlate, therefore it is nothing" (mediation is not fabrication)
THREE CAUTIONS: subjective = FIRST-PERSONAL, not unreal | ineffable = resists ADEQUATE expression,
  not contentless | neural correlate = MEDIATION, not disproof.
THREE DISTINCT ACHIEVEMENTS: psychological genuineness | doctrinal authenticity | metaphysical
  veridicality. Winning one does not win the others.
THE ONE RULE: every stem here has a NATURE half and an OBJECT half. Answer both, and say whether
  the object is ENCOUNTERED (relation) or REALISED (identity).

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/25: Why the clause exists, and the only datable anchors
ORIGIN RAIL (conceptual, not calendar)
 1 a subject reports an experience that seems to present a sacred or ultimate reality
   -> 2 the report is FIRST-PERSONAL; its felt certainty is not transferable as it stands
     -> 3 the subject's own tradition supplies the concepts that NAME what was met
       -> 4 other traditions report INCOMPATIBLE objects with equal conviction
         -> 5 a test is demanded, and every candidate test assesses AUTHENTICITY, not existence
            ==> the clause exists because stages 3, 4 and 5 cannot be waved away.
DATED PRINT ANCHORS (assert these years; invent no others)
 1902 James, The Varieties of Religious Experience (Gifford Lectures 1901-02)
 1912 Durkheim, The Elementary Forms of Religious Life   1917 Otto, The Idea of the Holy
 1927 Radhakrishnan, The Hindu View of Life   <== the NAMED work in the 2025 stem
 1957 Zaehner, Mysticism Sacred and Profane   1957 Eliade, The Sacred and the Profane
 1960 Stace, Mysticism and Philosophy         1971 Rachels, "God and Human Autonomy"
 1978 Katz, "Language, Epistemology and Mysticism"   1979 Stump, "Petitionary Prayer"
 1991 Alston, Perceiving God
CITE BY WORK, NEVER BY YEAR: Schleiermacher (The Christian Faith); Swinburne (The Existence of
 God); Hick (Philosophy of Religion); Forman (The Problem of Pure Consciousness); Aquinas
 (Summa Theologiae); Teresa of Avila (her criteria of discernment); Habermas (translation proviso);

ANACHRONISM CONTROL: Otto (1917) answers James (1902); Stace (1960) answers Zaehner (1957);
 Katz (1978) answers the common core; Forman answers Katz. That order IS the argument.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/25: James I — the marks of mystical states, graded two-plus-two
 ============================ DEFINING — these two settle it ============================
 (1) INEFFABILITY   "defies expression"; must be DIRECTLY experienced; more like a feeling
                    than a proposition; James calls it the "handiest" mark
 (2) NOETIC QUALITY "states of insight into depths of truth unplumbed by the discursive
                    intellect"; carries a curious sense of AUTHORITY
                    = experienced insight, NOT independently demonstrated knowledge
 ---------------------------- USUAL — present in most cases ----------------------------
 (3) TRANSIENCY     cannot be sustained long; may RECUR and DEVELOP on recurrence
 (4) PASSIVITY      voluntary preliminaries may facilitate onset; once it sets in the mystic
                    feels GRASPED AND HELD by a superior power
 SCOPE: marks of MYSTICAL STATES, not of every religious experience.
 AUTHORITY CLAUSE: authoritative for the one who has them; NO authority over outsiders.
   ==> this is the hinge of the veridicality question; James is not a proof of God.
 WHY THE GRADING PAYS: transiency is only usual, so a STABLE, ESTABLISHED liberating knowledge
   (jnana) still counts as mystical. A flat four-mark list defines the Indian case out.
 TRAP: the flat list of four equals is the single commonest error in this clause.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/25: James II — method, medical materialism, fruits, the "MORE"
 METHOD: empirical (first-person documents) + pragmatic (consequences) + anti-reductive.
   Religion is PERSONAL BEFORE INSTITUTIONAL; theology and church are secondary growths.
 x REFUSED TEST — "MEDICAL MATERIALISM" (James's own term, Varieties, Lecture I)
     discrediting a state by its physiological or pathological origin
     REPLY: causal origin does not by itself settle truth or worth; the same argument would
     discredit every insight that arrived to a tired or unusual brain.
 v ACCEPTED TEST — FRUITS: "by their fruits ye shall know them"
     Matthew 7:16/20. James ADOPTS the maxim; he does NOT coin it.  <== attribution matters
     enduring moral integration, courage, charity, transformed conduct
 THE OBJECT JAMES ALLOWS HIMSELF: the "MORE" — a wider reality with which the self feels
   connected THROUGH THE SUBCONSCIOUS. Deliberately under-described, so the psychology does
   not smuggle in a theology.
 OBJECTION: a false belief that makes people kind would be vindicated by the fruits test.
 REPLY: fruits are a criterion of AUTHENTICITY and VALUE, not a deductive proof of the object
   — which is exactly why James cannot function as an argument for God's existence.
 VERDICT SHAPE: strongest as phenomenology and pragmatic assessment; silent on the object.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/25: Otto — the anatomy of the numinous
 THE HOLY (das Heilige) splits into
   [A] NON-RATIONAL CORE = THE NUMINOUS        [B] RATIONAL / MORAL SCHEMATISATION
       Otto's discovery                            goodness, justice, purpose, personality
       |                                           added LATER, not first
       +-- MYSTERIUM ... wholly other; not merely unknown but INCOMMENSURABLE with ordinary
       |                 categories
       +-- TREMENDUM ... awefulness | overpoweringness (majestas) | energy and urgency
       |                 ==> CREATURE-FEELING: one's own nothingness before the incommensurable
       +-- FASCINANS ... it attracts, entrances and beatifies EVEN WHILE it terrifies
                         dread and longing in ONE state, in ONE subject, at once
 LATIN, WRITTEN IN FULL ONCE: mysterium tremendum et fascinans (Otto's own Latin, 1917).
 IT IS NOT: fear of a danger (a danger is a comparable thing), nor an intensified moral awe.
 PRESUPPOSITION, STATED HONESTLY: that the phenomenological distinctiveness of awe supports a
   distinctive OBJECT rather than merely an unusual emotion. That is what the critic denies.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/25: Otto — non-rational, Schleiermacher, and the cross-cultural limit
 >>> NON-RATIONAL is NOT IRRATIONAL <<<
   non-rational = not reducible to conceptual and moral judgement   (Otto CLAIMS this)
   irrational   = contradicting reason                              (Otto DENIES this)
   moral and rational predicates are retained as LATER SCHEMATISATIONS of a prior datum
 SCHLEIERMACHER, for contrast (The Christian Faith)
   essence of religion = the FEELING OF ABSOLUTE DEPENDENCE
   Otto's implicit correction: dependence is a feeling ABOUT MYSELF; the numinous is
   constituted by DIRECTEDNESS TO A WHOLLY OTHER — it has an object-pole built in.
 OBJECTION: psychology can explain awe — evolutionarily, socially, neurologically — without any
   numinous object; Otto has reified a culturally trained emotion.
 REPLY: he describes the INTENTIONAL STRUCTURE of the state, and intentionality is a form, not
   an extra feeling. Fear-of-a-tiger is not tiger-flavoured anxiety; it is directed.
 BOUNDED: describing a state as object-directed does not show the object exists. Say both halves.
 CROSS-CULTURAL LIMIT: Indian traditions often organise the sacred through PURITY and
   AUSPICIOUSNESS (pavitra, suddha, mangala), not through dread. A real limit on Otto's claim.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/25: Stace — two types, five shared marks, one common core
 EXTROVERTIVE  the unifying vision is achieved THROUGH the physical senses; the mystic still
               sees the multiplicity of objects but perceives the ONE SHINING THROUGH them,
               with an apprehension of an inner subjectivity or life in all things
 INTROVERTIVE  ALL sensory and conceptual content is EXCLUDED, leaving a unitary, CONTENTLESS
               pure consciousness — non-spatial, non-temporal.
               Stace regards this as the HIGHER and more complete type.
 FIVE SHARED MARKS (reported WITHIN the descriptions, not imposed afterwards)
   sense of objectivity or reality | blessedness and peace | feeling of the holy or sacred |
   paradoxicality | ineffability
 THE DECISIVE METHODOLOGICAL MOVE
   separate the EXPERIENCE ITSELF from its INTERPRETATION
   -> the experiences converge cross-culturally; the interpretations (theistic, monistic,
      Buddhist) diverge   ==> the classic PERENNIALIST / COMMON-CORE thesis
 OBJECTION: "interpretation" is doing all the work — subtract enough of it from any two
   experiences and they will look alike.
 REPLY: the five marks are reported inside the descriptions; the claim then narrows from
   "identical core" to "recurrent reported marks" — weaker, and more defensible.
 ANCHOR: W. T. Stace, Mysticism and Philosophy, 1960.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/25: Zaehner — three types, no common core, and a contested ranking
 (1) NATURE MYSTICISM (pan-en-henic, "all-in-one")
       a sense of oneness with nature; Zaehner controversially groups certain DRUG-INDUCED
       states here, IN EXPLICIT OPPOSITION to Aldous Huxley's claims for mescaline
 (2) MONISTIC MYSTICISM
       isolation or identity of the soul with an IMPERSONAL absolute
       — Samkhya-Yoga's aloneness (kaivalya); Advaita's non-dual realisation
 (3) THEISTIC MYSTICISM
       loving union with a PERSONAL God in which the creature/creator distinction is PRESERVED
       — Ramanuja, Sufism, Christian mysticism
 !! ZAEHNER RANKS THEM, theistic highest. Widely criticised as CONFESSIONALLY MOTIVATED
    (he was a Catholic convert). State the criticism — and state what survives it.
 WHAT SURVIVES: monistic and theistic reports differ in LOGICAL FORM.
    IDENTITY abolishes one term; RELATION requires two. A common-core theory must EXPLAIN that
    away, not assume it.
 EXAM MOVE: KEEP the classification, DROP the ranking.
 STACE vs ZAEHNER ARE RIVALS, NOT COMPLEMENTS: 2 types + a core, against 3 types + no core.
 ANCHOR: R. C. Zaehner, Mysticism Sacred and Profane, 1957.

 KATZ'S THESIS, EXACTLY: "there are no unmediated experiences". A Jewish mystic does not have a
   Buddhist experience differently described; they have DIFFERENT EXPERIENCES.
 FORMAN'S REPLY: the PURE CONSCIOUSNESS EVENT — a contentless state has nothing for concepts
   to shape, so the thesis fails at the introvertive limit Stace called the higher type.
 HONEST VERDICT: constructivism is STRONG for extrovertive and theistic reports, WEAKEST for
   the contentless case — which is exactly where its opponents concentrate.
 WHAT IT DEFEATS: the CONVERGENCE INFERENCE, not the occurrence. Mediated perception is still
   perception; separate OCCURRENCE, INTERPRETATION and VERIDICALITY and argue each on its own.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/25: Advaita — the route, the image, and the terminological firewall
 THE ROUTE
   hearing (sravana) -> reflection (manana) -> contemplation (nididhyasana) -> realisation
   instruction PREPARES and REMOVES; it does not manufacture an object.
 THE IMAGE — THE TENTH MAN
   nine men count the party and each forgets to count himself; they grieve for a man who was
   never lost. Instruction removes IGNORANCE OF AN ALREADY PRESENT FACT.
   This is the exact model of how sruti is supposed to work: removal, not production.
 STABILITY OVER ECSTASY
   it is not a temporary altered state alone; stable knowledge (jnana) matters more than
   episodic ecstasy. Assessment is SOTERIOLOGICAL: does it remove ignorance and bondage?
 >>> TERMINOLOGICAL FIREWALL <<<
   do NOT call this experience anirvacaniya.
   In Advaita's technical vocabulary anirvacaniya = "indescribable as either real or unreal"
   (sad-asad-vilaksana) and is the status of MAYA and the WORLD-APPEARANCE — never of Brahman
   or of Brahman-realisation. A 2025 Religious Language question prints anirvacaniyata in its
   own proper sense; importing it here is a scripted error.
 OTHER TRAPS: do not write "a subject perceiving Brahman as object"; do not translate ananda
   as "happiness" or "ecstasy".

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 14/25: Vedantic plurality — the 2021 stem says "tradition", not "Advaita"
 SANKARA (Advaita)        highest realisation = NON-OBJECTIVE RECOGNITION OF IDENTITY;
                          subject-object duality belongs to ignorance; Brahman is self-luminous
                          consciousness presupposed by every act of knowing
 RAMANUJA (Visistadvaita) rejects the erasure of difference: Brahman is PERSONAL; selves and
                          world are REAL MODES dependent on God; fulfilment is loving knowledge
                          and communion in which the worshipper is NOT dissolved
 MADHVA (Dvaita)          hardens the distinction: God and soul remain ETERNALLY DIFFERENT;
                          saving experience is a GRACE-ENABLED RELATION
 ==> Vedanta contains BOTH identity-recognition AND intentional encounter. A script that
     reduces "the Vedantic tradition" to Advaita has misread the printed word.
 EPISTEMIC STATUS VARIES WITH THE SCHOOL
   Advaita: self-certifying, because self-luminous consciousness needs no second cognition
   Devotional schools: greater weight on scripture, grace and the transformed relation
 COMMON TEST ACROSS ALL SCHOOLS: SOTERIOLOGICAL — does it remove ignorance, egoism, bondage?
   That is what distinguishes Vedantic anubhava from an unregulated private impression.
 OBJECTION: incompatible structures suggest doctrine imposed on ambiguous states.
 REPLY: concede MEDIATION, deny FABRICATION. Each school supplies practice, grammar and tests
   of transformation — but those criteria are INTERNAL, and internal criteria do not prove the
   metaphysical object to an outsider.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/25: The object — sacred, holy, God (2018 Q7(a), 20 marks)
 EXACT STEM: "The terms 'Sacred' and 'Holy' have come to serve as generic names for the object
   of religion. Do you agree that one can have God as the object of religion? Discuss."
   ==> it GRANTS the generic category and asks about the SPECIFIC one. Answer in DEGREES.
 WHY 'SACRED/HOLY' BECAME GENERIC
  (1) OTTO      the HOLY names a quality of the object disclosed in numinous awe, PRIOR to and
                independent of moral and rational predicates -> more basic than "God"
  (2) DURKHEIM  (1912) the SACRED defined RELATIONALLY, by separation from the profane and by
                the prohibitions that protect it -> needs no deity; covers totemic religion
  (3) ELIADE    (1957) HIEROPHANY: the sacred manifests and structures sacred space and time
  (4) DECISIVE  non-theistic religions have a sacred but NO God: nirvana and the Dharma; the
                Tirthankara and the siddha; the authorless (apauruseya) Veda; the Tao
 THE GRADED ANSWER
   YES for theistic traditions — classical theism, Visistadvaita, Saiva Siddhanta, Islam,
       Judaism — where God is not one sacred item but the SOURCE AND MEASURE of holiness
   NO as a generic definition — that would stipulate Buddhism, Jainism and Mimamsa out of
       religion.  ==> 'SACRED' IS THE GENUS; GOD IS THEISM'S SPECIES OF IT.
 DISCRIMINATOR THAT EARNS MARKS: Otto's HOLY is NOT Durkheim's SACRED. Phenomenological
   quality of an object vs social classification. Treating them as one word misses the
   strongest objection to each.
 INDIAN PARITY: pavitra/suddha, mangala, punya, tirtha, ksetra, devalaya; parvan days, muhurta,
   the ritual calendar; the Veda's sanctity as apauruseya — SANCTITY WITHOUT A SANCTIFIER.
 OBJ 1 over-breadth (flags, nations) -> REPLY add the SOTERIOLOGICAL requirement
 OBJ 2 God subordinated to a genus  -> REPLY the subordination is CONCEPTUAL, not ontological

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 17/25: Veridicality — the warrant ledger and the burden clause
 FOR VERIDICALITY
   CREDULITY (Swinburne): if it SEEMS to S that X is present, probably X is — absent defeaters
   TESTIMONY: others' reports are probably as reported — extends warrant to the HEARER
   NOETIC QUALITY: the state presents itself as disclosure, not as imagination
   CONVERGENCE: mystics across traditions report recurrent marks
 AGAINST — DEFEATERS
   (a) NATURALISTIC EXPLANATION: Freud's projection and wish-fulfilment; social and
       psychological formation; neural mediation
       >>> BURDEN: the critic must show the mechanism is UNRELIABLE, or that it FULLY explains
       the seeming WITHOUT the proposed object. A correlation alone is not a defeat. <<<
   (b) UNRELIABLE CONDITIONS: drugs, pathology, suggestion, deprivation
   (c) DIVERSITY  <== THE SERIOUS ONE: personal God / non-dual Brahman / emptiness reported
       with equal conviction. Which object is real?
 FOUR CRITICISMS AND THEIR REPLIES
   naturalism      -> origin does not by itself falsify content; defeaters must show unreliability
   conflicting     -> interpretation may vary while contact is common
   objects            REJOINDER: this reply can become UNFALSIFIABLE
   ineffability    -> limits exhaustiveness, not every statement; analogical report survives
   moral fruits    -> a criterion of AUTHENTICITY, not a deductive proof of the object
 RANKING (this is what earns the top band): naturalism is the LOUDEST and WEAKEST defeater;
   diversity is the QUIETEST and STRONGEST, because it needs only incompatibility.
 BALANCE TO WRITE: prima facie warrant for the SUBJECT + testimonial weight for the HEARER
   - public demonstration of the OBJECT.  "Causal explanation and epistemic defeat differ."

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 18/25: Alston — six steps, the exact conclusion, and the concession
 (1) THEORY OF APPEARING: perception = something PRESENTING ITSELF; sensory content is not
     essential to that structure, so a non-sensory presentation can still be perception
 (2) MYSTICAL PERCEPTION SHARES THE STRUCTURE: apparent object + mode of presentation +
     resulting belief   ==> M-BELIEFS (from mystical perception)
 (3) BELIEFS ARE FORMED WITHIN DOXASTIC PRACTICES: socially established, internally organised,
     with their own inputs, outputs and OVERRIDE procedures
 (4) NO DOXASTIC PRACTICE CAN BE SHOWN RELIABLE WITHOUT CIRCULARITY: sense perception cannot
     be validated except by using sense perception; likewise memory and induction
 (5) WE ARE NONETHELESS PRACTICALLY RATIONAL in engaging in sense perception
 (6) THEREFORE engagement in an established religious practice (Christian Mystical Practice,
     CMP) is PRACTICALLY RATIONAL, absent sufficient reason to think it unreliable
 CLAIMED:     prima facie justification for M-beliefs; PRACTICAL RATIONALITY of engagement
 NOT CLAIMED: proof that the practice is reliable; ultima facie justification
   >>> "Alston proved religious experience reliable" is the standard overstatement. <<<
 ILLUSTRATION: a novice cannot show sense perception reliable without already relying on it;
   the demand for non-circular validation, uniformly applied, destroys ALL our practices.
 DIVERSITY OBJECTION — WHICH ALSTON HIMSELF CALLS THE MOST SERIOUS: CMP, Buddhist, Advaitic and
   Islamic practices are each established and self-supporting; if that makes engagement
   rational, it makes engagement in INCOMPATIBLE practices rational.
   HIS ANSWER: it is rational to "SIT TIGHT" absent a neutral adjudicating ground — conceding
   the discomfort and that a better argument might overturn it. An honest answer stops there.
 SECOND OBJECTION: no cross-checking, prediction or intersubjective agreement.
   REPLY: CMP has internal OVERRIDE SYSTEMS — discernment criteria, doctrinal tests, spiritual
   direction — weaker than science's, but not absent.
 DISTINGUISH FROM SWINBURNE: credulity is about SEEMINGS; Alston's argument is about PRACTICES.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 19/25: Diversity — the defeater, the three exits, and the price
 THE DATA: personal God (relation) | non-dual Brahman (identity) | emptiness and cessation
   (no such term) | isolated consciousness (withdrawal) — all reported with equal conviction
 THE DEFEATER: apply the principle of credulity EVENLY and it licenses INCOMPATIBLE conclusions.
   So either the principle is restricted (on what principled ground?) or some seemings are
   non-veridical (which ones, and by what test?).
 EXIT 1  HICK — PLURAL MEDIATION: one Real an sich, experienced through culturally conditioned
         lenses.  PRICE: a Real beyond all determinate description cannot ground the
         DETERMINATE claims traditions make; and the reply can become UNFALSIFIABLE.
 EXIT 2  EXCLUSIVISM: one is right, the rest mistaken. Bites the bullet; owes an account of the
         others' sincerity, discipline and fruits.
 EXIT 3  CONSTRUCTIVISM (Katz): they are DIFFERENT EXPERIENCES, not one differently described.
 THE THREE-WAY SPLIT THAT SCORES
   Did something occur?      diversity is SILENT; the reports stand
   How was it interpreted?   diversity is DECISIVE; interpretation is tradition-relative
   Was it veridical?         diversity is a GENUINE DEFEATER; incompatible objects cannot all
                             be met
 OBJECTION: diversity would equally defeat ordinary perception, where observers also disagree.
 REPLY: the disanalogy is PROCEDURAL. Perceivers can move closer, measure, repeat, ask a third
   party. Religious practices have INTERNAL override systems but no CROSS-PRACTICE adjudicating
   procedure. Nothing in the practice is designed to settle disagreement ACROSS practices.
 SAFE RESIDUE: claim FAMILY RESEMBLANCE or ANALOGOUS TRANSFORMATION, not identity of content.
 BOUNDARY: complete pluralist theory belongs to the Religious Pluralism owner.

 SIGNIFICANCE IN RELIGIOUS LIFE (the second printed deliverable) — three functions

   RENEWAL  later mystics re-authenticate the tradition from within

 INDIAN PARALLEL, BOUNDED: sruti removes ignorance while realisation is the cognitive event —
   instruction to insight, not message to messenger.

   projection. REPLY: the distinction is preserved by DISCERNMENT, not by phenomenology — and
   discernment tests AUTHENTICITY, not truth.
 BOUNDARY: faith's RECEPTION of revelation belongs to the Reason, Revelation and Faith owner.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 21/25: Prayer and worship (2020 Q7(b), 15 marks)
 AXIS                | PRAYER                        | WORSHIP
 --------------------+-------------------------------+------------------------------------
 Essential act       | ADDRESS / communication       | ASCRIPTION OF SUPREME WORTH (homage)
 Direction           | person-to-person (I-Thou)     | creature-before-the-holy
 Form                | verbal or silent; spontaneous | ritual, embodied, rule-governed
 Locus               | often private                 | characteristically corporate
 Presupposes         | a God who can HEAR and RESPOND| a reality of supreme value;
                     |                               | RESPONSE NOT REQUIRED
 Non-theistic avail. | strained                      | available
 --------------------+-------------------------------+------------------------------------
 OVERLAP: ADORATION — address (prayer) AND ascription of worth (worship).
 LIMIT CASE: wordless contemplative prayer — address persists after request disappears.
 KINDS OF PRAYER: A-C-T-S = Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving, Supplication (+ intercession,
   contemplative prayer).
 PROBLEM 1 — PETITION: omniscient (already knows) + perfectly good (will already do the best)
   + immutable (cannot be changed) ==> petition is idle or manipulative.
   AQUINAS: we pray to OBTAIN WHAT GOD HAS DETERMINED SHALL BE OBTAINED THROUGH PRAYER — a
     genuine SECONDARY CAUSE inside the causal order, not an override of the primary one.
   STUMP (1979): asking PROTECTS THE RELATIONSHIP; unrequested benefaction from an overwhelming
     superior risks spoiling the recipient.  RESIDUE: justifies petition's VALUE, not that
     outcomes causally depend on it.
 PROBLEM 2 — WORSHIP AND AUTONOMY, RACHELS (1971): worship entails unconditional obedience;
   moral agency forbids abdicating final judgement; so nothing could be WORTHY OF WORSHIP.
   REPLIES: worship recognises WORTH, not blind obedience | a perfectly good being's commands
   coincide with moral judgement | the autonomy assumed is atomistic.
 THREE INDIAN CASES THAT DECIDE IT
   JAINISM  the Tirthankara is free of passion (vitaraga) and CANNOT RESPOND; image-veneration
            (caitya-vandana) = worship without any possibility of petition being answered
   MIMAMSA  efficacy lies in the unseen potency (apurva); the deity is a GRAMMATICAL DATIVE in
            the injunction, not an agent who grants
   ADVAITA  contemplative worship (upasana) of a qualified form is efficacious at the everyday
            level (vyavaharika) and is TRANSCENDED, not repudiated, at the ultimate level
   BUDDHISM ritual honour (puja) is commemorative and dispositional (Pure Land complicates it)
 PLACEMENT: prayer = RELATIONAL organ; worship = EVALUATIVE and COMMUNAL organ.
   A religion can survive the loss of petition; NONE survives the loss of worship, because
   nothing would then mark the ultimate as ultimate.
 VERDICT FORMULA: prayer presupposes a RESPONSIVE ultimate; worship presupposes only a
   SUPREMELY VALUABLE one.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 22/25: Public discourse (2019 Q7(c), 15 marks) — the ladder and the line
 THE EXACT QUESTION: not "is religious experience real?" but "HOW FAR can it be made a topic of
   public discourse?" ==> a DEGREE is required, never a yes/no.
 CASE FOR NO
   (1) INEFFABILITY resists the articulation discourse requires
   (2) PRIVILEGED ACCESS — James concedes authority only for the one who has the state
   (3) NO SHARED CHECKING PROCEDURE, unlike perception
   (4) WITTGENSTEIN's private-language considerations: a wholly private criterion is none
   (5) RAWLSIAN PUBLIC REASON: for coercive decisions, offer reasons others could accept
 CASE FOR YES — SIX INTERSUBJECTIVE CRITERIA (ascending publicity)
   (1) PHENOMENOLOGICAL DESCRIPTION — James, Otto, Stace, Zaehner built public typologies out
       of first-person documents: PUBLIC WORK ON PRIVATE DATA. Even ineffability is reportable.
   (2) COHERENCE — internally consistent, and consistent with what is otherwise known
   (3) DISCERNMENT NORMS — Teresa of Avila's criteria; the guru's verification; the acarya's
       testing
   (4) MORAL AND PRACTICAL FRUITS — transformation of conduct is OBSERVABLE
   (5) REPLICABILITY THROUGH DISCIPLINED PRACTICE — yoga, dhyana, nididhyasana: repeatable
       procedures with stated preconditions and predicted outcomes. Vivekananda and
       Radhakrishnan press this as an "experimental" CLAIM.
       LIMIT: the experiment cannot be run by an uncommitted observer -> closer to the
       training-dependence of EXPERT PERCEPTION than to a public laboratory test.
   (6) ALSTON'S DOXASTIC PRACTICES — legitimate topic of PUBLIC ARGUMENT ABOUT PRACTICES, if
       not of public verification of episodes
 ================================ THE LINE ==========================================
   All six are publicly assessable and test AUTHENTICITY. None transfers the EVIDENTIAL FORCE
   the experience has for its subject. CONCEDE THIS EXPLICITLY — the concession earns marks.
 ====================================================================================
 BRIDGE — HABERMAS: religious citizens may speak religiously in the INFORMAL public sphere,
   subject to an institutional TRANSLATION PROVISO before formal state decision-making.
   ==> the "how far" gets a determinate answer: FULLY in the informal sphere, CONDITIONALLY in
   the formal one.
 VERDICT: publicly DISCUSSABLE and privately PROBATIVE — not publicly demonstrative.
