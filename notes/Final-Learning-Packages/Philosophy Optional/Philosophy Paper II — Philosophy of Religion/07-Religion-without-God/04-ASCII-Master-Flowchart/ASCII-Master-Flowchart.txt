ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/26: Buddhism III - the devotional complication, handled correctly
LATER MAHAYANA devotion to Buddhas and bodhisattvas is real, widespread and religiously serious.
TWO TEMPTATIONS, BOTH WRONG
  X "so Buddhism became theistic"       - function mistaken for ontology
  X "so it must be a later corruption"  - an evidential claim you cannot support
THE CORRECT LINE
  V Buddhas and bodhisattvas may perform religious FUNCTIONS - refuge, gratitude, aspiration,
    even grace-like assistance - WITHOUT thereby becoming an omnipotent creator.
  V NON-THEISTIC DOES NOT MEAN DEVOTION-FREE. One sentence disarms the objection.
THREE OBJECTIONS AND THEIR REPLIES
  O1 karma is providence renamed      R1 causal-moral regularity is IMPERSONAL: nothing selects,
                                         forgives, rewards out of love, or answers
  O2 devotional Mahayana is theism    R2 performing a function is not occupying the creator role
  O3 Dharma is God renamed            R3 functional similarity does not establish ONTOLOGICAL
                                         IDENTITY
THE COST TO CONCEDE: petition, covenant and grace require a responsive God, and Buddhism does
 without them. Its devotional structure is DIFFERENTLY SHAPED, not absent.
VERDICT: a strong ACTUAL counterexample - PROVIDED religion is defined multidimensionally rather
 than stipulated as God-relation, in which case it would be excluded before being examined.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/26: Mimamsa II - the five-step argument for apurva
 1  a Vedic injunction PROMISES a result (heaven from the jyotistoma)
 2  the result does NOT follow immediately upon the act
 3  the act is momentary and PERISHES
 4  a PERISHED CAUSE cannot produce a LATER EFFECT
 5  .: the act must generate an UNSEEN POTENCY that persists until the result matures
    = APURVA, "the not-before" - something new that did not previously exist
EPISTEMIC STATUS: a POSTULATE (arthapatti), NEVER a perceived entity. Mimamsa's own epistemology
 licenses postulation to explain an otherwise inexplicable fact - so this is a SANCTIONED move,
 neither an observation nor an arbitrary invention.
WHY THIS REMOVES GOD, PART TWO: no DISPENSER of karmic fruit is needed, since apurva does that
 work - so the Nyaya argument that a superintendent of karma (karmadhyaksa) is required is
 ANSWERED DIRECTLY rather than ignored.
STATE ALL FIVE STEPS OR NONE: a bare assertion that ritual generates apurva, without the
 perished-cause problem, reads as vocabulary rather than as argument.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 14/26: Carvaka I - perception alone, and the four-element ontology
WHY ONLY PERCEPTION (pratyaksa)?
 1 a means of knowledge (pramana) must yield CERTAINTY about its object
 2 perception is self-evident and immediately certain
 3 every other candidate - inference, comparison, testimony, postulation - is either reducible to
   perception or DEPENDS ON A GENERAL RULE that perception cannot certify
 4 .: perception alone is a pramana, and knowledge does not extend beyond the perceptible
THE ONTOLOGY THAT FOLLOWS
 reality = the FOUR GROSS ELEMENTS (bhutacatustaya): earth, water, fire, air
 ETHER IS REJECTED because it is IMPERCEPTIBLE - not an omission but a consequence
 the human ends (purusarthas) reduce to KAMA and ARTHA, because the other two require a
 supersensible order to be intelligible
THE CERTAINTY CONDITION IS THE HINGE - and it is where the school is most vulnerable:
 PARITY OBJECTION: perception itself faces illusion, dream and defective organs, and is corrected
 only by further cognition. So either the certainty condition is weakened - and inference
 re-enters - or perception fails its own test.
NOTE THE SHAPE OF THE CONCLUSION: it is ALREADY DOMAIN-RESTRICTED in its own statement -
 inference "works by luck or habit" in daily life and is worthless where no perceptual check
 exists. That restriction is what makes the moderate reading available in Panel 17.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/26: Carvaka II - the attack on inference, in full
INFERENCE (anumana) depends on VYAPTI - an INVARIABLE, UNCONDITIONAL concomitance between the
 mark (hetu, e.g. smoke) and the inferred (sadhya, e.g. fire).
CARVAKA'S QUESTION: by what means is VYAPTI ITSELF known?
 +---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
 | (1) NOT BY PERCEPTION - perception grasps only PARTICULAR co-presences, here and now.       |
 |     Vyapti is a UNIVERSAL covering all cases, including unobserved past, future and remote  |
 |     ones, and no perception reaches those.                                                  |
 | (2) NOT BY INFERENCE - establishing this vyapti by another inference requires that          |
 |     inference's own vyapti: INFINITE REGRESS or CIRCULARITY.                                |
 | (3) NOT BY TESTIMONY - testimony's authority would itself have to be established, and for   |
 |     the Carvaka it is not a pramana at all.                                                 |
 | (4) THE UPADHI PROBLEM - even repeated observation cannot rule out an unnoticed CONDITIONING|
 |     FACTOR (upadhi) that makes the concomitance hold only contingently. Standard example:   |
 |     fire is accompanied by smoke ONLY WHEN THE FUEL IS WET. Unless every possible upadhi is |
 |     excluded - and that exclusion is itself unverifiable - the concomitance remains merely  |
 |     PROBABLE: deviation (vyabhicara) stays possible.                                        |
 +---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
CONCLUSION: inference cannot deliver CERTAINTY. Where it works in daily life it works by luck or
 habit; and precisely in the supersensible domain - God, soul, karma, afterlife - no perceptual
 check is EVER available, so there inference is worthless.
TRAP: "Carvaka rejects inference" states a CONCLUSION and omits the ARGUMENT the 2025 directive
 asked for. Give all four moves, including the upadhi residue.

 1 SELF-REFUTATION   "no inference is valid" is itself a UNIVERSAL claim about all inferences,

   ==> REJOINDER A: advance the denial DIALECTICALLY, as a PRASANGA (reductio) internal to the

 2 PRAGMATIC         no one, including the Carvaka, can eat, speak, teach or argue without

   (i) repeated uncontradicted co-observation (bhuyodarsana)
   (ii) NON-OBSERVATION OF ANY COUNTER-INSTANCE (vyabhicara-adarsana)
   (iii) TARKA - hypothetical reasoning showing that denying the concomitance leads to absurdity
   plus the technical device samanya-laksana-pratyasatti, explaining how a universal is
   cognitively available at all
 4 PARITY            perception itself errs - illusion, dream, defective organs - and is corrected

 5 (?) THE MODERATE STRAND - and the one that separates a top script

   ==> REJOINDER B: on this reading the school is NOT committed to self-refuting global

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 18/26: Carvaka V - the classification verdict and its payoff
 IS CARVAKA A "RELIGION WITHOUT GOD"?   ==>   NO. And saying so precisely is the mark-earning move.
   no soteriology | no sacred | no community of practice | no ultimate concern
 ITS PLACE IN THE CLAUSE: the LIMITING CASE at the other end.
   Buddhism, Jainism and Mimamsa show that religion can survive the LOSS OF GOD.
   Carvaka shows what happens when the EPISTEMIC ROUTE TO ANY SUPERSENSIBLE OBJECT is closed:
   you get not a godless religion but NO RELIGION AT ALL.
 THE PAYOFF: the contrast establishes that what non-theistic religions retain is NOT merely a
   substitute deity but an ACCEPTED SUPERSENSIBLE ORDER - dharma, karma, nirvana, apurva -
   which Carvaka alone rejects.
 VERDICT FORMULA (write it): "Carvaka's critique succeeds exactly as far as its epistemology does.
   If the STRONG thesis is held - perception alone - it is self-refuting and pragmatically
   unliveable. If the MODERATE thesis is held - inference is admissible only where its
   concomitance can in principle be perceptually checked - the critique is powerful, unrefuted,
   and fatal to every anumana-based natural theology, including Udayana's."
 WHY THE MISCLASSIFICATION IS SO COSTLY: candidates reach for Carvaka as the obvious "godless
   Indian school", and thereby show they think ABSENCE OF GOD is the criterion. Placing it
   correctly is the fastest available demonstration that you know what the criterion actually is.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 20/26: Nietzsche - diagnosis, genealogy, and the ownership gate
THE ARGUMENT IN FOUR STEPS
 1 modern truthfulness and science ERODE traditional belief
 2 inherited values DEPENDED on that framework
 3 their collapse creates NIHILISM
 4 overcoming it requires REVALUATION OF ALL VALUES and self-creation (the Ubermensch)
PRESUPPOSITION: values have GENEALOGIES tied to forms of life, and can be assessed as
 life-enhancing or life-denying.
CANONICAL EXAMPLE: RESSENTIMENT reverses aristocratic value terms and converts weakness into
 moral superiority.
THE DISTINCTION THAT MUST BE MADE: "God is dead" is a CULTURAL DIAGNOSIS, not a syllogism proving
 that God does not exist, and not an empirical report about an event.
OBJECTION -> REPLY: revaluation risks elitism and arbitrary power ==> the Nietzschean reply
 appeals to creative self-overcoming, though a universal normative standard remains difficult.
 The self-reference is acknowledged rather than escaped.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 21/26: Agnosticism - the definition, the two strengths, the relation
PART ONE - WHAT AGNOSTICISM IS
 the existence of God is UNKNOWN or UNKNOWABLE ==> suspend judgement.
 DISTINCT FROM ATHEISM, which DENIES; and from INDIFFERENCE, which does not raise the question.

 COINAGE: "agnostic" was coined by T. H. HUXLEY in 1869.
 ADJACENT POSITIONS: Kant's practical faith; Spencer's "Unknowable".
PART TWO - HOW AGNOSTICS RELATE RELIGION AND GOD (the SECOND printed deliverable)
 the two are SEPARABLE. An agnostic may affirm religion's ETHICAL dimension (conduct, compassion),
 its EXPERIENTIAL dimension (contemplation, awe), and its COMMUNAL and RITUAL dimensions, while
 WITHHOLDING assent on the EXISTENCE of God ==> a NON-THEISTIC or MINIMAL religiosity.
 PRESUPPOSITION, stated honestly: religious commitment can be GRADED and need not include
 metaphysical certainty.
 CANONICAL CASE: a person may participate in Buddhist ethical and meditative practice while
 withholding creator claims.
 OBJECTION -> REPLY: this reduces religion to ethics or aesthetics ==> community, ritual,
 transformation and orientation to ultimacy EXCEED ordinary morality.
TRAP: answering only Part One forfeits half the marks on the 2023 stem.

THREE OBJECTIONS -> THREE REPLIES

     ritual drama.
 (ii) MOTIVATIONAL: the practice's power derives from believing it true. R: fictionalists appeal
     to the sustained efficacy of narrative and ritual - an EMPIRICAL claim that remains CONTESTED.
 (iii) INTERNAL: believers themselves reject the description, so the account is REVISIONARY of the
     very thing it purports to save.
================= THE INDIAN COMPARISON - DO NOT OMIT =================
 ADVAITA already contains a TWO-LEVEL apparatus doing structurally similar work WITHOUT
 fictionalism: worship of the qualified absolute (saguna) is GENUINELY EFFICACIOUS at the everyday
 level (vyavaharika) and is SUBLATED, not exposed as fiction, at the ultimate level (paramarthika).
 ==> SUBLATION IS NOT FALSIFICATION.
 BUDDHISM's TWO TRUTHS (samvrti / paramartha) permit full participation in conventional religious
 practice while denying ultimate substantial reference.
 THE COMPARATIVE POINT THAT EARNS MARKS: Indian systems offer GRADED TRUTH where Western
 fictionalism offers PRETENCE - the Indian device preserves the practitioner's SINCERITY.
