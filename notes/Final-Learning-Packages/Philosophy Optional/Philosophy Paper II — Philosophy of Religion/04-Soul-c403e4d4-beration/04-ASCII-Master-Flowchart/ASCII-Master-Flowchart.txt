ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/32: Hick's replica and the duplication objection
   STAGE 1  a person disappears here and an exact psychophysical replica —
            same body-pattern, same memories, same character — appears elsewhere
        |   ordinary criteria would in practice treat this as the same person
   STAGE 2  the same thing happens at the moment of death
        |
   STAGE 3  the replica appears not elsewhere on earth but in a DIFFERENT SPACE,
            a resurrection world
        |
   HICK'S CLAIM: for every purpose that matters — memory, character, recognition,
   responsibility — this IS the same person.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   DUPLICATION OBJECTION
     if God can make ONE replica, God can make TWO
     two replicas cannot both be numerically identical with the original
     therefore either the criterion is not identity, or identity depends on the
     accident that no rival was produced
   HICK'S REPLY : restrict the case to a UNIQUE replica; ordinary criteria are then met
   REJOINDER    : the bare POSSIBILITY of duplication shows the criterion tracks
                  QUALITATIVE SIMILARITY, not NUMERICAL IDENTITY
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   STATUS CONTROL: the replica is a THOUGHT-EXPERIMENT DEFENCE of the coherence of
   resurrection identity. It is NOT evidence that anyone survives.
   Source: John Hick, Philosophy of Religion / Death and Eternal Life.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/32: Plato in the Phaedo, arguments 1 and 2
   ARGUMENT 1 — FROM OPPOSITES (CYCLICAL)
     PREMISE  opposites come to be from opposites: larger from smaller,
              weaker from stronger, waking from sleeping
     APPLY    being dead comes from being alive; by symmetry being alive
              must come from being dead
     CONCLUDE souls exist in some state between deaths, or the supply of
              the living would exhaust itself
     ASSESS   the pattern fits qualities admitting DEGREE, not existence and
              non-existence; and it yields a cosmic balance-sheet, not the
              survival of THIS soul
        |
   ARGUMENT 2 — FROM RECOLLECTION (ANAMNESIS)
     PREMISE  we judge sensible things — two sticks — to be DEFICIENTLY equal
     STEP     to judge a deficiency one must already possess the standard,
              Equality itself, which no sense-experience supplies
     CONCLUDE the soul acquired that standard before embodiment;
              learning is recollection
     EXAMPLE  equal sticks never perfectly instantiate Equality, yet we judge
              their shortfall by a standard not derived from them alone
     ASSESS   establishes PRE-EXISTENCE ONLY — which is why Plato must pair it
              with the cyclical argument to close the post-mortem direction
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   DIRECTIVE CONTROL: "critically examine Plato's a priori proofs" (2023 Q6(a), 20m)
   = FOUR SEPARATE MINI-ASSESSMENTS, never one summary with one general complaint.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/32: Plato in the Phaedo, arguments 3 and 4
   ARGUMENT 3 — FROM AFFINITY
     PREMISE  two kinds of thing: composite, visible, changing, perishable;
              and non-composite, invisible, stable, imperishable
     STEP     the body resembles the first, the soul the second
     CONCLUDE the soul is akin to the indissoluble, hence itself indissoluble
     ASSESS   Plato presents this as LIKENESS, with more caution than summaries
              suggest; resemblance to the imperishable does not entail
              imperishability, and simplicity is assumed rather than proved
        |
   ARGUMENT 4 — THE FINAL ARGUMENT: THE FORM OF LIFE
     PREMISE  some things bring a Form with them wherever they go:
              fire brings heat, snow brings cold
     STEP     soul brings LIFE wherever it is
     STEP     what essentially brings a Form cannot admit that Form's opposite
     CONCLUDE soul cannot admit death; it is deathless and indestructible
     ASSESS   the inference from "cannot admit the opposite" to "cannot cease"
              needs a bridge: fire does not become cold, but fire GOES OUT.
              The argument shows a soul cannot be a DEAD soul; the critic asks
              why it may not simply cease to exist
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   WHAT EACH ARGUMENT ACTUALLY BUYS
     1 persistence through the transition, both directions (weak analogy)
     2 PRE-EXISTENCE only
     3 likeness to the indissoluble
     4 essential predication, with an unbridged step to indestructibility

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/32: Simmias inside the dialogue, Kant outside it
   SIMMIAS' OBJECTION — THE SOUL AS ATTUNEMENT (HARMONIA)
     the soul is an attunement of bodily elements, as the tuning of a lyre is
     of its strings; when the instrument is destroyed the attunement perishes,
     however "divine and beautiful" it was
        |
   PLATO'S TWOFOLD REPLY
     (1) recollection shows the soul existed BEFORE the body — but an attunement
         cannot precede the instrument it tunes; so the two doctrines are
         incompatible and Simmias must give one up
     (2) attunements admit of degree and never oppose their own components;
         the soul RULES the body, opposing appetite and desire, which no
         attunement does
   NOTE: the harmony objection is the ancient ancestor of every modern
   brain-dependence argument. Omitting Simmias is the standard band-loss on 2023.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   KANT — THE PARALOGISMS OF PURE REASON
     the "I think" that accompanies all representations gives a FORMAL UNITY of
     consciousness, not knowledge of a simple persisting SUBSTANCE
     rational psychology mistakes a condition of thought for an object of knowledge
     CONSEQUENCE: immortality cannot be theoretically demonstrated
     RELOCATION : in the second Critique it returns as a POSTULATE OF PRACTICAL
                  REASON, required by the moral law's demand for the complete good
   CONTROL: Kant does NOT claim the soul does not exist. He denies that it is a
   knowable substance. This relocation is exactly what 2020 Q5(b) invites.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   INDIAN PARALLEL, USED CAREFULLY: atman (Vedanta), jiva (Jainism) and the Nyaya
   self are ETERNAL in their systems, and Gita 2.23 gives the canonical image —
   but the Indian route is scriptural and systematic, not a priori in Plato's sense.
   Compare CONCLUSIONS, never METHODS.

                             |
   VERDICT: an immortal soul is SUFFICIENT for rebirth, NOT NECESSARY.
   What rebirth requires is a CONTINUITY-BEARER, and the dispute is only about
   whether that bearer must be a substance.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   SECOND PRESSURE, FROM INSIDE THE SUBSTANTIALIST CAMP
     Samkhya-karika 62-63: strictly NO PURUSA is bound, released or transmigrating;
     it is PRAKRTI who binds herself, transmigrates and releases herself.
     So even a school with an eternal consciousness denies that IT undergoes rebirth.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE SAME RAIL, APPLIED TO LIBERATION (2024 Q6(a))
     ROBUSTNESS TEST: a conception of liberation is robust if it fixes
       (i) what bondage is  (ii) what is liberated
       (iii) what the terminus is  (iv) why the terminus is final
     Buddhism satisfies all four WITHOUT an immortal soul.
     VERDICT: rebirth (or an equivalent bondage-HISTORY) is effectively necessary;
     immortality of a SUBSTANTIAL soul is not.
   TEMPLATE LINE: "Immortality of a substantial soul is SUFFICIENT for rebirth but
   not NECESSARY: Buddhism secures re-becoming through causal continuity alone."

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 17/32: Embodied liberation and the real-agent question
   WHY THE BODY REMAINS AFTER LIBERATING KNOWLEDGE
     knowledge destroys SANCITA (stored) and AGAMI (future) karma
     PRARABDHA has already begun to ripen and RUNS ITS COURSE
     IMAGE: the potter's wheel continues to spin after the potter's hand is withdrawn
     at its exhaustion the body falls -> VIDEHAMUKTI
   OBJECTION: if ignorance is destroyed, why does empirical activity continue?
   REPLY    : distinguish destroyed BONDAGE from residual embodied MOMENTUM;
              the residue is located in the body-mind complex, not in the knower
   REJOINDER: critics say a trace of ignorance is smuggled back under another name
   CROSS-SCHOOL CONTROL: Samkhya faces the same phenomenon and explains it
   DIFFERENTLY — SK 67 attributes the continuing body to the momentum of the GUNAS,
   not to karma; SK 68 gives videha-kaivalya at the body's fall. Shared image,
   different metaphysics.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   2022 Q7(b): CAN LIBERATION BE CONCEIVED WITHOUT A REAL AGENT?
   ADVAITA — YES, AND IT MUST BE
     agency belongs to the empirical individual, which is superimposed
     at the ultimate level (paramarthika) there is no agent, no bondage and hence
     no one who is literally liberated
     the tradition itself says bondage and release are stated "from the standpoint
     of ignorance"
   VISISTADVAITA — NO
     an unreal agent cannot have real bondage, real striving, real devotion,
     or a real reward; liberation must happen TO someone, so the jiva is
     eternally real
   HONEST VERDICT: each position pays a price. Advaita must explain how an unreal
   individual undertakes a real discipline; Visistadvaita must explain how a really
   distinct soul is nonetheless WHOLLY dependent on God.

     pleasure or bliss

                negative and complete: no pain can arise again, because the

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 24/32: The three-means dispute — apparatus and positions
   THE EXACT QUESTION: are knowledge, action and devotion ALTERNATIVE routes,
   SEQUENTIAL stages, or COMBINED? Naming the disagreement IS the answer.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THREE DISTINCTIONS THAT SEPARATE A 7 FROM A 12
     (1) DIRECT MEANS (saksat-sadhana)  vs  AUXILIARY AID (arad-upakaraka)
     (2) MEANS  vs  QUALIFICATION (adhikara) — karma may qualify one for knowledge
         without being a means to liberation
     (3) PRODUCTION MODEL  vs  REMOVAL MODEL of liberation
           if moksa is a RESULT to be produced -> action can produce it
           if moksa is the REMOVAL of an error -> only knowledge can remove it
           SAMKARA'S ENTIRE CASE RESTS ON THE SECOND
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE POSITIONS
     PURVA-MIMAMSA  KARMA ALONE — the Veda is essentially injunctive; knowledge-
                    passages are subordinate to ritual action. This is the purvapaksa
                    Samkara argues against, and naming it shows why the dispute exists
     SAMKARA        JNANA ALONE liberates. Moksa is not a producible effect (sadhya)
                    but the eternally accomplished nature of the self (siddha),
                    obscured by avidya. REJECTS jnana-karma-samuccaya-vada.
                    Karma and upasana remain indispensable PREPARATIONS
     RAMANUJA       SEQUENTIAL CULMINATION — karma-yoga purifies, jnana-yoga discloses
                    the self as a mode of God, both flow into BHAKTI-YOGA as the
                    direct means; prapatti is the accessible alternative. Accepts a
                    graded samuccaya that Samkara denies
     MADHVA         knowledge of the Lord's supremacy is prior; bhakti ripened from it
                    is the means; prasada is decisive
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 25/32: The three-means dispute — text signal, objections and verdict
   THE TEXT'S OWN SIGNAL
     Gita 5.4-5: only the immature (balah) declare Samkhya (knowledge) and Yoga
     (action) to be different; one who reaches by one reaches by the other.
     -> CONVERGENCE, which is why SAMKARA claimed it for jnana, RAMANUJA for bhakti,
        and TILAK (Gita-Rahasya) for karma.
     VIVEKANANDA: four yogas (jnana, bhakti, karma, raja) matched to TEMPERAMENT —
     psychological, not metaphysical, pluralism.
   OBJECTION: convergence is diplomatic mush — knowledge that dissolves individuality
   and devotion that preserves it cannot terminate in the same state.
   REPLY    : they converge in FUNCTION (dismantling egoic self-appropriation) and
              diverge in TERMINUS (identity vs communion). Say exactly this.
   SECOND OBJECTION: if karma cannot liberate, why does the Gita command action?
   REPLY    : niskama-karma does not liberate BY PRODUCING moksa; it liberates by
              dismantling the agency-sense that sustains bondage.
   VERDICT FORMULA: KARMA QUALIFIES · BHAKTI RECEIVES · JNANA REMOVES.
   Which is DIRECT is settled by a prior question: is liberation PRODUCED or UNCOVERED?

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 27/32: Identity failures both ways, and rebirth versus reincarnation
   THE IDENTITY PROBLEM, BOTH WAYS
     FOR RESURRECTION: the GAP PROBLEM (what exists between death and raising?) and
       the RE-ASSEMBLY / "CANNIBAL" objection (if matter is shared, whose atoms rise?)
       AQUINAS ......... the rational soul is the FORM of the body, securing continuity
       VAN INWAGEN ..... God PRESERVES the body (or a core) and leaves a simulacrum
       HICK ............ a unique psychophysical REPLICA with memory and character IS
                         the same person for all purposes that matter
       DUPLICATION OBJECTION -> if one replica, why not two? Hick restricts to a
       UNIQUE replica; critics answer that possibility of duplication already shows
       the criterion is not identity
     FOR REBIRTH: the MEMORY OBJECTION — no recollection of prior lives.
       Reply: psychological memory is not metaphysical continuity; latent samskaras
       and vasanas connect lives. (?) Coherent, but evidentially empty.
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   REBIRTH vs REINCARNATION (2019 Q8(a) DEMANDS THIS)
     REINCARNATION (re-enfleshing) presupposes a SUBSTANTIAL soul re-entering a body
       -> apt for Vedanta, Nyaya, Jainism
     REBIRTH is the WIDER, neutral term and the ONLY correct one for Buddhism's
       punarbhava without a transmigrating substance
     Gita 9.21 (pitryana): those who exhaust their merit RETURN to the world of
       mortals — even heaven is a WITHIN-SAMSARA stage, not resurrection
   VERDICT FORMULA: resurrection asks how a person can survive when the person is
   essentially embodied; rebirth asks how moral desert can be discharged when a
   single life is too short. NEITHER IS A VERSION OF THE OTHER, and the identity
   problem is unresolved on both sides — for OPPOSITE reasons.
