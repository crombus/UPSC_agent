ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/22: Mackie's logical problem and the two bridges

   J. L. MACKIE, "Evil and Omnipotence", MIND (1955)

   THE SET AS USUALLY PRINTED          THE SET AS IT MUST BE WRITTEN
   (1) God is omnipotent               (1) God is omnipotent
   (2) God is perfectly good           (2) God is omniscient
   (3) Evil exists                     (3) God is perfectly good
                                       (4) Evil exists
   -> NOT a formal contradiction:      + BRIDGE A (goodness): a perfectly good being
      nothing in (1)-(3) plus logic       eliminates every evil it can, unless it has
      alone entails p and not-p           a morally sufficient reason
                                       + BRIDGE B (power): an omnipotent being has no
                                         relevant limit on preventing any evil whose
                                         prevention is logically possible
                                       -> NOW the set is inconsistent
                                    |
   MACKIE'S OWN TAXONOMY
   ADEQUATE SOLUTIONS   honestly abandon one of the propositions
   FALLACIOUS SOLUTIONS appear to keep all three while covertly limiting power or
                        redefining goodness — e.g. "good cannot exist without evil",
                        "evil is a necessary means to good", "the universe is better
                        with some evil", "evil is due to human free will"
                                    |
   THE THEIST'S THREE EXITS — EACH DENIES SOMETHING
   deny BRIDGE A      -> insert "unless there is a morally sufficient reason"
                         -> the entire space of DEFENCES and THEODICIES opens
   deny BRIDGE B      -> Plantinga (God cannot causally determine free rightness) or
                         process theism (classical omnipotence rejected outright)
   deny (4)'s force   -> privation (Augustine) or the empirical level (Advaita)
                         COST: the seriousness of suffering
                                    |
   VERDICT: the logical problem is widely regarded as ANSWERED. The live debate has
   MIGRATED to Rowe's evidential form, where consistency no longer suffices.

   CAN AN OMNIPOTENT BEING CREATE A THING IT CANNOT AFTERWARDS CONTROL?
                                    |
        +---------------------------+---------------------------+
       YES                                                      NO
   there is now something it CANNOT do:                 there is something it CANNOT do:
   control that thing                                   make such a thing
   -> FIRST-ORDER power limited                         -> SECOND-ORDER power limited
   (power to act on existing things)                    (power to determine what powers exist)
        +---------------------------+---------------------------+
                                    |
   MACKIE: unrestricted omnipotence in BOTH senses is incoherent.
                                    |
   THREE RESTRICTIONS THAT DISSOLVE IT, WITH THEIR COSTS
   (a) LOGICAL-POSSIBILITY   omnipotence covers only the logically possible
                             COST: modest, near-universally conceded; but it does not
                             settle whether "free agents who never go wrong" is
                             actualizable by God
   (b) SELF-BINDING          second-order sovereignty exercised IN creating free agents
                             COST: restraint becomes VOLUNTARY, re-opening the question
                             why God does not intervene in the worst cases
   (c) ABANDONMENT           process theism drops classical omnipotence
                             COST: no guarantee of any final victory over evil
                                    |
   LINK TO EVIL — THE STEM'S CONTEXT CLAUSE IS MANDATORY
   the FREE-WILL DEFENCE REQUIRES (a) or (b): God creates significantly free agents and
   cannot causally guarantee that they choose well.
   PLANTINGA: God CHOOSES not to override.   GRIFFIN: God CANNOT coerce.
   VERDICT: the paradox does not refute theism; it forces theism to SPECIFY which sense
   of omnipotence it defends, and to pay for the choice.
   BOUNDARY: the attribute's independent coherence belongs to Notions of God.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/22: Plantinga's free-will defence and transworld depravity

   ALVIN PLANTINGA, God, Freedom, and Evil (1974) — A DEFENCE, NOT A THEODICY

   (1) A world with significantly free creatures is more valuable, other things equal,
       than a world with no free creatures at all.
   (2) Significant freedom entails genuinely available alternatives: an agent free with
       respect to an action can perform it or refrain.
   (3) God cannot CAUSALLY DETERMINE a creature to FREELY do only what is right —
       that description cancels itself.
   (4) Therefore God can create free persons only by accepting the POSSIBILITY of misuse.
   (5) Therefore moral evil is COMPATIBLE with omnipotence and perfect goodness.
                                    |
   MACKIE'S OBJECTION: why not create free agents who ALWAYS freely choose the good?
   That is logically possible, so omnipotence covers it.
                                    |
   REPLY — TRANSWORLD DEPRAVITY: it is POSSIBLE that every creaturely essence God could
   instantiate would go wrong in some circumstance. If so, a world of free agents who
   never go wrong was not available to God to actualize.
   MODAL STATUS: POSSIBILITY ONLY. Plantinga never claims it is ACTUAL, and does not need to.
   -> attacking it as "empirically unsupported" misses the modal point entirely.
                                    |
   PRESUPPOSITION: libertarian freedom is coherent and sufficiently valuable.
   A compatibilist denies premise (3) and rejects the defence at its root.
   RESIDUE: NATURAL EVIL is untouched unless a further hypothesis is added.
   ANSWER LINE: a world of programmed beneficence lacks the MORAL AUTHORSHIP of a world
   containing genuinely responsible agents.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/22: Augustine — privation, sin and punishment (2023 Q8(a), 20 marks)

   AUGUSTINE, Enchiridion and City of God — RECONSTRUCT BEFORE OBJECTING

   (1) Being, insofar as it is being, is GOOD.
   (2) God creates BEINGS; God does not create evil substances.
   (3) Corruption is PARASITIC: it depends on a good nature to corrupt.
   (4) Moral fault is DISORDERED WILLING — the will turning from a greater to a lesser good.
   => EVIL = PRIVATION OF A DUE GOOD (privatio boni), not a rival creative principle.
                                    |
   THE 2023 THESIS: "all evil is either sin or the punishment for sin"
   +-------------------------------+--------------------------------+
   | MORAL EVIL = SIN              | NATURAL EVIL = PUNISHMENT      |
   | misuse of created freedom     | a fallen, disordered order     |
   | the will's own defect         | consequent on the Fall         |
   +-------------------------------+--------------------------------+
   CANONICAL EXAMPLE: rot is not a rival substance to wood; it is the loss of the wood's
   proper integrity.  Blindness is a REAL lack in a creature whose nature calls for sight.
   -> PRIVATION DOES NOT MAKE PAIN IMAGINARY. This is the commonest misreading.
                                    |
   FOUR OBJECTIONS
   ! innocent suffering — infants and the congenitally afflicted fit punishment poorly
   ! animal and pre-human suffering — no choice, no sin, no growth
   ! privation is strained for intense POSITIVE pain, which does not feel like an absence
   ! INHERITED GUILT vs INHERITED CONDITION:
       personal guilt for an ancestor's act        -> morally the harder claim
       inherited damaged condition + disordered    -> defensible, BUT still owes an account
       social-natural order                           of why the condition and its unequal
                                                      effects are permitted
                                    |
   COMPARATORS: HICK replaces PUNISHMENT with DEVELOPMENT; KARMA replaces a single FALL
   with a BEGINNINGLESS series and answers DISTRIBUTION, at the cost of verifiability.
   VERDICT: preserves divine goodness for moral evil; INCOMPLETE for natural, innocent
   and animal suffering.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/22: Hick's Irenaean soul-making

   JOHN HICK, Evil and the God of Love (1966)

   (1) Ready-made virtue is not ACHIEVED virtue.
   (2) Courage, compassion and trust require real difficulty, need and risk.

   (4) Humans are created IMMATURE, at an EPISTEMIC DISTANCE from God, so that faith and

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/22: Rowe's evidential problem and the inductive step

   WILLIAM ROWE, "The Problem of Evil and Some Varieties of Atheism" (1979)

   (1) There exist instances of intense suffering that an omnipotent, omniscient being
       could have prevented without thereby losing some greater good or permitting some
       equally bad or worse evil.
   (2) An omniscient, wholly good being would prevent the occurrence of any intense
       suffering it could, unless it could not do so without losing some greater good or
       permitting some equally bad or worse evil.
   (3) Therefore there does not exist an omnipotent, omniscient, wholly good being.
                                    |
   THE CANONICAL CASE — THE SUFFERING FAWN
   a fawn trapped in a forest fire started by lightning, severely burned, lying in agony
   for days before dying; UNOBSERVED by any human; NO moral growth follows.
   WHAT IT IS FOR: premise (1) — an EVIDENTIAL claim.
   WHAT IT IS NOT: a deductive contradiction.
   WHY BUILT THAT WAY: it removes free will, soul-making, desert and observer benefit
   from the case ALL AT ONCE.
                                    |
   THE INDUCTIVE STEP — THE WHOLE BATTLEGROUND
   "I can see no justifying reason for this evil."
        -> "There is no justifying reason for this evil."
        -> "Gratuitous evil exists."
        -> "An omnipotent, wholly good God probably does not exist."
   The step is an inference from IGNORANCE to ABSENCE. It is not automatically fallacious:
   we make such inferences correctly whenever we WOULD EXPECT to see the thing if it were
   there. Everything turns on whether a divine justifying reason is such a thing.
                                    |
   THREE THEIST ROUTES
   (a) attack the INFERENCE      -> skeptical theism (Panel 12)
   (b) supply a POSITIVE REASON  -> theodicy (Panels 9, 10, 13)
   (c) revise the GOD-CONCEPT    -> process, finite God, non-creator systems
   ! (a) and (b) sit AWKWARDLY together: one says the reason is unknowable, the other
   names it. Noticing the tension earns marks.
   Rowe claims EVIDENTIAL FORCE, not demonstration, and allows the theist may stay rational.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 12/22: Skeptical theism, CORNEA and its price

   PROPONENTS: STEPHEN WYKSTRA · WILLIAM ALSTON · MICHAEL BERGMANN · DANIEL HOWARD-SNYDER
   IT IS A DEFENCE, NOT A THEODICY. IT TARGETS THE EVIDENTIAL, NOT THE LOGICAL, PROBLEM.

   WYKSTRA'S CORNEA (1984) — Condition of ReasoNable Epistemic Access
   "It appears that p is absent" is a legitimate claim ONLY IF it is reasonable to believe
   that, given one's cognitive situation, p WOULD LIKELY BE DISCERNIBLE if it were there.
                                    |
   BERGMANN'S REPRESENTATIVENESS THESES
   our sample of (i) possible GOODS, (ii) possible EVILS and (iii) the ENTAILMENT
   RELATIONS between them is NOT representative of the total space
                                    |
   THEREFORE the NOSEEUM inference — "no see 'um, so none there" — is UNWARRANTED for
   goods that would justify divine permission.
                                    |
   ANALOGIES
   ALSTON : a novice examines a chess grandmaster's move, sees no point in it, and has no
            warrant to conclude there is none
   WYKSTRA: an infant cannot see why a painful procedure is performed; that inability is
            no evidence that there is no reason
                                    |
   THREE STANDING COSTS
   ! MORAL SCEPTICISM (Almeida, Russell, Wielenberg): if we cannot judge an evil
     gratuitous, we equally cannot judge that we ought to PREVENT it — the duty to rescue
     is threatened. REPLY: obligation is fixed by what we KNOW. Critics: unstable.
   ! PARITY / DIVINE DECEPTION: the same scepticism undercuts divine promises and
     revelation. REPLY: restrict it to the value-entailment domain. Critics: ad hoc.
   ! BRACKETING: extended, no empirical evidence could ever count against theism —
     unfalsifiability is a cost even if it is not a refutation.
                                    |
   INDIAN PARITY: unseen moral potency (adrsta) is beyond the senses (atindriya), known
   only through testimony (sabda); the precise ripening of karma is among the Buddhist
   imponderables (acinteyya). Genuine parallel — and it inherits the SAME objection:
   a mechanism placed permanently beyond checking is evidence neither for nor against.
   TRAP: "God's ways are mysterious" is NOT skeptical theism. State CORNEA or Bergmann,
   or do not use the position at all.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 13/22: Horrendous evils — balancing off versus defeat

   MARILYN McCORD ADAMS, Horrendous Evils and the Goodness of God (1999)
   PROMOTED TO CORE, 15 AUGUST 2026 — not an optional extra.

   DEFINITION (technical): a HORRENDOUS EVIL is one whose participation — as PERPETRATOR
   or as VICTIM — gives the participant PRIMA FACIE reason to doubt whether their life
   could be A GREAT GOOD TO THEM ON THE WHOLE.
   -> not a synonym for "very bad evil"; graphic detail is unnecessary.
                                    |
   THE ARGUMENT
   (1) Standard theodicies work at the GLOBAL level, balancing evil within the world's
       total value.
   (2) But horrendous evils threaten the INDIVIDUAL'S LIFE AS A WHOLE: a global balance
       can be struck while a particular life remains, for that person, a net disaster.
   (3) A God who is good TO PERSONS — not merely a good PRODUCER OF WORLDS — must DEFEAT
       the evil WITHIN the frame of the individual's life.
   (4) Only resources internal to a particular religion — divine identification with human
       suffering, beatific intimacy as an INCOMMENSURABLE good — can accomplish that.
                                    |
   THE DISTINCTION THAT EARNS THE MARKS
   +------------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
   | BALANCING OFF                | DEFEAT (Chisholm's term)                  |
   | the good OUTWEIGHS the evil  | the evil becomes an ORGANIC PART of a     |
   | in a sum; evil remains       | whole whose value it CONTRIBUTES to;      |
   | EXTERNAL to the good         | evil is INTEGRATED, not offset            |
   +------------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
   | GLOBAL defeat (world level)  | LOCAL / PERSONAL defeat (this life)       |
   | what theodicies deliver      | what Adams says is required               |
   +------------------------------+-------------------------------------------+
   ! COMPENSATION IS NOT DEFEAT. A later good can compensate for loss while leaving the
   evil externally balanced.
                                    |
   OBJECTIONS AND REPLIES
   ! purely eschatological, unverifiable, seemingly instrumentalising
     REPLY: Adams DENIES instrumentality — the evil need not be REQUIRED, only DEFEATED
   ! religion-specific resources abandon the neutral arena
     REPLY: the atheological argument targets a PARTICULAR theism, so a particular theism
     may answer
   INDIAN PARITY: karma explains DISTRIBUTION but not personal worth; Advaita's ultimate-
   level sublation and Buddhist liberation dissolve the standpoint from which the life is
   evaluated — critics call this evasive, defenders therapeutic.
   CONTRAST TO NAME: DEFEAT WITHIN the life  vs  EXIT FROM the life-series.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 14/22: Animal and pre-human suffering — the standing residue

   THE ARGUMENT
   (1) Animal suffering PREDATES human moral agency.
   (2) It cannot be the misuse of human free will.
   (3) It cannot straightforwardly be the environment of MORAL growth for the sufferer.
   (4) It cannot simply be called retributive.
   (5) THEREFORE standard theodicies leave a MAJOR REMAINDER.
   Rowe's fawn is the evidential illustration, not a deductive contradiction.
                                    |
   THE FIVE REPLIES, EACH WITH ITS COST
   NEO-CARTESIAN     animals lack the higher-order awareness that makes pain MATTER
                     morally (canvassed by MICHAEL MURRAY, Nature Red in Tooth and Claw,
                     2008)                COST: philosophically and empirically contested
   NOMIC REGULARITY  a stable law-governed world is needed to support any life at all
                                          COST: explains the KIND of world, not the AMOUNT
   SOUL-MAKING       real danger is the only condition in which courage, compassion and
   EXTENDED          stewardship TOWARD ANIMALS are possible
                                          COST: uses animals INSTRUMENTALLY
   ESCHATOLOGICAL    animal fulfilment or redemption in a final state (minority line)
                                          COST: unverifiable and undeveloped
   FALLEN-ORDER      a cosmic fall distorts nature prior to human history
                                          COST: ? historically and scientifically strained
                                    |
   INDIAN PARITY
   karma extends moral causation to ANIMAL BIRTHS (tiryak-yoni)
   JAINISM ascribes living selves graded sense-capacity and grounds NON-INJURY (ahimsa)
   BUDDHISM places animals within the same field of SUFFERING (duhkha) and extends
   COMPASSION (karuna) to sentient beings
   COST is symmetrical to karma's general cost: unverifiable, and never to be used to
   infer that present suffering is deserved.
   OBJECTION: an animal cannot LEARN from a fruit it cannot understand.
   REPLY: karmic maturation is CAUSAL, not PEDAGOGICAL; the compassion it demands is
   unaffected by whether the animal grasps it.
                                    |
   VERDICT: only NOMIC REGULARITY survives on the Western side, and it explains the KIND
   of world rather than the AMOUNT of pain in it; Indian karma answers the DISTRIBUTION
   question the West cannot, at the price of an unverifiable trans-life mechanism.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/22: Process theodicy (2025 Q6(b), 15 marks) — the six tenets

   SIX TENETS — "MAIN TENETS" IS AN ENUMERATION DIRECTIVE, SO NUMBER THEM
   (1) CREATIVITY, not God, is the ultimate; actual entities are self-creative occasions.
   (2) God is DIPOLAR:
         PRIMORDIAL NATURE  ordered envisagement of possibilities, supplying each
                            occasion its INITIAL AIM
         CONSEQUENT NATURE  God's reception and preservation of what the world becomes
   (3) POWER IS INTRINSICALLY DISTRIBUTED: to be actual is to have some self-determination.
   (4) THEREFORE divine power is PERSUASIVE BY METAPHYSICAL NECESSITY (Griffin), NOT by
       self-limitation — the line that separates process theodicy from the free-will defence.
   (5) EVIL = finite occasions DEVIATING from the divine lure; God is not culpable.
   (6) God is "the FELLOW-SUFFERER WHO UNDERSTANDS" (Whitehead) — solidarity, not detachment.
                                    |
   NAME THE LAYER — DO NOT WRITE "PROCESS THEODICY (WHITEHEAD)" AND STOP
   WHITEHEAD  Process and Reality (1929): dipolar God, initial aim, consequent nature,
              the fellow-sufferer
   HARTSHORNE The Divine Relativity (1948); Omnipotence and Other Theological Mistakes
              (1984): classical omnipotence is INCOHERENT — to have ALL the power is to
              leave none to anything else; surrelativism/panentheism makes God supremely
              RELATED, not supremely UNAFFECTED
   GRIFFIN    God, Power, and Evil: A Process Theodicy (1976); Evil Revisited (1991):
              persuasion is a METAPHYSICAL NECESSITY, not a chosen restraint
   COBB       A Christian Natural Theology: the theological systematisation
   IT IS A THEODICY, NOT A DEFENCE: it claims God's ACTUAL situation and is therefore
   refutable in a way Plantinga's defence is not.
                                    |
   CRITICISM: a God who cannot guarantee victory over evil may be UNWORTHY OF WORSHIP;
   the metaphysics is contested; classical theists DENY that actuality entails
   self-determination.  REPLY: divine solidarity, and the OBJECTIVE IMMORTALITY of achieved
   value in the consequent nature — but the objection does not disappear.
   VERDICT: process theodicy purchases GOD'S INNOCENCE at the price of GOD'S SOVEREIGNTY.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/22: The non-classical reply-space and its costs

   THE REPLY-SPACE AROUND THE CLASSICAL THEODICIES — EVERY ENTRY IS PRICED
   GREATER-GOOD / STABLE ORDER  risk and regular law are needed for freedom, agency,
                                courage and compassion
                                COST: explains the KIND of world, not the AMOUNT or the
                                DISTRIBUTION of suffering
   LEIBNIZIAN BEST WORLD        Theodicee (1710), which also gives the word "theodicy":
                                a perfect God chooses the best total COMPOSSIBLE order,
                                not a world containing every local good
                                COST: there may be no unique best world, and world-total
                                optimisation can neglect goodness to particular persons
   ESCHATOLOGICAL FULFILMENT    no finite suffering is final; later fulfilment,
                                restoration or union can prevent a life from being
                                finally wasted
                                COST: compensation after suffering is not automatically
                                justification for permitting it
   DIVINE MYSTERY               finite minds may fail to grasp providential reasons
                                COST: unfalsifiable without an access principle;
                                skeptical theism is the disciplined evidential version
   PROTEST / ANTI-THEODICY      refusing to justify suffering protects truthfulness,
                                lament and the moral priority of resistance
                                COST: diagnoses the ETHICS of explanation but settles
                                neither the logical nor the evidential argument
                                    |
   THE REVISION STRATEGY IN GENERAL
   weaken OMNIPOTENCE  -> process, finite God           goodness preserved
   weaken PERSONALITY  -> impersonal Absolute, Advaita  evil at the empirical level only
   remove CREATORSHIP  -> Buddhism, Jainism, Mimamsa    no defendant at all
   EVERY revision purchases relief with a RELIGIOUSLY SIGNIFICANT LOSS.
   Naming the loss is what converts description into evaluation.
                                    |
   ! COMPENSATION IS NOT DEFEAT. Pastoral responsibility requires listening, protection
   and action BEFORE speculative explanation; a theodicy never licenses passivity toward
   preventable harm.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 17/22: The Indian response — karma and the schools

   WESTERN QUESTION: why does a good God PERMIT evil?
   INDIAN QUESTION : how does BONDAGE ORIGINATE, and how is it ENDED?
                                    |
   KARMA-SAMSARA: suffering is the fruit of one's own past deeds across lives; the moral
   law is SELF-OPERATING. Evil therefore raises no problem for a creator's goodness —
   it is COSMIC JUSTICE, not divine cruelty.
                                    |
   THE SCHOOLS DIFFER — KARMA IS NOT ONE DOCTRINE

                                                   -> THERE IS NO DEFENDANT

                                                   a JUDGE, not an arbitrary cause of evil
   ADVAITA       evil is real at the EMPIRICAL level (vyavaharika) and sublated under

                                    |
   BEGINNINGLESSNESS: the action-consequence series is treated as BEGINNINGLESS, so no
   first wrongful act need be identified. It blocks ONE regress question and VERIFIES
   NOTHING about any claimed past-life cause.

     past act" is UNAVAILABLE to ordinary observers; using karma that way converts
     explanation into VICTIM-BLAMING
   ! natural suffering, collective harms and unequal starting conditions still press the

   ! karma does not cancel present duties of COMPASSION, PROTECTION and RELIEF

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 19/22: Evil and the profane — the three theorists and the sequence

   2019 Q6(b), 15 marks. THIS IS A FUNCTIONAL, NOT A THEOLOGICAL, QUESTION.
   It does not ask whether evil disproves God. It asks what evil and the profane DO.

   THE THREE CANONICAL THEORISTS — DO NOT INTERCHANGE THEM
   DURKHEIM  The Elementary Forms of Religious Life (1912) — SOCIAL SIDE
             all known religious beliefs presuppose a classification of all things into
             two opposed classes, PROFANE and SACRED; religion = a unified system of
             beliefs and practices RELATIVE TO SACRED THINGS, uniting adherents into a
             single moral community, a CHURCH.  The definition is deliberately GOD-FREE.
   OTTO      The Idea of the Holy (1917) — OBJECT SIDE
             the sacred is an A PRIORI category of response: the NUMINOUS,
             mysterium tremendum et fascinans
   ELIADE    The Sacred and the Profane (1957) — SPATIAL / TEMPORAL
             the sacred irrupts as HIEROPHANY, generating sacred SPACE (a fixed centre,
             AXIS MUNDI, against homogeneous profane extension) and sacred TIME
             (recoverable and repeatable, against irreversible profane duration)
                                    |
   THE FOUR-STEP FOUNDING SEQUENCE
   (1) PREDICAMENT  something is wrong: sin · impurity · suffering (duhkha) · ignorance
                    (avidya) · karmic bondage · alienation
   (2) BOUNDARY     pure/impure · sacred/profane · saved/lost -> ritual, prohibition, code
   (3) INSTITUTION  priesthood · initiation · purification · penance · community discipline
   (4) SOTERIOLOGY  the ordered path OUT
   => evil and the profane are not embarrassments TO religion but part of its STRUCTURAL
      FOUNDATION: without a diagnosis of what is wrong, salvation-language has no work to do.
                                    |
   FOUR CATEGORIES THAT MUST NOT BE MERGED
   EVIL = moral or natural harm | PROFANE = the ORDINARY, the non-consecrated
   IMPURE = the ritually polluting | DEMONIC = the actively anti-sacred
   ! DURKHEIM'S PROFANE IS NOT EVIL. A ploughed field, a market and a weekday are profane
   and none of them is wicked. Conflating them is the commonest error on this stem.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 20/22: Evil and the profane — Indian parity, objections and verdict

   INDIAN PARITY (MANDATORY)
   BUDDHISM   the FIRST NOBLE TRUTH makes suffering (duhkha) the explicit foundation of
              an entire religion — the clearest world case of evil AS religion-founding
   JAINISM    inflow (asrava) and bondage (bandha) name the predicament;
              stoppage (samvara) and shedding (nirjara) name the exit
   ADVAITA    ignorance (avidya) is the founding defect; liberating knowledge is the exit
   BRAHMANICAL purity and impurity (suddhi/asuddhi) and cleanliness (sauca) organise
   RITUAL      practice; auspicious/inauspicious (mangala/amangala);
               demerit and merit (papa/punya)
   MIMAMSA    ritual purity codes; correct performance is the rectification
   ! RIDER: India's founding defect is more often IGNORANCE or BONDAGE than MORAL EVIL —
   diagnosis-and-insight rather than sin-and-forgiveness. This changes the SHAPE of the
   resulting religion and is the highest-value comparative sentence in the answer.
                                    |
   PRESUPPOSITION: that religion can be defined FUNCTIONALLY by structure rather than
   SUBSTANTIVELY by its object; and that the sacred/profane binary is genuinely
   cross-cultural.
                                    |
   OBJECTIONS AND REPLIES
   ! the binary is a Durkheimian imposition; many traditions GRADE rather than
     dichotomise; Eliade's universal archaic ontology is unfalsifiable
     REPLY: treat the pair as an IDEAL TYPE that organises comparison, not as a law
   ! founding religion on evil makes religion PARASITIC on suffering, so a world without
     suffering would be a world without religion
     REPLY: worship, wonder and gratitude are INDEPENDENT religious roots; the sacred is
     not exhausted by the negative pole
                                    |
   VERDICT: evil and the profane found religion STRUCTURALLY RATHER THAN DOCTRINALLY —
   they supply the predicament, the boundary and the soteriological task without which
   religious practice would be unintelligible. But they found RELIGION, NOT THEISM, which
   is why the same analysis covers Buddhism and Jainism and leaves the theistic problem of
   evil entirely untouched.
   OWNERSHIP: this file owns the EVIL/PROFANE pole; the SACRED/HOLY-AS-OBJECT pole belongs
   to Religious Experience (2018 Q7(a)). The questions differ: here the category is
   RELIGION-FOUNDING, there it is OBJECT-IDENTIFYING.

   THE UNIVERSAL SPINE — USE AT EVERY MARK LEVEL
   (1) NAME the version: logical | evidential | existential | horrendous | animal |
       distributional
   (2) STATE the set WITH ITS BRIDGES, or the probability judgement with its inductive step
   (3) RECONSTRUCT the reply as NUMBERED PREMISES, correctly attributed
   (4) OBJECT and REPLY, then state the RESIDUE
   (5) COMPARE with one deliberate Indian or Western comparator that does argumentative work
   (6) GRADE: what it explains · what it preserves · what it COSTS · what it leaves untouched

   NEVER END DEVOTIONALLY. "God's purposes are ultimately good" is not a verdict.
   QUALIFIED CONCLUSION: the problem of evil is a stress-test of a PARTICULAR God-concept,
   not of religion as such. Weaken omnipotence, weaken personality, or remove the creator
   and it dissolves — but every dissolution purchases relief with a religiously significant
   loss, and every surviving theodicy is priced. A theodicy is not refuted by its cost;
   it is MEASURED by it, and the answer's final task is to state that price.
