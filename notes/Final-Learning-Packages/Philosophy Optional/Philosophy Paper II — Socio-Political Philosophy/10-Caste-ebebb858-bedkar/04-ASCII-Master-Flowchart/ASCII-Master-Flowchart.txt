THE THREE-STEP REPLY THAT ENDS THE DEFENCE

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/22: Gandhi's argument against untouchability -- five steps
THE DOCTRINE, STATED AS AN ARGUMENT RATHER THAN AS A SENTIMENT
  1. ALL LIFE PARTICIPATES IN A MORAL-SPIRITUAL UNITY, so persons are of
     equal spiritual worth whatever their birth-group.
                 |
                 v
  2. DEGRADING ANOTHER PERSON VIOLATES NON-VIOLENCE (AHIMSA) AND TRUTH.
     Untouchability is therefore not merely unkind; it is a breach of the
     two commitments on which the whole Gandhian ethic rests.
                 |
                 v
  3. INHERITED EXCLUSION CORRUPTS BOTH PARTIES -- the excluded and the caste
     Hindu conscience that practises the exclusion.
                 |
                 v
  4. THEREFORE REFORM MUST INVOLVE REPENTANCE AND CHANGED CONDUCT BY THE
     PRIVILEGED. Responsibility is placed on the dominant, not on the victim.
                 |
                 v
  5. LEGAL PROHIBITION IS NECESSARY BUT CANNOT BY ITSELF GENERATE FELLOWSHIP.
     Law can forbid an act; it cannot manufacture regard.
        |
        v

  +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
  | UNTOUCHABILITY IS A SIN AGAINST HUMANITY AND AGAINST RELIGION       |

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/22: Ambedkar IV -- rights, representation and the argument for power

     the authority of a social majority.
  5. DEMOCRACY MUST BECOME A MODE OF ASSOCIATED LIVING, not only periodic
     voting.

REVERSE DISCRIMINATION -- OBJECTION AT ITS STRONGEST, THEN FIVE REPLIES
  OBJECTION: persons must be treated as individuals; selection should track
  qualification; reservation allocates by ascriptive membership; the excluded
  individual is personally innocent; so a fresh injustice is inflicted using
  the very logic -- allocation by birth-group -- that is being condemned.

       candid about it.
  R2 MERIT AS PROXY  merit is FUNCTIONAL capacity for the role; examination

       nutrition, language and schooling. CONTEST THE MEASURE, NOT THE VALUE.
  R3 ADVANTAGE, NOT GUILT  no culpability is attributed; the claim is that
       some are UNJUSTLY ADVANTAGED by a continuing distribution.

       within the beneficiary group; (ii) the cost falls UNEVENLY, near the
       margin rather than on the most advantaged. NAME BOTH.

       stigma tracks the assumption that the unreserved baseline measured
       worth; representation is claimed as RIGHT AND POWER, not concession.

THE INDIAN RECORD -- STATE THE TYPE, THE DATE AND THE LIMIT OF EACH
  ARTICLE 17 ......... constitutional rule ABOLISHING untouchability and
       forbidding its practice. NOT evidence the practice has disappeared.
  SC/ST (PREVENTION OF ATROCITIES) ACT, 1989, in force 1990 ... an ENACTED
       penal and protective STATUTE.
  MANDAL COMMISSION ... the Second Backward Classes Commission, appointed
       1979, reported 1980. A COMMISSION REPORT RECOMMENDS; IT DOES NOT
       ENACT. Government action on OBC reservation in central services
       followed in 1990.
  INDRA SAWHNEY v. UNION OF INDIA (1992) ... a nine-judge SUPREME COURT
       JUDGMENT: upheld OBC reservation in central services under Article
       16(4); directed EXCLUSION OF THE CREAMY LAYER; held reservation should
       ORDINARILY NOT EXCEED 50 PER CENT save in extraordinary situations.
       ATTRIBUTE NOTHING FURTHER TO IT.

       inserting ARTICLES 15(6) AND 16(6), permitting special provision

       in JANHIT ABHIYAN v. UNION OF INDIA (2022).
