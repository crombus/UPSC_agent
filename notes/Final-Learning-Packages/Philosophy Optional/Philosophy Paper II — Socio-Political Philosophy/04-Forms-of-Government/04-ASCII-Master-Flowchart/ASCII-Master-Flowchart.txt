ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 20/24: Schumpeter and Michels -- the two objections that redefine democracy
SCHUMPETER -- DEMOCRACY AS A METHOD
  REJECTS the CLASSICAL DOCTRINE that democracy realises the WILL OF THE PEOPLE through
  representatives who execute it
  DEFINES democracy as the INSTITUTIONAL ARRANGEMENT in which individuals acquire the power to

  ARGUMENT: 1. the COMMON GOOD is not uniquely determinable | 2. no determinate WILL OF THE
  PEOPLE exists -- aggregate preferences are UNSTABLE and MANUFACTURED | 3. the ordinary
  citizen's grasp of REMOTE issues is WEAK | 4. therefore democracy cannot be defined by the
  CONTENT of the outcome | 5. it can be defined by a METHOD | 6. hence produce, not govern
  STRENGTHS -> EMPIRICALLY USABLE | distinguishes democracy from PLEBISCITARY ACCLAMATION |
  isolates the MINIMUM: an OPEN, UNCERTAIN, REPEATABLE contest
  OBJECTION -> DEFLATIONARY: citizenship becomes PERIODIC CHOICE AMONG ELITES; cannot
  distinguish a DELIBERATIVE election from a WELL-MANAGED one
  REPLY -> his is a MINIMUM, not a CEILING; participation WITHOUT competition can be MOBILISED
  ACCLAMATION. ? RESIDUE -> the minimum treated as the WHOLE definition licenses HOLLOW
  ELECTORALISM (Panel 21)

        v

  THE IRON LAW OF OLIGARCHY: every LARGE organisation -- INCLUDING one founded on explicitly
  DEMOCRATIC and EGALITARIAN aims, such as a socialist party or a trade union -- tends toward
  rule by a SMALL LEADERSHIP
  ARGUMENT: 1. large-scale ORGANISATION is indispensable for mass action | 2. it requires
