ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/19: Austin -- the determinate superior, command and sanction
PROJECT -> ANALYTICAL JURISPRUDENCE: define law and sovereignty with conceptual precision;
  separate what law IS from what law OUGHT TO BE
        |
        v
DEFINITION -> A SOVEREIGN IS A DETERMINATE HUMAN SUPERIOR who
  (1) is IDENTIFIABLE -- not vague or mystical
  (2) receives HABITUAL OBEDIENCE from the BULK of a given society
  (3) habitually OBEYS NO ONE else
  CORRECTION -> the sovereign is NOT "the state" in the abstract. It is a DETERMINATE
  PERSON OR BODY; a legislature or monarch fits better than diffuse democratic opinion.
        |
        v
LAW = COMMAND + SANCTION
  COMMAND -> an expression of DESIRE by a SUPERIOR
  SANCTION -> the EVIL or PAIN attached to NON-COMPLIANCE
  => law is a SPECIES OF IMPERATIVE inside a structure of superiority and obedience
  SEPARATED FROM -> morality, custom, divine law
  BEST FIT -> penal law   WORST FIT -> enabling, permissive and procedural rules
        |
        v
FIVE ATTRIBUTES -- D-A-I-I-I, AND WHY THE LIST IS FORCED
  DETERMINATE ..... otherwise the ORIGIN of validity is unlocatable
  ABSOLUTE ........ otherwise a superior law-maker exists above it
  INDIVISIBLE ..... otherwise two rival origins conflict with no resolution
  ILLIMITABLE ..... otherwise validity is pushed to a further source
  INALIENABLE ..... otherwise finality is transferable and therefore not final
  => each attribute CLOSES A GAP in the chain of legal validity
        |
        v
HONEST CONCESSION -> Austinian indivisibility is STRICTER than empirical political
  reality. It is a LOGICAL feature of the scheme, not an observation about states.
        |
        v
THE INTERNAL DEFECT AUSTIN CANNOT ANSWER -> POWER-CONFERRING RULES.
  Rules for making a valid will, contract or marriage confer FACILITIES; a defective
  will is a NULLITY, and nullity is NOT a sanction. -> doorway to Kelsen and Hart.
TRAP -> calling Austin's sovereign "the state". TRAP -> calling nullity a sanction.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/19: Kelsen -- the hierarchy of norms and the basic norm, 1945
THREE PROBLEMS AUSTIN CANNOT SOLVE FROM INSIDE HIS OWN SYSTEM
  (1) SUCCESSION ..... why is the new sovereign's FIRST command law, before any habit of
                       obedience has formed?
  (2) PERSISTENCE .... why does a centuries-old statute, made by a sovereign long dead and
                       never habitually obeyed by anyone alive, remain valid?
  (3) POWER-CONFERRING ... rules of will, contract and marriage confer FACILITIES, not
                       orders; a defective will is a NULLITY, not a punishment
  => each points the same way: legal authority is carried by RULES, not by HABITS or the
     PERSON of a commander
        |
        v
THE PYRAMID -- VALIDITY DERIVES FROM A HIGHER NORM, NEVER FROM A FACT
        BASIC NORM (GRUNDNORM)  -- presupposed, not enacted
                 | authorises
        CONSTITUTION
                 | authorises
        STATUTE
                 | authorises
        ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER
        |
        v
THE FIVE-STEP ARGUMENT
  1 a norm's validity cannot derive from a FACT -- "is" does not yield "ought"
  2 therefore validity must derive from a FURTHER NORM
  3 an infinite regress would leave nothing valid
  4 therefore the chain TERMINATES in a norm PRESUPPOSED rather than derived
  5 law is a SELF-REFERRING HIERARCHY of validity, distinct from morality AND brute power
PRESUPPOSITION -> a PURE theory of law is possible: describe validity while excluding
  morality, sociology, politics and psychology
PROVENANCE -> General Theory of Law and State, first English edition 1945. NEVER 1961.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/19: Kelsen -- the dissolution of the personal sovereign
EFFECT ON SOVEREIGNTY -> KELSEN DISSOLVES the Austinian sovereign. No determinate human
  superior stands above the law; the "sovereign" is the PERSONIFICATION of the order's
  HIGHEST NORM-CREATING COMPETENCE. Sovereignty belongs to the NORMATIVE ORDER.
        |
        v
OBJECTION -> the Grundnorm is a fiction doing Austin's work less honestly
REPLY -> it is not claimed as a FACT but as a TRANSCENDENTAL-LOGICAL CONDITION of treating
  anything as valid law; it confers VALIDITY, not moral obligation -> still positivist
OBJECTION -> revolution replaces a constitution with no authorising norm
REPLY -> when a new order is BY AND LARGE EFFECTIVE, jurists presuppose a NEW basic norm;
  efficacy is a CONDITION of validity, not its SOURCE
RESIDUAL -> this quietly readmits the fact of power the theory excluded -- CONCEDE IT
PROVENANCE -> General Theory of Law and State, first English edition 1945. NEVER 1961.

  2 Laski is a PLURALIST, not an anarchist
  3 Bodin is NOT lawless -- divine law, natural law, leges imperii
  4 Kautilya is NOT Austinian absolutism -- dharma, welfare, prudence, interdependence
  5 Kautilya is NOT only foreign policy -- saptanga, dandaniti, yogaksema
  6 sovereignty does not "disappear" -- it is RECONFIGURED, constrained, pooled, negotiated
  7 do not confuse LEGAL and POLITICAL sovereignty
  8 Bodin and Austin are NOT identical -- higher-law limits vs formal illimitability
  9 pluralism is NOT mere decentralisation
 10 do not turn examples into fabricated historical detail
 11 Kelsen = General Theory of Law and State, 1945. NEVER 1961.
 12 Hart REJECTS the command model -- nullity is not a sanction
 13 the Grundnorm is NOT a moral standard
 14 do not MERGE Kelsen, Hart and Laski into one critique
 15 do not develop DWORKIN here -- name and route to Individual and State
 16 do not omit PERMANENCE and UNIVERSALITY from the classical attributes
 17 popular sovereignty is NOT national sovereignty
 18 India is NOT parliamentary sovereignty; the judiciary is NOT sovereign
 19 the 2025 printed maxim is PROMPT WORDING, not a verified quotation
