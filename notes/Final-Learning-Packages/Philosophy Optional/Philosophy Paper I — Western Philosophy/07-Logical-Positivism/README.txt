Scope note
----------
The printed clause names three doctrines and one instrument: the
verification principle fixes cognitive meaning, and the rejection of
metaphysics and the linguistic theory of necessary propositions are what
that instrument does when it is turned on metaphysics and on logic. The
clause owns 7 of 112 Western Philosophy question-parts in the 2018-2025
corpus, and 2022 is the only year without one. Popper's falsifiability is
a criterion of scientific demarcation rather than of meaning and he held
metaphysics to be meaningful; Quine's holism, ontological relativity and
the positive case against analyticity belong to Quine and Strawson;
meaning as use, language-games and the private-language argument belong to
Later Wittgenstein; the picture theory and saying and showing belong to
Moore, Russell and Early Wittgenstein and appear here only as a source;
Hume's epistemology, Kant's transcendental machinery and Hick's
eschatological verification are named only as bounded boundary notes.
Wittgenstein was never a member of the Vienna Circle, Ayer attended
meetings in 1932-33 as a visitor, and Popper described himself as the
Circle's official opposition.
