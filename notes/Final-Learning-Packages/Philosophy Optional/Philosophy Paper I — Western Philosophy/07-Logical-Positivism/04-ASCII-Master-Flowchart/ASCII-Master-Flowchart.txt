ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/30: THE CRITERION - A TWO-KEY LOCK
==============================================================================
   COGNITIVELY MEANINGFUL  =  KEY 1 turns  OR  KEY 2 turns
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   KEY 1  ANALYTIC
     wording   true or false SOLELY in virtue of the meanings of its terms
     yields    necessity, apriority, certainty - and ZERO factual content
     examples  'All bachelors are unmarried' | '2 + 2 = 4'
               'All objects are either red or not red'              <-- 2024 Q3(b)
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   KEY 2  EMPIRICALLY VERIFIABLE
     STRONG        conclusively verifiable by a FINITE set of observations
     WEAK          some possible observations are RELEVANT to its truth or falsehood -
                   would render it PROBABLE or IMPROBABLE
     IN PRINCIPLE  we can SPECIFY the observations even if no one can presently make them
     examples  'This page is white'  <-- 2024 Q3(b) | 'There are mountains on the far side
               of the moon' | 'The earth is flat' (FALSE, therefore MEANINGFUL)
   ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   NEITHER KEY TURNS -> PSEUDO-STATEMENT (Scheinsatz)
     'The Absolute is beyond time' | 'The nothing nothings' | 'The soul is immortal'
     'The Good transcends Being' | 'The Soul of Superman is Good'      <-- 2020 Q1(e)
     VERDICT  no truth-conditions, hence NO TRUTH-VALUE
   THE BICONDITIONAL  left-to-right it LICENSES science, logic and common sense;
   right-to-left it EXCLUDES everything else. An 'exposition' must give BOTH halves.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 17/30: REJECTION OF METAPHYSICS - THE IDENTIFICATION PROCEDURE
==============================================================================
   STEP 1  Is the sentence ANALYTIC - true or false by meanings and rules alone?
              YES -> MEANINGFUL and factually EMPTY.   STOP
              NO  -> Step 2
   STEP 2  Can ANY possible observation count FOR or AGAINST it (in principle)?
              YES -> MEANINGFUL, synthetic.            STOP
              NO  -> Step 3
   STEP 3  DIAGNOSE the failure - WHICH ROUTE?
              ROUTE 1  a constituent TERM has NO EMPIRICAL CRITERION OF APPLICATION
              ROUTE 2  individually significant terms are COMBINED so that NO
                       TRUTH-CONDITIONS RESULT
   STEP 4  VERDICT  PSEUDO-STATEMENT (Scheinsatz).
              Record what is NOT claimed: not false; not unproven; not unintelligible;
              expressive and practical force untouched
   SOURCE  CARNAP, 'Uberwindung der Metaphysik durch logische Analyse der Sprache',
   Erkenntnis, 1932.  AYER: a metaphysical sentence 'purports to express a genuine
   proposition, but does not.'

   QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
   Logical positivism proposed a single test to separate what can be said from what only
   looks as if it were said, and the test failed in four different ways - two of them
   discovered by positivists themselves. It survives not as a doctrine but as a discipline:
   the difference-argument as a heuristic of significance, the demand for explicit rules
   rather than obscurity, the permanent separation of criteria of MEANING, of TRUTH and of
   SCIENTIFIC STATUS, and an agenda - confirmation, demarcation, holism, analyticity - that
   is simply the list of problems the Circle could not solve.
