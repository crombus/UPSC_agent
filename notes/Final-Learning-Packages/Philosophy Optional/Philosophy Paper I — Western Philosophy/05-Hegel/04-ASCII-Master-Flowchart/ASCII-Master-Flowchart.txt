ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/55: The dialectical method - the four steps, stated exactly
==============================================================================
STEP 1  ABSTRACT IMMEDIACY (Unmittelbarkeit)
   a determination is taken as self-standing, needing nothing else
   IMMEDIATE = unmediated | ABSTRACT = drawn away from relations
   NOTE: immediacy is a CLAIM ABOUT STANDING, not a kind of object.
   "pure being", "the isolated individual", "the lord's independence",
   "the Stoic's inner freedom" are all immediacies in this sense.
        |
        v
STEP 2  IMMANENT EXAMINATION
   hold the determination to ITS OWN claim; a SPECIFIC insufficiency
   or self-opposition appears - GENERATED by the claim, not imported
        |
        v
STEP 3  DETERMINATE NEGATION (bestimmte Negation)
   negation OF THIS CONTENT -> the successor must answer THE PRECISE
   DEFECT disclosed
   CONTRAST: an INDETERMINATE negation of X yields only "not-X", i.e.
   everything else in the universe -> the sequence would be arbitrary
   and the method worthless
   TEST YOU CAN APPLY TO YOUR OWN SCRIPT: can you say WHY the
   successor had to be THAT one? If not, you have written a triad.
        |
        v
STEP 4  SUBLATION (Aufhebung) - all three senses TOGETHER
   CANCEL   the claim to isolated sufficiency is negated
   PRESERVE the valid determinate content is retained
   RAISE    the content is reorganised in a richer mediated unity
   DROP "cancel"   -> nothing has been criticised
   DROP "raise"    -> two positions merely draw
   DROP "preserve" -> refutation-and-collapse; the cumulative history
                      of philosophy is destroyed
        |
        v
STANDING WARNING: not every passage has a visible three-beat form,
   and the transition must be DEMONSTRATED in each case. Naming
   "contradiction" or drawing a triangle is not yet an argument.

CONTRADICTION (Widerspruch) - THE MARKS-SAFE FORMULATION
   the SELF-OPPOSITION of a FINITE determination ABSOLUTISED beyond
   its proper limits
   NOT the assertion of P and not-P in the SAME RESPECT
   NOT a licence to infer anything from inconsistency (ex falso
   quodlibet)
   OBJECTION (formal logicians): if contradiction is permitted,
      anything follows and the system collapses
   REPLY: Hegel's contradiction is a TENSION OF OPPOSED DETERMINATIONS
      within a concept that demands resolution - dialectical, not
      truth-functional
   RESIDUAL: whether that reply is finally coherent remains DEBATED
   CONTESTED: whether Hegel ALSO commits himself to genuine
      ONTOLOGICAL contradiction

   to what it initially EXCLUDES

   relation is INTERNAL to the term's own determinacy

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/55: Four misreadings of the first triad, blocked - and the honest limit
==============================================================================
X  "Nothing is a kind of Being."
   -> NO. Both are equally indeterminate; neither is a species of
      the other.
X  "First there was Being, then Nothing appeared, then Becoming."
   -> NO. The sequence is LOGICAL, not temporal. There is no before.
      This is not Genesis.
X  "Becoming is a compromise between the two."
   -> NO. It is the TRUTH of their inseparability, and it has genuine
      content of its own.
X  "This proves the whole system."
   -> NO. It EXHIBITS the method.
        |
        v
THE HONEST LIMIT THAT STRONG ANSWERS STATE
   CONTESTED: critics may deny that INDISTINGUISHABILITY establishes
   TRANSITION. From "these two thoughts cannot be told apart" it does
   not obviously follow that either PASSES INTO the other; one might
   instead conclude that the starting point was ill-formed.
   HEGEL'S REPLY: the vanishing of each into the other IS what we
   were trying to think, and BECOMING names it.
   CORRECT EXAM FORMULATION: the triad ILLUSTRATES Hegel's method
   WITHOUT PROVING every later transition. Each later transition
   carries its own burden of demonstration.
   -> saying this protects you from the TRENDELENBURG objection
      landing unanswered
        |
        v
WHY THE PASSAGE IS WORTH USING
   It is the ONLY example where the method can be shown with a
   starting point that assumes NOTHING. Use it whenever a question
   says "explain the dialectical method": ONE WORKED CASE BEATS THREE
   DEFINITIONS.
        |
        v
ANSWER LINE: two blanks that cannot be told apart, and one movement
   that is the difference between them.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/55: Abstract versus concrete universal, and the whole quotation
==============================================================================
THE ABSTRACT UNIVERSAL - UNITY BY OMISSION
   dog + horse + human -> delete the differences -> "ANIMAL"
   a thin residue that is NOWHERE ACTUAL, hovering ABOVE its
   instances
   HEGEL'S CHARGE: a SHADOW. Its unity is real but PURCHASED BY
   OMISSION, and what has been omitted is precisely the determinacy
   that made the instances intelligible.
   -> it cannot EXPLAIN its instances, because everything that
      distinguishes them has been removed from it
        |
        v
THE CONCRETE UNIVERSAL - UNITY BY SELF-DIFFERENTIATION
   ANIMALITY -> particularises itself -> DOG / HORSE / HUMAN
             -> and is ACTUAL only as THIS dog, THIS horse
   RETAINS and ORGANISES its own differentiations WITHIN itself
   the differences are what the universal DOES
        |
        v

   "The True is the Whole. But the Whole is nothing other than the
    essence completing itself through its development."
                        (Phenomenology of Spirit, Preface P20)
   -> "Whole" does NOT mean "everything added together"
   -> it means the ESSENCE COMPLETED THROUGH ITS OWN DEVELOPMENT
   -> therefore the Whole is a RESULT, and truth is not a proposition
      one grasps but a movement one traverses
        |

QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
   Hegel's system is ONE CLAIM WITH TWO FACES: the method IS the
   Absolute's life, so the dialectic that shows how a finite
   determination destroys itself is the same movement by which the
   whole articulates itself, and neither can be assessed without the
   other. ITS PERMANENT ACHIEVEMENTS are the demonstration that a
   determinately known limit belongs within thought, the replacement
   of a fixed appearance/reality dualism by identity-in-difference,
   and the account of development as CUMULATIVE through sublation.
   ITS PERMANENT COSTS are that each transition's necessity is
   visible only RETROSPECTIVELY, that a method proceeding from
   concepts cannot generate the sheer THAT of existence, and that the
   preservation of the particular within the whole is ASSERTED more
   securely than it is DEMONSTRATED.
