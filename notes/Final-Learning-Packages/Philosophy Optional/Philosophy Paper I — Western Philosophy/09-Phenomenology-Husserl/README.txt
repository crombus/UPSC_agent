Scope note
----------
The printed clause names three limbs in a dependency order: the method opens a
field, the theory of essences says what can be found in it that is more than a
private episode, and the avoidance of psychologism is the standing prohibition
that keeps the first two from becoming empirical psychology. Intentionality,
the natural attitude and the reductions are indispensable method-prerequisites;
profiles and horizons, hyle and morphe, time-consciousness and
intersubjectivity are bounded prerequisites that make the routed questions
executable. The clause owns 7 of 112 Western Philosophy question-parts in the
2018-2025 corpus, and 2018 is the only year without one — although that year's
Q4(a) still opens by asking what the epoche is before turning to Heidegger, and
it belongs to Topic 10. Being-in-the-world, Dasein, authenticity and Sartre on
freedom belong to Topic 10 and appear here only as critics of a method they
inherited; methodic doubt, the cogito's proof, the God-proofs and mind-body
dualism belong to Topic 02 and are needed only as the contrast term in the 2025
part; the categories and apperception belong to Topic 04; verification,
pseudo-statements and the Vienna Circle belong to Topic 07 and are a rival that
shares the anti-metaphysical direction while rejecting eidetic intuition
outright; the phenomenology of religious experience in Otto and Scheler belongs
to Paper II. The life-world and the Crisis are bounded enrichment worth one
evaluative paragraph.
