ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 1/30: THE CENTRAL QUESTION AND THE ONE MOVE THIS CLAUSE MAKES
==============================================================================
   CENTRAL QUESTION
   Stop asking WHETHER the world exists. Ask HOW anything comes to have MEANING
   for consciousness at all - and what would count as evidence for the answer.
                                     |
                                     v
   THE ONE MOVE
   The existence-question is NOT answered and NOT denied. It is PUT OUT OF PLAY,
   so that the MODES IN WHICH OBJECTS ARE GIVEN can be described without
   prejudice. Everything else in the clause is the working out of that move.
                                     |

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 7/30: THE NATURAL ATTITUDE AND ITS GENERAL THESIS
==============================================================================
   THE NATURAL ATTITUDE (natuerliche Einstellung)
   the LIVED, PRE-PHILOSOPHICAL stance in which the world is PRE-GIVEN: things
   are THERE, have determinate properties, persist unobserved, and are
   encountered by others. The world is NOT CONCLUDED TO - it is the HORIZON
   within which anything is concluded.
                                     |
                                     v
   ITS BELIEF-CHARACTER: THE GENERAL THESIS (Generalthesis)
   'the world exists, has always existed, and would exist without me'
   ! LIVED, NOT ASSERTED. Nobody walks into a room thinking 'the world exists'.
     The thesis becomes visible ONLY when a philosopher neutralises it.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 9/30: SUSPENSION - WHAT IS BRACKETED, WHAT SURVIVES
==============================================================================
   EPOCHE (Greek epoche, 'suspension', 'withholding of assent') - the SCEPTICS'
   word, with its PURPOSE REVERSED: for the ancient sceptic suspension
   TERMINATES inquiry; for Husserl it OPENS an inquiry the natural attitude had
   blocked.
   BRACKETING (Einklammerung), IDEAS I SECTIONS 31-32: 'put out of play'
                                     |
   +---------------------------------+---------------------------------+
   |  BRACKETED                      |  NOT BRACKETED                  |
   |  * the EXISTENCE-POSIT of the   |  * the STREAM of experience     |
   |    world and of every object    |    with ALL its intentional     |
   |  * the naive validity of the    |    contents                     |
   |    natural sciences             |  * the FULL CONTENT of what     |
   |  * my own psyche AS A WORLDLY   |    appears: colour, shape,      |
   |    THING (empirical psychology) |    distance, side, horizon      |
   |                                 |  * the object's claim to be     |
   |                                 |    THAT KIND of object          |
   +---------------------------------+---------------------------------+
                                     |
                                     v
   THE RESIDUE (Residuum)  the ENTIRE FIELD OF PURE EXPERIENCE as lived,
   phenomena in their SELF-GIVENNESS
   ! WIDER, NOT NARROWER. Before the epoche the description leant constantly on
     assumptions about what the object REALLY is; afterwards it can record only
     what is GIVEN - including the horizonal and perspectival features the
     natural attitude never notices.
   ONE-LINE CORRECTION FOR THE COMMONEST LOOSE FORMULA
   X 'the epoche brackets the world'    OK 'the epoche brackets the world's
                                            EXISTENCE-POSITING'
   THE BRACKET FALLS ON A VALIDITY-CLAIM, NEVER ON A CONTENT.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 12/30: THE THREE PURPOSES - THE 2023 ROUTE
==============================================================================
   THE DEMAND  2023 Q3(b) 15m asks for the SIGNIFICANCE of bracketing and
   reduction. SIGNIFICANCE = WHY, not WHAT. Technique without purpose is the
   classic half-mark answer.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   PURPOSE (i)  SECURE FIRST-PERSON EVIDENCE UNDER A CHANGED ATTITUDE
        the stream is APODICTICALLY UNAVOIDABLE AS EXPERIENCED
        ! QUALIFY AT ONCE: apodicticity is NOT EXHAUSTIVE TRANSPARENCY.
          Particular self-descriptions remain INADEQUATE and CORRIGIBLE -
          exactly as IDEAS I SECTION 24 requires by limiting evidence to what
          is given WITHIN THE LIMITS of its givenness
        WITHOUT IT  there is no evidence-base at all
   PURPOSE (ii)  AVOID THE CARTESIAN SUBSTANCE-INFERENCE
        consciousness is analysed as INTENTIONAL LIFE, not established as a
        separate mental thing
        ! this REFORMULATES the mind-body problem; it does not magically
          dissolve it
        WITHOUT IT  phenomenology is Cartesian dualism with new vocabulary
   PURPOSE (iii)  OPEN THE EIDETIC AND TRANSCENDENTAL QUESTIONS
        suspending factual commitment clarifies STRUCTURES OF GIVENNESS;
        imaginative variation is then a DISTINCT method that need not be
        mechanically sequenced after every reduction
        WITHOUT IT  phenomenology is a diary of one person's experiences -
        which is EMPIRICAL PSYCHOLOGY, the very thing Limb 3 exists to exclude
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ANSWER LINE  Name the three purposes AND what each prevents. That pairing is
   the difference between a 9 and a 12 on a fifteen-mark part.

   * IDEAL       not spatio-temporal, not causally efficacious, and NOT

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 22/30: FREE IMAGINATIVE VARIATION - AND WHY IT IS NOT INDUCTION
==============================================================================
   THE TECHNIQUE, IN FOUR STEPS
   1  BEGIN with a factual instance - a perceived physical object, a melody, a
      judgement
   2  VARY it FREELY IN IMAGINATION - colour, size, material, duration, context
   3  ASK at each step: does the phenomenon SURVIVE the variation, or COLLAPSE
      into something else?
   4  WHAT CANNOT BE VARIED AWAY WITHOUT DESTROYING THE PHENOMENON IS ITS
      ESSENCE
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE WORKED CASE, WITH ITS OWN CAUTION
   change SIZE        -> still a triangle      remove ONE SIDE  -> BREAK
   change COLOUR      -> still a triangle      open a CORNER    -> BREAK
   change MATERIAL    -> still a triangle
   change ORIENTATION -> still a triangle
   THE INVARIANT  a CLOSED THREE-SIDED PLANE FIGURE
   ! THE ANGLE-SUM CLAIM DEPENDS ON THE GEOMETRICAL FRAMEWORK and must NOT be
     presented as the invariant of EVERY POSSIBLE GEOMETRY - a caution that
     simultaneously exhibits the method's OWN LIMITATION
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   WHY IT IS NOT INDUCTION - SAY BOTH HALVES
   INDUCTION generalises from MANY OBSERVED cases and yields PROBABILITY;
   further cases STRENGTHEN it
   VARIATION is an A PRIORI procedure conducted IN IMAGINATION; A SINGLE
   INSTANCE SUFFICES to launch it
   THE REASON  what is sought is a LIMIT, not a FREQUENCY - and NO NUMBER OF
   FURTHER INSTANCES MAKES A LIMIT MORE SECURE
   ! 'not induction' alone is an ASSERTION. The limit/frequency contrast is the
     ARGUMENT.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 26/30: PSYCHOLOGISM - DEFINITION, HOLDERS, REVERSAL
==============================================================================
   PSYCHOLOGISM  the doctrine that the laws of logic - non-contradiction,
   excluded middle, modus ponens, the laws of valid inference - are EMPIRICAL
   GENERALISATIONS ABOUT HOW HUMAN BEINGS IN FACT THINK; that logic is a branch,
   or at least a consequence, of DESCRIPTIVE PSYCHOLOGY.
   REPRESENTATIVES  JOHN STUART MILL | THEODOR LIPPS | CHRISTOPH SIGWART -
   AND, IN HIS EARLIER PHASE, HUSSERL HIMSELF
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ITS THREE CHARACTERISTIC CLAIMS
   1  'the principle of non-contradiction describes how we CANNOT think' - a
      psychological impossibility
   2  'logical laws hold BECAUSE OF THE CONSTITUTION OF THE HUMAN MIND' - they
      would be DIFFERENT for differently constituted beings
   3  'truth is what we are COMPELLED TO BELIEVE' - truth reduces to a
      psychological fact
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE ONE CONFUSION, SHOWN ONCE
   THE ACT                              |  THE CONTENT
   'I judged that 2+2=4 at 3 p.m. on    |  '2+2=4'
   Tuesday, in this brain, while tired' |  not at 3 p.m., not in this brain,
   a REAL, DATABLE psychic event        |  not tired
                                        |  an IDEAL, ATEMPORAL meaning
   PSYCHOLOGISM READS THE RIGHT-HAND COLUMN AS A FEATURE OF THE LEFT-HAND ONE.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE REVERSAL  1891 Philosophie der Arithmetik (number grounded in acts of
   COLLECTING and COUNTING) -> 1894 FREGE'S REVIEW (paraphrase only; the causal
   claim is DISPUTED - Foellesdal yes, Mohanty 1982 no) -> 1900-01 PROLEGOMENA
   TO PURE LOGIC, VOL. I: the demolition
   ! NAMING 1891 CONVERTS 'Husserl opposed psychologism' INTO 'Husserl REVERSED

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 27/30: THE FOUR PROLEGOMENA ARGUMENTS AND IDEAL OBJECTIVITY
==============================================================================
   (i)  NECESSARY vs MERELY PROBABLE
        psychological laws are INDUCTIVE - at best HIGHLY PROBABLE, never
        strictly NECESSARY. Logical laws are NECESSARILY true.
        YOU CANNOT DERIVE THE NECESSARY FROM THE MERELY PROBABLE.
   (ii) CONFUSION OF IDEAL CONTENT AND REAL ACT        <-- THE ROOT CONFUSION
        the CONTENT of '2+2=4' is an ideal, ATEMPORAL MEANING; the ACT of
        judging it is a REAL, DATABLE psychic event. Psychologism treats the
        TIMELESS PROPOSITION as a feature of the TEMPORAL ACT.
        ! THE OTHER THREE ARE THIS ONE, DRAWN IN DIFFERENT DIRECTIONS
   (iii) RELATIVISM AND SELF-REFUTATION               <-- STRONGEST AT 15/20
        if logical laws hold only 'for beings constituted like us', truth is
        SPECIES-RELATIVE (Anthropologismus). But 'truth is relative' is offered
        as ABSOLUTELY TRUE. SELF-CONTRADICTION.
        THE SHARP FORM  if logical laws were empirical generalisations about
        thinking, THE PSYCHOLOGIST'S OWN REASONING WOULD BE ONLY PROBABLE, and
        his conclusion would UNDERMINE ITS OWN SUPPORT
   (iv) CONDITIONS OF THINKING vs GROUNDS OF VALIDITY
        that we MUST think in accordance with non-contradiction is a CAUSAL
        necessity of our psychology; it does not EXPLAIN WHY THE LAW HOLDS,
        which is a question of LOGICAL VALIDITY. The CAUSE of believing is not
        the REASON for truth. -> THE GENETIC FALLACY.
   ===========================================================================
   THE POSITIVE RESULT: IDEAL OBJECTIVITY
   logical and mathematical truths have validity that is NOT IDENTICAL WITH ANY
   DATABLE PSYCHOLOGICAL ACT
   ! AVOID 'SUBSIST' if it suggests a separately located Platonic realm
   PHENOMENOLOGY STUDIES CONSCIOUSNESS AS THE SITE WHERE IDEAL MEANINGS ARE
   GRASPED - WITHOUT REDUCING THE MEANINGS TO THE ACTS THAT GRASP THEM.
   THE ACTS ARE REAL; THE CONTENTS ARE IDEAL.
   -> this DUAL recognition defeats BOTH psychologism (which denied the ideal)
      AND naive Platonism (which denied that the ideal is ACCESSIBLE)
   BOUNDARY CORRECTION  avoiding psychologism does NOT mean EXCLUDING
   CONSCIOUSNESS. Husserl studies intentional acts throughout; he refuses only
   to make LOGICAL VALIDITY DEPEND ON EMPIRICAL MENTAL LAWS.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 28/30: THE TRANSCENDENTAL-TURN WORRY - THE 2020/2022 ROUTE
==============================================================================
   THE CHARGE  after IDEAS (1913) all meaning-constitution is located in the
   TRANSCENDENTAL EGO. If truth is 'constituted in transcendental subjectivity',
   has psychologism RETURNED AT A HIGHER LEVEL?
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   THE THREE-PART REPLY
   1  THE TRANSCENDENTAL EGO IS NOT THE EMPIRICAL PSYCHE
      the psyche is a MUNDANE OBJECT WITHIN the world and is bracketed with it;
      the transcendental ego is the CONDITION under which world and psyche have
      meaning at all. Reducing to it is therefore NOT PSYCHOLOGY.
      ! the objection trades on treating 'subject' UNIVOCALLY
   2  CONSTITUTION IS NOT CREATION OR FABRICATION
      to say the sense of '2+2=4' is constituted in transcendental subjectivity
      is to say its SENSE IS DISCLOSED there - not that the subject MAKES IT UP.
      The ideal content remains ATEMPORAL and OBJECTIVE.
      SUBJECTIVITY IS THE MEDIUM OF ACCESS, NOT THE SOURCE OF VALIDITY.
   3  OBJECTIVITY IS GROUNDED IN TRANSCENDENTAL INTERSUBJECTIVITY
      not in the private preferences of one empirical subject. HOW SUCCESSFULLY
      HE REACHES OTHER EGOS IS THE FIFTH MEDITATION'S PROBLEM (Panel 29)
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   ? WHAT SURVIVES OF THE CHARGE  limb 3 TRANSFERS THE BURDEN. If
   intersubjectivity is not independently secured, limb 3 cannot carry the
   weight limb 2 leaves it - and SCHUTZ'S CIRCULARITY OBJECTION (1957) is
   exactly a challenge to that security.
   VERDICT  PSYCHOLOGISM PROPER IS ESCAPED: nothing in the transcendental
   account makes logical validity depend on how any mind happens to work. WHAT
   IS NOT ESCAPED IS TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM - a DIFFERENT and WEAKER charge.
   The reply is COHERENT BUT NOT DECISIVE; neo-Kantians, the analytic tradition,
   and later HEIDEGGER and MERLEAU-PONTY remain unconvinced.
   ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
   TWO QUESTIONS, ONE DIFFERENCE
   2020 Q1(c) 10m  what is WRONG with psychologism + how the METHOD addresses it
                   -> the four arguments PLUS the method-link
   2022 Q1(d) 10m  what IS psychologism + how Husserl avoids it IN THE DISCOURSE
                   OF TRANSCENDENTAL PHENOMENOLOGY
                   -> the four arguments PLUS this panel. STOPPING AT THE FOUR
                      ARGUMENTS ANSWERS HALF.

   AGREEMENT     both start from the indubitable SELF-GIVENNESS of the thinking
                 act; both seek a PRESUPPOSITIONLESS BEGINNING
                 ! DENYING THIS REMOVES THE AXIS AND THE ANSWER BECOMES TWO
                   PORTRAITS
   DIVERGENCE 1  SUBSTANCE. Descartes concludes to a THINKING SUBSTANCE.
                 Husserl SUSPENDS that mundane ontological inference.
   DIVERGENCE 2  WORLD-ACCESS. Descartes invokes DIVINE NON-DECEPTION to rebuild
                 world-knowledge. Husserl NEVER DENIES the world; he brackets
                 its POSITING.  NO GOD-BRIDGE IS NEEDED BECAUSE NO CHASM WAS
                 OPENED.
   DIVERGENCE 3  SCOPE. Husserl's ego is INTENTIONAL, TEMPORAL and ultimately
                 INTERSUBJECTIVE - not a sealed inner thing.

   THE COST: TRANSCENDENTAL SOLIPSISM. Husserl treats it as the MOST SERIOUS
   objection to his own programme and gives it the LONGEST Meditation.
   ! FIRST, CORRECT THE QUESTION. He is NOT trying to PROVE that other minds
     exist - a natural-attitude demand the epoche has suspended. He asks HOW THE
     SENSE 'another subject, for whom the world is also there' IS CONSTITUTED,
     with the evidence proper to it.   <-- STRONGEST SINGLE SENTENCE AVAILABLE

   THE FIFTH CARTESIAN MEDITATION - NINE STEPS
   1 REDUCTION TO OWNNESS (Eigenheitssphaere): abstract from all sense owing to
     OTHER subjectivity - cultural predicates ('hammer', 'book', 'road') and all
     objectivity-for-anyone. What remains is my PRIMORDIAL SPHERE
   2 MY BODY IS UNIQUE IN IT: the LIVED BODY (Leib) is the only physical thing
     that is also an ORGAN OF WILL, a FIELD OF SENSINGS, and the absolute HERE
     from which all else is THERE. Every other body is a mere KOERPER
   3 ANOTHER BODY APPEARS resembling mine in appearance and, crucially, in
     GOVERNED EXPRESSIVE BEHAVIOUR
   4 PAIRING (Paarung): a PASSIVE SYNTHESIS OF ASSOCIATION couples the two
     bodies - BEFORE any judgement or inference
   5 APPRESENTATION (Appraesentation): the other's subjectivity is CO-INTENDED
     without being itself presented - exactly as the UNSEEN BACK OF A TABLE is
     appresented in seeing its front
     ! AN ANALOGISING TRANSFER OF SENSE, *NOT* AN ARGUMENT FROM ANALOGY.
       HUSSERL EXPLICITLY DENIES THAT IT IS AN INFERENCE
   6 THE 'AS IF I WERE THERE': the other is constituted as an ALTER EGO - an 'I'
     at HIS here, which for me is a THERE. An INTENTIONAL MODIFICATION of my own
   7 NON-ORIGINALITY IS A NECESSITY, NOT A DEFECT: if the other's experiences
     were given to me ORIGINALLY, as my own are, HE WOULD NOT BE AN OTHER - HE
     WOULD BE ME. Inaccessibility belongs to the very SENSE 'other'
   8 VERIFICATION IS HARMONIOUS BEHAVIOUR: confirmed or disappointed by
     CONTINUING CONCORDANCE - the same fulfilment structure as ordinary
     perception
   9 RESULT: the world is constituted as available to PERSPECTIVES OTHER THAN
     MINE -> OBJECTIVITY. 'COMMUNITY OF MONADS' is a LEIBNIZIAN TERM; the
     CONTENT is embodiment, empathy and appresentation
   FOUR PRESUPPOSITIONS  P1 ownness is coherent | P2 my body is originally given
   as LIVED | P3 pairing is genuinely PASSIVE | P4 constitution is ASYMMETRICAL
   PROVENANCE  Paris lectures FEB 1929 -> FRENCH 1931 (Levinas and Peiffer) ->
   GERMAN 1950 (Husserliana I) -> Eng. tr. CAIRNS 1960

   LIFE-WORLD (Lebenswelt) - BOUNDED ENRICHMENT, ONE PARAGRAPH AT MOST
   the PRE-SCIENTIFIC, PRE-THEORETICAL world of everyday lived experience; the
   PRE-GIVEN HORIZON presupposed by scientific idealisation. The sciences
   ABSTRACT from it and substitute IDEALISATIONS (GALILEAN MATHEMATISATION).
   THE CRISIS  science has FORGOTTEN ITS OWN ORIGIN and treats its abstractions
   as 'the real'. SEDIMENTATION names how meanings and methods become inherited
   achievements - which is why the forgetting is STRUCTURAL, not careless.
   ! THE TARGET IS OBJECTIVISM, NOT SCIENCE. No physical result is disputed.

   QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
   Husserl's achievement is a METHOD that makes the constitution of sense a
   describable subject-matter, and a THEORY OF ESSENCES that secures ideal
   validity WITHOUT a separate realm and WITHOUT reduction to psychic events.
   The programme's own limits are equally clear and should be stated rather than
   hidden: COMPLETE PRESUPPOSITIONLESSNESS remains a regulative aspiration;
   the HYLE is absorbed into passive synthesis rather than defended; the
   SELF-CONSTITUTING FLOW is reflexively difficult; TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM is
   avowed rather than avoided; and the OWNNESS-FIRST route to the other remains
   exposed to circularity. PSYCHOLOGISM IS DEFEATED; IDEALISM IS NOT - and that
   distinction, held firmly, is the most defensible verdict on the clause.
