ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/74: Transcendental versus transcendent, and the shape of every argument
==============================================================================
TRANSCENDENTAL  = concerning the a priori CONDITIONS under which objects
                  can be given to us          ........... LEGITIMATE
                  e.g. the transcendental deduction; the transcendental
                  unity of apperception; transcendental exposition
TRANSCENDENT    = purporting to apply concepts BEYOND all possible
                  experience                  ........... ILLEGITIMATE
                  e.g. inferring a soul-substance from the "I think";
                  proving a first cause of the world
TRANSCENDENTAL  = the NATURAL and UNAVOIDABLE semblance that arises when
ILLUSION          reason mistakes a REGULATIVE demand for CONSTITUTIVE
                  knowledge ..... DIAGNOSABLE, NOT CURABLE
                  compare: the moon still looks larger on the horizon
                  after we know why
        |
        v
THE STANDING ARGUMENT FORM - EVERY KANTIAN PROOF IN THIS TOPIC
   1 identify a fact of cognition both parties accept
       there IS mathematics | there IS a distinction between an
       objective event and a merely subjective sequence | my
       representations are all MINE
   2 ask what conditions must hold if that fact is to be possible
   3 show the conditions cannot be supplied by the OBJECTS, since then
     they would be contingent and a posteriori
   4 conclude that they are supplied by the SUBJECT as A PRIORI FORMS
   5 NOTE THE PRICE: the conclusion holds for objects AS THEY CAN
     APPEAR TO US, and for nothing beyond
        |
        v
STEP 5 IS WHERE MARKS ARE WON AND LOST. A transcendental argument buys
   NECESSITY at the cost of SCOPE. An answer that reports the necessity
   and omits the restriction has reported half of Kant.
TRAP: using "transcendental" and "transcendent" as synonyms destroys the
   whole Dialectic, because Kant's diagnosis is precisely the slide from
   the first to the second.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/74: The synthetic a priori showcases, argued not listed
==============================================================================
ARITHMETIC   "7 + 5 = 12"
   SYNTHETIC because the concept of the SUM of 7 and 5 contains only the
     thought of UNITING them; the number twelve is not thought in it and
     must be brought in by counting in PURE INTUITION
   A PRIORI because no experiment could falsify it and no counting
     establishes its NECESSITY; it holds strictly universally
GEOMETRY     "the straight line between two points is the shortest"
   SYNTHETIC because "straight" is a QUALITY and "shortest" a QUANTITY;
     no analysis of the quality yields the quantity
   A PRIORI because it is CONSTRUCTED in pure spatial intuition and
     holds of every object of outer sense
FUNDAMENTAL SCIENCE  "every event has a cause"; "in all change of
   appearances substance persists"
   SYNTHETIC because the concept of an EVENT (something that begins to
     be) does not contain the concept of a determining antecedent
   A PRIORI because it cannot be induced from experience without
     circularity - objective time-order already presupposes it
METAPHYSICS AS ATTEMPTED  "the world has a beginning in time"; "the soul
   is a simple substance"
   AMPLIATIVE, certainly - but HERE THE CLASS FAILS. Metaphysics
   ASPIRES to synthetic a priori status and cannot earn it.
        |
        v
THE GRADING IS THE ARGUMENT: mathematics is the SECURE case; the
   principles of natural science are the case that ANSWERS HUME;
   speculative metaphysics is the case that COLLAPSES.
QUALIFICATION, STATED HONESTLY: non-Euclidean geometries and their
   physical application removed the assumption that ONE Euclidean
   geometry is uniquely valid of experience. "7 + 5 = 12" is untouched;
   "the straight line is the shortest" AS A TRUTH ABOUT PHYSICAL SPACE
   is not. The CLASSIFICATION survives the loss of an example; the
   ARGUMENT FROM GEOMETRY does not survive it intact.
TRAP: offering only "7 + 5 = 12". The physics example is the one that
   engages Hume; give at least one from each domain.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/74: Hume's fork superimposed, and the quid juris question
==============================================================================
HUME'S FORK
   RELATIONS OF IDEAS ...... discoverable by thought alone; denial is
                             self-contradictory; = ANALYTIC A PRIORI
   MATTERS OF FACT ......... contrary always conceivable; established
                             only through cause and effect;
                             = SYNTHETIC A POSTERIORI
   >>> THE FORK HAS NO THIRD TINE <<<
        |
        v
CONSEQUENCE FOR HUME: causal necessity cannot be a relation of ideas
   (its denial is conceivable) and cannot be given in a matter of fact
   (no impression of necessary connexion) -> therefore it is a FELT
   DETERMINATION of the mind, i.e. CUSTOM; and metaphysics, containing
   neither abstract reasoning about quantity nor experimental reasoning
   about fact, must be committed to the flames.
        |
        v
KANT'S RESPONSE IN ONE MOVE: the third box is OCCUPIED, so the FORK IS
   INCOMPLETE. He does not widen the fork to save metaphysics; he shows
   the fork was never exhaustive - and the very class that saves
   mathematics and physics is the class metaphysics CANNOT reach.
        |
        v
THE QUESTION IS quid juris, NOT quid facti
   quid facti  the EXISTENCE of synthetic a priori judgements is the
               DATUM; Kant exhibits instances
   quid juris  BY WHAT RIGHT do we assert a judgement that goes beyond
               its concept and yet claims necessity?
   ANSWER: because the predicate is not extracted from the object but is
   a CONDITION under which alone the object could be given to us.
TRAP: claiming Kant PROVED the existence of the class from first
   principles. He exhibits instances and then asks how they are
   possible.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/74: The change of standpoint, the supreme principle and four denials
==============================================================================
THE HYPOTHESIS (Bxvi-xviii)
   Hitherto it was assumed that all our cognition must conform to
   objects; on that assumption every attempt to establish something
   about objects A PRIORI failed.
   "Let us therefore try whether we may not have better success ... if
   we suppose that OBJECTS MUST CONFORM TO OUR COGNITION."
   RESTRICTING CLAUSE, part of the hypothesis and not a later
   concession: what conforms are objects AS OBJECTS OF POSSIBLE
   EXPERIENCE - appearances. THINGS IN THEMSELVES ARE EXEMPTED.
   PROVENANCE: Kant invokes Copernicus at Bxvi; the LABEL "Copernican
   revolution" is the COMMENTATORS'.
        |
   THE ANALOGY, STATED PRECISELY: Copernicus stopped explaining the
   apparent motion of the heavens by moving the HEAVENS and began
   explaining it by moving the OBSERVER. Kant likewise explains a priori
   knowledge by the CONSTITUTION OF THE KNOWER.
   NOT the analogy: that man is the centre of the universe - Copernicus
   DISPLACED the earth, so the popular reading INVERTS the comparison.
        |
        v
SUPREME PRINCIPLE OF ALL SYNTHETIC JUDGEMENTS (A158/B197)
   THE CONDITIONS OF THE POSSIBILITY OF EXPERIENCE IN GENERAL ARE AT
   THE SAME TIME CONDITIONS OF THE POSSIBILITY OF THE OBJECTS OF
   EXPERIENCE.
   -> anything that must hold if experience is possible must also hold
      of everything that can be an object within that experience
        |
        v
WHAT THE HYPOTHESIS EXPLAINS - THE TEST KANT SETS IT
   1 MATHEMATICS: space as our form of outer intuition makes geometry
     both ampliative and necessary
   2 FUNDAMENTAL SCIENCE: categories as rules of unification make the
     persistence of substance and the causal rule necessary of all
     appearances
   3 THE FAILURE OF METAPHYSICS: forms valid only of what can appear
     explain not only THAT metaphysics fails but WHY it fails in the
     systematic ways catalogued in the Dialectic

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/74: Transcendental Aesthetic - the doctrine and the load-bearing asymmetry
==============================================================================
SPACE  = the a priori FORM OF OUTER SENSE - the subjective condition
         under which alone outer intuition is possible for us
TIME   = the a priori FORM OF INNER SENSE, and thereby of ALL
         appearances whatsoever, since every representation - whether or
         not it has an outer object - belongs as a determination of the
         mind to the inner state
BOTH   = PURE INTUITIONS: singular, immediate representations given
         prior to and as the condition of all particular perceptions
        |
NOT    (a) self-subsistent things existing independently of the mind
           (NEWTON's absolute space and time) - REJECTED
       (b) properties of, or relations among, THINGS IN THEMSELVES
           (LEIBNIZ's relational view) - REJECTED
       (c) empirical concepts abstracted from experience, since we need
           space and time ALREADY IN PLACE to have any experience at all
        |
        v
THE ASYMMETRY THAT DOES ALL THE LATER WORK
   OUTER representations are in SPACE and, being MINE, also in TIME
   INNER representations are in TIME ONLY
   ==> TIME IS THE FORM OF *ALL* REPRESENTATIONS; SPACE IS NOT
   ==> only TIME can supply the mediating SCHEMA (see Schematism panel)
        |
        v
PLAIN TEST OF FORM VERSUS CONTENT
   You can think a space empty of objects; you cannot think space away.
   You can think a stretch of time in which nothing happens; you cannot
   think an experience that is at NO time.
   Whatever can be subtracted is CONTENT. Whatever cannot be subtracted
   is FORM.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/74: The metaphysical exposition - four arguments, two jobs
==============================================================================
ARG 1  NOT DERIVED FROM EXPERIENCE
   To refer sensations to something "OUTSIDE ME", and to represent
   things as in different places "beside one another", the
   representation of space must ALREADY be presupposed. Space is
   therefore PRESUPPOSED BY outer experience, not abstracted from it.
                                                  ==> A PRIORI
ARG 2  NECESSARY
   We can never represent to ourselves that there is no space, though we
   can very well think that NO OBJECTS are encountered in it. Space is
   the condition of the possibility of appearances, not a determination
   dependent on them.                             ==> A PRIORI
ARG 3  SINGULAR, NOT A DISCURSIVE CONCEPT
   We can represent only ONE space; the many "spaces" are LIMITATIONS
   within it. A general concept has particulars falling UNDER it,
   whereas the parts of space are IN it and are given only through
   limitation.                                    ==> INTUITION
ARG 4  GIVEN AS AN INFINITE MAGNITUDE
   Space is represented as an infinite GIVEN magnitude. A concept can
   have infinitely many representations UNDER it, but no concept can
   contain an infinite set of representations WITHIN itself.
                                                  ==> INTUITION
        |
        v
CONCLUSION ASSEMBLED, NOT ASSUMED: space is a PURE INTUITION.
        |
        v
THE PARALLEL EXPOSITION FOR TIME
   simultaneity and succession PRESUPPOSE time and cannot yield it
   time cannot be removed from appearances, though appearances can be
     removed from time
   different times are PARTS of one and the same time
   the original representation of time is given as UNLIMITED
TRAP: listing four arguments without SORTING them. Say which pair
   delivers APRIORITY (1-2) and which delivers INTUITIVITY (3-4), and
   the 2019 answer has a structure.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 17/74: Transcendental exposition, the two rivals and the double result

WHAT A TRANSCENDENTAL EXPOSITION IS
   the explanation of a concept as a PRINCIPLE from which the
   possibility of OTHER synthetic a priori cognitions can be understood.
   It PRESUPPOSES the metaphysical exposition and puts it to work.
        |
   SPACE -> GEOMETRY. Geometry determines the properties of space
     synthetically and yet a priori. Possible only if space is a pure
     intuition in us: figures are CONSTRUCTED in pure intuition, and
     what is demonstrated of the construction holds of every object of
     outer sense, since such objects can be given only under this form.
   TIME  -> ARITHMETIC and the PURE DOCTRINE OF MOTION. The concept of
     alteration, and with it motion as alteration of place, is possible
     only through and in the representation of time; NUMBER is generated
     by successive addition of homogeneous units = time-determination.
        |
        v
PYQ 2019 WORDING CAUTION
   "How does Kant argue for the TRANSCENDENCE of Space and Time?"
   "Transcendence" must NOT be read as space and time existing BEYOND
   experience - that is the TRANSCENDENT error Kant attacks. Read it as
   the A PRIORI and TRANSCENDENTALLY IDEAL status, established by the
   metaphysical and transcendental expositions. Say this in ONE sentence
   and then give the expositions.
        |
        v
THE TWO RIVALS AND WHY BOTH FAIL
   NEWTON  space and time are REAL, SELF-SUBSISTENT containers existing
           even if nothing exists in them
           FAILS: two eternal non-entities that are nevertheless
           conditions of everything real, and no experience could ever
           acquaint us with them
   LEIBNIZ space and time are CONFUSED representations of RELATIONS
           among things in themselves
           FAILS: relations abstracted from things could never be known
           with NECESSITY; geometry would lose apodictic status; the
           difference between spatial and conceptual order would be only
           one of CLARITY

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 19/74: Metaphysical deduction - the table of judgements and the categories
==============================================================================
PREMISE: THE UNDERSTANDING IS A FACULTY OF JUDGING. All acts of the
   understanding reduce to judgements, so the FUNCTIONS OF UNITY IN
   JUDGEMENT exhaust the functions of the understanding.
   NOTE: this premise does enormous work. If the understanding did
   anything OTHER than judge, the table could not be complete.
        |
   IN GENERAL LOGIC the FORM of judgement is considered apart from all
   content. IN TRANSCENDENTAL LOGIC the same form is considered as
   applied to a MANIFOLD GIVEN IN INTUITION. The CATEGORY is the concept
   of the SYNTHESIS of that manifold according to the logical function.
   WORKED EXAMPLE: the HYPOTHETICAL form "if p then q" merely relates
   propositions in logic; applied to a manifold given IN TIME it becomes
   the rule "this state follows that one ACCORDING TO A RULE" = the
   category of CAUSALITY AND DEPENDENCE.
        |
        v
 LOGICAL FUNCTION OF JUDGEMENT      ->  PURE CONCEPT OF THE UNDERSTANDING
 QUANTITY                               QUANTITY
   Universal   "All S are P"        ->    UNITY

   In every subsumption the representation of the object must be
   HOMOGENEOUS with the concept - the empirical concept PLATE and the
   intuited round dish share ROUNDNESS.
   But NO CATEGORY IS LIKE ANY APPEARANCE. Categories are pure,
   intellectual, non-sensible; appearances are sensible, in space and
   time. They are HETEROGENEOUS.
   The DEDUCTION proved that the categories MUST apply. It did NOT
   explain HOW they CAN apply.
THE SOLUTION
   A THIRD THING (ein Drittes), homogeneous with the category on one
   side and with appearance on the other: IN ONE RESPECT INTELLECTUAL,
   IN ANOTHER SENSIBLE. That mediator is the TRANSCENDENTAL SCHEMA, and
   every schema is a TRANSCENDENTAL DETERMINATION OF TIME

   IMAGINATION (produktive Einbildungskraft).
   PRECISION: the schema is NOT a mixture, half intellectual and half
   sensible. Time is homogeneous with the category in ONE RESPECT (a
   priori, universal, rule-governed) and with appearance in ANOTHER (the
   form under which every appearance is given).

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 34/74: First Analogy - permanence, alteration and the conservation clause
==============================================================================
NUMBERED ARGUMENT
  1 All appearances are in time.
  2 Time itself cannot be perceived.
  3 Time has two modes to be determined - SUCCESSION and SIMULTANEITY -
    and both presuppose a SINGLE time in which they are located.
  4 To represent things as being in one and the same time, there must
    be, IN THE APPEARANCES THEMSELVES, something that stands PROXY for
    time's own permanence, since time does not change and is not itself
    an object.
  5 That something is SUBSTANCE: the persisting real (das Beharrliche)
    of which all change is mere alteration of determinations.
  6 Therefore coming-to-be and passing-away are NOT the arising and
    perishing of SUBSTANCE but the ALTERATION (Veranderung) of the
    STATES of a persisting substance. WHAT CHANGES ARE ACCIDENTS;
    SUBSTANCE DOES NOT CHANGE, IT *HAS* CHANGING STATES.
  7 Therefore "in all change of appearances substance persists, and its
    quantum in nature is neither increased nor diminished" (B224).
        |
        v
WHY THIS DEFEATS HUME WITHOUT CONTRADICTING HIM
   Hume was RIGHT that there is no IMPRESSION of substance, and Kant
   AGREES ENTIRELY. Kant's move is TRANSCENDENTAL: substance is not
   something perceived IN experience but a CONDITION of perceiving
   anything as located in ONE TIME. The empiricist demand - "show me the
   impression" - is therefore THE WRONG DEMAND, because what is at stake
   is not a content but a condition.
PRESUPPOSITIONS
   P1 time is unitary and unperceivable
   P2 time-determination must proceed through a PERCEPTIBLE PROXY
   P3 "alteration" is intelligible only against a PERSISTING BEARER
STRONGEST OBJECTION
   The argument may prove only that SOMETHING OR OTHER must be treated
   as RELATIVELY persisting for the purpose of time-determination - not
   that there is a sempiternal, quantitatively conserved substance.
   Kant's conservation clause ("its quantum neither increased nor
   diminished") looks like NEWTONIAN PHYSICS smuggled into
   transcendental philosophy.

THIRD ANALOGY - COMMUNITY / RECIPROCITY
   Simultaneity, like succession, cannot be read off unperceivable time.
   If A and B are simultaneous I can perceive them in either order -
   moon then earth, or earth then moon - but I can also perceive a
   NON-simultaneous pair in either order unless something FIXES them in
   one time.

   are simultaneous only insofar as they stand in THOROUGHGOING
   RECIPROCAL DETERMINATION. This is the schema of the category of
   COMMUNITY.
   USE: the least examined and the most useful for showing COMPLETENESS
   - it demonstrates that the three Analogies EXHAUST the modes of time

WHY THE REFUTATION OF IDEALISM EXISTS
   1781: the GARVE-FEDER review (Gottingen) presents the Critique as a
   version of Berkeley's idealism in which bodies are reduced to
   representations. Kant regards this as a complete INVERSION of his
   position, since he holds that objects in space are EMPIRICALLY REAL.

THESIS (B275): "THE MERE, BUT EMPIRICALLY DETERMINED, CONSCIOUSNESS OF
   MY OWN EXISTENCE PROVES THE EXISTENCE OF OBJECTS IN SPACE OUTSIDE ME."
        |
THE PROOF, NUMBERED
  1 I am conscious of my own existence AS DETERMINED IN TIME - I know
    that this inner state came AFTER that one; inner experience has a
    determinate order. DESCARTES MUST GRANT THIS: his own cogito is a
    DATED episode among others.
  2 All determination of position in time presupposes SOMETHING
    PERSISTENT in perception. [= the FIRST ANALOGY; the two sections are
    a single argument.]
  3 This persistent CANNOT be an intuition IN ME: all my inner
    determinations are exactly what is to be DETERMINED by it; the flux
    of representations cannot supply the fixed point against which the
    flux is measured. Hume's bundle is not a candidate; nor is
    Descartes' res cogitans, since I do not INTUIT a persisting soul, I
    only THINK one.
  4 Therefore the persistent must be something OUTSIDE ME and DISTINCT
    FROM MY REPRESENTATIONS - an actual thing in space.
  5 Therefore consciousness of my own existence in time is AT THE SAME
    TIME immediate consciousness of the existence of things outside me.
    OUTER EXPERIENCE IS NOT INFERRED FROM INNER EXPERIENCE; IT IS A
    CONDITION OF IT.
  6 COROLLARY (B278-9, Note 2): "INNER EXPERIENCE IN GENERAL IS POSSIBLE
    ONLY THROUGH OUTER EXPERIENCE IN GENERAL." Descartes' order of
    certainty is EXACTLY INVERTED.

   P2 I have DETERMINATE, not merely felt, temporal knowledge of my own
      states
   P3 NOTHING INNER can serve as the persistent  <== THIS STEP DOES ALL
      THE WORK, and it is the step an examiner will probe

FOUR TERMINOLOGICAL CAUTIONS THAT PROTECT MARKS
  1 THING IN ITSELF (Ding an sich) = an object considered independently
    of our sensible conditions.
  2 NEGATIVE NOUMENON = something considered merely as NOT an object of

THE AFFECTION PROBLEM - JACOBI'S DILEMMA, STATED HONESTLY
   If the categories, ESPECIALLY CAUSALITY, apply only to phenomena,
   Kant cannot straightforwardly say that things in themselves CAUSE our
   sensations. He needs RECEPTIVITY without theoretically describing its
   noumenal source.
   JACOBI: "without the assumption of things in themselves I cannot
   enter the system; with it I cannot remain."
   DEFENSIBLE POSITION, THREEFOLD: (i) the tension is GENUINE and should
   be stated; (ii) Kant's own language is more careful than his critics
   allow - he speaks of the object that AFFECTS sensibility without
   claiming theoretical cognition of a noumenal cause; (iii) on the
   two-aspect reading the pressure is reduced, since affection is the
   receptive aspect of ONE cognitive relation rather than a transaction
   between worlds.
   NOT DEFENSIBLE: writing that "the noumenon is a thing behind
   appearances that causes them" AS THOUGH IT WERE KANT'S OWN DOCTRINE.
   This is THE SINGLE MOST COMMON KANT ERROR IN SCRIPTS.

   The unknowable in-itself looks like an EMPTY ABSTRACTION produced by
   FIXING a permanent opposition between appearance and reality. Hegel
   seeks to overcome that fixed boundary through the IMMANENT
   DEVELOPMENT of thought's determinations - NOT by claiming ordinary
   inspection of a hidden object.

TRANSCENDENTAL ILLUSION - WHAT KIND OF ERROR THIS IS
   LOGICAL illusion arises from carelessness and VANISHES once the
   fallacy is pointed out.
   TRANSCENDENTAL illusion does NOT vanish. It is grounded in the nature
   of REASON ITSELF. Even after we understand exactly why the inference
   fails, the pull remains - as the moon still looks larger on the
   horizon to someone who knows the explanation.
   TWO CONSEQUENCES: (i) Kant is not saying metaphysicians were stupid;
   the error is STRUCTURAL. (ii) Critique is PERMANENT HOUSEKEEPING, not
   a one-off cure: reason must keep restraining itself.

   The UNDERSTANDING unifies appearances under rules; REASON (Vernunft)
   unifies the rules of the understanding under principles. Its supreme
   principle: IF THE CONDITIONED IS GIVEN, THEN THE ENTIRE SERIES OF

THE FOUR PARALOGISMS - rational psychology's inferences from the bare
   "I think"
   1 SUBSTANTIALITY .. the soul is a SUBSTANCE
   2 SIMPLICITY ...... the soul is SIMPLE, hence immaterial
   3 PERSONALITY ..... the soul is NUMERICALLY IDENTICAL over time
                       (personal identity)
   4 IDEALITY ........ the soul's existence is distinct from that of
                       bodies in space
   NOTE: the FOURTH Paralogism IN ITS ORIGINAL FORM is A-ONLY; in 1787
   it is replaced and its work is done by the Refutation of Idealism.
        |
KANT'S REPLY: all four commit the fallacy of EQUIVOCATION
   (Paralogismus) - a FORMALLY FALLACIOUS SYLLOGISM turning on a middle
   term that SHIFTS ITS SENSE between the "I" as the LOGICAL SUBJECT OF
   THOUGHT (a formal condition) and the "I" as a REAL OBJECT GIVEN IN
   INTUITION (a substance).
   WORKED CASE (the first, which generalises): "that which cannot be
   thought otherwise than as subject does not exist otherwise than as
   subject, and is therefore substance." The PREMISE is a truth about
   the FORM OF THOUGHT; the CONCLUSION is a claim about EXISTENCE. The
   "I think" is the VEHICLE of all concepts, and the vehicle cannot be
   one of the items carried.
   AGAINST HUME AND DESCARTES AT ONCE: Hume looked for the self among
   impressions and found none - Kant AGREES, since the "I think" is a
   condition, not a content. Descartes' rational psychology converted
   the same "I think" into a SUBSTANCE - Kant REFUSES, since a formal
   condition is not a real object. The Kantian self is neither a BUNDLE
   nor a SUBSTANCE but a FUNCTION.
   WHAT SURVIVES: the UNITY of apperception is fully real and
   indispensable; what fails is the INFERENCE from it to a KNOWABLE
   SOUL. And I know myself only as I APPEAR to myself through inner
   sense.

WHEN DOES PURE REASON ENTER THE REALM OF ANTINOMIES?  [PYQ 2020 Q3(c)]
   Pure reason enters antinomy when its LEGITIMATE search for conditions
   demands an ABSOLUTELY UNCONDITIONED, COMPLETED world-series and then
   treats that totality AS AN OBJECT IN ITSELF.
   THE ANTINOMIES ARE THE CONSEQUENCE OF *IGNORING* THE PHENOMENON /
   NOUMENON BOUNDARY - NOT A CONSEQUENCE OF MAINTAINING IT CORRECTLY.

HOW THEY ARE CONSTRUCTED - FOUR FEATURES, ALL FOUR MUST BE NAMED
  1 A COSMOLOGICAL IDEA IS SELECTED. Each antinomy corresponds to one
    category-group applied to the world-series: QUANTITY (magnitude in
    time and space), QUALITY (composition), RELATION (causality),
    MODALITY (necessary existence).
  2 BOTH SIDES ARE PROVED INDIRECTLY. Each proof is a REDUCTIO AD
    ABSURDUM of its opposite. Kant gives both proofs their FULL FORCE;
    he does not caricature either.
  3 BOTH PROOFS SHARE A PREMISE: that the world is a GIVEN WHOLE - a
    completed totality of which one of the two contradictory predicates
    must hold.
  4 THE CONFLICT IS EXHIBITED AS NECESSARY, NOT ACCIDENTAL. Given the
    shared premise, BOTH conclusions follow. That is what makes the
    antinomy an argument AGAINST THE PREMISE rather than against either
    party.

    headings in the Antinomy chapter - safe to use here, unlike the
    triad often misattributed to Hegel.

THE TWO RESOLUTIONS IN KANT'S OWN LOGIC
  MATHEMATICAL (1 & 2): the error is treating an empirical series that
    is always IN PROGRESS as if it were a FINISHED totality. Since the
    world-whole is never an object of possible experience, calling it
    finite or infinite is EQUALLY SENSELESS. Both sides err by treating
    phenomena as noumena. The regress is INDEFINITE - you may always go
    further, and there is no completed answer to go to.
  DYNAMICAL (3 & 4):
    THIRD ANTINOMY (FREEDOM). In the phenomenal world every event is
      determined by prior causes - THE ANTITHESIS HOLDS FOR PHENOMENA,
      and Kant does not weaken it. But the SAME AGENT, considered as a
      NOUMENON, may possess FREEDOM - a spontaneous causality not
      subject to temporal determination. Kant thus makes ROOM for moral
      freedom alongside natural determinism.
      CAUTION: ROOM, NOT PROOF. Theoretical reason establishes only that
      freedom is THINKABLE WITHOUT CONTRADICTION. "Assigned from a
      practical or intelligible standpoint, not theoretically cognised."
    FOURTH ANTINOMY (NECESSARY BEING). No absolutely necessary being
      need occur AS A MEMBER of the phenomenal series. An intelligible
      ground OUTSIDE that series is not thereby contradicted - but the
      antinomy supplies NO theoretical proof that such a being exists.
TRAP: giving ONE resolution for all four is the commonest structural
   error at 20 marks. Mathematical: BOTH FALSE. Dynamical: BOTH MAY BE
   TRUE. And say WHY - homogeneous versus heterogeneous series.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 53/74: Significance of the antinomies, and the limit of the indirect proof
==============================================================================
  1 THEY DEMONSTRATE THAT DOGMATIC SPECULATIVE METAPHYSICS OF THE
    SUPERSENSIBLE CANNOT BE A SCIENCE. Pure reason generates OPPOSED
    conclusions when it treats the world-totality as a knowable object.
    Kant's own critical inquiry into reason's conditions and limits
    NEVERTHELESS REMAINS POSSIBLE - the antinomies do not indict reason
    as such.
  2 THEY MOTIVATE THE CRITICAL TURN. Knowledge must be confined to
    phenomena; but PRACTICAL reason can legitimately POSTULATE freedom,
    God and immortality as conditions of the moral life.
  3 THEY ARE KANT'S MOST DRAMATIC ARGUMENT against pre-critical
    rationalist COSMOLOGY and unrestricted speculative reason.
  4 THE INDIRECT PROOF OF TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM. If REALISM ABOUT
    APPEARANCES GENERATES the contradictions and TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM
    DISSOLVES them, then idealism is VINDICATED BY ITS CONSEQUENCES.
    A doctrine argued ANALYTICALLY in the Aesthetic and Analytic is here
    vindicated SYNTHETICALLY, by its explanatory yield.
    LIMIT OF THAT PROOF: it succeeds ONLY IF THERE IS NO THIRD
    DIAGNOSIS - and HEGEL SUPPLIES ONE, namely that reason's
    contradictions are PRODUCTIVE rather than PATHOLOGICAL. State this
    limit; it is what converts a strong answer into a distinguished one.
        |
        v
THE 2020 SECOND LIMB, RESOLVED IN TWO REASONS
  REASON 1  The phenomena/noumena distinction is the CURE. Once
    appearances are not things in themselves, the world-series is given
    only as a regress that may always be continued, so the MATHEMATICAL
    antinomies dissolve with both sides false.
  REASON 2  The distinction is what PERMITS the DYNAMICAL resolution:
    natural determination holds of appearances while spontaneous
    causality remains thinkable of the same agent considered as
    noumenon.
  VERDICT: reason enters antinomy by HYPOSTATISING A REGULATIVE DEMAND;
    Kant's dualism RESOLVES the conflict rather than producing it.
TRAP: "the antinomies show that reason is unreliable." NOT QUITE. They
   show that reason, WHEN APPLIED BEYOND EXPERIENCE, generates
   contradictions; within its proper domain (phenomena) reason is
   perfectly reliable.

GENERAL FRAMEWORK
   God (the ens realissimum) lies BEYOND possible experience -> the
   categories (existence, causality) have objective employment only
   through their SCHEMATA and hence only WITHIN experience -> ALL
   THEORETICAL PROOFS FAIL.
   EXHAUSTIVENESS CLAIM - itself an argument: there are only THREE
   possible types of speculative proof. Either one begins from the MERE
   CONCEPT (ontological), or from the EXISTENCE OF SOMETHING
   INDETERMINATE (cosmological), or from the DETERMINATE CONSTITUTION OF
   THE WORLD as revealed in experience (physico-theological). No fourth
   route is available. THIS IS WHAT LICENSES ONE REFUTATION FOR ALL
   THREE.

    FIXED appearance/reality opposition.

2 WHY EXACTLY TWELVE CATEGORIES? The derivation from the table of
    judgements is ARTIFICIAL. (Fichte, Hegel, later Kantians)
    REPLY: the table of judgements is claimed EXHAUSTIVE and SYSTEMATIC,
      and no more complete rival was offered.
    RESIDUAL: the completeness claim depends on the logic Kant
      inherited. DETACH STRATEGY FROM INVENTORY - as Strawson does in
      his austere reading - and the Deduction's core result stands:
      OBJECTIVITY IS A CONDITION, NOT A DISCOVERY.
3 TRANSCENDENTAL IDEALISM COLLAPSES INTO BERKELEY'S SUBJECTIVE
    IDEALISM. (Garve, Feder, 1781)
    REPLY: Berkeley DENIES the existence of external objects; Kant
      AFFIRMS it - objects are EMPIRICALLY REAL and only
      TRANSCENDENTALLY IDEAL. The REFUTATION OF IDEALISM (B274) is
      Kant's explicit answer, and the Aesthetic answers the premise.
    RESIDUAL: none serious; this criticism is DECISIVELY answered.
4 THE AFFECTION PROBLEM. If we cannot know things in themselves, how do
    we know they EXIST or CAUSE our sensations? (JACOBI: "without the
    assumption of things in themselves I cannot enter the system; with
    it I cannot remain.")
    REPLY: Kant speaks of what AFFECTS sensibility without claiming
      theoretical cognition of a noumenal cause.
    RESIDUAL: a GENUINE TENSION; the two-aspect reading REDUCES but does
      not DISSOLVE it.

    PROBLEMATIC AS DESCARTES'. (Hegel, post-Kantians)
    REPLY: ONE WORLD UNDER TWO ASPECTS - how things appear to us versus

    RESIDUAL: the textual dispute between two-world and two-aspect

  10  Kant's third class is a genuine DISCOVERY, not a compromise: it
      names judgements that are INFORMATIVE YET NECESSARY, which Hume's
      fork had declared impossible.
  15  ...its cost is that its EXAMPLES are hostage to science -
      non-Euclidean geometry and relativity removed two showcase cases;
      "7+5=12" survives, "the straight line is the shortest" does not.
  20  The doctrine SURVIVES ITS EXAMPLES. What Kant identified was not a
      stock of privileged truths but a KIND of claim - presuppositional,
      revisable in content, unavoidable in form. Read that way, later
      attacks on the analytic/synthetic distinction damage Kant's
      CLASSIFICATION while confirming his real point, that experience
      rests on commitments not themselves given in experience.
SPACE AND TIME
  10  Neither things (Newton) nor relations among things (Leibniz) but
      FORMS OF INTUITION - the mind's contribution to the possibility of
      appearing.
  15  ...the Transcendental Exposition ties this to the apodictic status
      of geometry; when geometry ceased to be uniquely Euclidean the
      ARGUMENT FROM GEOMETRY lapsed - the Metaphysical Exposition
      (space as a GIVEN SINGULAR WHOLE, not a general concept) does not.

      Newton-Leibniz debate is unanswerable because both sides treat
      space as something ENCOUNTERED. Once space is the FORM of
      encounter, the antinomy DISSOLVES - which is precisely the pattern
      repeated for the world-whole in the Dialectic.

ANALOGIES OF EXPERIENCE
  10  The Analogies are the SCHEMATISED CATEGORIES OF RELATION doing
      work: substance secures duration, causality secures succession,
      community secures simultaneity - all because TIME ITSELF CANNOT BE
      PERCEIVED.
  15  ...the Second Analogy answers Hume at the level of ENTITLEMENT to
      the concept of cause, not at the level of knowledge of PARTICULAR
      causal laws; Strawson's "non-sequitur" objection shows the gap
      between ORDER-NECESSITY and CAUSAL NECESSITATION.
  20  The Analogies are Kant's TRUE REPLY TO EMPIRICISM, and the part of
      the Critique most nearly INDEPENDENT of transcendental idealism -
      which is why Strawson could rebuild them without the doctrine of
      appearances. Their permanent result is that OBJECTIVITY IS
      RULE-GOVERNED TIME-DETERMINATION, and that anyone who accepts the
      objective/subjective distinction in temporal order has already
      accepted more than Hume allowed.
REFUTATION OF IDEALISM
  10  Kant INVERTS Descartes: outer experience is not inferred from
      inner experience but is its CONDITION.
  15  ...the proof delivers only EMPIRICALLY external objects, and
      Strawson and Guyer argue it establishes at most a need to
      REPRESENT a persistent - Kant's own footnoted revision (Bxxxix n.)
      suggests he felt the strain.
  20  The Refutation is the moment transcendental idealism turns out to
      be an EMPIRICAL REALISM. Its lasting force is STRUCTURAL: the
      sceptic's own starting point - determinate knowledge of his inner
      life - is shown to PRESUPPOSE what he doubts. Wittgenstein's
      private-language argument is the same manoeuvre without the
      machinery, which is the best evidence that Kant located the right
      pressure point.
ANTINOMIES
  10  The antinomies are NOT contradictions in reason but the SYMPTOM OF
      A FALSE SHARED ASSUMPTION - that the world is a given totality.
  15  ...the MATHEMATICAL antinomies are resolved by declaring BOTH
      sides FALSE (the world-whole is not an object); the DYNAMICAL by
      allowing BOTH to be true IN DIFFERENT RESPECTS - freedom
      noumenally, causality phenomenally.

  20  The Antinomies are the INDIRECT PROOF of transcendental idealism:
      if realism about appearances generated contradictions and idealism
      dissolves them, idealism is vindicated by its consequences. The
      move is POWERFUL BUT NOT DECISIVE - it succeeds only if there is
      no THIRD diagnosis, and Hegel supplies one, namely that reason's
      contradictions are PRODUCTIVE rather than pathological.
GOD-PROOFS
  10  Kant does not DISPROVE God; he disproves the PROVABILITY of God by
      theoretical reason, and then reinstates God as a POSTULATE OF
      PRACTICAL REASON.
  15  ...the core is "being is not a real predicate" - adding existence
      adds no content, so no analysis of a concept can yield existence;
      and since the cosmological and physico-theological proofs both
      terminate in the ontological, ONE REFUTATION KILLS ALL THREE.
  20  Kant's critique clears theoretical metaphysics PRECISELY IN ORDER
      to make room for faith ("I had to deny knowledge in order to make
      room for belief", Bxxx). The result is not atheism but the
      RELOCATION of God from an object of proof to a condition of the
      moral life - a move which secures religion from Hume's scepticism
      at the price of conceding that theology can never be a science.

QUALIFIED CONCLUSION - THE ONE PARAGRAPH THAT FITS EVERY KANT ANSWER
  Kant's critical philosophy is a single argument with two faces: the
  same demonstration that secures mathematics and the fundamental
  principles of natural science as SYNTHETIC A PRIORI cognition also
  forbids theoretical knowledge of soul, world-totality and God, because
  the forms that make necessity possible reach only what can APPEAR to
  us. Its permanent achievements are the relocation of necessity from
  objects and habits to the CONDITIONS OF OBJECTHOOD-FOR-US, and the
  demonstration that objectivity is RULE-GOVERNED TIME-DETERMINATION.
  Its permanent costs are equally clear: the completeness of the table
  of categories is inherited rather than proved; the affection of
  sensibility by things in themselves cannot be described in the terms
  the system allows; and the boundary between appearance and reality can
  be STATED more securely than it can be EXPLAINED - which is why Hegel
  could plausibly claim that Kant's greatest insight was betrayed by
  Kant's own limits.
