Scope note
----------
The printed clause is entirely theoretical. Kantian ethics as a doctrine —
the good will, duty, the categorical imperative, autonomy and the kingdom of
ends — is not owner content, and no 2018-2025 PYQ routes an ethics question
here. The only practical material admitted is the bounded postulate of God,
freedom and immortality that follows the failure of the speculative proofs.
