ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/43: The wax argument: the eight numbered steps
================================================================================
1  TAKE THE CLEAREST POSSIBLE CASE OF A BODY - wax fresh from the honeycomb
      SENSIBLE CATALOGUE: taste of honey | scent of flowers | a colour |
      a shape | a size | hardness | coldness | a sound when rapped
        |
2  MOVE IT TO THE FIRE -> EVERY item in that catalogue is destroyed or
   replaced: taste and scent vanish, colour alters, shape and size are lost,
   it becomes liquid and hot, and it no longer sounds
        |
3  YET I JUDGE, WITHOUT THE LEAST HESITATION, THAT THE SAME WAX REMAINS
        |
4  THEREFORE what I take the wax to BE is not identical with any item at
   step 1, since all of them were replaced while the wax persisted
        |
5  NOR IS IT DELIVERED BY IMAGINATION: heat can give the wax an INDEFINITE
   number of shapes and sizes, and I cannot run through indefinitely many
   images.  THIS IS THE FORCE OF flexibile et mutabile ("flexible and
   changeable").
        |
6  THEREFORE the wax is grasped as an EXTENDED, FLEXIBLE, CHANGEABLE thing by
   the INTELLECT ALONE - by inspectio mentis, rendered "purely mental
   scrutiny" in the Cottingham-Stoothoff-Murdoch translation and "inspection
   of the mind" in Haldane-Ross
        |
7  FURTHER: at every stage - judging the wax present, doubting it, being
   deceived about it - I perceive MYSELF judging. Each perception of a body is
   ipso facto a perception of the mind that perceives, and a MORE CERTAIN one,
   because it survives even if the body does not exist
        |
8  THEREFORE (a) BODIES ARE KNOWN BY UNDERSTANDING, NOT BY SENSE
             (b) THE MIND IS notior - MORE EVIDENTLY KNOWN - THAN ANY BODY

PAIRED CASE IN THE SAME MEDITATION: "HATS AND COATS". From a window I say I

PRINTED SUB-TITLE OF MEDITATION II: "The nature of the human mind, and how it

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 14/43: The three proofs of God and their division of labour
================================================================================
PROOF 1 - TRADEMARK / CAUSAL  (Meditation III)
   1 I have an idea of God: an infinite, perfect being.
   2 The cause of an idea must have at least as much FORMAL reality as the
     idea has OBJECTIVE reality.
   3 I, finite and imperfect, cannot be the adequate cause of an idea of an
     infinite perfect being.
   4 THEREFORE God exists as its cause - He "stamped" the idea on my mind as
     a craftsman stamps his mark on his work.
   ANSWERS: where could this idea have come from?

PROOF 2 - CONSERVATION / COSMOLOGICAL VARIANT  (Meditation III)
   I exist now; nothing in me gives me the power to sustain myself from one
   moment to the next; the cause of my continued existence must therefore
   ultimately be God.
   ANSWERS: what keeps me in existence?

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 16/43: The Cartesian Circle: objection, reply and residual
================================================================================
THE OBJECTION  (ARNAULD, FOURTH OBJECTIONS)
   Descartes uses CLEAR AND DISTINCT PERCEPTION to prove that GOD exists;
   he then uses GOD'S VERACITY to guarantee that CLEAR AND DISTINCT
   PERCEPTION is reliable.  The guarantee therefore presupposes what it is
   meant to establish.

        criterion  ------ proves ------>  God
             ^                              |
             |                              |
             +-------- certifies -----------+

THE PICTURE IN ONE LINE: Descartes builds a bridge out of God, and the planks
   were cut from the far island he has not yet reached.

THE REPLY - TWO SITUATIONS MUST BE DISTINGUISHED
   (i)  A clear and distinct perception ACTUALLY BEFORE THE MIND compels
        assent and needs no external certificate. Some planks are solid enough
        to stand on while you are looking at them.
   (ii) What needs certifying is the perception merely REMEMBERED as having
        been clear, and any EXTENDED CHAIN of reasoning whose earlier steps
        are no longer in view.
   GOD SECURES CLASS (ii), NOT CLASS (i).

THE RESIDUAL - SAY THIS, OR THE ANSWER IS INCOMPLETE
   The proofs of God are NOT single-flash intuitions. They are arguments with
   premises, and by the time the conclusion is reached the earlier premises
   are being REMEMBERED rather than attended to. So the memory reply SOFTENS
   the objection without eliminating it.

COMPETING READINGS (optional depth, one clause at most)
   vicious-circle reading (Arnauld's line) | memory-guarantee reading |
   stronger anti-circle reading on which the cogito and simple intuitions are
   self-certifying while attended to.

CONSEQUENCE FOR EVERY LATER PANEL: whatever divine veracity underwrites -
   mathematics, memory, the external world, the conservation of created
   substances - inherits this vulnerability.
