Scope note
----------
The printed clause names three limbs and one method: meaning is investigated
through use, uses are organised as language-games embedded in forms of life,
and the critique of private language is what that investigation does when it
is turned on the inner life. Four supports make the printed limbs executable
and are therefore taught in full: ostensive definition and the prepared
place, family resemblance, rule-following, and the therapeutic conception of
philosophy. The clause owns 7 of 112 Western Philosophy question-parts in the
2018-2025 corpus, and 2021 is the only year without one - that year's
Wittgenstein part, 2021 Q4(c) on "Whereof one cannot speak", belongs to Topic
06. The picture theory, logical atomism, saying and showing appear here only
as the position being revised and belong to Topic 06; verification,
pseudo-statements and the Vienna Circle belong to Topic 07 and are a rival
rather than an ally, since the objection to one essence applies equally to
one criterion; Quine's holism and Strawson's persons belong to Topic 11;
fideist and religious-language uses of "form of life" belong to Paper II;
Ryle, Austin and speech-act theory retain their own ownership. Aspect-seeing
and On Certainty are bounded enrichment with an explicit mark budget.
