     70  'But then the use of the word is unregulated!' - it is not EVERYWHERE

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 15/33: FOUR DENIALS, THE STANDARD OBJECTION, AND THE SERVICE RENDERED
==============================================================================
   FOUR THINGS THE DOCTRINE DOES NOT CLAIM - each is a marked error
     X that NO concept has necessary and sufficient conditions ('triangle' does)
     X that no two games have anything in common (many share several features)
     X that definition is impossible or worthless
     X that ANYTHING may therefore be called a game
   THE OBJECTION  Everything resembles everything in SOME respect: a chair
   resembles chess in being man-made. A network of 'similarities' constrains
   nothing.
   THE REPLY  RELEVANCE IS FIXED BY THE PRACTICE, not by bare resemblance - by what
   we do with games, how the word is taught, which extensions are ACCEPTED and
   which are CORRECTED. The concept is bounded by USE, not by definition.
   THE RESIDUE  Whether use bounds it tightly enough in EVERY case is genuinely
   open. Concede it: that is the 'critically' mark.
   THE SERVICE RENDERED - the 2018 answer in four steps
     1 THE DEMAND DISARMED   refusing to define 'language' is no longer a defect
     2 THE ANALOGY EARNED    'game' IS the paradigm family-resemblance concept
     3 THE PICTURE SUPPLIED  games have TRAINING, RULES, MOVES and STANDARDS
                             WITHOUT an essence - exactly what the Tractatus
                             thought only a shared LOGICAL FORM could give
     4 RELATIVISM BLOCKED    games are norm-governed, so meaning is not arbitrary

==============================================================================
   (i) THE PARADOX
     'THIS WAS OUR PARADOX: no course of action could be determined by a rule,
      because EVERY COURSE OF ACTION CAN BE MADE OUT TO ACCORD WITH THE RULE.'

     THE FIRST STEP WAS THE MISTAKE.
