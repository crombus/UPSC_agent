ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 2/53: THE FAMILY-LABEL WARNING - SAY THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  'EXISTENTIALISM' IS A RETROSPECTIVE FAMILY LABEL, NOT A SCHOOL.
    KIERKEGAARD  died 1855.  Never met the word.
    HEIDEGGER    DENIED he was an existentialist - 'Letter on Humanism',
                 written 1946, published 1947.
    SARTRE       ACCEPTED the label and DEFINED it - lecture October 1945,
                 published 1946.
                                   |
  WHAT ACTUALLY UNITES THEM IS A REFUSAL, NOT A THESIS
    Each DENIES that the being of a human individual is settled in advance by
    a general nature.
      KIERKEGAARD  against Hegel's MEDIATING SYSTEM
      HEIDEGGER    against the CARTESIAN SUBJECT who is first a thinking
                   thing and only then in a world
      SARTRE       against the DIVINE ARTISAN and the DETERMINIST
    UNITY = FAMILY RESEMBLANCE.  Overlapping problems, NOT a common creed.
                                   |
  WHAT THE CLAUSE IS NOT
    NOT PESSIMISM ... anxiety, despair, death are DISCLOSIVE STRUCTURES
    NOT NIHILISM .... K grounds value in faith; S in the responsibility free
                      commitment generates; H in formal ownership of finitude
    NOT WHIM ........ appropriation, choice and resoluteness are SITUATED and
                      RESPONSIBILITY-LADEN
    NOT SELF-HELP ... the clause is ontology, ethics and religious thought
  FATAL OPENING: 'The existentialists held that ...'

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 6/53: KIERKEGAARD - THE TARGET AND THE STRUCTURAL COUNTER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE TARGET: HEGEL'S SYSTEM, AS KIERKEGAARD RECEIVES IT
    Reality is the self-development of ABSOLUTE SPIRIT (Geist).
    Every opposition is MEDIATED (aufgehoben) into a higher unity.
    The individual is a MOMENT in a total rational process.
    'The truth is the whole.'
    FENCE: the dialectic and Absolute Spirit belong to TOPIC 05.  Two clauses
    of target-setting here, then the counter.
                                   |
  THE COUNTER - STRUCTURAL, NOT A COMPLAINT ABOUT DIFFICULTY
    The system explains everything EXCEPT THE EXISTING INDIVIDUAL WHO IS
    SUPPOSED TO UNDERSTAND IT.
    To exist is to be PARTICULAR, TEMPORAL, PASSIONATE and DECIDING - none of
    which is a category the system can accommodate without CANCELLING it.
    THE SENTENCE TO DEPLOY:
      'A LOGICAL SYSTEM IS POSSIBLE; AN EXISTENTIAL SYSTEM IS IMPOSSIBLE.'
    NO SYSTEM CAN CONTAIN THE ONE WHO BUILDS IT: the systematiser must still
    EXIST, CHOOSE and DIE.
                                   |
  THE THREE CONTRAST PAIRS THAT CARRY IT
    EITHER/OR         vs  BOTH/AND     to exist IS to choose one thing
                                       rather than another
    THE LEAP          vs  MEDIATION    no argument delivers me from one
    (Springet)                         sphere to the next
    INDIRECT          vs  THE LECTURE  the form must not hand over a result
    COMMUNICATION
                                   |
  BOUNDED VERDICT
    Kierkegaard EXPOSES a first-person appropriation that a completed system-
    view risks LOSING.  He does NOT refute Hegel's ontology and offers NO
    rival comprehensive system.  A Hegelian may reply that the existing
    individual is an unreflective stage philosophy SUBLATES.
  PYQ ANCHORS: 2023 Q1(d) 10m; 2020 Q3(a) 20m; 2022 Q4(a) 20m.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 7/53: KIERKEGAARD - 'TRUTH IS SUBJECTIVITY' AND ITS THREE DENIALS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ATTRIBUTION
    Argued by the pseudonym JOHANNES CLIMACUS in the CONCLUDING UNSCIENTIFIC
    POSTSCRIPT, 1846.  Climacus is a HUMORIST who says explicitly that he is
    NOT A CHRISTIAN - which is exactly why he can pose the problem of
    BECOMING one.
                                   |
  WHAT IT ASSERTS
    For ETHICAL and RELIGIOUS truth the MODE OF APPROPRIATION is decisive:
    HOW one holds a belief - with INFINITE PASSIONATE INWARDNESS, personal
    COMMITMENT, RISK - is the locus of truth FOR THE EXISTING SUBJECT.
    WORKED CONTRAST: to 'know' that God exists as an item in a philosophical
    system is NOT the same as EXISTING BEFORE GOD in faith and trembling.
                                   |
  THREE DENIALS - WRITE ALL THREE
    (i)   NOT relativism.  It is not the claim that anything I believe is
          true.
    (ii)  NOT a denial of objective truth in logic, mathematics or history -
          only of its SUFFICIENCY for existential and religious truth.
    (iii) NOT a general theory that FACTUAL truth varies with the passion of
          the believer.
                                   |
  WHY THE FORM FOLLOWS
    If existential truth is a MODE OF EXISTING rather than a RESULT, it
    cannot be handed over directly without being FALSIFIED - which is why
    Kierkegaard writes PSEUDONYMOUSLY and INDIRECTLY (Panels 13-14).
  PYQ ANCHORS: 2020 Q3(a) 20m; 2022 Q4(a) 20m; 2024 Q1(e) 10m.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 8/53: KIERKEGAARD - THE ANTI-RELATIVIST COMPLEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  'SUBJECTIVITY IS UNTRUTH'
    The dialectical complement, also Climacus.  The existing subject is ALSO
    IN UNTRUTH - in SIN, in FINITUDE, in NON-IDENTITY WITH THE ETERNAL - and
    therefore NEEDS THE LEAP.
    A RELATIVIST HAS NO USE FOR THIS SENTENCE.  CLIMACUS CANNOT DO WITHOUT
    IT.  That is the INTERNAL EVIDENCE against the relativist reading, and it
    should be quoted in every subjectivity answer.
                                   |
  'THE CROWD IS UNTRUTH'
    The SOCIAL form of the same point.  The anonymous public permits ETHICAL
    EVASION, because a responsibility DISTRIBUTED among everyone is BORNE BY
    NO ONE.  Authentic existence requires the individual to stand ALONE -
    before God, or before the ethical demand.
                                   |
  DO NOT MERGE WITH HEIDEGGER'S DAS MAN
    STRUCTURALLY PARALLEL, DOCTRINALLY DIFFERENT.
      KIERKEGAARD  target = evasion of RESPONSIBILITY BEFORE GOD
      HEIDEGGER    target = a LEVELLING OF POSSIBILITIES, with NO
                   theological content and NO blame attached
  TRAP: writing that Kierkegaard's crowd and Heidegger's das Man are 'the
  same idea in different words' collapses a theological category into an
  ontological one.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 10/53: KIERKEGAARD - THE LEAP, THE SUSPENSION, AND THE TWO KNIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE LEAP (Springet)
    Transition between spheres is a QUALITATIVE CHOICE - not a Hegelian
    deduction, not a psychological development.
    PROVENANCE CAUTION: Kierkegaard writes of THE LEAP and of FAITH.  The
    fused English phrase 'LEAP OF FAITH' belongs to the ENGLISH TRADITION.
    Write: 'the leap - what the English tradition calls the leap of faith.'
                                   |
  THE TELEOLOGICAL SUSPENSION OF THE ETHICAL
    Analysed by JOHANNES DE SILENTIO in FEAR AND TREMBLING (1843) through
    ABRAHAM, commanded to sacrifice Isaac.  The ETHICAL (the universal) is
    SUSPENDED in relation to a higher TELOS - the absolute relation to the
    Absolute.
    THE TEXT STAGES THE PARADOX OF FAITH.  It must NOT be cited as
    Kierkegaard's straightforward LICENCE for private revelation to override
    morality.
    'DE SILENTIO' = the SILENCE of one who ADMIRES faith but DOES NOT HAVE
    it - he can DESCRIBE Abraham but not UNDERSTAND him.
                                   |
  THE TWO KNIGHTS - THE DIFFERENCE IS THE DOCTRINE
    KNIGHT OF INFINITE RESIGNATION   gives the finite UP, and the
                                     renunciation SHOWS.  Visibly heroic,
                                     visibly apart from ordinary life.
    KNIGHT OF FAITH                  gives it up AND RECEIVES IT BACK.
                                     OUTWARDLY INDISTINGUISHABLE from a
                                     bourgeois citizen: lives FULLY in the
                                     finite world WHILE relating absolutely
                                     to the Absolute.
    THE DOUBLE MOVEMENT: infinite resignation FIRST, THEN faith - which is
    why faith is HIGHER than resignation, not easier.
    USE: the fastest available refutation of 'existentialism is world-
    denying'.
  OBJECTION: FIDEISM / IRRATIONALISM.  REPLY: reason is not WRONG but
  LIMITED - it cannot deliver what the religious individual needs.  Whether
  that is a reductio or a valid critique of rationalism DEPENDS ON ONE'S
  EPISTEMOLOGY.  Leave the verdict open.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 11/53: KIERKEGAARD - ANXIETY AS THE DIZZINESS OF FREEDOM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SOURCE  THE CONCEPT OF ANXIETY, 1844, by VIGILIUS HAUFNIENSIS ('watchman of
          Copenhagen'), writing as a PSYCHOLOGICAL OBSERVER and deliberately
          NOT as a dogmatician.  Lowrie's title: THE CONCEPT OF DREAD.
                                   |
  OBJECT ............ NONE.  'THE OBJECT OF ANXIETY IS A NOTHING.'
  WHAT APPEARS ...... POSSIBILITY - 'the possibility of being able'
  FORMULA ........... 'ANXIETY IS THE DIZZINESS OF FREEDOM'
  AMBIVALENCE ....... 'a SYMPATHETIC ANTIPATHY and an ANTIPATHETIC SYMPATHY'
                      - we are DRAWN to what we SHRINK from
  CANONICAL EXAMPLE . the man at the edge of an abyss grows dizzy - but
                      'THE DIZZINESS IS AS MUCH IN THE EYE AS IN THE ABYSS'.
                      The vertigo is not produced by the drop but by the
                      disclosure that NOTHING PREVENTS HIM
  IN INNOCENCE ...... there is NO knowledge of good and evil, yet anxiety is
                      ALREADY there, because freedom's possibility is already
                      sensed
  ROLE .............. anxiety is the PRESUPPOSITION of the LEAP by which sin
                      - and selfhood - enters.  NOT its CAUSE: a caused leap
                      would not be a leap
                                   |
  TRAP: defining anxiety as INTENSE FEAR.  The difference is IN KIND, not
  degree - fear has a DETERMINATE OBJECT, anxiety has NONE (Panel 33).


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 12/53: KIERKEGAARD - DESPAIR AS A MISRELATION IN THE SELF
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SOURCE  THE SICKNESS UNTO DEATH, 1849, by ANTI-CLIMACUS - a Christian at an
          EXTRAORDINARILY HIGH level, ABOVE Kierkegaard himself, and the
          deliberate MIRROR IMAGE of Climacus, who says he is NOT a Christian
                                   |
  THE DEFINITION
    THE SELF IS NOT A THING.  It is A RELATION THAT RELATES ITSELF TO ITSELF
    - and, in relating to itself, RELATES TO THE POWER THAT ESTABLISHED IT.
    DESPAIR = A MISRELATION IN THAT RELATION.
    NOT an emotion.  NOT sadness.  A STRUCTURAL FAULT THAT MAY BE ENTIRELY
    UNFELT - which is the most examinable feature of the doctrine, because it
    is what allows the claim that despair is universal to be STRUCTURAL
    rather than an empirical exaggeration.
                                   |
  TWO FORMS
    FORM 1  NOT WILLING TO BE ONESELF - fleeing the self one is; wanting to
            be someone else, or no one
    FORM 2  DEFIANTLY WILLING TO BE ONESELF - insisting on being oneself
            WITHOUT the grounding God-relation.  THE DEEPER FORM, precisely
            because it looks like STRENGTH
                                   |
  THE FENCE THAT MUST BE STATED
    KIERKEGAARDIAN DESPAIR (theological: a misrelation before God)
        IS NOT
    SARTREAN DESPAIR (desespoir, 1946 lecture: acting without relying on
        outcomes beyond one's control and reasonable probabilities)
    SAME ENGLISH WORD.  TWO UNRELATED DOCTRINES.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 13/53: KIERKEGAARD - INDIRECT COMMUNICATION IN SIX STEPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CORE STATUS: promoted OUT of optional-enrichment status on 15 AUGUST 2026.
  Marks-essential for any question on subjectivity or the critique of Hegel.
                                   |
  1  OBJECTIVE TRUTH - historical, scientific, metaphysical - can be
     transmitted DIRECTLY.  I state a result, you receive it, and NOTHING
     ABOUT YOUR EXISTENCE NEED CHANGE.
                                   |
  2  ETHICAL AND RELIGIOUS TRUTH is not a result but A MODE OF EXISTING, an
     APPROPRIATION IN INWARDNESS (Climacus, Postscript 1846).
                                   |
  3  IF such truth were communicated DIRECTLY, the recipient would take it
     over AS A RESULT - as information, as doctrine, as something one can
     KNOW WITHOUT BECOMING.  THE COMMUNICATION WOULD THEREBY FALSIFY EXACTLY
     WHAT IT COMMUNICATES.   <-- THE LOAD-BEARING STEP
                                   |
  4  THEREFORE ethical-religious truth requires INDIRECT COMMUNICATION
     (indirekte Meddelelse): a form that WITHHOLDS THE RESULT, REFUSES TO BE
     AN AUTHORITY, and forces the reader to relate to the possibility FOR
     HIMSELF.
                                   |
  5  THE MODEL IS SOCRATIC MAIEUTICS - the midwife who brings to birth what
     the other must produce, and who, in Kierkegaard's phrase, 'DECEIVES INTO
     THE TRUTH'.  The author must become a VANISHING OCCASION, not a teacher
     with a doctrine.  The 'deception' is FORMAL, not moral: the reader is
     denied a result FOR HIS OWN SAKE.
                                   |
  6  THEREFORE PSEUDONYMITY.  The pseudonyms are NOT disguises for a shy
     author but PERSONIFIED EXISTENCE-POSSIBILITIES, each writing from INSIDE
     a sphere of existence it CANNOT SEE BEYOND.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 15/53: KIERKEGAARD - THE DOUBLE AUTHORSHIP AND THE DECLARATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE DOUBLE AUTHORSHIP
    SIMULTANEOUSLY, AND UNDER HIS OWN NAME, Kierkegaard published the
    UPBUILDING / EDIFYING DISCOURSES.  The signed religious writings stand
    BESIDE the pseudonymous works as the DIRECT POLE of a SINGLE STRATEGY.
    Saying this shows pseudonymity is a METHOD, not an evasion.
                                   |
  THE PRINTED INSTRUCTION
    'FIRST AND LAST DECLARATION', appended to the POSTSCRIPT (1846).  He
    ACKNOWLEDGES authorship while insisting he is only the 'AUTHOR'S AUTHOR',
    a PROMPTER (souffleur) who produced CHARACTERS, and ASKS THAT ANYONE
    CITING THEM QUOTE THE PSEUDONYM'S NAME, NOT HIS.
                                   |
  OBJECTIONS AND REPLIES
    (a) UNATTRIBUTABILITY - the method insulates him from criticism.
        REPLY: the signed discourses are the direct pole, and THE POINT OF
        VIEW (WRITTEN 1848, PUBLISHED POSTHUMOUSLY 1859) gives an explicit
        account.  RESIDUE: that text is a RETROSPECTIVE self-interpretation
        and is DISPUTED as evidence.
    (b) SELF-UNDERMINING - if subjective truth cannot be said directly, the
        THESIS cannot be said directly either.
        REPLY: CLIMACUS CONCEDES THIS - the POSTSCRIPT ENDS BY REVOKING
        ITSELF.  Cross-link in ONE CLAUSE to Tractatus 6.54 (Topic 06) and
        Hegel's Unhappy Consciousness (Topic 05).  Do NOT expound either.
    (c) HEGELIAN SUBLATION - the existing individual is an unreflective stage.
        COUNTER: no system can contain the one who BUILDS it.
  EXECUTABLE VERDICT: the method's COST is that it is SELF-CONSUMING; its
  POWER is that the cost is INTENDED.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 16/53: SARTRE - 'EXISTENCE PRECEDES ESSENCE': THE ARGUMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SOURCE  EXISTENTIALISM IS A HUMANISM - lecture OCTOBER 1945, PUBLISHED
          1946.  NOT Being and Nothingness (1943).  Sartre later REGRETTED
          publishing it.
                                   |
  P1  For a MANUFACTURED object, the artisan conceives the DESIGN
      (essence/function) and THEN produces it.  ESSENCE PRECEDES EXISTENCE.
      SARTRE'S EXAMPLE: the PAPER-KNIFE (coupe-papier).
  P2  Theism treats MAN analogously: God conceives 'human nature' and creates
      individuals to that blueprint.
  P3  THERE IS NO GOD.
      ATHEISM IS A PREMISE OF SARTRE'S ARGUMENT, NOT A CONCLUSION FROM IT.
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C   There is NO prior design.  'MAN FIRST OF ALL EXISTS, ENCOUNTERS
      HIMSELF, SURGES UP IN THE WORLD - AND DEFINES HIMSELF AFTERWARDS.'
      EXISTENCE PRECEDES ESSENCE.
                                   |
  WHAT FOLLOWS
    No predetermined blueprint EXHAUSTS human reality; the for-itself must
    TAKE UP a project; RESPONSIBILITY is radical.
  WHAT MUST BE QUALIFIED
    Biology, history and society form FACTICITY and CONSTRAINT.  'Man is
    nothing else but what he makes of himself' is the 1946 LECTURE's formula
    and must be BALANCED BY SITUATION.  The slogan denies a PRIOR ESSENCE; it
    does NOT deny that circumstances limit what can be done.
  OBJECTION: if there are NO a priori values, on what basis do I choose?
    DECISIONISM (Iris Murdoch, The Sovereignty of Good; Marxist critics).
    REPLY: the ENGAGEMENT of choice generates its own criteria.  CRITICS FIND
    THIS CIRCULAR.  VERDICT: the doctrine foregrounds PROJECT and
    RESPONSIBILITY but leaves VALUE-CRITERIA and SITUATED CONSTRAINT
    UNDERDEVELOPED.
  PYQ ANCHOR: 2024 Q4(a) 20m; opening of 2020 Q4(a) 20m.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 17/53: THE SLOGAN'S OTHER TWO OWNERS - REPUDIATION AND ABSENCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HEIDEGGER REPUDIATES IT
    'LETTER ON HUMANISM', written 1946, PUBLISHED 1947.
    * 'THE ESSENCE OF DASEIN LIES IN ITS EXISTENCE' (BEING AND TIME,
      SECTION 9) does NOT mean 'existence precedes essence' in Sartre's
      voluntarist sense.  It is an ONTOLOGICAL claim: Dasein's 'what'
      (essence) is not a fixed QUIDDITAS but its very MANNER OF BEING - its
      being-open-to-Being, its EXISTENZ, its standing-out into the clearing
      of Being.
    * SARTRE MERELY REVERSES the essence/existence priority WHILE KEEPING
      the humanistic framework, with man at the centre.  He therefore
      remains inside the METAPHYSICS OF SUBJECTIVITY.
    * The question is not man's SELF-CREATION but BEING.  Man is the
      'SHEPHERD OF BEING', not the LORD OF BEINGS.
                                   |
  KIERKEGAARD NEVER WROTE IT
    He died in 1855 and could not accept its PREMISE: the God-relation is
    what makes the single individual possible at all.  His refusal of an
    antecedent essence takes an entirely different form - the EXISTING
    INDIVIDUAL who cannot be completed inside a system.
                                   |
  WHAT THE THREE ACTUALLY SHARE - THE CORE OF ANY 2024 Q4(a) ANSWER
    ALL THREE DENY that a general essence settles in advance what it is for
    THIS individual to exist.  HOW they deny it differs:
      SARTRE       by REMOVING THE DESIGNER
      HEIDEGGER    by RECONCEIVING WHAT 'ESSENCE' MEANS for the entity whose
                   being is at issue
      KIERKEGAARD  by insisting that EXISTING IS A TASK, not a classification
  TRAP: 2024 Q4(a) asks what 'the existentialist THINKERS' mean by the
  slogan.  CORRECT THE ATTRIBUTION WHILE ANSWERING - do not merely complain
  about the premise.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 19/53: SARTRE - NIHILATION AND THE IMPOSSIBLE SYNTHESIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  'CONSCIOUSNESS IS WHAT IT IS NOT AND IS NOT WHAT IT IS' - UNPACK BOTH HALVES
    'IS WHAT IT IS NOT'    consciousness is always AHEAD OF ITSELF,
                           projecting toward possibilities it does not yet
                           possess.  It IS (is defined by) its FUTURE -
                           WHICH DOES NOT YET EXIST.
    'IS NOT WHAT IT IS'    consciousness can NEVER COINCIDE with its PAST or
                           its FACTICITY.  THE WAITER is a waiter
                           (facticity) and simultaneously IS NOT a waiter
                           (he transcends the role, is free to quit).
                                   |
  HOW IS THIS POSSIBLE?  NIHILATION (neantisation)
    Consciousness introduces NOTHINGNESS (neant) into being: it STANDS BACK
    from what is and NEGATES it - QUESTIONS it, IMAGINES alternatives,
    PROJECTS beyond it.  Those three are the concrete faces of nihilation.
    THE POUR-SOI IS 'THE BEING BY WHICH NOTHINGNESS COMES INTO THE WORLD'.
                                   |
  THE IMPOSSIBLE SYNTHESIS
    The pour-soi PERPETUALLY DESIRES to become en-soi - full self-identity
    WHILE REMAINING CONSCIOUS.  That would be a POUR-SOI-EN-SOI = GOD, and it
    is a CONTRADICTION.  Human reality is defined by this impossible desire:
    hence 'MAN IS A USELESS PASSION'.
                                   |
  WHAT THE SINGLE STRUCTURE GENERATES
    FREEDOM ............. nothing in me is fixed enough to determine me
    BAD FAITH ........... the attempt to be self-identical ANYWAY
    TEMPORALITY ......... the for-itself exists as past-facticity and
                          future-project
    BEING-FOR-OTHERS .... the Look confers an outside I cannot occupy
    THE DESIRE TO BE GOD  the contradictory ideal above
  OBJECTION: is NON-SELF-IDENTITY coherent, or a contradiction dressed in
  phenomenological language?  REPLY: the for-itself has NO COMPLETED NATURE
  with which to be identical - it exists as a RELATION TO ITS OWN
  POSSIBILITIES.  CONTESTED.
  PYQ ANCHOR: 2023 Q3(c) 15m.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 20/53: SARTRE - FREEDOM, NON-CHOICE AND UNIVERSALISABILITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE STRUCTURAL THESIS
    Freedom is NOT a property we possess; IT IS WHAT WE ARE.  The pour-soi IS
    the nihilating, self-transcending movement that continually surpasses
    what it is toward what it is NOT YET.
    'MAN IS CONDEMNED TO BE FREE' (lecture 1945, published 1946): we did not
    choose to exist, yet cannot escape TAKING UP A STANCE within situations.
    ATTRIBUTION CARE: 'THROWN' (Geworfenheit) IS HEIDEGGER'S TERM and should
    not be silently made Sartre's own.
                                   |
  NON-CHOICE IS CHOICE
    'I CAN ALWAYS CHOOSE, BUT I OUGHT TO KNOW THAT IF I DO NOT CHOOSE, I AM
    STILL CHOOSING.'
    Inaction, deferral and conformity are CHOICES; the attempt to escape
    choice is ALREADY a choice, and TYPICALLY A CHOICE OF BAD FAITH.
    Because the outcome of a non-choice is PRODUCED BY that non-choice,
    responsibility is NOT diminished by passivity.
                                   |
  CHOICE LEGISLATES
    'IN CHOOSING MYSELF, I CHOOSE MAN.'  Every choice implies an IMAGE OF
    WHAT HUMAN BEINGS OUGHT TO BE.  QUASI-KANTIAN UNIVERSALISABILITY.  If I
    choose to marry, I affirm that monogamy is valuable FOR EVERYONE; I
    cannot choose 'just for me' in moral isolation.
    THIS IS WHERE THE WEIGHT OF RESPONSIBILITY, AND THE ANGUISH, COME FROM.
    INTERNAL TENSION: whence universalisability if there are NO a priori
    values?  REPLY: the FORM of universalisability is inherent in the
    STRUCTURE of free choice, not imported from a pre-existing ethics.
    Kantians and communitarians press the point; Sartre CONCEDES TENSION, and
    the posthumous NOTEBOOKS FOR AN ETHICS was NEVER COMPLETED.
  PYQ ANCHOR: 2021 Q4(b) 15m; second half of 2024 Q4(a).

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 22/53: SARTRE - FACTICITY, TRANSCENDENCE AND BAD FAITH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE STRUCTURE
    Human reality has TWO DIMENSIONS AT ONCE:
      FACTICITY (facticite) - the GIVEN: body, past, social position,
        situation
      TRANSCENDENCE - the SELF-SURPASSING: freedom to NEGATE my facticity, to
        project myself beyond what I presently am
    BAD FAITH (mauvaise foi) occurs when I identify EXCLUSIVELY WITH ONE
    DIMENSION AT THE EXPENSE OF THE OTHER.
    IT IS THE COLLAPSE OF THE TENSION - NOT 'LYING TO ONESELF' IN GENERAL.
                                   |
  THE TWO CANONICAL EXAMPLES
    THE WAITER
      He performs the role with exaggerated, THING-LIKE precision.
      READ IT CAREFULLY: Sartre reads this as DENIAL THAT A SOCIAL ROLE
      EXHAUSTS THE FOR-ITSELF.  ROLE-PERFORMANCE ALONE IS NOT AUTOMATICALLY
      BAD FAITH.  The bad faith lies in the CLAIM TO BE the role IN THE MODE
      OF THE IN-ITSELF, not in doing the job well.
    THE WOMAN ON THE DATE - A DOUBLE LIE
      A man takes her hand; she leaves it there, 'not noticing', treating the
      hand as a mere physical thing.
      (i)  She denies her TRANSCENDENCE - her freedom to accept or reject the
           advance - by reducing herself to pure FACTICITY, a body-object.
      (ii) She simultaneously denies the FACTICITY of the gesture - its
           sexual meaning - by treating it as purely neutral.
      TWO DENIALS, IN OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS, IN ONE ACT.
                                   |
  EVERYDAY SOCIAL FORM: THE 'SERIOUS SPIRIT' - treating values as though they
  were PROPERTIES OF THE WORLD rather than products of commitment.
  BAD FAITH IS A PERMANENT TEMPTATION, NOT A RARE PATHOLOGY, because the
  tension it flees is CONSTITUTIVE.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 23/53: SARTRE - THE PARADOX OF SELF-DECEPTION AND ITS SOLUTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE PARADOX
    To deceive MYSELF I must SIMULTANEOUSLY
      KNOW the truth ......... in order to CONCEAL it
      NOT KNOW it ............ in order to BE DECEIVED
    SARTRE REJECTS THE FREUDIAN UNCONSCIOUS, so he CANNOT split the person
    into a DECEIVING CENSOR and a DECEIVED SUBJECT.
                                   |
  SARTRE'S SOLUTION
    CONSCIOUSNESS IS NON-SELF-IDENTICAL - the pour-soi 'is what it is not and
    is not what it is' (Panel 18) - so it can hold TRUTH and DENIAL in a
    SINGLE PRE-REFLECTIVE STANCE: I am aware NON-THETICALLY without
    reflectively AVOWING.  NO SECOND PERSON INSIDE THE PERSON IS REQUIRED.
                                   |
  STATUS
    CONTESTED.  Fingarette (SELF-DECEPTION) and analytic philosophy of mind
    press whether a non-thetic awareness can do the work.  STATE THE
    OBJECTION AND RULE ON IT; do not present the solution as settled.
  TRAP: 'solving' the paradox by invoking the unconscious CONTRADICTS the
  position being expounded, and removes the reason Sartre needs the
  non-self-identity thesis in the first place.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 25/53: SARTRE - FREEDOM AND DETERMINISM, AND THE 2020 SENTENCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE KEY ARGUMENT (Being and Nothingness, 1943)
    1  A MOTIVE - a desire, an emotion, a situation - can become a REASON FOR
       ACTION only through the MEANING I confer on it.
    2  Meaning-conferral is itself an ACT of the pour-soi - nihilation,
       transcendence, projection.
    3  THEREFORE no motive determines me UNLESS I FREELY CONSTITUTE IT AS A
       MOTIVE.  Remove the project and the same desire motivates nothing.
    4  THEREFORE determinism MISDESCRIBES the for-itself: it treats a
       nihilating being as an EN-SOI, a thing among causes.  THE 'PROBLEM' OF
       FREEDOM AND DETERMINISM IS A FALSE PROBLEM.
                                   |
  THE 2020 SENTENCE, WORD BY WORD
    'YOU CAN ALWAYS MAKE SOMETHING OUT OF WHAT YOU HAVE BEEN MADE INTO.'
    'WHAT YOU HAVE BEEN MADE INTO' = FACTICITY.  SARTRE CONCEDES THE MAKING:
      class, body, upbringing, history, the gaze of others.  THE SENTENCE
      BEGINS BY GRANTING THE DETERMINIST HIS DATA.
    'YOU CAN ALWAYS MAKE SOMETHING OUT OF' = TRANSCENDENCE.  The for-itself
      must still TAKE UP that facticity in a project.
    'ALWAYS' = THE STRUCTURAL CLAIM.  There is NO SITUATION IN WHICH THE
      FOR-ITSELF BECOMES A THING.  NOT a promise that every outcome is
      available.
                                   |
  THE CRITICISM THAT LANDED, AND THE EVIDENCE THAT IT LANDED
    OBJECTION  slavery, torture, mental illness and structural oppression
      limit not only ACTION but the COGNITIVE RANGE of alternatives
      (Merleau-Ponty, ADVENTURES OF THE DIALECTIC; Marxist critics;
      de Beauvoir; Fanon).
    FIRST REPLY  distinguish FREEDOM-OF-CONSCIOUSNESS (I can always
      interpret) from FREEDOM-OF-ACTION (limited by situation).  BUT THIS
      WEAKENS THE RADICAL THESIS.
    DECISIVE EVIDENCE  in the CRITIQUE OF DIALECTICAL REASON (1960) SARTRE
      HIMSELF integrates SCARCITY, SERIALITY and the PRACTICO-INERT, and
      introduces the GROUP-IN-FUSION.  USE IT AS A CONCESSION, NEVER AS THE
      DOCTRINE OF 1943.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 26/53: SARTRE - THE LOOK AND BEING-FOR-OTHERS: SEVEN STEPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BOUNDED RELEVANCE: necessary for ALIENATION, BAD FAITH and INTERSUBJECTIVITY
  questions, but it must NOT DISPLACE the printed freedom/choice spine.  ONE
  DEVELOPMENT, NEVER A SECTION.
  EXACT PRINTED SUBTERMS: le regard (the Look) - l'etre-pour-autrui (being-
  for-others) - la honte (shame) - le corps-pour-autrui (the body-for-others)
  - 'objet-autrui' / 'sujet-autrui' - 'la chute originelle' (the original
  fall) - CONFLIT as 'the original meaning of being-for-others'.
                                   |
  THE KEYHOLE - TWO STAGES, LEARN BOTH
    1 ABSORBED  Out of jealousy or curiosity I am bent at a keyhole, looking
      through.  I am WHOLLY ABSORBED: there is NO EGO in my consciousness,
      only the scene and my project.  Consciousness is PRE-REFLECTIVE, non-
      positional about itself.  THERE IS NO SELF TO BE ASHAMED.
    2 CAUGHT  Footsteps in the hall.  I AM SEEN.  Instantly my whole being is
      transformed: I am no longer pure surveying transcendence but A PEEPING
      TOM, an object with a nature, seen from outside, judged.
                                   |
  THE ARGUMENT
    1 In being looked at I experience SHAME - and shame is not a private
      feeling I might have alone; by its structure it is SHAME OF MYSELF
      BEFORE SOMEONE.
    2 'SHAME IS BY NATURE RECOGNITION': in being ashamed I RECOGNISE that I
      am as the Other sees me.  I do not INFER the Other; I LIVE his presence
      as the condition of my own new mode of being.
    3 The Other is NOT reached by explicit inference.  Sartre contrasts the
      Look with Husserlian APPRESENTATION and Heideggerian BEING-WITH -
      though neither rival is fairly reduced to an argument from analogy or
      to neutral co-existence.  THE OTHER IS GIVEN AS THE SUBJECT FOR WHOM I
      AM AN OBJECT.
    4 THEREFORE 'THE OTHER IS THE INDISPENSABLE MEDIATOR BETWEEN MYSELF AND
      ME': I acquire an OUTSIDE, a 'nature', a being I AM without having
      chosen it and without being able to know it from within - my
      BEING-FOR-OTHERS.
    5 The Look reveals a dimension of FACTICITY: the meanings of my acts and
      my body are constituted from a perspective I CANNOT CONTROL.  This is
      ONE limit on mastery, not the only constraint Sartre recognises.
ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 27/53: SARTRE - THE LOOK: THE FOUR PRESUPPOSITIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PRESUPPOSITIONS
    P1 shame is IRREDUCIBLY RELATIONAL and cannot be analysed away as self-
       assessment
    P2 pre-reflective consciousness is EGOLESS, so the ego that appears under

    P3 SUBJECT AND OBJECT ARE EXCLUSIVE - I cannot be looked-at and looking

    P4 there is NO THIRD TERM - no 'we-subject' of the kind Marxists posit -

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 28/53: SARTRE - CONFLICT AND THE FOUR ACCOUNTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  6 THE DIALECTIC OF CONCRETE RELATIONS (Being and Nothingness, Part III,
    ch. 3).  Objectified by the Other's freedom, I can respond in only TWO
    directions - AND BOTH FAIL.
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    (a) ASSIMILATE the Other's freedom - possess the freedom that judges me
        WHILE IT REMAINS FREE
        FORMS: LOVE, LANGUAGE, MASOCHISM
        WHY IT FAILS: love wants to be loved FREELY - but TO BE LOVED IS TO
        BE MADE AN OBJECT, so the very freedom sought is DESTROYED IN THE
        POSSESSING.  Masochism collapses because I cannot succeed in being
        PURE OBJECT while REMAINING CONSCIOUS.
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    (b) TRANSCEND the Other's freedom - reduce him to an object so his look
        cannot fix me
        FORMS: INDIFFERENCE, DESIRE, SADISM, HATE
        WHY IT FAILS: AN OBJECT CANNOT CONFER THE RECOGNITION I WANTED; the
        moment he looks back, the whole structure REVERSES.  Hate wants the
        Other's DISAPPEARANCE - which would leave my BEING-FOR-OTHERS INTACT.
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
  7 THEREFORE 'CONFLICT IS THE ORIGINAL MEANING OF BEING-FOR-OTHERS.'
    The line of HUIS CLOS (NO EXIT, 1944) - 'L'ENFER, C'EST LES AUTRES' -
    follows from this.
    QUOTE IT, THEN CORRECT THE POPULAR MISREADING.  Sartre explained, in a
    1965 RECORDED PREFACE to the play, that the line does NOT mean relations
    with others are always poisonous; it means that IF one's relations are
    twisted, the Other's judgement becomes the medium of one's own self-
    torment - AND THAT THE THREE CHARACTERS ARE DEAD, i.e. FIXED, unable to
    change their acts.  MAKING THIS CORRECTION IS A HIGH-VALUE MOVE.
                                   |
ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 29/53: SARTRE - THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE OTHER, AND OBJECTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE FOUR ACCOUNTS OF THE OTHER - A TABLE WORTH MEMORISING
    HUSSERL   passive PAIRING and APPRESENTATION, not a discursive analogy-
              inference.  The Other is NON-ORIGINALLY GIVEN; ownness-first
              circularity remains
    HEIDEGGER Dasein is ALWAYS ALREADY BEING-WITH (Mitsein).  Social
              existence is CONSTITUTIVE, though Sartre presses for the
              conflictual concrete encounter
    SARTRE    the LOOK - the Other is revealed in the SHAME of being
              objectified.  CONFLICT; recognition STRUCTURALLY UNATTAINABLE
    LEVINAS   the FACE (visage) of the Other, which COMMANDS before it is
              known.  ETHICAL ASYMMETRY - RESPONSIBILITY PRECEDES FREEDOM;
              explicitly a reply to BOTH Husserl and Sartre
                                   |
  STRONGEST OBJECTIONS
    (i)   Sartre GENERALISES FROM PATHOLOGICAL encounters (peeping Tom,
          sadism, seduction).  REPLY: the claim is ONTOLOGICAL, not
          statistical.  RESIDUE: strong objection; Sartre's own move to the
          GROUP-IN-FUSION in the Critique (1960) is the best evidence it
          landed.
    (ii)  LOVE, TRUST and BEING-CARED-FOR are equally basic and are not
          objectifications.  REPLY: Sartre analyses love and finds an
          internal contradiction - compelling ONLY IF P3 IS ACCEPTED.
    (iii) LEVINAS: to encounter the Other only as a RIVAL FREEDOM is still to
          think him FROM THE SELF.  SARTRE HAS NO REPLY WITHIN BEING AND
          NOTHINGNESS.  THE STRONGEST SINGLE LINE OF CRITICISM.
  EXECUTABLE VERDICT: the Look gives a powerful NON-INFERENTIAL account of
  being exposed to another perspective, but Sartre may GENERALISE CONFLICT
  from selective examples.  Later solidarity, feminist and postcolonial
  accounts TEST rather than simply repeat the subject/object model.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 30/53: HEIDEGGER - THE BEING-QUESTION AND DASEIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE PROJECT: FUNDAMENTAL ONTOLOGY.  BEING AND TIME (Sein und Zeit), 1927.
    The question is THE MEANING OF BEING.  NOT an existentialist psychology.
    NOT a general ethics of authenticity.
    PUBLISHED UNFINISHED: only Divisions One and Two of PART ONE appeared;
    DIVISION III ('Time and Being') and the whole of PART TWO were NEVER
    PUBLISHED.  NEVER write that Heidegger 'completes' his analysis of Being.
    Later: THE TURN (Kehre) toward the history of Being, language and art.
    Whether that is FAILURE or DEEPENING is DEBATED.
                                   |
  THE ONTOLOGICAL DIFFERENCE
    BEING (Sein) ...... the INTELLIGIBILITY in virtue of which entities show
                        up AS entities
    BEINGS (Seiende) .. the entities themselves
    BEING IS NOT ONE SUPREME ENTITY.  Treating it as such is the 'onto-
    theological' mistake Heidegger charges the tradition with.  This single
    distinction blocks the misreading in which 'Being' becomes a metaphysical
    super-object or a synonym for God.
                                   |
  WHY DASEIN IS THE ROUTE - TWO TEXTUAL REASONS
    (i)  It ALREADY HAS A PRE-ONTOLOGICAL UNDERSTANDING OF BEING - it copes
         competently with a world long before it theorises about it.
    (ii) ITS OWN BEING IS AT ISSUE FOR IT.
    DASEIN IS CHOSEN FOR A METHODOLOGICAL REASON, not because human beings
    are metaphysically special.
    DASEIN IS NOT a biological synonym for 'human being', NOT consciousness,
    NOT a subject, NOT a soul.
                                   |
  SECTION 9 AND THE REPLACEMENT OF CATEGORIES
    'THE ESSENCE OF DASEIN LIES IN ITS EXISTENCE': Dasein's 'what' is not a
    fixed QUIDDITAS but its MANNER OF BEING.  Therefore EXISTENTIALIA (the
    structures of Dasein) REPLACE CATEGORIES (which apply to things).
    THIS IS THE SENTENCE SARTRE APPROPRIATES AND HEIDEGGER REPUDIATES.


ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 31/53: HEIDEGGER - THE EPOCHE AND ITS REJECTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  STEP 1  WHAT THE EPOCHE IS - ATTRIBUTE IT TO HUSSERL (TOPIC 09)
    The SUSPENSION of the natural attitude's EXISTENCE-POSIT.  NOTHING IS
    DENIED OR DOUBTED; the belief-character is put OUT OF PLAY so that what
    is given can be described purely.  The residue is TRANSCENDENTAL
    SUBJECTIVITY.  It is A CHANGE OF ATTITUDE, NOT A SCEPTICAL DOUBT.
    ONE ACCURATE SENTENCE.  DO NOT EXPOUND TOPIC 09 HERE.
                                   |
  STEP 2  HEIDEGGER'S OBJECTION - IT IS ABOUT ORDER, NOT ACCURACY
    A REFLECTIVE SUSPENSION is available only to a SPECTATOR ALREADY SET OVER
    AGAINST A WORLD.  But reflection is NOT the first relation Dasein has to
    anything: Dasein is PROXIMALLY AND FOR THE MOST PART ABSORBED IN TASKS,
    using equipment competently and understanding its situation practically.
    THEREFORE SUBJECT AND OBJECT ARE DERIVATIVE ABSTRACTIONS FROM PRACTICAL
    INVOLVEMENT, and a method that starts by bracketing the world's existence
    has SILENTLY RETAINED THE FRAMEWORK IT MEANT TO SUSPEND.
    The residue it isolates - a TRANSCENDENTAL EGO - is on this reading NOT
    the ground of world-disclosure but a LATE ABSTRACTION from a Dasein that
    was ALREADY disclosing a world.
                                   |
  STEP 3  WHAT HEIDEGGER DOES NOT DO
    He does NOT reject careful phenomenological description.  He REDIRECTS it
    - from TRANSCENDENTAL CONSCIOUSNESS to the DISCLOSEDNESS OF BEING through
    CARE, EQUIPMENT and TEMPORALITY.
                                   |
  TWO FORBIDDEN EQUATIONS
    DO NOT equate BEING-IN-THE-WORLD with SPATIAL CONTAINMENT.  Dasein is not
      'in' the world as water is in a glass.
    DO NOT equate it with SARTREAN CONSCIOUSNESS.  The for-itself is still a
      CONSCIOUSNESS FACING BEING - precisely the framework this structure
      dissolves.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 32/53: HEIDEGGER - BEING-IN-THE-WORLD AND WORLDHOOD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE HYPHENS ARE THE DOCTRINE
    BEING-IN-THE-WORLD (In-der-Welt-sein) is a UNITARY phenomenon - a SINGLE
    hyphenated structure, NOT three separate elements (subject + relation +
    world).  It is intended to DISSOLVE the Cartesian split between an inner
    subject and an external world.
    Dasein does NOT first EXIST and then subsequently ENCOUNTER a world; it
    IS constitutively IN a world - practically engaged, absorbed, concerned.
    CONSEQUENCE: the Cartesian BRIDGE PROBLEM ('how do I know the external
    world exists?') DOES NOT ARISE - the gap it bridges was an ARTEFACT of
    the decomposition.  Heidegger treats the PERSISTENCE OF THE DEMAND as the
    scandal, not the lack of an answer.
                                   |
  WORLDHOOD (Weltlichkeit)
    The 'WORLD' is NOT the totality of physical objects (the universe of
    natural science).  It is the REFERENTIAL CONTEXT OF MEANING within which
    things show up AS SIGNIFICANT.
                                   |
  THE TWO MODES
    READY-TO-HAND (Zuhandenheit) - THE PRIMARY MODE.  Entities are met as
      EQUIPMENT (Zeug) that we USE in a practical context.  The hammer is NOT
      first 'an object with properties' and THEN 'used for hammering'; it is
      ORIGINALLY encountered as EQUIPMENT-FOR-HAMMERING, within a totality of
      references.
    PRESENT-AT-HAND (Vorhandenheit) - entities THEMATISED as OBJECTS WITH
      PROPERTIES; prominent in THEORETICAL INQUIRY.  DERIVATIVE.
    THE CLAIM IS ABOUT ORDER OF FOUNDATION: the theoretical stance is a
    MODIFICATION of practical engagement, NOT the neutral baseline from which
    practice is a special case.  THIS REVERSES THE MODERN ORDER OF
    EXPLANATION and is the real content of the doctrine.
                                   |
ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 33/53: HEIDEGGER - THE REFERENTIAL TOTALITY AND BREAKDOWN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE REFERENTIAL TOTALITY - THE 'IN-ORDER-TO' (Um-zu)
    HAMMER --in-order-to--> drive the NAIL --in-order-to--> fix the BOARD
    --in-order-to--> build the HOUSE --in-order-to--> SHELTER
    --for-the-sake-of--> DWELLING, i.e. A WAY OF BEING FOR DASEIN
    EVERY piece of equipment REFERS BEYOND ITSELF.  THERE IS NO SUCH THING AS
    ONE PIECE OF EQUIPMENT.  THE CHAIN TERMINATES NOT IN ANOTHER TOOL BUT IN
    A POSSIBILITY OF DASEIN'S OWN BEING.
                                   |

    UNUSABILITY  the hammer is too heavy, the head is loose

  THE PRELIMINARY THAT DECIDES THE ANSWER
    AUTHENTICITY (Eigentlichkeit) IS NOT A MORAL VIRTUE, AND INAUTHENTICITY
    IS NOT A MORAL FAILING.  They are EXISTENTIAL MODIFICATIONS - modes of
    Dasein's SELF-RELATION.  Dasein is PROXIMALLY AND FOR THE MOST PART

    BOTH 2019 Q3(a) AND 2022 Q2(a) DIRECTLY DEMAND THIS CLARIFICATION.
                                   |

    Dasein exists WITH OTHERS (Mitsein) and tends to lose itself in what
    'ONE' (man) does, says, thinks - the ANONYMOUS PUBLIC.
    DAS MAN IS NOT 'OTHER PEOPLE'; it is the IMPERSONAL NORM-SETTING ANYONE
    in whose name things are done - and in whose name NOBODY IN PARTICULAR IS
    ANSWERABLE.

      IDLE TALK (Gerede) ....... passing along received opinions WITHOUT
                                 genuine understanding [of DISCOURSE]

                                 never dwelling [of SIGHT]
      AMBIGUITY (Zweideutigkeit) everything SEEMS accessible, nothing is
                                 genuinely appropriated [of UNDERSTANDING]
    FALLING (Verfallen): Dasein's tendency to be ABSORBED in the world of its

    BLAME.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 38/53: HEIDEGGER - WHAT AUTHENTICITY IS NOT, AND ITS CRITICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  FOUR THINGS AUTHENTICITY IS NOT
    NOT 'living a morally good life'
    NOT 'being true to yourself' in the popular sense
    NOT rugged individualism or social withdrawal - INDIVIDUALISATION BY
      DEATH DOES NOT ABOLISH BEING-WITH; it MODIFIES HOW DASEIN OWNS ITS
      ALREADY SOCIAL POSSIBILITIES
    NOT contemplating suicide or fearing the empirical event of dying
  IT IS: a FORMAL modification of everydayness - OWNING one's THROWNNESS and
  projecting upon one's OWNMOST possibilities.
                                   |
  THE STRONGEST OBJECTION: FORMAL EMPTINESS
    Authenticity prescribes NO CONTENT - so, critics say, A NAZI COULD BE
    'AUTHENTIC', a charge sharpened by HEIDEGGER'S OWN 1933 COMPLICITY and by
    ADORNO'S 'JARGON OF AUTHENTICITY'.
    HEIDEGGER'S REPLY: authenticity is DELIBERATELY NON-PRESCRIPTIVE; it
    tells you HOW to hold your existence, not WHAT to choose.
    HONEST VERDICT: THE FORMALITY IS DELIBERATE AND IT IS THE DOCTRINE'S
    DEEPEST PROBLEM.  A criterion that tells you to OWN your choices without
    telling you WHICH to make can DIGNIFY ANY RESOLUTE COMMITMENT.  The
    defence - that FUNDAMENTAL ONTOLOGY IS NOT ETHICS - is TEXTUALLY CORRECT
    AND PHILOSOPHICALLY UNSATISFYING.
  SECOND OBJECTION: MORBIDITY AND THE OTHER.  LEVINAS - the ethical relation
  to the Other, not death, is primary.  ARENDT - NATALITY against MORTALITY.
  REPLY: death is not morbidity but the DISCLOSURE OF FINITUDE that makes ANY
  authentic engagement possible, INCLUDING CARE FOR OTHERS.  Levinas's
  alternative remains a POWERFUL COUNTER and should be NAMED, not dismissed.
  PYQ ANCHORS: 2019 Q3(a) 20m (first half); 2022 Q2(a) 20m (second half).

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 43/53: FEAR AND ANXIETY - THE ARGUMENT AND THE NATURALIST REPLY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE ARGUMENT IMPLICIT IN ALL THREE
    1 MOODS are not private colourings added to a neutral world; they are
      ways in which one's situation is DISCLOSED.
    2 FEAR discloses an entity as threatening, and so KEEPS US WITHIN the
      world of concern.
    3 ANXIETY has NO entity as its object; therefore it CANNOT disclose an
      entity.
    4 YET it plainly discloses SOMETHING, since it has definite content and
      is not mere confusion.
    5 THEREFORE what it discloses is not an ENTITY but a STRUCTURE.
    6 THEREFORE anxiety is philosophically PRIVILEGED: the one mood in which
      existence AS A WHOLE becomes an issue.
  PRESUPPOSITIONS: P1 moods are COGNITIVELY DISCLOSIVE, not merely affective;
  P2 the difference between an INDEFINITE and a DEFINITE intentional object
  is one of KIND; P3 what a rare mood discloses is not thereby LESS REAL.
  NATURALIST OBJECTION: anxiety is a pathological or neurochemical state with
  no cognitive credentials.  REPLY: the claim is not that anxious people KNOW
  MORE, but that the STRUCTURE of the mood - HAVING NO OBJECT - is itself the
  datum requiring explanation, and no account in terms of an unnoticed object
  has succeeded.  VERDICT: a mood can DISCLOSE A STRUCTURE without becoming
  INFALLIBLE COGNITION; naturalistic and clinical explanations remain
  COMPATIBLE COMPETITORS.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 50/53: THE ANSWER SPINE AND THE QUALIFIED CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DIRECTIVE DECODER - WHAT EACH COMMAND WORD OBLIGES YOU TO DO
    'COMMENT / ELUCIDATE THE STATEMENT'  locate the sentence IN ITS OWN
      THINKER'S SYSTEM, then unpack the technical terms.  Identify the work,
      the PAIR OF TERMS it turns on, two consequences, a judgment.
      FATAL: treating it as a general maxim about life.
    'DISCUSS EXISTENCE PRECEDES ESSENCE'  no divine artisan -> no
      predetermined blueprint -> persons take up projects IN SITUATIONS.
      Then Heidegger's rejection.  FATAL: attributing the slogan to all.
    'CRITICALLY EXAMINE AUTHENTICITY'  ASSESSMENT DOMINATES, and the NON-
      MORAL reading is the marked point.  FATAL: 'being true to yourself'.
    'DISCUSS BEING-IN-THE-WORLD'  THE HYPHENS ARE THE DOCTRINE.  Refuse the
      subject/object split FIRST, then Zuhandenheit/Vorhandenheit, the broken
      hammer, care, and ONLY THEN temporality.  FATAL: 'a human being in the
      world'.
    'HOW DOES KIERKEGAARD OPPOSE HEGEL?'  a STRUCTURAL opposition.  Existing
      individual vs the system; either/or vs both/and; the leap vs mediation;
      indirect communication vs the lecture.  FATAL: 'he disliked
      abstraction'.
    'EXPLAIN FREEDOM AND DETERMINISM IN SARTRE'  MEANING-CONFERRAL together
      with FACTICITY.  FATAL: pretending coercion or material limits are
      unreal.
    'FEAR AND DREAD/ANXIETY'  compare object-directed fear with existential
      anxiety WHILE PRESERVING THINKER DIFFERENCES.  FATAL: one mood theory.
    'ASSESS / DO YOU AGREE?'  A DEFENDED RULING IN THE FIRST LINE.  FATAL: a
      neutral survey.

ASCII MASTER FLOW - PANEL 51/53: THE GRADED VERDICT BANK - CLOSE AT THE RIGHT GRADE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GRADED VERDICT BANK - CLOSE AT THE RIGHT GRADE
    EXISTENCE PRECEDES ESSENCE
      10 Sartre's atheistic argument denies a predetermined blueprint; it
         does not erase biological or social facticity.
      15 ...It is SARTRE'S slogan, not existentialism's common creed.
      20 The doctrine foregrounds project and responsibility but leaves
         value-criteria and situated constraint underdeveloped.
    RADICAL FREEDOM
      10 Freedom is the for-itself's non-coincidence with facticity,
         exercised ONLY IN SITUATION.
      15 ...Motives acquire action-guiding significance WITHIN a project.
      20 Coercion, trauma and material structures pressure early radicalism;
         later scarcity and seriality broaden Sartre without retracting.
    BAD FAITH
      10 It evades the TENSION of facticity and transcendence.
      15 ...Pre-reflective, non-thetic awareness is the proposed answer to
         the self-deception paradox.
      20 Influential but NORMATIVELY INCOMPLETE: positive authenticity
         remains thin.
    THE LOOK
      10 Shame discloses myself as object for another subject; not a proof of
         other minds.
      15 ...Improves on inferential models but may generalise conflict.
      20 Love, solidarity, de Beauvoir, Fanon and later Sartre pressure
         subject/object exclusivity - BOUNDED criticisms, not replacements.

  QUALIFIED CONCLUSION - THE POSITION THIS PACKAGE DEFENDS
    'Existentialism' names a SHARED REFUSAL, not a shared doctrine: no
    general essence settles in advance what it is for THIS individual to
    exist.  Beyond that refusal the three printed thinkers diverge at every
    point that matters - on God, on method, on what freedom is, and on what
    authentic existence would be.  Kierkegaard's account is powerful against
    systematic closure and exposed to the FIDEISM charge and to a thin
    treatment of community.  Sartre's is powerful on project and
    responsibility and underdeveloped on value-criteria and situated
    constraint, as his own 1960 revision concedes.  Heidegger's is the most
    rigorously ontological and the most normatively empty, and the defence
    that fundamental ontology is not ethics is textually correct and
    philosophically unsatisfying.  An answer that says all this - and says it
    with the right dates, the right pseudonyms and the right section numbers
    - has done what this clause asks.
