Scope note
----------
The printed clause names two thinkers and two limbs, and the limbs are not
shared: the critique of empiricism is Quine's printed burden and the theory of
basic particulars and persons is Strawson's. The two men are not allies —
Grice and Strawson defend, in "In Defence of a Dogma" (1956), exactly the
distinction Quine attacked in 1951 — so the only legitimate joint statement
is that empiricism failed twice over, about how theories are confirmed and
about how we so much as refer. The clause owns 9 of 112 Western Philosophy
question-parts in the 2018-2025 corpus, split Quine 5 and Strawson 4, with two
parts each in 2018 and 2021, one each in 2019, 2020, 2023, 2024 and 2025, and
none at all in 2022; the clause's only 20-marker is Strawson's, in 2023. The
verification principle, Ayer, Carnap's Aufbau and the Vienna Circle belong to
Topic 07 and enter only as the position Quine dismantles; Hume's impressions
and ideas, the bundle self and induction belong to Topic 03; Kant's synthetic a
priori and transcendental deduction belong to Topic 04 and enter only through
the 2018 framing and Strawson's affinity; Russell's theory of descriptions
belongs to Topic 06 and enters only as the rival "On Referring" answers;
meaning as use and the private-language argument belong to Topic 08 and enter
only as a bounded comparison; Strawson's "Freedom and Resentment" (1962), his
treatment of induction and his Kant interpretation are bounded or cross-owned
and own no part of the corpus; and personal identity over time, the soul and
immortality belong to Paper II. Quine's ontological commitment, radical
translation and gavagai, inscrutability, indeterminacy, ontological relativity
and naturalized epistemology are taught in full as bounded system-enrichment
and are deployed only after the printed two-dogmas burden has been discharged.
