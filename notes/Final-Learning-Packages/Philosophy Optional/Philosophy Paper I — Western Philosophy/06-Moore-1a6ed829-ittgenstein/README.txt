Scope note
----------
The printed clause names three thinkers and seven doctrines, and it is
the only Western clause in which attribution is itself examinable. Moore
owns the defence of common sense and the refutation of idealism; Russell
owns incomplete symbols, logical constructions and logical atomism;
early Wittgenstein owns the picture theory and saying and showing; and
logical atomism is jointly listed because Russell's 1918 lectures and the
Tractatus are two different atomisms. Moore's naturalistic fallacy and
Open Question Argument belong to ethics, the verification principle to
Logical Positivism, meaning as use and the private-language argument to
Later Wittgenstein, and Strawson's positive semantics to Quine and
Strawson; each appears only as a bounded boundary note. Russell's
paradox, the theory of types, later neutral monism and the solipsism
remarks appear only to owned depth in the clearly labelled optional
block.
