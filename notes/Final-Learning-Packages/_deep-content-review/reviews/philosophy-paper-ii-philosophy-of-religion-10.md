## Findings

### 1. Critical — Aquinas's doctrine is materially misstated

The session says that analogy of attribution means `"God is good" because God is the cause/source
of goodness in creatures`. In *Summa Theologiae* I q.13 a.2, Aquinas explicitly rejects a
cause-only account: goodness is predicated of God substantially and eminently, not merely because
God causes goodness.

The package also presents `analogy of attribution` and `analogy of proportionality` as Aquinas's
own fixed two-type taxonomy. These are useful later Thomist, especially Cajetanian,
systematisations, but should not be attributed without qualification as Aquinas's settled
classification.

Affected source:
`upsc-ai-kit\knowledge\Philosophy\paper-2\philosophy-of-religion\Religious-Language.md`
and generated session PDF p.8.

### 2. Critical — PYQ-essential Core material is placed under Optional Advanced

The learning-session coverage table explicitly routes canonical owner §§9–19 to Optional
Advanced, while the same document labels much of this content `CORE` or `PYQ-TRIGGERED CORE`.

The Basic teaching must include:

- Aquinas's complete analogy mechanism;
- Tillich's complete symbol/sign and Being-Itself account;
- verification, falsification and eschatological verification;
- Braithwaite, Hare, Mitchell and Wittgensteinian use;
- Advaita negation, indication and the exact *anirvacanīyatā* distinction;
- secular versus religious use, including Austin, Evans and Ramsey;
- the mechanism and counter-case for symbolism leading to mysticism;
- the cognitivist–non-cognitivist spectrum;
- Core comparisons, objections, traps and factual discipline.

True Optional Advanced material can retain Radical Orthodoxy, fictionalism, specialist metaphor
debates, the pluralism bridge and surplus comparative enrichment.

### 3. High — Wittgenstein is flattened into settled non-cognitivism

The Basic session presents Wittgenstein as teaching that religious language is non-factual
language-game use. His lectures distinguish Last-Judgment belief from ordinary empirical
prediction, but whether this entails non-cognitivism remains disputed. `Language-game` and
`form of life` are safest as Wittgensteinian developments; D. Z. Phillips expressly resisted the
non-cognitivist/reductionist label.

### 4. High — current research is real but misidentified

The ICML page exists and supports the substantive Sanskrit–English semantic-drift illustration.
However, `Machine translation for civilizational knowledge systems` is the opening wording of the
abstract, not the paper title.

The official page title is:

`AI as Cultural Mediation: Agentic Sanskrit–English Translation with Linguistic Grounding`

It is an ICML 2026 workshop contribution. The session must provide its exact title, event status,
URL, access date and authors where available, and clearly label it a current research
illustration—not doctrinal evidence.
