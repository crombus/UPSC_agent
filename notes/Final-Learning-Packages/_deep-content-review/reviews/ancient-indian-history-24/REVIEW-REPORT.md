## Blocking defect found on final audit (reopened)

- Every "Demand decoding" line for the 3 PYQ routes and 6 original Mains models
  read `The directive **answer** requires a direct position on ...` regardless
  of the item's actual directive verb (Discuss, Examine, Compare, Trace,
  Justify/"why must", explain-style "how did"), violating directive fidelity.
- Several quoted item headings were truncated mid-sentence with an ellipsis
  (`"...preserved..."`) instead of naming the item's actual content.
- The "Executable exam-length answer / compression plan" and "How to improve
  this answer" lines were generic templates that varied only by marks value
  (10/15/20) or repeated "add one additional named site, text, inscription,
  coin, ruler or scholarly position" verbatim regardless of the item.
- The Adjacent PYQ 3 scaffolding block was misplaced after the "Original
  solved Mains questions" section header instead of directly under its own
  item.
