## Residual limitations retained

- The broad c. 300 BCE–300 CE frame is heuristic, not an exact synchronized period.
- Literary rulers and deeds remain source-qualified; poems are not court annals.
- Pattanam is a major Muziris candidate, not a conclusively named identification.
- Imported ceramics and coins cannot independently establish resident identity, total trade volume or Roman political control.
- Megalithic assemblages are not ethnic labels.
- Dependency is evidenced, but a uniform slavery system or numerical scale is not recoverable.
- The Kalabhra interval remains a sparse, later-source problem rather than a proven total dark age.
