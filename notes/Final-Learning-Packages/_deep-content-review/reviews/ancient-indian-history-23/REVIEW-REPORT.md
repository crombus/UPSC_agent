separate. All 22 sessions now have a topic-specific definition, unique
answer-grabbing line, five or six examinable keywords, visual-first explanation,
named evidence and an explicit paragraph demonstration. The former live-link
padding was folded into the front-matter status note; Session 22 is now the
UPSC answer architecture, ownership-boundary and memory-map synthesis.

The standalone workbook was rebuilt in place to exactly 32 original hard MCQs
before PYQs. The key sequence is
`ABCDABCDABCDABCDABCDABCDABCDABCD` (A 8 / B 8 / C 8 / D 8). It contains 128
separately tagged option-specific explanations and 32 unique examiner
traps/remedial targets, with no duplicated remedial bank and no stem overlap
with the learning session's distinct 16-question loop.

PYQ discipline is explicit:

- 2024 GS-I Q2: exact locally verified wording; 10 marks; 150 words; complete
  137-word model answer.
- 2025 Prelims GS-I Q14: exact locally verified wording/options; official Set-A
  key A.

  extracted code/options are defective and the official key is unavailable, so
  only the historically inferred order `2-4-1-3` is stated.
- 2026 Prelims GS-I Q4: official paper verifies the stem and four temple
  statements; the solution assesses all four statements and infers statements
  2 and 3, but code/options are not reconstructed and the provisional key is

claim-evidence-analysis-qualification: two 10-mark answers (128 and 132 words),
two 15-mark answers (198 and 186 words), and two 20-mark answers (201 and 198
words), all within their stated 150/250-word limits.

Sources checked in order were the canonical Basic and Advanced owners, embedded
official syllabus/PYQ routing, the locally held official 2020/2024/2025/2026
papers and 2025 key, R.S. Sharma's OCR-searchable chapter 28 (principally PDF

early-medieval agrarian sections (principally PDF pp. 1468-1479 and 1530-1544,
with locality/economy checks at pp. 1577-1583). Qdrant was not required.
