## Coverage preserved and taught

Period and evidence discipline; second urbanisation and asynchronous regional pathways;
agrarian base (Sanghol archaeobotany, Deccan paddy/cotton, occupational villages);
textile, dyeing and oil chains (Mathura *sataka*, Uraiyur and Arikamedu dyeing vats,
oil-miller records); durable crafts (Ujjain agate/jasper/carnelian, Telangana iron, ivory,
Roman glass, glass-blowing, moulds and wasters); artisans and settlement (*Mahavastu* 36
worker categories, *Milindapanha*, Karimnagar quarters); *shreni*, *nigama*, *sarthavaha*,
*setthi*, the *kumhakara seniya* and *shulaphalayikanam* sealings, Maharashtra guild deposits,
the Mathura flour-makers' endowment and the banking-analogy limit; internal trade, *chattu*
caravans and market-node functions; *Uttarapatha*/*Dakshinapatha* corridors and the
Taxila–Mathura–Ujjain–Bharuch line with the Kaushambi link; ports, monsoon regimes and the
port-hinterland model; Mediterranean exchange, Pliny's rhetoric and the colony-claim limit;
coinage series, civic and *negama* issues, Pali denominations and the monetisation limits;
urban morphology (Sirkap, Sirsukh, Hastinapura, Sunet); northern/central and Deccan/southern
centres; urban–rural reciprocity with asymmetry; mobility within hierarchy; religion and
trade; state as enabler/extractor/participant; the transformation-versus-decline debate;
historiography and regional comparison; and the bounded heritage/evidence-recovery linkage.
