ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/12: Three designed traits, one package
NEUTRALITY -> impartial service to any elected government; not the absence of policy influence
PERMANENCE -> continuity and institutional memory across changes of political government
ANONYMITY -> confidential advice; the minister answers publicly for the policy outcome
RULE -> the three were designed together; weakening one destabilises the other two
MUST REMEMBER: Civil services provide continuity, expertise, coordination, implementation...

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/12: Neutrality: what it is and what it is not
IS -> impartial implementation of lawful policy irrespective of personal preference
IS -> the reason the same officer can serve successive, ideologically different governments
IS NOT -> silence, passivity or the absence of technical and expert policy advice
IS NOT -> loyalty to the government of the day rather than to the Constitution

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 3/12: Constitutional setting card
ARTICLES 309 TO 311 -> recruitment, service conditions, pleasure doctrine, dismissal safeguards
ARTICLE 312 -> Parliament may create an All India Service after the Rajya Sabha resolution
GOVERNANCE USE -> tenure protection is the legal basis on which neutrality rests
BOUNDARY -> commission composition, appointment and removal detail is Polity-owned

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/12: Three layers of civil-service legitimacy
STRUCTURAL -> neutrality, permanence and anonymity built into the design
PROCEDURAL -> conduct rules, vigilance machinery, disciplinary process, appraisal
PERCEPTUAL -> sustained public belief that unelected discretion is exercised impartially
CLAIM -> the first two can be formally sound while the third erodes independently

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 5/12: Six pillars of the capacity programme
1 POLICY FRAMEWORK -> the reform intent and its stated shift to role-based learning
2 INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK -> including the Capacity Building Commission
3 COMPETENCY FRAMEWORK -> behavioural, functional and domain competencies mapped to roles
4 DIGITAL LEARNING FRAMEWORK -> the online platform that delivers modules at scale
5 ELECTRONIC HUMAN-RESOURCE MANAGEMENT -> the personnel data spine of the reform
6 MONITORING AND EVALUATION -> the pillar on which the verdict must ultimately rest

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/12: Output against outcome
OUTPUT -> enrolment, course counts, completions, certificates on a live dashboard
OUTCOME -> competency-to-post fit and trained behaviour visible in work products
OUTCOME -> service timelines, grievance recurrence, appeal outcomes, tenure stability
DISCIPLINE -> no platform total is asserted anywhere; the number changes daily

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 9/12: Generalist, specialist and lateral entry
GENERALIST -> breadth for coordination across sectors and levels of government
SPECIALIST -> depth for technology, finance and climate policy complexity
LATERAL ENTRY -> bounded, periodic recruitment at Joint Secretary, Director, Deputy Secretary
LIMIT -> shorter contractual tenure and weaker socialisation into permanent-service norms

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 10/12: How neutrality erodes
UNCONSTRAINED POSTING AND TRANSFER DISCRETION
-> officers anticipate the political executive's preference before advice is asked for
-> permanence protects the office, not the post: displacement is easier than dismissal
-> anonymity turns asymmetric: public blame in controversy, invisibility in achievement
-> perceived partiality erodes the legitimacy of unelected discretionary power

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 11/12: Reform conflict ladder
FIXED MINIMUM TENURE -> repairs arbitrary transfer; can entrench a poor performer
PERFORMANCE ACCOUNTABILITY -> repairs non-performance; can become a political lever
LATERAL ENTRY -> repairs domain deficit; raises representation and memory questions
CONTRACTUAL MISSION-MODE STAFFING -> buys speed; weakens tenure and neutrality guarantees
