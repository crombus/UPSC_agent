ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 7/10: Society and polity: evidence without invented kings
EVIDENCE: house variation + scarce goods + craft control + standards + public works
                         |
      +------------------+------------------+
      |                  |                  |
centralised state?   city/regional states? corporate or mercantile elites?
      +------------------+------------------+
                         v
NO secure palace, royal tomb or deciphered king-list; hierarchy existed, form is open.
VERDICT: coordinated institutions are certain; empire-level sovereignty is unproven.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/10: Script, seals and religion: evidence ladder
SECURE: short signs, seals/tablets, motifs, ritual installations, burials, figurines
PROBABLE: identity/transaction marking and differentiated ritual practices
DISPUTED: script language; proto-Shiva; mother-goddess system; universal fire altars
REJECT: reading later Hindu doctrine directly into every Harappan image.
                         |
                         v
Undeciphered script blocks direct access to office, belief and political vocabulary.
Use object + context + distribution before analogy.
