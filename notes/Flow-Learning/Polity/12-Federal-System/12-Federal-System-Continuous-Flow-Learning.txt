ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 1/9: Federalism: constitutional autonomy within an indestructible Union
FEDERAL SYSTEM
  +-- Constitution divides power between Union and State governments
  +-- each level operates directly on people within assigned fields
  +-- independent judiciary interprets the division

INDIA: ARTICLE 1 -> UNION OF STATES
  +-- federation is not founded on a secession-permitting compact
  +-- Union is indestructible; State areas, boundaries and names are alterable by law
  +-- holding-together design creates a stronger Union and asymmetric arrangements

CLASSIC FEDERAL FEATURES
  +-- dual polity
  +-- written and supreme Constitution
  +-- constitutional division of powers
  +-- rigidity for federal provisions
  +-- independent judiciary
  +-- bicameral federal representation

TRAP: alterable States remain constitutional governments, not Union departments.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 2/9: Why India has a Union bias without becoming unitary
CENTRALISING FEATURES
  +-- Union List breadth and residuary power with Parliament
  +-- Parliament's exceptional access to State subjects
  +-- emergency provisions and Article 356
  +-- Governors appointed by the Union
  +-- All-India Services
  +-- integrated judiciary, election machinery and audit architecture
  +-- single citizenship and flexible State reorganisation

FEDERAL CHECKS
  +-- State List autonomy and elected State governments
  +-- Rajya Sabha and State ratification for entrenched changes
  +-- independent judicial review
  +-- Finance Commission / GST Council / intergovernmental bargaining
  +-- federalism protected as Basic Structure

VERDICT
Union strength supports integrity and coordination; unchecked use can become central domination.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 4/9: When Parliament enters the State List
PARLIAMENT ON STATE SUBJECTS
  +-- Article 249: Rajya Sabha national-interest resolution
  +-- Article 250: during National Emergency
  +-- Article 252: two or more States request legislation
  |     +-- other States may adopt later
  |     +-- Parliament alone amends or repeals that law
  +-- Article 253: treaty / international agreement implementation
  +-- Article 356: during President's Rule for the State
  +-- Article 246(4): territories not included in a State

CONTROL QUESTIONS
What is the trigger? Who initiates? How long does authority last? Who can amend or repeal?

CLOSE OPTIONS
249 begins in Rajya Sabha; 252 begins with State resolutions; 253 can reach State subjects.
Exceptional Union access does not permanently erase the State List.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 6/9: How federalism works: cooperation, competition, bargaining and asymmetry
MODE             MECHANISM                         BENEFIT / RISK
cooperative      GST Council, councils, schemes     coordination / Union leverage
competitive      investment and policy innovation   learning / unequal capacity
bargaining       coalition and fiscal negotiation   accommodation / opacity
confrontational  courts, consent withdrawal, veto   rights assertion / paralysis

SHARED-RULE INSTITUTIONS
Rajya Sabha | Inter-State Council | Zonal Councils | GST Council | Finance Commission
courts | All-India Services | inter-State dispute mechanisms

ASYMMETRY
special constitutional arrangements respond to history, geography and tribal autonomy
asymmetry != inequality without reason; it needs constitutional source and purpose

LOCAL GOVERNMENT
73rd / 74th Amendments deepen federal participation
but local bodies are not a co-equal third sovereign tier under the Constitution.

ASCII MASTER FLOW — PANEL 8/9: Representation, Governors, CBI and current federal friction
REPRESENTATIONAL FEDERALISM
  +-- Rajya Sabha gives State voice but unequal population-based representation
  +-- Lok Sabha delimitation can redistribute federal political weight
  +-- defeated 131st Amendment Bill's 850 ceiling is not current law
  +-- census conduct dates are not publication or delimitation dates

GOVERNOR ASSENT: 20 NOVEMBER 2025 ADVISORY CONTROL
  +-- no judicially fixed timelines
  +-- no deemed assent
  +-- prolonged unexplained inaction may attract a direction to act
  +-- court cannot dictate the constitutional choice

CBI CONSENT
  +-- DSPE section 6 ordinarily protects State territorial police autonomy
  +-- constitutional courts may order investigation without State consent
  +-- 2024 West Bengal maintainability ruling did not decide every merits issue

REFORM: consultation, neutral offices, reasons, transparent finance and timely forums.
