## Clause 12: Cārvāka — theory of knowledge and rejection of transcendent entities

**Controlling words (official item 12):** “Carvaka : Theory of Knowlegde; Rejection of Transcendent Entities.” Original spelling retained in the controlling file. **Primary owner:** `upsc-ai-kit\knowledge\Philosophy\paper-1\indian\Carvaka.md`. The 2026 bank assigns no part to Cārvāka; its Nyāya–Vaiśeṣika Q5(a) is not reassigned here merely because inference can be compared.

The two literal syllabus halves form an **inference**: limiting valid knowledge to perception undercuts independent warrant for soul, karma, God and post-mortem goods. The link must be argued, and the additional premise "only independently warranted entities should enter ontology" made explicit. A strict refusal of inference and a pragmatic restriction of supersensible inference are distinguishable readings, not interchangeable historical claims.
